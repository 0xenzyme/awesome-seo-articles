# Introducing our global Google+ page for webmasters

We've recently launched our global
[Google Webmasters Google+ page](https://google.com/+GoogleWebmasters).
Have you checked it out yet? Our page covers a plethora of topics:

- Official news and announcements like
  [when our blog launched en français](https://plus.google.com/+GoogleWebmasters/posts/YHbxCGrmBpM)
- Tips such as
  [how to handle `404` errors correctly](https://plus.google.com/+GoogleWebmasters/posts/VByNiTLZh6r)
- [Q&A office hours with our team](https://plus.google.com/+GoogleWebmasters/posts/dy8p9QtQDf5 class=)
- Exclusive behind the scenes including
  [planking puns](https://plus.google.com/+GoogleWebmasters/posts/jWyrQhFpJrK)
  and
  [adventures with Googlebot](https://plus.google.com/+GoogleWebmasters/posts/cQaXMKQGTzv)

Follow us at
[google.com/+GoogleWebmasters](https://google.com/+GoogleWebmasters)
and let us know in the comments what else you'd like to see on our page! If you speak
[Italian](https://plus.google.com/communities/112294948416370608274),
[Japanese](https://plus.google.com/communities/115069764931066832848),
[Russian](https://plus.google.com/communities/107952911147065863501)
or
[Spanish](https://plus.google.com/communities/107174145581457353344),
be sure to also join one of our webmaster communities to stay up-to-date on language and
region-specific news.
