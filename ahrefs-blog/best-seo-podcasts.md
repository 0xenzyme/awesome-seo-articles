# The 15 Best SEO Podcasts in 2025

This isn’t another run-of-the-mill roundup of the “best” SEO podcasts out there. I polled Twitter, and trawled through the recommendations to find quality ones that are still producing regular episodes. Then I topped up the list with a few of my personal favorites.

![Rebecca's tweet asking for suggestions on popular, under-the-radar podcasts](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/image1.png)

From broader discussions on SEO to more technical deep dives, these 15 podcasts are ideal for every kind of marketer.

## 1. The Ahrefs Podcast

**Host:** [Tim Soulo](https://twitter.com/timsoulo)
**Topics:** SEO, marketing
**Frequency:** It varies
**Avg. episode time:** 90 mins
**Website:** [Ahrefs Podcast](https://ahrefs.com/podcast)
**Listen on:** [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/4RQV6OqWt54aZyLyyWaKB0), [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/ahrefs-podcast/id1735946821), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ahrefspodcast), [Amazon Music](https://music.amazon.com/podcasts/96178e66-4a82-46c8-9b30-ffb1f1a6c9b9/ahrefs-podcast), [Audible](https://www.audible.com/podcast/Ahrefs-Podcast/B0CXJVY967)

Looking to learn from the brightest minds in online business and digital marketing? Ahrefs Podcast is for you!

In each episode, Tim Soulo (Ahrefs’ CMO) interviews one smart marketer, digging into their career, philosophy, goals, and more.

In SEO, it’s easy to get distracted chasing algorithm updates and testing trending content tactics. I love that this podcast takes me out of that bubble, showing me high-level strategies that actually work, and the wider business implications of content decisions.

For example, in an episode with [Dom Wells](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8EDZkjun1I), CEO of Onfolio, I was super interested to learn that companies who flip sites built on low-quality [PBNs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/types-of-backlinks/) can face post-sale administrative clawbacks, where money gets forcibly returned to investors. It was a great reminder of the real-world implications of shoddy content practices!

You can tune-in wherever you get your podcasts but, in my opinion, it’s worth watching the recorded interviews on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ahrefspodcast) or [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/4RQV6OqWt54aZyLyyWaKB0https://open.spotify.com/show/4RQV6OqWt54aZyLyyWaKB0) for the production value alone.

Our awesome team of video editors (who also edit [Ahrefs YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/AhrefsCom)) have made the podcast seriously watchable.

Plus there’s a swish studio set-up, and some funny bloopers you might miss on audio!

## 2. The Authority Hacker Podcast

**Hosts:** [Gael Breton](https://twitter.com/gaelbreton), [Mark Webster](https://twitter.com/markwebster1)
**Topics:** SEO, marketing, content marketing
**Frequency:** Weekly
**Avg. episode time:** 60 mins
**Website:** [Authority Hacker](https://www.authorityhacker.com/show/)**Listen on:** [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/1D6MzYn5pFiNlr8aNaoqEn?si=187496afeee64fa2), [Soundcloud](https://soundcloud.com/authorityhacker), [Apple Podcasts](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/authority-hacker-podcast-learn/id1073349789?mt=2)

This well-loved podcast is equal parts casual and informative. Gael Breton and Mark Webster make a convivial pair, and discuss all things SEO in between witty banter.

Apart from covering Google updates, Gael and Mark tackle SEO questions and share their experiences running authority websites and blogs—including how they built and sold one of their websites for a six-figure sum.

## 3. SEO Unplugged

**Host:** [Itamar Blauer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/itamarblauer/), SEO Director at SEO Agency [StudioHawk](https://studiohawk.co.uk/)

**Topics:** SEO

**Frequency:** Monthly

**Avg. episode time:** 30 minutes

**Website:** [Itamarblauer.com](http://itamarblauer.com)

**Listen on:** [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLu5vi5reT_6LSJQ_g8sVf9oiagtkTBcje), [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/28O7xTSiSOvKGao1qatz5r?si=ff7f78fd0fae46f7), [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/seo-unplugged/id1614500878), [Google Podcasts](https://podcasts.google.com/feed/aHR0cHM6Ly9hbmNob3IuZm0vcy84MDA5N2NiOC9wb2RjYXN0L3Jzcw)

If you’re looking for an SEO podcast that cuts straight to the chase, then this is the one for you.

Each month, Itamar interviews a different SEO expert, diving deep into their specific area of knowledge and asking smart questions to guide the conversation.

Tune-in for fresh takes on SEO developments—like in episode #25, when [Lily Ray dissects](https://youtu.be/Q3ToMDN-IXI?feature=shared) the Helpful Content Update which, instead of boosting authentic content, actually [erased many independent and affiliate publishers](https://ahrefs.com/blog/affiliate-publisher-site-reputation-abuse/) from the SERPs.

In the episode, Ray advocates for search results that showcase diverse voices rather than limiting online experience.

So far, there are ~30 episodes, with some impressive household names—including [Aleyda Solis](https://youtu.be/Rna_si_SFeY?feature=shared), [Kristina Azarenko](https://youtu.be/7_fKmOWeiOs?feature=shared), and [Mark Williams-Cook](https://youtu.be/kIY0r9LKgaM?feature=shared).

## 4. SEO Campfire Chat

**Host:** Noah Learner

**Topics:** SEO

**Frequency:** Bi-monthly

**Avg. episode time:** 60 minutes

**Website:** [The SEO Community](https://theseocommunity.com/events/campfire-chats)

**Listen on:** [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/1JKb6rgI9hNwKDYCDtYpRu), [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/ca/podcast/seo-campfire-chat/id1802260479), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHkzMU1PJwlEAEUm2VDkK8ipqEgbX67hD), [Amazon Music](https://music.amazon.es/podcasts/bc26fc5f-5b68-4a69-911d-f7a3962d8d80/seo-campfire-chat)

SEO Campfire Chat is a podcast and YouTube series hosted by Noah Learner, the founder of one of my favorite Slack groups: [The SEO Community](https://theseocommunity.com/).

Each episode features in-depth conversations with SEO experts, focusing on their workflows, decision-making processes, and the challenges they face every day.

If you want practical, hands-on SEO tips, look no further. In an episode with [Dana DiTomaso](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfHx8DypA4E), for example, you can learn how to [track traffic from AI and large language models](https://ahrefs.com/blog/track-analyze-ai-traffic/) (LLMs) in GA4.

Another thing I love about this podcast is that Noah digs into his expert guests’ most unconventional or controversial opinions.

For example, [Jes Scholz discusses](https://youtu.be/ZZ5I80FGPAM?list=PLHkzMU1PJwlEAEUm2VDkK8ipqEgbX67hD&t=117) why she’s a rank-tracking skeptic, while [Rand Fishkin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PF_zdpnAYLU) myth busts the idea that LLMs are a threat to Google.

Definitely worth a listen!

## 5. The SEO Patent Podcast

**Host:** [Mark Williams-Cook](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/markseo)

**Topics:** SEO

**Frequency:** Weekly

**Avg. episode time:** 15 minutes

**Website:** [The SEO Patent Podcast](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_today-on-the-seo-patent-podcast-notebooklm-activity-7273334852408557569-urP8?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAABoEm7QBekJrKEnvC5H6nTAmYwOb4R1k5GE)

**Listen on:** [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/1Iwm2ZLAbXu8QjroU8auZ2), [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-seo-patent-podcast/id1779944089), [iHeart Radio](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1333-the-seo-patent-podcast-239121013/), [Rephonic](https://rephonic.com/podcasts/the-seo-patent-podcast)

While I’m in no doubt of the incredible potential of [Google’s NotebookLM tool](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-ai-marketing-tools/#notebooklm), there’s still a part of me that balks at the AI-generated chat show feature—it’s all a bit Black Mirror!

But I can put aside my angst for [The SEO Patent Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/1Iwm2ZLAbXu8QjroU8auZ2), created by the formidable Mark Williams-Cook. It’s the one wholesale AI podcast I will listen to from start-to-finish.

Mark takes [Google’s latest search patents](https://patents.google.com/?assignee=GOOGLE+LLC&oq=GOOGLE+LLC&sort=new) and feeds them to NotebookLM, translating highly technical documents into a fascinating SEO podcast.

In other words, he uses Google to decode Google.

If you listen, you’ll get exclusive insights into the algorithm, and have a much better understanding of where search is headed.

For example, lately there’s been some debate over the [importance of links when it comes to ranking](https://ahrefs.com/blog/impact-of-links/). In a recent episode, we find out that [Google still very much uses PageRank](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7312801437749596161-O_EU?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_android&rcm=ACoAABoEm7QBekJrKEnvC5H6nTAmYwOb4R1k5GE) and link graphs to rank documents on the web.

NB. the AI chat show hosts make some pretty hilarious filler noises throughout the podcast, in an attempt to replicate real-life conversational backchanneling cues—like in the first 10 seconds of [this episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4CkEfofZQnNHHT3jzS41tN?si=3a058ff3fa2e45b0). I’d tune in for this alone.

Looking for succinct SEO tips but short on time to trawl the web? Turn to Voices of Search.
