# How to Track Local SEO for Multiple Locations with Semrush

Do you need to reach more customers in more than one city or town?

Let’s say your plumbing company wants to expand outside of your base location. With keyword research, you can see where the most demand is in your local area and target potential customers living in that zone.

This guide will show you how to start. We’ll cover how to search keywords locally and track your SEO in multiple locations.

## Why Track SEO in Multiple Locations?

Local search results can vary between cities or even neighborhoods.

For example, someone searching for a plumber in downtown Philadelphia will see different Google results than someone searching from a neighboring town like Bryn Mawr, Camden, or Collingswood.

![map with multiple cities for SEO campaign to target](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/31/c6/31c6f837b9944c70823b0db181bab535/2cee5d1463bc1ac0240a4744206d1e99/AD_4nXd4KbCokaT44LijDOSTLp7r6qIHGV7IvjjXj4lz9dNWCMVNc0ZZEKQuWx9Ij5nXh4zKkG6cZXIWapKKa06vsXJmDOuc_hRsu0yfKWH9UTTJnTO4pjs38neM_9DNG8n-OnZafFy3Jw.png)

Depending on the size and nature of your business, you’ll want to know how your SEO is performing in each area.

In general, there are two common scenarios where multi-city SEO tracking is crucial:

- **Multi-location businesses**: If your business has multiple locations or stores, each with its own Google Business Profile (GBP), track how each ranks locally.
- **Service-Area Businesses**: If you serve multiple cities from one location—like a plumbing company with one office but clients in surrounding cities—track your performance in each city to assess local visibility.

## Search Keywords Locally

Before tracking rankings, it helps to know the search demand in each target location.

Keyword research shows how many times people search for something like “plumber near me.” For example, if there are 200 searches a month, that means people search for plumbers in your area 200 times every month.

Most keyword research tools default to national search volume, but Semrush allows you to zoom in on a specific city or region. Here’s how to find local keyword data:

**Open the [Keyword Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/) tool** in Semrush (found under Keyword Research).

In the search bar, enter your target keyword (1). For example, let’s say you want to check the term “plumber near me” in different cities.

![keyword overview tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/06/0a/060a330ce0ddbba15e3c46411b0e92cf/ae44e8bde1473d8d57415261c637272a/AD_4nXeAXIzyJoyLcuAVHp3unvR0NILX1rkGOdWseHLNEEiGGw89lx6YSg6wQGlQfcFHxVBJKUXh0k1OQEX4r7PIv5tQ8Du_jhxLaoeMBhXkGF5l9tT_pL0wwA7YbV0DKqHjqucRJ_Aa.png)

Then**,** click the location drop-down and type in the city name, county, or even postal code (2).

**Now you can review the local metrics for this keyword.**

![keyword overview results page for "plumber near me"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7c/f5/7cf512dfbe330f6eb698f819c3bb8b9e/13e7f167a4d3b00825f485cf70723e43/AD_4nXcUs90t8sZRC6bQOcwJ9YMF-VTg3u1XfAZ4FJt4mFzduO1W5W-fc4jtDcSoiCGQ8sQpuLEQXo699-8XWfJO4Q97d9APy_MJeb0C92nAmtrFufTTyPaQHY38Yh7mpya-YCk7Ch6p.png)

The Keyword Overview report will now show the monthly search volume *for “plumber near me”* specifically in San Francisco, along with other metrics like Keyword Difficulty and SERP Features.

You can see how the local keyword metrics compare to the U.S. totals/averages. In this case, the keyword difficulty is higher and the cost per click is twice as high as the national average ($40.14 vs. $20.57).

Want to compare keyword metrics in one city to another?

Just change the location to another city and the volume will update accordingly – giving you a quick way to compare interest level by city.

![local results in keyword overview](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/69/45/69455f08059f0abb0c0b5be0e4375e6c/db6d25775ae0d81c5d90abed78676a0d/AD_4nXfIVvZ_zlWA0WTHzPpqI31qrYcPDEuhkbH_ju4u_l_RibNz_91wA_bkRTNs-vLCwBYpJ7IcrXjkiFyI9SW_BV7ATcMsH5kAllJHRk6gdvax2aAXF_E6jMj2vAnM8LGAh3RQgiMxoQ.gif)

For instance, here we see “plumber near me” has varying levels of volume, difficulty, and competition in several suburbs around Philadelphia.

Repeat this process with search terms around your services and notice which keywords have higher volume in which cities. These insights can guide your content and local SEO strategy.

For example, if “plumber near me” is surprisingly popular in one particular suburb, you could create a page targeting that locale or adjust your GBP to highlight your services there.

## Benchmark Your SEO in Each City

After doing keyword research, set up tracking to measure where your site stands in multiple target cities.

To do this on Semrush, take the following steps:

1. Set up a [**Position Tracking**](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) campaign and target a specific city showing local volume
2. Add your target keywords and start tracking
3. Once your campaign is running, click on the link below the project name and find the “+ Add new target” option

## Identify Competitors in Multiple Areas

Tracking rankings in multiple cities also reveals who you’re up against in each market.

Often, you’ll find that each city has its own set of top competitors.

On Semrush, this can be done in two ways:

1. **For Competitors in Different Cities, Regions, or Devices: Use Position Tracking**

After setting up your campaign with target keywords and multiple locations, the Competitors Discovery report identifies the top competitors in each area.

2. **For Competitors in Different Neighborhoods within a City or Region: Use Map Rank Tracker**

This tool (available in the Local Toolkit) lets you track your local rankings on a grid overlaid on a map. And, once it's set up you can check out who the biggest competitors are in each specific area.

### Identify Local Competitors Across Multiple Cities with Position Tracking

To identify local SEO competition across multiple cities, use Position Tracking

If you have a Position Tracking campaign set up, you can review the **Competitors Discovery** report to see your local competition.

![position tracking competitors discovery and location](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ef/8e/ef8eadaac70a65fc98cf8a7d81c8878b/06aa50b1ee39cc58bf2fdeb9b8f8f2b8/AD_4nXcAJvsj2tjIa3mZoSRlhEAADdkYxYWWwlysgGBl26WdeSsT5liQxSNNlFamAF0NQcu96ZmWLSLeQwFThcutYG-MIzhDGJ2tRnM14KnheosZObR15Z_cKm1rUWevu7nEEqQlwajkkg.gif)

### Identify Local Competitors in Your Area with Map Rank Tracker

If you run a restaurant or local shop, tracking competitors in your immediate area is more important than analyzing businesses across multiple cities.

Why?

Because your customers are mainly looking for nearby options. Someone searching for "coffee near me" isn't comparing your shop to one in another town.

To find these cross-town competitors, use the [Map Rank Tracker.](https://www.semrush.com/map-rank-tracker/)

Go to the tool and enter your business name and location to get started.

![Map rank tracker](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c4/ef/c4ef46d22681f03dd14b51228c7d3325/8d86f597e09afc3642364211259fd884/AD_4nXd6K08BLXE4bNmczw9mOHrlVi9ImHjQJcGkvoquwQXZqzyXVK-pRK8TVk3qHu5PmkzlaxSRlnVmjnNEjb1-HYxYN_jeD7KFNIXJCNZgkeWxt-VFabf5bGgxXjTXvRxFphXqrC-SpQ.png)

Set up a campaign by:

1. Entering your target keywords (or choose from those suggested by AI)
2. Selecting the radius (typically you choose your service area, which could be a few blocks for a cafe and a city for a car rental business, for instance).
3. Selecting your grid size (5x5, 7x7,11x11, 13x13, or 15x15)
4. Choosing your schedule (how often you want your rankings to be collected: daily, weekly, monthly, custom)

Hit > Run campaign and wait a couple of minutes.

Once the data is collected, you can see your Heatmap report.

## Analyze Your Competitor’ Top Landing Pages by City

If you want to know how your competitors are getting ranked ahead of you, just take a look at their landing pages. You might discover some ideas for your own strategy based on what works well on their website.

Finding a competitor’s top-performing landing pages on Semrush is easy. Just take the following steps:

- Use the “Pages” tab in Position Tracking
- Select a competitor’s domain from the drop down
- Sort by Estimated Traffic or keyword count
- Click to inspect high-performing pages
