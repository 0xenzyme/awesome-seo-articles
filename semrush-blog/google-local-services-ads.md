# Google Local Services Ads: What They Are & How to Set Them Up

## What Are Google Local Services Ads?

Google Local Services Ads are paid ads that help local service businesses collect leads directly through phone calls and message requests.

They only appear for relevant local businesses to the person searching. And at the very top of Google’s search results.

These ads can display the company’s name, reviews, ratings, city, phone number, opening hours, and more.

Like this:

![Google Local Services Ads](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/27/a6/27a65301dcf575bf5dfc4c3427ff9de5/dhI-5kBDhpwod9GDq7knRpERQ6thE4CkpeiwFetqjdnMvgYFgtjVC6JArj-5fxqsZ-Hmu1dDRRqHP2sm0Ju28wdrH3g2jg7fZ1qOnU0oRcbmvBO379EyzeEt0RGF2drg5qZ5_ITp9OEuuq8tmdFe58w.jpeg)

So businesses can connect with qualified leads, ready to convert.

## The Difference Between Local Services Ads and PPC

You pay per lead with Local Services Ads (LSA). Unlike pay-per-click (PPC) advertising, where you pay when someone simply **clicks** on your ad.

There’s no direct keyword bidding on ads for Local Services. You can instead let Google set your bid. Or manually set your own maximum bid per lead.

Another important difference is where your ads appear.

PPC ads show up above or below Google search results.

Like so:

![PPC ads](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e2/98/e298c76cb31d39da8e36e842b8ee11d9/ne7Pn13EGpkukRg_yGEyPhwKq7pGB2u0glPMZPgqvxzH5MHB0dhEcAqtFqkIP0t5VfsIoa_X8E0I7s_oDE8JgXZjjUE1Q95mZymoNHBTLLd2izXacdU4PAsCfTDrOFguc95uJ9Ym4wez-JM9p9VP2Bw.jpeg)

And on other Google search partner websites.

![Google Search Partner Ad](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/cf/1d/cf1dcddd21c51440dcf6c4031b2d80b6/9QFPWQNFB83red2GMqOZ4UjbCLLQujEDA9zZyt1CO1AzDBr6WiYmhbu57YRtBh2LRkntXz-xeKMT_oL_MC0q0I2y1yxyN6hbjwJnzLqXoj1b50d565fZSwq_od8JQIPXI6ZSItTsOkVi1xdiNhAW3Dc.jpeg)

Whereas Local Services Ads always appear at the top of Google’s search results.

Like this:

![Local Services Ads in google serp](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/27/a6/27a65301dcf575bf5dfc4c3427ff9de5/dhI-5kBDhpwod9GDq7knRpERQ6thE4CkpeiwFetqjdnMvgYFgtjVC6JArj-5fxqsZ-Hmu1dDRRqHP2sm0Ju28wdrH3g2jg7fZ1qOnU0oRcbmvBO379EyzeEt0RGF2drg5qZ5_ITp9OEuuq8tmdFe58w.jpeg)

Plus, with PPC, you need to do the hard work of keyword research and A/B testing to show up for the right search queries and find customers.

But with LSAs, Google automatically generates your ads and determines rankings by your review rating, response rate, and proximity to a searcher’s location.

And you’ll only pay if a customer calls, books, or messages you directly through the ad.

**Note:** Google LSAs shouldn’t replace PPC ads or organic listings. They simply provide a way to reach different customers at a different stage of the marketing funnel..

Then, [set up your Local Services](#how-to-set-up-google-local-services-ads) account and wait for approval.

## How Much Do Google Local Services Ads Cost?

Local Services Ads are pay-per-lead and can cost around $25 USD per lead. Costs can vary widely. Your exact LSA cost will depend on your location, industry, and competitors.

For a rough estimate, check out Google’s [cost tool](https://ads.google.com/intl/en_us/local-services-ads/cost/).

![Google’s cost tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/95/a6/95a6486426e927a37e6254d87975c7e3/lqPy_DAg6ECjcODyR-o6ZP4Hxw4nvDz5RO3LQgCQsWYbnlrqahsuVs5YcLU-K5nxz0gBxgqsU696Qgh-CFfAZqLuzio0_r6AC6TteByBkfpczZIFfmkkvlDHS0N9h72f7tyhrLNxuDN-AZEFwIsgzf4.jpeg)![Google’s cost tool estimated monthly budget](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/20/56/205635fd0c515e84a13f8ceac875b839/33LN3kdNfGRazwG9zyZg9vCqwb0IbY6M_zDPLH7lRUwVBx_1PMw69atzDZRu8W-GfQr0SUgzjUYOz0vs1SNWZQwW_q8jrPWdUBiv3rOvV4PmQo7f_dCgt32CZ90-hh0AAJRgEDwQ90r1BS5FORas0fA.jpeg)

The great thing about Google Local Ads, though, is that you control your budget.

You set your budget based on the number of leads you want per week.

And once you reach your monthly limit, your ad stops showing. Unless, of course, you increase your budget.

## What Is Google Guarantee?

The Google Guarantee is a badge that appears in Local Services Ads for home service providers. It shows potential customers that Google has vetted your business.

Only home service businesses that have passed Google’s screening and verification process can get a badge. Which can give customers peace of mind.

This is what the Google Guarantee looks like:

![Google Guarantee badge](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/56/0c/560c3ce0df185be57878af8f17d5b749/_OVUIA5ZjZfbVy16SUfekWyEg_RQtDDp9uj46BezSJ5BZp3mYnt2LONJ6cY-XLL_CQhvStEPhOAbcgrUfbSqEaOUPJ3KIGH7PNWnksLv6O45OoKE7B5tOR4m2cnqWXbMsaec7ZjFL1EzCvWgd7U9BNs.jpeg)

An additional benefit of the Google Guarantee badge is that Google may reimburse customers who are dissatisfied with the quality of your work. At no cost to you.

These are the lifetime reimbursement limits for each country:

- United States: $2,000

- Canada: CAD $2,000

- United Kingdom: GBP £1,500

- Ireland: EUR €1,500

- Spain: EUR €1,500

- France: EUR €1,500

- Italy: EUR €1,500

- Germany: EUR €1,500

- Austria: EUR €1,500

- Belgium: EUR €1,500

- Switzerland: CHF ₣2,000

**Note:** Customers must book services through Local Search Ads to be eligible for reimbursement. And reimbursement is solely at Google’s discretion.

Google will not reimburse customers for add-ons or future projects, damages to property, dissatisfaction with price or provider responsiveness, or cancellations.

Read [Google’s reimbursement guide](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7125526) to learn more about how the process works for customers.

The Google Screened badge covers professional services, including the following:

## How to Set Up Google Local Services Ads

Setting up Google Local Services Ads is easy.

Here’s the process, step by step:

### 1. Create Your Profile and Check for Eligibility

Set up your account by navigating to the [Google Local Services Ads page](https://ads.google.com/intl/en/local-services-ads/).

If you already have a Google Ads account, click “**Sign in**.” If you don’t, click “**Get started**.”

![Google Local Services Ads homepage](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3e/12/3e1247de972a8fa8fb3bf851328c92e8/0npcw1H0P-fIpxOgbW-82xAgaVaSMq-rBOsMVt3Tc0M0StwXV2oAob0z3rMwEvlkzPdXKP4K990ldTu8d-kK2QpgrJcNZwintPU1D4g9y0str6KVhA7DCDMN6ypMVva9PoesTy7eZbsSY4TxTaJzoGc.png)

Next, fill in your location information and job (services) category. Click “**Check Eligibility**.”

![Check Eligibility button highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/84/de/84dece40dcde1c1c00ab29b1a17ff5d3/7eQDEwB4yVEJAYAITf7pENt_JjupjRrWOy-LQfgthr1dPl-QH3vyayvDnl0pUW22hTcNpEKsd-63FQBQxkbTIL6ODj-8Q2v0sSCuZ45iJPMxeJa3-bslZx8GKPOSBUCzoxY02AQIR_RhVp03eF9ld_s.png)

Then, create your profile by filling in all your business details. Including name, address, website, service areas, service types, business hours, and more.

Some fields are optional. But the more information you give Google, the better your chances of getting approved.

![Business details in Google Local Services Ads](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/77/e5/77e5c6075cdcccc0ceae45f1a568ac0e/sloMqo6ayKBdxZUA56ySEKgssnWgjUFtLrWnv9PgRoSLvj3oPfjoXE6w-NZm8FpSusm7bZX6VK9CzY310gHwmnKmsrXwWPwDQJwxrCSUu0OC4ARLSmpXrujj2E1Pw0H4iYht1MMbw-ltxFrEUwo8rbI.jpeg)

The final step is a generic preview of your ad and how it’ll be displayed.

You can’t edit anything in this step. It’s just a preview of what your ad can look like in Local Services Ads.

![Preview your ad page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/14/0d/140d2930673dd005caef7e293773e149/muMM-EFluCiDNqDGqzuTdIBROr0sph3kt33Mo3V-7xArc_KtqYaPTkh0WZPaAQw1FENcvaHXKWLKllN_D0iWxkrTa5nMa19Bawu6WvxyHz8bubISqQ0vGrIVhz8YlH4N6mpdMsxzUla548iPoUYEfMo.png)

### 2. Prove Your Business Is Trustworthy

In the next step, you’ll get the chance to let Google know your business is trustworthy.

![Verify your business page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/83/ee/83ee8405c2158bf24d609deac4aa6c20/Sl7sDgZlmPp0e2WPceyugGytFxu5ybZ3RKJYPfQcLeFLbraFRwyOBUDp4sVTwXnCCIGWhRKcGmtQ291a-gPyyDMz7T2eOLK1S9CRqiKFHyVkTZZvcjtrhbSk75Mg-E0gakDuCBd6iRYqhYXHT9fuifc.png)

Depending on your location and service category, you’ll be asked to provide more or less information.

For example, Google may ask for details regarding your business license or proof of insurance.

![Business License page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/3a/1a3a578bd31139a4225dc9e7b801dc44/ByzTnZkRk0uvm10IBc2uQfrB-FetBhX2x9aKvDq4F9EUl7yCjXt7tUTGNAyLqSxTPrtllK3-Iy5f0osMqCNfHvmv02acX1vM1kDsJ-8dF60QsFtrj-u2c4h_JRUmbbBcS7F1aOQedLLdZp8YrJI06us.png)

Then, with all the information you’ve entered, Google will automatically start looking for reviews on your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/).

**Note:** Your review score and the number of reviews play a big role in how Google ranks your business.

![See link options button](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/88/7e/887e2c0d0bcef09de2e645461a1edf5c/dVQNSOf5KrVZXvLOk9Fbt3-JP3OdhCqKVtn-NW2oC9C2dVQrB7UoudC56ln25YA2FrXqNzbccd0_yyuYzs3qeSTPhiRPNfODlKjz9hZbPGiaPmByqTfINxCmEvojvRNnP7kXkK6XGBpChLivJw0eL6o.jpeg)

![Budget page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/22/95/2295052980b2f371a450545a38c1152d/DSDGz6rAkYjf-B9vHEZYTDnhXpL4FutBPWsPgfFq2Z0x-LHVZKtUOWY7nGP_07tWowHVkrYVLB7VyjFbLbbF99XIklI-deYNY0ByOz6f9xi8kO3loZjdkER6V35JsIiJ_1DEzJPqxsvxbvRYyrtfwFc.jpeg)

The final step is adding your billing information.

![Billing information page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0f/34/0f3482ddc4765a092ee1a70e9d2c987b/XyErSJl5NBlDv1KDMRBb-D1Fv84y3GELA-l6F75vSPIG5jhhL4YpCAx65Rf3wATSMnt6nYewGVCXD1q_0meFfyLGqsAA9tws5RJ6_In1YIOxZUqAzyC-8xRg7xvQhOGEbXtddhCpB5Tri55Dp9A2g7E.jpeg)

If you already have a Google Ads account, Local Services can use your existing payment information.

If you don’t, fill this section in.

And you’re done!

### 3. Wait for Approval

Google’s screening and verification process can take anywhere from a couple of days to weeks.

It depends on your business type and the state, province, and country in which you operate. (Among other factors.)

To learn what’s required to complete your screening and verification, check out [Google’s requirements page](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/12174778?hl=en&ref_topic=10491040).

There, scroll down to select your country and business category.

## How to Manage Your Local Services Ads

Once approved, you’ll see your account in the “Live Accounts” section within your dashboard.

But Google Service Ads don’t launch automatically.

To start running ads, click on your “**Account name**” and then hit “**Edit Profile**.”

Then, you’ll see a switch button that says, “Your ad is paused.” Toggle it on to launch your Local Services Ads.

### Manage Your Account

After turning your ads on, scroll down to view and edit your account details.

The “**Budget**” box shows your bidding mode, weekly budget, and leads.

![Budget box](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0d/ae/0dae170f61cd7e00b5af931a8a087f38/BJWPsw7GBsB13OoSUjPvWJyMijFONdRImthYDT5LxGaTY-uPXfnYlxTN77AjAsNaZW3Znu1FvEACD-qsocbchbqcDQtZW9tXNqoN1H-Wz1itVNsTjGKQklX2RCuR873udtRFEiKnprkclXQwsjVIEpU.png)

### Manage Your Leads

Leads are folks who have shown interest but aren’t customers yet. So, the sooner you follow up, the higher your chances of converting that lead into a customer.

To see (and manage) your leads, click on “**Leads**” in the left-hand column.

![how to navigate to leads](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/96/b7/96b75ed44501f4bfd5cc8a04d8f82687/VXdJHXezJnZpQjFv09aK3qJPluw-T9Fv4GaD14OWMhKsk7PoC9ybD0zIJmBAvihluOKIDB7r9NLNjKTpbyZ0eF-JbGBz0dzevjF_eEjES1Ayqb9VeeG0jejiuztuEx4Lk6_XkalfsMn4Oui7bREwON8.png)

**Note:** You can also manage your leads via the Local Services app on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.ads.homeservices) and [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/google-home-service-ads/id1069142544?mt=8).

This dashboard shows your new, active, booked, and completed leads.

As well as a list of each customer, job type, location, lead type, and last activity.

If you click on a lead, you’ll see a summary.

Including the status, lead type, business category, and a few sections for you to add additional information.

You can even listen to call recordings (U.S. and Canada only).

Here are the steps you can take after reviewing your leads:

- Reply by sending your message as an email or text message (U.S. and Canada only)

- Call the lead based on the phone number they listed

- Decline the request

Once you respond to a lead, their status becomes “Active.”

You can find all active leads in the “**Active**” section of the dashboard.

After you book a job, mark that lead as “booked” to track upcoming jobs.

Like so:

This feature helps you schedule upcoming appointments, send confirmation emails, and gather customer reviews.

### Manage Your Reports

To review your budget and lead performance, head to the “**Reports**” section.

![how to navigate to reports section](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d6/ff/d6ff45e90c0544e0fc923796894c304e/rGPjJE30Ra0YPLwXgZqD6XYfEaAUEOCTOHQ_T6iaVjzjFodhFrjNb_-Blft3KUKcwSPAJ54SXQ4_WJ7wNDQJvBQHkD2pKZ_NAk1KrwoFaVXfqkb99PuTaIi59rKe-6B5fmN0VG3d3qg8oxiYPym7GrQ.jpeg)

You’ll see an overview of your budget, how many leads you’ve been charged for, and any appointments booked.

![Overview page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/32/e5/32e56e2a57e746e61991b5dafffaf98b/0DTXP-tYdg7mcPPvkV0saYDCUft-yrHHJ9-iITFDxVRiH687tD7MMo4z4gCFK3H4Q8L5o54CF3kphxjn7moF4J9SlHjhIb_yExXEbPskJM5MiALXTVzmi9D0qeqFKpfKMIHhYwfxZ9MOQMGYVuIRlWY.jpeg)

This specific information can help you evaluate whether the leads coming in are high-quality. Or if you’d need to tweak anything to get better results.

Plus, this is data coming directly from the ad platform. Something you don’t get with regular Google Ads campaigns.

You can also use the drop-downs to change the “charge status” and “lead status.” And get more granular information.

### Manage Your Reviews

Your star rating and number of reviews impact how Google ranks your business within Local Services Ads.

Businesses with higher ratings and more reviews stand out. And tend to book more jobs.

Which means: When managing your Local Services Ads, one of the most important elements is [generating reviews](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/how-to-generate-reviews/). And following up with them.

To start, go to the left-hand column and click “**Reviews**.”

You’ll see an overview of the reviews you’re getting.

And a list of the jobs you’ve completed for your customers.

Click on “**Ask for review**” under the “**Action**” column to ask customers about leaving a review on Google for you.

Customers are more likely to leave a review if the request comes directly from you.

So don’t be afraid to reach out and ask customers directly for [Google reviews](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/how-to-get-reviews-on-google/).

Also, reply to every review. It shows customers you value their business and feedback.

But you can’t respond to any reviews from your Local Services Ads account.

You’ll need to do that directly on your Google Business Profile. Or use a tool like Semrush’s [Listing Management](https://www.semrush.com/listings-management/start/).

## Improve Your Local Services Ads Performance

Though Google Local Services Ads always show up at the top of Google’s search results, there is still limited space.

Up to three Local Services Ads can appear on desktop. And two on mobile.

Meaning you need to optimize your profile as much as possible to get higher rankings.

Google ranks Local Services Ads by evaluating the following:

- Your maximum bid amount per lead

- Your proximity to the searcher’s location

- Your responsiveness to customer questions and requests

- Your review rating and number of reviews

So, play around with your budget to find that range where you feel comfortable paying for the leads that are coming in.

Being responsive is very important for optimizing local services ads. If you’re unable to answer phone calls after hours, use an AI receptionist like [Voice Assist](https://www.semrush.com/apps/voiceassist/).

And pay very close attention to your reviews. Ask for as many reviews as you can. And reply to all of them in a timely manner.
