# Voice Search Marketing: What It Is & Why You Need a Strategy

Quick Summary

- Voice search marketing is fast becoming the primary consideration for optimising data and content for digital marketers
- Massive improvements in voice search accuracy, as well as the adoption of home-based devices like smart speakers, have driven its growing popularity
- Most voice search users are looking for local businesses, so being in complete control of your listings can improve your chances of appearing in results

---

## What Is Voice Search Marketing?

Voice search marketing is the optimization of data and content with the intention of it appearing in voice search results (Google Assistant or Apple’s Siri, for example).

![Examples of voice search assistants: Google Assistant, Siri, Copilot, and Alexa.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f7/cb/f7cbbf28b217bd0d6238b3fad27571c5/992dbf6bcb868e254807fbf58c1b9288/AD_4nXekoahsEDJ1wTMvI_epIp5BwF66UHXEKdaMkmFzd7OJests7s1al6XBDmScdBIEIGw8_kOYyYmC93vkoTiJ6Qh4aAHGkcnVetmCdCJd4F1xq1gu3crE301xDdiNqDHQ0mJzKnxrtw.png)

What started out as funny videos of completely misunderstood voice search queries has quickly progressed to a 95% accuracy rate (in English) and a widely used method for getting fast, convenient information.

Voice search marketing has fast become one of the hottest topics for content marketers and SEOs. A massive [72% of US residents](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/library/consumer-intelligence-series/voice-assistants.html) who are aware of voice search have used the functionality, so optimising for it is essential for getting eyes on your business.

## Why Is Voice Search Marketing Important?

With vast improvements in accuracy has come a much bigger uptick in users leaning on voice search for hands-free convenience.

![Google Assistant response to a hands-free voice query about how to cook an egg.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ae/2f/ae2f5ef7e5d8685507d077053c3d2cfb/65ef7244fbe957ed05e1fd25f6fb170f/AD_4nXcfLr9dTnFboTeAdtbiMh7exfp5C4cQnOT6QUUZjjCzoz7TjSDAtTxFtg526JmmEbAKDvtpPnHfrfp6eiBYaLuy2dUnC6BVZo0eCsUt_qUodpe1UE85WCdNGR1tLa3uTS1saEN0.png)

Consumers no longer want to waste time typing out their search by hand, especially when on the move, and marketers need to consider this carefully when crafting content.

Smart speakers have normalised voice commands in our home lives, with online shopping and appliance control by voice becoming commonplace, which in turn has made users more comfortable with voice searches in general.

## How Is Voice Search Different Than Traditional Search?

Typed searches tend to be minimalist, using the fewest words possible, such as “best bakery Toronto.”

But with voice search, the user expects a more conversational exchange and will likely ask a grammatically correct question like “which is the best bakery in Toronto?”

So to rank for voice search, you’ll want to craft the most natural-sounding response so it feels like a real person has understood and answered the question.

Voice search digital marketing is all about providing the best possible answer for questions, as well as having a great review generation strategy to rank for questions like “what’s the best cocktail bar near me?”

## Tips for Optimizing for Voice Search

Writing in a concise, natural tone is key to getting your content to rank for voice search.

According to Semrush’s 2020 Voice Search for Local Businesses Study, 23 words is the sweet spot for voice search results across all assistants (Google Assistant goes a bit deeper and returns an answer of 41 words on average).

As many as [70% of voice search results](https://www.semrush.com/blog/voice-search-local-seo/) are pulled from featured snippets, so the same best practices that apply to ranking in this coveted Google real estate also apply to voice search marketing.

Build content around commonly searched questions and use the queries as headers to organize your text. You can also leverage [schema markup](https://www.impactplus.com/blog/what-is-schema-markup-and-how-to-implement-it) to make your most important and commonly searched data clearer to search engines.

![A featured snippet that might rank for voice search.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/40/28/4028539b728f8850c3e3b331431b6d4d/278c0e1a250acce279016e5d346f467e/AD_4nXdbjUPaVovsE1hmDIkjmZnbyY6g_fBLrZsFGjT3XSH3ynU5hoqXmUGD67F_tuQk7kwGafUGnXUM-k37yziz4eLHgOZQPnAmOOGalKzcBU72LNRLPlB8vP1j8rhmfhAWLeezjv7E.png)

SEO health factors like quality backlinks and a high authority score are also deciding factors in appearing in voice search results.

But don’t try to rank for voice search by simply gaming the algorithms; focus on the human being on the other end of the search and what they would like to hear spoken back to them.

### Build Citations and Links to Boost Rankings

![Getting into Google’s local snack pack will help you rank for voice search.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a5/62/a5626de857175dc6cf92a2566b57c0b6/cd35e50e0bf263f602a1fa89d527b4f1/AD_4nXcQ0xHvW9q1XFsq5kk4gbbPuCD4_S1OlZ2XyxJZTFOs5fZBO1c2EjS5KJH6_NL3AmGuUthoamvdo67Yy11rrD80630XFVXG4TC11XSB0rb9lcfB9rzopA9oFtEChVLtK9x1twwV.png)

While location and distance from the user are important ranking factors, citations and links also play a key role. Search engines want to be sure they’re displaying accurate data on your business, so will need to check several reliable sources for your name, address and phone number (NAP). If they can’t see multiple citations, or find inconsistent data, they’re far less likely to recommend your business to a voice search user.

Voice assistants simply aren’t going to feature low-ranking results in their responses. Getting your business in the map pack (the top local listings) is essential if you want to stand a chance of being the answer to a user’s local query.
