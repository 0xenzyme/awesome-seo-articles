# Search Central Live Deep Dive Europe 2026: Meet the community speakers

Wednesday, September 16, 2026

It's happening! We are absolutely pumped that we're just about two weeks away from
[Search Central Live Deep Dive Europe 2026](/search/blog/2026/07/search-central-live-deep-dive-europe-2026)!
From **September 30 to October 2, 2026**, the Google Search Central team is heading
to the gorgeous city of **Barcelona, Spain** for three days of deep technical
exploration, networking, and collaboration.

We just finalized the agenda, and while a large chunk of the content is going
to be the same as last year's Bangkok event (the underlying foundation of Search isn't changing
THAT much year over year), we are also bringing brand new, mostly AI focused topics. Why? A
"few" people said they're interested in AI; weird, but OK :)

But let's talk about the real stars of the event: **our community speakers**.

Apart from Google speakers, we are once again handing the mic over to an incredible lineup
of experts from across the industry. Building on last year, our community presentations will be
delivered in two distinct formats to ensure every speaker can share their work in the way that suits
their topics best.

## What is a Lightning Talk vs. a Poster Session?

If you've attended previous Search Central Live events, you're likely familiar with some of these
formats, but here is a quick refresher:

- **Lightning Talks:** These are fast, high-energy, 7-minute presentations delivered
  from the main stage. The speaker focuses on a highly specific case study, technical solution, or
  core learning, using a small number of slides. It's quick, punchy, and keeps the energy in the
  room at a maximum. It's designed to spark ideas and kick off conversations that continue
  organically in the hallway and throughout the days.
- **Poster Sessions:** If you're looking for a dedicated room to dig deeper rather
  than catching someone on the run, this is where the magic happens. Popularized in the academic
  and scientific conference world, poster sessions are a vibrant, interactive marketplace of
  ideas. Instead of delivering a slideshow on stage, presenters display their findings on large
  physical posters (which we print for them!). During the session, attendees browse the poster
  hall at their own pace, stopping to engage in deep 1:1 or small-group discussions directly with
  the presenter for about 40 minutes. It's the ultimate format for complex technical diagrams,
  troubleshooting workflows, professional development insights, and meaningful networking. We've
  had a BLAST with this format in Bangkok, and we're committed to have the same experience this
  year also!
