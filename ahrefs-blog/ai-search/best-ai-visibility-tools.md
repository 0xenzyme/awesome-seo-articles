## 1. Waikay

**Best for:**Finding granular instances of AI brand misinformation

![](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-12.40.48.jpg)

I recently got a demo of [Waikay](https://waikay.io/) from SEO expert [Dixon Jones](https://dixonjones.com/about/) (ex-[Majestic](https://majestic.com/)) at BrightonSEO, and immediately saw its value for AI brand protection. The tool works by building a knowledge graph of your brand (using [inLinks](https://inlinks.com/) data) and compares it against what LLMs understand about you.

What stood out to me was how it tackles misinformation. Waikay extracts specific facts that LLMs assert about your brand and lets you flag incorrect ones, as an internal reminder to take further action and resolve the inaccuracy—crucial for reputation management.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

Gemini (both grounded/RAG data and training data), Claude, ChatGPT, Sonar

### Features

- **Fact verification**: The platform helps you identify incorrect information LLMs state about your brand, allowing you to track and address inaccuracies.
- **Topic benchmarking**: Compares your brand against competitors across individual topics, highlighting coverage gaps.
- **Link health analysis**: Evaluates the quality of sources citing your brand, not just counting mentions.
- **Real-time monitoring**: Captures brand presence across different LLMs with snapshot saving in paid plans.

### Pricing

- Free plan for basic monitoring (monthly)
- Single brand: $19.95/month (8 reports)
- Multi-brand: $69.95/month (30 reports)
- Agency: $199.95/month (90 reports)

## 2. Ahrefs Brand Radar

**Best for:** Enterprise AI search monitoring across all brand platforms

[Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar/) is huge database of 263M+ monthly prompts prompts which can help you track your brands, competitors, and core topics in AI.

You may have already started tracking a handful of self-engineered brand prompts to see how you show up in AI platforms.

Whilst that is definitely useful for things like fact checking and understanding how AI perceives your brand, it’s often just not enough data to account for all of the [variance that comes with tracking AI](https://ahrefs.com/blog/you-cant-track-ai-like-traditional-search/).

There’s also the issue that you’re engineering what you *expect* to see, rather than what might actually be popular.

We built Brand Radar with these kinds of things in mind:

- It shows you your AI visibility across tons of prompts and responses that you wouldn’t have thought to monitor (your unknown, unknowns).
- It lets your monitor your visibility across thousands of conversations—not just a handful—to smooth out all of the variance that comes with AI tracking, and give you the clearest visibility signals.
- It seeds each prompt with real search demand data—not synthetic assumptions—to give you an idea of the topics that are popular and most likely to matter, so that you can prioritize them.

[Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar) is the best AI search tool for monitoring your brand holistically in AI, search, and across the web.

I’ve been using it since its beta launch, and constantly discover new use cases—like spotting gaps in our AI Overview visibility vs competitors, and tracking co-mentions of our brand alongside relevant topics, to understand our share of that market.

It even helped me track the impact of Google’s March Core Update on the visibility of AI Overviews, when [AI overview visibility doubled.](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-growth/)

### The AI data sources behind the tool

You can track your mentions across a total of eleven different indices, all in one dashboard:

1. [Ahrefs Site Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/site-explorer) for organic search data (or “Search demand”—i.e. your branded keywords and associated traffic)
2. [Ahrefs Content Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/content-explorer) for web results (or “Web visibility”—i.e. your brand mentions in content across the web)
3. YouTube (referred to as “YouTube visibility”—i.e. your brand mentions in YouTube videos)
4. TikTok
5. Reddit
6. AI Overviews
7. AI Mode
8. ChatGPT
9. Gemini
10. Perplexity
11. Copilot

And now you can track all of your custom prompts within Brand Radar too.

Meaning you can dive deeper into your niche, go further into the funnel, and build out the AI Visibility insights you need to see by adding prompts that aren’t already in the Brand Radar database.

### Features

- **Share of AI mentions**: Analyze your brand ownership of specific topics in global regions, benchmark that visibility against competitors, and see how that performance plays out over time.
- **Millions of prompts:** Tap into millions of prompts, and conduct top-level, directional analysis of your brand ownership across tons of topics.
- **Response excerpts**: See actual AI responses with your brand highlighted, providing crucial context about how your brand is presented in AI—great for sentiment analysis and brand reputation management.
- **Competitor gap analysis**: Find instances of AI responses that don’t mention your brand, but *do* mention your competitors—then target these topics to increase your AI search visibility.
- **Share of AI citations**: Find out which brands are linked to in AI responses—not just mentioned—with the “Domains” report.
- **Share of web mentions**: Track your brand mentions in titles, content, and URLs all across the web. You can even track co-mentions of certain topics. This is incredibly useful if you’re running targeted PR campaigns, to associate your brand with certain entities and boost your AI visibility.

### Pricing

Available on Ahrefs Lite Plan, from $129 per month ($108/month annually)—this includes both “Search demand” and “Web visibility” indexes.

Indexes for AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, and Gemini are available as separate add-ons from $199/month.

## 3. xofu

**Best for:** Analyzing bottom-funnel AI mentions

While most AI search monitoring tools try to boil the ocean, [xofu](https://xofu.com/) goes deep on a single critical area: bottom-funnel buyer prompts. Powered by [CitationLabs](https://citationlabs.com/), it tracks when ChatGPT recommends your brand (or worse, your competitors) in high-intent purchase scenarios—the moments when real revenue is on the line.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

ChatGPT alone

### Features

- **Bottom-funnel focus**: Tracks brand mentions in high-intent buyer prompts when purchase decisions are most likely.
- **Competitive ranking**: Shows where your brand ranks across key bottom-of-funnel prompts compared to competitors.
- **Citation analysis**: Identifies which sources influence AI mentions of your brand to inform content strategy.

### Pricing

Custom pricing available on request.

## 4. Morningscore ChatGPT Tracker

**Best for:** Simple, low-cost weekly tracking

Rather than just telling you if your brand appeared, [Morningscore](https://morningscore.io/) captures excerpts of prompt responses on a weekly basis.

It’s a very straightforward AI search visibility tool, with few bells and whistles.

Like most of the tools on this list, you have to enter your brand/competitors to begin tracking.

But, unlike most of the other tools, you are also expected to input your own prompts manually–making the setup slightly more time consuming.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

ChatGPT alone

### Features

- **Visual verification**: Provides excerpts of how and where your brand appears in ChatGPT responses.
- **Weekly updates**: Monitors your specified prompts and updates results every 7 days.
- **Simple tracking**: Input specific prompts and your brand details to start tracking.

### Pricing

Included as part of Morningscore SEO packages starting at $49/month, with a separate AI beta access offer.

## 5. Ahrefs Web Analytics

**Best for:** Measuring AI referral traffic

[Ahrefs Web Analytics](https://ahrefs.com/web-analytics) is a free, cookie-less, user-friendly version of GA4–and it’s also a great LLM optimization tool.

Unlike other analytics platforms, Ahrefs Web Analytics doesn’t bury [AI traffic](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/) under generic “referral” sources.

It breaks out AI search visitors as a distinct channel, showing which pages get actual traffic from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other assistants.

You can use these reports to identify which content types consistently drive AI clicks, to prioritize your AI efforts where they actually deliver measurable results.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

All AI referral traffic (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude, Mistral, Meta, and more)

### Features

- **AI traffic segmentation**: Break down traffic from different AI platforms using a dedicated “LLM” channel filter.
- **Real-time AI traffic reporting**: Monitor AI referrals hour-by-hour to track content performance and visibility.
- **Content performance analysis**: See which pages are most cited by AI platforms and drive the most referral traffic.
- **Geographical insights**: Understand where your AI traffic is coming from to inform regional optimization strategies.

### Pricing

Free access for all websites, as part of [Ahrefs Webmaster Tools](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools).

## 6. Airank (Dejan AI)

**Best for:** Mapping AI entity links

Dan Petrovic’s was one of the first-to-market with [Airank](https://airank.dejan.ai/). He built a tool that maps the semantic relationships between your brand and other entities in AI knowledge graphs.

It uses the [DEJAN methodology](https://dejan.ai/blog/beyond-rank-tracking-analyzing-brand-perceptions-through-language-model-association-networks/) of querying the LLM on a weekly basis on a “Brand-to-entity” (e.g. “List ten things that you associate with a brand called [Brand Name].”) and “Entity-to-brand” basis (e.g. “List ten brands that you associate with [Entity/Keyword]”).

It’s all about seeing how AI actually conceptualizes your brand—not just where it mentions you. Other than brands, it can track people’s names, products, services, and events.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

Google and OpenAI LLMs (both grounded/RAG and ungrounded)

### Features

- **Entity association tracking**: Maps relationships between your brand and other entities in AI models.
- **Grounded vs. ungrounded analysis**: Monitors brand rankings in both grounded (search-based) and ungrounded (model knowledge) AI responses.
- **Location-specific monitoring**: Customize tracking by location to understand regional differences in AI responses.
- **Brand-entity gap analysis**: Identifies entities your competitors are associated with that your brand isn’t.

### Pricing

You can get a free demo for 1 brand, 1 domain, and 10 tracked phrases. Contact for custom pricing.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

### Features

- **Citation analysis**: Identifies which sources influence AI mentions of your brand.
- **Sentiment tracking**: Monitors the emotional tone of AI-generated content about your brand.
- **Multi-brand dashboard**: Centralizes tracking for agencies managing multiple brands.

### Pricing

Contact for pricing.

## 8. Mangools AI Search Grader

**Best for:**Free AI search monitoring

[Mangools](https://mangools.com/) deserves credit for creating a free entry point into AI search optimization.

Their AI Search Grader gives you a simple 0-100 score across multiple LLMs without requiring a major investment. I appreciate tools that democratize access to emerging AI tech like this.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude, Google Gemini, Mistral AI, Meta’s Llama 4

### Features

- **AI Search Score**: Provides a combined metric (0-100) reflecting your brand’s visibility and ranking performance weighted by market share.
- **Visibility tracking**: Measures how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers.
- **Ranking analysis**: Shows your average position when featured in AI results.
- **Competitor benchmarking**: Analyze how your brand performs against competitors in AI visibility.

### Pricing

Free access to basic features with three AI models (ChatGPT, Google Gemma, and Meta’s Llama 4). Full access to all six models requires creating a free account.

## 9. Cloudflare Radar AI Insights

**Best for:** Tracking AI crawler traffic

[Cloudflare Radar](https://radar.cloudflare.com/) approaches AI optimization from an entirely different angle—focusing on the crawlers themselves rather than just the outputs.

Launched in February 2025, it gives you visibility into which AI bots are accessing your content, how they’re treated by robots.txt, and overall crawler traffic patterns.

For technical SEOs, this data is pure gold.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

AI crawlers and bots across the web

### Features

- **AI crawler traffic analysis**: Tracks traffic trends from known AI bots and crawlers across the web.
- **AI user agent monitoring**: Shows which AI bots are accessing content and how they’re treated in robots.txt files.
- **Model popularity insights**: Provides visibility into which AI models are most active and commonly used.
- **Data Explorer integration**: Allows deep dives into AI traffic patterns using Cloudflare’s powerful analytics tools.

### Pricing

Free access through Cloudflare Radar.

## 10. Seer GenAI Answer Tracker

It grew out of their fascinating research on [correlations between SEO factors and LLM visibility](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/what-drives-brand-mentions-in-ai-answers).

After analyzing over 10,000 AI responses, they built a report that quantifies exactly which websites influence AI mentions the most.

It works by monitoring the questions and topics you’re associated with across LLM answers.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

Various LLMs (specific models not disclosed)

### Features

- **Brand Signals Score**: Quantifies your brand’s AI visibility with a clear scoring system.
- **Source influence analysis**: Identifies which websites have the greatest impact on AI mentions.
- **Category tracking**: Monitors brand visibility across specific industry topics and categories.
- **Competitive analysis**: Maps your visibility against competitors in AI-generated answers.

### Pricing

Custom pricing, with a one-time analysis available.

## 11. xfunnel

**Best for:** Segmenting AI visibility data

[xfunnel](https://www.xfunnel.ai/) specializes in market segmentation for AI visibility, allowing brands to analyze their presence across different regions, customer journey stages, and user personas.

Instead of one-size-fits-all tracking, xfunnel segments your LLM presence by geographic region, customer journey stage, and even user persona.

This level of granularity reveals which audience segments are encountering your brand in AI—and which aren’t.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

Multiple LLMs (specific models not disclosed)

### Features

- **Geographic segmentation**: Analyzes AI visibility by country and region—crucial for global brands.
- **Journey stage mapping**: Tracks how your brand appears at different stages of the customer journey.
- **Persona-based analysis**: Shows how different customer personas encounter your brand in AI responses.
- **Market opportunity scoring**: Identifies high-value markets and segments for targeted optimization.

### Pricing

Free (100 queries); Pro $199/mo

## 12. Am I On AI?

**Best for:** Simple “yes/no” check for AI brand presence

[Am I On AI?](https://www.amionai.com/) answers the fundamental question keeping marketers up at night: “Is my brand appearing in AI at all?”

To set it up, you just input your website, select the AI model, and choose some AI suggested topics to track.

You’ll get an email that your report is in the “oven”, but it does take a little long to “bake”–we waited ~40 hours, but thankfully Am I On AI founder, [Tomer Dean](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tomerdean/), has since extended our free trial.

Once ready, the report will give you an overall AI visibility score (Ahrefs shows up 69% of the time), showing you how you size up against your competitors, and which proxy-prompts generate a response that contains your brand name.

The “Competitors” tab reveals the other sites that return in those same responses, and in the “Queries” tab you’ll be able to study your ownership of 50 of those proxy-prompts (you’ll see more with a paid account).

Then you have the “Sources” tab, revealing which websites have the most influence on your AI mentions, to inform your PR and content placement efforts.

And finally, there’s the “Growth” tab which captures how your AI visibility score changes week-over-week, when the entire report gets regenerated.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini

### Features

- **Source impact visualization**: Maps which websites have the greatest influence on your AI mentions.
- **Prompt reference graphing**: Shows where you’re getting the most references by topic.
- **Weekly change tracking**: Monitors shifts in your AI presence with intuitive trend lines.
- **Multi-platform coverage**: Tracks mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini.

### Pricing

Starting at $49/month for basic monitoring.

## 13. Profound

Another stand-out feature is its ChatGPT Shopping visibility tracker.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Amazon Rufus

### Features

- **Conversation explorer**: Estimated AI search volume approximating what users ask ChatGPT vs Google.
- **Answer engine insights**: Visibility tracking showing which sources drive AI mentions of a brand.
- **Agent analytics**: Monitoring of AI crawler activity and which pages get cited in responses.
- **Multi-platform coverage**: Cross-platform analysis across all major AI engines by region and language.

### Pricing

Contact for custom pricing.

## 14. Peec

**Best for:** Tracking visibility in niche LLMs—e.g. DeepSeek

[Peec](https://peec.ai/) is designed for marketing teams that need straightforward LLM insights, and recommendations.

It monitors brand mentions across six AI platforms including less-tracked ones like Llama and DeepSeek.

You can check what visibility might be strong on one AI assistant but weak on another, track custom or suggested prompts, see in-context AI mentions, and get alerted when your AI visibility changes.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, Llama, Claude, and DeepSeek.

### Features

- **Platform-specific visibility**: Breaks down your presence by individual LLM platform.
- **Source targeting guidance**: Shows you which publications to pursue for content placement.
- **Custom prompt library**: Creates and saves prompts specific to your business for ongoing monitoring.
- **Competitive tracking**: Compares visibility against up to five competitors.

### Pricing

Starter plan from €90, with enterprise options available.

## 15. Scrunch AI

**Best for:** Building AI-centered personas

[Scrunch AI](https://scrunchai.com/) monitors how brands appear in AI search platforms like ChatGPT and Perplexity, tracking competitive positioning, content attribution, and identifying gaps that hurt visibility.

It helps you optimize content for AI through configurable user personas, keyword tracking, and actionable recommendations.

### The AI data sources behind the tool

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Meta

### Features

- **Content gap detection**: Identifies missing information and outdated data that prevents optimal brand representation in AI responses.
- **Source attribution analysis**: Shows which content sources (first-party, competitor, third-party) influence how AI platforms represent your brand.
- **Persona-based testing**: Reveals how different user profiles receive varied AI-generated brand messaging across demographic segments.

### Pricing

Custom pricing based on monitoring needs and scale.

## Final thoughts

These are some of the best AI search monitoring tools for understanding how AI assistants perceive your brand.

That said, it is possible to track your AI search visibility without third-party tools.

You can query various AI platforms on a regular basis with consistent prompts about your brand and industry–then document the responses in a spreadsheet to monitor mentions over time.

If you do decide to invest in an AI visibility tool, start small. Pick one, and focus on fixing your biggest visibility gaps first.
