# The 14 Best Affiliate Marketing Programs to Try

You’ve probably come across creators participating in an affiliate marketing program when scrolling through social media.

This is when a creator promotes a product or brand using a unique link or promotional code. And earns money for the resulting business they generate for the brand.

It’s a relatively straightforward method. So, many people have joined these programs to supplement their income.

To save you time on researching, we’ve put together a list of the 14 best affiliate marketing programs out there.

Let’s begin.

## 2. HubSpot Affiliate Program

Make the most out of your business content with [HubSpot’s affiliate program](https://www.hubspot.com/partners/affiliates).

![Hubspot's affiliate program homepage with the buttons "Apply now" and "Log in"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/79/50/7950d81ae67205b90f106ccaaf62a321/9e1a972bf5b82897a2150ee888215c6e/image.png)

Affiliates enjoy HubSpot’s program because it pays you more when you refer more customers. The program is based on a tier system that rewards higher performance with increased revenue share, additional bonuses, and even custom commissions.

- **Niche**: CRM software
- **Good for**: Content creators, reviewers, digital educators, B2B marketplaces
- **Commission rate**: 30% recurring commission for up to 1 year
- **Cookie duration**: 180 days
- **Payout methods**: Direct deposit or PayPal
- **Application requirements**: Active website with relevant content

## 3. GetResponse Affiliate Program

[GetResponse](https://www.getresponse.com/affiliate-programs) is an email marketing and automation platform that offers an affiliate program through [PartnerStack](https://partnerstack.com/).

![GetResponse Affiliate Program landing page.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a4/39/a439386fdcafeea1c7b1e2c518ce55eb/af7d020fc5a6e567b25d92f34d0ca61c/AD_4nXfcW-WghwNwf9QK3vI2SwyAqlj-8dV5Y6BTpf68C24cVZbbVdcPl1D6XGtgYUatH_8h04qVL5xg5VFkYh7REScGvYlpaW1BuCwlwFdIVCs_akqkyzJAsU1BMYfaKKDokH5NvytK.png)

The program has three commission tiers.

Everyone starts at the Bronze tier. And you could move up to either the Silver or Gold tiers after 12 months depending on how many sales come from your referrals.

For all tiers, you’ll get promotional materials and monthly newsletters. Silver and Gold tiers also come with extra perks like quarterly webinars detailing the product roadmap and tickets to marketing conferences.

- **Niche**: Email marketing
- **Good for**: Freelancers, content creators, marketing consultants, and affiliates looking to join a new program
- **Commission rate**: 40%-60% for 12 months, depending on the tier
- **Cookie duration**: 90 days
- **Payout method**: Chosen by the program participant within PartnerStack
- **Application requirements**: Website link and how you plan to promote GetResponse

## Choose the Right Affiliate Marketing Program

There’s a lot to consider when choosing an affiliate marketing program.

So, don’t just pick a program because you’ve seen other people participate in it. Instead, prioritize options that are relevant to you.

For instance, if you love content marketing and want to help others do it, the [Semrush Affiliate Program](https://www.semrush.com/lp/affiliate-program/en/) might be the right fit for you.

And if so, it’s a good idea to get as familiar with the platform as possible. Get started with a free trial.
