Modern customers discover brands everywhere: Google, ChatGPT, social media, AI-powered surfaces, and more. Your job is to show up consistently across all of them.

A free Semrush account gives you access to essential SEO, AI visibility, competitor research, and content tools that help you get found online—without paying a cent. You’ll get limited queries per day, but enough to understand your visibility, fix issues, and uncover some opportunities.

When you’re ready to go deeper, you can unlock the full platform with a [7-day free trial](https://www.semrush.com/signup/?src=lp-trial-page&custom=ai-seo&trial=force&redirect_to=%2Fseo%2F).

## How to use Semrush for free (and where you'll hit limits)

To set up your free account, simply provide an email address and password (no credit card required).

Once you’re set up, here’s how to get the most value from each tool:

### 1. Benchmark your brand’s online visibility

Today’s customer journey spans both traditional and AI-based search. That means your visibility across Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and other AI models matters just as much as your visibility in the SERPs.

The [Domain Overview tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/) inside Semrush’s [SEO toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/seo/) gives you a high-level snapshot of how well your brand shows up across both.

With the free plan, you can run 10 domain overview queries per day, which is enough to check your own domain plus several competitors.

Just enter any domain and click “**Search**.”

![img-semblog](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/14/ae/14ae6277b2e86e9619b7a608b5e017d7/1ddaab30c9f2797e3491b375e44e30d6/image.png)

Semrush pulls all your essential SEO indicators into one view, including:

- **Authority Score**: A Semrush metric that evaluates your domain’s overall reputability by looking at backlink quality, organic traffic, and site authenticity
- **Organic Traffic**: Estimated monthly visitors from search engines
- **Organic Keywords**: The number of keywords your domain ranks for
- **Backlinks**: Total external links pointing to your site

![Semrush free SEO metrics](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c0/f2/c0f29864cd83b664889d72d7e1d9f8b7/1750a09bd66754b8dddd3061fe0742a3/image.png)

You’ll also get a clear view of how often your brand shows up in AI-generated responses:

- **AI Visibility Score**: A benchmark showing how present your brand is in AI answers compared to your competitors
- **Mentions**: The total number of prompts or generated responses where your brand appears. More mentions = more exposure in AI-driven experiences
- **Cited Pages**: How many of your URLs are referenced or linked within AI answers
- **Platform-Specific Mentions and Citations**: A breakdown across major AI systems like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini

![Semrush free AI visibility metrics](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/74/3e/743e8d03753f522c8894d2845f2ba7c4/a2793923af761bb8c1eb2925c1a4d023/image.png)

You can run up to 10 Domain Overview queries per day, which is enough to build a strong visibility baseline before expanding your research or activating a trial.

This is enough to test and identify high-potential search terms for your website.

For example, let’s say you have an online shop that sells coffee.

Search "organic coffee" in Keyword Magic to find related keywords like "organic coffee beans" or "organic decaf coffee." These niches might be less competitive but highly relevant for your business.

Semrush’s [Backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/) tool reveals who's linking to you, what they're saying about you, and how you compare to competitors.

FAQs about Semrush's free vs. paid plans

**Do I need a credit card to create a free account?**

No, you can sign up without any payment information. However, if you want to start the 7-day free trial, you’ll need to enter credit card details.

**How quickly do most subscribers hit the free account limits?**

The daily limit of 10 queries can be reached in a single research session, especially if you’re actively exploring keywords or analyzing multiple domains. Tracking limits (10 keywords, 100 pages) may also become restrictive as your strategy grows.

**What's the biggest difference between free and paid accounts?**

It comes down to scale. The free plan allows you to track 10 keywords—great for a personal site or testing the waters. But if you're running a blog, managing clients, or growing a business, you'll quickly outgrow those limits.

With a paid plan, you can track up to 500 keywords (Pro) or 1,500+ (Guru), audit entire websites, and unlock AI-driven tools that help you compete at a higher level. It’s the difference between checking your SEO health once in a while—and having a real strategy.

**Can I upgrade to a paid plan later and keep my projects?**

Yes, all your projects and data are preserved when you upgrade from a free account to a paid plan.

**Can I test out the full capacity of the Semrush tools for free?**

Yes! You can sign up for a [seven-day free trial](https://www.semrush.com/semrush-free-trial/) and use all that Semrush has to offer before deciding on a plan that suits your needs. You can cancel at any time during the trial period, and you won’t be charged.
