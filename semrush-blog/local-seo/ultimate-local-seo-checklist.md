# Local SEO Checklist for 2025 [+ Free Interactive Template]

Follow our ultimate local SEO checklist to increase your local business’s search visibility and get more customers in 2025.

To track your progress with these local SEO tasks, use our [**free interactive checklist**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gUIdbAL_svqatJXgX9Lf8fDhUScdfz8MLA8jUHbTRiU/template/preview) in Google Docs.

## 1. Set Up Your Google Business Profile

Set up your [Google Business Profile (GBP)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) so your business is eligible and more likely to appear in Google’s map-based search results.

This is probably the most important task on this local SEO checklist because many users discover businesses through Google’s local results.

For example, a user searching for a pizza parlor would see results like this:

![SERP shows local map pack with an expanded business profile.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/55/38/5538a73604e4fccd3e5858da60dc5687/f32a1d801350524ab6b6b4b5d09ddca5/AD_4nXf3ALkiYjoMD9ML1-_TKTYanVhUN-I2uAQrxx0O9ipdTyWzBpQ54RyzZsBN4SBaQwiTdXhDhL5I13YlVMqj8lAGZVyDkugvjbFBshpQrFOw9y80I5trI6_cJtK4gt1lHsq_uFIXlg.png)

Here’s a checklist for setting up your GBP:

![Add or claim your business on the GBP website, verify your business, fill out your profile, create a plan to manage your profile, test out Semrush's GBP Optimization tool.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ef/a2/efa280f02a032e7a077b48356cf0ff81/5321266676e06eedb0ccec559409f81e/AD_4nXfW-6Yz_pkzX2axevlALmC5uaKHxl3-1dShZ-h9mn9tGELliiXeE9vDpW7XbVB1kS1Qja9ACXQgVOd91AjCGIlxOJmODBCZ1eyAIR0L2dxioVEHvg23g4g7eGAQGsH0o07_TlJV_w.png)

First, if you haven’t already, add or claim your business on the [GBP website](https://business.google.com/create/new). And go through the business verification process.

Then, fill out and optimize your GBP by providing **complete and accurate** information about your business. So users can get all the details they need before visiting or contacting you.

If users have submitted any questions, answer them directly:

![A potential visitor asks if the local business is dog friendly in the Google Business Profile Q and A section.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/89/de/89ded8ff444bf26ee5e3ed339cd457b4/83e1a2b4d0ff5a086e837ffc1a1a29e8/AD_4nXfj0DNvOwHKxJ5qcK2lAYt2ROSci1M-qAglRNStCaFp17JMoI0IGkB9KLfmz8i66f1ovrFlDFB6LtNjbHhs0b4GeCpSNj2ugvzXGxzg-8QJOjKqlnt8NnVG4irPsSiM0UcKYzGn4Q.png)

Finally, outline a plan to manage your GBP.

Ask yourself the following questions:

- Do you want to [post on GBP](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-business-profile-posts/)? If so, what’s your strategy?
- How will you respond to future questions quickly and strategically?
- Who’s responsible for keeping your GBP up to date?

## 2. Manage Your Online Reviews

Manage your online reviews to maximize the value they bring to your business.

Here’s a checklist to help you manage your reviews efficiently:

![Add your business to relevant review platforms, update your existing profiles, choose a primary review platform, ask customers for reviews, create a review response strategy, test out Semrush's Review Management tool.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/6a/1a6a8fff5a7a836fbea659a6125ce7a0/7cb267190cd527739ae9a68f4f2e1787/AD_4nXdggPUHQk_0tIyPZ5XVSxQjHgZntfj_J5CsTS61bBnF6dQU5npDYt6VjXDWlj3TKWMjT5UjiPTBTkBZJzVlbJp8FiqL3VSqtBDi2ZhuDQoxKZYBluwioNGvXvId6t87Tn_0wneD7A.png)

Start by adding your business to relevant [review platforms](https://www.semrush.com/blog/review-platforms-seo/) and updating your existing business profiles, where necessary.

This allows you to expand your visibility and maintain control over your online presence.

![A local business's Yelp profile shows average star ratings and total reviews.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ef/7f/ef7f3bab4eb15f66bf9d86186c4eea18/96bb7f77188fb1d3c7ef2a73d12f3044/AD_4nXebZ7ws9cNfWiSrRH36whgMpvo0SR0Hmpw7dBvchs7ZZeSJGHORr6UtRKLVNTqLvdXBVVVjEWtdxOL97-xPes_e_l1pOokTYckdJHXWmftgooDY2bg5l02VPk34FMaWunGCE-3XPg.png)

Next, identify the review platform that’s most likely to drive results for your business. This is the platform you should direct customers to and focus your strategy around.

Google is usually the most important review platform because Google reviews can boost your GBP’s visibility in search results. But the best platform depends on your specific needs.

For example, businesses targeting tourists may benefit more from Tripadvisor reviews.

After identifying a review platform, start asking your customers for reviews.

The best way to get reviews on your preferred platform depends on the nature of your business. Here are some options to inspire you:

- Send customers review request emails featuring a link to your reviews page
- Add a reviews widget to your site so visitors can easily see and submit reviews
- Tell customers that you appreciate reviews when speaking to them face-to-face
- Convert your reviews page link into a scannable QR code to include on business cards, posters, and other printed materials

When you receive reviews, [respond to them](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-reply-to-google-reviews/) as quickly and tactfully as possible. To show you care about customer service.

## 3. Build Local Citations

Build local citations (online mentions of your business’s name, address, and phone number—or NAP) on relevant, high-quality websites.

This is one of the most important local SEO tasks because it helps you reach more potential customers. And citations can boost your GBP’s rankings in Google search results.

Use this checklist to start building local citations:

![Add your name, address, and phone number to your social media profiles, list your business on relevant directories and review sites, ensure your citations are consistent, test out Semrush's Listing Management tool.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/89/80/898087c247a46e1b44f26a18b38ddbae/8cb4c64a694e028afb3661321ae5f320/AD_4nXcEeX5vRmI__AWdxHfAJ-nnACnZnm5mHh6yj1ScZ2ZZNbVrqUMNdHQxTqULVdW4YrTfvU9OnfOvutm1sfsW_9RWQJPp6YOrKReCa5whncBH2HjN8taWSMOyRSd-z-q34u-u-uKT6w.png)

You can build your citations in numerous places, including:

- [**Business directories**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-business-listing/): List your business on directories like Yelp, Bing, and Apple Maps
- [**Social media platforms**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-popular-social-media-platforms/): Add details to your existing social media profiles
- [**Review platforms**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/review-platforms-seo/): Add your business to review websites like Trustpilot or Tripadvisor
- **Local websites**: Ask community bloggers or local news outlets to cite your business
- **Industry-specific sites**: Get mentioned on websites related to your business's industry

Use the **exact same** name, address, and phone number across every site. So Google can link all your citations together and trust your information.

## 4. Acquire Local Backlinks

Local backlinks are links to your site from other local websites. These [backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) can drive valuable traffic and help you rank higher in web search results.

Use this local backlink checklist to get started:

![Consider business affiliates; find local influencers; identify local coalitions, events, or awards; check for unlinked mentions; read Google's link spam policies.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/06/f5/06f533466d9462bda5f88fe43d009fce/d5132e7746008be98a393633327c76c0/AD_4nXdSS2C8dc9u3QkfgS8zk3CY-h_uzKSROvKrkRWlkBfe52Hcx4C-PB4QsQfLwVCYxoJUt54On2h8OvRvvVemXr1W5-FJzgVmW4GFnvc7YANTvp0se82iaKML_WQfdK-YTwHtvd1qfQ.png)

Here are some quick ideas on how to build local backlinks:

- Host community events and list them on local event directories
- Provide testimonials for your local suppliers to display on their websites
- Invite local bloggers or reporters to review your establishment
- Sponsor local events, sports teams, or charities and ask them to link to your website
- Enter local business awards and competitions
- Post job opportunities on local job boards
- [Write press releases](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-write-a-press-release/) about any newsworthy business updates (e.g., product launches or hiring drives) and distribute these stories to local media outlets
- Check for unlinked mentions of your brand name and ask creators to add a link

Before you begin, familiarize yourself with [Google’s link spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). Violating these policies can lead to a Google penalty that harms your visibility in search results.

## 5. Improve Your On-Page SEO

[On-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) is the process of optimizing specific webpages to rank more prominently for relevant search engine terms, and it can be one of the most effective ways to increase local website traffic.

Use this checklist to improve your on-page SEO:

![Perform local keyword research; choose a primary keyword for each page; optimize title tags, meta descriptions, header tags, URL slugs, and body content.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d5/89/d589e364d6ca3f793b24995648c73660/80291ddcbfc04b1be223b60ea722d113/AD_4nXdEIPVqhknj8b7lQnYLsdN8PNwJlE11faWg5HRroRWGYxGf14TGN5kFFYNQMDetdbtHTAxzYlgNhTMwsXes5s03jDm_i1XB7v90U2Wr7U9s9mi-PdAEsrRjKSsR60MYYTLgegHA.png)

To start, perform [local keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-keyword-research/). This process tells you what search terms people use to find local businesses like yours.

For example, “restaurants near me” or “restaurants in Chicago.”

Here are a few ways to find relevant local keywords:

- Use [Google Search Console](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-search-console/) to see what terms you already rank for
- Start typing relevant terms into Google’s search bar and look at the suggestions
- Search for relevant terms using Google and look at the related searches
- Search a keyword database with a tool like our [keyword generator](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).
- See what keywords competitors rank for with a tool like [Keyword Gap](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordgap/)

Adding keywords to relevant pages (in a natural way) can help you rank higher for those keywords.

Choose a primary keyword for each page and add it to the following locations:

- [**Title tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/): The page title that can appear in search results
- [**Meta description**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/): The page summary that can appear in search results
- [**H1 tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/h1-tag/): The main on-page heading
- [**URL slug**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url-slug/): The unique part of the webpage address
- The first paragraph of body content

You can repeat your keyword—and any other relevant keywords—throughout the body content and other [heading tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/header-tag/), where appropriate.

## 7. Add Local Business Structured Data to Your Website

[Local Business structured data](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/local-business-schema/) is code that labels different types of local business information on your website so search engines can properly use and display this information in search results.

Use this checklist to guide your Local Business structured data implementation:

![Identify relevant properties on your site, use Structured Data Markup Helper, publish the structured data to your website.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e0/4e/e04eb481b592e57a641d08531a508e71/3a7a4c6581c21addf0cd95e739183804/AD_4nXdym8NeG6tf9QCkUf9XPhmX0NA13NgiWxntUzM-YTKdranJjvyY3pnZaDUdGfp3mQuCrUJGO2Xo_VtyM5mP0RJDqK9WddS52pWEBuuoA5VRp6x6qR6qCmFCn_11HnG_oTcdkOy3.png)

For help implementing structured data, enter your local business’s URL into Google’s [Structured Data Markup Helper](https://www.google.com/webmasters/markup-helper/).

![Location page's URL is entered with Local Businesses option checked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/06/8b/068b2cd665aa89060e76fb9cbce676fe/0731aa9feab27e84f248e0e7ec877160/AD_4nXdUd64nZilqVZE5zhoWg1hMc9TwPMy4jQgny0asobdYJmO1Mn2iK89eeese9aAba6JnrxSrd9ILVSMSsAFr5fpcpDMNzv-hcdZkZOiv66ooJaq83Xty0G_6Y0_SejhH7SH8tyHjIQ.png)

Highlight a piece of data you’d like to tag. And select the correct property type.

Google requires you to include your business’s name and address in Local Business structured data. But you should also add [optional properties](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business#local-business-properties) like opening hours, where relevant.

Once you’ve tagged all your data, click “**Create HTML**.”

![NAP is tagged along with other helpful information for the specific location.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/54/df/54df00956ed157f4bc17793ed5d59dea/3042c865c224564a395d8a77d27b93b5/AD_4nXcXMV-GIsdNATHvGHA97HkOOp6Un3Z4St7TjvQqGrscVxD0n5s7Js3gP0JJKweHhsXCFgSjA77SgYV1LTK24kZANw-KMi2Vauc9BwWXwFjU_6URGrgYarvPsckTbuLWmlKryQpT7Q.png)

Google will provide the structured data for you to add to your site. And you can choose JSON-LD or microdata formats.

If you’re not sure what format is best or where to insert this code, speak to a web developer.

## 8. Run a Technical SEO Audit

A [technical SEO audit](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo-audit/) highlights technical issues that could harm your website’s search visibility.

Use this checklist as a guide for your technical SEO audit:

![Set up your website on Google Search Console, review and fix flagged issues, run a detailed audit through Semrush's Site Audit tool, create a plan to manage your technical SEO tasks.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e5/51/e551c5a03092873008e7bd9bdb985792/dcc3e0fe59cca7571d8f0215ec8b46cb/AD_4nXdTltGovYp4hg3pUOBcIoeMipudh4ViZPzpSJBG6td4yrmF8M-yzAcD5aKTRjwRMNoKR0-z6wFLnRo13-8L7j1RWRS7fvxmpJE8W6gPeJ022p-BvvsSMXCz_JX6OC-WqVVpAIKnXQ.png)

If you haven’t already, set up Google Search Console. This is a free platform that helps you manage your website’s visibility on Google.

Then, check out these reports:

- **Page indexing**: Shows which pages on your site are and aren’t indexed (stored in Google’s database of possible search results). This report helps you identify [crawling and indexing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-crawlability-and-indexability-of-a-website/) issues.
- **Core Web Vitals**: Shows webpage speed and performance metrics called [Core Web Vitals](https://www.semrush.com/blog/core-web-vitals/). This report flags any URLs classed as “poor” or “need improvement.”
- **HTTPS**: Flags any non-HTTPS URLs on your website. Google recommends that you use [HTTPS](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-https/) (a secure version of the HTTP protocol) across your website.
- **Manual actions**: Shows whether you’ve received a [manual penalty](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-penalty/) that harms your search visibility. You can receive penalties for violating [Google’s link spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- **Security issues**: Highlights any issues related to hacked content, malware or unwanted software, and social engineering on your website. You should fix these issues urgently.
- **Sitemaps**: Shows the latest [XML sitemap](https://www.semrush.com/blog/xml-sitemap/) submitted to Google. Submitting an up-to-date sitemap (that lists all the pages you want to index) reduces the risk of indexing issues.
