# Data freshness

Common feedback we hear from webmasters is that you want us to improve the freshness of the data
in Webmaster Tools. Understood. :) We've increased the update
frequency for your
[verified sites'](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35179)
data, such as
[crawl](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35253),
[index](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35256),
and
[search query](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35252)
stats. Much of this data depends on the content of your site. If your content doesn't change very
often, or if you're not getting new links to your site, you may not see updates to your data
every time you sign in to Webmaster Tools.

Please continue to post your
[Suggestions and feature requests](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Requests/topicsh3)
in the
[Webmaster Help Group](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Helph3).
It's one of our most important sources of feedback from the webmaster community. We seriously
take it seriously.
