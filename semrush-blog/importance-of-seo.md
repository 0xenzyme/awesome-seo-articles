# Why Is SEO Important? 10 Reasons It Matters

In this article, we’ll explain why SEO is important for almost every business.

But first, let’s start with the basics.

## What Is SEO?

[Search engine optimization (SEO)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-seo/) is the process of optimizing a business’s website content, structure, and overall online presence. To improve its organic (unpaid) visibility in search engine results pages.

In other words, SEO can help you earn Google rankings like this one:

![search for "chicago zoo" shows an organic, unpaid result for Lincoln Park Zoo](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/89/73/897372d2e1bf2757fd8c9576995f41af/cc0d57f97a362c2641621eb7fe74945d/AD_4nXdaXTKayTOa8P-6QJuLtJwoRE3f_ND1LTi2rwLw19WOswjhGEnB5yC0EgmI1Rbvn7Cvee7hUlfj6Zm9VMwBbbd77l3Ok_q1yQNpOm0zycKihMrMiWKYBFVBsiyox4ZxRanlBaG1m2--H87FdjFV9Xj1hBU.png)

(Most marketers focus on Google because it’s the [most popular search engine](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-engine-list/).)

SEO is one of the two main pillars in [search engine marketing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-engine-marketing/).

The other is [pay-per-click marketing (PPC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-ppc/). This is when you pay to appear as a sponsored search result.

![search for "chicago coffee subsciption" shows a sponsored, paid result for a coffee club and an organic, unpaid result for chicago coffee brand Dark Matter Coffee](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/03/f3/03f3329402568579544cf66fb8d2e707/c11ec5a800da1fbec2020e76c9f76f5c/AD_4nXcqmlweZeTBlBKBrIBYpFnXU4fEjvfAkTdE-QAh0Ln-pZLK3mv_y3ICnwAZiR0Fh6J8UvIzVMLt1Sch4jUCQpSNBn90Nc3DokvdaADHge6S05ZKFcjxzAwQd7bYoTP84Iz6vvmi0v-kDISmyLFrRduIC9Ym.png)

## How Does SEO Work?

SEO aligns your website and content with the [Google algorithm](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-search-algorithm/). Or, in other words, what Google looks for in good search results.

Basically, Google wants to rank webpages that:

- Satisfy [search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/) (what the searcher is looking to achieve)
- Demonstrate experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness ([E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/#how-to-improve-your-site%E2%80%99s-e-e-a-t))
- Load quickly and perform well
- Are easily navigable by users and search engines

So, SEO is about making sure your pages tick these boxes.

Some of the most common SEO tactics include:

- [**Keyword research**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/): Identifying and analyzing the search terms target audiences use
- [**Content creation**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-creation/): Creating quality content that meets your target audiences’ needs
- [**Link building**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/): Getting other websites to link to your site so that it appears more trustworthy
- [**Technical SEO**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/): Improving the technical performance of your site for users and search engines

## Why Is SEO Important?

SEO is important because it can improve your organic visibility in search engine results pages. Which can translate to more brand awareness and website traffic. Which ultimately leads to more sales.

As our [success stories](https://www.semrush.com/company/stories/) prove, businesses of all shapes and sizes have used SEO to their advantage.

Here are 10 reasons why SEO matters:

### 1. Search Engines Generate Lots of Traffic

Ranking high in search engine results pages (SERPs) can get you lots of clicks. Which means lots of traffic to your website.

People use search engines to perform billions of searches every day.

And on average, [over 43%](https://www.semrush.com/blog/state-of-search-2023/) of searchers click an organic result.

**If you don’t capture the clicks available in your niche, your competitors will.**

SEO is the tried-and-tested way to rank on more search results pages. And rank in higher positions.

### 2. SEO Can Be Highly Cost Effective

One of the main benefits of SEO is that it’s usually cost-effective.

Why?

First, there are no advertising costs.

You don’t pay to appear on the results page. Or incur costs when people view or click your result.

Second, SEO can deliver long-term results.

Once you’ve achieved high rankings, it’s possible to maintain them with relatively little effort (compared to channels like [digital PR](https://www.semrush.com/blog/guide-to-digital-pr/) and [social media](https://www.semrush.com/blog/social-media-management/)). And relatively low costs (compared to [paid traffic channels](https://www.semrush.com/blog/paid-traffic/)).

Building a strong foundation also makes it easier to gain or improve other rankings in the future.

In other words, SEO is highly scalable.

Just look at [Morningside Acupuncture's success story](https://www.semrush.com/company/stories/morningsideacupuncture/). Over three years, the company used SEO to grow their traffic by 2,900%.

### 5. SEO Success Supports Other Marketing Efforts

SEO success can improve the results of your other marketing efforts. Because many people refer to search engines after discovering a marketing campaign elsewhere.

Let’s say someone sees your billboard. And they go to Google to learn more about it.

If you don’t rank prominently when the user searches, you may lose your opportunity to keep them engaged.

**It can be particularly beneficial to** [**use SEO alongside PPC**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ppc-and-seo-working-together/)**.**

Here’s why:

PPC ads usually appear at the top of the SERP, meaning they get more exposure.

But, the top 10 organic results are [more likely](https://www.semrush.com/blog/state-of-search-2023/) to get clicks.

So if you have both kinds of results, you get the best of both worlds.

- **Transactional keywords** are used to complete specific actions (e.g., purchases). So, they’re typically used in the decision stage of the buying journey.

### 7. Optimization Efforts Can Improve the User Experience

Many important SEO tactics are good for your website’s user experience (UX). And that can help your conversion rate—i.e., make users more likely to do what you want.

Essentially, Google wants to rank pages that leave its users happy. So you must create user-friendly content in order to succeed.

For example, these [Core Web Vitals](https://www.semrush.com/blog/core-web-vitals/) affect your performance in Google:

- **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)**: How long it takes for the largest content element on the page to load
- **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)**: Measures how much the page layout moves around unexpectedly as it loads
- **Interaction to Next Paint (INP)**: The longest time it takes for the page to display visual confirmation of a successful interaction (e.g., for a menu to open)

Optimizing these metrics can help you rank higher. And improve the experience for users on your website.

So, users are more likely to convert. Whether or not they discovered your site through Google.

### 10. It Helps You Protect Your Brand

SEO is important when it comes to [online reputation management (ORM)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/online-reputation-management/). Because it helps you control the results that come up when people search for your brand.
