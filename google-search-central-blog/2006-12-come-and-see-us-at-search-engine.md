# Come and see us at Search Engine Strategies Chicago

If you're planning to be at
[SES Chicago](https://www.searchenginestrategies.com/sew/chicago06/index)
this week, be sure to stop by and say hi to the many Googlers who are coming out to brave the
[cold and snow](https://www.google.com/search?q=weather+chicago).
Many of us will be on hand at the booth, speaking at sessions, and wandering the halls. Check out
[Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/061201-084842)
for tips on how to spot some of us and be sure to catch our sessions:
