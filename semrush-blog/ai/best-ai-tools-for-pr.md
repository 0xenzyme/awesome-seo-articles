## AI Tools for Written Content Creation

The best AI tools for creating written content include AI PR Toolkit, Content Toolkit, ChatGPT, Jasper, Surfer, Grammarly, Wordtune, and Claude:

### 1. AI PR Toolkit

- **Best for**: PR professionals who want an all-in-one PR tool
- **Price**: Starts at $149/mo. Free trial available.

Semrush [AI PR Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/pr-toolkit/) is a suite of tools that helps PR professionals discover media contacts, draft and send pitches, and track brand mentions from a single interface.

#### Pros

The Email Campaigns tool uses AI to generate pitches significantly faster than manual writing.

Input your general press release idea and receive mutliple story angles to develop.

![Three story angles to choose from on the AI PR Toolkit.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4b/de/4bde980a90c3498332b2369153b03481/a1945c10d20a0ec5609fd0c3066277cb/image.jpeg)

Once this tool has generated your draft press release, you can edit it to better reflect your brand’s style and goals.

![A press release along with an outreach email draft on the AI PR Toolkit editor.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/da/6f/da6f0015835fa3a879a0f0a8ba5b2850/177bebf30392cb27877515252f6917db/image.jpeg)

The AI PR Toolkit also uses your press release’s keywords to suggest relevant journalists for sending the press release to.

![The AI PR Toolkit showing a list of relevant journalists based on a press release's keywords.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e7/49/e749955f3887a2d98e62f4b7e2f35697/7935afb99a75868ea326c663711fe8e0/image.jpeg)

Finally, the Email Campaigns tool offers AI-generated subject line recommendations that align with your press release—to boost email open rates and clicks.

![A list of AI-generated subject line recommendations for a press release on the AI PR toolkit.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f2/49/f249e1d3e75f0aa3d824f1ceb075c7d8/5e497e17282186418b8d87de11e1b987/image.jpeg)

#### Cons

PR professionals who want to tailor their email campaigns’ send times to their recipients’ time zones may find the Semrush AI PR Toolkit lacking.

That’s because this tool lets you schedule emails to send at only one specific time—e.g., 9 a.m. ET. But this might not be a big issue if your recipients’ time zones are similar to yours. Especially since dynamic time zone settings aren’t a standard PR tool feature.

### 2. Content Toolkit

- **Best for**: Users who produce search engine-optimized PR content
- **Price**: Starts at $60/mo. Free trial available.

Semrush [Content Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/content/) helps PR professionals generate and optimize search-friendly press releases and thought leadership articles for long-term organic visibility.

![Content Toolkit dashboard showing content generated on the left and AI SEO improvement suggestions on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1c/64/1c640df8c3d75a139de155349c23e31e/2a89cea5a001835da10b6eb32fe6798e/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

While generating articles, the Content Toolkit’s AI Article Generator tool automatically identifies and adds relevant search keywords. Thus optimizing the articles for higher Google rankings for these keywords and improving their reach.

#### Cons

The Content Toolkit accepts 2,000 words’ worth of reference content to learn how to generate content in your brand voice. This limit may be restrictive for brands with a highly distinct or established voice.

### 3. ChatGPT

- **Best for**: Users who want to research and generate written content in a conversational AI interface
- **Price**: Starts at $20/mo. Free plan available.

[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) is an AI chatbot that can craft press releases, generate creative ideas, conduct research, and compose pitches and follow-up emails.

![ChatGPT generating a detailed press release for a consumer product based on a prompt.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e9/59/e9597584e1ea892313be1293cc7b5727/20819ff92e099f2a53dc037f0b5bd549/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

ChatGPT can produce high-quality PR content when prompted clearly and strategically. Think more accurate PR summaries, well-structured press releases, and so on.

Why?

Because ChatGPT uses one of the most advanced multimodal large language models in the market.

It also offers custom instructions and memory features for tailoring the content it generates. This will benefit large PR agencies that create content for multiple accounts, where each account has unique tone of voice and formatting specifications.

#### Cons

Users who value data privacy may want to look for a different tool. ChatGPT’s creator, OpenAI, may use the data you share with ChatGPT to improve the tool’s performance if you’re on a consumer-level plan.

ChatGPT may also not be the best option for users who want to be able to edit the AI-generated content directly. This tool also lacks a built-in text editor for directly refining generated content.

### 4. Jasper

- **Best for**: Organizations that want a general-purpose AI writing tool for creating marketing content
- **Price**: Starts at $69/mo. Free trial available.

[Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/) is an AI tool that can write [digital PR](https://www.semrush.com/blog/digital-pr/) blog articles, press releases, social media copy, and more in a chat-based interface.

![Jasper dashboard with an AI chat open on the left and the draft of a press release on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/51/1a/511a7f8094ea283265cb1176984f2527/a7093dfdca3c818a0c1d6cac3c75f98f/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Jasper helps save time in producing on-brand writing. It can incorporate your brand’s voice and knowledge into its content when you upload relevant assets, like your style guide and product information, to it.

#### Cons

Jasper’s feature set may appear bloated to users seeking a dedicated AI PR content tool.

This tool offers apps for creating a wide range of marketing content—ranging from email sequences to white paper outlines and Amazon product listings—and not all of them are relevant to PR.

### 5. Surfer

- **Best for**: PR teams that want to optimize content for higher search rankings
- **Price**: Starts at $99/mo

[Surfer](https://surferseo.com/) is a content optimization tool that helps PR teams create SEO-friendly articles, press releases, and web content with the help of AI. It analyzes top-ranking pages and offers clear suggestions on keywords, structure, and readability to boost visibility.

![Surfer dashboard showing content on the left and an overall score along with improvement suggestions on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/6a/1a6a3d4fdd5a75218a684bd94c7c35be/3c5c03b84e91f92276b18cf91afa37f3/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Surfer is a convenient tool for PR teams whose sites run on WordPress. It offers a plugin that can directly publish Surfer-created content on a WordPress site. Saving you time from manually transferring the content to WordPress.

#### Cons

Some of Surfer’s SEO recommendations—like suggesting that you use keywords a certain number of times throughout your article—are outdated.

They may mislead inexperienced SEOs who believe they need to rigidly follow the tool’s recommendations to produce high-ranking PR content.

### 6. Grammarly

- **Best for**: PR professionals who want to improve their writing’s readability, spelling, and grammar
- **Price**: Starts at $30/member/mo. Free plan available.

[Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) is an AI writing tool that can rewrite PR content, adjust its tone, and check it for spelling and grammar errors.

![Grammarly dashboard with a press release draft on the left and an AI Chat showing a writing quality score on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7b/0b/7b0b648d1c6b84a0f85be3cf6c226e69/9dee422c578a87404bc94c98335d65c6/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Grammarly helps PR practitioners improve the consistency of their brand messaging. By refining their PR content to incorporate the target tone of voice, style, and level of formality before publishing.

#### Cons

Users who deal with sensitive information should avoid revising their content with Grammarly. Depending on your plan and country, this tool may use the data you enter into it to improve its large language models unless you opt out.

### 7. Wordtune

- **Best for**: PR teams that want to improve their content’s impact
- **Price**: Starts at $13.99/mo. Free plan available.

[Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com/) is a tool for creating new PR content and improving existing text—in ways like rewriting executive statements for clarity, simplifying dense messaging, and correcting spelling and grammar errors in written interview responses—using AI.

![Wordtune dashboard with a press release draft on the left and an AI Chat on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/47/02/470271d2cdba11cf2d3b7e41363f68a7/a24cd1097e91b5a258ac773b75ced1a4/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Wordtune helps produce more impactful content that may increase engagement. It offers suggestions for improvement that go beyond the typical expansion or summarization of text.

For example, you can have the tool emphasize certain points, give examples, or provide counterarguments.

#### Cons

The tool doesn’t explain the grammar errors it identifies. Making it less useful for PR professionals who want to avoid making the same mistakes in the future.

### 8. Claude

- **Best for**: Users who want to create written content for small-scale PR efforts
- **Price**: Starts at $20/mo. Free plan available.

[Claude](https://claude.ai/) is an AI assistant that excels at writing, editing, and summarizing content. It’s well-suited for PR professionals who want a more thoughtful, conversational approach to drafting and refining messaging.

![Claude generating a press release for a software product based on a prompt.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c3/6d/c36db2d76eb8e20b63193374b85c3b76/94f3c8d650b7b8ac102874af1000c58f/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Claude offers pre-written prompts for writing various types of content, including interview questions and persuasive arguments. Thus helping you be more specific with your prompts, so you get content that’s more effective in boosting brand awareness and fostering trust.

#### Cons

Users who need to generate dozens of long-form PR content every day may need a more heavy-duty tool.

Claude’s usage capacity is relatively limited even on paid plans. For example, Pro plan users whose average Claude conversations contain 200 English sentences of 15 to 20 words can send [roughly 45 messages every five hours](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8324991-about-claude-s-pro-plan-usage).

## AI Tools for Visual Content Creation

The best AI tools for creating visual content—like images and videos—include Midjourney, Canva, Synthesia, and Beautiful.ai:

### 9. Midjourney

- **Best for**: Users who want to generate PR images for public use
- **Price**: Starts at $10/mo. Free trial available.

[Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) is an AI tool that can generate images for PR campaigns and other content in response to prompts.

![The Midjourney interface showing an image generation prompt, the results, and editing options.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/20/8d/208dad7befd1bf7352f889edd0f9d272/0281ae9f35423c161b31d29383394878/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Midjourney accepts image prompts, which is helpful if you want it to reference an existing image when generating a new one to communicate your intended message.

#### Cons

Midjourney is unsuitable if you need your source prompts and generated images to be kept confidential.

This tool’s terms of service give the company that develops Midjourney the general rights to reproduce or display your source prompts, plus the generated images themselves.

If you enable Stealth Mode, which is available on higher-tiered plans, the company will keep your chosen generated images private on a “best effort” basis. But your source prompts will not enjoy similar privacy.

### 10. Canva

- **Best for**: Users without design experience who want to create PR images
- **Price**: Starts at $15/user/mo. Free plan available.

[Canva](https://www.canva.com/) is a design tool that lets users create AI-generated images for PR content like infographics, press releases, and social media posts, and enhance these images with ready-made shapes and templates.

![Canva interface with a press release template on the right and images generated based on a prompt on the left.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f0/a9/f0a9644c166804c2cc03815dee0fc1fc/c641c23fc3e4e0bdb669d2e5dc3a1933/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Canva speeds up the image creation process—and thus the overall duration you spend putting your PR campaigns together.

Instead of drawing images from scratch, you can have Canva generate them in seconds with AI. Then, tweak your images further by using Canva’s built-in tools to erase parts of them, change their colors, adjust their saturation, and more.

#### Cons

Canva isn’t suitable for PR professionals who want to generate images based on reference ones. The tool’s AI image generator accepts only text prompts.

### 11. Synthesia

- **Best for**: Organizations that want to scale PR video production on a budget
- **Price**: Starts at $29/mo. Free plan available.

[Synthesia](https://synthesia.io/) is AI-based video creation software that lets you type prompts to create videos instead of filming and editing them manually. Thus reducing production costs for brands that rely on video messaging for product announcements and other PR campaigns.

![Synthesia interface showing an example of an AI-generated video for a PR campaign.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7e/46/7e46f45a0c6781b8dd3fa82ef58c238c/aed21878f0652ff6f0886706499f8a26/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Synthesia offers 240+ AI avatars that look and sound like real people. They’re helpful for PR professionals who want to make talking head videos to raise brand awareness despite not having available spokespeople.

#### Cons

If portraying authenticity is your priority, Synthesia may not appeal to you. The video content it creates may lack the look and feel of a real, recorded video.

### 12. Beautiful.ai

- **Best for**: PR teams that frequently produce slide decks
- **Price**: Starts at $45/mo. Free trial available.

[Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/) is an AI tool that creates pitch decks when you prompt it on the content the slides should include.

![Generating a presentation with AI on Beautiful.ai with options to customize the language, theme, image source, and style.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ee/40/ee4033242f65835964f1776fa20e7372/b2d4ca72d09b51547b0dd1c473f25fc4/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Beautiful.ai supports real-time collaborative editing. PR teams that involve multiple people in slide deck creation will find this a time-saver, as everyone can work on the same deck simultaneously.

#### Cons

Users who need full creative control over their media kit or pitch deck’s look may find this tool restrictive. It doesn’t let you move content blocks anywhere you want on the slides, but only to certain fixed locations.

## AI Tools for Meeting Summarization and Transcription

The best AI tools for transcribing and summarizing meetings include Fireflies, Otter, and Descript:

### 13. Fireflies

- **Best for**: PR professionals who regularly take notes during meetings
- **Price**: Starts at $18/seat/mo. Free plan available.

[Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/) is an AI-powered meeting assistant that transcribes, takes notes, and summarizes meetings across video conferencing platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams.

With it, PR professionals can focus on the conversation without worrying about missing critical information during press briefings, interviews, and internal meetings.

![Fireflies interface showing the summary of a meeting along with key notes on the left and a transcript of the whole meeting on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/07/fb/07fb267e1fd6038bbe140c33dd5027ed/132165ce0b9b927a1887ab70df4a8148/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

With Fireflies, you reduce the hassle of extracting the information you need from meeting transcripts.

It supports many apps, which you can install to assess a meeting’s adherence to the agenda. Or log all mentions of competitors during the meeting, for example.

#### Cons

Fireflies may not be suitable for PR professionals who switch between languages during meetings. The tool transcribes meetings in only one language at a time.

### 14. Otter

- **Best for**: PR professionals who want meeting transcripts for internal use
- **Price**: Starts at $16.99/user/mo. Free plan available.

[Otter](https://otter.ai/) is an AI tool that transcribes video meetings, identifying who spoke at which time, and also provides AI-powered meeting summaries. Thus saving PR practitioners time from listening to hours of meetings.

![Otter interface showing an overview of a meeting along with key action items on the left and an AI Chat with prompt suggestions on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/44/bd/44bd7ddc6dbaad47613e5ebab7ff1466/13850b0ed6737729afd66157846b664f/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Save time from reading transcripts in full. Otter’s AI chat feature lets you ask about topics discussed during a meeting, so you don’t have to go through the entire transcript yourself.

#### Cons

Otter isn’t the best tool for busy PR professionals who want to minimize the time they spend polishing meeting transcripts. The tool tends to keep uses of “um” in its transcripts, which you’ll need to remove manually.

It also doesn’t offer a convenient “Find and Replace” feature for making repetitive transcript edits—like removing “ums”—in bulk.

### 15. Descript

- **Best for**: PR teams that want to polish video- and audio-based PR content
- **Price**: Starts at $24/user/mo. Free plan available.

[Descript](https://www.descript.com/) is an all-in-one audio and video editing tool that lets users edit recordings as if they were text documents. PR teams can use it to produce interviews, podcasts, and videos for their campaigns without the need for technical skills.

![Descript interface showing a video script on the left, a video in the middle, an AI chat on the right, and video editing options at the bottom.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7d/8f/7d8fda4d2ad2ed8a65231727181258e8/2d836162cb4678f1b7aac46aac5ff0d1/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Descript can improve your videos to maximize positive brand perception. Among other features, it can remove filler words, silences, and background noise that distract viewers from digesting your message.

#### Cons

The tool doesn’t have a mobile app, which may not appeal to PR professionals who want to work on the go.

## AI Tools for Research and Competitive Intelligence

One of the best AI tools for research and competitive intelligence is Perplexity:

### 16. Perplexity

- **Best for**: Users who want to conduct in-depth research for PR tasks
- **Price**: Starts at $20/mo. Free plan available.

[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) is an AI-powered search engine that delivers direct, cited answers to complex queries—making it a valuable research companion for PR professionals who need quick answers, background information, or competitor insights when drafting press materials or preparing for interviews.

![Perplexity interface with the prompt "What do Reddit users think of Chewy products?" along with the response generated below.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/37/81/3781d2466b8708c385f53a115fb66c17/2c1cfe2916b571ae34346ac6263b010c/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Perplexity makes your research process more efficient by letting you customize the sources of its responses. So that you can tailor its answers for contexts like discovering social media users’ sentiments on your brand.

#### Cons

Perplexity may frustrate users who want short, concise responses. It tends to generate long answers, even for simple questions.

## AI Tools for Media Monitoring and Insights

The best AI tools for media monitoring and insights include CisionOne and Meltwater:

### 17. CisionOne

- **Best for**: Enterprise-level PR teams that want to manage large-scale outreach campaigns
- **Price**: Custom

[CisionOne](https://www.cision.com/) is a full-suite AI PR platform offering media monitoring, journalist outreach, and press release services. It’s widely used by PR teams to manage large-scale campaigns and measure media impact.

![The "Monitoring" tab of the CisionOne dashboard showing data like recent mentions, total audience, sentiment, and average react score.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/50/93/5093d15d387df898a5523109cc6873a0/62050ec7889b20751436473b9b2b77ce/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

CisionOne can help you maintain your brand reputation. It offers an AI-powered React Score that detects potentially damaging content, so you can take prompt action before it spreads widely.

#### Cons

CisionOne may not be suitable for PR teams on smaller budgets. It costs a four- or five-figure yearly sum to use, according to user reports.

### 18. Meltwater

- **Best for**: Enterprise-level PR teams that want to monitor brand mentions and sentiment
- **Price**: Custom

[Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/) is an AI-powered media intelligence and social listening platform. It helps PR professionals track brand mentions, do sentiment analysis, and discover emerging topics across news outlets, blogs, and social media.

!["AI journalist search" on Meltwater to find contacts writing about a specific topic.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e9/19/e919007571d3910275a036c3ec80d71e/b41086c590f6fc131378e69a513a5e85/image.jpeg)

#### Pros

Meltwater’s AI-powered journalist search feature helps recommend relevant journalists to pitch your brand to. So that you’re more likely to secure media coverage.

#### Cons

The platform’s media monitoring tool, Monitor, lets you export up to 20,000 content results to a CSV file at a time. This limit may be insufficient for PR teams doing bulk analyses of content mentioning their brands.

## Pick the Right AI Tools for PR

AI tools for PR won’t replace PR teams, but the right tools can significantly streamline daily PR work, from pitching and content creation to research and media monitoring.

The best choice depends on team size, workflow complexity, and where you spend the most time.

- **Solo PR professionals and small teams** often benefit from flexible writing and design tools like ChatGPT, Canva, or Wordtune to move faster with limited resources.
- **In-house PR teams** may prefer all-in-one solutions that reduce tool sprawl. Platforms like Semrush AI PR Toolkit and Content Toolkit combine content creation, outreach, and monitoring in a single workflow.
- **Enterprise PR teams** typically need advanced media databases, sentiment analysis, and reporting. Tools like CisionOne and Meltwater are designed for large-scale campaigns and complex monitoring needs.
