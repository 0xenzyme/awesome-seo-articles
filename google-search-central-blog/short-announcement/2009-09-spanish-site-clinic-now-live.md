# Spanish Site Clinic now live

The
[Google Webmaster Central blog in Spanish](/search/blog/?hl=es) has launched a
*[Site Clinic](/search/blog/2009/09/ayuda-en-busqueda-analizando-vuestras?hl=es)*
especially for the Spanish-speaking market. We're offering to analyze a series of websites in
order to share some best practices with our community using real web sites. The plan is to offer
constructive advice on accessibility and improvements that can lead to better visibility in
Google's search results.

![Spanish blog site clinic logo](/static/search/blog/images/import/430db3f0128725793d2aea6d5eee7486.png)

During this month, we will be receiving submissions from any legitimate website, but it must be
primarily in Spanish. So before you submit your site, please visit the
[original post](/search/blog/2009/09/ayuda-en-busqueda-analizando-vuestras?hl=es)
and if you want to participate fill out the form as soon as possible, because we will only be
selecting 3-5 websites from the first 200 submitted for this Site Clinic, so don't miss out!
