# How to Use SEO for Business Growth Over Time

SEO can impact your business’s growth and profitability.

And by the end of this guide, you’ll know exactly how you can leverage SEO to grow your own business.

But let’s start with the basics.

## What Is SEO?

[Search engine optimization (SEO)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-basics/) is a digital marketing practice aimed at improving your business’s visibility in search engines using organic (non-paid) tactics.

Instead of paying for ads, SEO helps your website naturally rank higher on search engine results pages (SERPs) when users search for relevant [keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/) (words/phrases users type into search engines).

For example, your potential customers might search for keywords like “best travel bags,” “durable travel backpacks,” or “lightweight carry-on luggage” if you sell travel bags. And see results like these:

![Google SERP for the term “durable travel backpacks” showing a list of paid and organic results.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/53/98/53984dd450d2a597e540599e0bd9a9c5/07bd61dbe05063eb30031523a523212d/AD_4nXdRNcdfFlmJti2LVSXdv2YMGNFbBdN1U73XxRpx-FSz6208evRwkH52psqxhyCrzfOShBnBEneLpD36NXmMtxu66sPhQ2sfAjAg7nrGXvBoT3OKXtdhrUaJwcMTDkgPvCyZqixSSA.jpeg)

### The 4 Main Types of SEO for Business Owners

You can break SEO down into lots of different types. But there are four broad categories that business owners should care about:

- [**On-page SEO**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/): This focuses on optimizing your webpages to help them rank higher in SERPs for your target keywords, which can bring more potential customers to your site
- [**Technical SEO**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/): This involves optimizing your site’s technical performance to improve the user experience and make it easier for search engines to find, understand, and store all of your important pages
- [**Off-page SEO**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/off-page-seo/): Off-page SEO involves building backlinks (links on other sites that point to your site), engaging in [digital PR](https://www.semrush.com/blog/digital-pr/), and other off-site activities to improve your business’s online visibility
- [**Local SEO**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/): This focuses on improving your business’s visibility for local search queries, and is often centered in part around optimizing your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/)

## Why Is SEO Good for Business Growth?

There are lots of reasons why you should invest in SEO as a business owner. But these are the most important ones from a growth perspective:

### Drive More Sales

Effectively implementing SEO tactics improves your website’s visibility in SERPs for relevant keywords, which helps you attract more [organic search traffic](https://www.semrush.com/blog/organic-traffic/) from people who might become paying customers.

Hepper, a pet furniture company, saw a staggering [2,964% increase in revenue](https://www.semrush.com/company/stories/hepper/) within two years of investing in SEO.

They used Semrush to identify the most relevant and high-impact keywords for their business and created 5,000 high-quality blog posts targeting those keywords.

![Graph of Google Analytics data for Hepper showing 98,824 pageviews per day.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a8/12/a812c37b5655683c17f9ed041210f326/b35a7f04c71978fe56654ab7d740673c/AD_4nXe2C2TYul_ixkPEAFnY5tbTyfWfkn05ix05h85_P4Gf-I04A8yqqTJPN3NIxHH8JbjT7pW3ZtrZwpPwHkn__npWt43lvtrJ42XgQ0tIDNTXpPDJYz_2D-xJtH-frD9z1jL5csO5Rw.png)

This is obviously an extreme example—you don’t need to start writing 5,000 posts. But even at smaller scales, the extra traffic that SEO can drive to your business’s website can add up to meaningful sales increases.

### Increase Brand Awareness

Higher search engine rankings make it more likely that your potential customers will see your brand in search results, which increases your brand awareness.

Aside from visibility, it also keeps your brand top-of-mind with your audience.

When potential customers see your site ranking every time they search for something related to your business, they’re more likely to perceive your business as a credible and authoritative source.

This repeated exposure can build trust with your target audience. Making them more likely to choose your business when they’re ready to engage or make a purchase.

### Reduce Reliance on Paid Ads

SEO helps businesses cut their dependence on paid advertising by driving consistent organic traffic without the need for continuous ad spend.

While paid ads can provide immediate visibility, they are often expensive. Especially in competitive industries where [cost per click (CPC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/cost-per-click/) is high.

For example, sectors like legal services or insurance often see CPCs ranging from $50 to $100 or more per click.

![Keywords with CPCs costing more than $100 per click including "car accident lawyer" and "truck accident lawyer."](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/11/d6/11d69a165963bc11dedb1e495cfdc32f/b65a364079d5aeaf9222c21ca4270034/AD_4nXdFwWq9qXkFzNca_h3Z-a89E6FYFaCrQW_Qj_ETci0ImOFPm-XDYwb-Tv5nO6SKG1LX1DXz2loIPUx_D4KXNVUqnx-poJj4J_NFhs-hlEeeIowT3Ch5E5D77y3LJi32ERhOTIQK.jpeg)

Online veterinary health and wellness platform BetterVet is a good example of just how impactful SEO can be compared to ads.

In December 2022, BetterVet struggled to increase their organic search traffic. This left the company heavily reliant on paid advertising.

But by the end of 2023, BetterVet made a remarkable turnaround by focusing on SEO to grow their organic search traffic [by over 2,000%](https://www.semrush.com/company/stories/bettervet/). And this allowed them to significantly reduce their ad spend.

![A graph showing an increase in organic traffic by over 2,000% by BetterVet using Semrush.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c4/91/c4911687153bcc5b5d1ea070f0f02797/3e81bd83ee0b00dfecdf74f2931a729d/AD_4nXfyvJh3HafwbCyCOPOZqxNs2bJdca2i8Kb-WuuEdYf-1eN5ci5QUwZutMa7r_ILM-aroBX0V26XS87PTu-yJjVL8_mWyp5-C8AvmiwRQ9lu8jdZiE30HiAuE3q615Ea9aGkxIeO.jpeg)

### Achieve Long-Term Results

SEO can deliver long-lasting results that continue to benefit your business over time without much work beyond the initial time and effort you invest.

You can continue to drive organic search traffic to your website for months or even years.

This is because organic search engine positions are determined by [Google ranking factors](https://go.semrush.com/Ranking-Factors.html)—not how much money you spend.

## How to Use SEO for Online Business Growth

SEO has a lot of moving parts, so start with these key steps to get the most out of your efforts:

### 1. Perform Keyword Research

[Keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/) is the process of finding the most relevant and valuable keywords you can create content around to help your site appear in search results when potential customers search for them.

How do you perform keyword research?

You can do it in a few ways, but using a reliable keyword research tool like the [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/) is the most efficient option.

Simply launch the tool and enter a [seed keyword](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seed-keywords/) (a broad term related to your business, product, service, or industry).

Select your target country from the drop-down menu and click “**Search**.”

![Keyword Magic Tool start with "gluten free cookies" entered and "Search" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b0/ec/b0ec27757f72ec44be1fd1adf68b3d08/7f830afcfd112481a746a274a94f2506/AD_4nXfHk2RW0W0rTcmkR9XMunHSUxqDDK2Bvohbmt0maLf6o4S_fb-phEY5NuIvu2OLSayG3h80E_o-WH_5zchMQJkEtgOkAp05Iylo5mwcUzjwxE-DgBW2zY6l9aH-wQlpu-4LGgl1.jpeg)

Next, you’ll see a list of related keywords. Along with additional metrics for each keyword, like:

- [**Search intent**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/): The purpose behind the search (more on this below)
- [**Search volume**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-search-volume/): How many searches the term gets each month
- [**Keyword difficulty (KD %)**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-difficulty/): A score out of 100 indicating how tough it is to rank in the top 10 search results for that keyword (lower is easier)

Use filters to identify the most relevant and promising keywords from the list.

For example, the “**KD %**” filter lets you identify keywords that are easier to rank for.

Simply select “**Easy**” or “**Very Easy**” to find keywords with low competition.

![Keyword Magic Tool with the "Keyword Difficulty" filter clicked and "Easy" selected from the drop-down list.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/76/f3/76f33c81006f89356a45e61d7a8e9c26/edc4f5e84fd3157aa54ef7284391f513/AD_4nXcqLKzxbK7z0ae7mchILM8xY3A9zE_Ra1A-gtZ1p0yaskQDOtYdLsv4wOw1ERvdQZx7JC0Z2Ka7o4KuKR1PgRiuhVkoZ_x4yOQq9lVGKk5Q7E0gVb3rzrXgXI-YBYFJ-ETxs_VWsQ.jpeg)

You might also want to filter by search intent to only see keywords that are likely to lead to a conversion.

There are four main types of search intent:

- **Navigational**: The searcher is looking for a specific website or page (like “youtube” or “facebook login”)
- **Informational**: The searcher is looking to learn more about a topic or concept (e.g., “how to unclog a drain”)
- **Commercial**: The searcher wants to do research or compare options to inform a decision (like “best gaming laptop” or “monday vs asana”)
- **Transactional**: The searcher is ready or almost ready to take action (e.g., “buy nike shoes” or “subscribe to spotify”)

Business owners are typically most interested in commercial and transactional intent keywords. Because the people searching for these are closer to making a purchase decision.

To filter for these keywords, just click the “**Intent**” drop-down, select the checkboxes next to “Commercial” and “Transactional,” and click “**Apply**.”

![Keyword Magic Tool with the "Intent" filter clicked, "Commercial" and "Transactional" selected from the drop-down, and "Apply" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c6/cc/c6cc52bad62e47efb2c2cbb4dd590376/c7377630df4421e8fa1de17f86c3716c/AD_4nXcehx_SfMcyr-4BcaaZA33nwQdzr9ierXIorDCDcAc9sUyGRNWktaBbUZBBUoCtvKKh31XSd6vL7HAWAKq_YwTIAieXIXdHC1oeRt7tUfrPyoIBdfwxrqcqriGffLUTFKcbRoyk.jpeg)

As you identify promising keywords, jot them down.

### 2. Build Topic Clusters

A [topic cluster](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/) is a group of connected pages on a website that are all focused on a main topic, and using them can help you build [topical authority](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topical-authority/) that leads to better rankings and more trust among visitors.

Each cluster has a main pillar page that gives a comprehensive overview of the topic. And then there are several subpages (or cluster pages) that dive into related subtopics in more detail.

By building topic clusters, you create a well-organized network of related content. This makes it easier for both users and search engines to find information on your site.

![Topic cluster example for the pillar page "Disney World Planning" with cluster pages like packing list, budgeting, best time to visit, itinerary, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/68/4a/684a8219cb86641fd040a3a8b8a364c6/65fbc8e53f7ca0e53c9ca56855380627/AD_4nXdWUkmeIlHbQqvxgsZjgyKtP5y8hhwA90lnvH29SA7XsCSUEbbZppL_lztqDi6HKBID3qgLL8UTBwjo08SKUxLB2Xz-me5O6mOcGi7X-DmbJ3stHQNyQnQNna_8oCU91qVpeGGPoA86LdassMqhI3BXHrAo.png)

### 5. Manage & Optimize Your Business Listings

[Business listings](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-business-listing/) are citations that display important information about your business (name, address, phone number, website, and opening hours), and they’re great for improving your visibility.

You can create listings on platforms like Google, Yelp, and Facebook to reach people who use these sites to search for businesses like yours.

### 7. Track and Monitor Your Progress to Improve Your SEO Strategy

Getting the most out of your SEO efforts involves tracking performance to get ideas on ways to improve.

The specific metrics you track will depend on your goals. But some common ones to track include:

- Organic traffic
- Keyword rankings
- Click-through rate
- Conversion rate

As more visitors land on your site, you can monitor:

- Which product pages or blog posts attract the most traffic
- Which pages your visitors spend the most time on
- What content generates the most engagement
- Which pages drive the most conversions

You can find a lot of this data using tools like Google Analytics (with [conversion tracking set up](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ga4-conversions/)).
