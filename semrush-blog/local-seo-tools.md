## Why Local SEO Tools Matter for Brand Visibility

Local SEO has always been about getting your business in front of the right people at the right time.

Today, people are searching in different places — Google Maps, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Local SEO tools help you stay visible across all of them.

Here's what they help you do:

- **Show up in AI-generated answers:** Build the kind of authoritative, consistent online presence that AI platforms draw from when recommending local businesses
- **Keep your business information consistent:** Accurate, consistent NAP data (name, address, and phone number) across the web gives AI platforms the confidence to recommend your business
- **Get found in local search:** Track your rankings across Google Search and Maps so you can spot drops and act fast
- **Build trust through reviews:** Collect, monitor, and respond to customer reviews. Positive reviews build credibility in the eyes of Google and AI assistants — making them more likely to surface your business in search results

Now, let's look at each local SEO tool in detail.

## 11. Google Search Console

[Google Search Console (GSC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-search-console/) is a free tool that lets you check your website’s performance in Google Search.

(It does **not** include data from your Google Business Profile. These analytics are available through Google Business Profile.)

In the “**Performance**” > “**Search results**” report, choose a date range to see:

- **Total clicks**: The number of clicks on your search results
- **Total impressions**: How many views your results generated
- **Average CTR**: The average click-through rate (CTR)—i.e., clicks divided by impressions
- **Average position**: Your average ranking position in organic (unpaid) results

![boxes checked to show total clicks and impressions as well as average click through rate and average position](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e3/51/e35161d34e27bba4f7de227682f798db/e49fe65b510d6e9cd3000c4d3f218b11/AD_4nXfoUuTYaoNz-_7sx4aRJrEO2CJrZlHB97g2WAI7-Qs3ICSNjCWgayoF9rodbaF6wO-ao8ObNu06B3Jly2p9VZpUXJVKG8t-416LbO3KySrQdCQZDcnT4nBSsdUiAggN8-s38Z-1wg.png)

All these metrics relate to your “top” keywords as chosen by Google.

You can review them in the “Queries” table below.

![queries table shows top queries and their metrics, sorted by number of clicks](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3b/b9/3bb9d447b21747e0b89412f3df3ab767/c82ab77def40835e763146b101abffe2/AD_4nXdJX4OjIRcD707yDTOj0Clt1EDTI_2UkNYcd_7p1cCXJYqb7eiEqp_ocUERzn3MmDx26IUFdmnCf7GBQh4Q9KCo-wHECKCPUDnsIBSiH3BGvhmwj5oblwmHkuv__FBe8_76CNRenw.png)
