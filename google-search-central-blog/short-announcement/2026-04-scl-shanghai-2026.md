# Search Central Live is Coming to Shanghai in 2026!

We are hosting **Search Central Live Shanghai 2026** on May 15.
This event is structured for the local community, with a specific focus on
optimizing sites that target users outside of China.

![Search Central Live Shanghai 2026 logo](/static/search/blog/images/search-central-live-apac/2026/search-central-live-shanghai-2026-logo.png)

The sessions, networking activities, and educational materials will be
delivered in Simplified Chinese and Mandarin.

**If you are fluent in Mandarin** and are interested, come
join us! Register by April 27, 2026. To review the complete event details,
agenda, and registration guidelines, see the full blog posts in Chinese :
