# SEO for Financial Services: What It Is & How to Do It

## What Is SEO for Financial Services?

SEO for financial services is the process of trying to improve a financial service company’s organic (unpaid) visibility in search engines like Google. With the ultimate goal of attracting more clients.

In other words, it’s SEO ([search engine optimization](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-seo/)) tailored to the finance industry.

It can help your business’s website and/or location appear prominently when people search relevant keywords.

Like this:

![search engine results page for "mortgage broker" shows webpages that answer what a mortgage broker is and how they work](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7d/1e/7d1e626a9cec349dc0f88c225d9b1064/754b6b7507edfbc6078765f2acc7321b/AD_4nXduTOHrYLKM-EvrbOV_rtsf2M2h0ISF0t1CDiFgdFRy1flZgBCLiRyFFK11vDfg9hJx92Pd8RJEbWqgKnvo-DTTmICviZ8s0jRcX0ZIFZ1Kfbip3JT32OE52Khm4OCW6Ms5pmvk0e-eCDKr7vDARfAMSWQ.png)

This visibility can help you get more brand awareness and organic traffic.

As for specific tasks, financial services SEO typically involves:

- Publishing quality content around relevant keywords
- Ensuring your website delivers a great user experience
- Making your website accessible to search engine bots
- Improving your brand’s online reputation and prominence
- Creating Google profiles for your business locations (where relevant)

## Why Does SEO Matter in Financial Services?

SEO matters in financial services because search engines drive **2.2 billion** organic visits to websites in the finance industry each month, according to our [Market Explorer](https://www.semrush.com/market-explorer/) tool.

It can also generate brand awareness, foot traffic, and concrete business results for your financial services company.

**To get a bigger slice of these benefits, you need a good SEO strategy.**

Otherwise, you’re likely to lose out to your competitors.

Plus, financial services SEO is primarily about making your website better for users. Those visitors may be more likely to become clients—whether they come to your site from a search engine or somewhere else.

## How to Do SEO for Financial Services

Here’s how to get started with SEO for financial services companies:

### 1. Conduct Keyword Research

[Keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/) is the process of finding and analyzing search terms your target audience uses that are relevant to your business.

It helps you figure out what kind of content to create. And how to optimize it.

To get started, go to Semrush [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).

Enter a broad term related to your business offering or expertise. Then, add your domain, choose your target country, and click “**Search**.”

### 2. Create High-Quality Content

To give yourself the best chance of ranking prominently for your target keywords (the terms you selected in the last step), create [quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/) that:

- Satisfies search intent better than competing content does
- Delivers a great user experience
- Demonstrates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness ([E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/))

E-E-A-T is a framework Google reviewers follow when evaluating the quality of content that appears in search results. So they can provide feedback aimed at improving the search experience.

And it’s particularly important in financial services SEO. Because finance is a [Your Money or Your Life (YMYL)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ymyl/) topic.

In other words, you could cause harm to users by providing inaccurate or misleading information.

One way to establish trust is to have financial experts create or review your content. And display their credentials.

Like JP Morgan does:

And always make sure your content is well-researched.

Sharing methodologies and sources helps prove that your content is reliable.

When doing SEO marketing for financial services, also be conscious of readability. Because the finance industry uses lots of technical terms that audiences may be unfamiliar with.

Use clear explanations, short sentences, and plain language to make your content more accessible. And build better relationships with your readers.

### 3. Follow On-Page SEO Best Practices

[On-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) is about making your webpages more search engine-friendly.

First, use your keywords strategically. So search engines (and users) understand your content is relevant.

Where natural, incorporate them into these elements:

- [**Heading tags**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/header-tag/): The main on-page heading ([H1 tag](https://www.semrush.com/blog/h1-tag/)) and any subheadings (H2s, H3s, etc.)
- [**Title tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/): The HTML page title that can appear in SERPs
- [**URL slug**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url-slug/): The unique part of the webpage address
- [**Alt text**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/): Image descriptions provided for SEO and accessibility purposes
- **Body content**: The main written content on the page

You should also write a [meta description](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/) for each page.

This is a page summary that can appear in the SERP (underneath the title that’s displayed). So, your main goal in crafting one is encouraging users to click through.

![The title tag and meta description in the code matches what appears on the SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/db/6d/db6d56421e598602a0c3479668ceab7b/01153fc85c73337d0105e82c82fd21b2/AD_4nXd8QxagtDQSx1g7M369Gs5OgigIbfaGw3KMbLjJ7h3MlR4h8bO032KLKCkWdQyASE8-sl_A_scXvSFxFqrzU-Gg09Tm0mkL1lOi9orzPedt-x-F_Xx7WDr3-FSOfDyrs4RdqnTE_hI_lBJffCybdiGYNRGl.png)

Some other best practices include:

- Adding [internal links](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/) to relevant pages on your site (making sure to use descriptive [anchor text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/anchor-text/))
- Using [schema markup](https://www.semrush.com/blog/schema-markup/) (code that provides contextual information about your content) to help search engines understand and categorize different types of data on your page

You can get tailored recommendations for your existing content with Semrush’s [On Page SEO Checker](https://www.semrush.com/on-page-seo-checker/).

It provides optimization ideas based on SEO best practices. And an analysis of your top-ranking competitors.

- [**Crawling and indexing**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-crawlability-and-indexability-of-a-website/): Processes that search engines use to find your content and make it available in search results
- [**Core Web Vitals**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/core-web-vitals/): Metrics that Google uses to evaluate page experience (e.g., how quickly your main content loads)
- [**HTTPS**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-https/): A security protocol Google recommends

And much more.

Because Google uses these profiles to populate map-based results.

### 6. Build Backlinks

[Backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) are links on other websites that point to your website.

![when external websites link to your website, these links are called backlinks.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/cc/87/cc873de8f5fa8cb19b1dfd2471df1b9d/0431a10fd325427e02f1e1687a0e3d9d/AD_4nXdShFOE5nCLS379icc7E_jvGjUODQ_HLh2iEuc-s_hBLioiyclUV6Rv8-hWMWpPe8IhPVI24G3hKCJZqgQZSpKBzmKSo8xbhPyF9toA-7-kBuzTdnSJOiYCAk_T9MS7sqvZiGIDYe-GXM7G35utsldcZIE3.png)

If you have backlinks from reputable and relevant websites, Google may perceive your site to be more trustworthy and authoritative.

And that can translate to better rankings.

For example, Chase Bank has backlinks from thousands of finance sites. According to Semrush’s [Backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/) tool:

![a list of referring domains in the finance category](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f4/cb/f4cb44351ee1e3ff935d20cf5cc89b3c/e1eaf10d5c789a8f78bbd8e9081c3b36/AD_4nXdwWqO359dpseGCUdCEPHgD2PVNRszAYasniBCgbgfOLhsTJz1Iejstg2faQjk3HISvJiHtPrYZE5y-xVbgAJikMv5Wsi_r1ycJwRN2Z8BgKQDtBxfhUR7iP50GqwfsriZ8lZz3HvZc_uezcTSfGHq8c13T.png)

The process of trying to acquire valuable backlinks is called [link building](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/).

And there are many tactics you can use, including:

- [**Link baiting**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-bait/): Create content that people naturally want to link to. For example, a financial report containing statistics that journalists want to cite. Or a free mortgage calculator tool that money bloggers can direct their readers to.
- [**Skyscraper technique**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/skyscraper-technique/): Create a better version of a widely linked resource in your niche—then try to inherit its links. For example, one of Chase’s most-linked pages is a home value estimator. So, you might try to create a better version and encourage people to link to yours instead.
- [**Email outreach**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/email-outreach/): Find writers who may be interested in linking to your content (e.g., because you have data that could add value to their article). And email them with your proposal.
- [**Unlinked mentions**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/unlinked-mentions/): Use the [Media Monitoring](https://www.semrush.com/apps/media-monitoring/) app to find unlinked mentions (instances where people refer to your brand without linking) on the web. Then, get in touch with the writer and ask them to add a link.
- [**Broken link building**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/broken-link-building/): Use the [Backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/) tool to find broken backlinks to your own or competitors’ websites. Then, try to get them replaced with functional links to your website.
- **Media requests**: Use a tool like [Connectively](https://www.connectively.us/) to find and respond to relevant media requests (e.g., journalists might request expert commentary about the latest inflation figures). If your submission is used, you’re likely to receive a backlink as attribution.
