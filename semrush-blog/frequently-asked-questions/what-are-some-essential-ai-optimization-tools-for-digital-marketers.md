# What are some essential AI optimization tools for digital marketers?

Semrush [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/en/), [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/brand-performance/), [Yext Scout](https://www.yext.com/platform/scout), and [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/) are some essential AI optimization tools for digital marketers.

These tools provide lots of features that help digital marketers track, analyze, and optimize their presence in AI platforms.

AI platforms are a new frontier for digital marketers. Lots of people are using AI to discover information, find products, or even make purchase decisions. If your brand is invisible in AI responses, you're missing out on potential customers.

AI optimization tools can set you up for success by:

- **Tracking brand visibility.** Monitor how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, and other platforms.
- **Analyzing competitor performance.** See which competitors dominate AI responses in your category and identify opportunities to close visibility gaps.
- **Optimizing content for AI citations.** Learn which pages, formats, and topics get cited most often and adjust your [content strategy](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-marketing-strategy-guide/) accordingly.
- **Monitoring sentiment.** Catch when AI systems describe your brand negatively or incorrectly, so you can work to improve your portrayal.
