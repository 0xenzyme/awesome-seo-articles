## Why It’s Important to Track Metrics for Local SEO

Businesses that know how to measure local SEO can better assess what’s working and identify areas for improvement. Properly tracking local SEO metrics allows managers to make more informed decisions about:

- **Visibility**: Search rankings, website traffic, and Google Business Profile interactions highlight how visible a business is in local searches.
- **Service improvements**: Reviews reveal insights into customer satisfaction and areas for improvement.
- **Outranking competitors**: Keep up with and even surpass your rivals by adapting to their strategies, trends, and customer behavior.
- **Areas to target**: Local SEO metrics help identify which locations generate the most traffic and conversions. With this data, businesses can focus on regions that offer the highest returns.
- **Resource allocation**: Decide where to focus your marketing budget and efforts based on data-driven ROI insights.
- **Refining other marketing campaigns**: Analyzing local search queries helps businesses understand which keywords are most effective. This data informs targeted local SEO advertising and content campaigns that truly resonate with the audience. Better targeting leads to higher engagement and conversion rates.

The benefits of tracking metrics for local SEO are clear. In fact, you could be putting your business at risk by ignoring them. But first, you have to know exactly which local SEO metrics to prioritize.

Tracking the right metrics for local SEO can make or break your digital marketing strategy. Focus on key performance indicators that are most likely to contribute to the success of your local SEO campaign.

### Local Citations

Local citations are mentions of your business on other websites, including directories and social media.

![Yelp results for restaurants in Beverly Hills.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4a/3f/4a3fb74632086ee8e47770b0044ac845/503e4267d52f8219e1d7a0e710b11330/AD_4nXdoVn-RYQFcQ_k1c9wYj4DBE0VjsvMQ8mF5J3pB3uw4e4kt20i52J-WYDtbewf_uRtA7tjsBluNyyUhhXS3X_aIJXRRU9VPq3Yxa4fpxJFjdyyiH5AgPFmXnf9jtoHkcnj6C2vb.png)

Consistent and accurate citations are vital for local SEO. Check the number of citations and ensure that they match your Google Business Profile information to avoid confusion.

### Competitor Metrics

Understanding what your competitors are doing can offer invaluable insights for your own local SEO strategy. By monitoring their SEO tactics, you can identify areas where they excel and uncover opportunities to outperform them.

Keep track of the number of reviews they receive, how well they respond to customers, and where they rank for keywords you're targeting.

### Website Traffic from Local Search

Track organic traffic from local search queries and check how much of that volume is converting.

## How to Measure Local SEO Metrics: 3 Tools

To effectively measure and improve local SEO, businesses need robust tools that provide comprehensive data and insights. While there are several top-quality local SEO tools on the market, these three cover practically everything you need.

### Google Analytics

Google Analytics is a free tool that tracks website traffic, user behavior, and conversions. For local SEO, it helps you understand how much of your traffic comes from local searches. The tool can also reveal which pages are most effective in engaging local visitors.

1. Open the **Reports** menu
2. In the left-hand menu under **User**, click **Demographic details**
3. Break down your users by country, region or city and filter for other metrics

### Google Search Console

Google Search Console offers insights into your website’s search performance. It shows which search queries bring traffic to your site, your search rankings, and any issues that might affect your visibility. You can also discover new search terms to inform your content marketing strategy.

![Google Search Console metrics for local SEO.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/53/c7/53c78be49ef60545d9d47647d37605d4/15a7a836e6875b30b9fd13728dd2c4b2/AD_4nXe3UMnEix7PczXj9udIGFwnlcq8NBIuLCRejrYZE_ceG19XXPto9DpSS4e1BZu-FQS5-vuHLOMLeCrHe4bF3I46XNKc1DAXIWgTNzG5xoq4mHWTc6166xP4KYf_r1xzW9RJ8vKqGw.png)

1. Under the **Performance** menu, click **Search results**.
2. At the top of the page, click **+ New.**
3. Here you can filter for **Queries.** Enter local keywords that you’ve been targeting with website content.
4. You can also filter by **Country** or **Page** to see how a local landing page is performing.
