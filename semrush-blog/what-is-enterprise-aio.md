# What is Enterprise AIO?

[Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/) is Semrush's AI Optimization solution that tracks and improves your brand's visibility across AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

When people ask AI assistants about products or services in your industry, you need to know if your brand gets mentioned—and how accurately it's described. Enterprise AIO gives you that visibility through advanced monitoring capabilities.

Here's what Enterprise AIO does for your brand:

- **Track AI mentions across platforms.** Monitor how often your brand appears in responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and other AI systems when users ask relevant questions
- **Analyze mention sentiment and accuracy.** See whether AI descriptions of your brand are positive, neutral, or negative, and catch any factual errors about your products or pricing
- **Benchmark against competitors.** Compare your [AI visibility](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-visibility/) to rivals to understand your share of AI-generated recommendations in your category
- **Identify content gaps**. Discover what questions users ask AI systems about your industry that you're not adequately addressing in your content strategy
- **Monitor algorithmic footprint changes.** Track how algorithm updates or content changes affect your brand's presence in AI responses over time

Enterprise AIO helps you actively manage your "algorithmic footprint" rather than letting AI systems define your brand narrative without your knowledge or input.

As more customers use AI for research and recommendations, understanding and optimizing your AI presence becomes essential for maintaining competitive visibility.
