# How do you improve SEO on Amazon?

You improve [Amazon SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/amazon-seo/) by optimizing your product listings with relevant keywords, high-quality images, and competitive pricing to increase visibility in Amazon search results.

Amazon SEO focuses on making your products more discoverable when customers search for items you sell, leading to more clicks and sales.

Key tactics to improve Amazon SEO:

- **Research buyer keywords.** Use Amazon's search bar suggestions and analyze competitor listings to find terms customers actually use when shopping
- **Optimize your product title.** Include your main keyword in the first 80 characters along with key features like brand, size, or color
- **Write benefit-focused bullet points.** Highlight product features and benefits using secondary keywords naturally throughout the descriptions
- **Add high-quality product images.** Use professional photos that fill 85% of the frame, include lifestyle shots, and add videos when possible
- **Maintain competitive pricing and Prime shipping.** Price competitively and offer fast shipping to improve your chances of winning the Buy Box
- **Encourage genuine customer reviews.** Use Amazon's Request a Review feature to ask satisfied customers for feedback
- **Keep inventory stocked consistently.** Running out of stock hurts your search rankings and momentum

Drive initial sales with Amazon PPC campaigns using Sponsored Products, then monitor performance through click-through rates and conversion rates to continuously optimize your listings.
