# What is SEO Copywriting?

[SEO copywriting](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-copywriting/) is writing content that ranks well in search engines while persuading readers to take action.

It combines keyword optimization with persuasive writing to create content that both Google and your audience will value, driving both traffic and conversions.

Key elements of effective SEO copywriting include:

- **Keyword integration.** Naturally including target keywords in headlines, subheadings, and throughout the content without [keyword stuffing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-stuffing/)
- **Addressing** [**search intent**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/)**.** Understanding what users really want when they search and providing comprehensive answers to their questions
- **Persuasive elements.** Adding strong calls-to-action, benefits-focused messaging, and social proof to guide readers toward your goals
- **Readable structure.** Using short paragraphs, bullet points, and clear headings that both users and search engines can easily scan
- **Conversion optimization.** Balancing SEO requirements with elements that drive action, like compelling headlines and strategic link placement

For example, an SEO copywriter creating a "best email marketing tools" article would target that keyword while also including comparison tables, pros and cons, and clear recommendations that help readers choose the right tool.
