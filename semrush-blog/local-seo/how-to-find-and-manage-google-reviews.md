# How to See Your Google Reviews and Easily Manage Them

Prospective customers frequently read Google reviews to make decisions about a business.

That means it’s important to know what people are saying in their reviews about you.

In this article, you’ll learn exactly how to find your reviews on Google. And even get some tips on how to manage them.

## How to See Your Google Reviews on Desktop

You can find Google reviews on a desktop device by searching for your business’s name in Google Search or Google Maps.

Let’s go over both methods:

### How to Find Your Google Reviews with Search

Search your business name on Google, and you should see your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) (GBP).

At the top of the profile, you’ll see your average star rating and total number of reviews.

Click “**# Google reviews**.”

![Google search result for Gramercy Tavern with star rating, photos, and options like call, directions, and reserve a table](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ab/7c/ab7cb4be4c2dbee2b409244457a83309/7de87d71b2d6994209d1dc5546d5e74c/AD_4nXedeufmICheEWuBPaDo6vcH2_hb-Qon5R9jHHnHdFgTozbOFrSzj9RhE2V1W7LK7g8e2dV3nBtfZGxf6ClWcnmIwzm7-M81Wi97hvO8jyRakzzzbP0SxfiNpAzXlBkLtiMXbTZyVQ.jpeg)

You’ll see a pop-up window showing a full list of your reviews.

![Google reviews panel for Gramercy Tavern showing review filters, sort options, a detailed review, and owner reply](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ff/a1/ffa190bd237d0e7e133fc3483988b758/45e21271ed78c392696d6abd9db9d89d/AD_4nXdMLpKtVvpqz6WGxguVqXx1HxzUFBzb6QYbbsDjQmgkGkX-8tTMd86xJcAHxSeFFWrwAvXVI9euThkSTYWuyigTlTZOGJJnF5MIeTu5a7OSpoar6P1f9ricpYSSuT2JZXxkRplc9A.jpeg)

### How to Find Your Google Reviews with Maps

Head to [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/), search for your business name, and click on your business in the suggestions that appear in the drop-down menu.

![Google Maps search for Gramercy Tavern with suggestions and map view showing nearby areas](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b4/39/b4398547211455a61de6f91b7021a58b/193f6caff23bc388a8804e5a6fa92f51/AD_4nXf0R63BaKogD7EFodPW-NrLW2hHVw1E4ZQ0ABDqqXjZz5Zgq0PRx3h3054WhccjOdmxrX0LC91ku9VGCN6I48SdAUjqnseDowcGb_P9U8MNgricOlHauOJYW2sL6GjoCIan1bfGYQ.jpeg)

Your Google Business Profile will appear on the left, showing your star rating and number of reviews near the top.

Click the “**Reviews**” tab near the top to view all reviews.

![Google Maps listing for Gramercy Tavern with star rating, reviews tab highlighted, and map showing location and nearby places](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8f/6a/8f6a6457aa1448f237341ac62d654d17/6c23b9d5b51a17898de9409e9aa28c49/AD_4nXdb-1sRork787x927XMT2h2hPzt8TYv-lAocnh6p2ExPQ0RrNFcYx0C-hwHYZoAx_f6fUOYkeBYIxGOrH0jyToAzyKVvFdY7Po-KtHE2cHmcljIY2b_rKWW1UYX7diaBc3Z0ZasOw.jpeg)

This opens the full review section for your business.

![Google Maps screen showing Gramercy Tavern’s reviews tab with star rating, rating breakdown, and nearby map](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fe/fb/fefbb0bbe3ae322a3c265199fb6d38f1/04491b4be87712533f54fa4ce60b4057/AD_4nXczbJoUruT8g7sy2zxv6ReWxblG1pXsPZgg-OtOfN4ZpE4hgxaOHug7LgPkDUaZkUa1RI_goKKA8TBjS-jkQrQUDKqr63peeLI3rMmf5iKt-ETBIcUw4CL4qUjRehW0mQv3tjq3VQ.jpeg)

## How to See Your Google Reviews on Mobile

You can check your Google reviews on mobile using the Google Search app, any browser, or the Google Maps app.

### How to Find Google Reviews with Search on iPhone & Android

Open the Google app or go to Google in your mobile browser and search for your business name.

You’ll see your Business Profile at the top, along with your average star rating and number of reviews.

![Google search result for Gramercy Tavern with star rating, photos, and options like call, directions, and reserve a table](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9a/f9/9af9d699be51741cc79450fdc2f7425f/b5c00db6737ee229a00d3226f7efc81a/AD_4nXctOWJtRY5O59L4aWE35UcupzWwPdoIbG0kuw5nlA_yItpBWoxv7n-5zIvrKTNp3mmjSS8AVBgwdKDOy5eITdjgoVmznHpxrhRnc5hWEL4h2VHcLRS4ar72Tpdjk19PhOlLGmnJ.jpeg)

Tap the “**Reviews**” tab to view all visible reviews from customers.

![Google reviews page for Gramercy Tavern showing average rating, review summary, and detailed customer reviews](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/80/73/8073a39b3592c894bfc4b0cfb391ea29/707dc34e3ae7f8d1fc89f249cb6d6e7d/AD_4nXc4bbsi1TYpIq_fuqQkdWo2U9InbI2BgNDkRrVDOTR5NM0-wbUxgvqyorG3wa7BsVm0uJmtzC1sWmUZfr-PI6H6xcvjrjDd5S_FJD7c4eWMz_d_34krFib3pz-J7DMmccvy-emN_w.png)

### How to Find Google Reviews with the Maps App on iPhone & Android

Open the Maps app, search for your business name, and tap your listing from the drop-down menu to see your Business Profile.

From there, tap the “**Reviews**” tab to open the full list of reviews.

![Google Maps business profile for Gramercy Tavern with star rating, photos, directions, and reviews tab highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/35/1d/351dec431a7e067bc27b86c2e2e69458/75165e2e7ee0518c4b612b9692db70c0/AD_4nXe7ZwG5o7EFvG4SQ5VJHzN6h_6AcuyosLV-BKzy8jMtZkeO3LVQiUPtYxMu7qaN0-AbpbS836L49EVZUaukXUdtYpjhNpN1M_-VvYFpW706yKmgvyhW--2ZRVI9tOpLp2c-PkWUfA.jpeg)

## How to Analyze Your Google Reviews

Analyzing your reviews helps you see what customers like, what they dislike, and what topics show up often.

Let’s use Doughnut Plant’s Flatbrush location in Brooklyn as an example.

The average rating is 4.5, with most reviews at four or five stars.

But there are more one-star reviews than two-star, which may point to more intense dissatisfaction when things go wrong.

![Google review summary with star rating bars from 1 to 5 stars and a write a review button](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/05/b2/05b23bc5938e7e3f76ba0b2d135c8fcb/eb72997d8dfc6488d7cf29fe6f6dd74b/AD_4nXc23Xooa4EXzC77-ZOkpiXG4t8DDw6NcX9o73-AHrbrP4z3iiUSkWuG9eDcTXwRtYRXa8n1FxpZPvkVdr72_jFtgaTSIDH4C2wTqvNZQiquNETHl69sTADNP0OQ-qR6fGG8gZ57kg.png)

Google also highlights common topics mentioned in reviews.

For instance, “creme brulee donut” shows up 14 times.

![Google reviews filter with selectable tags like service, meal, vegan, and highlighted creme brulee donut tag](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fa/a4/faa49a8b3bca1c752c5f49df35188891/0305a3d516f14f8acebe71957607ec5d/AD_4nXetWEaUuc4MMnktWx8AtMBEKjhJmanFRRregOwQfIcfLRtP1FfKxX_1v_ZJCRXEzXVtXnxHRc9Lo4KI-QLIVhvPk1ZngpSxftJ85FA9-NDBrKu5MnOR_ymP2pGBt53K5c18x06VFQ.png)

Clicking into it reveals some insights about visitors’ taste preferences

You’ll also notice many of the reviews come from Local Guides.

These carry more weight publicly and may show more prominently in search.

![Two Google Local Guide reviews with star ratings, review text, and photos of donuts and café interiors](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/32/0e/320e4e879d6ddb60f109f9228dfed7f4/2a0103870257f190517f9477f8e3a548/AD_4nXdKePEGZNvROe2tH2FvIHQK6kL6SxsSmmBD36ILVUWNe3KBn8GulQh_pHcQLnYE4XTPTchKsv7GMLOhQPwL9ZR-VvMzfaNDkNq7136qwsAzHG-6n2jZOvpZalHUPBCF0EtrMFJklw.jpeg)

## How to Manage Your Google Reviews

Managing and responding to your Google reviews shows potential customers that you're active, engaged, and willing to address feedback.

You can manage your Google reviews from your Google Business Profile dashboard.

Go to your GBP dashboard and click “**Read reviews**.”

![Google Business Profile dashboard with icons for editing profile, photos, updates, and a highlighted Read reviews button](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a7/dd/a7dda3494f8b428776d3265fdad23d63/2e764c163dc923b2251e220927c10140/AD_4nXcy6OrYFuGi_S_1DgGw6eAlTnU-J_D7syvPwew20SOjXOIOOxXXbH-F8cxDLV_jKErgkSZUYBhFBQm632z_t49ZCbARWZ9ylg1J3Gr9nXhBaGi4i6CFSmAzQTnXOqGcUeh51G5lHw.png)

You’ll see a list of all reviews left for your business.

From there, you can reply, edit a reply, or report a review.

## How Users Can Edit or Delete Their Own Google Reviews

If you've left a review for another business and want to update or remove it, you can do this from Google Maps, either from your desktop or the mobile app.

We'll show you how to do this on desktop, but the process is nearly identical on mobile.

Open Google Maps, click the menu icon in the top-left corner, and click "**Your contributions**."

![Google Maps menu section](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e9/87/e987aa6ba0eed57295e98137b12fd657/47c1afa9f24805bc3d19f69074f09492/image.png)

Then go to the "**Reviews**" tab. You'll see every review you've ever left.

![Reviews tab in Google Maps](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5c/39/5c39d4d3b58cfe1d04753bfd54100d7c/a01dbbf4625b9fa2832e1a068ca70de9/image.png)

Find the review you want to change and click the three-dot icon next to it. From there, you can either edit the review (to change your star rating or update the text) or delete it entirely.

![Editing Google Maps review](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e1/32/e132605385baa8aa84645188f1f41856/6125423a6b04685e2f2b0b275bfcfb67/image.png)
