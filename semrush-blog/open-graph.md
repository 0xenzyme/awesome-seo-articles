# How to Use Open Graph Tags to Boost Social Engagement

## What Is Open Graph?

Open Graph tags are snippets of code you add to your webpages that define how your content appears when users share it on certain social media sites.

OG meta tags belong to the [Open Graph protocol](https://ogp.me/), which Facebook created to standardize how metadata is pulled from webpages and displayed in its feed.

For example, take this blog post shared on Facebook with an Open Graph image tag:

![Blog post shared on Facebook by Semrush with an open graph image tag.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/95/f4/95f46697040e6761230430a659dd61df/8b38b1f8b1149660c7140db66e4506b2/AD_4nXe7hQgEjXMNfeFjjo-rvyP0YTzVIYCk4_uRsLMcrsY9CQXRHSus5lo1tsw51CR8L5GXF1RnRW_T3rbRDnaQxBSRJvFUnNiNlqVlibjxiPGNuLUuPMt7DkM2Ju-w2plcH3y6QbgMOQ.jpeg)

Compared with this post without the OG image tag:

![Blog post shared on Facebook without an open graph image tag.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/eb/78/eb78f1094ec13aa723d7f6a169d6f54f/b36027116cb6830fd5c91cd183490198/AD_4nXeR3Kb_1q6wxt0kH17wcvAhp25C-qQFhQtkCQifkwwYGKrm7ljtIoeq-LJUECtt5JmEE1AUaUO4aDZYZCKUpmyPQKgLItaMXfuur-BuhORZegnBKyNDFRiZ2WzcibtqODCswHnEkg.jpeg)

OG tags give you more control over how your content looks when shared on sites like Facebook, LinkedIn, X (formerly Twitter), and Pinterest.

## Why Are Open Graph Meta Tags Important?

Open Graph meta tags create appealing and informative previews of your pages, which can improve [social media metrics](https://www.semrush.com/blog/social-media-metrics/) like engagements, impressions, and reactions.

A [2024 INMA study](https://www.inma.org/blogs/research/post.cfm/research-photo-posts-produce-significantly-more-engagement-than-link-posts-on-facebook) showed that Facebook posts with images had 100% more engagement and 114% more impressions than posts without images.

Other Open Graph tag benefits may include:

- **Higher** [**click-through rate (CTR)**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/click-through-rate/): Link previews are more enticing to click on, which can lead to more referral traffic to your site
- **More shares**: People are more likely to share content with link previews
- **Stronger branding**: Enriched previews help maintain [brand voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/brand-voice/) and consistency across social platforms
- **Professional look**: Missing tags can make your brand appear neglected
- **Indirect SEO benefits**: More clicks and shares can generate [backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) and user interactions that benefit [SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-seo/)

## Open Graph Types: Required & Optional Tags

Open Graph tags enrich how social media platforms display your page previews. Some tags are required. Others are optional. This section provides standard code snippets, best practices, and examples.

### Required Tags

The below tags must be present for social media sites using the Open Graph Protocol to display enriched previews.

#### og:title

The og:title tag specifies a title for your content and shows users what your page is about.

`<meta property="og:title" content="Insert Your Title Here">`

**Best Practices**:

- Match the webpage’s content
- Encourage clicks with descriptive text
- Keep titles between 55-60 characters to avoid truncation
- Use title case or sentence case, but avoid all caps

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:title" content="250+ Ways to Grow Your Business Fast">`

#### og:image

og:image adds an image to your preview that takes up a lot of visual space, which can increase clicks.

`<meta property="og:image" content="Insert image URL here">`

**Best Practices**:

- Use images at 1200 x 630 pixels for most social media platforms
- Use JPEG or PNG formats
- Ensure your OG social image matches your page content
- Use high-resolution images

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:image" content="​​https://www.businessnewsdaily.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.businessnewsdaily.com%2Fapp%2Fuploads%2F2016%2F05%2F06125310%2Fhire-the-right-people.png&w=1080&q=75">`

#### og:url

og:url is the [uniform resource locator (URL)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url/) of your page and tells social media sites where to pull the content from.

`<meta property="og:url" content="Insert your canonical URL here">`

**Best Practices**:

- Use an exact URL.
- Use the [canonical URL](https://www.semrush.com/blog/canonical-url-guide/) for any duplicates, which ensures the social media site can access the right metaproperties, even if other versions of the page are shared

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:url" content="​​https://talkroute.com/8-basic-reasons-your-small-business-isnt-growing-tips/">`

#### og:type

og:type describes the type of content and should match one of the following [object type](https://ogp.me/#types) values:

- Website
- Article
- Book
- Profile
- Video
- Music

`<meta property="og:type" content="Insert the type here"/>`

**Best Practices**:

- Choose an OG type that matches the page’s content. If you have multiple content types (e.g., a blog post with images and videos), choose the primary one
- Use the correct corresponding object type value

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:type" content="article"/>`

### Optional Tags

These tags are not required, but they can improve how social media sites display your content.

#### og:description

The og:description tag provides a brief summary of your content to motivate clicks.

`<meta property="og:description" content="Insert your description here">`

**Best Practices**:

- Keep your descriptions concise, accurate, and enticing
- Use fewer than 120 characters to prevent truncation
- Avoid repeating the og:title

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:description" content="Learn how to generate 10X more leads for your business with our free guide">`

#### og:site\_name

The og:site\_name tag lists the site, business, or brand associated with the content.

`<meta property="og:site_name" content="Insert your brand name here">`

**Best Practices**:

- Verify spelling and match your branding
- Avoid repeating your brand name if it appears in the og:title

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:site_name" content="IMDb">`

#### og:locale

The og:locale tag specifies your content’s language and country so social media sites can categorize it.

`<meta property="og:locale" content="Insert language and country in correct format">`

**Best Practices**:

- Use the correct [language and country code](https://saimana.com/list-of-country-locale-code/)
- Confirm the correct order (i.e., language\_country)
- Use og:local:alternate for additional regions or languages

**Example**:

`<meta property="og:locale" content="en_AU">`

## How to Set Up OG Tags

Here’s how to add Open Graph tags on a few popular [content management systems](https://www.semrush.com/contentshake/content-marketing-blog/content-management-system/).

### In Wordpress

In WordPress, you can add Open Graph tags through various plugins, but we recommend Yoast SEO.

To access and use the plugin for Open Graph, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your WordPress account
2. Navigate to “**Plugins**” and search for “Yoast SEO” (the Premium version is required)
3. Install and activate the plugin
4. Navigate to “**Yoast SEO**” > “**Settings**”
5. Slide the toggle beneath the Open Graph feature to enable it
6. Use the “**Social**” tab in the plugin to customize tags on individual pages

![Social media appearance settings on Yoast with options to customize the image, title, and description.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d4/24/d424dbbdca669e3bdb5ddb1fe36b21e6/07bb9815f1bbe7cfe229a4b6ec5eb746/AD_4nXfh1Bq_cCCY5gUWWAucKW8oFK9Rw2OgfsWG7hIdwM-7ve4mp2rh1wTAxNEMvM0vZkPAuVCd5k61ywfJGyf-nugBHhsI1YZZC25LzYp7c6kYPK_YXJQVAaDH9kMdEL_HWDrMpUIWZA.jpeg)

### In Wix

Wix adds Open Graph tags to your pages by default based on your site’s data and Open Graph best practices.

To customize them, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Wix account
2. Go to “**Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**” in your dashboard
3. Click on “**SEO Settings**”
4. Select the page type you want to edit (i.e., Blog post, Product page, etc.)
5. Click “**Edit**” next to “SEO basics & social share”
6. Scroll down to “Social share” and apply your custom tags

![Social share tab on Wix showing a preview of the post along with options to customize the title and description.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7b/f7/7bf7a401f80f35ccef776c897719a1f0/54a42e37e191c042f1e1f097a2549251/AD_4nXfDatPgW982VN6oj_vTuFfrYrtbEiQfU7eeoCd_7l5vytZQoiqrcE6rvB5MevehqE2WW9WIaJWTzAHdQd9xiMo-RmMoamX_nJdycfB-GwCIZUxTZiush6dXH0tDYkLhM8k4_N_7.jpeg)

### In Squarespace

Squarespace adds essential OG types, but you can customize them on a per-page basis.

Here’s how:

1. Log in to your Squarespace account
2. Hover over the page to edit and click on the gear icon next to “Menu”
3. Click “**Advanced**”
4. Copy and paste your OG tags into “Page Header Code Injection”
5. Click “**Save**”

![Advanced settings for a page on Squarespace with the 'Page Header Code Injection' table and the 'Save' button highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6c/79/6c79f52de55f692870a1540b27befaca/049452f8623d7b72a65450743d775d60/AD_4nXdIui-Kl6j3zqeREWxRNAFNeTtubvEORoJ18odnDfD3usqA_2ogES5aQJrddrHnxYIT_clmxV89VfjwovbFJ1EnIw7SFxA_WHzaEx4ooaWBgoo-IHruWRov6-pmCN1_N6Rd6EgkxA.jpeg)
