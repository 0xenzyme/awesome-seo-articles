# 16 Best Website Monitoring Tools in 2025 (Free & Paid)

Website monitoring tools help ensure you provide a great experience for your users.

They can help you keep track of:

- **Website performance**: Track your website’s speed through metrics like page load time and Google’s Core Web Vitals
- **Usability**: Learn how visitors use your website through user session recordings and heatmap analysis
- **Uptime**: Monitor the availability of your website to prevent downtime
- [**Search engine optimization (SEO)**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-seo/): Track how well your website is optimized for search engines and detect any issues that might be holding you back in search rankings

Below, we’ve gathered the 16 best website monitoring tools for a variety of use cases.

## 1. Domain Overview

**Best for**: Monitoring your competitors’ organic search performance
**Pricing**: Starts at $139.95/month, free trial and free account available (10 searches per day)

Semrush’s [Domain Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview) tool lets you monitor a domain’s search traffic, market share, and search visibility. This lets you monitor your own—or your competitors’–organic search performance.

Just type in a domain and click “**Search**.”

![Domain Overview tool start with "chewy.com" entered as the domain and "Search" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/dd/6c/dd6c6d7b3d8a5e8fd60f4b2e63f4fcf7/ea5e8b9f55918234cda80c8081e6cb26/AD_4nXe133lj2AR7x-3zrmWS-PuKm0mIBAlBB_O8w3jnBZa1JiA7KhktYlda56a4UNXs7jysGJz029OqNIPJEi4p9ZbvSac6eWtrvmC-ah4PG6DNnwl6iEjHEI5F-vnrKmSveN3sa6Tc.jpeg)

The tool will then generate a detailed report on the domain.

You’ll be able to see the website’s total organic and paid search traffic. And the number of [backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) pointing to it.

![Domain Overview report with organic search traffic, paid search traffic, and backlinks highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5c/a5/5ca575bbebedd3ca1dc9d269dbe58272/6bdbd2512f63e7c1f591ab6506d67ff5/AD_4nXdDyZmK92whHKfzmelZ4SBsAD7iT7mpUM0d_AdWUvHgdBZKz9lfJFJ2nEwG56osrXU7EQORiOezIJgRBFv9lcquzFGmrTNOOvm-Cic41nUS8ZTP8k-9hAZ5xvnb1LUIfwI933BaYQ.jpeg)

If you scroll further down, you can examine the geographical breakdown of the domain’s search traffic. Along with traffic and keyword ranking trends over time.

This makes it ideal for monitoring your own performance as you roll out SEO campaigns. Or for keeping tabs on your rivals’ content marketing efforts.

![Domain overview report with distribution by country, organic traffic, and organic keywords sections highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1d/4b/1d4b04fe56408b03e840b70e94027005/3ff4c9ef75982e109e0682596685b4ec/AD_4nXfI43TP1jYtHxCBedTC1jyTTcXj7hhSksqlv7vWu7Ba2tnNh4RdjikmHRC51TCfOBXSZQTL4XRbRW4H_9tzsB_4kZglKZRIOvcL7h5RrLHcr9lJw7PRuGuX1jmTw4SM-wlpchvYBw.jpeg)

Head to the “**Growth Report**” tab to compare any domain’s performance over specified time periods.

![Growth report on Domain Overview showing organic traffic and keywords performance over time.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e8/4e/e84e01f9c8bc173623ff85e0c80f3f50/80a920d7cb5da7e76e91038aeaf0be3d/AD_4nXcy1t8o62UuREBqAohICjsIZjbVGBwkp0C_hl3toXkMji3W-8cQS3WBG0JUigzOPmHkOm-YVaKkF-UsmD0ghlCzKlooMMYMgGeWXDlToMsWaU1t92WhgMwXP2L4J8QkdED16J98jQ.jpeg)

This lets you spot traffic patterns, ranking changes, and seasonal trends. Whether for your own website or to keep an eye on your competitors.

## 2. Site Audit

**Best for**: Monitoring your website for SEO issues
**Pricing**: Starts at $139.95/month, free trial and free account available (crawl 100 URLs)

[Site Audit](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) monitors your website for SEO issues that might be preventing you from achieving higher search engine rankings. It covers site performance, [Core Web Vitals](https://www.semrush.com/blog/core-web-vitals/), [internal linking](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/), [crawlability](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-crawlability-and-indexability-of-a-website/), and more.

Here’s how to use it:

Enter your domain and click “**Start Audit**.”

![Site Audit tool start with a domain entered and "Search" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d9/4e/d94ebb426a0a384c0ffc49544a2dfca5/be448c915a7d980e6dbcd6626f28cbf4/AD_4nXenSR3BMhebwrHFHHFt9a6kKODr4QwEgd9UdZ-yX8MCehXR4_OJeJl4wVpZ2tBW6zJHcmSzkQ4P0Yd6bVsqZajtWPSLkyIoHuUVgUPontURrTui4F-qeHTXz5h1UjXhCm5G1gQFwg.jpeg)

On the next page, you’ll be able to adjust a number of settings for the audit, including the total number of pages to check, the user agent for the site crawl, and more.

In most cases, you can stick with the default settings and click on “**Start Site Audit**.”

![Site Audit settings with options to select the crawl scope, source, and limit of checked pages.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/71/74/7174b959db941a3d3987f4870e39cb69/21f29ca8923a3c211ed80bf775217724/AD_4nXctmriRjY6yEOrFdO6t8L9EBgg30Nthih-8lj2yxlzAyZkcu8O0wHUfTeFS1A6xSNul4dQumKeuSbHLwWKQRxkp8v5gqjrQzrSmWuz6S8MbRiMZNCReJq_Gz0lmtDGspzvUbJ1nGQ.jpeg)

The tool will then crawl your website and present you with a detailed overview report. This gives you a rundown of your site’s overall health, crawled pages, and top issues.

![Site Audit overview report showing a site health score, different thematic reports, errors, warnings, and notices, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f4/40/f440a2b09d791ee52e4d4eaefc5f3085/cebc24486219ef3cd802ef0a451b433a/AD_4nXcIGwSX_Fn4EdKHD_PuqBcJA_4TaZ8BRFbBoH_AkrJuNsqq4wvajQbRHtVmbGo_4vO7F82nZcqK359nY4dJzQfXqejLqleRvJEam6egBG128qP21ehzTMy2Uj9mjy6CmutmI_F8Fw.jpeg)

Go to the “**Issues**” tab to see a list of all the discovered SEO issues on your site.

Click the linked part of an issue to see all the pages where that specific issue is present. Or click “**Why and how to fix it**” to learn more about the issue.

!["Why and how to fix it" clicked next to an error on Site Audit issues showing more information on how to fix the specific issue.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/73/71/73713a747d21cab61f972dc6de3a6e11/b498ae565897c3692bb0d09b4171c571/AD_4nXftTrtqrRYf23H1ecQuFRcIsLLm19UgGOvPMPZT_GSN2TAYMZdUZPpXvFMw7q0vZtD33a6ueZGU3eg-17PZidrFKtIM2Ztzjs2lhwefP6j7z0HlbLclRq71Wrgkwte3RAkJNomXZw.jpeg)

You can schedule Site Audit to regularly crawl your site.

Then, head to the “**Progress**” tab to understand how your site's health is improving over time.

!["Progress" tab on Site Audit showing how a domain's site health is progressing over time.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/58/7d/587d15264a831609eee4c8a89622fe78/2e2946cd13c775742f7eacd352355e62/AD_4nXdvh7mJ9cDCF9jsZwy1Y43t-Z4KKXw8koXYzK0QD2gFmObnXJU3ua3Sb52636YLSUAkIlNx1CqyBr5Oxzh2Gnc7bEE-7ePhoc688cObxNF06wiYcKONcGXY8evx2YsWu5A3fZYmVw.jpeg)

This way, you can compare crawls as you (or your developers) fix your technical SEO issues.

## 6. Middleware

**Best for**: Synthetic monitoring
**Pricing**: $0.3 per GB of metrics, logs, or traces; free plan available

[Middleware](https://middleware.io/) is a cloud observability platform that lets you monitor website infrastructure, logs, and databases in real-time.

You can use its synthetic monitoring feature to spot issues in a **simulated****environment**. So that you can fix them before they impact **real****users**.

You can also use Middleware to perform real user monitoring. This can help you identify and address points of friction or confusion in the website experience (e.g., users not knowing where to click to move to the next step).

![Middleware dashboard showing different metrics like average error rates and details about each error.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ee/89/ee8998d5e3a3051480babafd4990ef1b/2946725aa4215164d782c35582cb76f1/AD_4nXeEcDZe8Zm5PSD7gHVvGjf2P6iAhx4KrdPFxz_dLVM1XZSHLJXwEwVk9fU_30kIdIUP7ICb6nKZLK0ZTvF-hi2R2Nzw-LkNIAXbEP232UHbxaBCknS5BIMld5CDO4zhugxXIWLz9A.jpeg)

## 7. Pingdom

**Best for**: Page speed monitoring
**Pricing**: Starts at $15/month, free trial available

[Pingdom](https://www.pingdom.com/) offers synthetic and real user monitoring from 100+ locations worldwide. And it sends you instant alerts when issues arise.

When it comes to page speed monitoring, Pingdom doesn’t just show you how fast your website is. It also provides recommendations on how to make your site even faster.

Since [page speed](https://www.semrush.com/blog/page-speed/) is a [known ranking factor](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2018/01/using-page-speed-in-mobile-search), following these recommendations can potentially help you improve your website’s rankings.

There’s also a built-in transaction monitoring feature. This helps you track key processes, such as user signups, logins, website searches, and purchases. And the tool immediately notifies you about any issues.

Plus, the platform is built with privacy in mind. Which means that it only records user interactions and not sensitive data.
