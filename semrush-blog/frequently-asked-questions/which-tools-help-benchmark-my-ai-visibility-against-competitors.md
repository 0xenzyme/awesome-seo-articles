# Which tools help benchmark my AI visibility against competitors?

Semrush [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/en/), [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/brand-performance/), [RankScale](https://rankscale.ai/), [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/), and [Scrunch](https://scrunchai.com/) can help you benchmark your AI visibility against your competitors.

Benchmarking AI visibility is crucial because it shows whether you're winning or losing the AI recommendation battle in your industry.

Here's what these AI benchmarking tools provide:

- **Share of voice tracking across AI platforms:** See exact percentages of how often your brand appears versus competitors in AI platforms for relevant queries
- **Query-level competitive analysis:** Understand which specific questions or topics competitors dominate and where you have opportunities to gain visibility
- **Sentiment comparison:** See whether AI describes your brand more or less favorably than competitors
- **Actionable competitive insights.** Get specific recommendations on how to close visibility gaps
