# How does rank tracking contribute to SEO success?

Rank tracking contributes to SEO success by providing measurable data on your search visibility, helping you make informed optimization decisions and prove [ROI](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-roi/) from your SEO efforts.

Instead of guessing whether your SEO work is effective, rank tracking shows exactly which keywords and pages are improving or declining in search results.

Key ways rank tracking drives SEO success:

- **Identify what's working and what isn't.** See which optimization efforts moved your rankings up and which strategies need adjustment
- **Catch problems before they hurt traffic.** Quickly detect algorithm impacts, competitor movements, or technical issues affecting your rankings
- **Spot quick win opportunities.** Find [keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/) ranking positions 8-15 that could reach the top 5 with targeted optimization
- **Monitor competitor performance.** Track when competitors gain or lose rankings for your target keywords
- **Discover** [**SERP feature**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/) **opportunities.** Identify chances to earn featured snippets, local packs, or other special search results
- **Measure actual business impact.** Correlate ranking improvements with traffic and conversion increases to prove SEO value

For example, rank tracking might show that your "email marketing tips" page moved from position 12 to position 4, resulting in 300% more organic traffic and 15 new leads last month.
