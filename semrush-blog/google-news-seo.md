# 5 Google News SEO Tactics to Help Your Articles Rank

Optimizing your content for Google News can give you visibility beyond standard SEO.

We’ll tell you exactly how to do that in this article, but let’s first go over the basics.

## What Is Google News?

Google News is a news aggregation service that curates content from publishers and presents users with personalized news articles based on their interests, locations, and preferences.

In other words, it uses technology to find and organize relevant stories from many different sources. Then shows those stories to people likely to find them interesting or useful.

## How Does Google News Select Stories?

Google News uses computer algorithms to select and rank content.

Here are seven key [ranking factors](https://support.google.com/news/publisher-center/answer/9606702) for Google News:

1. **Relevance of content**: The degree to which an article aligns with the user's search query or interests
2. **Prominence**: The significance of an event based on whether it’s being heavily covered by news outlets
3. **Authoritativeness**: The credibility of the source
4. **Freshness**: The recency of the content, with newer articles generally prioritized to provide up-to-date information for timely topics
5. **Usability**: The overall user experience on the site (page load times, mobile compatibility, etc.)
6. **Location**: The geographical relevance of the content to the user
7. **Language**: The language the content is written in and whether it matches users’ preferences

The Google News “For you” tab also involves aligning with interests users have specified. Or interests Google has gathered based on the users’ activity settings.

## How to Appear in Google News: 5 Tactics

Any site that creates timely, relevant, and original content (like traditional news publishers or sites that cover current events) can appear in Google News surfaces.

Here’s how to do Google News SEO:

### 1. Adhere to Google’s News Guidelines

[Google’s News guidelines](https://support.google.com/news/publisher-center/answer/6204050) emphasize transparency, accurate bylines, clear publication dates, and comprehensive information about the authors or organization behind the content.

Google also implements strict rules about ads, sponsored content, and transparency. Here are some specific guidelines to take note of:

- Avoid misleading previews (like clickbait headlines) that can misrepresent your content
- Display author names and publication dates for all articles
- Be transparent about your publication and any companies or networks behind it
- Clearly disclose sponsored content and ads
- Make sure promotional content doesn’t exceed editorial content

And comply with Google’s [article best practices](https://support.google.com/news/publisher-center/answer/9607104), overall [search policies](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/10622781), and [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

### 2. Find Trending Topics You Can Cover

Creating content around trending topics can help you rank on Google News because it helps you address the relevance ranking factor.

Find these topics by looking through the main [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/) page. And see if it makes sense to create content around any featured topics shown there.

For example, you might see that a certain celebrity is trending:

If your publication covers entertainment, an article discussing what’s going on with that celebrity could be a good way to try to attract some of the current interest in the topic.

Then, incorporate the trending term into your piece. And use the term as part of a compelling headline that makes users want to click on your article to read more.

Here are some tips for creating good headlines for news and other timely articles:

- Announce the topic of the article clearly and concisely
- Avoid puns and jokes for more serious news topics
- Use action words
- Write in active voice (e.g., “Veronika Slowikowska Joins SNL Cast” vs. “SNL Cast is Joined by Veronika Slowikowska”)

### 3. Create High-Quality Content That Demonstrates E-E-A-T

You can improve site visibility in Google News by creating content that demonstrates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness ([E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eat-and-ymyl-new-google-search-guidelines-acronyms-of-quality-content/))—a framework Google’s human reviewers use to assess the quality of and provide feedback on search results.

Google discusses the concept in its [Search Quality Rater Guidelines](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf) and makes it clear that the intent behind E-E-A-T is to ensure search results meet high standards. And stories that cover important topics with the potential to impact people’s safety and well-being are held to even higher standards.

Here’s a breakdown of E-E-A-T principles:

- **Experience**: Refers to the content creator’s first-hand experience with the subject matter
- **Expertise**: Refers to the content creator’s qualifications and knowledge of the subject matter
- **Authoritativeness**: Refers to the creator’s or website’s reputation as a credible source
- **Trustworthiness**: Refers to the extent to which the website is transparent, uses credible sources, etc.

There are a few ways to focus on E-E-A-T.

First, add detailed author profiles to your articles. This can include a photo of the writer/journalist, a descriptive bio detailing experience and credentials, and links to social media profiles.

![An author bio on Semrush which includes a photo, a descriptive bio detailing their experience, and a link to their LinkedIn profile.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8f/f8/8ff806318a55a23226081a790c131598/4cc84fe22cd37bc537e517fb3ae24b6d/image.png)

Second, create an “About Us” page.

This page should outline your site’s mission, editorial policies, overall team credentials, and commitment to journalistic integrity.

Include details about your fact-checking process, sources of funding, and any affiliations to demonstrate transparency and build trust.

It’s also wise to publish a clear corrections policy.

And prominently display corrections or updates to demonstrate transparency and accountability.

![The "Corrections" page on New York Times displaying corrections and updates on articles published on their site.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/31/a7/31a77cf932575c56915667de6a3bcc10/3c55b4e479c01aa8790c234ca6a2cb7e/image.png)

### 5. Improve Your Site’s Technical SEO

Optimizing your site for technical SEO ensures that Google is able to effectively crawl, index, and display your content—which makes you eligible to appear in search results.

One way news sites can optimize their technical SEO is using news-specific structured data (often called [schema markup](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-schema-beginner-s-guide-to-structured-data/))—HTML added to pages that helps Google understand your content.

The most popular structured data types for Google News are “Article” and “NewsArticle.” They provide fields for you to add more information about your article, such as:

- **datePublished**: The date and time when the article was first published
- **dateModified**: The date and time when the article was last updated
- **headline**: The title of the article
- **image**: The URL of an image from or related to the article
- **isAccessibleForFree**: A true or false flag showing whether the article can be accessed without paying
- **author**: The full name of the author of the article

Here’s a [Google example](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) of what “NewsArticle” structured data looks like:

![An example of structured data for a "NewsArticle" by Google.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/30/0f/300f64489a04079bdef61fca49d9a202/8d3a41dd0dcb196bdcf5f07dc91e718e/image.png)

Create news article schema markup using [Google’s Structured Data Markup Helper](https://www.google.com/webmasters/markup-helper/). Select the “**Articles**” type and generate your code.

Once you’ve created your structured data, add it to the <head> section of your page.

Then, verify that you provide a good [page experience](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience)—something Google notes as important—by making sure your site has the following characteristics:

- **Fast page speeds**: Make sure your page content loads quickly. One way to do this is by compressing images and converting them to modern image formats (like WebP) with tools like [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app/).
- **Optimized for mobile**: Use a responsive website design that adjusts to different screen sizes
- **Minimal intrusive ads**: In particular, avoid pop-up ads that stop users from easily viewing your content
