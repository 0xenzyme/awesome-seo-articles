# How do you optimize SEO for Google's AI Overview?

You optimize SEO for Google's [AI Overview](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) by creating comprehensive, well-structured content that directly answers user questions with clear, scannable formatting and demonstrates expertise in your topic.

Google's AI Overview pulls from pages that already rank well and provide the most helpful, authoritative answers. Your goal is to make your content the obvious choice for AI to reference and cite.

Here's how to optimize for AI Overview:

- **Start with a direct answer.** Place your main answer in the first 1-3 sentences so AI can quickly extract and reference your key point
- **Cover related subtopics and FAQs.** Include common follow-up questions and related topics on the same page to demonstrate comprehensive coverage of the subject
- **Use** [**structured data markup**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/schema-markup/)**.** Implement FAQ, HowTo, and Article schema to help AI understand your content structure and extract relevant information
- **Build strong** [**E-E-A-T**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/) **signals.** Add author credentials, cite reputable sources, and update content regularly to show Google your content is trustworthy and current
- **Write in natural language.** Use conversational phrasing and [long-tail keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-long-tail-keywords/) that match how people actually ask questions, rather than stuffing in awkward keyword variations
- **Include original assets.** Add your own data, screenshots, charts, or tools that provide unique value AI can reference but users need to visit your site to access
- **Create** [**topical clusters**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/)**.** Build content hubs around related topics rather than isolated single-keyword pages to establish topical authority

For example, instead of writing separate pages for "email marketing tips," "email marketing best practices," and "email marketing strategy," create one comprehensive guide covering all aspects with clear sections AI can easily reference.
