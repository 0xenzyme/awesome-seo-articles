# What are some key features to look for in LLM brand mention monitoring software?

The key features to look for in LLM brand mention monitoring software include the ability to track across multiple AI platforms, sentiment analysis, and competitor analysis.

Choosing the right LLM monitoring software can really help you understand how AI systems represent your brand and identify opportunities to improve your visibility

Here are the key features to look for:

- **Cross-platform coverage**: Make sure the software tracks mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, Bing Copilot, and other major AI platforms
- **Sentiment analysis**: The tool should flag whether AI systems describe your brand positively, negatively, or neutrally
- **Competitor benchmarking**: Look for the ability to compare how often your brand is mentioned versus competitors in AI responses, so you can measure market share
- **Alerting and reporting:** Real-time alerts when your brand is misrepresented—or when a competitor suddenly gains visibility—let you act quickly
- **Actionable recommendations:** Monitoring is only useful if it leads to action. Prioritize tools that suggest concrete steps to improve visibility in AI platforms.
