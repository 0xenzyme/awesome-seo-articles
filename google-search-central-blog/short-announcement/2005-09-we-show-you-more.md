# We show you more

Just about a month ago, Google Sitemaps added new
[statistics](https://search.google.com/search-console) about
problems Google encountered crawling your pages. This stats page showed you up to three URLs we
had trouble accessing for each type of error. You asked for more. So we're giving you more.

Now, once you verify your site, we'll show you up to 10 URLs we've had trouble accessing for each
type of error, for a maximum total of 60 URLs.

Keep posting your suggestions to our
[Google Group](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community)
and we'll keep listening. Thanks for your participation during our beta period.
