# 7 Google Trends Alternatives for Deeper Insights

Google Trends is a useful tool for discovering trending topics, but most businesses can benefit from an option with more advanced features.

Consider the following Google Trends alternatives.

## 1. Semrush

Semrush offers two tools to identify and analyze trends in your industry.

The first is [Keyword Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/), which shows a keyword’s popularity trend based on the estimated number of searches that keyword gets per month (i.e., search volume).

Enter a keyword that’s relevant to your business ​​and choose your location.

You’ll then see the “Trend” section. Which shows you how popular a keyword has been over the past 12 months.

![The trend graph is a bar chart showing the keyword's popularity among other metrics like search volume, global search volume, and more.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1e/fe/1efe7f92391fc49ca815f0cb438f210e/1f859d2e2d9273d68b23c91cef1f757e/AD_4nXcCy2mJWnzQaUvpNmz8tBiVWubi6-oswaX5mpduGstIjJ6lgS0bM7R4OSuJ3ntwWqKmVH3TUucVZWGoIUBQ8qc1OH5277S9fQmi34iipoIwBiDa7fz2JtSliQeppTOx2nKG0qPz.png)

The second tool is [**Topic Research**](https://www.semrush.com/topic-research/), which identifies trending topics related to a starting seed keyword (a broad term related to your niche).

First, type a seed keyword into the tool, select a location using the “**Location**” drop-down. And click on “**Get content ideas.**”

Once the tool generates a report, switch the “Trending subtopics first” toggle on to see trending topics for your keyword.

![Topic cards are sorted by search volume and include headlines for each subtopic.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f8/05/f805e3714cb34c9764b1b5463525088c/d01d0c61a9d986ab67a74e3cfa02b641/AD_4nXfAoEpaHOoeBsFdxB0yqVanz9qqmTdBLBkFDxek0c2Mrx4grL759uVZ2fDfUcjqyXoEInAB8I7a3yXsnyO-X8tZYBArbWCgzN5-mq7JFmXFsi3082IfV0W6WDgZpKu4se3jX_IyAQ.png)

If none of the cards have the flame icon, then your keyword has no related trending topics at the moment.

![Topic cards are sorted by search volume and include headlines for each subtopic.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/17/1a1795d7c722ba71f5ab0a1f63f904a9/43bc6bed506e776683ce9a7fd0672253/AD_4nXc7NkhUMDQKWZGlUxs4H5CoioKqS0CuC7n5lky1aozOmzjr38hPZjoZQSVs-LHE_LR7p3QMsuOroVWH1zUmsrPzTZ5pHipeniuB5kg2DY10Yn5NnYlGpQjvGywqUy-ji7dvMaVl5Q.png)

### Pricing

Semrush offers a free seven-day trial you can use to test out the software. [Paid plans](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/) include:

- Pro ($139.95 per month)
- Guru ($249.95 per month)
- Business ($499.95 per month)

## 2. TrendFeed

[TrendFeed](https://www.semrush.com/app/trendfeed/) helps you discover trending topics across dozens of industries and can send you alerts about trending news stories relevant to your business.

Here’s how to use it:

Select your industry in the left-hand menu to see trending stories and news articles.

![Industries include art, beverages, auto, banking, education, energy, and more](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d1/3e/d13e4ff38b917e70781369a0593ab3b1/f04dfc9501b5ac9fcdeebbb97e99ea5f/AD_4nXeg0pB2X8Yrm9eETK5fE6whWIWvCgJNpCTdRmo0xFgAC_H8tmdFLmNsooSqIYTuzU1q3Zz8yzWHnSpgksICA4xDM_DLuQao3ByamdNDEE0lkvUgNoisNNlIuYI15GoQlS_sZTq9lQ.png)

Click on a title to read the featured news story.

Use “**Save**” to bookmark the story for later. Or “**Share**” to post it on social media.

![Trending content is labeled as elevated, high, or extreme.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/51/7d/517da323287685576ace733aa7260661/763350327e5e48c70c379127b3f0ca2d/AD_4nXc0EgXeo3ZIU1C4ZB9SMMws6iUfvYHFpNgaekfJkxHBEswudl6xJIlV3LhzfZjumvQNAK-0QKFhmoUKFbB0Gd1mRPL4QE2xUjgnBqYGvU2-6XFqGbxHEvhaaNEof754M8OF9AMmJA.png)

You can also look up a keyword related to your business to see trending topics containing that keyword.

![Search bar is highlighted above trending content.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d9/e6/d9e6c32f28f40bee039b175d6b812be7/52a904de5ac172b1ecf600ea93606462/AD_4nXe4GCtdBrFT9EOXFwSPfd7U_Ssh5Zi7YzhJRr6tNBUQnzhdbnzlBTKtF0c9XHPT52tiRZCant6mh6AlxQyK2Njs6DixlG7cr3g3YuLtK1mhsPiVNylhms2SJ5aF0k0PA5bTVu9NZA.png)

### Pricing

TrendFeed offers a free seven-day trial. After that, a paid plan costs $25 per month.

## 3. Exploding Topics

It’s one of the more popular Google Trends alternatives in recent years.

You can use it to review trending topics and interest graphs from the past 15 years.

![This Google Trends alternative shows trending topics over the past five years and includes topics like walking pads, lash clusters, and AI logo generator.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b6/33/b633814eb0b7021310418cc1e73a2afb/4c7415f1a723b54a95a70d714f7ca006/AD_4nXcdYmWq1Lt1nfyTJx2SV8HFtx4v3vcZtOYfJjhbIt7x-aVJTnAZoHHo_Pe2RI8-RS8_GhEeUyZiGG45aGNjUJ3IPp-QeYdQAyxPDuldFPLKRrNfNSfZXYON8jNTRw0vyzoUtPnKkA.png)

![Trending topic keywords include AI, beauty, design, eco, education, fashion, finance, fitness, and more.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/08/f2/08f2301f68f01374a591e8e22524a866/01b40a4e153a40ca8bfc4228b1dd644b/AD_4nXcLSze2gqHTLAKZPjb4Go7xmOn5aIVQB0eP7ZrBKpE6yN9vey1sbHcc_2kHVbWwRg7BhltoSP4V9X9530bwXCLLj0v2zWdj3wJPWLGypOBoSyiWD3_IDbnmCINEUIgNWjdXWqss.png)

You can also set up alerts to receive notifications about new trends before they take off.

And Pro users can forecast interest and demand up to one year into the future.

## 4. Muck Rack

[Muck Rack’s trends tool](https://muckrack.com/trends/) shows you trending topics on social media, TV networks, and news sites.

![Featured trends are shown for topics like AI, weather and climate, and streaming platforms.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/22/3c/223ca9acd7c9b24c4cd77997989b8f4d/7e2ddbc929b24e3734a36a76acf0a317/AD_4nXf3-uEsUCa07AF-umaTlwlZdr_505DWaRkU01i9__XX3eJcE3KmfB5uVjgmh65GMAe4knZDAP_x_Gz4P3jcUmJp1U6tpbRptlv-N69fwWfsSTCKuLmMX8n_DTf9Qz4QWBk7qLY-.png)

You can use it to compare the popularity of different categories and [keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/).

Once you type in a term, the tool will generate a report showing how many articles were published on the topic in the past 12 months. And the top authors and outlets writing about the topic.

![The report shows a line chart for articles published over time and lists top authors and outlets.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e6/82/e682d49559531795d0ca413058098363/eb0e68df5460693ed0e5b75246ddbf15/AD_4nXc6fHgkK3Fy1Ux03skV3nE7t9tYe0n26O5wJZwFZioCbrIEyYoujhgBtsPCzaioNfYasz31uQ47HzZlnSyi9kzIMgPsjhfPtwf3BHEVS1ltPASAb74psoosDwZcl3NeHPww-GC5cA.png)

The Treendly team also manually curates top trends for 10 countries.

![Rising trends include lab grown diamonds, hostinger, and pilates socks.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d4/3a/d43a1d6e2152f07a7ce67ab53ffa8b51/a126274b470de46dce1c65dd83256719/AD_4nXfNoeJmOp6ETw3D5G3kkqh65o5CRAtc-K13dmVtLmdOUFq2gUKaqzGHJLNtsUrG_PodztspHkj8kGqWMBbigaz0-HM75mPZWnkt49rAvIDls1USC_5G3OA1wfRMAUbsvl_upSMKAA.png)

The tool monitors and analyzes a large number of online sources to extract trends. And enriches the data with related topics.

![Related searches section shows top amazon queries and top youtube queries.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a2/5a/a25ae0fa74a635fffbadd3a0c9784a17/65a608dddc4e62b6893af4ccf94e52de/AD_4nXcIyc5JG3hYYTy1VxvBrjqUWmhslivEcAXS04O7Oxfk0KPE7kzXNCpx5_YHec4JeD4QUVcktciHuefcBY5Gs0xpKkYrgIAYnJE4Ls8MVtVHq-KPzkJLglVh-XoOdF2Kvi0QNeH10w.png)

## 6. Trend Hunter

[Trend Hunter](https://www.trendhunter.com/) combines crowdsourcing and AI-powered analysis to curate trending topics.

It collects and displays trends across dozens of categories users can filter by type.

![Trends in the AI category include AI-powered airport restaurants, nutrition tracking tools, journaling apps, and more.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a0/60/a060a64b1f4c39464c5c5ad0a1a04867/f546eafe19255e8221041a07bd9549ed/AD_4nXeW5ZV7gTQ-J9q_8OD_ZQDXEsJM0BWEZ165ClPefhPiBmNxe3meTAW-003Lh0tkBamBGRUaJR1m8hnvK-AwCldXIyLlivJ8SVLYX5rBmKemgJaSNHBvwLTAYSmG8AZfWkvzL8LDHg.png)

If you need more specific data, you can order custom trend reports with relevant ideas for your brand.

Or use Trend Hunter GPT (an AI generator tool) to uncover trends and ideas for your industry based on text prompts.

### Pricing

Trend Hunter costs $199 per user per month, billed annually.

## 7. Pinterest Trends

[Pinterest Trends](https://trends.pinterest.com/) is a free Google Trends alternative that shows you the top trends in the U.S.

It reveals keywords users are searching for across Pinterest. And allows you to filter trends by category, user age, and user gender.

![Pinterest Trends page displays keywords, a trend line graph, weekly change, monthly change, and yearly change.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a1/2d/a12d59039d0c84c8ddf3842eb75ec747/04aa4084edccabe6db9a06dcf3b5dfac/AD_4nXcKSd3UCBkCUxyjN2Tv6KMNWEor_vLsfXxxiVVq2c3HXO75cu6RWmZtHrvbXPN903IM0okBlvJxKxtGcBWDlL4VTyPChdM28QOaO_63LxbUohFsEZRZ1ixJYJuFuejABK407n33Fg.png)

You browse through trending keywords across 20+ categories, which makes it a good option for if you don’t have a specific term in mind.

### Pricing

You can use Pinterest Trends for free.
