## PR Tools for Outreach

PR tools for outreach help you research, build relationships with, and pitch journalists who may be interested in writing about your business.

The best PR tools for outreach include AI PR Toolkit, Muck Rack, PitchFriendly, and Anewstip. To get the most from your outreach tool, pair it with a PR monitoring tool that lets you evaluate the effectiveness of your outreach efforts.

### 1. AI PR Toolkit

- **Best for**: Enhancing media outreach with AI
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Base ($149/month) and Pro ($279/month)
- **Free trial period**: Seven days

With the AI PR Toolkit, I took just 15 minutes to identify and email journalists who report on my industry. Doing this manually would have taken at least one hour. I could also set up—**in the same platform**—a media monitoring query to track online mentions of keywords relevant to my outreach campaign.

Using the AI PR Toolkit was easy. After launching the tool, I started a new contact search.

I entered my ideal journalist profile—including their topics of interest and location—into the “Search with AI” chat feature and clicked “**Search**.”

The AI asked some questions to clarify the scope of my search. After I provided the details it needed, the AI generated a list of relevant journalists in three minutes.

![Search results listing journalists who cover pet technology, with profiles and relevance explanations.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/dc/3f/dc3fdf74cc9811407e35e9df2b72029d/8195d27a6d0a208d95151e80ff71a25c/image.png)

From there, I could view the journalists’ profiles and add them to a shortlist.

This discovery process took about three minutes.

## PR Tools for Press Release Distribution

PR tools for press release distribution help you share your press releases with media outlets to publicize the latest news on your business.

Doing this can get your business more press coverage. It can also lead to increased visibility in traditional and AI search, which you can track using [SEO monitoring tools](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-monitoring/) and [LLM monitoring tools](https://www.semrush.com/blog/llm-monitoring-tools/).

Many press release distribution tools are enterprise-level ones that don’t offer free access. So, instead of testing them personally, I evaluated them based on the information available in demo videos, marketing and support documentation, and content on third-party sites.

The best PR tools for press release distribution include GlobeNewswire, Cision, and Business Wire:

### 5. GlobeNewswire

- **Best for**: Distributing AI-enhanced press releases
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Custom
- **Free trial period**: No

[GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/) is a tool for distributing press releases to audiences in geographies and industries around the world.

I encountered a delay in securing a GlobeNewswire demo. So, I used the platform’s marketing site, help articles, and [promotional YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=72oHDj0Z0NA) to assess its press release distribution capabilities.

To distribute a press release in GlobeNewswire, you’ll draft your press release in the tool, providing multimedia content as relevant.

Preview your draft after that. If it looks good, choose the audiences you want to distribute the press release to. GlobeNewswire’s editorial team will review your press release before distributing it.

That said, GlobeNewswire isn’t the best option for businesses that prefer a self-serve onboarding process to save time.

The only way to get access to GlobeNewswire is by submitting an application. The platform’s representatives will onboard you after that.

#### What I Like

GlobeNewswire helps businesses produce polished press releases with the help of AI.

Clients can enter a summary of their story, plus supporting information, into the platform’s AI press release generator tool to get a draft for refining further.

### 6. Cision

- **Best for**: High-volume enterprise-level press release distribution
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Custom
- **Free trial period**: No

[Cision](https://www.cision.com/) is a suite of PR tools for reaching out to journalists and monitoring media mentions, and distributing press releases under its Cision PR Newswire service, among other features.

I didn’t hear back from Cision after requesting a PR Newswire demo. So, I used Cision’s marketing site and [YouTube demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KRHQu-PyijY), and information on third-party sites, to review the platform’s press release distribution features.

When distributing a press release with Cision, you’ll first create the press release in PR Newswire, providing information like a headline, summary, and pull-out quote.

![Cision press release form with fields for headline, summary, city, and release body.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/79/d7/79d7da8e06eab63b710df2395e292a25/7e56d41e156b65132c48cff45797f2b4/image.png)

**Image Source**: [Cision](https://youtu.be/KRHQu-PyijY?si=NdSjxmpknDTmvEgd&t=56)

Next, you’ll upload visual assets, like images, to accompany your press release. And enter your business’s press contact details and social media handles.

![Cision assets screen for uploading images, videos, attachments, and press contact details.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/13/ab/13abbbeaee84b78b13c8d568c3463c4e/1dbf5721db6529391a9045053346d89e/image.png)

You’ll then choose your press release’s distribution date and time, plus the journalists to send it to.

Finally, you’ll confirm your press release’s look and settings before submitting it. Cision’s editorial team will review the press release and distribute it if it’s approved.

Cision may be more suited for businesses that have larger PR budgets and frequently issue press releases.

The platform’s pricing can be expensive. For example, distributing a 400-word press release in the U.S. can cost around $1,000, based on reported user experiences.

Cision also charges an annual membership fee. You may not find this fee justified if your business issues press releases only occasionally.

#### What I Like

Cision can help businesses secure widespread international reach. It offers press release distribution to over 170 countries in over 40 languages.

### 7. Business Wire

- **Best for**: Ad hoc enterprise-level press release distribution
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Custom
- **Free trial period**: No

[Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/) is a platform specializing in global press release distribution and investor relations services.

Business Wire’s sales development representative declined to provide a demo or share pricing. So, I limited my review to the information I could find on the platform’s marketing site and help articles.

Distributing a press release with Business Wire involves writing your press release in its distribution tool, adding multimedia as relevant.

After that, you’ll choose your distribution lists. Available ones include specific cities and states, industries, and trades in the U.S. and globally.

Next, in-house editors review your press release and prepare it for distribution. Business Wire then delivers your press release with its patented NX news delivery platform.

Business Wire’s pricing may be lower than Cision’s. But businesses with smaller PR budgets may still find Business Wire’s pricing prohibitive.

The platform charges $475 and up for distributing a 400-word press release via U.S. domestic local distribution.

#### What I Like

With Business Wire, businesses can reach multilingual audiences with less effort. The platform can help clients get professional translations of press releases as needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions About PR Tools

### What is the best free PR tool?

PitchFriendly and Anewstip both offer free basic versions for media outreach. For monitoring, Pulse has a free tier focused on Reddit tracking. Most enterprise-grade tools like Muck Rack, Cision, and Business Wire don't offer free plans. You'll need to request custom pricing.

### How much do PR tools cost?

PR tool pricing varies widely. Outreach tools range from free (PitchFriendly basic) to $279/month (AI PR Toolkit Pro). Press release distribution through services like Cision or Business Wire can cost $475-$1,000+ per release, plus annual membership fees. Media monitoring tools typically run $25-$500/month depending on features and keyword limits.

### What's the difference between PR outreach tools and press release distribution tools?

Outreach tools help you find and pitch individual journalists; you're building relationships and sending personalized emails. Press release distribution tools blast your announcement to a wide network of media outlets simultaneously. Most businesses start with outreach tools and add distribution as they scale.

### Do I need all three types of PR tools?

Not necessarily. If you're just starting with PR, begin with an outreach tool to connect with journalists. Add a monitoring tool once you're running campaigns and need to track results. Press release distribution is typically a later-stage investment for businesses with established PR operations and larger budgets.

### Can AI PR tools replace a PR agency?

AI tools can handle much of the research, writing, and sending that agencies traditionally manage, especially for straightforward media outreach. However, they can't replace strategic counsel, crisis management expertise, or established journalist relationships. Many businesses use AI tools to bring routine PR work in-house while reserving agency partnerships for high-stakes campaigns.
