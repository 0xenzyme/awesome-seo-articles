# How should you do LLM optimization?

You should do LLM optimization by building strong brand presence across the web, creating [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/), and ensuring your content is accessible to AI systems.

LLM optimization requires a holistic approach that goes beyond your own website. Since LLMs pull information from diverse sources across the internet, your visibility depends on the online footprint of your brand.

Here’s how you should do LLM optimization :

- First, get your brand mentioned on reputable and commonly cited websites within your niche—platforms like Quora and Reddit are especially influential
- Next, establish a unique value proposition that clearly sets your business apart, and communicate it consistently across your website, social media, and relevant online communities
- Create original and useful content tailored to specific user intents, since LLMs are more likely to cite content that’s both unique and helpful.
- Make sure your content is technically accessible to LLMs by prioritizing server-side rendering, keeping it publicly available, and following [technical SEO best practices](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/).
- Include specific data points, statistics, and expert insights that LLMs can reference when users ask for authoritative information in your industry

After implementing these LLM optimization strategies, use [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/) to track your brand's performance in AI platforms and measure the effectiveness of your optimization efforts.
