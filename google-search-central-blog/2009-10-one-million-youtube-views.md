# One million YouTube views!

Earlier this year,
[we launched](/search/blog/2009/02/introducing-google-webmaster-central) our very own
[Webmaster Central channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral)
on YouTube. Just today, we saw our total video views exceed one million! On the road to this
milestone, we uploaded 154 videos, for a total of nearly 11 hours of webmaster-focused media.
These videos have brought you
[conference presentations](https://www.youtube.com/@GoogleSearchCentral#grid/user/09B09220BA526712),
[updates on tools for webmasters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2WaJ7goqX7A),
[general tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G959-2RlPyA),
and of course answers to your
["Grab bag" questions](https://www.youtube.com/@GoogleSearchCentral#grid/user/841CB8F9F31BF5D5)
for Matt Cutts.

[captions](https://www.youtube.com/t/captions_about)
to all of our videos and plan to do so for our future videos as well. Thank you to everyone who
has watched, shared, and commented on our videos. We look forward to the next million views!
