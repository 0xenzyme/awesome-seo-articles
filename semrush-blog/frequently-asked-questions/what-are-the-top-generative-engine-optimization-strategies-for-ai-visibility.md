# What are the top generative engine optimization strategies for AI visibility?

The top generative engine optimization strategies for AI visibility focus on creating [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/), structuring the content properly, and building presence across the web.

AI systems pull from diverse sources to form their responses, so your visibility also depends on comprehensive brand building.

Here are some top GEO strategies to consider:

- **Consistently publish content around topics closely tied to your brand**: Regularly create high-quality, relevant content that aligns with your brand’s expertise and interests. This increases the chances of your content being referenced by AI systems.
- **Make your content easy to access and understand**: Ensure your content is well-structured, clear, and accessible to both users and AI crawlers. Make sure to use server-side rendering so AI crawlers can easily process your pages.
- **Earn credible mentions across the web**: Build your brand’s presence by getting mentioned on authoritative sites, including user-generated content platforms like Reddit, YouTube, and Facebook. Unlinked brand mentions can also boost your visibility in AI-generated answers.
- **Include quotes and statistics in your content**: Pages with quotes and statistics tend to perform better in AI responses, as these elements are often favored by generative engines.
- **Keep your content fresh and up-to-date**: AI tools prioritize current information, so regularly update your content to maintain relevance.
- **Consider having a Wikipedia entry for your brand**: Since Wikipedia is a major source for AI training data, an accurate Wikipedia page can increase your chances of being referenced.

Once you’ve implemented these strategies, the [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/semrush-ai-toolkit/) can help you track how often your brand appears in AI responses so you can measure your GEO success.
