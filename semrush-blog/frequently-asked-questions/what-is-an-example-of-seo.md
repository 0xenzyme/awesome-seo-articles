# What is an example of SEO?

An example of SEO is adding a specific keyword you want to rank for to a blog post. For instance, if “healthy dinner” is your chosen keyword, you would include it in the post’s title, slug, meta description, headings, and naturally throughout the content.

SEO connects people searching for something with the most relevant and helpful content. By placing keywords in key spots and matching your content to what users need, you show search engines that your page covers a specific topic. This helps them display your page to the right audience.

SEO is free, which means you don’t pay for ads. Instead, you’re optimizing your website to improve its visibility in organic search results. Common SEO techniques include using keywords strategically, creating helpful content, building relevant backlinks, and providing a great user experience (like fast loading times and a mobile-friendly design). These steps can help your site rank higher, reach more people, and potentially attract more customers over time.
