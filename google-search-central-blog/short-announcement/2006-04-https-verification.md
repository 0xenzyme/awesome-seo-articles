# https verification

Some of you have had trouble
[verifying ownership](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080)
of https sites (for instance, `https://www.example.com/`). We are working on this issue
and will have it resolved as quickly as possible. We'll let you know as soon as you are able to
verify https sites.
