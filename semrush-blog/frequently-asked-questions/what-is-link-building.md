# What is link building?

Link building is the process of getting other websites to link to your website. Each link indicates to search engines that your content is valuable and relevant. When done well, link building can improve your search rankings, increase online visibility, and attract more visitors.

Link building typically involves creating high-quality content, reaching out to site owners, and forming partnerships that benefit all parties. Low-quality or spammy links can waste resources and even harm your rankings, as search engines can detect manipulative tactics.

Because it strongly influences a website’s authority, rankings, and traffic, link building is a key component of SEO. With careful planning and consistent effort, you can build a strong network of links that helps your website stand out in search results.
