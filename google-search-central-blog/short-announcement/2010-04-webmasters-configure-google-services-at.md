# Webmasters - configure Google services at your hosting panel

Today, as
[announced](https://googleblog.blogspot.com/2010/04/google-services-now-available-at-your.html)
on the Official Google Blog, we've taken an additional step to improve access to Google webmaster
services.
[Parallels](https://www.parallels.com/), one of the leading
providers of control panel software to hosting companies, has integrated
[Google Services for Websites](https://www.google.com/webmasters/provider/)
into
[Parallels Plesk Panel](https://www.parallels.com/products/plesk/),
used by millions of website owners globally to manage their websites.

If you use Plesk for managing your hosting and website services, you can easily configure
Webmaster Tools, Custom Search, Site Search, Web Elements and AdSense for your website right from
within Plesk.

Since Plesk knows what domains you own, it automatically registers your domains to Webmaster Tools
and allows you to automatically login to the Webmaster Tools console to verify your sites, as
shown below.

![webmaster tools verification feature](/static/search/blog/images/import/c59c58a6f3e5dec9c7190d4db1324ffa.png)

We're always trying to make our tools easier to use and easier to access. Since you're probably
visiting your hosting control panel on a regular basis, we hope that you find this integration
convenient. If you have feedback please let us know in the
[Webmaster Forum](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community).
