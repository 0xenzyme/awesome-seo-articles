# 31 Top Social Media Platforms [+ Marketing Tips]

Social media platforms are websites and/or apps that let you share content (like text, images, and videos) and interact with other users.

As a marketer, you can use social media platforms to increase brand awareness, engage target audiences, and gather valuable competitor insights.

### 1. Facebook

**Leading markets\***: United States, India, and Vietnam
**Platform type**: Social networking
**Top demographic\***: Female, 25-34
**Best for**: Engaging with local communities
**Used by**: Coca-Cola, Samsung, McDonald’s

![facebook profile has an about section and recent posts](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e0/9f/e09fa914dfa4e192a2567d31294a4340/9d748332831139a4ff6289e30954675d/AD_4nXeD3Foxmkfcu40glOH5u-Xp7z18iBgt7A7x2nI4vILVHAjX9TpMrClaztGOb7h94xqfWo5IoZzH0iUxN-ziTt582JARgFDqG5UzBYx8wNpcC7dCsPv1OiASt_WhGoNg_aQ6EBHG.png)

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/) allows you to create a page for your business. Where you can post text, images, videos, events, and more.

It’s one of the best social media platforms for reaching local communities. And for reaching older adults in the U.S., who [use Facebook more](https://www.aarp.org/content/dam/aarp/research/topics/technology/internet-media-devices/2025-technology-trends-older-adults.doi.10.26419-2fres.00891.001.pdf) than other platforms.

To get started, sign up through [Meta Business Manager](https://www.semrush.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-facebook-business-manager/). Then:

- **Customize your page’s URL**. When your page is created, it will get a randomly generated URL (like “facebook.com/pages/yourbusiness/1838227”)—visit the [Meta Accounts Center](https://accountscenter.facebook.com/profiles) to create a “vanity” URL (like “facebook.com/YourBusinessName”).
- **Optimize your about section**. When editing your business page, include a description of your business, a link to your site, your hours, your phone number, etc.
- **Include an eye-catching cover photo**. Upload a cover photo that’s 851 x 315 pixels and best represents your business to appear at the top of your page.
- **Add a call to action (CTA)**. You can add a CTA (such as “Sign Up” or “Shop Now”) that will appear under the bottom right of your cover photo.

For help improving and executing your Facebook strategy, use Semrush’s [Social Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/social-media/). It includes a suite of interconnected tools to help you streamline and automate your Facebook marketing efforts—from identifying topic ideas to creating high-quality posts to tracking performance and beyond.

### 2. YouTube

**Leading markets\***: United States, South Korea, and India
**Platform type**: Long-form and short-form video sharing
**Top demographic\***: Male, 25-34
**Best for**: Distributing educational, entertaining, or promotional long-form video content
**Used by**: Red Bull, Apple, Salesforce

![youtube channel has bio and channel overview video](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a7/01/a70115e6e81e44c388922e5fb23507f1/91868f4143aa55e41a9d1434f5d17d93/AD_4nXf02T1kOMjchxcK3gFsJVEpp1b17BVDbjPnlPS8vzvPcJnS57m-gyAgEnxo42YpdwI-NAJ0BwjIUs5KB0LsSGk7CnUD5ncOkcwthBzsze6ii5uF5-Ou1xdQ43hQbsxxfdK1p3nY.png)

[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) is a video sharing platform that allows users to search for content and subscribe to their favorite channels.

Create your YouTube business channel to get started. Then:

- Create a channel trailer that encourages target users to subscribe
- Perform [YouTube keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/youtube-keyword-research/) to see what topics your target audience searches for
- Use [YouTube SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/youtube-seo/) tactics to help your videos rank in YouTube and Google results
- Interact with users via comments and likes
- Organize your videos into themed playlists
- Share your YouTube videos on your website and other social accounts
- Consider [advertising on YouTube](https://www.semrush.com/blog/youtube-ad-targeting/)

You can monitor views, subscribers, and other key metrics with the Social Tracker (part of the [Social Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/social-media/)). And use insights to guide your future strategy

### 3. Instagram

**Leading markets\***: United States, India, and Brazil
**Platform type**: Photo and short-form video sharing
**Top demographic\***: Female, 25-34
**Best for**: Lifestyle, fashion, and visually driven brands targeting younger audiences
**Used by**: Nike, National Geographic, Fenty Beauty

![instagram profile with stories and posts](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c4/a0/c4a0a0d760486ef067a8f39d5eb0c118/f0f23c86f44eaa341174804a3e1110d2/AD_4nXc4uhw40oDaB3TheDoFdoIawuBTQDKhzJmkoISyTrYm3_769sjfAvf3_Hu8xvMWSQriz5WmPHREBuZAsRE_RJG-bY0A81apquWyGE0ImNOawb1wvrf-guxler1850gWr27cODnV.png)

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/) is a social media platform where users share photos, carousels (multi-image stories), and reels (short videos).

The platform works well for sectors that rely heavily on visual appeal—like fashion, beauty, travel, food, and lifestyle. And Instagram is particularly good for reaching younger audiences.

Here are some tips for success:

- **Create a business profile**: This unlocks analytics, ads, and shopping features to help you understand your audience and sell products directly
- **Keep a consistent look**: A cohesive visual style makes your brand recognizable and attractive to followers
- **Be active**: Respond to comments, engage with other users’ content, and participate in relevant trends to increase your visibility
- **Monitor performance****metrics**: Instagram’s analytics help you track performance, understand what works, and refine your strategy

With the [Social Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/social-media/), you can schedule your Instagram posts, track important metrics, monitor your competitors, and more

**Platform type**: Discussion forum
**Top demographic\***: Male, 25-34
**Best for**: Brands and thought leaders looking to build or engage with niche communities
**Used by**: Nintendo of America, Audible, Cloudflare

![subreddit for work from home has multiple threads based on user questions](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ef/e3/efe3137c2076f65b6c2e665a248752e8/313231bf093809bfcec1c64b2887b716/AD_4nXf2Cpkn-sHpL7AXSuo-klu6vL7PkoDeF0kZSlTKB95ms5DzYSTvzvLKKUGxNUIECnaDaWJp_CNtKu_Kbn6DrE4NMS-B5kunKF52vh4AO7Y02wdAZmIMs3rNBrVfzdTNjCb6Gsyi.png)

[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/) is a social media platform where users create and join communities (known as subreddits) centered on specific topics. For example, there are subreddits dedicated to wedding planning and ethical fashion.

Here are some tips for [marketing on Reddit](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddit-guide/):

- **Participate in relevant subreddits**: Engage with communities related to your industry to build credibility and connect with potential customers
- **Create a brand subreddit**: If there’s enough demand, consider creating a dedicated community for your customers
- **Use Reddit Ads**: Leverage Reddit’s advertising options to target specific subreddits and demographics to increase your posts’ visibility
