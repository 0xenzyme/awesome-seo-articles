# How is Google's AI Overview affecting SEO blogging?

Google's [AI Overview](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) is affecting SEO blogging by reducing clicks to generic content through instant, synthesized answers at the top of search results, while rewarding blogs with original research and unique insights.

When users search for basic questions like "what is content marketing" or "how to write a blog post," they often get their answer directly from the [AI Overview](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) without clicking through to your blog. This shift particularly hurts generic "what is," "how to," and listicles that can be easily summarized.

Here's how to adapt your blogging strategy:

- **Create citation-friendly content with clear takeaways**. Write posts with quotable statistics, original data, and specific insights that AI can reference and link back to your site
- **Target specific comparisons and post-purchase help.** Instead of broad topics like "best email tools," focus on specific scenarios like "email tools for Shopify stores under 1,000 subscribers"
- **Build authority through genuine forum participation.** Engage authentically in [Reddit](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddit-guide/) and niche communities where AI Overview often sources information, establishing yourself as a credible voice
- **Add original visuals and downloadable assets.** Include charts, screenshots, and resources that users need to visit your site to access, providing value AI can't replicate
- **Diversify beyond blog content.** Create video companions, newsletters, and other owned media channels to maintain direct audience relationships

The key is evolving from generic information provider to trusted source with unique insights that AI systems want to reference and users want to engage with directly.
