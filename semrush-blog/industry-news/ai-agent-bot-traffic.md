# Bot traffic now exceeds traffic from human users

For the first time, bots generate more web traffic than human users, and AI agents are driving the surge. Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince [announced on X](https://x.com/eastdakota/status/2062212701414187452) on June 3, 2026, that bots have passed human traffic online for the first time. Cloudflare Radar currently shows bots at about [57% of web page traffic](https://radar.cloudflare.com/traffic#bot-vs-human), with the rest from human users.

The measure covers bots versus human requests for web pages on Cloudflare’s network, not all internet activity, and Prince said the exact crossover date is hard to pin down.

![A post on X by Matthew Prince about how bots have passed human traffic online for the first time in the Internet's history.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/09/b0/09b0e70ad4d801a9e78a2fe3868013a5/7e32213917f045d2d2b568e14dda0957/image.png)

Prince had initially predicted that agentic bot traffic would surpass human traffic by the end of 2027. It happened 18 months early. The shift isn’t Cloudflare’s alone. [HUMAN Security’s 2026 State of AI Traffic report](https://www.humansecurity.com/learn/resources/2026-state-of-ai-traffic-cyberthreat-benchmarks/), based on more than 1 quadrillion interactions, found automated traffic grew roughly eight times faster than human traffic throughout 2025, with traffic from AI agents and agentic browsers up nearly 8,000%.

## Why marketers should pay attention

Target audiences now include AI agents acting on behalf of humans. Treating agentic visibility as "just SEO" means losing out on a growing audience.

Those agents don't generate pageviews, bounce rates, or the other metrics search marketers have traditionally relied on. Marketers will need to track new indicators (citation rate, share of voice in AI answers, and referral traffic from AI platforms) to understand whether their optimization efforts are translating into visibility and business outcomes.

This milestone is also a useful internal case: Marketers can point to it when making the case for further investment in AI search and agentic optimization.
