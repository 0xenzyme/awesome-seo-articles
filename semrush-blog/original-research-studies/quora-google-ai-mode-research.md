# We Analyzed 26K Quora URLs Cited in Google AI Mode: Here’s What Works

Google’s AI Mode is rewriting the rules of online visibility—and Quora has quickly become one of its top sources.

We analyzed 26,000 Quora URLs cited in AI Mode and spoke with Quora’s own team to uncover the patterns behind which threads get surfaced.

Our research reveals why Quora threads surface, what signals matter most, and how brands can increase their visibility in AI answers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Quora is one of the top 4 sources in AI Mode**: About 1 in 14 answers (7.25%) included a Quora link, making it one of the most-cited domains
- **AI Mode rewrites Quora content:** On average, there was less than 50% overlap between an AI Mode answer and the closest Quora reply. This shows that Google blends multiple inputs rather than quoting directly.
- **Topic alignment matters more than keywords**: Quora threads were often cited even when the original question didn’t exactly match the AI prompt—indicating Google prioritizes broader topical relevance.
- **Engagement is a strong signal**: Cited threads averaged 37 replies, with higher activity making them more likely to surface in AI results
- **Quality signals carry weight**: Nearly 90% of cited answers were labeled *Most Relevant* by Quora’s algorithm, suggesting Google relies on the platform’s own quality markers

## Methodology

To understand how Quora appears in [Google AI Mode](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-ai-mode/), we built a dataset of **278,279 unique prompts** and tracked the responses.

From these, we identified **26,000 unique Quora URLs** that were cited at least once by AI Mode.

We then measured the frequency of Quora citations and domain share. We also looked at text similarity between AI Mode answers and the closest Quora replies, along with thread engagement and relevance signals.

In addition, **we spoke with experts from Quora’s team** who shared practical tips on how to get prioritized by Quora’s internal algorithm—and increase your chances of being cited in Google’s AI Mode.

Here are our top findings.

## 1. Quora Is the 4th Most Cited Source by Google AI Mode

Google AI Mode included a Quora link in **7.25%** of responses. That’s **20,189** citations across our dataset of 278,279 responses.

Simply put, about **one out of every 14** answers contained a Quora link.

This makes Quora one of the top four most-cited domains, behind only LinkedIn, Reddit, and Google.

![Most cited sources in Google AI Mode](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1b/41/1b41a8ce0187339e6cfadff94295d8e7/516b3515b40de19b253b778353b9861e/EN-Quora-AI-Mode-Research-07.png)

Our [previous research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/) also found that Quora is the most cited source in [Google AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/).

### What This Means for You

Quora marketing is worth including in your [AI visibility](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-visibility/) strategy.

It also requires the same level of planning you’d dedicate to LinkedIn or SEO—with its own strategy and resources. That means:

- Building a branded presence and publishing answers consistently
- Researching Quora threads to spot customer pain points and find content ideas
- Actively engaging with discussions instead of dropping one-off posts

## 2. AI Mode Blends Ideas from Quora Responses in Its Answers

AI Mode does not copy text directly from Quora. Instead, it blends together elements from multiple Quora replies and restructures them into new text.

On average, Google AI Mode responses had an overlap score of just **0.435** with the closest Quora answer.

Meaning **less than 50%** similarity.

### What This Means for You

Make your Quora answers easy for AI to reuse:

- **Write complete answers that stand on their own**. Explain the “why,” outline steps, or compare options so your expertise has weight

- **Ground your points in specifics**. Use data, examples, or case studies—concrete details survive paraphrasing better than vague claims

- **Make your key message simple**. Phrase your point in clear, everyday language so it still comes through even when AI rephrases

- **Organize your response into 2-3 clear points**. Use short sections or bullets so each idea is easy for AI (and readers) to extract and blend

## 3. Topic Wins Over Keyword Match

AI Mode doesn’t surface answers because you repeated the exact words in the question. It looks for responses that address the intent of the query.

On average, the similarity between the user query and the Quora question title was just **0.1**.

This shows that AI Mode pulls Quora content based on topic alignment—not prompts.

![Similarity between Google AI Mode prompts and Quora questions](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5c/a1/5ca10ac2b24b36361128ff01142eb00a/ff4893c69cf1e148274749d58bcd5462/EN-Quora-AI-Mode-Research-03.png)

### What This Means for You

Stuffing keywords in your Quora answers or relying on exact phrasing won’t improve your chances of being cited.

You need to show you actually understand the subject:

- **Answer the question directly, then expand**. Add reasoning, evaluation criteria, and examples that give your answer depth

- **Cover related angles**. Touch on adjacent subtopics (alternatives, use cases, pitfalls), so your answer applies across variations of the same query

- **Write for intent, not phrasing**. Think about what the user is really trying to solve, and frame your answer around that problem

## 4. Engagement Matters: Surfaced Threads Are Active Discussions

Google AI Mode also favors Quora threads with many replies, upvotes, and longer, thoughtful answers. In other words, signals of collective validation.

The cited responses had, on average, **37** **answers** per thread, **15 upvotes**, **one** **comment**, and were at least **535 words** long.

![Engagement patterns on Quora answers that get cited](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5e/d8/5ed8783a386118d6b98fd5d78c7b22c3/fa0440c788241508761ffd45e8397509/EN-Quora-AI-Mode-Research-04.png)

### What This Means for You

If you want your answers to surface in AI responses, join active discussions.

Look for questions that already attract a steady flow of answers and engagement.

Then, contribute thoughtful, detailed responses and try to:

- **End your answers with a question** to invite more perspectives and spark ongoing discussion.
- **Engage with commenters** to keep the thread active and signal that the conversation is still relevant.
- **Link to related Quora answers** so readers can explore further and add their own replies.
- **Remember, more replies = stronger signal**. Threads with sustained activity are more likely to surface
- **Prioritize quality.** A thoughtful, in-depth reply attracts both engagement from users and credibility signals for AI Mode

## FAQs

### How Does Quora’s Algorithm Mark Answers as Relevant or Irrelevant?

Quora’s algorithm for picking relevant answers comes down to a few core things like: structuring your content (answering the question directly and making it easy to read), avoiding overly promotional content, and providing genuine value.

### Should I Include Links in My Answers on Quora?

Include links in your Quora answers, but sparingly. One or two well-placed links that add context or proof are fine. A long list of links might sometimes feel overwhelming and less trustworthy. The most important factor is making sure the links you include add value.

### Does Formatting My Quora Responses Increase the Chances of Being Cited by Google’s AI?

Structured, scannable answers make it easier for both readers and AI systems to extract the key points. Just remember: Formatting only helps if the content itself is strong.

### How Do I Find the Right Questions to Answer on Quora?

To find relevant topics, start with Quora’s search bar. Type in broad themes relevant to your industry, then drill down into related questions. Explore the “related questions” box on the right side of any thread.

### What if There Are No Questions on Quora About My Product Yet?

You can ask your own questions if you can’t find any relevant topics on Quora. Use Quora’s “limited” setting when creating a question if you don’t want it tied back to your profile. It will still be discoverable to others.

### Should CEOs or Executives Answer on Quora as Individuals?

Having your employees and executives write on Quora is generally a good idea. For example, a CEO sharing firsthand experience builds credibility and humanizes the brand. Remember: People trust people.

### How to Address Misinformation About My Brand or Biased Answers on Quora?

To address potential misinformation on Quora, engage and respond thoughtfully. Jump into comments and correct misunderstandings, provide your perspective, and back it up with data or sources. That way, you can control the narrative around your brand.
