# How to Conduct a Social Media Audit in 9 Simple Steps

Want to know which of your social media accounts contribute to business results (and which don’t)?

Conduct a social media audit.

This article explains what a social media audit is, shows how to audit your social accounts, and recommends useful social media tools.

Download our [free social media audit template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IzSWMssSXEoBpPSTNyjGl9QOoqHIyl0Z2r73PiLMIiE/template/preview) so you can follow along.

## What Is a Social Media Audit?

A social media audit is a structured review of your social media activities to understand what works, what needs improvement, and how to strengthen your online presence.

Social media audits are important because many organizations have multiple social accounts on different platforms.

For example, most brands have accounts on Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, Pinterest, and LinkedIn with consistent branding and levels of activity.

![Social media accounts from one brand on Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), and LinkedIn](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/25/2b/252b281e654fe8ea7d4524b9fee426fb/2a07e98b9e39c896fc22973ab913cc01/AD_4nXePiN3yuv_obegVaFhBFRLtNyhWio2oQUt0H_-NvHM4mZvDrw8JvnU3GaMyh7EudXFuaN1w66Udmaoj9A-y0nPIAPGIMFeFDJ3Z_xr60Bhs1rt4Uy_c25exP7P4Ku2RjV3BOoZpCw.jpeg)

However, with that many accounts, letting one slip through the cracks can be easy.

The same brand on Reddit, for instance, might have a logo with a different color or little to no social media activity.

![Lenovo Reddit account](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8f/ef/8fefc6ca31cd75fdc20e4608edac650f/ae81755a588bc942f4b0c2b917662527/AD_4nXcGwvdd70i8kDw_vpoT_9t15YJ8Teir6_wMZFEZXt2YMlgxBeKnTDVS1_VwNWyVEe-yp94F3Orx8ZdtxuZyaHDUil1JtrUxk3PZrLmFjR16DgE_mEdRV7x1_Nv-921LP-w4zxMnWg.png)

Regular audits help maintain awareness of the following:

- Which platforms have accounts that need updates
- Which platforms the target audience engages with the most
- Which content types the audience prefers
- Which accounts contribute the most to overall success

Conduct an audit at least once per quarter. This matters even more if you have profiles on major platforms.

## How to Do a Social Media Audit in 9 Simple Steps

![A visual of how to do a social media audit in 9 steps](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/75/9e/759e9046e91db79b3893e6117220c159/9d0c2bdee398b32b0afc5b11a14c64d3/AD_4nXf6fp1_bG305nM6AS8Iif0cgQT8dENIXMA978NSrFQcP--4iSPEcyonhRgUtKmhGz904l8cUfi9GZRiiLFLfgffr0T4P5jOH7mYOTWafREaEJ-iCkrINUtEU8p1ceQ5LcTm1G8kXw.png)

### 1. Make a Comprehensive List of Your Social Media Accounts

List all current social media accounts. This identifies which ones are active and which ones might need removal.

If you’re unsure about an account’s existence, search for your brand or product name on each platform.

Here are the platforms we recommend checking:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- X (formerly Twitter)
- TikTok
- Snapchat
- Pinterest
- LinkedIn
- Reddit
- Quora
- Tumblr

**Action:** Add each account to the “Profile URL” column on the [audit template’s](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IzSWMssSXEoBpPSTNyjGl9QOoqHIyl0Z2r73PiLMIiE/template/preview) “Summary” page.

![“Profile URL” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8f/ab/8fab43ed07c2799ab6881e620d25e2af/3046a989b0ecdd999753e5b45b52afa4/AD_4nXeMqzckX3CIBh-RGy5dN6JkQVEp5YhWiY2gFkcKg6wqtCNGURSiPl3x7LeXgfIlgFXJkOG7ymlh3z5KzCsHydn4vX7lZgda0rOBUxBbk-38KYY0Y7lcHFRmSxB2bLCTLEB1cGs19A.png)

If you find unknown or forgotten accounts, include them in the list.

### 2. Find Out Who Manages Each Account and Report Impostor Accounts

Determine which accounts are official, which are duplicates, and which might be impostors.

- A sole operator manages all accounts
- A larger team should note who handles each account

**Action:** Ask the social media team about who manages each account. **Record these names on the “Summary” page of the audit.**

![“Who owns the channel?” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b2/d3/b2d3a21cde9ba02da2f141a724e6e526/3695df275d6b18011a803ca1a75595f6/AD_4nXfB9gXaRR78R07FuUtFflLEXCuuROqWYdRf5r58rT053eX-47OMpzo_VX72YPSYXLiyM9NIX2Bema7H4qWqw0DaBN8epa2v6wcRsub7ChR_BzMzq5CRuXoUyIVevqn3xaa1K_mFYQ.png)

Delete duplicate accounts to avoid confusion. Report malicious accounts to the corresponding platform so they don’t harm your brand reputation.

### 3. Check Which Accounts Are Verified

Verification on social media means the platform confirms an account’s authenticity.

This helps prevent fraudulent or imposter pages, preserving the legitimacy of official profiles.

Verification often appears as a check mark on the profile.

For example, this is what the verified icon (blue check mark) looks like on Instagram:

![Pas Normal Studios Instagram account (verified)](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/68/f7/68f7950407da4af90dc2da5f964a3964/bfd6625f7e02587d428d98613d943dcd/AD_4nXdTfYvx1_mnzgjF5-TZ0LOSfC6sKT_FI9XKuekMk2HZi2GUs6Rx48fzbPchPVaQr3uZ5SuQI4WM9QnQMpCHcCHTNziwpa699cbpKIWe14jyIJ7W1dD2dc_xZJlt5xWOtduyTTCrUA.png)

**Action:** Review each social account and note which ones are verified. Add these details to the audit sheet.

![“Verified?” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/60/70/607057b10f153be6b7fb9bfa7a940c40/d934734f0dd18b0cd34dee636d326a2c/AD_4nXe-_TvMn7yVfGWnnQ_Yb0S5glba5NhWhGbh0-aHY6vP4W-1hEU_Jdn4N_qCmi9ARxSayilNlkDoFthSlDIMYzGtX0zkz472PpkmA0zmtfbb-VZpjY_vePoiK3IZ2wIH-8M0Ugy8EA.png)

If you have unverified accounts, plan to verify them once the audit is complete.

### 4. Revisit Your Social Media Goals

Social media goals should align with wider objectives and marketing strategy.

This alignment ensures all marketing channels contribute to organizational aims.

For example, if the objective is to “increase sales by a factor of ten this quarter,” a matching social media goal could be to “get 100 link-in-bio clicks weekly.”

**Action**: Add each goal to the “Goal” column on the audit’s “Summary” page.

If you need guidance creating goals, apply the SMART framework (Specific, Measurable, Actionable, Relevant, Time-bound).

![SMART stands for specific, measurable, actionable, relevant, and time-bound. This infographic shows the definition of each of those elements, as well.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/98/26/9826f5daea3660ad0d4ef7dcfaa896da/358cb70be6534cc6ab12a30432482cd5/AD_4nXdeGWbanaPSBdA3i1wB3aKlBSbK3F8gVpNR1gbSA09M2oaI-k_WrT0kiK1lrnyNgKyR9rQUBPNfsw9HcFFm6p7fUzOuw5L4RYE8ziZEW6OfW9Fekf6jgmkKEPDiMGN38cgybK-pCQ.png)

**Action:** After setting goals, record them in the social media audit sheet.

### 5. Check that Your Branding and Messaging Are Consistent

Consistent branding shapes a clear identity linked to business culture and mission.

This encourages familiarity, builds emotional connections, and fosters long-term customer relationships.

Here are the key elements of your profile that you should check for consistency:

- **Profile picture**: This should visually represent the business or individual. Many organizations use logos; personal brands often use personal photos.
- **Cover or banner Image**: Offers another space to highlight brand identity, usually cohesive with the profile picture
- **Bio or description**: Conveys essential information in the [brand’s voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-define-your-tone-of-voice/). Explains who you are, what you do, and how you help.
- **Username or handle**: Includes the brand name for easy recognition and searchability
- **Link in bio**: A clickable URL that appears on certain social media accounts within the “bio,” as the name suggests

![HERSHEY'S Twitter (X) profile](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/eb/f3/ebf39491cc5352121b2b3a07b8c0135c/d86795c01756ce8a647b8b496f89ca47/AD_4nXdOeoaR29tD7tdDQvlR8wL73tH41hYmxi5cJSdqerdjGjElqqhSv7Ad6cDDmnSNcUM5lCyT9IuR1LZSpATMTmjUfowkihpbDNRw-0SDSTslCwkyK0vSfrxnpDUeMGfd3zw-dciHdQ.png)

**Action:** Confirm each element matches across every social account. Record these details in the audit sheet.

!["Brand" section of the social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9d/04/9d04f51d9e093c711f105171cd40350c/c37a4e25b45c6b88cf5c4dac2a1a88a1/AD_4nXdk9_Fk3QrrjdfJRlMI6xfc46t9t-Ao9M6XdaRJmbn54jPfQ-IaprIIwUzLM-oNHt8j7mQ76stzxEovq2rHZRoaeg-lEXlYiBxu5PourNoQS2EGv0A0hkr1G6Vpc_zKWWdOogOu.png)

If a profile picture or link differs on any account, note that discrepancy and update it later.

### 6. Keep Your Branded Hashtags Consistent

Branded hashtags organize content and build a recognizable identity. They also make finding [user-generated content (UGC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/user-generated-content/) easier.

For example, major retailers sometimes create hashtags using their brand names.

This unique but recognizable tag can encourage users to share social posts featuring the brand’s products.

The brand can then search for that hashtag on social media to find UGC and repurpose that content on their own accounts:

![Calvin Klein’s Instagram post using #MyCalvins hashtag](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a2/52/a2526e568cfc5cba4e146ebe73744c4d/16c2f54548de2bc62d01a06377fd1b52/AD_4nXctmIapFz8HwL9V9ymBEa7YjXzURLVGeahzxf9sLMHnV7gU-CgQ6TgiKBHF-uBV0_lXjnOEFn7sPQFgDW02zStMBhTFcv9rQxn1iZ1oorMXlEqWXxrCDtMqzBLjC5OsIFkr58E0.jpeg)

Source: [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CpVEPzVPral/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igshid=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==)

**Action**: Review branded hashtags on all accounts. Update or refine them if needed. List them in the audit template.

### 7. Analyze Channel Performance

Review social media analytics to identify successful strategies and improvement areas.

Focus on [social media KPIs](https://www.semrush.com/blog/tracking-social-media-kpis/) to learn:

- Which channels and content meet goals
- Which posts drive engagement
- Which campaigns work
- How audience targeting evolves

Track the following social media KPIs:

#### Impressions

The social media impressions metric measures how many times users see a post.

For example, if 50,000 followers each view your content twice, that equals 100,000 impressions.

Find this number within [Social Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/social-media/?tool=analytics).

!["Impressions" section in Social Analytics Tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/74/f6/74f6b81af206678cb1676b78d14bbcd4/3ee223ec66ab5e48c76a8b7c29b892cc/AD_4nXeTaLb76s7PszGpTFjW_hv2WkVTrUCBBR3n4JqFaQzSsEuKElYbrtSeogdrDRTs9e-9Nf25Ybmp1qMjc_N4x38ceZTnkmBTDmiFN3QYKgf7nN6lL7zql2VwDdyq4f6g3CqUUI7wLw.png)

You can also find social media impressions on a social media platform’s analytics page.

For example, YouTube Studio’s Analytics tab contains impressions data:

![YouTube Studio’s Analytics "Content" tab](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a8/f4/a8f41abd3e2d4dd17ba4b3de352343f6/d59824e81f1bcb193975ee7907a13229/AD_4nXcQrTvRyjcRN3ZK99NjOEM7UTA1bKbyotmWL3XIR2iUBgFusIbw08BZgWW6LLcMsVX8ksIndRva2jIruavkfXjPk5-ph6v7Tre9yyouIrnJTIFSbhxE6_5C48mILINhnAEuSIK6kw.png)

**Action:** Add impression counts across all owned platforms to the “Profile Performance” section.

![“Impressions” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/00/7a/007a7ee1bc5495871eeef5cbc4fd3fe2/0172936cf2349395b49e2971ce40dd6d/AD_4nXfZ4hBBbQke_UbfbXx5dohsgW_OOHu9Wr2H00uB8uGCb0UF9kNop7gNRP9sKdTCR6C4jBqsIkWHEPIG0pChIMG8vfnIJSbLxVtWPYouh79WqBBYieA4u8KUBnrBeSGs7F97KdnxPw.png)

##### If You Have Few Impressions:

- Use relevant hashtags
- Develop engaging, repeatable content
- Schedule posts to go live at peak times

#### Reach

Social media reach measures unique views.

For example, a post seen by 33,000 unique users means a reach of 33,000.

Reach differs from impressions—one user can view a post multiple times, raising impressions but not reach.

![A visual defining "impressions" and "reach"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/11/71/117100bd49cd3d1edc7d92c5a23bd61a/d4ac72cdd6de49470cf6d3b6abbf4669/AD_4nXdW-O5eFRwmdhMR4jeIRnDPf-oQ6BZJ61-qMt-_l5Ri7s3xsIDFWCTcD6oNXDQUPp81i9VPZxLo1Q9yifiLl7mzkNdSXqsHL2IUFaQ5anN6BlTEqt0JVzdi0KqluyLeYvNqJBmJ.png)

**Action:** Add reach metrics to the “Profile Performance” section of your template.

![“Reach” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e7/79/e7793f5ccf0fa2a1890ffc5b02ed2cef/883fe5ac1a18dcf79f42bd857061673e/AD_4nXeyC_g8L2nkRWnqXdvWF8t1KbX3vzDXGRBrS7NvMVg638Q2SMbtGEc1btHLvualfqzUPzg_UsXkJYxQzj5-7nWculCQn7gX89o4AfAWVH-JLqZi8up22tvnNCwBC_VkDEqqbhby.png)

##### If Your Reach Is Low:

- Share high-quality visuals
- Confirm the platform fits the audience
- Create and publish evergreen content

#### Engagement Rate

Engagement rate gauges likes, comments, shares, or saves. It reveals if audiences connect with the content.

The formula for calculating engagement rate is (Total Engagements / Reach) x 100.

![Social media engagement rate formula](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e2/bd/e2bd62ffb98b4bc9e86cb88461d497cb/13c26048d4b868eeb8951647f2217ffb/AD_4nXf4cuf1-poHSrM_E-bQJeJd5HkuVa5qbXwioaLOuzz7OPa7rcSnwdAqKEPL_K650-ye7uDZKGbFGZDP2MkrEDAIcAtocKteVB6tuD9I3o4chF_dCfTZZTOPJ2TxikfzYKIJRxGr.png)

For example, if a post gets 600 engagements and reaches 15,000 people, the engagement rate is 4%.

Find your total engagements and reach for all social accounts in Semrush’s [Social Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/social-media/?tool=analytics).

You can also find engagement rates on a social media platform’s analytics dashboard.

For example, the analytics dashboard looks like this on Instagram:

![Instagram analytics "Overview" page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/43/07/43076e44b2154d23ec87fd3d1d32ad8c/05970079b7ff2d75867704ca56c8f5b3/AD_4nXdHCeCxy8_d1OXqJqm6qZ7tS5W5msl3mJ2C9dI28Z-bu1ArM3dL8xbNy84jZxqkbX--ldEgIdLT8fbtm1k_JWS_PGW4Z8k7IEBzvsM9SLkGbY7TZfYUwdQZ3WmojB22WZ4Lhe46mA.png)

**Action:** Add your engagement rate to the social audit sheet.

![“Engagement rate” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/40/b6/40b6783382e0382a336a64362c1547ff/80f352737ddb5d95a487ac25875b199c/AD_4nXduO_ZS5N_-A5yd7FvOO-ncm9HTsk6st4HFA3rUNOf_-H2u6yoxV7-8UVQuzNMtCnYgwaf9kZdc7fiqtXeI0RpLgQL-X66RSkUVlrxpzRYhabRVI29ip29Fnm5e-pFDdYOUCWQcNw.png)

Take note that engagement rates can vary by audience size and industry norms.

A larger social media following typically means a lower engagement rate. Additionally, average engagement rates vary across industries and platforms.

For example, a B2B audience on TikTok is less likely to engage than a B2B audience on LinkedIn.

##### If Your Engagement Rate Is Low:

- Align messaging with audience needs
- Explore audience preferences and pain points
- Respond to comments and ask questions

#### Followers

Follower count shows potential audience size and growth trends.

However, quality matters more than quantity. Irrelevant or fake followers reduce engagement.

Additionally, fake or bot followers are easy to obtain, further troubling the relevancy of this metric.

**Action:** Add your social media follower count across all owned platforms to the “Profile Performance” section.

![“Follower count” column of social media audit template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b8/07/b807ebdadbecc5f6dc381088d6809726/62acc7680ef3b6459f204c5eac6bf647/AD_4nXcbwb-TK5LLdTXUWYKPreSJ8pg8m-i_W-aVBzRcpQwETTCgDcFPk7g80D23DnLteYwD8iyi4Bc-LelOpKoWASSituEewUc5HziA-6OeZP4nJKR-E4k1AEzkA5jJBYp7k8kmHyf-WQ.png)

##### If Your Follower Count Is Low:

- Post compelling or entertaining content
- Maintain a clear brand identity
- Collaborate with suitable influencers

#### Referral Traffic

Referral traffic indicates the number of website visits from social media. It reveals which platforms attract visitors and which campaigns perform well when tracked with [URL parameters](https://www.semrush.com/blog/url-parameters/).

To find referral traffic data, go to Google Analytics.

Then, click on “**Reports**” > “**Acquisition**” > “**Traffic Acquisition**” to analyze your social referral traffic.

![Google Analytics “Traffic Acquisition” report](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2d/75/2d75e1a075e824a0ff6436ceb27dbfa6/fc76a9a937e8699b05dcee835b247d4c/AD_4nXfcjUzW_Z9YmKz06WYbYHsvQNnanUP5PCY9dYcIPsFX2TasYiUZnAK5AZDh8t0oocl37zQqdNxYNIrHSt95kmjrfK1Nvfe337bF4ffidKRaGnvnKLIGSURQOIY2fwIYqCajttmM.png)

**Action:** Add referral traffic data from all your owned accounts to the “Profile Performance” section.

##### If Your Referral Traffic Is Low:

- Include clear links and CTAs in your posts and profile
- Encourage social sharing
- Improve impressions and reach

### 9. Perform a SWOT Analysis and Establish New Objectives and Action Items

A [SWOT analysis](https://www.semrush.com/blog/swot-analysis-examples/) uncovers missed opportunities, points of improvement, and competitive insights.

This approach helps refine the social media strategy

**SWOT** stands for **strengths**, **weaknesses**, **opportunities**, and **threats**:

![How to do a SWOT analysis infographic with relevant questions for each quadrant of the analysis](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f3/f7/f3f77543b5462b51b35ada53120f87bb/177b089fde043515d347d761ae21aa4e/AD_4nXc9PkmssIUX3UtRxCxVWvLh4VAOraVTDPyGXJY5CWdv_m3wzAuYx2oOOCrwuJUop12HblIl9Wg5asySXfO4hn0GSdinaiXvFBeOWSUvR6oC95yr5miHDnCSulI20h3nZyr_HTQR8A.png)

And here’s how you perform a SWOT analysis:

- **Strengths**: Identify high-performing areas. Examples include video content that boosts CTR or a campaign that increases signups by a measurable amount.
- **Weaknesses**: Pinpoint aspects needing improvement, such as delayed comment responses or an outdated social calendar
- **Opportunities**: Consider external factors that could spark new growth, like a newly launched social media platform. Explore competitor strategies with [Social Tracker](https://www.semrush.com/social-media/?tool=tracker) from Semrush Social to find techniques and channels worth adopting.
- **Threats**: Watch for external issues that could hinder progress. A new algorithm update or privacy regulation could disrupt your current plans. Competitors might also release an innovative product.

**Action:** Use the SWOT analysis tab to list out your findings. Then, make a list of action items. Add these to your social media audit.

## Social Media Audit: Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Definition of a Social Media Audit?

A social media audit is a structured review of your social media activities to reveal strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities.

Goals and metrics confirm that social media contributes to broader objectives.

### Is It Necessary to Conduct a Social Media Audit?

Yes, it’s necessary to conduct a social media audit at least once a year.

Social media audits uncover which platforms and posts resonate with audiences and drive results.

They also highlight areas for growth.

### When Should You Do a Social Media Audit?

You should do a social media audit quarterly to provide regular performance tracking.

Also do a social media audit before changing social strategies.

At a minimum, audit annually, but quarterly is often better.
