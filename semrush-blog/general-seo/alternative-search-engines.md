# 12 Alternative Search Engines to Try (Instead of Google)

If you’re looking for a search engine to use instead of Google, you’ve come to the right place.

By the end of this guide, you’ll know the best alternative search engines. And why each one might be a better fit.

Let’s get started.

## 1. Bing

[Bing](https://www.bing.com/) is a great alternative if you want a Google-like search experience enhanced with AI-enabled results powered by OpenAI.

Its standout feature is Copilot—a conversational AI assistant built directly into the Bing interface.

You’ll sometimes see Copilot responses directly in the search results, along with sources it referenced.

And you can go straight to Copilot answers from the homepage. Or from the Copilot tab at the top of results.

![Copilot logo is found next to the search bar.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/72/08/7208738467513ea326c2501d543b6dbc/a532767983557affdf318cf86a7dd2e7/AD_4nXePW0WUbp1xf7GanaQQJDPykL76zAeVxB02PN3S5Is0CLRhWW9w-P_BNf8HUyD3KAi0JGx31bVb8rkwnoQNWrb_15L1ftUZSor9rO2Drz12omAjrI03kXesdBC-ONyBlbaddQYf.png)

If you open a dedicated Copilot chat, you can click on follow-up prompts to explore the topic further.

![Bing's CoPilot response includes sources and follow-up prompts.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b9/26/b926ce90dc686d87ca6d444433df2be0/c817047e2aff4922e387d7ec3c2ace3b/AD_4nXeDAk9wsmQrYuK06emtU0fQO6Ozs7788QhZblpIaEM6ayIvmcvkpBtXmQ_AGDJzgnL3S7IPMTg1TtFWxCJ8Hq7pBKTvWQ5Du7zfR9QU3CAMM80DO7lQii4-sqIVG-7ihHOGS9oG.png)

And there’s a small bonus to using Bing: There’s a rewards program. Every query earns you points you can redeem for gift cards or donate to causes you care about.

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

I love Bing’s Copilot feature—it’s the main reason I use Bing.

When I tested Bing Copilot across 12 real queries (including “how to dispute a medical bill,” “best places to visit in california,” and “how to explain SEO to a beginner”) the follow-up prompts consistently matched what information I wanted to look into next.

Like these follow-up prompts for “how to explain seo to a beginner”:

![Copilot follow up prompts include give me a simple analogy to explain SEO and more.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3a/30/3a3083027090ad0ddd1774d39bd61434/ecf7fc8250e325879f24d7cb32b4f846/AD_4nXdXD9ygSoIGyKFzUcpdSQ4l9lfMyWCBzaz24wETcvZP5gqpCYaGIQ2UTSnh9JvYff_TZsiJ-5c2R6pBncH9ocwf_easP3cu1Olhhgps6vNOr5QWkfsXVtNw7j61Fm908Mog25fi.png)

It saved me time typing in additional queries and felt closer to a conversation than a search.

## 2. DuckDuckGo

[DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/) is a reliable alternate search engine that doesn’t track your searches, store your history, or personalize results based on your browsing history.

You can even switch your location manually to tailor to different locations.

![Search has label "Always protected," and the search location drop down is highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/de/bf/debfe76897385f35f1ad93f09c7b3677/01aa0ef957b282e2a27d35a9900fb992/AD_4nXd5zuY-fbXzQ1VtJ4K2iRWrYwWI6orlgQGqKIEtoxHCM07sGwE90DkQRC0JXl8YHNc4-7tIwaTFA1bax4u3qZl5mVIYxsc84eGfbkh1KtePUoL5JhgUDuJw4m3E2luJed_ckdNgAg.png)

DuckDuckGo pulls results from its own crawler (DuckDuckBot) and trusted sources like Bing and Wikipedia.

It also offers helpful features like bangs (“!”)—shortcuts that let users search other sites (Reddit, Wikipedia, Amazon, GitHub) directly from the DuckDuckGo search bar.

In 2024, DuckDuckGo launched Duck.ai—a privacy-first AI chatbot that gives you access to several AI models, including GPT-4o mini, Claude 3 Haiku, and Llama 3.3 70B. And it anonymizes every query and stores recent chats only on your device (not the cloud).

![Pick a chat model pop up shows GPT, Llama, Claude, and Mistral.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6c/8d/6c8d49053fccad56d1f7c60aa362593d/e4ea28ce8cbe81a40d8c4c24e6d3fb51/AD_4nXfSOCJUDemvidqfUL7vF7pMLtQ_PdbkX2r1CscYNls6mxVn_vUfcK60IAFXjnKjTVybrPLgk_t9haSB1xMGMgV7m-TTmfii5pe-hJIdqjxom-2uYsRUg2-Ms_VHHJR5bc5Vf3qeKA.png)

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

I like that Duck.ai lets me choose an AI model before starting a chat without needing to sign in.

For instance, I used Claude 3 Haiku to draft a newsletter privacy policy. Then, I opened a new session with GPT-4o mini to rewrite it in a more professional tone.

Also, bangs are wildly underrated.

I searched “!amazon noise cancelling headphones” and went straight to Amazon’s results page.

It’s an easy way to quickly go where I want to look.

## 3. Yahoo

[Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/) combines a traditional search engine with AI search and a full-featured content hub.

When you land on the homepage, it feels more like a personalized dashboard than a plain search bar.

You get a mix of trending news, stock tickers, weather updates, and entertainment stories—all automatically curated.

And Yahoo offers dedicated sections like:

- **Yahoo Finance**: For stock tracking, company news, and market insights
- **Yahoo Sports**: For live scores, fantasy leagues, and player updates
- **Yahoo Life**: For content on wellness, lifestyle, and parenting
- **Yahoo Mail**: For sending and receiving emails

![Yahoo categories menu shows subtopics for each.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/69/94/6994b827d961b585e4ad5792ef8809c2/10503ff3e82534a35015952e05cfdff9/AD_4nXfaq8ohgwDU2C6PMn0CEIc-Z9NVFSI1BwoDOY2rIAeHBPEp9jdmB6KpJhMpUKAidUxtqGy6Im1xJb_xxlFPrZ42XkLMw59LzfmBPAN-cB0Q9a9qOEuNukGZuXIyks7JLEVjO-he3A.png)

Yahoo also offers Scout, an AI-powered search assistant that generates conversational answers. It appears across Yahoo products including Mail, Search, News, Sports, Shopping, and Finance. You can also use it as a standalone tool at [yahoo.com/scout](http://yahoo.com/scout).

Search results are powered by Bing, so you get reliable answers for everyday queries.

You may occasionally see an AI-generated summary, but it’s not the focal point like it is for Google.

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

Yahoo stands out because of its homepage. It’s packed with curated content—news headlines, lifestyle stories, horoscopes, finance, weather, and even email access.

What I love is that it lets me search while I’m reading the news.

I was reading an article on Yahoo about taking vitamins when something caught my attention. Without leaving the page, I used the search bar at the top to look it up.

![Search bar is visible while reading an article.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/02/f3/02f398c098be6223212586e593db6a33/86e8a0c769e9e9f1b358c20769a4bc17/AD_4nXcYhSXlFDviocVT3F2gQ6wp6kIGGDHKz8tkq7arkxy48XAAX_vuZmOOsJUfULawRZWEsOcQRuJHfAHczYzIm0EIXQgti8mUYwEN0mcn2sVURgc1cBzaT_qs5lCrRQRbW7DMraQi.png)

I also like how Scout links directly to dozens of relevant sources in AI answers so I can easily verify facts.

And since the results are powered by Bing, the quality is solid. Plus, the way Yahoo lays out results makes them easy to scan.

## 4. Wayback Machine

The [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/) is unique in these search engine alternatives to Google in that its purpose is to let you see how different websites looked in the past.

It lets you access outdated or even deleted pages going all the way back to 1996.

## 5. ChatGPT Search

[ChatGPT search](https://chat.openai.com/) is OpenAI’s search experience built into ChatGPT, and it blends the AI chatbot’s functionality with real-time web results to help you find information quickly and easily.

ChatGPT will sometimes pull from the web automatically when it makes sense for the query. You can also choose to use the search function right away by clicking the “**Tools**” drop-down and selecting “**Search the web**.”

![Option is highlighted in the search bar that makes the chat an alternative search engine.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f7/ef/f7efdc514b271eb376a3ec1d9e236667/11201e917ca7cc607b6d558fe1a9a01b/AD_4nXd1HcR1JK0MLqx-BRew7E13c1lMchDwBqwFJXgD8b4d36oQQWGh3V1Vm8AW4aQmYjiugOFZExm8gUC3Q6LrKXA_ZZP4ZOb4GCFuvuWM1JOMzlUK1jiCJHcqY2fE8IHC5ab5zGZvVA.png)

Instead of showing traditional search results, ChatGPT search gives you AI-generated summaries with clickable sources to explore further. These sources are pulled in real time from across the web.

![Summarized response and list of citations.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9a/96/9a963b7b5ad551a99b4728111a69a625/2df2c819450bf9bed0fdc468454559f0/AD_4nXfSk65tHZAePf0IM8sa4Zn1mqbZ5WQ4H-xXLh2WMnyR8OlNMncyhm-qOyqPIGAd4N29Rf9BcqpgPrXGnyAdXwRb5wExQZIFKYm-PFevZFxUn0XLL0TWBTjlTunzj0DrDj-bdnGnVQ.png)

### Why I Think It's a Great Google Alternative

ChatGPT search often surfaces sources that don’t appear in Google’s top results—and that can be helpful.

ChatGPT gave me a genuinely useful answer when I searched “is there a difference between lean marketing and agile marketing?”

It included a side-by-side table and a clear summary. And even explained how both approaches can work together.

![Summary table explains key differences for both concepts.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/76/39/7639a3575fe3f2670892721ff637c7be/81abb0edd8163f9673d0176e5e42f852/AD_4nXcSL-OvICTiLVRH6AqsqOe-zV1LkhNlapClF4qUr8HNCO8bkbAyMEm_z1V64-1VlHP6l2IRdqTVBl8UDkQapthVm47oQxXDPuKVH2qaW6e8xpR0j9Hh2DpzsbiAc862QSovdrPBCw.png)

It cited multiple sources, including some Reddit threads I didn’t see on Google.

![Sources include Reddit threads.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f3/5d/f35d06025d108518f85557d25b50cd4a/5d07002af71b9f82b420eb11dac73573/AD_4nXf4IyXTnb03aKMRw3VSC6vFzYkilXtJqvKzBQ3PkwxRAGyubTRBWNgXUAyQOZvthwsuz73tTF6nGh_S9sLfryLLaP7GZMyRsm0KKxjn8Pw2sRXwGWUP_GsQiw2esLnPXxqSnPB7.png)

With ChatGPT, I didn’t need to rephrase my search or click through multiple tabs. The summary was detailed, and the sources were clearly listed to allow for fast fact-checking.

And although ChatGPT has begun placing paid ads into certain responses, the ads appear at the bottom and are clearly labelled. This keeps the focus on the response, rather than on paid placements.

## 6. Yandex

Yandex is one of the best alternate search engines for users who speak Cyrillic-based languages because it understands local context that Google and Bing can miss.

It holds [2.45%](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share) of the global search engine market share.

![Yandex search engine homepage allows you to search with a text input, or for images, video, maps, translate, weather, mail, and games.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/42/7b/427b07ba81441e30b4f7862d419d8827/360b0874112c7ff963abf04e1db226f2/AD_4nXe9M-74y7592JpUCwlZBEhoZ1me9g1fMTxOgQeK1vmy_FNTt9SJPjqCajjq0lY90kgwEMhd24xQ4aBuZf05KIdLDYOHFnpbN5HPB1a4iDGY4neMipi3Rviqq7ZZ0rMtPqxa1lKa5w.png)

Similar to Google, Yandex is an entire digital ecosystem.

You get Yandex Mail, Yandex Maps & Navigator, Yandex Translate, Yandex Taxi (a ride-hailing service), Yandex Market, Yandex Music, Yandex Eats, Yandex Disk (cloud storage), and even Alice—a Russian-language AI voice assistant.

You can use a single Yandex account to move between these services smoothly.

On the search side, Yandex gives you great filtering options that let you:

- Filter results by location to see content from a specific area
- Select a language, which is useful if you want Russian-only or English-only results
- Adjust date ranges to fetch results from different time periods

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

I love Yandex’s “Document language” filtering feature. You can set your preferred language right from the search results page.

You can do this in Google as well, but it takes a bit more work.

![Document language filter is applied.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7f/f7/7ff7e5f8e996e94d0e7ec81b5a6093cb/1affe1aa91306b1c5f263535d19e2a82/AD_4nXdsfeKa8VJ6-KiBi7-yPAScz4W8z74YE-BxM9e5w6mwvSpQg6qNV9ZcRSFUnBzInQ5f8wah7XEEXwdJQrTH1lS6SlEieq2AnRJpbOnamSpoEzl0jhpHySIfflD1o8jw1sWsZWlhbA.png)

This is especially helpful for language learners.

So, if you want to read search results in other languages to practice, Yandex makes that super easy.

## 7. Brave Search

[Brave Search](https://search.brave.com/) is a privacy-first search engine that uses its own crawler and index, which makes it one of the few true Google alternatives.

It’s part of the Brave ecosystem, which includes a cookieless browser, a built-in VPN, a crypto wallet, and an AI assistant called Leo.

![Brave search engine ecosystem landing page.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6b/ea/6beab29388b0aa1cd9462f807d69e21b/538f44f116ec449be6cc01fac96f35fa/AD_4nXdiAkNbopPNE7FmpZzX5OkJ7WYoW8knzbEC3-pO1vPOdCJcbwXiw1FCjbkLzU7z0RRpKAehs1FZ1glT37jPxJRRuNBZqZOTTaC9WweTC3tiabuQF-PIawHWUZEzg8kJ5r7fZjSzrA.png)

While Brave Search is free to use, it does show some search ads by default. If you prefer a completely ad-free experience, you can upgrade to the premium plan to remove them entirely.

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

One feature that really stands out to me is Goggles.

Goggles let you change how search results are ranked. I’ve used them to filter out big news outlets or to only see results from sources like Reddit and personal blogs.

When I’m looking for opinions or first-hand experience, I apply the Reddit Goggle. It cuts through all the affiliate-heavy listicles to show me real conversations.

![Goggles filter is applied with Reddit Search results.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fd/3a/fd3a92f43e0d46d99b1fa95b38307ba9/15615d27dc607a77040e0665b2c27253/AD_4nXdwlfODpzX3JNQbpfJD-HETbzWE4npWroWO5llMl6dhMGMHy256R5pm0ddp3BMCzdJk6USdImuaekFVkPQouiemk30ySjhVvcpLfPRoFYfiCPGTGsMrTTwrQr5j4UGajbDW_lGyOQ.png)

You can use pre-built Goggles or even create your own if you want more control.

## 8. Startpage

[Startpage](https://www.startpage.com/) is a search engine alternative to Google that gives you the same results as Google—without collecting your IP address, search history, or location.

![This alternative search engine has a similar homepage to Google, with a single search bar and autocomplete suggestions.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/55/c0/55c0678157f1c879611fcefa87964a16/f351a5ab3a71a8c22de58aec1552346e/AD_4nXezGpWwcb_rv2Q4nOVtdW9rNHEQc9Tct8wvCGHaZVd_nPsrLNAZI79ACgF4YGeq0Dc26SUZaPFtGNWLcIm0Po4uUAwsCp2TTsB9487j5zKe3PygnarzmqGwefgbnycQEuNJMckx.png)

You get a “Visit in Anonymous View” link under each result, so you can visit sites without revealing who you are. This prevents those websites from tracking you.

![Button appears below the meta description.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d4/77/d4773a63e8e67f7bea7b3dbc0ef0dded/085ce3f07d5ec2354991cbb3c069573a/AD_4nXf5gpQUZ8eGjJ7hqK18JcMlHP-H2zvEnJg2r2spigFne1OzKACdGiSJlFvMn9dmqRAJZt8TG7YoYbJL8K_T-JzO27ZHIDJdrlvWyh9eBf3RJsRWu-XycGCJcJKJX1VglMGkwCSt9Q.png)

Startpage supports over 80 languages, making it a good option for global users who want Google-level relevance with none of the tracking.

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

The “Visit in Anonymous View” feature is what sets Startpage apart for me.

When I research tools, I don’t want to tip off every site I visit. Because I don’t always want to be served [retargeting ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/retargeting/).

With Startpage, using this feature lets you visit sites while masking your information. There’s a blue bar at the top that clearly shows your session is being anonymized.

![Startpage feature masks your DNS, IP address, location, and user agent.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/46/be/46bed8c75fda3185a1cf994295e932fc/993b51aa3c9e1e78883a1f5428a08253/AD_4nXenfV05aUaaf-ZqH-uZ8kY8V-ynlovbbzKBFVnt83xUraks2oM8VONinyy7Vc5h_aLhW5T30iV158wmTSMtWQlyn9xqOfa7nhbONX3xLWrc-w4FR-IHehjpdK20Gftl8l8cGtI4.png)

It feels like regular browsing but with a built-in privacy shield.

## 9. Perplexity

[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) is an AI-powered answer engine that uses real-time web information to give you concise responses with clickable sources included.

Like this:

![Query appears, then sources, then summary.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0c/aa/0caa0055630bf1a7f247027dfd326abf/f7accf5441ec8b47881779bc44015e84/AD_4nXcuzdsYK_IPCH7UuvBoQkamnGBdcxhvaxw6sX9HnPXO9XVV9Wn7AOTOgqzUeT6MdPoK2CnF_3Nm0mZuqE9pL6oUuMc27Vyp_rkIbWJZX7gD7VzjR8ZIPv6-hkgbnOkHsK8HWfqHSQ.png)

You’ll find a “Discover” tab filled with timely stories across categories like tech, science, finance, sports, and culture.

Then there’s the “Spaces” tab you can use to upload files, give Perplexity instructions, and build collaborative research hubs to work with your team and keep all your research in one place.

![Create a space feature asks for title, description, and custom instructions.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b7/41/b7417961b6a49f532f3f4a915febce19/7e610423a48d98b2ed54fdcffbf23300/AD_4nXddYWsLux-pESS_gIpn9RbWoADVFYBGq1OjZ37l6dR_vIehN_MNqMzcGf8N21RvTZUN9F4e9j3uWTP_hOIgNBL6vnJZczNYYzz9TakQ1CrygPoGu6TnaW1OiPcJa_ruARaFxqIb2g.png)

Perplexity has a free option, but the Pro plan gives you more comprehensive answers, access to more advanced models (like GPT-4.1 and Claude 3.7 Sonnet), etc.

### Why I Think It's a Great Google Alternative

Perplexity is really helpful for finding specific statistics.

I wanted a stat on how many Gen Zers use TikTok for product discovery.

I tried Googling it first, but all I found were blog posts mentioning the same numbers from years ago.

So I entered this prompt into Perplexity:

*“What percentage of Gen Z uses TikTok for product discovery in 2025? Source it from a research study or survey.”*

It pulled stats directly from recent research.

![Recent research is highlighted in the provided citations.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/24/97/24973be68cfe503334ea06cd24d298b9/6db03f33cb70cf4d0485b5586826c530/AD_4nXcUf7A91-zlHta_bPV58obitohRgR6xFiz2-ynd24Z36y2UMZ8eioMvBHgs3yqlp27S8XrZMDssnwZuuCj50p1Lo1fKbtp5ieByh_vUpt1HphK4-agmChCHoFpv0PkfoMel52zIYQ.png)

That can save a lot of time that would otherwise be spent scouring the web and manually extracting relevant stats.

## 10. AOL

[AOL](https://www.aol.com/) works mainly as a news and media platform with a search engine (powered by Bing) built in.

![AOL homepage has categories, headline news, and search bar.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/56/75/5675d645f46f5065053989842eb4ef3a/2ce89f13b7ebcdf12bdfa2d11ac36f30/AD_4nXe7DPcRcGZ-bp51lQtkl78VC1xOv1esfkZegwCVy5b82RAtZR85M9eG1aKo1JFOcc88Q8LjziLRUWO011ESFuh8eAXYrZc0s_eGPPPYbw8eWEu_A3yilgJ4XeI-wxGT_vbMBDCZvg.png)

The homepage is what makes AOL stand out. It’s packed with news headlines, lifestyle stories, horoscopes, finance updates, and email access.

If you like browsing curated content while you search, it’ll fit into your routine.

Just note that there are more ads than some might prefer.

### Why I Think It’s a Great Google Alternative

What I like about AOL is that when I’m reading the news and something catches my attention, I can search for it right away without having to launch a separate tab (similar to Yahoo).

I was recently reading a news story on AOL’s homepage. I ran a search about a health term the article mentioned right in the built-in search function.

It felt less like switching tasks and more like continuing the same thought.

## 11. Baidu

[Baidu](https://www.baidu.com/) is China’s leading search engine. Its algorithm is built for Mandarin, so it understands its main users’ grammar, tone, and slang far better than Google.

And Google Search is banned in China, so Baidu more or less owns the market.

You can start with a regular search.
