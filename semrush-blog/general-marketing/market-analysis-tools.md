# 8 Best Market Analysis Tools (Free & Paid)

To grow your business, you must understand your market.

But how?

Start by conducting a market analysis.

In this post, you’ll learn what a market analysis is and why it matters.

You’ll also discover eight tools to help you perform a market analysis and dominate your niche.

Let’s start.

## What Is Market Analysis?

Market analysis is the process of evaluating a market in a specific industry.

It helps answer questions like:

- How large is the market and how fast is it growing?
- Which companies are the major competitors in the market?
- Who are the customers and what are their online behaviors?
- How can you attract your ideal customers within this market?

Conducting a market analysis is essential for any business that wants to grow and succeed in a competitive environment.

But how can you conduct an accurate and reliable market analysis?

Tools can help.

## 1. Market Explorer

Semrush’s [Market Explorer](https://www.semrush.com/market-explorer/) tool lets you analyze your market and uncover crucial insights for your business.

The tool provides a top-level overview of the market landscape.

Want to identify who leads your market? The tool can show you.

To get started:

## 6. Census Business Builder

[Census Business Builder](https://cbb.census.gov/cbb/) provides access to U.S. consumer data that’s especially useful for brick-and-mortar businesses seeking new geographic markets for expansion.

To get started:

1. Open the tool
2. Browse and select your business type
3. Choose the data type you want to see
4. Input the location you want to analyze

![an example of search for commercial bakeries in New York with Census Business Builder tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b8/09/b809db14b72e4caf5612233e0527bd4e/87d869d8a8811375cff5d4809a618676/AD_4nXejFb3kqpStRIBc4GFE0oCdxynbqX11-Ok59x7A0cxVkgv2MJKF5OWcXcv57nhF2tdlZKsSnsAoijfFdkSQ5zUR64Hq9WdycE4PPQdfONxWq7Lmp0-g_87Z9xapyJcYt6XtANKm3Q.png)

You’ll get consumer-related data, such as:

- The annual household spending on your business category in that area
- The annual spending compared with other states
- The median household income in the area
- The percentage of consumers who have completed high school or degree education
- The number of businesses that sell related goods in the area
- The homeownership rate in the area

![an example of dashboard with results in Census Business Builder](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/06/bd/06bd4bd6273223bbdcfdf2f3f23780ef/a50621dd65668e05827e79e733c5b498/AD_4nXdOaE7HFwOM70Ugy4dQUEM-1dJbspKHa0XHA4Y9KwQlSY2smvqHKx_ny3V8-cK6auwxL3l8ZBw9ODU84XMj7eYYdj8kn72M62v6E_5T5eMG6gKlVI4qDcHSZUT3jNIBiFBOg3s1YQ.png)

Use the tool’s data to evaluate a location’s business potential.

For example, high spending on your category may indicate strong demand for your product or service in the chosen area.

And a high median household income indicates a location where people have more disposable income.
