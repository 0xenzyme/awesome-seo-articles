# Top YouTube Searches

This article includes several lists with YouTube search data, including:

- Top YouTube searches in 2022

- Top YouTube searches of all time

- Most subscribed YouTube channels in 2022

Plus, information about [how to get more insights into your YouTube SEO](#get-more-insights-into-youtube-seo).

Let’s dive in.

## Top YouTube Searches in 2022

The most searched thing on YouTube in 2022 was “ukraine,” followed by “pink venom” and “duy beni.” In the United States, the top YouTube searches were “tiktok mashup 2022,” “glorilla,” and “msa.”

![youtube searches in 2022](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5d/10/5d10ed51d6c1a0c0c2f5b2fbf2cb894f/Top-YouTube-Searches-in-2022.png)

In the US, music takes the win—40% of the top 20 YouTube trending searches are related to music.

American rapper [GloRilla](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9bZ9eWvF0eXVqrxK9ve7Nw) makes the top of the list, followed by [Yeat](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCV4UK9LNNLViFP4qZA_Wmfw) and [PGF Nuk](https://www.youtube.com/@pgfnuk5439).

And interestingly, children’s TV comes in at a close second, taking 20% of what is most searched on YouTube in the US.

[MSA](https://www.youtube.com/@MSA.official) (previously My Story Animated) takes first place. Closely followed by [Ms. Rachel](https://www.youtube.com/@msrachel) and Disney’s [Encanto](https://www.youtube.com/results?sp=mAEB&search_query=encanto).

![Youtube video thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/hTqtGJwsJVE/hq720.jpg)

It’s an entirely different story for the worldwide stats.

TV is the obvious leader, with 35% of the top global YouTube searches.

As a country, Turkey wins with TV show [Duy Beni](https://www.youtube.com/@DuyBeniDizi).

Closely followed by India with four TV shows in the top YouTube searches worldwide —[Naagin 6](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=naagin+6), [Pushpa](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=pushpa), [Bhagya Lakshmi](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Bhagya+Lakshmi), and [Madam Sir](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=madam+sir).

![Youtube video thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/739wwm69Db0/hq720.jpg)

## Top YouTube Searches of All Time

What is the most searched thing on YouTube?

These are the top 20 searches **of all time**on YouTube in the United States and worldwide.

![top youtube searches of all time](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/bf/1abff08c1024859190169b135329a537/Top-YouTube-Searches-of-All-Time.png)

Games and entertainment take center stage with all-time top searches on YouTube.

[Fortnite](https://www.youtube.com/@fortnite) and [Minecraft](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=minecraft) make it to the top of the list, both in the US and worldwide.

TikTok comes in at a close second. And that’s no surprise. The platform has grown steadily to over [90 million users](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1100836/number-of-us-tiktok-users/) and shows no signs of slowing down.

As for the rest of the list, music still takes center stage. Both in the US and worldwide.
