# The Ultimate Guide to Casino SEO: How to Rank Higher, Faster

## What Is Casino SEO?

Casino SEO is the process of improving gambling website rankings in search engine results pages (SERPs). Compared to traditional SEO, however, it poses unique challenges due to the strict regulations governing gambling, esports, and online lotteries.

The gambling industry is also very competitive, with thousands of casinos fighting for the same slice of the pie.

They offer similar products and services, target similar audiences, and use similar tactics to gain exposure on the internet.

Regardless of the challenges, you can still rank well and get results, even if you don’t have a billion-dollar business.

Below, we’ll share the best SEO strategies for online casinos and how to overcome the hurdles around it.

## How Does Casino SEO Differ from Traditional SEO?

Whether you run a gambling site, an online store, or a fashion blog, it’s essential to follow [SEO best practices](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-best-practices/).

These include [keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/), [link building](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/), [on-page optimization](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/), [creating compelling content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/), and more.

Applying these SEO tactics may enhance the visibility and ranking potential of your casino website in search engine results.

At its core, casino SEO follows the same rules as traditional search engine optimization. The difference lies in the small details.

For instance, the keywords related to this industry are volatile due to a number of factors, such as the high competition and ever-changing regulations.

Potential customers use search terms to explore specific games or online casinos. They may also look for information related to gambling.

Some examples are “free poker games,” “play poker online,” “how to play blackjack,” “iGaming,” and related phrases. (iGaming is the practice of betting or gambling over the internet.)

However, these keywords may vary in performance based on regulatory changes, seasonal factors, industry trends, and economic events.

If a particular game is banned in a country or state, the keywords related to that game may suddenly get fewer searches from users living in that region.

The tight competition between casinos plays a role, too.

Many gambling companies have deep pockets. Therefore, they can afford to compete for top-performing keywords and experiment with various SEO tactics.

This aspect leads to constant changes in keyword rankings, so you’ll have to constantly refine and update your SEO strategy.

## 2. Optimize Your Site for Target Keywords

Now that you have a list of keywords, it’s time to use them throughout your site. This practice may help you get higher rankings, which can lead to more organic traffic.

![Organic vs. paid search traffic on SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1b/17/1b17ac26c0a6c7f5a738e041e8a8688d/a4d8df0760f186632c9d105c6b22f5b9/ssB9J07_AcG08HCwGa7oJnll_ocFtDqSeL7Q19WF4Z70LIvO-LdaUk_07j5kiOGUR7vY8V5LI05C4iIZH9u5tLoBPmceK8Q1PoyGqwiA_6amjYG-nmE2HOCZ-v5dN93Ayolcymetkedfu0ViEOptHl0.png)

[Organic traffic](https://www.semrush.com/blog/organic-traffic/) refers to the people who land on your site from unpaid search results. Your content will appear in [SERPs](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp/) based on its relevance to search queries, resulting in “free” traffic.

Choose one [primary keyword](https://www.semrush.com/blog/primary-keywords/) per web page to focus your content around. This primary keyword should be the most relevant and important term for that page.

You will also want to use [secondary keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/secondary-keywords/) that complement the primary keyword and cover related topics and queries.

The primary keyword forms the core of your content, while secondary keywords help in broadening your content’s relevance and reach, giving you more opportunities to appear in various searches related to your topic.

For example, a page dedicated to poker games could use “online poker” as the primary keyword and “Texas hold’em poker,” “five-card draw,” and “play poker online” as secondary keywords.

Your primary keyword should appear in the page title, URL, body text, and other strategic locations, including:

- [Image alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/)
- [Anchor text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/anchor-text/)
- [Meta description](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/)

Most importantly, use your keywords naturally throughout the content.

Avoid stuffing them into your copy to get traffic. This can hurt your search engine rankings and deter potential customers.

## 3. Streamline Your Site Structure

A well-structured website can improve the user experience (UX) and boost your SEO efforts.

In a [2021 survey](https://topdesignfirms.com/web-design/blog/website-redesign-checklist), 42% of respondents said they would leave a website with poor functionality. Consumers also evaluate a website’s layout, navigation, and design before deciding whether to stay or leave.

Here are some SEO techniques to optimize your site structure:

### Optimize Your Navigation

To ensure a logical website flow and keep visitors on your site, group your pages into categories and subcategories. You can also link to the user login page and registration page from your main navigation.

Ideally, customers should be able to find what they are looking for on your website without having to click the mouse more than three times.

For example, your homepage should direct users to the main pages, such as “slots,” “casino,” “poker,” and “bingo.” Like GameTwist’s:

![GameTwist’s homepage](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/97/e7/97e76bca7b169c3112e6dc93f6016b1e/bf012b022a83af57667d82da4f332038/l5PZBN6HL_-_5f7NJa5mFA64BVWUEtHjU038U_b9bzP6KtqzGf8fbIynZ1rcUyveGyW0bytxyExuR1ETICKv91rnzMz2saVx7srC59SxPqjQxX1dbN_FywLO5dj4wl7UfPChpYFXrDLWoGIfkp_tToY.png)

### Group Related Content

Organize your blog posts, casino games, and services by category and link to the main categories from the navigation menu. This can improve website navigation and help search engines crawl and index your content more easily.

On a similar note, you can link related pages together.

For example, if a blog post about casino games mentions Texas Hold’em, include a link to another blog post revolving around this particular poker game.

This practice is called [internal linking](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/).

### Use Headings Hierarchically

Header tags, or headings, allow visitors to quickly scan your pages and find the information they’re looking for.

These HTML elements also help search engine bots understand what a page is about. They are numbered from 1 to 6, starting with the [H1 tag](https://www.semrush.com/blog/h1-tag/) and followed by H2, H3, etc.

![A visual hierarchy of header tags](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b0/d0/b0d08d824e99d8a5d024ae51494d4df1/cd1598679db82d1bda69d5597feee962/SGNz83BlFeBzpH332EFINBXwlrEM_j33uIrAv1ST_in31bRDaKUTO2S2UjyaZgHn9-GdXfY2OokZ5S9nepflZJ9MlD3zaf09dgW-G0yAtLEK3UL8Ik5fDnGfSmymd_usMVrKlH4rMTs8faLFxOUpfqg.png)

### Optimize Your Headings

Each page should have one H1 tag that includes your target keyword.

If your primary keyword for a page is “online poker games,” your H1 tag could be “Online poker games for beginners.” Ideally, use your target keyword and/or secondary keywords in other headings, too.

### Create SEO-Friendly URLs

Customers should be able to figure out the main subject of your web page just from seeing the [URL](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url/).

Keep URLs short and relevant by removing unnecessary elements, such as page numbers (e.g., https://yourwebsite.com/blog?page=2) or parameters related to user preferences (e.g., https://yourwebsite.com/settings?theme=dark).

Use hyphens instead of underscores to separate the words in URLs, and include your target keyword whenever possible. Like this example from CardPlayer:

![CardPlayer's page with URL that reads "cardplayer.com/rules-of-poker/how-to-play-poker"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6d/36/6d361c1152f89fc3b779b8b0a2501ea2/531ab73f6c9fe42fe9c6486b166b6096/dwOUb86O-kzT86tFVONajfrVS6-c7wQxSt-CI1I5r0RyJJbp1fDOGBkYgznCAraxta9LKvN-xo0vn2Qi0DDk6v5LKPWzqozB-GzwRc3ST4qoj-78gDhUp55aJwXa4_dET6l405DXOZgcVmL38JepU5Y.png)

### Set up a Sitemap

Search engines use [sitemaps](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-sitemap/) to understand a website’s structure and determine the most important pages. This file lists all the pages on a site, including the date when they were last modified.

You can create one manually or use a sitemap generator like [XML Sitemaps](https://www.xml-sitemaps.com/) or [WriteMaps](https://writemaps.com/). Some [content management systems (CMSs)](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/content-marketing-blog/content-management-system/), including WordPress, will automatically generate a basic sitemap.

## 5. Leverage Content Marketing

[Content marketing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-content-marketing/) involves creating and distributing relevant and engaging content to prospects and existing customers, which has many benefits.

### Create Topic Clusters

A [topic cluster](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/) is a group of webpages structured around a central pillar page that gives a broad overview of a main topic. The pillar page is linked to multiple related pages, each covering a specific subtopic.

In this cluster, not only does each subtopic page link back to the pillar page, but they may also interlink with each other.

This network of internal links between the pillar and subtopic pages helps organize content and enhances its discoverability. Therefore, [keyword clustering](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-clustering/) is beneficial to both search engines and users.

Here’s an example:

![Topic cluster example with "Disney world planning"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/db/4b/db4bf3b3ddb23718f6c730fddc37f36c/c78968cab38d8666099131d50f87a31d/jisi0k-CK6l646Vkq6QmRLC7qv6voblWoM6mdC_5VxDsFfb6-ON2OBjCMBvnNS3OrRn9R_OGkGKRIbmcLQX6LmBVNv-0hdu-cR814UUrvX87LBc5nCP59YTce0dg9X_yHbDu9zsVlVvRVllxnq3zof8.png)

## 6. Improve Technical SEO

Search engine optimization is a multifaceted process with multiple components.

Internal linking, keyword research, content creation, and the other practices described in this guide are all essential components of on-page SEO.

But you also need to consider the practices illustrated below, which fall under [technical SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/). This is a subset of on-page SEO.

![img-semblog](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/72/be/72beaaaedf08463576a0263fa6f2239e/27865d57d25fc20835233e9ee946b9f8/EN-Technical-SEO-Infographic.png)

For example, ensure your website looks good on mobile devices to avoid losing relevant traffic.

According to a [study conducted in the UK](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/report/gambling-participation-in-2019-behaviour-awareness-and-attitudes/gambling-participation-in-2019-online-gambling-behaviour-devices-used), 50% of all online gamblers access their favorite games from their smartphones. Another 16% use their tablets.

Additionally, [78% of all bets](https://www.softswiss.com/news/sb-h1-23-results/) are placed via mobile devices.

Given these figures, it makes sense to choose a responsive theme, which adjusts its layout to any screen or device.

You’ll also want to compress large images and minify your code. The latter practice involves removing unnecessary elements like comments and whitespace from a website’s code.

Also, optimize your forms and [call-to-action (CTA)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-call-to-action/) buttons for different screen sizes. For example, you’ll use larger CTA buttons for mobile users so they can spot them more easily.

These practices can improve the mobile user experience, potentially resulting in higher conversions.

Go one step further and optimize your content for voice search, which was one of the top [SEO trends in 2023](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-trends/).

For instance, a potential customer might ask, “What’s the best way to win at poker?”

To appear in voice searches, your content should have a conversational tone and include specific, relevant keywords, such as “the best way to win at poker” or “how to win poker tournaments.”

You also need to consider [page load speed](https://www.semrush.com/blog/page-speed/), a factor that can impact your search engine rankings and the user experience. A web page that takes too long to load may deter potential customers.

You can use [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-pagespeed-insights/) to check how fast your web pages load.

If they are rather slow, you may need to compress large files, clean up your code, or remove unnecessary plugins. In some cases, it may be worth switching to a different web host or upgrading your hosting plan.
