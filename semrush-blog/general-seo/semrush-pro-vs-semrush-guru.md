# Semrush Pro vs. Semrush Guru: Which Is Right for You?

If you've decided to start using Semrush, you might be trying to decide whether you should go with a Pro or Guru plan.

You've come to the right place.

By the end of this article, you'll have a clear understanding of how the plans differ. And which is right for your needs.

## Semrush Pro vs. Semrush Guru: Main Differences

The Semrush Pro plan covers the basics like keyword research, site audits, and rank tracking.

The Guru plan unlocks historical data, larger limits, and more advanced features like content tools.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | **Pro Plan** | **Guru Plan** |
| **Who it’s for** | Freelancers, solopreneurs, small businesses, and small teams managing a few projects | Scaling businesses, growing agencies, and dedicated marketing teams at mid-size companies |
| **Price** | $139.95/month | $249.95/month |
| **SEO Projects** | 5 | 15 |
| **Daily keyword tracking** | 500 keywords per account | 1,500 keywords per account |
| **Monthly crawl limits** | 100,000 pages | 300,000 pages + JavaScript rendering |
| **Content marketing tools** | Limited access | Full access to Topic Research, SEO Content Template, and SEO Writing Assistant |
| **Historical data** | ❌ | ✅ |

**Semrush Pro Overview**

The Pro plan provides plenty of capabilities for smaller operations with specific needs:

- **Manage up to five SEO projects**: Track performance, spot issues, and optimize up to five websites at once
- **Conduct keyword research**:Identify valuable search terms and use AI to get access to metrics that can help you find the best terms for your specific site
- **Track 500 keywords per account**: Monitor your daily keyword rankings to measure where you stand compared to the competition
- **Run competitive analyses**: Leverage SEO tools to understand your competitors’ strategies and identify untapped opportunities in your market
- **Make technical SEO improvements**: Crawl and examine up to 100,000 pages each month to find issues that affect site performance and rankings. And get tips on how to fix those problems.
- **Build and monitor backlinks**:Get access to backlink reports and analysis tools. So you can monitor your link profile, uncover prospects worth getting backlinks from, and track new and lost links over time.
- **Measure performance holistically**: Connect Google Analytics through the My Reports tool to show your SEO results next to key events like purchases.

**Semrush Guru Overview**

The Guru plan builds on Pro's foundation with expanded limits and exclusive tools:

- **Manage up to 15 SEO projects**: Monitor performance, track issues, and optimize up to 15 domains or subdomains at once. All from one dashboard.
- **Track 1,500 keywords per account**: Monitor keyword performance across various locations with multi-targeting capabilities.
- **Create search-optimized content**: Unlock content marketing tools that provide suggestions for making your content high-quality and likely to rank highly in search
- **Analyze historical performance**: Identify seasonal trends and find year-over-year changes with historical data
- **Audit complex websites or many different sites**: Crawl up to 300,000 pages per month—including those with dynamic content and JavaScript—for a deeper technical analysis

For John Lawless, UK Content & SEO Manager at Limitless Digital Group, the Guru plan has been worth it:

> "Across my team, we enjoy the SEO content template tool very much. It has helped us to achieve several featured snippets and allows us to accurately determine what changes we need to make to existing content in order for it to rank."
>
> John Lawless

## Semrush Pro vs. Semrush Guru: Content Marketing Tools

A Semrush Pro plan offers access to tools that give you a basic starting point for content analysis.

In contrast, the Guru plan gives you access to a complete content marketing workflow. Which makes it easier to plan, write, and optimize content that’s likely to rank and deliver results.

Here’s how Guru expands your content capabilities.

Start with [Topic Research](https://www.semrush.com/topic-research/) to quickly find content topic ideas. Just enter your seed keyword and click “**Get content ideas**.”

The tool presents a variety of related topics organized into subtopics, each displayed on cards.

### Semrush Pro vs. Semrush Guru: Historical Data

A Pro plan allows you to gather current information about keywords and your competitors’ SEO performance.

Guru takes it one step further to give you access to data as far back as January 2012. Which is useful for seeing seasonal shifts, how competitors have adapted, and more.

Here are a few places you can access historical SEO data with a Guru plan:

- [**Organic Rankings**](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/): Analyze competitors’ organic (unpaid) traffic and keyword rankings over time
- [**Keyword Overview**](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/): Research how a specific keyword’s search volume, difficulty, and intent type (a reflection of what users are trying to achieve) have changed over time
- [**Domain Overview**](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview/): Get a holistic view of a domain’s search performance, including trends for traffic, keywords, and backlinks.

### Semrush Pro vs. Semrush Guru: Rank Tracking

The Guru plan expands this to 1,500 keywords and allows tracking across up to [10 different locations](https://www.semrush.com/kb/692-multi-targeting) or devices within one project. Giving you a broader view of your performance.

Here’s how to set this up in [Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/):

Open the device type drop-down and select "**+ Add new target**.”

### Semrush Pro vs. Semrush Guru: Reporting Capabilities

With the Pro plan, you can build up to three PDF reports each month using data from Semrush, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console to show the impact of your SEO work.

Guru subscribers can do the same and also add Semrush data to [Looker Studio](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-data-studio-seo-template/) dashboards. Which is helpful for telling a complete story.

For example, you can pull metrics from [Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) that help you gauge your website's performance in search engines.

## Should You Choose Semrush Pro or Semrush Guru?

When deciding between Semrush Pro and Semrush Guru, consider your specific needs and the level of data analysis required for your projects.

**Choose Semrush Pro when**:

- You’re managing SEO for just a few websites
- You need basic rank tracking, site audit, and keyword research features
- You’re building simple reports and don’t need year-over-year comparisons
- You have a small-scale content strategy or are just getting started

**Choose Semrush Guru when**:

- Content creation is a major focus, and you want tools to plan and optimize with confidence
- You need to automate polished reports, compare performance over time, and track rankings across locations
- You’re managing multiple websites and want the flexibility to scale without limits

The bottom line?

Pro is great for starters. But Guru gives you room to grow.
