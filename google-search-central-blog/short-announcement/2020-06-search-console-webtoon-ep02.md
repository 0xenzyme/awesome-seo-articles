# Why are my pages not shown on the search result page?

We published episode 2 of our webtoon series in Korean. Here's a summary of the episode in English:

She is excited to learn about the queries that led the band's fans to her site, but also realizes one of her biggest mistakes: the band's schedule has been uploaded as an image file. While Cherry and Eco Bag discuss various aspects of analytics in the Performance report, and making it easier for search engines to understand a website, Cherry's father Choco Muffin stops by and asks for help to promote the Small Business Association.
