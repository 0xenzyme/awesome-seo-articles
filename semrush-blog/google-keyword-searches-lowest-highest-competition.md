## Methodology

Data was collected from 20 billion keywords in the [Semrush Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/start) database, in March 2021.

Competition was based on the Semrush Keyword Difficulty metric — a calculation of domain authority and page authority for organic SERPs.

The most competitive results had a minimum of 1,000 monthly searches and sorted by the highest Keyword Difficulty score. Some modifiers had to be adjusted based on their results. For example, ‘affordable’ was sorted on a minimum of 100 monthly searches due to limited results.

The least competitive results had a minimum of 100 monthly searches and sorted by the lowest Keyword Difficulty score.

Lists were cleaned to remove: NSFW, religion-based queries, drugs and pharmaceutical queries, sex-related queries, ‘buy a degree’ queries, firearms and hunting queries. Direct COVID queries were also removed to avoid skewing results.

Year-based searches were removed for anything prior to 2020.

Some non-transactional keywords were also removed that were potentially skewing results, such as ‘Best Buy customer service’ related queries that dominated the ‘best’ modifier.

Any repetition from keyword variants that are grouped by Google were removed, such as multiples or country suffix. For example, ‘Rightmove houses for sale’, ‘Rightmove house for sale’, ‘Rightmove houses for sale uk.
