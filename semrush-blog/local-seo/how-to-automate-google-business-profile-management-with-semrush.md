# How to Automate Google Business Profile Management with Semrush

Managing your Google Business Profile (GBP) takes time, and most business owners don’t have much of it.

But leaving your GBP unattended can limit your online visibility over time. Google Search, Maps, and AI-powered platforms look for signals that a business is active and up-to-date, such as often posting, using fresh photos, and responding to customer reviews. In general, profiles that show consistent activity are more likely to appear when customers search.

That doesn't change the fact that maintaining a healthy Google Business Profile requires ongoing effort, which can sometimes feel like a full-time job.

Semrush Local’s [GBP AI Agent](https://www.semrush.com/gbp-ai-agent/start) reduces the time and expertise barriers by automating many routine Google Business Profile tasks. The tool handles tasks like publishing posts, uploading photos, and replying to reviews, eliminating the need for constant manual effort.

In this guide, you’ll learn how this time-saving tool works and how to set it up in a few quick steps.

## Why busy businesses use Semrush’s GBP AI Agent

GBP AI Agent manages ongoing tasks to help keep your Google Business Profile active, which can support visibility and trust over time.

Once you set it up, GBP AI Agent works in the background to maintain your profile without needing your weekly attention.

Here’s what GBP AI Agent can do for you:

- Draft an improved business description for your approval
- Recommend a posting schedule for posts and photos
- Create and publish Google Business Profile posts
- Select images from your uploaded photo library and schedule them automatically
- Upload fresh photos at a regular cadence to maintain consistent profile activity over time
- Write and publish replies to customer reviews in your chosen tone
- Track visibility and engagement metrics in one place

Next, you’ll learn how to connect your Google Business Profile so GBP AI Agent can run its initial audit.

## Step 1: Connect Your Google Business Profile

To use GBP AI Agent, you need to connect your Google Business Profile. Fortunately, this takes just a few clicks.

First, go to Local in the **Semrush side menu** and click **GBP AI Agent**.

Click **Activate my AI Agent** and choose the Google account that manages your GBP.

## Step 2: Run the GBP Audit

Now you’re set up, GBP AI Agent will run an audit to understand how active your profile is as of today. An active profile isn’t just about posting often—it’s about using posts and photo updates in a consistent, strategic way.

To do this, the tool audits your posting habits, photo activity, business description, and keywords your profile is currently associated with. It then uses these insights to recommend an effective strategy for your automated posts and updates.

The audit focuses on activity and content signals, not technical SEO or ranking guarantees.

### A Refined, Keyword-Rich Business Description

GBP AI Agent generates an optimized business description tailored to your services and category. You can choose to use this updated version when activating or keep your existing description.

### A recommended posting frequency

The audit suggests how often you should publish posts to stay competitive.

Regular photo update recommendations

GBP AI Agent checks how often you upload photos and recommends a weekly posting rhythm to help support your profile’s visibility across search results, including AI-powered searches.
