# Google unveils Gemini 3.5 Flash and a redesigned ‘intelligent Search box’

Google announced Gemini 3.5 Flash — designed to be Google’s most capable agentic and coding model to date — and an "intelligent Search box" that supports longer queries as well as images, videos, and file uploads.

Google made these announcements at Google I/O 2026. And both features are live now for all users.

Gemini 3.5 Flash is now powering Google Search, AI Mode, and the Gemini app. And it's available to all users — not just paid subscribers. Google’s DeepMind team [called it](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/) the "strongest agentic and coding model yet."

The Gemini 3.5 Flash-powered intelligent search box means users can now interact with Google Search the same way they'd interact with any other type of conversational AI.

![The new intelligent Search box lets users enter conversational prompts alongside images.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a5/13/a513237a3954e3b2e80b6bbb7fabdb77/4eee9c4b501f7f9cf068e9fb0139712a/image.png)

## What this means for marketers

The rollout of Gemini 3.5 Flash and the intelligent Search box are signals of what Google is building toward: a highly-personalized AI search experience that aims to deliver the most relevant and useful information to users.

The intelligent Search box doesn't just accept longer inputs — it actively encourages them. The box expands as you type.

And multimodal queries route users directly to AI Mode. The new design nudges users toward more detailed, specific prompts that could lead to increasingly more experiences in AI Mode compared to traditional results.

For marketers, these updates mean content needs to answer the specific questions searchers are actually asking. And that visibility inside AI responses matter just as much if not more than organic rankings.
