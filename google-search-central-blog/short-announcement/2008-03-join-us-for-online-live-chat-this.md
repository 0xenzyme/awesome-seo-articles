# Join us for an online live chat this Friday!

Many of us Webmaster Help Group guides have happily gotten to meet Group members at various
functions around the world. Over sandwiches in San Jose. Salads in Stockholm. Sweets in Sydney.
And it's been super!

But some of you are hard to find and so we haven't had the pleasure of chatting with you.
Therefore, we've decided to visit you right through your beloved monitor screen.

On **Friday, March 28 at 9am PDT / noon EDT / 16:00 GMT**, we'll be having our first-ever
all-group live chat, where you'll have a chance to hear and see us answer some of your most
pressing questions. All that's required is a phone (we'll pay for the call), a
[sufficiently-modern](https://support.webex.com/support/system-requirements)
web browser, and an internet connection.

We'll be posting a "sticky note" with more details in
[the Random Chitchat section of the Group](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-chit-chat/topics)
a day or two before this online meetup, and we're looking forward to chatting with you soon!

**EDITED ON MARCH 26 TO ADD:** More information about the chat is now available in
[the Webmaster Help group](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help/web/google-webmaster-help-group-chat).
