# Why Are My Competitors Showing up in AI Search and Not Us?

## Why Does It Feel Like AI Search Favors My Competitors?

It might feel like AI systems favor your competition, but that’s not because AI systems think your competitors are better than your brand, but rather, your competitors likely have stronger authority signals (like [backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) and [branded mentions](https://www.semrush.com/blog/brand-mentions/)) and content that is easier for AI systems to read.

Authority signals help AI determine which sources to trust. When competitors have stronger signals—even with comparable content quality—AI systems will mention their content in citations more often.

And content that’s easier for AI to understand has a better chance of being cited within AI outputs. If competitors answer questions in their opening sentences with clear structure, AI can pull those segments cleanly. AI might skip content that buries answers or uses complex phrasing.

### Are My Competitors Really Showing Up More Than Me?

Your competitors may genuinely appear more often in AI responses, but you can't know for sure without measuring it.

Manual spot-checks can mislead you. AI responses vary by query phrasing, user context, and when you search. So searching a few queries where competitors appear doesn't prove visibility gaps.

To verify a visibility gap, use Semrush's AI [Competitor Research](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/competitor-research/) tool.

Enter your domain and up to four competitors. Click “**Run competitor analysis**.” Review your AI visibility score against your competitors to see if they’re really showing up more than you.

AI platforms don't rank brands in a fixed order like traditional search results. They select based on authority and relevance for each specific query.

## How Do AI Engines Decide Which Brands to Feature?

[AI engines](https://www.semrush.com/blog/best-ai-search-engines/) feature reputable brands with strong authority and trustworthy content.

Authority can come from quality backlinks, [brand mentions](https://www.semrush.com/blog/brand-mentions/), and consistent use of your business name and product names across trusted sources.

Trustworthy content includes verifiable claims with explicit sources, clear author credentials, and data from credible research.

### Where Do AI Platforms Get Their Information About Brands and Products?

AI platforms source brand and product information from training data, licensed content partnerships (like Reddit and academic publishers), and indexed web content.

For example, say you [launch a new product](https://www.semrush.com/blog/product-launch-ideas/) and you want AI systems to mention it. You might create detailed [product pages](https://www.semrush.com/blog/product-page/) with clear descriptions and specifications, publish announcement content on your blog, earn coverage in industry publications, and generate discussions on platforms like [Reddit](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddit-seo/) or relevant forums.

Publishing in the sources that AI systems reference can improve your chance of appearing in AI answers.

### Why Do Some Domains Get Trusted More Than Others?

AI platforms trust domains with established authority signals, verified credentials, and consistent citations across high-quality sources over newer or less-cited sites.

Domains earning frequent citations typically have strong backlink profiles from authoritative publications. Along with verified organizational information and consistent mentions in trusted contexts. AI systems use these signals to assess source reliability before including content in responses.

### How Stable Are AI Answers and Citations?

AI answers and citations change frequently based on model updates, query phrasing, and which sources the system retrieves at the time of the request.

For example, “best CRM?” and “what is the best CRM?” are similar queries that produce different results.

![A comparison of how two very similar queries on Perplexity produce different results.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8f/8d/8f8df6e7aa5012d1527a3f2ba154c20c/59d13e290dbc7a5d4b64404fa86a2b3a/image.jpeg)

### How Do Different AI Search Engines Decide Who to Recommend (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, etc.)?

Different AI engines decide who to recommend based on distinct ranking signals, training data, and retrieval methods which cause brand visibility to vary across systems.

[Our research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-keyword-ranking/) also found that domains ranking in Google's top 10 are often cited across AI platforms, too.

## What Are My Competitors Doing Better in the Eyes of AI Platforms?

Your competitors may do better in the eyes of AI platforms because they have stronger authority signals, broader topic coverage, and more frequent mentions across trusted sources.

If your competitors are outperforming you in AI search, you should:

- Focus on building consistent brand mentions online through [digital public relations](https://www.semrush.com/blog/digital-pr/) (PR), partnerships, and contributing expert commentary to industry publications
- Optimize your content with clear headings and easy-to-find answers that AI systems can easily extract
- Earn quality backlinks from authoritative sites in your industry to strengthen [trust signals](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-trust-signals/)
- Expand topic coverage to match competitor breadth
- Stay present on public forums that AI platforms reference like Reddit or Quora

### Are They Just More Present in the Training Data?

Brands with consistent mentions across the sources that AI models train with are more likely to be referenced.

To build training data presence for future model updates:

- Publish consistently on your own site and earn coverage in authoritative publications that likely appear in training datasets
- Get mentions in Wikipedia and academic papers
- Maintain active profiles on platforms AI companies license for training data, such as Reddit and major news outlets
- Create linkable assets (research, data, tools) that other sites reference

### Are They Mentioned More Often Around Key Topics and Situations?

Yes, competitors mentioned frequently alongside specific topics and use cases appear more often in AI responses for those contexts.

AI systems learn topic associations from repeated patterns across sources. When trusted sites consistently mention a competitor around key topics, AI platforms associate that brand with those contexts and cite them for related queries.

### Do They Have Stronger Authority Signals and Clearer Entities Than We Do?

Competitors with stronger authority signals and well-defined entities (subjects that AI systems recognize like brands, people, places, products, or concepts) may perform well as AI systems prioritize trustworthy, recognizable sources.

Authority signals include quality backlinks from trusted domains, consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across directories, and mentions in authoritative publications.

### Is Their Content Easier for AI Systems to Quote and Justify?

Yes, competitors with clear structure, direct answers, and explicit sources make extraction and citation easier for AI systems and competitors following these patterns may get cited more often even with comparable expertise.

AI platforms prefer content that answers questions in opening sentences, uses clear headings, and provides verifiable claims with linked sources. Like this:

## What Should I Fix First to Start Showing Up in AI Answers?

You should first fix content structure by adding clear headers with answers directly below each header. Then address authority gaps through backlinks and consistent brand mentions.

Start with your most important pages (like [landing pages](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-landing-page/) and product pages) that target queries your competitors already rank for in AI responses.

Restructure these pages to answer questions directly in opening sentences, use headings that mirror the prompts people use, and add explicit sources for all claims.

Next, focus on authority building. Secure mentions in online publications by sharing newsworthy updates—like product releases—and offer expert quotes to relevant outlets through platforms such as [HARO](https://www.helpareporter.com/).

You can also grow your authority by building backlinks to your site. One way to find backlinks is with Semrush's [Backlink Gap](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/gap/backlinks/) tool, which identifies the backlinks your competitors have that you don’t.

![Backlink Gap report showing a list of competitor backlinks which a brand doesn't have.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/56/8b/568b109e34b47f2c01658c020c4d43c2/b92e2be080bd4d84816bc14bf31f7187/image.jpeg)

Try to get links from the same sources through [backlink building strategies](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building-strategies/) like [outreach](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building-outreach/) and link bait (content that naturally attracts backlinks like research reports).

Also build brand mentions through PR, expert commentary, and industry publication contributions to strengthen recognition signals.

### How Do I Make Our Existing Content More AI-Friendly?

Make your existing content more AI-friendly by restructuring answers to appear early, adding clear headings, and providing explicit source attribution.

Here are more tips to [optimize content for AI search engines](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-optimize-content-for-ai-search-engines/):

- Rewrite opening sentences to directly answer the query in complete, standalone statements. Move supporting details and context to follow the direct answer rather than leading with background information.
- Add descriptive [H2 and H3 headings](https://www.semrush.com/blog/heading-tags/) that match question formats users actually ask. Replace vague headings like "Getting Started" with specific questions like "How Do I Set Up Keyword Tracking?" that mirrors real prompts.
- Link to sources for every factual claim, statistic, or expert statement. AI systems prefer content with verifiable attribution. Making cited content more likely to be extracted and referenced. Use authoritative, primary sources rather than secondary aggregators whenever possible.

Create content around AI prompts by finding out what actual people are asking AI platforms.

You can get prompt ideas with tools that [track prompts](https://www.semrush.com/blog/llm-prompt-tracking/) and their outputs.

For example, Semrush’s [Prompt Research](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/prompt-research/) tool is like keyword research for AI. Enter your topic and click “**Analyze**.”

![Prompt Research tool showing data like topics, prompts, intent, brands mentioned, etc. along with a list of prompt ideas for a topic.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/34/dd/34dd285bac160b1e336f4c9c47170229/c4a05b66bb250aa9b43ed7e630a30f64/image.jpeg)

Then, select relevant prompts then build content that addresses complete situations rather than isolated keywords.

Structure new content by using the prompt phrasing in your headings and opening sentences.

### Do I Need a Separate AI SEO Strategy, or Is This Just SEO Done Differently for AI Answers?

You don’t need a separate [AI SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-seo-tips/) strategy because core [SEO best practices](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-best-practices/) (like strong backlinks, good technical [site health](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-health/), and helpful content) also help AI SEO.

AI optimization adds onto your [SEO strategy](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-strategy/), and things like direct answers, explicit source attribution, and brand entity consistency make existing content more extractable for AI systems.

### How Do I Align SEO, Content, and PR So They Reinforce Each Other in AI Search?

Align SEO, content, and PR by targeting the same topics and entities across all channels, creating consistent signals that AI systems recognize and trust.

Coordinate topic selection so your content team writes about the same subjects your PR team is pitching and your SEO team is optimizing for.

Then, when PR secures coverage about your new feature, you can ensure that corresponding content exists on your site that AI can easily reference and extract.

Balance SEO and AI SEO by integrating AI optimization into existing workflows rather than creating separate initiatives.
