# What are some case studies demonstrating successful enterprise SEO initiatives?

Successful [enterprise SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/enterprise-seo/) initiatives are demonstrated by case studies from companies like Amazon, WebMD, Airbnb, and others that achieved massive organic growth through strategic, scalable implementations.

Learning from these case studies helps you scale your own organic traffic. Here are proven success stories across different industries:

- **Amazon's programmatic SEO dominance.** Amazon created millions of algorithmically-generated pages for every conceivable product combination and search query. The result? Amazon captures an estimated 50%+ of all e-commerce searches.
- **WebMD's medical content authority**. WebMD became the top health information site through comprehensive content creation. They built medically-reviewed pages for over 10,000 conditions, symptoms, and treatments. According to Semrush's [Domain Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview/) tool, they now get around 107 million monthly organic search traffic.
- **Airbnb's international expansion strategy.** Airbnb scaled to over 220 countries and regions by creating localized landing pages for every destination. This strategic approach grew their monthly organic traffic to around 21 million.
- **Walmart's site migration success.** Walmart faced a complex challenge: migrating millions of product pages from multiple domains. They successfully consolidated everything into a single, unified domain. Despite this massive undertaking, they actually grew organic traffic by around 30%.
- **Canva's template SEO strategy.** Canva built SEO-optimized landing pages for every design template type. This focused approach grew their organic visibility to millions of users in under five years.
- **Pinterest's image SEO mastery**. Pinterest became the dominant platform through strategic image optimization. Their boards and pins now rank for hundreds of millions of queries, driving over one billion monthly organic visitors to their platform.
- **Glassdoor's user-generated content engine.** Glassdoor built an SEO empire on employee reviews. They rank for virtually every "[company name] reviews" search. According to Semrush's Domain Overview tool, they get around 8 million in organic search traffic because of this strategy.

Enterprise SEO success comes from identifying scalable opportunities and executing with precision across technical, content, and strategic dimensions.
