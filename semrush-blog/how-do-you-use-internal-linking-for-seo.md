# How do you use internal linking for SEO?

You use [internal linking](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/) for SEO by strategically connecting related pages on your website with descriptive [anchor text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/anchor-text/) and creating clear [site architecture](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-structure/) for search engines and users to navigate through your site.

Internal linking is one of the most powerful [on-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) techniques that helps search engines crawl your site more effectively while keeping users engaged longer.

Here's how to use internal linking for SEO properly:

- **Link to relevant, related content naturally.** Connect pages that are related or where one page provides additional context for another. For example, link from a general SEO guide to specific pages about keyword research or technical SEO.
- **Use descriptive anchor text with keywords.** Instead of generic phrases like "click here," use specific, keyword-rich anchor text that describes the destination page, such as "comprehensive keyword research guide" or "local SEO best practices."
- **Create** [**topic clusters**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/) **and pillar pages.** Build topic clusters by linking a comprehensive pillar page to related subtopic pages, then linking those subtopic pages back to the pillar and to each other when relevant.
- **Prioritize your most important pages.** Link frequently to pages you want to rank higher, as internal links pass authority and signal importance to search engines. Focus on key landing pages, product pages, or high-converting content.
- **Maintain a logical site structure.** Ensure important pages are no more than 3-4 clicks from your homepage, and create clear navigation paths that both users and search engines can follow easily.
- **Avoid over-optimization and link stuffing.** Include internal links where they genuinely add value for users rather than forcing links just for SEO purposes. Aim for 2-5 contextual internal links per page.
- **Monitor and update broken internal links.** Regularly check for broken links that create poor user experience and prevent search engines from properly crawling your site.
