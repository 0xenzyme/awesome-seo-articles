# Seamless verification of Google Sites and Blogger with Webmaster Tools

Verifying that you own a site is the first step towards accessing all of the great features
[Webmaster Tools](https://search.google.com/search-console)
has to offer, such as
[crawl errors](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9679690)
and
[query statistics](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35252).
The [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/)
and [Blogger](https://www.blogger.com/)
teams have worked hard to make site verification as simple as possible. In the following videos,
I'll walk you through how to verify sites created in Google Sites and Blogger.
