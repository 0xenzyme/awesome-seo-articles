I've spent months testing dozens of free and paid ChatGPT alternatives to find the best solution for each situation.

In this guide, I've listed my top 11 picks for you to consider—with output examples, top features, and feedback for each.

## 1. Claude: The Best ChatGPT Alternative for Human-Like Writing

- **What does it do?** Helps you analyze, summarize, and generate human-like texts
- **Who is it for?** Users looking to generate engaging copy in various formats

![Claude, the best ChatGPT alternative for writing human copy](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/77/52/77526321eec208dc91f9036e6d1a5f3b/5116b39345c5f79d1acf328a184db5df/AD_4nXcW5UnGZiJCmRayhDCYVGnIAzNbuBpTl909VQ86SWAKcMqz-1Sblv2GcUJXLoiwbk4qTrRVoA5ka_5CNroHmHtSoDwtjFkEP-7F4dpz73k9gR3i4JD4eKifqrW__vCMuJJ2fmFqjQ.png)

Anthropic’s [Claude](https://claude.ai/new) can chat with you, answer questions, and lend a hand with all sorts of tasks.

It now includes web search and a new [Research feature](https://www.anthropic.com/news/research), giving it live internet access and the ability to provide citations.

But what really sets Claude apart from ChatGPT is how well it matches your tone and style.

I like using it for quick writing tasks and creative content. It sounds more human, less robotic.

Let's look at an example.

I gave Claude and ChatGPT the same guidelines and prompts to write a blog post section entitled 'What is a brand voice?'.

Claude generated text that was highly structured, informative, and easy to read. It maintained a neutral tone of voice that didn't sound at all robotic.

![Generating content with Claude](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/74/20/74201cc6ea509aab020df9b4eff07681/a0247df574b7edd52e8aa699f6ea338d/AD_4nXdVfHxdwuUSNv6nJBJaCT0s5Cs6jIxVLQklk-QaN3SGYtSvJAsIh8Hqx73MMclTzF2ongJPSZtRc-CBcyG51nwWt3gBw0LTs3QJETqCMLddQySy2pn1TY_QcpehOaUKmKiGk5ol.png)

ChatGPT, on the other hand, generated a wall of text that was quite hard to read and follow. The information was helpful, but the tone felt too formal and too formulaic.

![Generating copy with ChatGPT](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/47/45/474523a0e4debbdfdc3add440a6b4309/ecf008d544d5a0b2d56fca3f3b606de9/AD_4nXefmZ39oF_F5ppM9nCOOXggwMWa-gzgVSymkzOLnJHAGG9tFlMdk5Hf01lt9xO3-Szz9ILBhXjesWeLFdLuDIrEzxfEVl_QnD-esjRzvDOS7ApnDv5CfsW9MkbK5kxrETO9de62PA.png)

For long-form content, ChatGPT tends to write content that feels more like an outline than a finished piece. Claude, however, usually produces copy that flows well and looks more complete.

|  |  |

| **Pros** | **Cons** |

## 3. Copilot: The Best Conversational AI for Microsoft Users

- **What does it do?** Conversational and creative text and image generation
- **Who is it for?** Researchers, users of Microsoft 365 apps, and businesses needing text and image generation

![Copilot: The best conversational ChatGPT alternative for Microsoft users](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/88/29/8829493bc66695eec2a14a7358d37aac/e231fc33b0a276ca6947c81a9b76d974/AD_4nXfKQuI5BcG386o57DqYEKPpEVfo9AtCYs8z4drlD1ujHjBk3O-Rr0kQiZPxD8biXz7vayvd6UrPVW2IZio68_hlmzckI7DYzxGMqphrN_wRYLCnkGL6BFejxpTcGsDAYrl8lxhA.png)

[Microsoft Copilot](https://www.bing.com/?/ai=) is a large language model (LLM) that allows you to generate text, create images, and interact with Microsoft products and services.

Copilot is powered by OpenAI’s language models: Pro users get access to GPT-4 Turbo and DALL-E 3 for [AI image generation](https://www.semrush.com/contentshake/content-marketing-blog/best-ai-image-generator/).

![Creating images with Microsoft Copilot](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/68/ab/68ab335cdf44bf08a4879bf223144a9f/01df49260aa54c5023b01a1743a1888a/AD_4nXehTCTtMtaIQiTTyCAKeFXkUUus3EbozzKU9SNaiJnXaP9bjv_uKB3nbaAS2jt6SEtcRw4DFdAOKz0Fx--7bQA8rwLbjn8cnvM9ynWa6KXqQpg2gzun2CQ_vYVENgcDJxJFmYAU.png)

Copilot is also deeply integrated with Microsoft products for different use cases:

- Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Security, and Microsoft 365 have their own tailored versions of Copilot
- Data from Microsoft Graph and your Microsoft ecosystem lets Copilot provide personalized and contextually relevant responses
- Copilot also works with Microsoft applications like Edge, Dynamics, and Power Platform

For example, you can use Copilot within Microsoft Teams to summarize conversations and find important information faster:

![Using Microsoft Copilot in Teams](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/21/56/2156a125399449b1857f7ee677fd9087/ce37d36e5383fe70a9792e06e2d0a95f/AD_4nXfkVazdvPs-7YvZ5ngZ4Xt-inn3qbMoPLObgFDEGkxFJl7NLH3D9PCRDRd07a2v_0tOwKridJ5IM8pAEQhXTEbDlMQkPorrmxguuYTk8fjM6upkNREUaA2VpyNz2fTUSpaulenEyA.png)

Source: [Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/use-copilot-in-microsoft-teams-chat-and-channels-cccccca2-9dc8-49a9-ab76-b1a8ee21486c)

## 4. Gemini: The Best ChatGPT Alternative for Integrating with Google’s Ecosystem

- **What does it do?** Analyzes and generates text and images and connects with Google apps
- **Who is it for?** Google Workspace users and those looking for more human-like text generation

![Gemini: The best ChatGPT alternative for integrating with Google’s Ecosystem](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ce/87/ce870760519b9e8ac050cdffc4d44922/2f6a92c27525f313c91e2f579e816f29/AD_4nXdq-q8-_YW0eTTZCYuFe3JJBi9j7bj7XqeG-Heutqh52pve0hSrFcMlom4gaBU-t6MRW4xSEWh6H27vUajQ_Q1ujXp3uXTALbL5Il6aGcoYyG4luLrvldCbf7G_IEtCnfwtE10Q.png)

[Google's Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) is a multimodal large language model that analyzes and generates text, code, and images with its new Imagen 3 model. It’s powered by Google's own family of LLMs, developed by Google DeepMind.

Gemini works great for structuring information and producing more human-sounding language. I’ve always felt that it [works better than ChatGPT](https://www.semrush.com/contentshake/content-marketing-blog/gemini-vs-chatgpt/) when it comes to content writing.

To put this to the test, I asked both AI chatbots to write a blog intro.

While Gemini did come up with a few AI cliches, it was easy to read and used a casual [tone of voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-define-your-tone-of-voice/):

![Creating content with Gemini](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6c/08/6c08e6b24103ac8f4e1ab9625d38f0c4/135ebdd2f491ee0858e98de5233249c6/AD_4nXc-oM5c4Nv4Kix24z8pg9yBHOS2xuwPclZHedFsSqlghZdNJKkMmbeK1dazxs8UjnpA3EGFR09na8u6xTHu1XVEdf06gzwqM5QZdV6r0Z7Hcg12QPxzdvfFFrbJqIgb3Va0nmdoog.png)

ChatGPT, by comparison, began with its all-time favorite intro line, “In the rapidly evolving world of…” The tone was more formal and the language more convoluted—which is always a red flag for me.

![Creating content with ChatGPT](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1f/54/1f54d9151947cf5f36cefcb3cacd3f99/7ed01b63819b1e305465e242ae91cde7/AD_4nXdzl_ufJTWPKlkOzZddC6qTcHSN-l1Yfjf2iGbC4Hg7yp3CAmwFkg9Q2ZBXBQTcagUia8d0RaCEYawp5q8bKklIuCT14XYkUE7YWWHC8Jgd1DKf795VOh6vd6_6Q16SFfDk2gd5DA.png)

Another key advantage Gemini has over ChatGPT is how deeply it connects with Google's ecosystem. It accesses your content across Google Workspace, drawing directly from Gmail, Docs, Drive, Calendar, Keep, and Tasks.

This means Gemini can help draft emails, summarize documents, locate files, check your calendar, take notes, and manage your to-do list—all within the Google apps you already use.

![Using Gemini in Gmail](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4a/d7/4ad7d4c4099d06fdebda4722523b38c6/670ed27ca48ee918cd8315cfc4c10161/AD_4nXce9X18cxiuu88F9cn__HW4AExwuMdU3veTDJsBgDCbmLz4WS_1GDZXNBD-TNoDmmYRgb2gC7WabNcWbyhcjMzSQqQQhNUx7B5IvaIviYYbZcErnDby9jHVnBuOzkoQ71J5sR8Scg.gif)

## 5. Llama 4 by Meta: The Best Open Source ChatGPT Alternative

- **What does it do?** A free open-source AI assistant integrated into Meta's platforms for content creation, image generation, and social interactions
- **Who is it for?** Meta platform users, developers looking for customizable AI solutions, and users who want a free ChatGPT alternative

![Llama 4 by Meta: The best open source ChatGPT alternative](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a5/dc/a5dcbe435652bb11e24a07a61b8ea756/b0ff30ce01a9a6c29bc552ed97808866/AD_4nXcRWBJg_LY96Rm7lRvPWnNbHbmtt6MS6oA3bNTRCFSaeXeZ0FVQk3RCSe6rr4EagjPf9qgmMZhEaJgFgPhDaOUG7S77oqMy9BdL6rmVnknJ4u_M0dzyTPtUedset-QAHDkinCQU.png)

​​[Meta AI](https://ai.meta.com/) now runs on [Llama 4](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/), the newest model released in April 2025.

It’s a big upgrade—Llama 4 is faster, smarter, and better at understanding both text and images. It also works in more languages and handles longer, more complex conversations without glitching out.

Just like before, it’s built into Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger. For example, here’s what it looks like for Instagram users:

![Meta AI Instagram](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/58/91/58919f3d6f1b0b5041fb363e3144c2c0/4cd7f6c293ec84a3aae3280b0ae0c3e9/AD_4nXcoNhueEojZXXecL1FENhfz0teKLoN4uXquUgRnUGFsXBcLHku1rlCbXwpkPIIRlm1S7ziR8O1gPIggug3icUbJ-Sp_SU_fNc0ajcv17CZAM85HUtsLFoviN2sac_rNQtNEJrK9.png)

But now it responds more naturally, understands context better, and can even help with things like coding or more advanced content creation.

You can also use it on the web at [Meta.ai](https://meta.ai), or plug it into your own tools if you want to customize how it works—that’s the upside of it being open source.

### Pros and Cons

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Pros** | **Cons** |
| Llama 3.2 is free and accessible to everyone | A lack of control over open-source LLMs might lead to inconsistent answers and unintended consequences |
| Open-source nature allows for customization and transparency | Raises concerns about security and data privacy |
| Integrates with Meta's social media platforms | May have less extensive knowledge compared to ChatGPT |

### Pricing

- Llama 3.2 is an open-source model. It’s free to download and use for research and commercial purposes.

It allows you to use multiple models in one platform, such as the latest versions of GPT 4, Claude, Mistral, and its own in-house models.

It also draws answers from different live sources and provides source citations.

![Conducting online research with Perplexity](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7e/b2/7eb26f38fdba3034cb7b34334d9ab929/c9a6de413798df23ce50652749c5dfef/AD_4nXc14hvgLOX_B0LNl-z7m5IwFqq-apmk3RHepRmcXS_5g90-gN9g7qCsFx-D6z1-d-3MY_poAwJ4Clt-uptSwaAbsUtT2PaLbT8w4cnIC1I3uKNSZIgPsK4aiOrdke51h6LVbHCf.png)

This feature lets you dive into real-time sources, browse the web, and pull citations from academic papers, news sites, and forums.
