# 14 Best AI Assistants for Marketers

From managing your schedule and providing information to assisting with content creation, an AI assistant can help you complete time-consuming or resource-heavy tasks.

They’re personal sidekicks that use artificial intelligence to streamline your workflow and increase productivity.

But with so many AI tools on the market, choosing the right one can be overwhelming.

To help you cut through the noise, we’ve curated a list of some of the best to make daily work more efficient and convenient.

## 1. AI Video Marketing Automator: Repurpose Your Content

[AI Video Marketing Automator](https://www.semrush.com/apps/ai-video-marketing-automator/) uses AI to turn prompts, and even entire blog posts, into engaging videos in seconds.

You can then upload your videos to your website or social media channels to drive traffic, increase engagement, and improve your [search engine optimization (SEO)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/video-seo/).

Like all the best AI assistants, AI Video Marketing Automator produces results with minimum effort.

Paste in an article URL, choose which illustration type you want to use (e.g., 3D illustrations, hand-drawn illustrations, or simple illustrations), and click “Create Video.”

!["URL to Video" window in Instant Video Creator](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2b/68/2b6876d0900c0a76ac68f46bc3423ccb/d755760a7f215ebf834a92c1f91f0233/aZpURVeFOOOpppPsLwLWTUZfMN2YJCPBa49B-FO6WADwoYCBXHB06_kla2J1ih_vcBA0L09RB_WIKNSHRSTIN8hv7bf_3XtQkDHCwxAVJKMWxPQNLx_SaKtwbv6bKn_TrY9-tAokrTR9wOwwLnDA008.png)

AI Video Marketing Automator takes your headings, text, and images. And turns them into an engaging draft video, read aloud by a human-like AI voiceover.

You can—and should—then review and edit the draft to change the voice, music, and style to match your brand.

![Instant Video Creator editor](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4b/ef/4bef97ca1b7bcddc77ed1c57624f5630/f50a7f499e05e879b84c61aa45dffa72/qBCA1LmpQFzOo3LF6Gr7RsX18aDie4vZXd07M825OJ5SX0Ei1CUgNRH0YLdSp8dD7m8Z4k1ywmywBwBBaynzCLdM_Vf7iJu-StZAy2kIlvJms68PR4do-l7sqS4G18C2w6l6NGKy3Oe4DY8B0W5swF8.png)

Once you’re happy, you can export your video in landscape, portrait, or square format.

As well as blog posts, you can create one-click videos for:

- Frequently asked questions
- Customer reviews
- Quotes
- Case studies
- How to guides
- Company news

You can also make videos from scratch. Or, have AI Video Marketing Automator’s AI generate content from a prompt, such as a topic or product description.

If you’re investing hours of your time to create helpful content for your audience, you deserve to squeeze every last drop of value out of it. AI Video Marketing Automator lets you do it without having to invest significant time or resources.

## 3. Google Assistant: Your Personal AI Assistant

[Google Assistant](https://assistant.google.com/) streamlines daily tasks such as managing your schedule or reading messages out loud. It also offers quick access to information, like the chance of rain in the next hour, so you can go about your day more efficiently.

![Google Assistant's "Hi, how can I help?" page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4f/4b/4f4b9e8a7a05df86a1303684e8e58c27/63346c7e99f6a5bb18443015f36374a9/tIzZnyUHeEQ7xjYgPpYobq4yI90Foxb8AqddCKeMWrs30jiZ54PrTTmior2z0aVkXn6u9Jd2fTHd91c3OrdFjqTdSV4x8Sx47c2qAhpFOE9UKr7EcEDsCvlpCPuwkX8EEsOnsVMnmXjZt4N447jiruA.png)

If you own an Androidsmartphone, Google Home, or Wear OS device, you can activate Google Assistant with the touch of a button. Or turn on voice activation and start using it by saying, “Hey, Google.”

Once you do, Google’s artificial intelligence is ready to help you get things done in a natural, conversational way.

Say you tell the AI assistant, “Hey, Google. Remember that I parked my car in Zone B.” Google will respond with something like, “Ok. I’ll remember that you parked your car in Zone B. I’ll also save a map of your location.”

You can use voice commands to [ask Google Assistant](https://assistant.google.com/learn/) to do anything you’d typically use your phone for. For example, you can:

- Make phone calls
- Check the weather
- Send text messages
- Set an alarm
- Find the best route to a destination
- Get the latest news

![A list of commands you can ask Google Assistant to do for you](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7f/04/7f04c298e4532b988ea097512219f4a6/7cd9bf2a8c95f978aaa65d0b6a9ca94c/g9ibtt_ly5HSvXdcHIdHP5CIvhhTVQ2FdO6w93X8PkrNwqC2q8Ij6z4O5NC7_fwPxSOUtZ1PDp4jyg8uK6sNKtZWfUPS-6Jh2TWb6DFfI1VhjLh2la0O_EoXg6MH_Cf9EzShfv_iap3z3u-s8lKyQh0.png)

You can also ask Google Assistant to send you meeting reminders, read out messages, and update you on upcoming appointments.

It’s all designed to keep you organized, as any good assistant should.

And Google is rolling out a new feature to create an even more personalized helper.

[Assistant with Bard](https://blog.google/products/assistant/google-assistant-bard-generative-ai/) integrates Google’s chat-based AI tool Bard into the Assistant to extend its functionality to other Google tools, such as Gmail, Google Docs, and Google Drive.

Assistant with Bard can scour your emails to find important information, write email replies, and create content.

For example, you can open the Assistant on a photo and ask it to write a social media post. Assistant with Bard uses the image as a visual cue to understand the context and create an engaging caption.

![A social media image of a dog and caption generated by Assistant with Bard](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/84/89/84899e7ac02d71da8716a4d8d585dbcb/397c51d19acc71241abbbeea1cc47026/K8vX0w3sXmyp-DMcPSPLlZtO7BV6Gb7KCDQYq8rYHcQQ22bue6EP5vgTFUOGL1659jwwlnGK0dTFu7gqfRlqd9dq0KcjYjac4QBONaGrZ5VRELR5Dd9Pz7gQ8G5JC8AFOCVVLVTeU2GrPgN-OsV6oeU.png)

Image Source: [Google](https://blog.google/products/assistant/google-assistant-bard-generative-ai/)

Like the current Google Assistant, Assistant with Bard is opt-in. So you can choose whether or not to use Bard’s capabilities.

If you do a lot of your business on your smartphone, Google Assistant is a powerful feature to increase your productivity.

If you’re not an Android or Google user, don’t feel left out.

Google Assistant isn’t the only voice assistant in town. [Apple’s Siri](https://www.apple.com/uk/siri/), [Amazon’s Alexa](https://alexa.amazon.com/), and [Microsoft’s Cortana](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/cortana) all offer similar features. And are great options for your preferred phone, desktop, or smart home device.

### Price

Google Assistant is free with a Google Account.

## 4. MarketingBlocks: Create Marketing Assets for Your Business

[MarketingBlocks](https://hey.marketingblocks.ai/) eases the workload of content creation (and the potential cost of outsourcing) by generating human-like marketing assets from your keywords.

It’s powered by an AI assistant called Ethan.

![MarketingBlocks's chat with AI assistant Ethan](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a3/67/a367af1b804074eda66feac05b1628b6/b326dcd9a02d2b6f8f3668e79f2569b3/SW-vgqDW0_--SB1ZWBIGojfBzKaiF5T9aw9YQ6ROuBLLhJi79i0LzO6xewjgUcNkgJEhD0EDdlcdd3v0NUildjh-3bXXZripd0jZJ-AXsMQG-lPd78keUAh64WK7QDoZn8fDKNWzZpdVqFyl2M4i93g.png)

Tell Ethan your business niche or product name and description. Then, choose what you want it to create for you.

The AI generates ready-to-use original marketing assets for your audience.

Ethan can create:

- Website copy
- Ad copy
- Landing pages
- Videos
- Emails
- Graphics
- Chatbots
- And plenty more

You can also use its AI to turn text into videos featuring real humans or avatars. Or ask Ethan to write and turn a script into audio for a podcast or audiobook.

Essentially, if you need something for a marketing campaign, Ethan can probably take some of the ideation and generation off your plate.

Does it nail the brief every time?

Not always.

Ethan creates content based on what it’s learned works for your keywords. But tone of voice, images, and graphics won’t always fit with your brand.

So, while Ethan can generate marketing assets for you, it’s important to check everything and make edits before sharing.

But to speed up content creation and keep your calendar full, MarketingBlocks is a great AI assistant.

### Price

## 6. ChatGPT: Get Support with Everyday Work Tasks

[ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/) is an AI chatbot that can help with a range of tasks, from writing content to simplifying complex ideas.

Give ChatGPT a prompt and it uses its natural language processing (NLP) to provide a helpful, conversational response.

For example, you can ask ChatGPT to come up with content ideas for your marketing. Here, we’ve asked ChatGPT to generate five blog post ideas. These are the results:

![ChatGPT's response to "I run a local cake making business in New Jersey. Can you create 5 ideas for blog posts?" prompt](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bd/d6/bdd63f63c0f9b02e74ee82f496a73fb6/1ac035f619886deaf8c3b09c1db6bc1d/Ld_dqAtKrkkTmoBufq7iLW_eZwMI-eCra9VNv-F_FVq7MeuKSvG2Pqn7lRoV5HhG0nrkrKn7IDQaicPM4K1rLH6r19uZoAa7KnDU8GLga9w0FUPfJyjSLnm1lTv6OTeCK7q_LPPcy4INgKkd6BcaCas.png)

If you’re not satisfied with the output, you can ask ChatGPT to regenerate the list.

When you’ve found a result you’re happy with, you can ask follow-up questions to turn an idea into an outline.

![ChatGPT's response to a follow up prompt: "Can you create a blog post outline for idea number 4?"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/85/a0/85a00ee1d2c43bd91f54a2fc0385fb47/fc690e6467690a66625383f127734cd8/m2dBLAU_Y5ry7wuruF0tQlbsSmf_krr5yJCnWGZByt1T0nGMvZX6YPzy6X3Y7OKkkv5kDqudE70nF5bU9oy5u9f9wjD3AP6e2lRlohGHnu4DdClYisKs7nOemqjFQkNzPndAFt2g1FCPSj5goh3Fcss.png)

That’s just one use case. There are all kinds of other scenarios where the tool can help, depending on your needs.

ChatGPT can assist with:

- **Market research:** Ask Chat GPT to find competitors, gather publicly available data, or develop buyer personas.
- **Keyword research:** Ask Chat GPT to generate a list of unique terms related to your business.
- **Translation:** Paste in your content and ask Chat GPT to rewrite it in a different language.
- **Editing and proofreading:** Paste in your content to spot typos, improve clarity, and suggest ways to make writing impactful.
- **Learning new subjects:** Ask Chat GPT to explain complex ideas in simple terms or give you the need-to-know information about a topic.
- **Writing code:** Ask Chat GPT to generate snippets of HTML, Python, PHP, C#, Java, or Javascript. Or have it create Excel spreadsheet formulas.

To get the most out of ChatGPT make prompts specific and detailed.

For example, if you want ChatGPT to write an email, give it details on your target audience, product, offer, and tone of voice.

![ChatGPT's response to asking it to write an email](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/cd/28/cd28e0a05f5681a4dbccec5bc1596b3e/dc359205b311c477eea7f458f1da2c54/XHwDK4v2KwuPvUNBNKtmCAGrzQ311qX6rOv0AZ8N9IlGedpYF3Jrvm-utwDWOkVF_ue_Cpr4hMbE8pVxAoZJffooiEoOektv0q2rUPg2ioVgwm5-hFODwx0Kyr_7MvAFjmDutcdrWNlpoFiRe4xuyjk.png)

And don’t expect perfect results every time. Ask it to provide sources or revise answers where necessary. But accept that it won’t always be accurate.

ChatGPT is language-based, not experience based, so it cannot offer unique perspectives about a topic. So, it’s important to edit any content it generates before publishing.

Simply put: use ChatGPT to assist you in your work, rather than to do it for you.

### Price

Chat GPT is free. OpenAI also offers [ChatGPT Plus](https://openai.com/blog/chatgpt-plus), with benefits such as faster responses and priority access to new features for $20 per month.
