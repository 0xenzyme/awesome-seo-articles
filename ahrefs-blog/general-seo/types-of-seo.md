# 68 Types of SEO. Did We Miss Any?

Here are all SEO types known to humankind, up to the minute!

But no worries, we’ve organized them neatly to save you a headache. Click the links to explore a deep dive into each SEO type.

## Core types of SEO

1. [**On-page SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/on-page-seo): (aka on-site SEO) refers to anything you can do on the page itself to improve its rankings. It focuses on helping Google and searchers better understand and digest your content.
2. [**Off-Page SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/off-page-seo): anything you do outside your website to try to improve its search engine rankings. It’s often used synonymously with link building, but it goes beyond that.
3. [**Technical SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/technical-seo): search engine optimization that helps search engines find, crawl, understand, and index your pages. The goal is to be found and improve rankings.

## Technical SEO

4. [**Mobile SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/mobile-seo/): ensuring a website is optimized for mobile devices, considering factors like responsive design and mobile user experience.
5. [**Accessibility SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/accessibility-seo/): ensuring that a website is accessible to all users, something which can also positively impact SEO.
6. [**Edge SEO**](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/edge-seo/273983/): a technique that lets you make changes faster and easier without needing deep technical knowledge or waiting for a developer’s help.
7. [**JavaScript SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/javascript-seo/): part of technical SEO that makes JavaScript-heavy websites easy to crawl and index, as well as search-friendly.
8. [**React SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/react-seo/): the process of optimizing React-based web apps for search engines to ensure they are properly indexed and ranked
9. [**NextJs SEO**](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/nextjs-seo/): refers to optimizing websites built with Next.js, a React framework, for search engines.
10. [**Headless SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/headless-cms-seo/): the practice of optimizing your headless CMS so that it meets search engine optimization best practices and gives your content the best chance of ranking for relevant keywords

## Content SEO

11. [**Landing page SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/landing-page-seo/): the process of enhancing webpages created to generate leads, focusing on attracting traffic from search engines.
12. [**Blog SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/blog-seo/): the process of writing and optimizing blog content to rank in search engines like Google.
13. [**Content SEO**](https://yoast.com/ultimate-guide-content-seo/): focusing on creating and structuring high-quality, relevant content that targets specific keywords and topics.
14. [**Semantic SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/semantic-search/): focusing on the meaning and context behind user queries, beyond exact match keywords.
15. [**Multimedia SEO**](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbesbusinesscouncil/2022/08/24/why-your-seo-strategy-should-include-multimedia-content/?sh=1c47a3051fa7): optimizing various types of non-text content, including audio, video, and interactive media.

## Local and international SEO

16. [**Local SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo)**:** practice of improving your online presence to get more business from local searches.
17. [**International SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/international-seo/)**:** optimizing your website for users in different countries and speakers of different languages.
18. [**Multilingual SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/multilingual-seo/): the practice of optimizing website content for multiple languages to improve visibility and ranking on search engines in different linguistic markets.

## Platform-specific SEO

19. [**Ecommerce SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ecommerce-seo/)**:** the process of optimizing an online store to improve its visibility and rankings in search engines like Google. It focuses heavily on improving the performance of category and product pages.
20. [**YouTube SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/youtube-seo/)**:** the process of optimizing your videos, playlists, and channels to rank high in YouTube’s organic search results for a given search query.
21. [**App Store SEO**](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/): SEO for mobile apps in app stores, focusing on app title, description, reviews, and ratings.
22. [**Amazon SEO**](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-seo): SEO specifically for ranking products higher in Amazon’s internal search engine.
23. [**Etsy SEO**](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663987-Search-Engine-Optimization-SEO-for-Shop-and-Listing-Pages): SEO specifically for ranking stores and products higher in Etsy’s internal search engine.
24. [**Shopify SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/shopify-seo/): ditto, but for Shopify.
25. [**Squarespace SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/squarespace-seo/): ditto, but for Squarespace.
26. [**WordPress SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wordpress-seo/)**:** refers to the practice of optimizing a website or blog created and managed using WordPress.
27. [**Wix SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wix-seo/): ditto, but for Wix.
28. [**Social media SEO**](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-seo/): optimizing social profiles and content for betting visibility in social networks**.** Not to be confused with leveraging social media platforms to [indirectly influence website SEO](https://ahrefs.com/blog/social-signals/).
29. [**Yep SEO**](https://yep.live): [Yep](https://yep.live) is a private, revenue-sharing search engine created by the team behind Ahrefs, one of the leading SEO toolsets worldwide. (Yes, this list includes even SEO types from the future).
30. [**News SEO**](https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/navigating-google-news-seo/): Optimizing content for appearing in Google News and other news search engines.
31. [**Google Discover SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/google-discover/): optimizing content specifically for Google Discover.
32. [**Marketplace SEO**](https://brodieclark.com/marketplace-seo/): the process of optimizing online marketplaces for buyers and sellers on search engines such as Google and Bing (thanks for the tip, [Brodie Clark](https://twitter.com/brodieseo)).
33. [**Google SGE**](https://blog.google/products/search/generative-ai-search/) **SEO**: “there will be a niche discipline/service for people targeting one of the elusive top 3 SGE carousel results as these are typically different results from normal search results (soon to be pushed way down the page)” (thanks for the ~~tip~~ prophecy, [Steve E. Bates](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stevenebates/)).
34. **Datafeed SEO**: refers to the optimization of product data feeds for better visibility and ranking in search engine results on marketplaces like [Google Shopping](https://searchengineland.com/google-shopping-optimize-free-product-listings-430104), particularly in shopping or product comparison searches (thanks for the tip, [Andy Beard](https://twitter.com/AndyBeard)).
35. [**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**](https://foundationinc.co/lab/generative-engine-optimization): the process of optimizing content to increase visibility inside of generative AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity (thanks for the tip, [Alex Nigmatulin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nigmatulin/)).

## Specialized types of SEO

36. [**Image SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/image-seo/): optimizing images to rank in image search results, including aspects like file names, alt text, and image compression.
37. [**Video SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/video-seo/): the process of getting more video views from Google and utilizing videos to increase organic traffic to your website.
38. [**Programmatic SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/programmatic-seo/): refers to the creation of keyword-targeted pages in an automatic (or near automatic) way.
39. [**Holiday SEO**:](https://ahrefs.com/blog/holiday-seo/) the practice of optimizing web content for increased search engine visibility during specific holidays, targeting holiday-related queries to capture seasonal search traffic.
40. [**Featured Snippet SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/featured-snippets/): targeting the featured snippet or “position zero” in Google search results.
41. [**Long-Tail SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/long-tail-keywords/): targeting typically less competitive but highly targeted search queries (i.e. the long tail).
42. [**SaaS SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/saas-seo/): making a SaaS business easy to find on Google and other search engines. The goal is to attract people who are looking for solutions that your product can provide
43. [**Enterprise SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/enterprise-seo/): search engine optimization for large, enterprise websites. The goal is to improve the quality and quantity of traffic coming from search engines to webpages on enterprise companies.
44. [**Niche SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/find-niche-keywords/): refers to the specific set of tactics especially effective in a specific business niche. The list is endless; there are as many types here as niches. For example, [SEO for startups](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-for-startups/), [SEO for lawyers,](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-for-lawyers/) [plumber SEO](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-for-plumbers/), [wedding photographer SEO](https://ahrefs.com/seo/for/wedding-photographers), and so on.
45. [**Voice SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/voice-search/)**:** optimizing for voice search queries, often focusing on natural language and question-based content.
46. [**AI SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/chatgpt-for-seo/): using artificial intelligence like ChatGPT to inform and automate SEO strategies.
47. [**Taxonomy SEO**:](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/taxonomy-seo) refers to the practice of organizing and categorizing website content in a logical and hierarchical manner. It involves creating a classification system that makes it easier for users to navigate the site and find relevant information.
48. [**Barnacle SEO/Parasite SEO**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FrqVe7Rg454): attaching content about a brand to highly authoritative websites to gain more visibility in search (thanks for the tip, [Chris Haines](https://twitter.com/chris_at_b449)).
49. [**Syndicated SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/content-syndication/): refers to a strategy where content is distributed or syndicated across various websites to increase visibility and drive more traffic to a primary site (thanks for the tip, [Andy Beard](https://twitter.com/AndyBeard)).
50. [**SEO for academic journals**](https://wostrategies.com/optimizing-google-scholar-vs-googlebot/): refers to the process of enhancing the visibility and accessibility of academic journal articles in search engine results, including but not limited to Google Scholar (thanks for the tip, [Katherine Watier Ong](https://twitter.com/kwatier))
51. **Advanced SEO**:techniques and tactics that go beyond basic search engine optimization, for instance, [advanced link building](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1sCaEIIwMQ4) or [advanced keyword research](https://ahrefs.com/blog/advanced-keyword-research/) (thanks for the tip, [Raju Khadka](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rajukdk/)).
52. **Branding SEO** **(aka** [**Brand SERP**](https://kalicube.com/learning-spaces/faq/brand-serps/what-is-brand-serp/)**)**: optimizing a brand’s online presence to improve its visibility and reputation in search engine results (thanks for the tip, [Onur Kılıç](https://www.linkedin.com/in/onur-kilic/)).
53. [**Podcast SEO**](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/podcast-seo/333205/): optimizing podcast content to make it more discoverable and accessible through search engines, including Google and podcast-specific platforms like Apple Podcasts and Spotify (thanks for the tip, [Martin Hinterdorfer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martinhinterdorfer/)).
54. [**Forensic SEO**](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/what-is-forensic-seo/326595/): “a form of SEO that specifically deals with the analysis of visibility loss either over time or with a sudden traffic crash, and how to strategically recover quickly while minimizing the negative effects of optimizing too much too soon. It often deals with recovering from Google penalties and optimizing toxic backlink profiles.” (thanks for the tip, [Madison Sawyer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sawyer-madison/), I couldn’t have defined it better).
55. [**Travel SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/travel-seo/): optimizing a travel-related website to rank higher in search engine results, with the goal of attracting more organic traffic from people interested in travel topics   (thanks for the tip, [Liubov Liashko](https://www.linkedin.com/in/liubov-liashko/)).
56. [**B2B SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/b2b-seo-strategy/): refers to the strategies and tactics used to increase the visibility of a business’s website in search engine results, specifically targeting other businesses as opposed to individual consumers (thanks for the tip, [Stephen](https://twitter.com/s_watts_seo)).
57. **B2C SEO**: ditto but you’re targeting individual consumers (thanks for the tip, [Stephen](https://twitter.com/s_watts_seo)).
58. [**Paywall SEO**](https://www.seoforgooglenews.com/p/best-practices-for-paywalls-and-seo): optimizing content behind a paywall for search engines. It includes showing snippets to search engines for indexing, using structured data to indicate the presence of a paywall, and balancing content access to improve search rankings and attract subscribers (thanks for the tip, [Andy Beard](https://twitter.com/AndyBeard)).

## SEO philosophies

59. [**White Hat SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/white-hat-seo/): refers to the use of SEO strategies, techniques, and tactics that are within Google’s guidelines.
60. [**Black Hat SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/black-hat-seo-tactics/): practices that violate search engine guidelines, often used for quick gains, but risky and unethical.
61. [**Grey Hat SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/grey-hat-seo): techniques that are not explicitly against search engine guidelines but are questionable in ethics and sustainability.
62. [**Negative SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/negative-seo): unethical practices aimed at lowering a competitor’s search engine rankings. Awareness is crucial for defense (by the way, [here’s how to deflect them](https://ahrefs.com/blog/negative-seo/)).
63. [**Sustainable SEO**](https://yoast.com/sustainable-seo/): long-term SEO strategies that focus on steady growth and resilience against algorithm changes.
64. [**Continuous SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/continuous-seo/): an approach to SEO that practices it as an always-on strategy without a final date or goal.
65. [**DIY SEO**:](https://ahrefs.com/blog/diy-seo/) the self-managed approach to search engine optimization, where individuals or businesses implement SEO strategies themselves without hiring professionals.
66. [**Holistic SEO**](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/holistic-seo): An all-encompassing approach that considers every aspect of a website, from content to user experience, as part of SEO.
67. [**Hustle Bro SEO**](https://twitter.com/michalpecanek/status/1750512943333032323): SEO advertised as a get-rich-quick scheme. A hustle bro culture phenomenon. You’ve been warned 😉 (thanks for the tip, [Michal Pecánek](https://twitter.com/michalpecanek)).
68. [**Search Experience Optimization (SXO)**](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-experience-optimization/): a strategy that focuses not only on optimizing for search engines but also on optimizing the overall experience for users who arrive from search results. It blends SEO tactics with user experience design and conversion rate optimization (thanks for the tip, Eric d’Amérique).

## Final thoughts

Let’s be real — you don’t need to master every single type of SEO that pops up on the web. They are a mix of buzzwords, styles, sets of tactics, and services that some SEOs specialize in. And it becomes evident only after you see a list of each and every one of them.

Focus instead on the essentials: [core SEO principles](https://ahrefs.com/seo) and platform-specific tactics if you want traffic from search engines other than Google.
