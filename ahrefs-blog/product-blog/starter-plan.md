# Ahrefs’ $29 Starter plan – what’s included and how it compares

If you haven’t heard yet, we’ve launched a new plan at Ahrefs.

Meet our Starter plan: a $29/month offering that’s great for SEO beginners who are looking to go beyond the basics offered in our free [Ahrefs Webmaster Tools](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools) plan.

Here’s what you need to know.

## Who’s the Starter plan for?

The Starter plan is perfect if you’re just starting out, or want to explore our platform without a big commitment. It’s designed for light usage and gives you a fantastic introduction to the potential of the Ahrefs toolset.

If you end up loving Ahrefs and start using our tools intensively, you may outgrow the Starter plan quickly – but don’t worry, upgrading is super easy. You can find a new plan that fits your needs right on our [pricing page](https://ahrefs.com/pricing).

## What’s offered in the Starter plan?

Like our Lite plan, the Starter plan is credit-based.

On a Starter plan, you’ll get **100 credits each month**. You consume these credits when accessing reports and requesting new data from most core tools.

You’ll have access to these Ahrefs tools:

- [SEO Dashboard](https://ahrefs.com/dashboard)
- [Site Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/site-explorer)
- [Keywords Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/keywords-explorer) (not included in Ahrefs Webmaster Tools)
- [Site Audit](https://ahrefs.com/site-audit)
- [Rank Tracker](https://ahrefs.com/rank-tracker) (not included in Ahrefs Webmaster Tools)
- [Ahrefs SEO Toolbar](https://ahrefs.com/seo-toolbar)
- [Web Analytics](https://ahrefs.com/web-analytics)
- [Social Media Manager](https://ahrefs.com/social-media-manager)

Note that Site Audit and Rank Tracker **do not consume credits** since they have their own limits.

## Terms & Conditions

- Additional users, credits or any other add-ons cannot be purchased on the Starter plan.
- You cannot downgrade from a Lite or higher plan to a Starter plan.
- The Starter plan can only be renewed monthly.
- Features and limits on this plan may be changed without prior notice.
