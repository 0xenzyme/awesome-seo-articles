## AI Visibility Toolkit: Understanding and Improving Your Brand's AI Presence

The [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/) helps small- and mid-size businesses track how often they appear in AI-generated answers, identify missing mentions or citations, and take targeted actions to improve how they’re represented.

We released the entire toolkit in 2025, and it’s already helping customers measure and strengthen their presence across major AI search platforms.

Here’s what you can do with it:

### Analyze Your Brand’s AI Visibility

![Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit for AI search visibility tracking - AI Overview report](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/01/e4/01e4a2f7da9b140f5dfb179f1a80df44/d29d64bcb7135925c7e2b3a163921df8/image.png)

Get a complete view of your brand’s presence across major AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s AI-powered features.

Track key metrics like:

- **AI Visibility Score**: Shows how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers. A low score means potential customers may never encounter your brand while researching on AI platforms, which can directly affect conversion. Available in the [Visibility Overview](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/overview/) tool.
- **Overall Sentiment**: Shows how AI platforms describe your brand. Negative or neutral sentiment can weaken how your brand is perceived in AI recommendations, reducing the chances customers choose you. Available in the [Brand Performance](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/brand-performance/) tool.
- **Brand mentions and citations**: Shows how often your brand is mentioned in AI answers and how frequently AI platforms cite your pages as sources. Consistent appearance signals that AI platforms consider your brand a reliable authority in your category. Available inside the [Visibility Overview](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/overview/) tool.

### Perform a Competitive Analysis

![Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit for AI search visibility tracking - Competitive Analysis report](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ac/fd/acfd6faed801af6eb586b42fc2802277/a4bda00ffb3a30bfab7bb579bdd9d621/image.png)

Compare your AI visibility against key competitors to see the prompts where they show up, and you don’t, and the sources that strengthen their AI visibility.

Here’s what you can uncover using the [Competitor Research tool](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/competitor-research/):

- **Compare overall visibility strength**: Review how your AI Visibility Score stacks up against key competitors to understand which brands AI platforms surface more often in your category.
- **Spot prompt-level gaps**: Identify the prompts where competitors appear, but your brand doesn’t. Targeting these prompts can help you improve your AI footprint and capture the category demand they’re currently benefiting from.
- **Find citation sources competitors benefit from**: See which external domains consistently cite your competitors. This gives you a list of publications and sources to prioritize for outreach or content contributions.

### Discover and Monitor High-Value AI Prompts and Topics

![Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit for AI search visibility tracking - Prompt Research report](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/79/0e/790eeb8476609c869dc67516a34143fb/3b880364c810e0d756c63a2c2407313a/image.png)

Identify the real prompts your audience uses on AI platforms so you can improve your [AI visibility](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-visibility/) and show up when buying decisions are made.

Here’s how to uncover them with our AI Visibility Toolkit:

- **Start with manual prompt research**: Enter a seed prompt inside the [Prompt Research tool](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/prompt-research/) to generate a list of related high-value prompts. This helps you identify the conversations where your brand should appear to capture customer interest.
- **Analyze prompt-level gaps against competitors**:Inside [Competitor Research](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/competitor-research/), find the prompts and topics where your competitors appear but you don’t. Targeting these prompts can help you show up in the same high-intent conversations your competitors benefit from.
- **Understand topic clusters and intent patterns**: Use the [Questions report](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/questions/) to get a comprehensive list of real user questions grouped into themes, along with the intent behind them.

You can then use the [Prompt Tracking tool](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) to follow how your visibility shifts day by day for important prompts.

![Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit for AI search visibility tracking - Prompt Tracking tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/88/4f/884f671438c0e537c815d720ca5a78d4/5910da11ab1ef32b1aacdd964df1cf4d/image.png)

This shows whether your efforts are improving your presence in AI-driven customer journeys.

### Audit Technical Barriers Limiting Your AI Visibility

![img-semblog](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/74/a5/74a5c4d8e7bb5c22487df4a5d26dfb68/f3f9892f42b256714083dd2c94e9eb3e/image.png)

Finally, you can run a full website LLM visibility audit using the [Site Audit tool](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) to identify technical issues that may be preventing AI crawlers from accessing your web pages.

It’s one of the first tools built to diagnose AI-specific crawlability issues.

For example, it can spot issues like:

- Links with no anchor text
- Pages that require content optimization
- Technical issues like [llms.txt](https://www.semrush.com/blog/llms-txt/) not found. This emerging file gives LLMs a clean, structured view of your key content, and adding it can help AI systems interpret your site more effectively.

Running this audit helps you catch and fix the technical issues that quietly limit your AI presence, ensuring the time and effort you invest actually pays off.
