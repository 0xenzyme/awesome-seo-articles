# Types of SEO: What Does Your Site Need to Succeed?

Having a website alone won’t bring traffic. Sometimes, diagnosing why your site is not getting the traffic you want can be difficult.

For that reason, you might consider improving SEO. You could hire an in-house SEO specialist, a marketing agency, or a freelance SEO. You could also do the work yourself.

Understanding the types of SEO can help you choose the right services to boost your website’s visibility. This knowledge can also help you fix issues on your own before passing the rest to a specialist.

## What Are the Types of SEO?

Three main types of SEO exist:

1. **On-page SEO:** Actions you take with website content to improve rankings (for example, using relevant keywords).
2. **Off-page SEO:** Actions that happen outside your website to improve rankings (for example, building backlinks).
3. **Technical SEO:** Actions on the technical side of your site (for example, improving page speed).

Let’s look at each type more closely.

### On-Page SEO

[On-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) focuses on the content of your website. Search engines favor content that is easy to find, easy to understand, and genuinely [helpful](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/08/helpful-content-update).

Examples of on-page SEO services include:

- Assigning a target keyword to each important webpage
- Optimizing page titles for those specific keywords
- Updating item descriptions in an online store
- Adding fresh content to a blog
- Restructuring an old article or service page
- Adding structure to content with correct HTML tags
- Creating a content plan for each stage of a customer journey

If the content on your site doesn’t target keywords or is generally unhelpful, you may need better on-page SEO.

### Off-Page SEO

[Off-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/off-page-seo/) addresses factors that occur outside your website and can influence your site’s rankings. One major off-page factor is high-quality [backlinks](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/backlinks).

Here is an example of how to strengthen off-page SEO: First, you research and publish an informative article about cat allergies on your website. Then, another website cites your article in a study about common household-pet allergies and links to your site.

That link becomes your backlink, which may boost your SEO. Search engines, such as Google, often see backlinks from reputable sites as a positive signal.

Off-page SEO involves more than backlinks. It can include:

- Managing social media
- Conducting outreach and digital PR
- Creating content for other platforms (such as YouTube)
- Managing reviews
- Monitoring spammy backlinks that could hurt SEO

If nobody is visiting your website because nobody is talking about it, you may need stronger off-page SEO.

### Technical SEO

![AD_4nXdkWTQGTt2qb132WVVbkgcSlEnBo1P9MS6POv9Qz8a0rbZ0t1aMSHiSsIHyxxkbLjpfGrmQLGJLNM8ea1vH5iotuIvtRujTaLMe_JUjO1_GcacXk5R7JVlBw8QjSQcrOzye65wNqw?key=2D485SqywaDuM90KOgaynyW2](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/21/72/21728648883eb2656413d5786b2f42f1/15bce81a475cec37cb100a0d8a27bef6/AD_4nXdkWTQGTt2qb132WVVbkgcSlEnBo1P9MS6POv9Qz8a0rbZ0t1aMSHiSsIHyxxkbLjpfGrmQLGJLNM8ea1vH5iotuIvtRujTaLMe_JUjO1_GcacXk5R7JVlBw8QjSQcrOzye65wNqw.png)

[Technical SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/) addresses backend site factors that affect crawlability, user experience (UX), and site speed. Good technical SEO lets both search engines and people navigate your site easily.

Slow-loading pages, broken images, or poor navigation hurt user experience and reflect bad technical SEO.

Common technical SEO tasks include:

- Conducting a technical site audit
- Finding and fixing duplicate content
- Using a robots.txt file to guide Google on how to crawl your site
- Creating a clear sitemap
- Optimizing code and file sizes for faster page loading
- Managing a crawl budget
- Migrating a website
- Making the site mobile-friendly

A key part of technical SEO is ensuring that both humans and search engines can access your important pages. For instance, a clear and useful site architecture helps everyone find and understand your content.

## Other Types of SEO

Beyond these three major SEO categories, other optimization strategies can boost your search engine visibility:

### Image SEO

[Image SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/image-seo/) ensures search engines can understand and rank your images, helping more users find your content.

Images need optimized HTML tags, much like other content on your site. Proper image SEO helps your images appear in features like Google’s image carousels.

### Video SEO

[Video SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/video-seo/) ensures search engines can interpret and rank your videos, guiding more viewers to your content.

Video SEO requires optimization of a video’s title, URL, tags, and description. Adding structured data to your site’s code can also help [search engines display your videos](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video) in rich results.

YouTube automatically adds structured data for key moments, but you can add or customize it on your own site.

Different video SEO techniques can also increase views on YouTube, which is another niche of SEO.

### Ecommerce SEO

[Ecommerce SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ecommerce-seo/) is essential for online stores to rank higher in search results and attract more customers.

Stores that sell products or services online can use ecommerce SEO to increase traffic and boost sales.

Improving an ecommerce site in search engines goes beyond updating product titles and descriptions. It can also involve optimizing a brand on external platforms, such as etsy.com or amazon.com.

Most ecommerce markets are highly competitive, so strong SEO is critical to attract online shoppers.

### Mobile SEO

[Mobile SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/mobile-seo/) is important for reaching smartphone users and ensuring they can easily find and navigate your site.

A popular [study by bankmycell.com](https://www.bankmycell.com/blog/how-many-phones-are-in-the-world) found over 83% of the world owns a smartphone. Brands cannot succeed without making sure their sites work on all platforms.

A main part of mobile search optimization is making it easy for searchers to use your site on their phones. That goes back to technical SEO—optimizing your code so the mobile version of the site is fast and easy to use.

Another aspect of mobile SEO is finding keywords that people search for on their phones, which may differ from desktop searches. If you want to find those keywords, conduct [mobile keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/mobile-keyword-research/).

### Local SEO

[Local SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/) is important for businesses with physical locations to attract nearby customers.

Businesses that have physical locations can use local SEO to get more foot traffic. Optimizing content on your site and on platforms like [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) (also known as Google My Business) can help put your brand in front of a local audience.

## How to Decide Which SEO Services You Need

Effective SEO is subjective because it depends on your audience and your market’s competition. The best SEO plan for an online kid’s clothing store could differ from the best plan for a large hospital.

Because there are many ways to improve SEO, you must decide what works best for your site and your brand.

Here’s how to figure out what kind of SEO services you might need:

### Link Building

Backlinks can boost SEO. One popular SEO service is to create link-worthy content and find the right audience for it. A specialist might do this in several ways:

- Writing public relations releases
- Guest blogging
- Publishing new, interesting research
- Conducting an outreach campaign to build new online connections and backlinks

Part of [link building](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/) is creating material that people want to link to. Another part is researching and contacting potential websites to pitch that content to.

If you’re considering hiring a link building service, ask how they find prospects, what tactics they will use to reach out, and how many links they plan to build for you.

Focusing on high-quality links, not just quantity, is also important.

### Web Design, User Experience, and Web Development

Large-scale changes may be necessary to improve your site’s SEO and create a better experience for visitors. A web developer or designer might work on your site’s:

- Speed and load time
- Indexability and crawlability (the ease with which search engines can find and navigate your site)
- Mobile SEO

They will run a [technical audit](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-audit/) to identify problem areas and then fix them.

Other common services in this area include:

- [Migrating a website](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-migration-checklist/) to a new domain
- Migrating themes
- Finding patterns of when and where visitors exit a site
- Creating a sitemap and robots.txt file
- Creating clean site architecture
- Auditing and cleaning up heavy code
- Fixing duplicate content issues

### Content Marketing (Keyword Strategy, Content Strategy, Content Production)

Content marketing is the process of producing and sharing online material that sparks interest in your products or services without directly promoting them. It helps you build brand awareness, gain trust, and increase conversions by offering valuable information to potential customers.

A strategy alone does not work without actual content production. A good content writer or developer uses the selected keywords to create a piece of content that is optimized for search.

Content specialists may also update old content on your site if it is underperforming. They could improve on-page SEO by suggesting the right words for meta tags.
