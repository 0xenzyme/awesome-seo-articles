# 29 Eye-Opening Google Search Statistics for 2025

Google Search is a digital giant.

But how big is it, exactly? What should you know about its search users’ habits? And how is the platform adapting to recent advancements in AI?

Let’s dive into the latest Google Search statistics to find out.

## Google Statistics Highlights

- There are an estimated 9.5 million Google searches per minute
- Over 60% of searches occur on mobile devices in the U.S.
- AI Overviews appear for 13.14% of all Google searches
- Google accounts for 89.66% of the global search engine market
- Google’s Shopping Graph has over 35 billion product listings
- People use Google Lens for 12 billion searches each month

## General Google Search Statistics

### 1. Google Processes 9.5 Million Searches Every Minute

Google sees more than 5 trillion [search queries](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-query/) annually, which averages out to 9.5 million searches per minute.

On a per-person basis (assuming the United Nations has the most recent global population estimate at [8.2 billion people](https://news.un.org/en/story/2024/07/1151971)), this translates to every person conducting about 610 searches each year.

([Google, 2025](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ai-personalization-and-the-future-of-shopping/))

### 2. Top-Ranking Organic Search Results See a 22.4% Click-Through Rate

Anyone familiar with [SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-basics/) already understands the value of achieving a high position in Google’s search engine results pages (SERPs).

And the stats speak for themselves. Our 2023 data shows that the average click-through rate (CTR) is 22.4% for position 1, 13% for position 2, and 10% for position 3 on mobile.

![Average click-through rate for organic results decreases as you go down search result positions.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b4/dd/b4dd25cdc2cdf49c92502fc7b147b50a/c57067042993581d3eddd6752f43eaea/AD_4nXfw550fRtuu0R-5SwoJGB_v0mbkT5ZHxbvFi7BLrwfKndljvnq0-MvvnFTBtU92OtuTZ0Qw5weNEruzBgyhDr4KohtgXjHsm0afb8uXHfnaCJ73-83bPJMCdfODJlFIZw.png)

It’s important to take this number with a grain of salt. Because search results look much different now—particularly for queries that show [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) (AIOs).

This means it’s reasonable to assume that CTRs have changed.

([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/blog/state-of-search-2023/))

### 3. Google’s Search Index Is Over 100,000,000 GB in Size

Every time you perform a search, Google scours hundreds of billions of webpages stored in its [index](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-index/) to return relevant results.

This index is built through Google’s web crawlers, which systematically move from webpage to webpage via links. And make records of what they find.

And since content on the web is always changing, Google’s crawlers are constantly at work to ensure the data in the index stays up to date.

([Google, Date Not Specified](https://www.google.com/search/howsearchworks/how-search-works/organizing-information/))

### 4. Over 60% of Searches Occur on Mobile Devices in the U.S.

Desktop devices no longer account for the majority of searches. And Google has updated its systems accordingly.

In October 2023, [Google confirmed](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/10/mobile-first-is-here) that its switch to [mobile-first indexing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/mobile-first-indexiing/) was finally complete. In other words, Google now crawls and indexes pages from the perspective of a mobile browser.

This means mobile webpages must be optimized to perform well in search results.

([Statista, 2022](https://www.statista.com/statistics/297137/mobile-share-of-us-organic-search-engine-visits/))

### 5. Almost 27% of Google’s Desktop Traffic Comes from the U.S.

The U.S. accounts for more Google desktop visits than any other country.

India is in second place, accounting for 4.51% of visits. And Brazil comes in third, at 4.37%.

![Desktop traffic to Google by country shows the UK and Japan holding the fourth and fifth positions.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ef/92/ef929dfc4d6cd7afe30fe287beeeea53/eaf4f9493c18cf9c887e9832b166e343/AD_4nXdvZzgF9_Psh0qIUKUr4Fxm_43PJh7AbzWM15_nO5ZSQeKJNXYNR-5sNDlvYFWn4kbjf1iYHqgxLmrbxMKoZcwE5aKE7FabqfRbQrJ4V_sR-gqp_PN76J0ew78tVtibzg5CNk1qQg.png)

([Statista, 2024](https://www.statista.com/statistics/276737/distribution-of-visitors-to-googlecom-by-country/))

### 6. The Most Googled Terms in 2025 Are ‘YouTube,’ ‘ChatGPT,’ & ‘Facebook’

The term “youtube” receives almost 1.4 billion monthly searches globally, according to Semrush data collected in February 2025.

Meanwhile, “chatgpt” and “facebook” receive approximately 618 million monthly searches each.

([Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-searched-keywords-google/))

### 7. Most Google Search Queries Are 3 to 4 Words Long

The average length of a [search query](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-query/) in the U.S. is 3.4 words. Which is a slight increase from about 3.3 two years ago.

In the U.K., the average query length is closer to 3.2 words. In Germany, it’s a bit lower.

([Growth Memo, 2025](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/do-google-ai-overviews-really-change))

## AI-Focused Google Search Statistics

### 8. AI Overviews Appear for 13.14% of Google Searches

AI Overviews are a [SERP feature](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/) that now appear for 13.14% of all searches, based on Semrush data from March 2025.

This portion has increased over time—AI Overviews were only showing for 6.49% of queries in January 2025.

![Share of queries triggering AI Overviews about doubled from January 2025 to March 2025.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2f/90/2f909e6bad3af13122ad45bbc7c2188a/d8f32161dff7f5a45184416eb5505536/AD_4nXfYW8FvwR-80z-TrVh4XVrvhePyUZgMzMUiryKSSdDnjQAsN1ED84VxMGRkTvGv7xQbp2QWG-nKfbpqwjustcbd_dd7MZOwKSdbC5zx2NOR7sIP9S7hF9pDA4BTEamluhFTUdVo.png)

([Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/))

### 9. Google’s AI Overviews List an Average of 5 Sources

AI Overviews link to about five sources per query, according to a study of over 400,000 searches.

Of the sources mentioned in AIOs, 52% also appear in the top 10 organic search results.

This indicates there’s an opportunity for sources that aren’t on the first page to gain more visibility.

And given what we know about how Google’s [Navboost](https://www.semrush.com/blog/navboost/) system rewards positive user interactions, any sources listed in AI Overviews that don’t already rank on the first page may be able to climb closer to page 1 if users find the content valuable.

([Surfer, 2024](https://surferseo.com/blog/ai-overviews-study/))

### 10. Circle to Search Is Available on 200 Million Devices

Google’s [Circle to Search](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/14508957?hl=en) feature that’s available for certain Android phones lets users circle, highlight, or tap anything (images, videos, or text) on their mobile screens to initiate a Google search.

It’s now available on 200 million Android devices. And users who have tried the feature go on to use it to start 10% of their searches.

This feature is blurring the lines between different types of search. And stands to make search experiences more seamless.

([Google, 2024](https://abc.xyz/assets/e0/99/7331ada54f6a9f65d08eeadd25d5/2024-q4-earnings-transcript.pdf))

### 11. Google’s Global Traffic Declined 7.91% After ChaptGPT’s Release

We worked with Statista to compare search engine traffic following ChatGPT’s release and found that Google’s global traffic decreased 7.91% from 2023 to 2024.

Other search engines saw similar declines. But AI- and privacy-focused search engines actually saw increases.

![Search engine traffic comparison after ChatGPT release shows domains like search.brave.com, naver.com, tiktok.com, YouTube, and more.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1d/75/1d752796ee196259156de0afeeebfa63/97286bf86ee9faeb5fb4001d108a7577/AD_4nXdIRSt6jPrGrRtYC4EVOWRHTFFGQJ_VjN_oR42sD_X_BrgOhm-tsHijwLkVtpnMJrmCFaT-sw0Bob_JTE-3eEr5dyxstcBD3yNYd99Fn53ilbZZsH7ji1hFzQcXtqnsfSNajMP1.png)

([Semrush, 2024](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-report/))

## Google’s Search Engine Dominance Statistics

### 12. Google Accounts for 89.66% of the Global Search Engine Market

Despite increased use of [ChatGPT and its alternatives](https://www.semrush.com/contentshake/content-marketing-blog/chatgpt-alternatives/), Google Search still has a 89.66% market share.

Bing is the closest competitor with a 3.88% global search engine market share.

![Yandex, Yahoo!, and DuckDuckGo hold the remaining search engine market share.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/31/4e/314e0de7e1d4e5686913d049f97705bb/e1c26a8941be52a9eef7b4898ce586eb/AD_4nXdaw80cuJBzGs8Aze0h4Nyq2H-9GNmMBq2CklK7oAs4_oCoiLGI2dTIxgLTqLEey1JGQCo9WXn1W8RyBN5f1I7M19JEiBMSFRqw8COQQe0lQDSwT8tvi_BxyAWSbNz8Nv_wWryjRA.png)

([StatCounter Global Stats, 2024](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share))

### 13. Google Sites Generated 234 Billion in Ad Revenue in 2024

Google website’s ad revenues reached an all-time high of 234 billion in 2024.

And these revenue figures have increased every year since 2001—it’s actually grown by over 334,000% since that time.

([Statista, 2024](https://www.statista.com/statistics/266242/advertising-revenue-of-google-sites/))

## Desktop vs. Mobile Search Statistics

### 14. Users Take More Time to Make a Decision When Searching on Mobile

On desktop, about 45% of users’ decisions on what to do next occurs within five seconds of searching. On mobile, this figure drops to 33%.

![Number of seconds per user decision chart shows mobile users make decisions faster.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/06/e1/06e19416d236e95fe903c2772d779edb/4833316197f0e149fe166c512fcd94df/AD_4nXd8cCkM0c6rSyUo1uUkNksmo0mkiedF4JGYLrC4fcXbk41JtZB5vPbM4AfT_B670_nSoXfQ1u5sRMwA95eTuNV0Cee7IbhteZiYr8G2WHceG16A6V2eoxJf061bgAfe1Owg0WCgSA.png)

This could be because mobile users have a smaller screen to work with, meaning they spend more time scrolling through Google’s results.

([Semrush, 2022](https://www.semrush.com/blog/zero-clicks-study/))

### 15. Users Reformulate Their Initial Searches More Frequently on Mobile

Google users often refine their initial searches when they don’t find what they expect in the search results.

On desktop, users change their queries 17.9% of the time. On mobile, they do it 29.3% of the time.

([Semrush, 2022](https://www.semrush.com/blog/zero-clicks-study/))

## Consumer Behavior Search Statistics

### 16. Over Half of U.S. Consumers Start with Google When Shopping for a Product Online

Google is the second most popular choice for new product searches, just slightly behind Amazon.

The next most popular destinations for beginning product searches are Walmart, YouTube, and Facebook.

([Jungle Scout, 2025](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/reports/consumer-trends-2025/))

### 17. U.S. Consumers Say Search Ads Are the Most Influential Promotions

Search ads like those that appear on Google have the greatest impact on consumers compared to other types of advertising—only Gen Z found other types to be more influential.

This is useful insight for marketers who are looking to make the most of their advertising budgets.

([Jungle Scout, 2025](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/reports/consumer-trends-2025/))

### 18. Online Shoppers Click on 3D Images 50% More Than Static Ones

Google encourages merchants to make the most of its advanced [3D shopping capabilities](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13675100?hl=en).

![Search results for a popular product allows you to rotate the product with 360 degree view.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/57/c4/57c49da549e3372d93f593deddec4a0f/bf0158a3d74bc3afaab35b4dbdce81d3/AD_4nXc_QghwA_9iWFtCp64Vv2I1C40BgLMFZXQrvKmeZIyXYwW99ZUlpLBsFA1_8Snd0-PYsrkxjyYFCoa-XtBhPFgnQy3LwTNXvOcieMMOgkyv78VU4E3H0LqwcWQ09TYBXhgBAROw8w.png)

But 3D shopping experiences are currently only available in the U.S. and are limited to shoes and home goods.

([Google, 2022](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/marketing-strategies/search/search-on-updates/))

### 19. Google’s Shopping Graph Contains Over 35 Billion Product Listings

Google’s Shopping Graph is an AI-powered, real-time data set storing billions of product listings.

And over 1.8 billion of those listings are refreshed every hour to give users the most up-to-date, reliable product results.

([Google, 2023](https://blog.google/products/search/generative-ai-search/))

## Local Google Search Statistics

### 20. About 75% of Consumers Use Google When Looking for Local Business Information

This data on individuals from the U.S., France, Germany, and the U.K. shows people rely heavily on Google to discover information about businesses in their areas.

For this reason, businesses that maximize their online presence through [local SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/) stand to significantly increase local traffic, awareness, and even sales.

([Statista, 2021](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1260363/consumers-using-search-engines-to-find-local-business-info/))

### 21. Over 20% of People in the U.S. Use the Internet to Find a Local Business Each Day

And 32% use it to research a local business multiple times per week.

This widespread use of Google to find local businesses underscores the need to optimize your [local search](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-search/) presence.

([Statista, 2023](https://www.statista.com/statistics/315709/online-local-business-search-frequency-us-canada/))

### 22. Google Maps Searches for ‘Shopping Near Me’ Increased Globally by Over 100% in 2022

Google’s traditional search results aren’t the only way to discover information about local businesses—Google Maps is also a go-to source for nearby business information.

Creating and optimizing your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) (GBP)—pictured below—helps ensure your business is visible to local Google Maps users.

### 23. Businesses with Verified GBPs Get About 50 Calls Per Month from Their Profiles

Businesses get about 595 calls annually from their verified [Google Business Profiles](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/). Which is about 50 per month.

Adding multiple contact options to your GBP and website makes life easier for potential customers. And you could get more inquiries, visits, and sales as a result.

([Birdeye, 2025](https://birdeye.com/blog/state-of-google-business-profiles/))

### 24. Almost 90% of Consumers Use Google Maps

Consumers prefer Google Maps over Apple Maps—88% use the former, but only 12% use the latter.

Businesses seem to feel the same way. Over half of businesses included in the same survey haven’t claimed their listing in Apple Maps.

([BrightLocal, 2023](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/are-businesses-using-apple-business-connect/))

## Google Visual and Voice Search Statistics

### 28. Most Voice Searches Are Related to Weather, Music, and News

Weather-related queries are the most frequent types of voice searches across the U.S., the U.K., and Germany.

Interestingly, more than half of voice search users in the U.S. have used the technology for food delivery—compared with just 36% in Germany.

([Statista, 2022](https://www.statista.com/statistics/879943/us-voice-assistant-usage-purchasing/))

### 29. People Use Google Lens for 12 Billion Searches Every Month

Google Lens is a visual search tool that uses AI to detect objects and text in images.

For example, if you point your camera at a famous landmark, Google Lens can recognize and provide facts about what it sees.

The number of searches made using Google Lens has increased by four times since 2021.

([Google, 2023](https://blog.google/products/search/generative-ai-search/))
