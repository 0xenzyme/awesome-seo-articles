# 65 Customer Retention Statistics You Need to Know in 2025

Holding on to customers is a top priority for most businesses. And it’s much easier to do that when you truly understand what your customers want and expect from your business.

Customer retention stats offer valuable insights into customer expectations, preferences, and behavior. They reveal the common factors that keep customers coming back and what tends to drive them away.

In this article, we’ll share 65 of the most important customer retention statistics.

The more you align your business strategies and services with these insights, the higher your chances of delivering a stellar customer experience and building a strong base of loyal, repeat buyers.

## Key Takeaways

The customer retention statistics we’ll discuss below highlight several important points, including:

- Customer retention costs significantly less than [acquiring new customers](https://www.semrush.com/blog/customer-acquisition-guide/)
- Existing customers tend to spend more (and more often) than new customers
- Improving customer experience is a major driver of customer loyalty
- A well-structured loyalty program can be highly effective at enhancing customer retention and increasing sales
- Customer retention rates vary significantly across different industries

With these important points in mind, let’s dive into the numbers.

## General Customer Retention Statistics

The statistics below provide an overview of the costs involved in customer retention, and shed some light on repeat customer buying habits:

1. Customer churn costs U.S. providers $168 billion per year ([CallMiner](https://learning.callminer.com/c/whitepaper-churn-index-2020?x=CFl8z6&lx=amFxJO&xs=225891#page=1))
2. U.S. companies could save over $35 billion per year by focusing on keeping their existing customers happy ([CallMiner](https://learning.callminer.com/c/whitepaper-churn-index-2020?x=CFl8z6&lx=amFxJO&xs=225891#page=1))
3. In 2013, ecommerce businesses paid an average of $9 to acquire a new customer. By 2022, this had risen to $29 (a 222% increase). ([SimplicityDX](https://www.simplicitydx.com/blogs/press-release-brands-losing-a-record-29-for-each-new-customer-acquired))
4. Businesses have a 60% to 70% chance of selling to an existing customer, while for a new prospect it’s just 5% to 20% ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/patrickhull/2013/12/06/tools-for-entrepreneurs-to-retain-clients/), quoting the book “Marketing Metrics”)
5. Repeat customers spend 67% more than new customers ([BIA Advisory Services](https://www.bia.com/small-business-owners-shift-investment-from-customer-acquisition-to-customer-engagement-new-report-by-manta-and-biakelsey/))
6. Some 52% of customers have gone out of their way to buy from their favorite brand ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
7. Two-fifths of companies place an equal focus on customer retention and acquisition ([Invesp](https://www.invespcro.com/blog/customer-acquisition-retention/))
8. As much as 44% of companies still don’t calculate their customer retention rate ([CustomerGauge](https://content.customergauge.com/retention-management-to-combat-churn))
9. Almost three-quarters of consumers feel loyalty towards a particular brand or company ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
10. Over 55% of consumers are loyal to a brand because they love the product ([Yotpo](https://www.yotpo.com/blog/what-makes-consumers-loyal-to-a-brand/))
11. A third of consumers say it takes three purchases to create brand loyalty ([Yotpo](https://www.yotpo.com/blog/purchases-brand-loyalty/))

## Impact of Customer Retention on Profits

Perhaps the most important impact customer retention can have on a business involves its profits. The statistics below illustrate the power of customer retention on a business’s bottom line:

12. Some 84% of companies that work to improve customer experience notice an uplift in revenue ([Dimension Data](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-research-from-dimension-data-reveals-uncomfortable-cx-truths-300433878.html))
13. Customer retention is vital for the 61% of small businesses that say over half their revenue comes from repeat customers ([BIA Advisory Services](https://www.bia.com/small-business-owners-shift-investment-from-customer-acquisition-to-customer-engagement-new-report-by-manta-and-biakelsey/))
14. Small ecommerce businesses generate 35% of their revenue from the top 5% of loyal, repeat customers ([Smile.io](https://blog.smile.io/repeat-customers-profitable/))
15. After buying from your online store for the first time, a customer has a 27% chance of buying again. After a second purchase, there’s a 49% chance they’ll buy again. And after a third purchase, the likelihood increases to 62%. ([Smile.io](https://blog.smile.io/repeat-customers-profitable/))

## Customer Service Statistics

One of the most important factors that can impact customer retention is how your business handles customer service. See the statistics below to understand just how vital a positive customer experience can be:

34. Over 90% of consumers think that a positive customer service experience makes them more likely to buy again ([Salesforce](https://c1.sfdcstatic.com/content/dam/web/en_us/www/documents/research/salesforce-state-of-the-connected-customer-4th-ed.pdf))
35. Around 83% of U.S. consumers believe good customer service is either an important or a critically important driver of brand loyalty ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/676798/brand-loyalty-factors-usa/))
36. Some 58% of consumers say customer service is very important to their loyalty to a brand ([Microsoft](https://info.microsoft.com/rs/157-GQE-382/images/2018StateofGlobalCustomerServiceReport.pdf))
37. Customers that receive “value enhancement” during service interactions have an 82% probability of staying with that company ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/customer-service-support/insights/customer-loyalty))
38. In 2021, 58% of customers said their expectations of customer service were higher than in 2020 ([Talkdesk](https://www.talkdesk.com/resources/infographics/the-customer-experience-cx-revolution-in-retail/))
39. Over 60% of customers believe the ability to resolve issues quickly is one of the most important aspects of a good customer service experience. Over 40% think the same thing about 24/7 support. ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
40. More than half of customers cite long hold times as one of the most frustrating aspects of a bad customer experience ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
41. Nearly 70% of customers are annoyed when their call gets transferred between departments ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
42. Around 44% of customers want businesses to ensure that customer service and support teams resolve their issues effectively and efficiently ([CallMiner](https://learning.callminer.com/c/whitepaper-churn-index-2020?x=CFl8z6&lx=amFxJO&xs=225891#page=1))

45. Around 40% of millennials and Gen Z customers typically resolve issues with a company through messaging ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
46. Two-fifths of 16- to 34-year-olds are more likely to shop with an online retailer if they can track returns in one app, compared to just 33% of 45- to 54-year-olds and 25% of those over 55 ([Klarna](https://www.klarna.com/assets/sites/3/2019/11/14080024/6310-KLA-Re-thinking-Returns_DD1-28.02.191.pdf))
47. Surveys show 46% of U.S. consumers, 40% of U.K. consumers, and 88% of Brazilian consumers have a more favorable view of brands that respond to questions and complaints on social media ([Microsoft](https://info.microsoft.com/rs/157-GQE-382/images/EN-CNTNT-Report-DynService-2017-global-state-customer-service-en-au.pdf))

## Customer Retention Behavior Statistics

Understanding customer behavior can show you what people prioritize when it comes to staying loyal to one company or another. The data below sheds light on the main reasons customers tend to stick or switch:

49. A survey found that 31% of consumers have switched brands because a company was lying about product performance ([YouGov](https://today.yougov.com/technology/articles/37498-how-do-data-leaks-affect-customer-loyalty))
50. Poor customer service has caused 61% of consumers to switch brands ([Microsoft](https://info.microsoft.com/rs/157-GQE-382/images/2018StateofGlobalCustomerServiceReport.pdf))
51. Almost 74% of consumers are likely to switch brands after a bad call center experience ([CallMiner](https://learning.callminer.com/c/whitepaper-churn-index-2020?x=CFl8z6&lx=amFxJO&xs=225891#page=1))
52. About 50% of consumers would switch to a competitor after one bad customer experience. In the case of more than one bad experience, this increases to 80%. ([Zendesk](https://d1eipm3vz40hy0.cloudfront.net/pdf/cxtrends/cx-trends-2020-full-report.pdf))
53. Research shows that 62% of shoppers won’t buy from a retailer that doesn’t offer free returns. And 84% of shoppers won’t buy from the same retailer again after a poor returns experience. ([Klarna](https://www.klarna.com/assets/sites/3/2019/11/14080024/6310-KLA-Re-thinking-Returns_DD1-28.02.191.pdf))
54. In 2020, 81% of consumers switched providers, despite only 63.9% planning to do so at the start of the year ([CallMiner](https://learning.callminer.com/c/whitepaper-churn-index-2020?x=CFl8z6&lx=amFxJO&xs=225891#page=1))
55. Over 60% of consumers blamed high or increased prices for switching service providers in 2020, with 34.6% doing so because they felt they weren’t being treated fairly ([CallMiner](https://learning.callminer.com/c/whitepaper-churn-index-2020?x=CFl8z6&lx=amFxJO&xs=225891#page=1))
