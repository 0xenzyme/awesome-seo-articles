# Top 40 Trending EV Companies (Q1 2025)

We analyzed the organic traffic growth of over 8000 websites to discover the hottest trending EV companies. At the top, you’ll find Green V, Vayve, Zeekr, En Plus, and EV Joints.

To find the top trending EV companies in 2025 we calculated the percentage growth of organic traffic to their websites between January 1, 2024 and January 1, 2025 using [Ahrefs API](https://docs.ahrefs.com/docs/api/reference/introduction). Organic traffic growth is a strong indicator of a startup’s momentum because it reflects increasing brand awareness, customer interest, and effective digital marketing strategies.

We also divided the list into electric vehicle makers (all kinds: cars, bikes, bicycles, etc.) and other companies in the EV sector (battery makers, charging station providers, etc.) to give you top companies throughout the entire EV ecosystem.

## Top 5 trending EV makers

Here’s a quick look at the top 5 electric vehicle makers with the greatest growth in organic traffic last year (graphs come from [Ahrefs’ Site Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/site-explorer)).

### 1. Vayve Mobility

**Year founded:** 2021

**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra, India

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 5,367%

![Vayve Mobility organic growth.](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/vayve-mobility-organic-growth-.png)

[Vayve Mobility](https://evayve.com) is an Indian company that has developed ‘Eva,’ a compact solar-electric city car designed for urban commuting. Eva features a sleek design, seating for two adults and one child, and offers a range of up to 250 km.

### 2. Zeekr

**Year founded:** 2021

**Location:** Ningbo, Zhejiang, China

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 3,848%

[Zeekr](https://zeekrlife.com) is a Chinese premium electric vehicle brand under the Geely Group, established to compete in the high-end EV market. Their lineup includes models like the Zeekr 001, a sleek shooting brake EV with a range of up to 372 miles per charge, and the Zeekr X, a compact SUV. Zeekr has expanded into European markets, with plans to introduce additional models tailored for global consumers.

### 3. Emotorad

**Year founded:** 2020

**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra, India

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 1,453%

[Emotorad](https://www.emotorad.com) is an Indian electric bike (e-bike) manufacturer that operates in Europe, India, Japan, Australia, and the UAE. They offer a wide range of e-bike models catering to various types of riders, from urban commuters to adventure seekers.

### 4. Tembo

**Year founded:** 2016

**Location:** Eindhoven, Netherlands

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 1,311%

[Tembo](https://temboelv.com/) is a Dutch company specializing in the electric conversion of fleets for ruggedized and customized off-road and on-road use. They provide electric utility vehicles (EUVs) for industries such as mining, agriculture, energy utilities, defense, and public transportation. Tembo’s mission is to support clients in decarbonizing their operations by offering safe, high-performance electric vehicles tailored to demanding environments.

### 5. Leapmotor

**Year founded:** 2015

**Location:** Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 1,230%

[Leapmotor](http://www.leapmotor.com) is a Chinese electric vehicle manufacturer that focuses on producing affordable and innovative EVs. Their product lineup includes models like the T03, a compact city car ideal for urban driving, and the C10, a versatile vehicle suitable for longer journeys.

## Top 5 trending EV technology & services companies

Here’s a quick look at the top 5 companies providing electric vehicle technology and services with the greatest growth in organic traffic last year.

### 1. GreenV

**Year founded:** 2021

**Location:** São Paulo, Brazil

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 16,380%

[GreenV](http://www.greenv.com.br/) is a Brazilian startup specializing in electric mobility technology. They focus on the distribution and installation of electric vehicle chargers and develop innovative solutions to advance electromobility in Brazil. GreenV partners with leading automotive brands like BMW, Audi, and Porsche for charger installations.

### 2. EN Plus Tech

**Year founded:** 2015

**Location:** Shenzhen, China

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 2,742%

[EN Plus](http://www.en-plustech.com/) is a leading manufacturer of EV chargers and a provider of one-stop EV charging solutions. They offer a comprehensive product portfolio including AC and DC chargers, power modules, and software platforms for charging management. They serve home, business, and fast-charging applications and have a global presence in over 50 countries.

### 3. EV Joints

**Year founded:** 2021 (incorporated November 25, 2021)

**Location:** Mumbai, Maharashtra, India

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 2,628%

[EVJoints](http://evjoints.com/) is an Indian company operating a platform to aggregate EV charging stations across different networks. They provide a mobile app that allows EV users to find and access charging stations, compare prices, and manage charging history. They aim to simplify the EV charging experience for users and help expand the EV ecosystem.

### 4. V2Charge

**Year founded:** 2015

**Location:** Spain

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 2,315%

[V2Charge](https://v2charge.com/) is a Spanish company that designs and manufactures charging stations for electric vehicles. They focus on innovative and connected charging solutions for both individuals and businesses, emphasizing technology, design, and sustainability. They offer a range of “e-Chargers” and related software for efficient energy management.

### 5. Einride

**Year founded:** 2016

**Location:** Stockholm, Sweden

**Organic traffic growth (12mo):** 2,311%

[Einride](http://www.einride.tech) is a Swedish technology company specializing in digital, electric, and autonomous freight solutions. They design and implement state-of-the-art technologies to help global companies achieve cleaner transportation, smarter charging, and safer autonomy. Einride’s clients include major brands such as Lidl, Oatly, GE Appliances, and Electrolux.
