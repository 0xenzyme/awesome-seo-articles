# 7 SEO Certifications Real Learners Recommend

An SEO certification is generally known as a credential awarded for successful completion of a search engine optimization (SEO) assessment or course.

By earning SEO certifications from reputable providers, you can improve your SEO knowledge and demonstrate relevant skills to clients or employers.

Here are seven SEO certifications that real learners recommend:

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Provider** | **Course** | **Price\*** | **Length** | **Best for** |
| Semrush | SEO Essentials with Semrush | Free | 1 hour 52 minutes | SEO beginners seeking a quick and practical way to get started |
| Udemy | SEO Training Masterclass 2025: Beginner to Advanced SEO | $109.99 | 18 hours 55 minutes | Beginners looking for a comprehensive SEO overview |
| HubSpot | SEO Certification Course | Free | 3 hours 35 minutes | Beginners looking to learn SEO basics |
| Blue Array | SEO Manager Certification | $667 | 30 hours+ | New or aspiring in-house SEO managers |
| CXL | SEO Course Bundle | $599 | 18 hours 20 minutes | Intermediate to advanced B2B marketers |
| Digital Marketing Institute (DMI) | SEO Online | $445 | 11 hours | Marketers looking to improve their employability |
| Coursera/UC Davis | Google SEO Fundamentals | $59 if completed in one month | 13 hours 26 minutes | Marketers seeking a mid-length Google SEO course |

*\*Please see the sections below for more detailed pricing information*

Keep reading to find out what these SEO certifications cover and why learners like them.

## 4. Blue Array: SEO Manager Certification

Blue Array is an SEO agency based in the U.K. that offers an [SEO Manager Certification](https://www.bluearrayacademy.com/courses/seo-manager-certification) program through its Blue Array Academy. Modules cover SEO fundamentals and specialist topics like ecommerce SEO, international SEO, and Google News optimization.

- **Instructors**: 13 SEO experts, including [Lucinda Wood](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucinda-wood/), [Tom Pool](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tom-pool/), and [Kim Dewe](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kimdewe/)
- **Length**: 30+ hours of content
- **Price**: £497 (approximately $668)

The program is great for SEOs who learn through doing, as it provides a series of tasks to complete. For example, you’re required to configure [Google Search Console](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-search-console/) and perform a [keyword gap analysis](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-gap-analysis/).

Learners also receive templates to help them create an SEO roadmap, technical backlog, and more.

To earn your SEO certification, you need to score at least 80% on multiple knowledge tests.

Senior SEO Executive [Laura Vaduva](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laura-vaduva-22584611b/) earned her SEO Manager Certificate in October 2024.

She says: “I took the course to refresh my SEO knowledge on areas I haven't been working on lately and to gain more knowledge on managing an in-house SEO team. I'm currently aspiring for a manager role.

“For me personally, the templates were the most useful part, as I've recently started to offer part-time freelance services in addition to my current in-house role. The course provided me with the clarity I needed.”

## 7. Coursera/UC Davis: Google SEO Fundamentals

[Google SEO Fundamentals](https://www.coursera.org/learn/seo-fundamentals) is a certification course created by the University of California, Davis (ranked #9 in [U.S. News list of Top Public Schools](https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges/rankings/national-universities/top-public)) and offered through Coursera.

- **Instructor**: [Rebekah May](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rebekahmay/)
- **Length**: 13 hours
- **Price**: $59 (if you earn your certificate in one month)

To earn your certification, you need to complete four modules covering on-page seo, off-page SEO, technical SEO, and keyword research.

Each module includes videos, readings, assignments, and discussion prompts to keep you engaged and reinforce your learning.

The course has a 4.8-star average rating from over 3.6K reviews.

A recent reviewer writes:

“The Google SEO Fundamentals course on Coursera is a well-structured and insightful introduction to search engine optimization. It covers essential topics like on-page and off-page SEO, technical SEO, and keyword research in a simple and easy-to-understand manner. The video lectures and quizzes help reinforce key concepts effectively.

“One of the best aspects of this course is that it provides a strong foundation for beginners. The explanations are clear, and the real-world examples make it easier to grasp SEO strategies.”

You can find more SEO certification programs on the [Coursera website](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/seo).
