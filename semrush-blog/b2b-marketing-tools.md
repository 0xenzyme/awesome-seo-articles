# 22 Best B2B Marketing Tools to Use in 2025

B2B marketing tools can give you the data and features to reach more people than your business size would usually allow.

In this article, we’ve gathered the best software to use for research, content creation, automation, collaboration, and other crucial actions for your marketing campaigns.

Here are 22 tools to improve your reach, engagement, and results.

With a Semrush account, you get access to over [55 tools and reports, along with](https://www.semrush.com/features/) a database of over 26 billion keywords for 121 countries, 808 million web domains, and over 43 trillion backlinks.

Some of the best Semrush tools to start with are Site Audit, Keyword Magic Tool, and Market Explorer.

Here’s how they work:

### Site Audit

The [Site Audit](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) tool analyzes the health of your website for issues that affect search performance and user experience.

You can use it to see where your website struggles and fix problems before they become crises.

Enter your URL and hit “**Start Site Audit**.”

The tool scans your site for over 140 issues, such as duplicate meta descriptions, slow load speeds, and broken internal links. When the report is ready, click the “**Issues**” tab to review the top errors and warnings.

The tool breaks down website issues into “Errors,” “Warnings,” and “Notices.”

![Overview of Semrush Site Audit Issues tab](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/69/45/69458bb46fa4cdf66baebdf31adec4b1/a2c1a60880054d7ee16e00e0f26dee98/image.png)

Prioritize addressing issues in the “Errors” section as they pose immediate threats to your website’s search performance or usability.

Then, move on to issues in the “Warnings” section. These issues are less harmful to your site, but fixing them will still help your website perform better.

![Site Audit issues dashboard](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1c/b9/1cb94f1e5300595ab7b482fb40bdc9d5/0ed1b02068e631e2d0e029f577b4ee79/ecQi-lNMaZ8EEzd6VwcqvEQ5m-gQt2Ns-31uxbICcAdfXMApXSR4y1rVaR55UDr5Ani38k6Qa1UhCw118gwrD7R25YFNfbngyiED77xknPMvtZKSG7Va9yW_gGV2r2FqwBIE0sLvzgmQYH22f5jXSaY.png)

Click “**Why and how to fix it**” next to any error or warning to learn more about the issue and how to solve it.

These tips explain how each issue affects your website, why it’s important to fix them, and what action to take next.

![Why and how to fix issues pop-up in Site Audit](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1f/c4/1fc4568dd13c3d557695aa5f81744048/dca061e53c308c285b039d987bd04f68/xSyfH1sLOj095HHsJvBh-VGMv3ZvypzhXYE6dSUV1mhBS9tvYJghsWZO3x-26CZbNzGyCQZufpLXe7lX1UsNkANiInitq1gsGi2jihD4DcNIl2ln4xgxVEWOYMZjzNfpkOWVCUpoziMC35Fizgz4aog.png)

### Keyword Magic Tool

The [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/start) is a keyword research tool with over 25 billion keywords in its database.

Use it to get topic ideas and keyword data.

Type in a word or phrase related to your brand. And the tool will generate a list of related keywords.

![Keyword Magic Tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/be/18/be184f2f65abda2ecacfa3fe20194b36/924437e9331a9b83c65452e0261ea5dc/image.png)

The Keyword Magic Tool sorts the results by metrics such as [search volume](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-search-volume/), [search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/), and [keyword difficulty](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-difficulty/). Click on any metric to rearrange results.

For example, click “**KD%**” to sort the list by keyword difficulty. Like this:

![Personal Keyword Difficulty](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e5/69/e56961eeeb2cc85bc535bff47ea355ea/0cc557f341e69dfe409157871f7a6af0/image.png)

Enter your domain into the AI-powered search box to get keyword metrics specific to your website. And hit enter.

![Keyword Magic Tool with Personal Keyword Difficulty](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4d/1d/4d1dd1051510707ac160530399ce49e3/f8445f4819e8799189e5c0532bb4154a/image.png)

The tool will generate a [Personalized Keyword Difficulty](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-keyword-research-updates/#how-are-these-new-metrics-calculated) score (PKD %). Use this to identify keywords that are easier for your website to rank well for.

Targeting keywords with a low keyword difficulty and high search volume can help you get pages ranked in search engines faster

It helps you identify competitors, understand their traffic sources, and learn about their target audiences. You can use this information to learn how your site (and its performance) compares to others like it.

To start finding competitors, go to the tool and enter your competitors into the space provided.

If you don’t know who your competitors are, click “**Find Competitors**,” enter your domain, and click “**Research a market**.”

![An Audience Overview dashboard](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/05/b3/05b371c6870357e37c728588046f63a4/ae3215fc5a6b4e7816c5771cb0001c84/oEND8Q8MFrLZSwdB8VtO6eJjSQUQSX7DI-nU45Sw8cCcTUtCw3h3_sf6q2gljNLy2jADcuoSPHIWnoOmqf3ZhIIUzKMLyHNfh7VQnBVVHYBIA_aGVo64Yk9e-n2TGu778xFB7Q6Do4B1x5Mp4nEus84.png)

Use what you learn about your competitors’ tactics and audience behaviors to fine-tune your marketing strategies.

## 3. Media Monitoring: Track Your Online Brand Mentions

The [Media Monitoring](https://www.semrush.com/apps/media-monitoring/) app lets you track what people are saying about your brand online. You can use it to measure the success of your PR campaigns, identify reputational threats, and build better relationships with brand ambassadors.

Or, you can use it to track your competitors’ mentions. This provides valuable insights into what’s working for your rivals—and what isn’t. So you can shape a more effective marketing and communications strategy.

To get started, enter the name of your brand in the “Keyword” section. Then, choose your language and click “**Create Project**.”

![Setting up project for "Salesforce" keyword in Media Monitoring app](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/33/df/33df9860571dcd4bbea3037401419547/ce7e1358d89ffa438e4a878b764e13d2/RUzfZ0xPrdpzRCpEqOFHNndkxpwk8AmWWVdSkeTE6c2hERMIlP-oQmJ18BRQG7I94wqjhMmmh7m5D3GPXNHfRT_Y5fFYAa3y89ERiz9mJt3wIqE_1gd2wPDUv-imgN7Vobw6s8pqvB_xhtygSOEVuMs.png)

You can then add other keywords to track (e.g., misspellings of your brand name). Or use required and excluded keywords to make your results more specific.

For example, if there’s another company with a similar name to yours, you can exclude the names of their products or services. To reduce the risk that their mentions show in your results.

!["Excluded keywords (optional)" field](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b7/ee/b7ee99f559f2a90f253bb66b4e6a7582/a1acfe52d169cf37f5f3a0e11a2a3b83/hbPZ5I2PQZysMLGjCESz44JL1FVUAOlYegxx8GJIkvOr34JpRvtdEs_ad2hyfUqqH58H_60pInLaPU_i2xoSIg0lYXiea8xJ5UUL0O7b8-5jI7kVR4Z3Q0MrTZPKRmbU16pyh-_UP1XvRJu5_i2Lg4Y.png)

Once that’s sorted, head to the “**Mentions**” tab and select the time range you’re interested in to see a full breakdown of your mentions.

Each one is automatically assigned positive, neutral, or negative sentiment. And you can use the filters on the right to focus on specific kinds of results.

For example, you can look for positive mentions in news articles and blog posts. These are opportunities to acquire valuable backlinks.

If the publisher hasn't included a link to your site, get in touch and see if they’d be willing to add one—[backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) can drive referral traffic and benefit your SEO.

!["Mentions" dashboard in Media Monitoring app](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/66/d3/66d3f3c4888e55a9c88cd54a2b709952/d398fb30151aca5845783dccb63f8553/VZbUN28mV8tTmnTUFRkU2AIoTzz1K2LYo4l027rEExN56z15FTf4ivmWkyCMcJ3aE1U-pqsO4mGXPYhsoKWYZbC2KOL9uUPBPmo7_zi3KvrPmjIykAP_Y7CTgGCP498ZAUrs97ttm6X7N-YQlD9g13g.png)

In the “**Analysis**” tab, you can see:

- How your mentions have fluctuated over time
- Your Presence Score (a measure of your brand’s online popularity) trend
- The balance between positive and negative mentions
- What level of reach your mentions are getting
- Which websites’ mentions are driving the most visibility
- Links that commonly appear alongside your mentions

![Hunter's domain search dashboard](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ad/73/ad73a2667913173f2fb3f927c3ecb3aa/650e3b8f3872f0b8592424721395f1fe/hPprLcNZHdznuLc145IrJlDQhr-uYmXsyTzcVtz_0LNrnke0jNfEd5L7Nx92N2OcGTPLcHTi3jv3oAm5knrG2KXWIhsyxE9UCsQo25fMqcGk5wyFxIg5AtrvfL6vCmZhZ-tkj4qsG8_JRMZuUTYIW5E.png)

Give Hunter a domain or company and it will find the best person to contact there so you can skip the chasing around.

It also lets you verify email addresses to avoid bounces and get contact details, including the contact’s name, position, and social media handles.

You can also look at decision makers’ social media profiles to learn about their interests. For example, if they’ve recently enjoyed a piece of content, you can reference it in your email or expand on the topic.

It’s these personalized touches that separate effective outreach from generic cold emails.

### Price

Hunter is free for up to 25 searches and 50 verifications a month.

If you want to increase limits and add features, Hunter offers [four paid plans](https://hunter.io/pricing):

- Starter: $34 per month
- Growth: $104 per month
- Business: $349 per month
- Enterprise: Contact for pricing

All plans allow for unlimited users.

## 22. Livestorm: Create Engaging Webinars and Demos

[Livestorm](https://livestorm.co/) is a video engagement platform for managing virtual events and training from start to finish. All from your browser.

Nine out of ten marketers say that video has helped them increase leads and user understanding, according to a [State of Video Marketing Report](https://www.wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/) by Wyzowl. And 87% say video has boosted sales.

You can use Livestorm’s tools to get similar results for your business.

![Livestorm video platform](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/44/95/44955fb582c4cef87e98c70281544a1f/20c6cbc9c9984df6621a495c5d656505/qHM2e8wR8KVgBVXxHWwLdvwZuvBHhSZzM9dV7yKFZTxfFKipD28qODoap7vVe36bEQPP-AeA4JhgjdGBkQ1LJzwLaXE81IINe50h_ijZ7qtPZ4ksENsDvXtKuYsY7qokmSfJexbtydqq2JkHL-1BF8o.jpeg)

Image Source: [Livestorm](https://livestorm.co/use-cases/webinar-software-video-podcasts-and-interviews)

The platform gives you the tools to create customer demos or webinars and interact with viewers in real time to answer questions that might otherwise stop them from converting.

For example, if a customer wants to know how a feature works, share your screen to give them a walkthrough and provide links to further reading in the chat.

Beyond product demonstrations, users can create custom registration pages to collect their most valuable data. This increases engagement by sending automated event reminders, and measuring event engagement to spot improvement opportunities.

Plus, it connects with over [1,000 apps](https://livestorm.co/integrations/integromat) to expand its potential.

With integrations, you can send leads to your CRM, retarget attendees with ads, host content on your customers’ preferred platform, and more.

### Price

Livestorm is free for up to 30 active contacts (a registrant or team member) per month, with sessions lasting up to 20 minutes.

There are [three paid plans](https://livestorm.co/pricing) to increase limitations:

- Pro: $99-495 per month for 100-500+ contacts
- Business: 500+ contacts, with pricing available on request
- Enterprise: 500+ contacts, with pricing available on request
