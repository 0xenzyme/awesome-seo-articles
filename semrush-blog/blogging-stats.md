# 46 Blogging Statistics to Know in 2025

Looking to increase the impact of your blogging strategy? Wondering what separates the top blogs from the rest of the pack?

You’re in the right place.

Let's dive into 46 statistics about blogging to give you a comprehensive view of the state of blogging right now.

Here are some top picks to get you started:

1. More than 2.5 billion blog posts are published each year ([Web Tribunal, 2023](http://webtribunal.net/blog/how-many-blogs))
2. Nearly 24% of high-performing blogs publish a new post every day ([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/))
3. Around 44% of bloggers are between the ages of 25 and 34. And nearly 35% are between 35 and 44 ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))

## Blog Post Creation and Management Statistics

The stats below provide a glimpse into how bloggers create posts and manage their blogs.

One standout insight is that both the average length of blog posts and the time spent writing them have increased significantly over the past 10 years.

4. The average blog post is 1,427 words long. That’s 77% more words than a decade ago. ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
5. The average length of a top-performing blog post is 1,152 words. Low-performing posts are an average of 668 words long. ([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/))
6. Only 3% of blogs regularly publish articles longer than 3,000 words ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
7. While 22% of bloggers publish posts on a weekly basis, only 2% post daily ([Statista, 2021](https://www.statista.com/statistics/487495/bloggers-posting-frequency-us/))
8. The average blog post takes 3 hours and 51 minutes to write ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))

![3 hours and 51 minutes: The average time spent writing a blog post in 2023.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e2/ab/e2ab1fd1c6167901598a2bf53ecb45dc/cf6efcf494b615aa784c917fbfa58b3d/4fbh7_xZxGnMbKF-qoFuxqn6VAahPDPbF2W6FTAfFGL-ZDdTB-0VJy_7-33_FP_1X-DuMe0WBa4AEpLl_3ciD1hDLC8Eue7NjJrUuGYgbQuxE7zekhzGgHfLi6C-07Vb55EFNTtmA83_Qrlm-PDAHnA.png)

9. Of surveyed bloggers, 23% spend between three and five hours per week writing their posts. And 6% spend more than 40 hours per week on their posts. ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))
10. The time bloggers spend writing their posts has increased by 60% in the past decade ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
11. Just over 73% of bloggers welcome guest posts on their blogs ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))
12. A solid 65% of bloggers use AI to some extent. And 43% use it to [generate ideas](https://www.semrush.com/blog/blog-ideas/) for their content. ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
13. More than 30% of bloggers audit their content twice a year. And 24% do it once a year. ([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/))
14. The proportion of bloggers who update their old posts increased from 53% in 2017 to 74% in 2023 ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))

## Blog Post Quality Statistics

Here, we consider what the data says about blog post quality.

Note how high-performing blog posts typically take longer to write, are easier to read, and are more likely to include original research.

15. What 39% of consumers find most annoying about content is excessive wordiness and/or bad writing ([Adobe, 2019](https://www.slideshare.net/adobe/2019-adobe-brand-content-survey))
16. The number of bloggers who work with editors has tripled since 2014 ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))

![3X more bloggers worked with an editor in 2023 compared to 2014.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ab/65/ab650b288f5fcabef45ac377e3817964/5c33a3670edfa2998a1751c645f7e9a7/DX3Xc5_f6rLAgAtOI2izBGSp6RArcOv4xSlV1UWPLzTayEg5FF6LpI3SOIqilQwwKUeP0xmbm9WxkyCuUSONtZQ7UoSuT6R4LsxLad1d0qRb00htXb5qRv2phWw01v3j9YlNg5a23km2TtKI7J2UtF0.png)

17. Bloggers who have a formal editorial process are more likely to experience strong results compared to those who don’t ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
18. Posts that are either easy or fairly easy to read make up 62% of high-performing blog posts. This compares with just 24% of low-performing posts. ([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/))
19. Of top-performing posts, 5% contain data studies ([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/))
20. Bloggers who spend more time writing their posts tend to experience more success. And 35% of those who spend more than six hours report strong results. ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
21. More than 30% of bloggers always check analytics related to their content ([Statista, 2019](https://www.statista.com/statistics/314486/blogging-analytics-access-and-usage/))

## Blog Performance Statistics

The stats below reveal how much traffic the typical blog receives, where that traffic comes from, and which [blogging niches](https://www.semrush.com/blog/blog-niche-ideas/) perform best.

It’s worth noting that the majority of bloggers still think their blog has a positive impact on their marketing efforts.

22. Less than 30% of blogs receive 1,000 or fewer visitors per month. Roughly 20% receive between 1,001 and 5,000 visits. Andjust 3.7% get more than 500,000 monthly visitors. ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))
23. More than 55% of bloggers say their blog drives some marketing results. And 26% say their blog drives strong results. ([Orbit Media, 2023](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))

![82% of bloggers say their blog drives at least some results.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/07/79/07795f2f82287394fdf59f3407e7bade/83248e1a855b16d961e55a67c89a1977/aTKlAatpiU9JXjSlQqz8NGgfhBDlAY9DhWs6MyHjxFLZeZJt3_FE5jocqtg0MUqQZsVLNc2RNZf65r4CHI0O2w79dEg5vn99ezI6cfm7Sva3F_vQ67ilHvjn81-MF6X0W8nkOLeO9-9CM5uJUImeVsM.png)

24. The most popular blog niches are lifestyle (11.4%), personal development (9.6%), and entrepreneurship (7.9%) ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))
25. Almost 50% of bloggers report receiving most of their traffic from social media. And 22% get most of their traffic from organic search. ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))

## Blogging Platform Statistics

The following stats highlight which platforms and devices bloggers use to keep their blogs up and running.

Unsurprisingly, WordPress remains the dominant platform in the blogosphere.

26. Tumblr boasts almost 600 million blogs on its platform ([Tumblr, 2024](https://about.tumblr.com/))
27. WordPress users generate around 70 million posts and contribute 77 million comments each month ([WordPress, 2024](https://wordpress.com/activity/))
28. Just over 22% of bloggers use WordPress.com as their blogging platform. Around 19% use Blogspot.com and 18% use WordPress.org. ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))
29. A whopping 72% of bloggers update their blogs from mobile devices ([First Site Guide, 2020](https://firstsiteguide.com/blogging-industry-survey/))
