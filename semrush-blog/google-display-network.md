# Google Display Network: What Is the GDN & How to Use It

## What Is the Google Display Network?

The Google Display Network (GDN) is a collection of millions of websites, apps, and videos. These channels display Google ads to a large, global audience.

Below is an example of how a display ad appears on the GDN:

![FedEx display ad on the GDN](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3b/9b/3b9b56fedb72f5bcd1dd4f4f22fefc80/00927635effa585103088809526391a9/AD_4nXdrn6e7gyANpqSC9rnJhfZzn-kemCFVFMiQN7ZWb7PLBzpwPE36kz1Zmix0JxUPZxIu-q5wHytgC8WJ39Ef5Yp4WoFDf2ztJ0HlbI5u-KxJNEcydpO-erSgYcSMiFA2b_6Ms-HPsQ.png)

Collectively, the GDN reaches a large percentage of internet users worldwide. Advertisers can create and use **display ads** within Google Ads to reach millions of potential customers.

## Why Use the GDN?

The Google Display Network offers extensive reach for marketers. It can place ads on a wide range of websites and platforms, which makes it possible to engage broad and diverse audiences.

The GDN can help reach users who aren’t actively searching for products or services on search engines. Almost every website and blog contains content that relates to at least one target [keyword](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/). The GDN can then display ads on those relevant websites and blogs.

This method increases brand visibility and awareness, and it can also drive sales.

## How Does the Google Display Network Work?

The Google Display Network (GDN) can show your [online ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/online-advertising/) on sites, apps, or videos that relate to your business. It can also show your ads to users who match the criteria you specify.

### Audience Targeting Based on Interests

Interest targeting allows you to display ads to people who show interest in your products or services, or who engage in related activities.

You can choose from three main interest-targeting categories:

- Affinity Audiences
- Custom Affinity Audiences
- In-Market Audiences

![Interest targeting infographic](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f2/cd/f2cd74d6ab9a8f01da0c649313b1eda2/6906192e02be5746a32b5f5ac2e0308a/AD_4nXdPWD_60d2n_3OXkSCucC0ojzFPWp3C0KfcirHhIwmqGxHypKDoSvNpYMNyZFMdL1YqYjxx4P55UgWv16HLvANc5QW-a1LdQK4bD_Jy5CufOG3n2E4kjIA-A91MoTN7MRewUiB1.png)

#### Affinity Audiences

This category includes approximately 80 groups based on various interests, such as “running enthusiasts,” “action movie lovers,” or “book lovers.” You can target one or more of these groups. There is no limit to the number of affinities you can add.

However, adding more affinities narrows your audience and reduces your possible impressions.

#### Custom Affinity Audiences

You can refine your targeting further by using keywords to build custom audiences. For example, if you sell triathlon gear, you could target keywords like “triathlon training,” “beginner triathletes,” or “triathlon events.”

Google will show your ads to people browsing sites that contain these keywords. You can also add specific URLs you expect your audience to visit and place your ads there.

For example, you might target triathlete.com or ironman.com.

![Example of ads showing on triathlete.com](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8a/7c/8a7cbacb33ced10d3e0d08ebb9ae04a5/853202b6c664e25332d9c9ecceb46111/AD_4nXfPspQzlSUNkQPJgK5w9SLg_uLY_OKPdyyiETj0GupUjfluRLEui3tdUj2d-pDkj0FutRId7GhOaloqMPnBjWxNUHm4O-NebzEl70Pduk05gm9lsbNehTCxF0EnbJFd3LUVAfiMlA.png)

#### In-Market Audiences

This targeting category allows you to find people who are actively looking at products or services like yours. It helps reach users with high purchase intent.

For example, if someone adds triathlon gear to their shopping cart on another site, or watches review videos of similar products, you can reach them with GDN ads.

### Audience Targeting Based on Demographics

Demographic targeting helps you reach potential customers based on age, gender, parental status, or household income. You can also combine demographic targeting with interest targeting to reach a more relevant audience.

For example, if you operate a yoga studio for women and want to promote a special class for working mothers, you can target the “Female” demographic, within the “25-54” age range, who are “Parents.” Then add affinity groups related to yoga, pilates, and fitness.

Be aware that more specific targeting may reduce the size of your audience. Test different combinations to find the approach that delivers the most conversions.

## Google Display Network vs. Google Search Network

The primary difference between the Google Display Network (GDN) and the **Google Search Network** (GSN) involves where ads appear. The GSN is a collection of websites and apps related to Google Search.

When people search for terms related to your keywords, your GSN ads can appear in Google search results and partner search sites.

For example, GSN ads might appear like this:

![Google Search Ad for dog food on SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1e/32/1e32b7cd4dab328dc209d3ea19ddec17/996aab40435eb8f4f7426091047aecb7/AD_4nXdbHWSVY_T-60oN5X-e9gh69DXdmjOsWSb5Mnv9Wlr9HyKcXgrT0-ZVyEdqVopql2TijtQeTQIehxujmIkYqRqaapA288hQ7MU9a5wXXL1J4pcfT_H1fXYfg_uDpi4gvxbs4SEIoA.png)

By contrast, the GDN shows ads when people visit websites, apps, and videos not necessarily related to active searches. Viewers may not be [looking for what you offer](https://ads.google.com/intl/en_id/home/resources/reach-larger-new-audiences/#:~:text=When%20running%20Display%20ads%2C%20you%20might%20not%20reach%20those%20who%20are%20actively%20searching%20for%20what%20you%20offer) at that moment.

For example, GDN ads might appear like this:

![Ads showing on "Vetted" website](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/55/46/55462b79871a913a5cb5995745cb92eb/e56aaaedb4225e846f7c67946f526b88/AD_4nXewNj-zCPkkXWHYbnlq9fZkkKg2rQEZQ9viHub2kxTiASCyeF7PedIiNFQltbPiJ4qkOgoWY85kb9pR3hQdr23MkJhH5KBYT84n0aMyiTS4mKulArosxuHsb5ini5uBHkMONPj2Fw.png)

Other differences exist as well. On the GSN, advertisers target keywords. On the GDN, advertisers target audiences, interests, and websites. The GSN typically captures higher-intent audiences, while the GDN is often better for brand awareness.

## How Much Does Using the Google Display Network Cost?

The average [cost per click (CPC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/cost-per-click/) on the GDN is often less than $1, though costs vary based on your budget.

The GDN uses a live auction system, charging you each time someone clicks your ads. Competition for keyword bids is often lower on the GDN, so you may reach users who show some interest in your products or services, even if their intent is lower.

As a result, you typically get more impressions, which helps build awareness.

## How to Create Google Display Ads

In the left menu, select “**Campaigns**” and click the plus button. Then choose “**New campaign**.”

## How to Optimize Your Ads for the Google Display Network

Use the following guidelines to create responsive display ads that perform well on the Google Display Network (GDN). Responsive display ads are the default ad type on the GDN.

Google will prompt you to upload assets (headlines, logos, images, and descriptions) and will automatically adjust them to fit various devices and ad placements.

### Stick to Google’s Image Size Guidelines

Images are essential for display ads because they help users understand your business, products, and brand. Proper image sizing is crucial for strong performance.

You can upload up to 15 images in three aspect ratios: square, landscape, and portrait. You may also select images from Google’s free stock library.

![Choose up to 15 images to use in your ad](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8a/1e/8a1e494a8748bb6fd9d42e0660d1401b/9dd37a4c6988168283d5ce3f41a9d429/AD_4nXexbp13fLxgGR8dh2CY-oy_A3_1HA9jCZm7gb_vgsDHhOSi_oEqYac3Qe8J7sQND-lI9iwKgcoDXnDvYV585tDZyCnl4zqQ0BoRiIZB93aB-iDLNm8NcuWvQ3TObkMHRr5hXM6qOQ.png)

If you choose images from the stock library, Google will let you set the appropriate ratio.

![Images rations highlighted in "Choose how to crop your image" window](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/98/71/987185779a7799f88b4cad40819cfd3c/f692516417af1af51aece7af80a80fbc/AD_4nXcbzkfkFGIX-WjIQcjKdEn6qhekp3656dRwBuMxjt4wveSDmku-q2KeW2SHkmmTJnRCFMA8lsKqwV_8hM0P5PZ6uqpGn2v6BfOgOjbebCz8AwwNPjPYkGdL9QrJrSlI6Tkho8nyxA.png)

If you upload your own images, follow these [size guidelines](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722134?hl=en&ref_topic=10284034#zippy=%2Cfollow-image-guidelines):

**Square Images**

- Ratio: 1:1
- Recommended size: 1200 x 1200
- Minimum size: 300 x 300

**Landscape Images**

- Ratio: 1.91:1
- Recommended size: 1200 x 628
- Minimum size: 600 x 314

**Portrait Images**

- Ratio: 9:16
- Recommended size: 900 x 1600
- Minimum size: 600 x 1067

![Ad image size guidelines](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ea/cb/eacb4e7aa45d03f939c4ce3824ab1d7e/042a2a1fec249ee1efc123ff69067b91/AD_4nXdwucFKHHZO7Ff8Oz4Q18YDeEZb4wkhKtVijn-ZEjl4fTK0QYJP3LUPeWazZty-ls9ifcazumKQ9HclzHkZwlwpNStdAZaprnB95aJ1Z4QoHKzvZiUjdh_vBrIl5WTXRzkrS9Xkow.png)

### Make Sure Your Images Are High Quality

Use high-quality images. Avoid images that are blurry, heavily filtered, upside-down, or outlined with borders.

**Don’t**:

- Overlay your logo on top of images
- Overlay text or other graphics
- Overlay buttons
- Use logos with colorful backgrounds

**Do**:

- Use single images rather than collages
- Make the product or service the focal point
- Show physical settings with natural shadows and lighting
- Use logos with transparent or white backgrounds

![Image quality do and don’t infographic](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d0/52/d0528c0f2c2abe5c9dc64600db09b8f8/5cb4c109e32a60470a6021aa3e41c6e2/AD_4nXdfhe2CIC4a9de3tWsVQ3Lmlk4V9M4Usr4V9Nb0h-PaPpwTbFi_jjyG7BCVgtMTWM-HhAmBvcz2xT_WY1BHMgiGMHV1EGEwNiX171M5kfZITWkan-I_GOtKvKZxqSVocmDkYkLBYQ.png)

### Follow Google’s Text Recommendations

Use clear, simple language to describe your product or service. Avoid generic text, all caps, and clickbait.

#### Short Headlines

You can add up to five short headlines in responsive display ads.

**Don’t**:

- End short headlines with punctuation
- Repeat the same copy in both headline and description
- Use your business’s name as the headline
- Use all caps

**Do**:

- Tell users what they can do: “Pay with a credit card”
- Spell out your offer: “Free next-day delivery”
- Make it unique and compelling: “Running shoes for less”

![Short headlines do and don’t infographic](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/37/09/3709cb568f141d784c05649510ffb9d4/b16fcb15a6d655db35d456675e9a8215/AD_4nXd_RdkXcGP6Q366LDVUePxE-ny3b__kgn-bLPX4ezw3uEO-9s8Vy9DD0JxBwtrEQIV8hAaE7zq2O-JDrDgqvlaULUR-5nWrAOUnT71hy-nQ1mWq9nkK_kqjo_r6JHCZajASMs43sA.png)

Keep all short headlines under 30 characters to prevent truncation. Ensure each asset is unique.

#### Long Headlines

You can add one long headline for your responsive display ads.

Make the long headline describe your brand’s or product’s value proposition in a useful and interesting way.

**Don’t**:

- Punctuate the end of your long headline
- Repeat copy from short headlines
- Use all caps
- Use clickbait

**Do**:

- Write a standalone headline. Google may display it without a description.

![Long headlines do and don’t infographic](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a6/df/a6df235904a5dc0fd3edcb151ae1080b/3f2822aa524b8e47675ebb38a5e1986f/AD_4nXf5XPhfwEU08EWOzXjkqItxPP65BT_WU5vly3zqGkwtZ_TlgwyVStBdief2jPoUib5iML7_W7b-ZERvpj2qHerBxi0IJDzAQL8ycGrsTcNUTBJH_QIPyVI_NEXxVHVWFSqEzFZBPg.png)

Keep your long headline under 90 characters to avoid cutoffs.

#### Descriptions

Google shows descriptions with short headlines, not long headlines. Write descriptions to complement your short headlines. You can add up to five descriptions. Use all five if possible.

**Don’t**:

- Use generic messaging
- Use all caps
- Use clickbait

**Do**:

- Make text easy to read and understand
- Use sentence-case capitalization
- Include detail
- Clearly articulate the value proposition

![Descriptions do and don’t infographic](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/43/30/43304b2aa77496ae6780f68a5b9f734d/97ca30637c9611ae8aeb27ef82ce8b28/AD_4nXfwK09iPgG7PQsUOMc7mKrdgxCE8U4E3Vc24MI4vXatR1ukAPMCodoBDGb7HoZXh8KGRpktFGgY3jK2qfOpEDreSmUk0o6hLbfe7t9-RDM9sPiz8IWmlKwrb1bG-uIH8lkFb0g7.png)

Keep descriptions under 90 characters to avoid truncation.
