# What strategies help grow AI visibility and guide customers to my brand?

The strategies that help grow AI visibility and guide customers to your brand include creating [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/), building authority in your niche, and getting mentioned on other popular websites.

Growing AI visibility requires thinking beyond your own website—AI systems evaluate your entire digital footprint to determine whether you're a trusted source worth recommending.

Here are the best strategies to grow AI visibility:

- **Get mentioned across the web:** Appear in reputable publications, business directories, and industry blogs. The more trusted sources reference your brand, the more likely AI is to surface you in responses.
- **Create high-quality content:** Publish articles, guides, and resources that directly answer user questions. Content that is clear, useful, and well-structured has a higher chance of being cited by AI systems.
- **Format content for easy AI extraction:** Organize content with descriptive headings, lists, and numbered steps so AI can quickly find and reference specific information.
- **Build strong expertise signals:** Showcase author credentials, link to reputable sources, and include real-world experience to demonstrate content expertise to AI systems.
- **Maintain consistent brand information:** Ensure your name, offerings, and positioning are uniform across websites, social channels, and directories so AI doesn’t encounter conflicting information.
