# 7 Best AI SEO Tools for 2026 (Tested Firsthand)

AI SEO tools can save hours on research, content, and reporting—but not every tool delivers on its promises.

After testing dozens firsthand, I narrowed it down to seven tools that actually perform, helping you:

- Track and grow visibility in AI-generated search results
- Plan and optimize content faster
- Automate technical, content, and PR workflows

Each recommendation below includes real test notes, feature highlights, and accuracy insights—so you know what data you can trust and where to stay cautious.

## 2. SparkToro

**Best for**: Content marketers who want to understand where their target audience spends time and what topics resonate most

**Free basic version available**: Yes (5 searches per month)

**Pricing**: Personal ($50 per month), Business ($150 per month), Agency ($300 per month)

[SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/) is an audience research platform that uncovers where your customers spend time online and what conversations matter most to them.

You can start with a structured input (like a keyword, website, or a social/bio term) to generate a full audience report detailing demographics, affinities, and platform behaviors.

For example, when I searched for “esports” (U.S.), SparkToro estimated 34,000-47,000 monthly searches and revealed which platforms and tools this audience prefers.

Twitch stood out at +172% above the U.S. average, while Reddit and ChatGPT were the most frequently used search and AI tools.

![SparkToro Overview with average monthly searches, top social networks, and top search & AI tools highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/23/c2/23c24f8983a78fdbd56f6a3bc2366ea3/24468210281eb3e1d653b7e4eae2d639/image.jpeg)

SparkToro’s newer [**AI conversational search**](https://sparktoro.com/blog/how-to-find-hyper-specific-audiences-with-sparktoros-new-ai-features/) lets you describe audiences naturally instead of relying on fixed keywords. You can type something like:

- *“Decision-makers at small U.S. law firms looking to improve their websites”*
- *“Tabletop role-playing gamers looking for alternatives to D&D”*

The AI translates your description into structured data and returns a detailed report—including websites, podcasts, channels, and affinity scores—so you can identify niche segments that would otherwise take hours to research manually.

In the Topics tab of your report, SparkToro ranks subjects by affinity (how strongly your audience cares) and saturation (how heavily covered they already are).

![Relevant Topics report on SparkToro ranking subjects by affinity and saturation.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7b/8c/7b8c615080c76665c00c1869f68cb55e/d50302ec63513b3fae62be24b4988308/image.jpeg)

*The example above is from a* [*product explainer video*](https://sparktoro.com/blog/how-to-find-hyper-specific-audiences-with-sparktoros-new-ai-features/) *by Rand Fishkin, founder of SparkToro.*

By creating [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/) around these high-affinity, low-saturation topics, you can meet your audience’s real interests. And because AI engines pull from multiple trusted sources, this also improves your chances of [being cited in AI-generated answers](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-optimization/).

### What I like

SparkToro shows which platforms your audience uses more (or less) than average.

For example, when I looked at visitors to *esportsworldcup.com* in the U.S., Discord usage was +93.2% above average and X (Twitter) was +22.3%, while Facebook trailed at -6.3%.

These insights make it much easier to prioritize the right platforms for ads, sponsorships, or content distribution. So you’re not wasting time on channels your audience doesn’t care about.

## 3. AlsoAsked

**Best for**: Bloggers and SEO specialists who want to uncover People Also Ask queries and build topic clusters that align with AI search behavior

**Free basic version available**: Yes (3 free credits per day)

**Pricing**: Basic ($15 per month), Lite ($29 per month), Pro ($59 per month)

[AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com/) is a keyword research tool that helps bridge the gap between traditional and AI search.

It pulls questions directly from Google’s [People Also Ask](https://www.semrush.com/blog/people-also-ask/) boxes and organizes them into branching visual maps, so you can see how real users expand on a query.

You can filter results by country, city, and language to reveal the exact questions people in your market are asking.

For example, when I searched “**AI SEO tools**,” the map surfaced follow-up questions like “Can SEO be done by AI?” and “Can ChatGPT help with SEO?”

![AlsoAsked dashboard showing a visual map of related queries for the term "AI SEO tools".](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/be/4d/be4d21848d7cdc79c50fe9725b2cccc0/3876471a1b0f2a5e4c0a5beacf544d6e/image.jpeg)

AI engines like ChatGPT and [Google AI Mode](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-ai-mode/) use a process called [query fan-out](https://www.semrush.com/blog/query-fan-out/), which breaks a user’s question into smaller sub-questions before generating a final answer.

![Fan-out query example showing how an AI engine breaks a query into smaller sub-questions before generating a response.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bb/30/bb30303d57891f5dbc847f97e3e98be9/12ef7775284d1cf5f216fab8629bf0c9/image.jpeg)

As AlsoAsked Founder Mark Williams-Cook explains in this “[The Search Session](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/from-people-also-ask-to-ai-search)” podcast episode:

“The People Also Ask questions surfaced are the closest in ‘intent proximity.’ If someone asks this, these are the most likely follow-ups. Now, with AI search and query fan-out, that process is automated.”

AlsoAsked gives you a way to see that process in advance.

By mapping related questions, you can plan content briefs, FAQ sections, or [topic clusters](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/) that mirror both how humans and AI systems naturally explore a topic.

For example, here’s the whole visual map for “**AI SEO tools**:”

![A visual map of related queries for the term "AI SEO tools" on AlsoAsked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b6/64/b664b84cb00932ec786c913878248f0e/cb93c21248233b7b5d169b777b1af2bb/image.jpeg)

### What I like

A standard search goes two levels deep and usually returns around 25 related questions. With a “deep” search, the tool goes three levels down, surfacing around 100 questions at once.

![AlsoAsked dashboard with "Search depth" clicked and "Deep" selected from the dropdown options.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/df/96/df969056c61a143ff93c43ec9bcfa850/3f44b5cef4bf2d1a904381e9d68f9ffe/image.jpeg)

For example, running a deep search for “laser skin treatment” reveals far more variations and follow-ups than a regular search.

This makes it much easier to build complete topic clusters and ensure your content covers the same questions an AI engine might “fan out” when generating an answer.

![A deep search visual map of related queries for the term "laser skin treatment" on AlsoAsked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4f/8f/4f8f52f1c134416e12637471f75fb5ad/cc8e4485eb87ed5dc6fabc1321b23161/image.jpeg)

**Note**: A “Deep” search requires a paid account (Lite or Pro) and costs four credits per search.

## 4. Keyword Insights

**Best for**: SEOs and agencies that want to cluster keywords, create briefs, and identify social channels that influence AI-generated answers.

**Free basic version available**: No. $1 trial for 7 days (600 credits included)

**Pricing**: From $58 per month (Basic plan: 10,000 credits)

[Keyword Insights](https://www.keywordinsights.ai/) is an AI-powered SEO platform that transforms keyword lists into organized topic clusters, complete content briefs, and even draft articles.

Its workflow moves seamlessly from research to planning to content creation, which lets you manage everything inside one interface instead of bouncing between tools.

A newer feature, Social Sources, highlights where user-generated platforms (like Reddit, YouTube, or Quora) appear in search results for your clustered keywords.

For example, after uploading a keyword list about email marketing, the tool grouped them into clusters such as email marketing templates and email marketing benchmarks.

Each cluster displays at a glance:

- Search volume
- Competition level
- Search intent (e.g., people looking for info vs. ready to buy)
- Social source presence (e.g., Reddit or YouTube dominance)

![Keyword Clustering on Keyword Insights showing metrics like search volume, competition level, search intent, etc. for each cluster.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1e/38/1e381e28a2758231a2553ad367f9f258/569dfcb73dd5b029a2a5ac0d3c0188d2/image.jpeg)

From any cluster, you can launch other in-platform tools:

- **AI Writer Agent** to generate a first-draft article
- **Writer Assistant** to polish or optimize existing copy
- **Content Brief** to auto-create structured outlines with headings, FAQs, and sources
- **SERP Analyzer** to inspect competitor pages already ranking for these terms

This integration means your keyword research instantly feeds your content pipeline—no exporting or manual rebuilding required.

![In-platform tools like AI Writer Agent, Writer Assistant, Content Brief, etc. highlighted next to a cluster on Keyword Insights.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f0/6c/f06c0aa5ebff717e459783b4f87e06f4/c492296bed450e3fbf5658b6634453c5/image.jpeg)

### What I like

The platform runs on a credit system. Each task uses a set number of credits.

This makes it flexible. So, you only pay for what you use, whether that’s clustering thousands of keywords or producing just a handful of briefs.

For example:

- 1 credit = one keyword clustered
- 100 credits = one content brief
- 200 credits = one article with the AI Writer Assistant
- 1,200 credits = one full article with the AI Writer Agent

## 5. SurferSEO

**Best for**: Content teams and freelance writers who want data-driven guidance to plan, write, and optimize content for higher visibility in search results

**Free basic version available**: No (7-day money-back guarantee)

**Pricing**: Essential ($99 per month), Scale ($219 per month), Enterprise (custom pricing)

[SurferSEO](https://surferseo.com/) is a content optimization platform that analyzes the top-ranking pages for any keyword and translates that data into actionable, on-page recommendations.

Its core feature, the Content Editor, evaluates word count, headings, keyword use, and media depth to suggest what it takes to compete in search.

For example, when I optimized an article for the keyword, “how to find YouTube influencers for your business,” Surfer gave the draft a Content Score of 40/100 and flagged:

- More paragraphs: 127+ vs. 85
- More images: 41-95 vs. 13
- Better keyword coverage across headings and body copy

![Content Score on SurferSEO showing an overall score along with insights on paragraphs, images, keyword coverage on headings and body, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0b/27/0b27a6b627c2c7efff56003f682bcac3/659a6b181661d37da2beeef3ece9cf19/image.jpeg)

These benchmarks aren’t rigid rules. Surfer itself notes that a Content Score of 67+ generally indicates “[optimum quality and relevance.](https://docs.surferseo.com/en/articles/5700317-what-is-content-score)” Writers should use these data points as guidelines, balancing them with editorial judgment and brand tone.

On the right side of the Content Editor, Surfer offers several AI-driven tools to streamline editing:

- **Auto-optimize**: Automatically inserts relevant terms into your draft without changing meaning
- **Explore missing facts**: Suggests additional data points to enhance credibility
- **Insert internal links**: Surfaces related pages (e.g., from [semrush.com](http://semrush.com) in my test) and lets you insert up to 10 at once

![AI-optimization tools like Auto-Optimize, Explore missing facts, Insert internal links, etc. highlighted on the right-hand side of SurferSEO.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9c/e6/9ce6354d96d31800dcfb0428259d820a/0a65bbc7359458f46e28b2a3bbf50a73/image.jpeg)

In my test, using Auto-Optimize took about five minutes. Surfer added relevant keywords, expanded a few sections, and all changes were fully reviewable and reversible.

!["Auto-optimize" results on SurferSEO with all the reviewable and reversible changes made appearing on the right-hand side.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/95/71/9571b1ca1901432faf8283baa98b1202/fc95e5a33cdf814f652540ce34736788/image.jpeg)

AI-assisted writing is efficient, but you should never publish output blindly. [Google’s guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content) allow AI content as long as it’s helpful and original.

Focus on [E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/) (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)—content that demonstrates firsthand knowledge and people-first value. Thin, generic or over-optimized AI text can still underperform or be filtered out.

## What I like

Surfer’s “[Facts](https://docs.surferseo.com/en/articles/10717145-facts)” tab (Coverage Booster) scans the top 20 ranking pages.

It uses LLMs to flag missing facts and topics, helping you close content gaps and improve your chances of being cited in AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

![Youtube video thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/TR7Mnr4RCfI/hq720.jpg)

I wasn’t able to test this feature directly, but the product demo shows how it works in practice. Surfer also claims that filling gaps with “Facts” can [increase AI citations by up to 25%](https://surferseo.com/blog/whats-new-at-surfer-august-2025/).

## 6. AirOps

**Free basic version available**: Yes (Solo plan)

**Pricing:** Scale (custom, 14-day free trial), Enterprise (custom)

[AirOps](https://www.airops.com/) is an AI-powered workflow platform built for SEO and content operations teams. It lets you design automated workflows that handle everything from keyword research to content refreshes using prebuilt templates or custom drag-and-drop logic.

For example, I built a “Refresh Existing Content” workflow using one of AirOps’ ready-made templates.

![Templates on AirOps with "Refresh Article Content" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/68/84/6884ee21ea1da0caf6c61d8a03ecef31/f6545c96af1e12884c747387cab95266/image.jpeg)

The workflow opened in a Grid view, where each article URL appeared in columns—title, body, meta description, last modified date—alongside action buttons that trigger automated steps like:

- **Extract Article from URL** pulls in the full article text, links, and images
- **AEO Visibility Refresh Suggestions** analyzes readability and AI citation potential
- **Apply Suggested Refresh Changes** implements AI-driven edits while preserving layout and links

![Refresh Existing Content on AirOps showing a list of URLs along with title, body, meta description, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b9/9b/b99bfce70f09cfeef95282941eee0102/6735c4779461ab1db9a558503e524830/image.jpeg)

I tested the workflow on five articles. Within about 15 minutes, AirOps had extracted the content, flagged AI-visibility improvements, and applied suggested edits.

Opening the Article Comparison column displayed a side-by-side view of the original versus the AI-edited version, with inline highlights showing what changed (e.g., clarifying context, tightening phrasing).

![Article Comparison on AirOps showing changes made to the original content along with a brief reason for each change.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c2/c2/c2c2e72cd207b4586c0c36c2a29a7647/365e50ebdce911b200c16643af2d574a/image.jpeg)

Beyond templates, AirOps allows teams to build workflows from scratch using a visual drag-and-drop builder. You can chain together actions like:

- “Analyze SERP for target keyword”
- “Create article from content brief”
- “Add internal links”
- “Optimize metadata”

This flexibility makes it possible to design end-to-end automation pipelines customized for your exact SEO process—whether that’s refreshing hundreds of pages or generating new content in bulk.

![Creating a workflow on AirOps with the "Power Agents" on the left-hand side highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/11/ad/11ad5f61952b2b3e1b8ee251e1a263ec/1c71a7f89da1a19db1963ca052cfa3c6/image.jpeg)

This gives the AI real context about your business, so the workflows generate copy that feels accurate and on-brand.

For example, a SaaS company could upload its support articles, and AirOps would use that knowledge when drafting blog posts or user guides.

## 7. Screaming Frog

**Best for**: SEO specialists and site managers who want to automate repetitive technical SEO tasks like generating image alt text or analyzing crawl data with AI.

**Free basic version available**: Yes (crawl up to 500 URLs)

**Pricing**: $279 per license per year

[Screaming Frog’s SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) is a desktop crawler that scans your entire website for [technical SEO issues](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/)such as broken links, missing tags, or redirect loops.

Its latest updates now integrate with AI tools like OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Google Gemini, which allows you to automate common auditing tasks with simple natural-language prompts.

For example, you can instruct the crawler to generate missing [image alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/) automatically, improving both accessibility and search visibility.

Just [add your AI API key](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/tutorials/how-to-crawl-with-ai-prompts/) in the settings, define a prompt such as “generate alt text for images,” and Screaming Frog fills in the tags during the crawl.

![Generating alt text for images at scale using AI on Screaming Frog.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/47/02/470280bd2ed840c1d16c5d00ac1745b8/185d88ac12a64000a49d81b3b7960987/image.jpeg)

**Testing note**: I was unable to run a hands-on test for Screaming Frog’s AI prompts due to paywall/access limits. Findings are based on the official docs (last updated September 2025).
