# How to Get a Yahoo Business Listing to Boost Local Visibility

## What Is a Yahoo Business Listing?

A Yahoo local business listing is an online mention of your local business information (i.e., a [local citation](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/how-to-build-local-citations/)) that can appear in Yahoo results.

These listings include key information about your business. Like your business’s name, address, and phone number (NAP).

Your listing can also appear in Yahoo map results when users search for nearby businesses.

![Search for plumbers denver shows local businesses in the area, similar to Google Maps.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8d/16/8d16ce599465ede6d816664bab8c0932/9d7324db23a0e0e624b03519d0347e05/AD_4nXfVoUVpDSGf-GTflaRvkwfw8AbMbxR5ksp4fEBEGixCbpsa1DsAcXu62KwEibspobkKUEfL3zhZgxGvSqaYqD0z4P9r0XeFeSRgQCkYHXA_pBAchShBuiBS7TFfmGauSFkoalokTw.png)

Yahoo has 2.24% of the [mobile search engine market share](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/mobile/united-states-of-america). Which means real people use it to look for businesses like yours.

Plus, it’s a good complement to your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/).

And since it's free and easy, it’s worth creating or claiming your listing to help your [local SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/).

## How to Add a New Yahoo Business Listing

To list your business on Yahoo, go to [Yahoo Local](https://local.yahoo.com/claim-business.html), select the “**New listing**” tab, and click “**Claim a new listing**.”

![Navigation for a new Yahoo business listing.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e0/aa/e0aaaa6253216fbf58fdb66f633f6f84/00ae38b21b86b6d8ce89a4e90dddb00c/AD_4nXf_O4oRgP8b6-EQmgDOxlxCiNiD4pu7T_1eiZ4WWHIn7sd7zcsza_F4YoEDsM_Xqz0ja0JFE8XGWzJAwgYV-a4Ylb4DfCU70qEEcsnpQwutvYpheI4l_l1XfU5ZFrrK2R4NbgCtqA.png)

You’ll be sent over to The Real Yellow Pages site to create your listing.

Yahoo no longer hosts its own listings. And it displays The Real Yellow Pages’ listings in its [search engine results pages (SERPs)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp/).

The Real Yellow Pages may already have information about your business. If it does, your business will appear in the drop-down menu when you look it up by location and name.

Click “**Claim Now**.”

![Business location and name are selected.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e2/0d/e20dc2e356e11f3511eb2a6bab2519bb/86550c852a192e3f9ddeb8c6526f3643/AD_4nXesYuFblssmJBjUltKXuBs1qvqXQEU0xyLPRphV0IVXatB2ZG5LCI2xLwzBtIhueQpcpwn--3-NRE4n3ZktQ0RwbFgJOsVoNSdig1CRxPpQBvYabgKdP_EQZPiWPwnJEYrTGVGHtg.png)

Next, add your contact information to claim your business listing.

![Page allows you to add more info.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/82/ea/82ea8bfc4d554cd61566623330b102ac/87eb3f184eb5714cf5199edbc34057bb/AD_4nXceDz0jL_MyesA8DMMYAvTb4d5LzltbOJus00242fIaA9-32ifALcDe_Q3lvsVQGoS8GVDs2iDWZdE1pld7GeCRVZOzj608Y6HqetTMpCAvj64u9Mdc9tgx9q7k3E6t7o7htZ3m.png)

If The Real Yellow Pages doesn’t have information about your business, no results will appear in the drop-down.

So, select “**click here**” (or call the phone number) to submit your information.

![Business location and name are selected, but no results are found. A blue hyperlink appears for next steps.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ae/42/ae422441cdba7ee5d63cf26fbe01d953/31a1a124e721edec5f8ddf35ffe5bed0/AD_4nXe79TTx24qPdha4MIuGqqbHU-xRvT1EEwbrGKpvxQqqvwtR-Pr547paEtYM3Mf-16NEwSJ97Hct8-q30d2vQx4I0k5GEpJ-F7xTDPfnmF_fvhye2BNY-NxkgAbLu30RpuWQIy_msw.png)

You’ll be taken to a new page where you can fill out your basic listing information.

Once you’ve completed the form, click “**Submit**.”

![Page allows you to fill out more details to connect with a Yahoo Business Advisor.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/11/0c/110c35061782f88fc5295f53b99a3360/6bddf66f4a91ace433fa49d98e6eddfe/AD_4nXeow3JYTelDakj56OgD_4Hjzu-rif0SffZlT2L09U-S5_GFtpPGP8JY-Ao6Q2cdttgTjOhWGIcPhv5ARIOCypsRp0ERS-ODxId5vQzG4WViaFqchv0dGPt-w0kK7kvvGhFfkBRy3A.png)

A Business Advisor will contact you to complete your listing verification. They’ll also discuss whether you want to upgrade to a paid plan that allows for more customization.

## How to Claim an Existing Yahoo Business Listing

To claim a Yahoo business listing that already exists, navigate to [Yahoo Local](https://local.yahoo.com/claim-business.html), select the “**Existing listing**” tab, and click “**Claim existing listing**.”

![Navigation for claiming an existing Yahoo business listing is shown.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/68/12/681217d4f5988873842dd8625c43d3f6/7e5c38f2b2f8cee0d79729a51894f682/AD_4nXdI0CkVNlCuL4oV559MtEq6kNzqdHi8sC9Zfdfw1A5M0htSl-RXsUpFpzVcPSH8yCVkxQDenP3ko4TSnEgAirP59ZY66kfaP6-ZB3WLwqC5ORSa3sj3SYRDY3-K1P3doxSTYUDk.png)

Look up your listing by location and business name and choose it from the drop-down menu. Then, click “**Claim Now**.”

![Business location and name are selected.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b7/56/b7566d4f4c83f568b82f2a514b452590/3a81a896e8fcfa09ca481b3712787fe5/AD_4nXe1db0uoRiwHzLmXkEcxYJ1QzYRxULQkxFBA7hpRwM_-wuJ6uguvLahAOR-PbDWt77e2z19vVMAl1W-eCFamVuNbQHtCWFi4bSE8-VRrZ2K3G2difDrvnso-LPfFAm9yYpTG96W.png)

Next, follow the prompts to update your contact information and claim your existing listing. Then complete your verification when a Business Advisor reaches out.

You can also look up an existing listing using the Yahoo search bar.

When you find your business in the results, click “**Verify your listing**” near the bottom of the listing.

![Verify listing is shown in the business profile on the Local maps page.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/08/ca/08ca7329d64374adf120a91ecda87802/1f671756918b633f9f7a1821573af8cb/AD_4nXdCBm2cY8xeFaJ2TtFWGZbBAkHkGMnz1WtDOaCl9d93MiweRMYR119oIzc2S2m-phxDSHJqlaMYo_-jPZ_WRDxXbD5aVWCmSV9UNjX3GQc0AkrkFGimxADqstRPFl0mZu4JFg5UMA.png)

You’ll then be directed to the page that lets you update your contact information.

## What Are the Benefits of Having a Yahoo Listing?

The benefit to claiming your Yahoo business directory listing is mainly that it helps you reach more prospective customers.

More specifically, having a Yahoo listing is good for:

- **Keeping your local business information consistent:** This ensures users have the correct details
- **Improving your organic (unpaid) rankings in Google:** Google looks for consistent and accurate information across [local directories](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-business-listing/) when determining what to prioritize in [local search](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-search/)
- **Connecting with people who use Yahoo to discover new businesses:** Appearing on Yahoo means more opportunities to reach new audiences
