# What steps ensure brand consistency across AI-driven platforms?

The steps to ensure brand consistency across AI-driven platforms start with making sure your brand information is accurate, consistent, and clear across the web.

Brand consistency in AI platforms is still a bit challenging because you don’t always have 100% control over what AI platforms say about your brand. However, you can try to influence AI outputs by managing your digital footprint more effectively.

Here are the essential steps for maintaining brand consistency:

- **Audit and update all digital properties regularly.** Ensure your website, social profiles, business directories, and Wikipedia (if applicable) all contain identical and accurate information about your products, pricing, and positioning.
- **Create a single source of truth.** Develop comprehensive brand guidelines and fact sheets that all teams reference when creating content, ensuring consistent messaging across all platforms.
- **Monitor AI outputs systematically.** Use tools like Semrush [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/en/) to track how different AI systems describe your brand and flag any inconsistencies or outdated information immediately
- **Respond quickly to inconsistencies.** When monitoring reveals outdated or incorrect information, update the source content immediately and track how quickly AI systems reflect the changes.
