# Webmaster Central YouTube update for June 22nd - 26th

Want to know what's new on the
[Webmaster Central YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral)?
Here's what we've uploaded in the past week:

As part of Google's goal to
[make the web faster](https://code.google.com/speed/),
we uploaded several video tips about optimizing the speed of your website. Check out
[the tutorials page](https://code.google.com/speed/articles/)
to view the tutorials and associated videos.

Matt Cutts answered a new question each day from the Grab Bag:

- [What are some best practices for moving to a new CMS?](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral#play/uploads/18/OoJAcUEasBU)
- [Is over-optimization bad for a website?](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral#play/uploads/8/Bz0KQNPDUoc)
- [Will having my software listed in low-quality directories harm my website's ranking?](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral#play/uploads/4/kXj73VDcSWk)
- [Can the geographic location of a web server affect SEO?](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral#play/uploads/3/keIzr3eWK8I)
- [Will I be penalized if all of my Sitemap URLs have the same priority?](https://www.youtube.com/c/GoogleSearchCentral#play/uploads/2/JfOk0gjNE5Y)

And during Adam Lasnik's visit to India, he was interviewed by Webmaster Help Forum guide
[Jayan Tharayil](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community/user?userid=10128391556890284137)
about issues related to webmasters in India. We have the
[full three-part interview](https://www.youtube.com/view_play_list?p=D9A24B47E308E6E2)
available.
