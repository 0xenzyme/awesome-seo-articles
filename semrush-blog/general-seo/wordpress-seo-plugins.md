# 16 Best WordPress SEO Plugins (Free & Paid)

Feeling overwhelmed by the sheer quantity of WordPress SEO plugins available? You’re not alone.

This article won’t try to list every WordPress SEO plugin.

Instead, we’ll share the most powerful plugins only.

Some plugins are free. Some aren’t. But they’re all useful.

Let’s start.

## 1. Semrush SEO Writing Assistant

Semrush’s [SEO Writing Assistant](https://wordpress.org/plugins/semrush-seo-writing-assistant/) plugin helps you write content optimized for both users and search engines.

![Semrush’s SEO Writing Assistant plugin](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f0/7a/f07ac04a40283d8154983d748e3b8458/1ihoOWg-BZ9HHPCww5zuuvl4ULx2hOH9gfrknjzQ3caxL4vl4GLj2U5hd-nEtkL6vgeRdRam3kB_dEgwBQrc1qCHy9qauZ32Mqj2KrZ5L2ihobittNXkbpRgvEQHsTOZ2XLr9SISCYloukNFK1aQMV0.png)

It scores your content based on readability, tone of voice, originality, and SEO. And offers advice to improve these aspects.

For example, if the plugin spots that your article’s title doesn’t include your target keyword—the search query you want to rank for in Google—it’ll highlight that as an issue. And recommend adding it to your title.

![SEO Writing Assistant plugin showing that article’s title doesn’t include target keyword](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/48/11/4811a9794e251c24eb62e86a060b2633/HDvotrteWGNUR9RzqjyBt7XWgY2_iWIp-LKh2IxWGuI1qM7Cn4IWGlQY7qTf3u4rzbyg1KLd8KjMnVbYckHdvPsNKQDm4CGX-BnP-mZuj2KaTgar4p_e86CM4AefcCkQhvFk8B_vtDRoJPtYzYTvs9A.png)

Or, if the plugin detects duplicate or plagiarized content, it will highlight the problematic text.

![SEO Writing Assistant plugin showing duplicate or plagiarized content](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0f/a3/0fa39d05d3a4a3e7f5da6bd51e993ca9/7Aa5VMJ9NlEP7lGCNqU1FJJdprSeJUyKKTKkcLcfckdz_WB4Obs-hRSy_gleW-oirLspMcm6DJjZ4UaRk7md-eWWt8Bg30k6jMeXmQE3HR5ZIN6Zjm4G6RBau2vic2BEA69NZABx7TCIPJva8XThvQs.png)

Having duplicate or plagiarized content on your website can lead to ranking problems (not to mention potential legal consequences if it’s plagiarized). The SEO Writing Assistant plugin is a valuable asset for your SEO strategy because it highlights issues like this.

### Key Features

The Semrush SEO Writing Assistant plugin comes with the following features:

- Recommendations on relevant keywords to use in your content for better Google rankings
- Rephraser feature to paraphrase content with AI
- Readability analysis to tell you how easy or difficult your content is to read
- Tone of voice analyzer to improve the writing style and tone of your content

And much more.

### Pricing

The SEO Writing Assistant is a freemium WordPress plugin. To use it, you need to have a Semrush account.

[Signing up for free](https://www.semrush.com/signup/) lets you check one article. To check more, subscribe to the [Guru plan](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/) for $208.33 per month (paid annually).

## 3. Yoast SEO

[Yoast SEO](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-seo/) is one of the most powerful SEO plugins for WordPress.

![Yoast SEO plugin](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c8/b5/c8b5128a159408626a2fe37bca30881d/tWpaOyHlFt0Jg3FVAGsY7Cq-dT8O9Wy9CD16xcUBJclvmkPyhGCrO2AXCCtXEuLU13Tm_BChta0tAJunKPX9eeGSeUYaW2TslsUIg-nmRwsFHHp42S1dsCaaj_FUPz8242O_NbIWkOWNNR_B_DAVa-M.png)

At its most basic level, Yoast allows you to set the [title tag](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/) (the title that shows in search results), [URL slug](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url-slug/) (the end portion of the URL), and [meta description](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/) (the brief summary of the page that may show in search results) on your pages. Which are all important from an SEO standpoint.

![set the title tag, URL slug, and meta description in Yoast SEO](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/43/5e/435eb50e24c631a869c5c92ee1456e1e/VujpEynwCBT3SloUWEBdqNmob3ouziCgKPfaNv3UHzFYhimsL8_BU6yEfbYcth0pJNw3i0365-xUKfZzwru4kFd1cUNwSeMhErDTqS9nS8M4sfB4Jt4l4IQAy1RLHgU_WlgLoRMu6BPzFMv1EBcD1Lo.png)

Plus, Yoast can help you manage technical tasks like:

- Setting up [canonical URLs](https://www.semrush.com/blog/canonical-url-guide/) (the main versions of pages that have duplicates) on your pages
- Setting up [meta robots tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/robots-meta/) (code that tells search engines how to crawl, index, and display the page)
- Providing titles for your pages’ [breadcrumb navigation](https://www.semrush.com/blog/breadcrumbs-for-websites/)
- Preventing search engines from indexing your pages
- Preventing search engines from following links on your pages

![Yoast SEO lets you manage technical tasks](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e7/3c/e73cbe98490ca28b5f10158b3a69f2c6/-S731Js1hodMil7k3vIImdlbZB6p5CFodlvb0-AC_3tGw374bxtx9iMjaJ43bDY9mXTh8kYG-9pMt6xiTg0eO2jxzjMHNZaxyppamg1hl7CI0UfEqceVjoYk7QYXfIdERgzKmChasMNTgRtU2D4mrq4.png)

When it comes to content-level optimization, Yoast identifies SEO-related issues in your content. And highlights opportunities for improvements.

!["Analysis results" page in Yoast SEO identifies SEO-related issues in your content](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/57/04/57048633f149762ae39cebb6244a3859/5f98bf194b0b9044fa46aa9ac11db2ce/JByVs0NX3aKzqyGnVI_flPdyDMLpmColIzVE21piyEPA3PiGYiLeG3aN8cJ8NokGHdDan08cLnR0DnKypTPNB3BZapnXchCruvC_OkmZyu_GqqKdz3bh7IYJtYqko0625o7Gx8e7EOywy8qU3xQ6PIc.png)

Note that these are just recommendations. And you don’t always have to follow every Yoast recommendation.

For example, Yoast suggests evenly distributing your focus keyword throughout the content.

But this is an outdated SEO tactic that won’t help you rank. In fact, it can even have the opposite effect in some cases.

Besides scanning your content for SEO-related issues, Yoast also analyzes your content’s readability. And provides suggestions for improvements. Such as:

- Using active voice
- Leveraging short sentences and paragraphs
- Making simple word choices

### Key Features

Here are a few stand-out features of Yoast:

- [Structured data](https://www.semrush.com/blog/schema-markup/) (also called schema) capabilities that make it easy to add different types of structured data to your website. This is code that helps search engines better understand your content, which can increase your chances of getting [rich snippets](https://www.semrush.com/blog/rich-snippets/).
- [Redirect management](https://www.semrush.com/blog/redirects/) that enables you to easily set up [301 redirects](https://www.semrush.com/blog/301-redirects/) for deleted pages so your users don’t encounter 404 errors
- [Internal linking](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/) suggestions based on words and phrases you could use to link to other content on your site
- [Semrush integration](https://yoast.com/features/semrush/) to get keyword suggestions directly in WordPress. This allows users to easily optimize content for the right keywords.

!["Related keyphrases" section in Yoast SEO](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/71/fc/71fc84005f4e8f0219aa0ca438f0c241/d3b8328591c4fc501ec231c597a02505/-dkxlL0AHP4P8NjeR9Z0lMvFPcW8aaDFQAoR1tAO5iKmz3_ooguEVrz715HyIbrdBWMtnjdbO4wh9SCBiaPUKBeD-kPE_LRNtMtESuJopSDUqYCSqyNayvc5NDT1yfe3_EsLEmkzhYvpLmxIwUUOMaY.png)

### Pricing

Yoast’s SEO plugin offers both a free and paid version.

With the free version, you’ll have limited features and functionality. The [premium version](https://yoast.com/wordpress/plugins/seo/) costs $99 per year and gives you access to all of Yoast’s features.

## 4. SEOPress

The [SEOPress plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wp-seopress/) allows you to improve your pages’ SEO from an intuitive user interface. Which makes it a popular choice for beginners and experts alike.

![SEOPress plugin](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/60/b0/60b01a922e44a8ce82b4a6d5f9b81ec2/XuVYrkLpU9-j_YvbBpWkDR_Bu-9OlXY-QyOOmUDpnZvAjvTQxB4bH5tkSLFCe1HD__3HinriHCGGp7dORsHo68-f4y_HMnei4mLbRO7Q_D7vptCtQ38PzV5_SABLcJt-P0vF6atlSuJdEfRiGjpXQT8.png)

SEOPress allows you to set up title tags and meta descriptions on your pages.

It will also show you if the title or meta description is too long. To help you make adjustments that prevent them from being cut off in search results.

![setting up title tags and meta descriptions in SEOPress](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/04/7a/047ac217215038599d580d49db87a3e5/YGCFOlX4P62XhE9jsQpWVad2gd0JWW78UWA4NOmQ-b3tHyPlrbZnifUsQLE-uVLES78IUukpDJJy6BGzggLhC2Rp-RhrFkV06FqPs5dW0UYkL41l--Gikln0jrsuYbeWuW7PjvbLftOM_FnY1d5dCa4.png)

Plus, SEOPress lets you define [Open Graph tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/open-graph/). These tags control how your preview links appear on social media platforms such as Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), LinkedIn, etc.

People are more likely to share content with optimized Open Graph tags. Because they make content more eye-catching in social media feeds.

SEOPress also has a feature that shows how well-optimized your page is for your target keyword(s).

!["Target keywords" page in SEOPress](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/20/1b/201bfa40098acdd518eca1b378086ba4/2b790df86ff93a046dd4700f8b026fef/C4zC3Vrum32ZfWvhRbuR6en3_XMuKN2w6Sc3RuKX7ExjHiOG5zRGdwbZ3g7wOMPaEWJMlGOvpVqX96MA0IQCn_4bLofL1nrenxwrRLu6GSXrWecVC99urNtHqI_ZTQKaqtaxw3SwcZL-3lCVSwraas4.png)

It looks at a variety of factors, including keyword density (how often your keyword appears in the text) and word count.

But those aren’t ranking factors and don’t tell you much about whether the page is optimized. So, focus on other elements like [canonical URL](https://www.semrush.com/blog/canonical-url-guide/) and structured data.

### Key Features

SEOPress has many solid features:

- Custom [XML sitemap](https://www.semrush.com/blog/xml-sitemap/) creation to help search engine bots find and index your pages
- Redirect manager that lets you set up 301, 302, and [307 redirects](https://www.semrush.com/blog/307-redirect/)
- Structured data support to implement different types of schema on your website
- OpenAI integration to automaticallygenerate title tags and meta descriptions with AI

### Pricing

SEOPress is a freemium WordPress plugin. The free version provides limited functionality.

The [paid version](https://www.seopress.org/pricing/) (which starts at $49 per year) provides additional features and more advanced functionality, such as the OpenAI integration, a redirect manager, etc.

## 7. WP Rocket

[WP Rocket](https://wp-rocket.me/) is a web performance plugin built to boost your site speed.

![WP Rocket plugin](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e1/a8/e1a8301a1b1a3f402c38b9e3f29b86c1/a-9kNaIHthZjjXppI5HZ0q7w4y4QEucZNVJfTXodhuKcA4Idm9_e3IjwINZxRlqhFP8SDjcJHY8LITei8YW8OiLu6cPIC1KtCDLl7k_N_3l8rmtcswR-Mfa1Va3e4bLnRTJR0GpdVn8tnhQRu0XmJy8.png)

[Site speed](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2010/04/using-site-speed-in-web-search-ranking) is a Google-confirmed ranking factor.

This plugin can help you make some useful optimizations to improve your site speed. Including browser and server caching.

Here’s what caching does:

- **Browser caching** saves common files directly on users’ devices so they don’t have to reload them the next time they visit your site
