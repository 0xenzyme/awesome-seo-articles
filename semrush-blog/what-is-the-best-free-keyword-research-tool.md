# What is the best free keyword research tool?

The best free [keyword research tool](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research-tools/) depends on your specific needs, but most users benefit from combining several free options for comprehensive research.

Different tools excel at different aspects of [keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/), so using multiple sources gives you better insights than relying on just one.

Top free keyword research tools include:

- **Semrush**[**Keyword Magic Tool**](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/). Provides comprehensive keyword data, [search volumes](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-search-volume/), and difficulty scores from a large keyword database
- [**Google Keyword Planner**](https://business.google.com/en-all/ad-tools/keyword-planner/)**.** Offers basic search volume data and keyword ideas, especially useful for Google Ads planning
- [**Google Search Console**](https://search.google.com/search-console/)**.** Shows keywords your site already ranks for, helping you identify optimization opportunities
- [**ChatGPT**](https://chatgpt.com/) **for keyword brainstorming.** Generates [long-tail keyword ideas](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-long-tail-keywords/) and content angles based on your topic
- [**Google Trends**](https://trends.google.com/trends/)**.** Identifies seasonal patterns and trending topics to time your content strategy
- [**Reddit**](https://www.reddit.com/) **and forums.** Discover how people actually talk about your topics and what questions they ask

For example, you might start with ChatGPT to brainstorm keyword ideas, use Keyword Magic Tool to find search volumes and related terms, then check Google Trends to see keywords growing in popularity.

Each tool serves different research needs, so combining multiple free options gives you the most complete picture for your keyword strategy.
