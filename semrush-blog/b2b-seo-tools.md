# 11 of the Best B2B SEO Tools for 2025

Business-to-business (B2B) SEO is about more than climbing rankings.

You pursue it for visibility. Your goal is to connect with business owners, decision-makers, and industry professionals who need your product or service.

Precision is key in this niche. The following B2B SEO tools will help you boost productivity, brand awareness, and conversion rates.

Semrush is a comprehensive online marketing platform that offers more than 55 tools and reports.

Its SEO toolkit has four main workflow groups:

- [Competitive Research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/competitive-analysis/)
- [Keyword Research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/)
- [Link Building](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/)
- [On-Page](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) and [Technical SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/)

Following these workflows covers all areas of your B2B website’s SEO.

## 2. Google (GSC, GA4, PageSpeed Insights)

Google’s search and website performance toolkit provides valuable data for making better SEO decisions.

The main tools are Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and PageSpeed Insights.

### Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

[Google Analytics 4](https://analytics.google.com/) shows how people find and use your website. It also shows how your site performs in paid search (i.e., [pay-per-click advertising (PPC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-ppc/)) and organic search.

![Google Analytics 4 home](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ca/2c/ca2c52622268934eda38dcf4d9456ea7/25709d3813319fb08be837b2461ee32f/AD_4nXc1hH33XifNGmfvoLW9gdd8-FgeONq6e8msobzv0DDs9fFRDyhvZhpqhPbZMveUrfTkro-xcJFGMoTy_kkN4nD4sTHbQ1cOTlDISXRyGq4c_YVwmXSgi30IZq6oWuLX6NZp66y2.png)

This data is crucial for measuring your B2B SEO strategy. It also reveals the impact of on-page SEO changes.

You’ll find data on:

- Users
- Sessions
- Page engagement
- Traffic sources

Use GA reports to assess your SEO efforts, fix inefficiencies, and repeat what works.

**Cost**: Free.

### Google Search Console

[Google Search Console (GSC)](https://search.google.com/search-console) monitors your site’s performance on Google’s SERPs. It also helps fix crawling and indexing issues.

!["Performance" and "Indexing" graphs in Google Search Console dashboard](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a2/77/a277545a69b132ab27ed1795de492ea0/b70f090af08aca71d2c34368e20b414a/AD_4nXdPElv5QPIhYaJlVA3Q7Y04O4YmRvzO1TNUX8yVBF3cxReF5uPt86Nbr6V80UNVqt9g4GpwTHLjllceXD-dQABk__nHDePAb69bhbsks2iqa4dpDxUQzorZkCNop1yRQrY99M1X.png)

GSC simplifies technical search data. It suits business owners with limited marketing or analytics experience, and reports are shareable with team members.

Submitting [sitemaps](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-sitemap/) and URLs for GSC to crawl helps Google index your site faster. That means your high-quality content can reach your audience sooner.

**Cost**: Free.

### PageSpeed Insights

[PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) measures your website’s technical performance and suggests ways to improve it. Faster pages can [boost your UX and ranking potential](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2020/05/evaluating-page-experience).

The tool checks your site’s [Core Web Vitals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals). This measures how fast pages load and how responsive they are.

Only [one-third of websites](https://www.semrush.com/blog/core-web-vitals-the-most-common-issues/) pass the Core Web Vitals threshold. Meeting these standards can help you beat competitors.

!["Core Web Vitals Assessment" results in PageSpeed Insights](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a3/64/a3646c34189da0b8eb75faa425a86d6b/d2def86c60f6b7654c6c09b037cea6dc/AD_4nXcJU6rYruBwe25q31zVIl3F2sI3vlWWeJWfK9x0bfr0CuX3v0PVj4BU20TfxWSsw8NWmWWAy8S8r-kb1RgIghIuAqvS1G7hF3t6qEWa_JOw3k-VmXWG2PFmn5bv7xE8mGO0FVy6.png)

Whether you pass or not, PageSpeed Insights offers recommendations for improving site performance.

!["Opportunities" section in PageSpeed Insights lists recommendations for improving your site](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7f/1d/7f1d63fe3d142a6fef276164da62bc9f/7c6bff91dca2c3b0b3746aeaf253db60/AD_4nXduIPFDG1Ss8OJI3zr4fuqdXwknWdxGVWSNmieorOgFt6BYjgZaXvSjgTyDzUAs89zBNowkchpJxi9TXi_BbMS44_W9X3GnwnykIlAeXsSBX1OKLVtrws8sJHcqRqm4ZTHti8_gAQ.png)

You get mobile and desktop insights—helpful for reaching business owners on different devices.

**Cost**: Free.

## 3. Screaming Frog SEO Spider

[Screaming Frog’s SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) finds technical and indexing issues that harm your site’s search performance.

It crawls your site like Google and checks for:

- [Broken links](https://www.semrush.com/blog/broken-link/) and faulty URLs
- [Duplicate content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/duplicate-content/)
- Missing metadata
- Unsuitable image sizes
