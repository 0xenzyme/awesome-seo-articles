# What is backlink analysis?

Backlink analysis is the process of evaluating the number and quality of external links pointing to your website. It helps you understand how these links influence your search rankings and reveals ways to build a stronger backlink profile.

When conducting a backlink analysis, look for factors like the linking sites’ authority, relevance to your content, and whether the links are follow or nofollow. Also assess the anchor text used in these links. This information helps you identify valuable links, spot those that could be harmful, and pinpoint opportunities for acquiring better links.

Based on your findings, you can:

- Build more follow or nofollow links for a natural distribution
- Increase or vary anchor text to improve distribution and relevance
- Target specific industries or pages that need more high-quality links
- Track link performance over time to measure improvements
- Remove or disavow links that appear spammy or low quality

By improving your site’s backlink profile, you boost credibility, which can lead to higher visibility and more organic traffic.
