# Introducing the Webmaster Video Series, now in Hindi

Google offers a broad range of resources, in multiple languages, to help you better understand
your website and improve its performance. The recently released
[Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Starter Guide](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide),
the
[Help Center](https://support.google.com/webmasters/h3#topic=3309469),
the
[Webmaster forums](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community/)
(which are available in 16 languages), and the various Webmaster blogs are just a few of them.

A few months ago, we launched the
[SEO Snippets](/search/blog/2017/12/the-seo-snippets-video-series)
video series, where the Google team answered some of the webmaster and SEO questions that we
regularly see on the Webmaster Central Help Forum. We are now launching a similar series in Hindi,
called the
[SEO Snippets in Hindi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MHUQcTgk8SE&list=PLKoqnv2vTMUO0d7lhi5lpPSHmLqs8pV8J).

From deciding what language to create content in (Hindi vs. Hinglish) to duplicate content, we're
answering the most frequently asked questions on the
[Hindi Webmaster forum](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community?hl=hi)
and the
[India Webmaster community on Google+](https://plus.google.com/communities/117770100248014494227),
in Hindi.

Check out the links shared in the videos to get more helpful webmaster information, drop by our
[help forum](https://support.google.com/webmasters/go/community)
and subscribe to our
[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWf2ZlNsCGDS89VBF_awNvA)
for more tips and insights!
