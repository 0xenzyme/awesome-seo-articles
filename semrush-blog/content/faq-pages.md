# 6 Examples of FAQ Pages and How to Write a Great One

## What Is an FAQ Page?

An FAQ page is a dedicated page on a website that answers frequently asked questions (FAQs) related to the business.

Many businesses have FAQ pages to help site visitors understand their products and services. And solve common problems.

For example, here’s Stella & Chewy’s FAQ page:

![Dog food FAQ page with question search box, faqs list, and contact us button.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/aa/45/aa45c4f8159879a9f23ef8d30ce0adf3/0448611168c771a00ede7a373d6be8df/9GWvUvlBbBbn44jdFI7vcG0mGYqQaKBUfSdrWWHIik0l4lUn4KejBxLNDms0T4sIxYBU349IpONe-5A5xBxzLDn9rC21KFMj4JXyWlcBW-eajrnwQERmMi5tw2E_BqC8V4tDNzX5uj-djk69VyQbQ5w.png)

It’s an effective FAQ page because it anticipates the audience's needs. It also answers common questions and guides visitors to other relevant site content.

FAQ pages may also include questions and answers about:

- Common industry questions
- Operating hours
- Return policies
- Payment options
- Shipping information
- Contact options
- Jobs

## Why You Need an FAQ Page

Here are the benefits of having an FAQ page.

### It Increases Site Traffic and Online Visibility

When people search for answers related to your business, your FAQ page can appear in search results. So creating an optimized FAQ page can improve your online presence.

For example, WhatsApp’s FAQ page about disappearing messages is a [featured snippet](https://www.semrush.com/blog/featured-snippets/) on the Google results page:

![google search for "what are disappearing messages on whatsapp" shows featured snippet for whatsapp help center](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/49/10/491092d12780119db8ae34b837554c9b/c41ac6584c7cb2f908b0c826e0ed38b8/2H-BDLTlxL56ZD5gXwm-3NqJHCBubh53THYGanPvTjuXv9QBhMilcUQbudRCQvKIho5PlVYXPTzy3dLeA-by3cKuGJh5Ewvs9hvNuxctHTYjuzq0Z6_kYn22QEKymfYTbAlPDfdZVgL-OVsRVJs5ENk.png)

FAQ pages are good for SEO as they have the potential to drive significant, relevant organic traffic. By targeting [question keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/question-keywords/) that potential customers use to learn more about your brand or products.

For example, WhatsApp’s FAQ page gets 650.8K visitors per month, according to Semrush’s [Organic Rankings tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/).

![WhatsApp FAQ page shows 658k in traffic via Organic Rankings tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/89/eb/89ebe83860ead77b078d4035a9dfb411/bffb2f48a330bcc397494cffb7e76316/GG2HazUEn6q9EuupXUiei0Q_EjTSPgUwqpYeig9_yNS-xb_ZuTBWZfB1q7VUlEhiubSCwZ9Al5F3FVT5cZ3h8vilVFTSZVXKY81DmBNE4xxXKHj_FGI6WIOwkIOYchNyF61OcaZOmlJk1Wp9vdKv_iw.png)

Because the page is ranking in the top 10 for several high-volume [question keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/question-keywords/), like:

- “How to know if someone blocked you” - 4.4K searches per month
- “How to download whatsapp” - 1.3K searches per month
- “How to delete whatsapp group” - 1.6K searches per month

![organic search positions report in the organic rankings tool for whatsapp faq keywords](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/67/41/674153f21d0c05e6b8730a985fa50c62/d049d853d28471711beb2d4f7949c7cb/w5m9IhS5JchiS-DxNWj97k4SAF_aacPQ2zYOWMD7pUKiog0iNLF7A_Z-LO7qSQyWvaPNhPkWXXLIeMfgSR82PSzsKyPJ8rdKLuZCUjH_B69akk5nHRUphqFSA8teZFDIwVbj5dtdyY-MGh86BzDndMY.png)

### It Improves the Customer Experience

An FAQ page improves the customer experience. Because it helps users get answers faster. Rather than having to dig deep into your website or reach out to customer service.

More specifically, FAQ pages do the following:

- Give potential customers information to aid their decision-making process
- Make key information readily available, which shows that your business is transparent and values customer satisfaction

When people find answers to their questions, they may forgo contacting support. Which can save your staff time and resources.

### It Strengthens Your Authoritativeness

FAQ content establishes authority, giving you an edge over competitors who may not be openly addressing common questions.

For example, Stella & Chewy’s uses their FAQ page to establish that they are dog food experts. By answering questions like, “How do I transition (my dog to new food)?”

![Brand links to tips pages to walk animal owners through transitioning pet friends to new food](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/03/9e/039e3f9db7270c8c28c91b64e4b94542/69ca9393fe6e0ea4d06ffc286202e943/chmoo7E8EeXSJcVyHjeto1318B3QRYfixTnrpSwDdr2MjBWa4tOj25c-2dT1xQfZpgvjbCm-TnG9-4Qj0J7KrTEKQDfn_f_uFaWvoofLRo57YnUii0WrFO2afESh3yRIV4flKemIbggg4ui1Q10HF44.png)

Doing this can demonstrate your expertise. Because your answers showcase your knowledge. Which can lead to increased customer trust.

## How to Create an Effective FAQ Page

Create an FAQ page that drives traffic and helps customers. With these five tips.

### Uncover Questions Your Customers Actually Ask

The primary goal of your FAQ page is to answer your customers’ and potential customers’ questions. So that they don’t need to contact your support team every time they need help.

There are two ways you can uncover what those questions are:

**First, ask your customer support and sales teams.**

Those two teams coordinate with customers on a daily basis. Ask them to compile recurring questions. And add those questions to your FAQ page.

If you’re using help desk or call center software like [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6040998-categorize-articles-based-on-tags#h_074564c3f9) or [Zendesk](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408835056154-Using-labels-on-your-help-center-articles), review the customer data.

This includes listening to recorded calls, looking through written details about specific customer issues, etc.

**Second, use Google Search Console. To see questions that you’re already ranking for.**

Sign in to [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/welcome) and select your site in the property drop-down.

Next, click on the “**Performance**” tab in the left-hand navigation menu.

![performance tab highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/06/7e/067ea0a4ec32141b1ffbff4528cdb2ef/498fcb18d1660a20e57975b0c9fd2a27/ZBkV-GMQPgqkcjws4VWpDnDw0BrU7nRIUmx9n0cBcTU_CDsDo-3LFZ6EtyIN1aa9qtaw5kbFUTS9KMJp3UpomGmLkx2hatxhAna67MDk26S0Y9_uIzlKu-7H7WHxTzDYNCtLuaedgZcqeecgy70WJ08.png)

And scroll down to “Queries” to see a list of search terms that you’re already ranking for.

![queries table with example search terms and their clicks and impressions data](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/01/07/0107c33f618ef3f29f1147f34daa043b/3f136fc1a507b6064422f122952773dd/g3S9hpZpGxCsPVByp-g9u2-WdQWQHfszFzuTSw0oCmEzpEhTKak42z1z9eZShtiFJUGa80KlgREmAo3I2JQ2cp63UEsKMe2RRQZt3wu0mN37gR2UVOjsG36jN48corH3mERYuzjSkVNXPMAXRwcW6vk.png)

Now, let’s narrow that list of queries. Into a list of question keywords.

Create a filter by clicking “**+****New**” > “**Query…**” at the top of the screen.

![at the top of the performance report the new query option is highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/35/45/35452f7164534ed2bc1b915f03e4695f/9e3f39115957b17d36aaf4b14d72fbd2/sBNSBGvaEsROscV4edLR6CRbXq1TmVVNmsa_rBXUKg0oB9Knc0iHdtptRvu3HC1o_QBzybCcvihirbDJyZ6IEJXZ57hb5uPI29k6XWnI6l9r475p5-mvQ42Q1Etruyg7QzjW4JR69VN00bMCKlfBLyI.png)

And, hit “**Custom (regex)**.”

![Queries containing drop down shows Custom regex option](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/19/8f/198fb7efb5c634cce8eef8e98ab9fb02/40f0ab3751d8d2a31a08cb6e3b5eb2b4/Slo3ZfYN8gXGDzu3UHAbu-OpoxX_R8OTyv8DiDALZb8_21pNh_Q1du5xs8LdjQ6WIlDm7N1GT3tgXSNXozspKeRzr4AamdptVPSEDU3BnWhu8UiOuV3w4YRpZU1XKNe7UZ0dklLrl3fUZxv7_DBG3rw.png)

Finally, paste the following expression into the open field. And click the “**Apply**” button.

`^(how|what|when|where|who|why)[" "]`

![expression pasted into the "enter regular expression" field](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/93/bd/93bd3761c45abeae1856d0c552f61657/51b8b3d276d8bc0dd4fedc6aa5188365/o4V44X5gkiGq7rvbnOnAete_dE_bKhr3VZjdNyH_cuiGvWMHNTeFvy5x8GcSFQbT2PGUS3ZyXHCcRMuW3YOc22BV9lCxUV9IIk0MapxVdLpWBEjoNjjyXt4YceTvUfUFrUKbD4u9oXZb5UmoGjwJU_Y.png)

The table will show you all question-based search queries.

Comb through the list to choose the most relevant questions for your business. And add them to your FAQ page.

![updated queries table with search terms containing who, what, when, where, why, and how](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b7/4f/b74f1d4b77228961d03d8a9bba9fd8f6/ff70ae3c73fd4c264b42f7fb8cb15cdf/ElsdoXlXenXpZv0MNvm1tn0LZWa6_BFq2DrxcWD8lgu6vH718MuOEqR2aOeVtJ9tSX1OLfTRzVHJ6N8S0z-QOWYKbOoCw4qy6nfmwDKrN4YlwMoAUtbNiP0vSpI1zoTFh_FnGVFxil8Uf7daZfCXCyk.png)

### Research Question Keyword Opportunities

[Keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/) can help you identify questions people enter into search engines.

Start your research by plugging your industry or brand name into the Semrush [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).

Let’s use the cosmetics company Lush to demonstrate.

Enter the brand name, choose your target country, and click the “**Search**” button.

![Search "Lush" brand name in keyword magic tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/15/34/1534d9d90b38129d928fb039e80d346e/11c5fffe0875561744063f4fff31aa35/V_KHF3GRL2NLlYeVeVPEdaTH8iixpW2LXgpxqQ-HXvvqYHRimtAJzXUcvuBoUiNVuHflLSqV-oWTxc4Nj2D8KwKEzqu-XfqdB3nvKOJt-GuwFpa9YcHUURwFWOri6bDvfwUQg5gJ2PhlymntfVoav0o.png)

You’ll see a list of keywords related to “Lush.”
