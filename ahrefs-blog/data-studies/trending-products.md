# Trending Products 2025: USA’s Fastest Climbers and Viral Giants (July Snapshot)

This edition surfaces the hottest U.S. consumer‑interest spikes using 6‑month Google‑search growth. We group items by growth‑curve shape (linear vs. exponential) and current size (emerging vs. popular) so marketers can see both scale and acceleration at a glance.

## Key Insights

- **Sports‑influencer merch is the standout story.** *Shedeur Sanders Browns jersey* posts a 700 000 % leap, confirming that NIL‑driven drops can reach nationwide search volume overnight.
- **Affordable luxury—fragrance & food—moves up fast.** *Nyla perfume* and *Dubai chocolate strawberries* show consumers are treating themselves in sub‑$50 splurges.
- **Trading‑card nostalgia widens.** Four separate booster‑box or set names sit in the overall top‑20, signalling a resurgence well beyond flagship Pokémon SKUs.
- **Footwear tech trends across styles and age groups.** From *Nike V5 RNR* to kids’ *Hoka Clifton 9*, searchers are hunting both performance and novelty silhouettes.
- **Bio‑hacking gadgets break out of niche.** The *Hydroh hydrogen water bottle* keeps exponential lift, while seed‑oil and ingredient transparency queries rise in parallel.
- **DIY home & specialty tools pick up linear steam.** Terms like *Great Stuff Wide Spray* and *Walabot DIY 2* hint at summer renovation season.
- **Micro‑tech upgrades remain sticky.** *Narwal Freo X Ultra* and *Galaxy 25 Ultra* show that even high‑volume electronics can still achieve double‑digit growth.

## Methodology

- **Data source:** Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, data exports captured **July 3–4, 2025,** covering the top 20 keywords per bucket.
- **Window:** 6‑month growth.
- **Curve fit** : “Linear” vs. “Exponential” designations come straight from Ahrefs’ trend analysis.
- **Popularity cutoff** – ≥**10 000** U.S. monthly searches → *popular*; otherwise *trending*.
- **Ranking** – Sort by highest 6‑month % growth; ties break on search volume.

## How to find trending products in your niche

[Ahrefs’ Keywords Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/keywords-explorer) gives you access to the same database we used in this study. Follow these steps:

1. Enter types of products specific to your niche (aka [seed keywords](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/seed-keywords)). For example, in pet supplies these could be “calming vests, car seat covers, cat leashes, cooling mats, dog bandanas, dog collars, gps trackers, orthopedic beds”, etc. Make sure to select the right country.
2. Go to the Matching terms report.
3. Make sure to enable growth metrics in the columns setting (3, 6, or 12 months).
4. Sort the results by growth. You can also set additional filters, such as minimum search volume or keyword difficulty (KD).

![How to find trending products](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/how-to-find-trending-products-scaled.jpg)

Tip: if your initial keyword list is mixed, use the Ecommerce preset. This will show you keywords with shopping results and/or shopping ads in the search engine result pages.

Examples:

tip

New in Ahrefs: now you can also apply trend type filters.

- **Linear growth**: Demand increases steadily over time at a consistent rate.
- **Exponential growth**: Demand starts off slow but then surges rapidly.
- **S-curve growth**: Demand builds gradually, takes off quickly, then levels out as the market saturates.
- **Damped growth**: Demand spikes early but quickly loses momentum and flattens.

## How to find trending products on ecommerce platforms like Amazon

This method shows products sold on ecommerce platforms like Amazon or eBay with the highest growth in popularity on Google Search.

Start by adding keywords related to your niche or do a blank search in Keywords Explorer to search Ahrefs’ entire keyword database. Make sure to select the correct country.

Next:

1. If you’re not doing a blank search, go to the Matching terms report first. Otherwise, skip to step 2.
2. Enter the ecommerce platform’s URL in the Target filter. Set the filter to “Show keywords target ranks in top 10”.
3. Turn on the Ecommerce preset.
4. Sort the results by Growth.
