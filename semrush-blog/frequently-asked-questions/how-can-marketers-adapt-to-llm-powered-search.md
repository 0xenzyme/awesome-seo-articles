# How can marketers adapt to LLM-powered search?

Marketers can adapt to LLM-powered search by trying out different optimization strategies that will lead to their brand being cited more frequently and accurately in LLM responses.

The key to success is understanding that LLMs don't just look at your website—they evaluate your entire digital footprint to determine whether you're a credible source worth referencing when users ask questions about your industry.

You need to rethink some of the traditional [SEO techniques](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-techniques/). Instead of focusing on individual keywords and rankings, you need to establish your brand as the authoritative source that AI systems naturally reference when answering user queries.

Here's how marketers can successfully adapt:

- **Build topical authority, not just keyword rankings.** Develop [content clusters](https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/) that thoroughly cover all aspects around the high-level topics, establishing your site as the go-to resource AI systems cite.
- **Establish credibility across the web.** Earn mentions on authoritative sites, participate in industry forums, and maintain consistent brand information everywhere AI systems might look
- **Focus on proving unique value and data.** Create original research, share data, and provide insights that AI can't generate on its own, making your content essential to cite
- **Monitor your AI visibility actively.** Track how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and [Google AI Overview](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) responses to understand your current presence and identify gaps.
- **Make sure your website is accessible to LLMs**. Check your robots.txt file to ensure AI crawlers aren’t blocked from accessing your website.
- **Chunk your content for easy extraction**. Break your content into clear sections with descriptive headings so LLMs can easily pull specific answers without losing context.
