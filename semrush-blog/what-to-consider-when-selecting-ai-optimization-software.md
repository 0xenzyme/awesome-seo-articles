# What to consider when selecting AI optimization software?

When selecting AI optimization software, consider the features, price point, user interface, and the learning curve required for your team to effectively use the tool.

Making the right choice will set you up for success. The wrong software can waste time and budget while failing to deliver the insights you need to improve your AI visibility.

Here are the key factors to evaluate:

- **Feature set**: Make sure the software covers your specific needs
- **Pricing**: Check if the software is within your budget
- **User interface:** See if the software hasa clean, intuitive interface. This will save time and make your team actually want to use the tool day-to-day
- **Platform support and coverage:** Check if the software monitors all major AI platforms—ChatGPT, Perplexity. Claude, Google, and others.
- **Learning curve and onboarding**: Evaluate how quickly your team can become productive and whether the vendor provides training resources
