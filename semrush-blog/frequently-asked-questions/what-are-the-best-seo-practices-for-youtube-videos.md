# What are the best SEO practices for YouTube videos?

The best SEO practices for [YouTube](https://www.semrush.com/blog/youtube-seo/) videos include optimizing your titles, descriptions, and content to rank higher in YouTube search and attract more viewers.

Start with keyword research using YouTube's search suggestions and tools like Semrush's [Keyword Analytics for YouTube](https://www.semrush.com/apps/keyword-analytics-for-youtube/) to find topics your audience actually searches for.

Key YouTube SEO practices include:

- **Create compelling titles with target keywords.** Include your main keyword naturally while making titles that encourage clicks
- **Design eye-catching thumbnails.** Use bright colors and clear text that stand out on mobile screens
- **Optimize video descriptions.** Put your main keyword in the first two lines with a clear summary of what viewers will learn
- **Add timestamps for different sections.** Help YouTube understand your content structure and let viewers jump to specific topics
- **Upload accurate captions.** YouTube indexes spoken words for search, improving your video's discoverability
- **Focus on viewer retention.** Create engaging content that keeps people watching since YouTube prioritizes high watch time
- **Organize content into playlists.** Group related videos together to keep viewers watching more of your content

Success on YouTube depends on balancing traditional SEO elements like keywords with engagement factors like watch time and viewer retention.
