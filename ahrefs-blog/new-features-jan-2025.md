# Web Analytics additions, share of voice charts in Rank Tracker, and more (Jan 2025)

This month we’re introducing some powerful Web Analytics additions, SoV + SoTV metrics and charts in Rank Tracker, and more.

Let’s get right into it.

## Web Analytics

**Google Search Console integration**

We’ve integrated Web Analytics with Google Search Console data. Just filter by `Source: is Google` to see the keywords that are actually getting you clicks under Traffic sources.

![](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/bloggscintegr.png)

**Visitor languages**

The Languages widget shows you your visitors’ browser languages, so you can tweak your content strategy accordingly.

**Page Inspect in Web Analytics**

Click the magnifying class in the Inspect column to open Page inspect and see the HTML history for pages directly in Web Analytics – no need to leave the tool.

## Site Explorer

**Keyword groups in positions chart**

The Organic keywords report now features a position chart that shows keyword distribution by positions over time.

This lets you easily track the movements and performance of keywords by popular ranking groups.

**Note**: this chart is already in Overview, but now you can access all the filters here to slice your data even more.

## Rank Tracker

**SoV + SoTV metrics and charts**

Use the Share of Voice and Share of Traffic Value metrics to measure your brand’s popularity and visibility for your tracked keywords across organic search.

**Available in**: Dashboard, Overview, and Competitors Overview reports.

**GSC overview > Keyword groups in positions chart**

We’ve added a keywords history chart with position groups in the GSC overview report.

Note: This feature doesn’t exist in Google Search Console itself, so take full advantage of it in Ahrefs to track performance for different position groups and adjust your content strategy accordingly.

**GSC overview > Page history chart**

Use the new page history chart to see how your published pages have evolved over time and cross-reference them with performance.

## General

**Project boosts available for all plans**

You can now purchase Project Boost add-ons for all verified projects, even if you’re on a free Ahrefs Webmaster Tools plan.

On top of all additional features listed, applying a Boost to a project also grants you 1 month of historical data and unlocks all reports in Site Explorer for that project.

For example, boosting a project while on a Starter or Lite plan lets you use advanced reports like:

- Page Inspect
- Site Structure
- Calendar
- Broken Backlinks
- Linking Authors
- Outgoing Anchors
- Outgoing Links

[Learn more about Project Boosts →](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10130957-what-are-project-boost-add-ons)
