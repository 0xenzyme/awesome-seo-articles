# Help Us Pick the Next Stop in Europe for Search Central Live Deep Dive 2026!

**Registration is now open!** Read our blog post announcing
[registrations](/search/blog/2026/07/search-central-live-deep-dive-europe-2026)
for a chance to secure your seat at Search Central Live Deep Dive Europe 2026.

Thursday, June 18, 2026

As we mentioned a
[few months ago](/search/blog/2026/03/scl-apac-2026#an-update-on-the-deep-dive-format),
we are bringing the Search Central Live Deep Dive format to the EMEA region. This SCL format
requires finding the absolute best home for the event—a place where all of you can truly
connect, learn, and enjoy.

![Search Central Live Deep Dive Europe 2026 logo](https://services.google.com/fh/files/events/logo-search-central-deep-dive-europe-color.png)

After much consideration, we have narrowed our primary candidate list down to six incredible
cities, and **we want your feedback to help us make the final call**.

Along with the location, we're looking for your input on the best timing. We also want to open
submissions for Community Lightning Talks early, so let us know what you'd love to share or hear
about. Drop your thoughts in the interest form!

## What is Search Central Live Deep Dive?

[Search Central Live Deep Dive](/search/events/search-central-live-deep-dive)
is a multi-day, in-person event series organized by the Google Search Central team that focuses
intensely on Google Search and SEO. Developed directly from community feedback requesting a more
comprehensive learning structure, it expands upon standard Search Central Live events into a
multi-day conference.

While standard events cover high-level topics in brief sessions, the Deep Dive format allocates
extended time for:

- **In-Depth Technical Discussions:** Highly detailed walkthroughs exploring the
  mechanics of Search, including crawling, indexing, ranking, and AI.
- **Hands-On Workshops:** Practical, interactive activities centered on essential
  tools like Google Search Console and Google Trends.
- **Real-World Case Studies:** Practical insights built around real examples, with
  opportunities for attendees to showcase their own optimization journeys.
- **Enhanced Community Engagement:** Activities like community talks, lightning
  talks, and poster sessions designed to connect participants and foster collaborative learning
  across Europe.

The SCL Deep Dive event is tailored to site owners, web developers, and SEO professionals who want
to move beyond basic concepts and fully master advanced search mechanics.

## Help us with the event specifics: Location, Time

We want to design this event around the community. To make that happen, we need your input on
these critical elements:

1. **The Location:** Our team has mapped out a few strong options based on logistics,
   accessibility, and cost:
   - Barcelona, Spain
   - Budapest, Hungary
   - Berlin, Germany
   - Frankfurt, Germany
   - Lisbon, Portugal
   - Prague, Czech Republic
2. **The Timing:** We are looking at running the event between **mid-September
   and early October**. Help us choose the ideal days of the week and the number of days
   so we can maximize attendance and ensure the schedule works best for your professional
   calendar.

## Propose Lightning Talks

Through community talks, poster sessions, and networking exchanges, you'll have the chance to
showcase your work, share unique experiences, and build lasting connections in a collaborative
environment.

Want to take the stage? We are officially accepting early Lightning Talk proposals using the
interest form. If you have advanced tool tips, deep-dive case studies, or optimization journeys
you're eager to share, pitch them now in the interest form.

By proposing your topic now, our team can start reviewing it immediately. While we won't finalize
decisions until applications officially open, this gives you a great head start and puts you on
our radar ahead of the official application window. We will follow up with you about the outcome
then. (And don't worry—if your plans change and you decide not to apply for the event later
on, there are no strings attached).

## Interested? Fill out the form

[Express interest and propose your lightning talk at SCL Deep Dive Europe 2026!](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/search-central-live-eu-2026)

We're still deciding on the location and fine-tuning the agenda. **If you are interested in
attending the event between mid-September and early October, please fill out
[this interest form](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/search-central-live-eu-2026)

to help us create the best possible experience.
**Applications will open by July 6, 2026**, when we will also announce the exact date
and location of the event. Because we're targeting a diverse audience in Europe, the event will be

your idea in the same form!**

Filling out the interest form helps us build the event framework, but it does not automatically
register you for the actual conference. While you will still need to sign up again once official

form to your registration profile. By submitting your feedback now, you guarantee that you will be
the first to know when we officially announce the Deep Dive dates and location. Furthermore, if
you pitch a lightning talk now, we will already start reviewing your proposals!

If you're located outside of Europe and are interested in traveling to attend, feel free to fill
out the form. However, be aware that this event will be primarily catered to a Europe-based
audience, meaning the content, examples, and style of activities and hospitality will be focused
on the European markets. Note that we are
[unable to provide assistance](/search/events/search-central-live#expandable-2) with
accommodation, visa, or transportation, including associated costs and logistics.
