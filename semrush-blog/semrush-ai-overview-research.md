# How to Track AI Overviews with Semrush

As of December 2025, Google’s AI Overviews appear in about 15% of search results according to [Semrush Sensor](https://www.semrush.com/sensor/).

And many of those summaries pull from platforms like Reddit, Quora, and YouTube—often before they cite traditional websites.

Because Google Search Console doesn’t report AI Overview impressions or mentions, it’s hard to know when your brand is included, how often you’re cited, or which competitors are gaining visibility you’re not.

Semrush’s AI Visibility Toolkit fills that gap with [tools to monitor AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-tools-to-monitor-ai-overviews/). It reveals where your brand appears across AI platforms—including Google’s AI Overviews—shows which pages and topics are being cited, and uncovers the prompts and competitors shaping that visibility.

This guide walks you through how to analyze your current presence, find new opportunities, and track your AI Overview performance using Semrush. So you can build a strategy based on real data, not guesswork.

## Step 1: Benchmark Competitor AIO Visibility

Before diving into metrics, start by establishing a baseline for what “good” looks like in your niche.

A competitor benchmark helps you understand who’s leading, where you stand, and where you can realistically gain ground. Without that baseline, your own numbers won’t have much context.

There are a few ways to evaluate how a domain/brand is visible in AI Overviews:

- Mentions - instances where a brand name is mentioned in the AI Overview’s text summary
- Cited Pages - pages from the domain are cited as sources in AI Overviews
- Keywords - search queries where a domain is cited/ranking in the AI Overview

To quickly check a competitor’s **AI Overview****mentions** and **cited pages**, enter their domain into the Semrush search bar to open [Domain Overview.](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview/)

At the top you’ll see metrics from the AI Visibility Toolkit showing this domain’s AI search presence.

![AI Overview mentions and citations shown on Semrush](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1c/94/1c94a9a37cf5067523d7cb12fa20f2ba/467b35321b8da5e15f8018ac401339b2/image.png)

Look for the AI Overview row to check this domain’s current number of mentions and cited pages within Google’s AI Overviews.

In this example, the domain has **114 mentions** and **1,200 pages****cited** in an AI Overview.

These numbers give you a high-level benchmark to now compare your own site to.

Next, to see how many keywords trigger AI Overviews for that domain, go to [Organic Rankings](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/overview/?db=us).

**Tip**:You can open Organic Rankings by clicking on the blue number under “Organic Traffic” in the Domain Overview.

From Organic Rankings, click on the **“Positions”** tab**.** Then filter by the **“SERP Features”** button.

With the SERP Features filter, select “Domain ranks” → “AI Overview”

![AI overview keywords shown on Semrush](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a4/6c/a46cc0268bc5c3993e159b34efcb5740/2be25bf83424b41a99da2f52909cb709/image.png)

This tells you the number of keywords where that domain ranks within the AI Overview. In the example above, it's **640 keywords**.

By now, you have three different benchmark metrics about this domain’s AI Overview visibility:

- 114 mentions
- 1,200 cited pages
- 640 keywords

Repeat those steps for a few competitors (and your site) to get a benchmark of the overall competition for AI Overviews in your niche.

## Step 2: Discover Your AI Overview Keyword Opportunities

Once you’ve outlined the competitive landscape, the next step is to identify keywords that trigger AI Overviews and align with your site’s strengths.

These terms represent your highest-opportunity chances for visibility and inclusion inside AI-generated summaries.

### Use the Organic Rankings Tool

In Organic Rankings, scroll down to the “**Organic Search Positions**” table to see the exact keywords where your competitor ranks alongside AI Overviews.

![organic search positions with AI Overviews](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c2/18/c218f5fdde49ab91a612bd8bfe063d5f/ae499b7314d16bda62db7f88491efe1f/image.png)

Look for:

- Relevant terms that align with your content strategy
- Content gaps where you could create more useful or in-depth content
- High-value topics with strong search demand

To zero in on the most actionable opportunities, use a few key filters:

- "**Advanced filters**" > "**Exclude**" > "**Keyword type**" > "**Branded**" > “**Apply**” to remove branded searches from your list. This leaves you with non-branded keywords—terms where you have a better chance to compete.

![applying a filter to exclude branded keywords](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/07/89/07895abcbb9b98dd459297eea3ec4fc0/f773ea6cff5248ab9f1a78d02bb9c614/image.png)

- “**SERP Features**” > “**SERP Features on SERP**” > “**AI Overview**:”This filter displays all of your competitor’s keywords where an AI Overview appears. This includes those where the competitor is not featured, even if they rank elsewhere on the SERP.

![applying a filter for AI Overviews](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/87/17/87177989b8eff5aaf4b488323c801212/1471c6df3be56206cf6f27e69734d631/image.png)

Explore the table of keywords, or export the list to save your research.

## Step 3: Monitor AI Overview Citations with Position Tracking

It’s helpful to check your data at least weekly to spot patterns. Signs of progress include:

- More keywords triggering AI Overviews where your domain appears
- Improved positions on SERPs that include an AI Overview
- New citations or mentions showing up in an AI Overview

[Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) lets you monitor your AI Overview presence over time so you can measure whether your optimizations are actually working.

Studies show that a few user-generated content (UGC) websites like [Quora, Reddit, and YouTube](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/#h-4-quora-is-the-most-cited-website-in-google-ai-overviews) get an overwhelming amount of visibility on Google’s AI Overviews.

![Most cited domains in AIOs](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d8/b4/d8b41bad4db70041d33ee73e9ec63e4f/b8a28b3d17b2c55b7e7cf0b530aeca84/AD_4nXcBzIipk8dPbOQvJWzV05AgAYbWK4ZWqey8iKsIJE1dkADQD7xxpgFDOiApthXxUnMoCTFwVSfv_2SoXiBVNHvtarCmHC7Mekwil5vn0YNQyhOffW-Coxq_PrmpqaoDfsoVH3qA.png)

This isn’t random. Google often includes experience-based and community-validated sources when generating AI Overviews.

As a result, platforms where real people share detailed answers and discussions are more likely to be cited in an AI Overview.

For brands, this creates an opportunity. Having a presence on the UGC platforms Google already trusts can increase your chances of appearing in AI Overviews—even when your main site isn’t ranking yet.

Be careful not to get overly promotional. Reddit and other UGC communities are famously skeptical of marketing.

Instead of pushing products, focus on social listening, engaging respectfully where appropriate, and learning from what people naturally say about your brand.

## Estimating the Value of AI Overview Visibility

We’re still in uncharted territory when it comes to measuring the impact of AI Overview visibility.

The value is less about direct search traffic and more about signaling brand reputation and authority.

When your brand is cited in an AI Overview about your niche, you gain a valuable moment of visibility over your competitors.

## Turn AI Overview Insights Into Strategy

Identifying keywords that trigger AI Overviews is valuable, but it’s how you respond that makes the difference.

### Don’t Treat All Keywords Equally

Some AIO-triggering keywords are worth your time. Others aren’t. Focus on:

- **Keywords tied to commercial intent**: Check the Traffic Cost in Organic Rankings to prioritize terms with business value.
- **SERPs where no strong competitor is featured in the AIO**: These are low-competition openings, especially when the AIO pulls from vague or non-authoritative sources.
- **Your own mentions inside AIOs**: If your brand is cited, even without a link, consider it a signal. Strengthen that page and expand its internal and external visibility.

### Increase Off-Page Signals to Support Inclusion

Google doesn’t just pull from your site. It pulls from trusted sources, including third-party platforms. Boost your chances of being mentioned in an AI Overview by:

- **Growing branded search and mentions**: A higher branded search volume may [increase AIO inclusion](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-visibility/). Use partnerships, campaigns, and brand awareness ads to spark interest around your name. Like when Lego and Adidas teamed up for this eye-catching partnership: ![sample brand partnership post with Lego and Adidas](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ff/ff/ffff9d73c00798e93f75dac7356c9033/08d932dbcf534b8ae62a8a2590d79d58/image.png)
- **Posting on credible external platforms**: Google often cites content from LinkedIn Pulse and similar sources. [Share insights on LinkedIn](https://www.semrush.com/blog/linkedin-seo/), YouTube, Reddit, Quora, or industry blogs to broaden your footprint. Like this AI Overview that cites multiple Reddit and Quora pages:

![sample AI Overview for "is beekeeping expensive"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/70/16/7016e7847e5e8d2988384c23be14b9b5/e1372e5a51681bde641b45f795ed3dd2/image.png)

- **Running** [**digital PR**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/digital-pr/) **and outreach**: Guest posts, podcasts, and media features build trust signals that improve your visibility, both in traditional rankings and in AI-generated results. For example, see how much this AI Overview cites various media sources for its results:

![sample of PR impact on AI Overviews](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/00/e4/00e4d7d04203b4dc752b44a8ace15f73/30f9a956974f08df9e274f508c3cdd9b/image.png)

The result? More trust signals mean more chances to show up in both traditional and AI-powered SERPs.
