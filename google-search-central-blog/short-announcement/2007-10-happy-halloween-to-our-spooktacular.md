# Happy Halloween to our spooktacular webmasters!

With apologizes to Vic Mizzy, we've written short verse to the tune of the
"[Addams Family](https://www.google.com/search?q=addams+family+theme)"
theme (please use your imagination):

We may be hobbyists or just geeky,
[Building websites](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=40349&topic=8522)
and acting cheeky, [Javascript redirects](/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#cloaking)
we won't make sneaky, Our webmaster fam-i-ly!

Happy Halloween everyone! You can
[join the discussion](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-chit-chat/browse_frm/thread/2996fc4764fcb00d/dd09bbff7e863c8d#dd09bbff7e863c8d)
and share your Halloween stories and costumes.
