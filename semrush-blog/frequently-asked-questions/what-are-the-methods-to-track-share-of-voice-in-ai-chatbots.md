# What are the methods to track share of voice in AI chatbots?

The methods to track share of voice in AI chatbots include using specialized monitoring tools such as Semrush [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/en/) or [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/brand-performance/), and manually querying AI platforms (although that’s inefficient and time-consuming).

Share of voice in AI chatbots measures how strong your brand presence is. This metric is crucial because it directly correlates with how many people see your brand when they search something about your industry.

To get a complete picture, brands should look beyond simple mentions and evaluate both frequency and position in AI answers, which is what Semrush Enterprise AIO considers when calculating the share of voice for your brand.

Frequency means how often your brand appears, while position refers to where your brand is mentioned in the response—whether it's the first recommendation, buried in the middle, or mentioned as an afterthought. Looking at both these things gives you a more accurate understanding of your share of voice.
