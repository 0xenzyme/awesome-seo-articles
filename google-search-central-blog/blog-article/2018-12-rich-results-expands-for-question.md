# Rich Results expands for Question and Answer pages

People come to Google seeking information about all kinds of questions. Frequently, the
information they're looking for is on sites where users ask and answer each other's questions.
Popular social news sites, expert forums, and help and support message boards are all examples of
this pattern.

![an example search result for a page titled 'How do I remove a cable that
            is stuck in a USB port' with a list of the top answers from the page.](/static/search/blog/images/import/bf8df932b7d07cb8875ae54ff2452a90.png)

A screenshot of an example search result for a page titled "How do I remove a cable that is
stuck in a USB port" with a list of the top answers from the page.

In order to help users better identify which search results may give the best information about
their question, we have developed a new rich result type for question and answer sites. Search
results for eligible Q&A pages display a preview of the top answers. This new presentation
helps site owners reach the right users for their content and helps users get the relevant
information about their questions faster.

![an example search result for a page titled 'Why do touchscreens
            sometimes register a touch when ...' with a preview of the top answers from the page.](/static/search/blog/images/import/45e2a8553473fd63878ca0f2290a2d8e.png)

A screenshot of an example search result for a page titled "Why do touchscreens sometimes
register a touch when ..." with a preview of the top answers from the page.

To be eligible for this feature,
[add Q&A structured data](/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/qapage) to your
pages with Q&A content. Be sure to use the
[Structured Data Testing Tool](/search/docs/advanced/structured-data)
to see if your page is eligible and to preview the appearance in search results. You can also
check out Search Console to see
[aggregate stats and markup error examples](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7552505).
The
[Performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553)
also tells you which queries show your Q&A Rich Result in Search results, and how these change
over time.

If you have any questions, ask us in the
[Webmaster Help Forum](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community) or reach out
on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/googlesearchc/)!
