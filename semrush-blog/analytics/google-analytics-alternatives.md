## Why Consider an Alternative to Google Analytics?

Some reasons to look for a [Google Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-analytics/) alternative include:

- **Steep learning curve**: Some users find Google Analytics difficult to use, especially after Google introduced Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
- **Data may not be reported in real time**: Google Analytics can take up to 48 hours to process large amounts of data. Reports may be outdated by the time you see them.
- **Google may use your data**: By [installing Google Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-set-up-google-analytics/), you allow Google to use your site’s data for targeted ads. You must choose a different tool if you do not consent to that.
- **Data privacy concerns**: Some European Union (EU) data protection authorities state Google Analytics is not automatically compliant with the [General Data Protection Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02016R0679-20160504) (GDPR)
- **No customer support**: Google doesn’t typically offer support for Google Analytics. Exceptions include users of the enterprise-level Analytics 360 or the Google Marketing Platform for ads.

## What to Look for in a Google Analytics Alternative

When choosing from Google Analytics alternatives, make sure the tool:

- **Offers essential data reports**: It should provide metrics such as [page views](https://www.semrush.com/blog/pageviews/), traffic volume, and [key events](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ga4-conversions/)
- **Is easy to install and use**: Setting up the tool (such as by adding a code snippet) should be straightforward. The interface should also be intuitive.
- **Provides real-time reporting**: Real-time data helps you make immediate, data-driven decisions
- **Focuses on privacy**: The tool should comply with data protection laws, such as GDPR. Some tools avoid storing personal user data, simplifying GDPR compliance.
- **Suits your use case**: Sophisticated analysis may require advanced data insights and flexible reporting. Software product developers may need features for tracking product usage.
