# Google Instant: Impact on Search queries

Webmasters, you may notice some changes in you
[Search queries](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35252)
data due to the launch of
[Google Instant](https://googleblog.blogspot.com/2010/09/search-now-faster-than-speed-of-type.html).
With Google Instant, the page updates dynamically to show results for the top completion of what
the user has typed, so this means people could be seeing and visiting your website much faster
than before, and often without clicking the search button or hitting "enter."

![google instant in action](/static/search/blog/images/import/93be6957226f57009e5bb81a408d3337.png)

While the presentation of the search results may change, our most important advice to webmasters
remains the same: Users want to visit pages with compelling content and a great user experience.

With Google Instant, you may notice an increase in
[impressions](https://www.google.com/support/webmasters/bin/answer.py?answer=35252)
because your site will appear in search results as users type.

![number of impressions in the webmaster tools top search queries feature may increase due to Google Instant](/static/search/blog/images/import/1e05004493b4c329600f911f90d6e943.png)

Impressions are measured in three ways with Google Instant:

1. Your site is displayed in search results as a response to a user's completed query (for example,
   by pressing "enter" or selecting a term from autocomplete). This is the traditional model.
2. **New:** The user begins to type a term on Google and clicks on a link on the page, such as
   a search result, ad, or a related search.
3. **New:**The user stops typing, and the results are displayed for a minimum of 3 seconds.

To give an example, let's say your site has lots of impressions for "hotels" and "hotels in santa
cruz". Now, because Instant is quickly fetching results as the user types, the user could see your
site in the search results for "hotels" after typing only the partial query "hote". If a user
types the partial query "hote" and then clicks on any result on the page for "hotels", that counts
as an impression for your site. That impression will appear in Webmaster Tools for the query
"hotels". The term 'hotels' would also be included in the HTTP referrer when the user clicks
through to visit your website.

It's likely that your site will still see impressions for queries like
"hotels in santa cruz", but because Instant is helping the user find results faster, your site may
see an increase in impressions for shorter terms as well.
