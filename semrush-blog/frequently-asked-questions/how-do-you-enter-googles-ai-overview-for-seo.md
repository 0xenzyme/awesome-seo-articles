# How do you enter Google's AI Overview for SEO?

You can't directly enter Google's [AI Overview](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) since it automatically generates answers from Google's existing search index, but you can increase your chances of being cited by creating content that's easily summarizable and demonstrates clear expertise and authoritativeness.

Google's AI Overview pulls information from pages that already rank well in search results. The system looks for trustworthy, [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/) that can answer user questions quickly and accurately.

Focus on becoming the most authoritative, easily-referenced source in your niche rather than trying to game the system.

Semrush's [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/) and [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/semrush-ai-toolkit/) help you monitor which content gets cited in AI Overviews and track your visibility compared to competitors across different query types.
