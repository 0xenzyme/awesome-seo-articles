# How accurate are rank tracking tools?

Rank tracking tools are generally accurate within 1-2 positions for most searches, with reputable tools achieving 90%+ accuracy rates.

Perfect accuracy is impossible because search results vary based on your location, device, search history, and timing — creating natural differences between what tools report and what you personally see.

Here's how to improve tracking accuracy:

- **Set specific locations for tracking.** Use ZIP codes for local searches rather than broad city or state settings
- **Choose consistent device settings.** Decide whether you want to track mobile or desktop rankings and stick with that setting
- **Focus on trends over exact positions.** Look for overall ranking improvements or declines rather than obsessing over single-position differences
- **Expect wider variance for local searches.** Local rankings can change block-by-block, so you'll see more fluctuation than national keywords

For example, Semrush [Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) works best when you focus on overall trends and visibility improvements rather than exact position differences day-to-day.

The key is using rank tracking tools to spot meaningful changes in your SEO performance, not to get pixel-perfect ranking data that matches every individual search experience.
