# The 50 Most-Cited Websites in Google AI Overviews (June 2026)

No AI search feature reaches more people than Google’s AI Overviews, which makes its sources some of the most consequential on the web.

AI Overviews are the AI-generated summaries that now sit above traditional Google results — and their scale is staggering. Google says they [reach over 2 billion monthly users across more than 200 countries](https://digiday.com/media/googles-ai-overviews-reach-over-2-billion-monthly-users/), roughly a quarter of everyone on Earth. When a domain gets cited here, it’s being surfaced to an audience no other AI assistant comes close to. That also makes AI Overviews a flashpoint for publishers worried about losing clicks.

To see which sites Google’s summaries favour, we tracked every domain AI Overviews cited across a broad set of US queries, then ranked them by **mention share** — each domain’s slice of all the citations the top sources collectively earn. The leader is **www.youtube.com**, capturing **20.9%** of citations.

## How this data is collected

The numbers come from [Ahrefs’ Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar), which tracks the responses AI platforms give to a large, search-backed set of real user queries. For this article, we looked at every domain Google AI Overviews cited across over **3 million** US queries spanning all topics, then counted how often each domain appeared as a source.

For each website we report:

- **Mention share** — a domain’s citations as a percentage of the summed citations of the top 50 most-cited sources. It shows how much of Google AI Overviews’s “source attention” each website captures.
- **vs last month** — how the domain’s rank has moved since the previous monthly snapshot (▲ climbed, ▼ fell, or **NEW** if it wasn’t in last month’s top 50).
- **Pages cited** — the number of distinct pages on the domain that Google AI Overviews cited, a measure of how broadly the site is referenced rather than how often.
- **DR (Domain Rating)** — Ahrefs’ 0–100 measure of a website’s backlink-profile strength, included for context on each source’s overall authority.
