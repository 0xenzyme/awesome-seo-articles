# What is domain authority and how does it impact SEO?

Domain Authority (DA) is a metric that can help you predict how well a website will rank in [search engine result pages](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp/) (SERPs), measured on a scale from 1 to 100, with higher scores indicating stronger ranking potential.

Domain authority was created by Moz as a metric to help SEO professionals understand a website's overall strength and competitiveness in search results. While it's not an official Google ranking factor, it correlates strongly with actual search performance.

Here's how domain authority impacts SEO:

- Websites with stronger domain authority scores typically rank better for competitive keywords
- Websites with higher authority find it easier to earn quality [backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/), as they get more exposure through search engines
- Search engines may [crawl and index](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-crawlability-and-indexability-of-a-website/) pages from high-authority domains more frequently and thoroughly than those from lower-authority sites
- Sites with strong domain authority may recover more quickly from the negative impacts of [algorithm updates](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-algorithm-update/)
