# Message Center: Let us communicate with you about your site

Today we're launching our Message Center, a new way for webmasters to receive personalized
information from Google in our
[webmaster console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
Should we need to contact you, you'll see a notification in your Webmaster Tools dashboard.

![webmaster tools message center](/static/search/blog/images/import/e579b8c52e12916c432fd75d02385cd5.png)

Initially the messages will refer to search quality issues, but over time we'll use the Message
Center as a communication channel for more types of information. Here's an example: informing the
site owner about hidden text, a violation in our
[webmaster guidelines](/search/docs/essentials).

![individual message in webmaster tools](/static/search/blog/images/import/44ab18f5a37cbeeca38c1d29186891a4.png)

For our webmasters outside the U.S., we're also pleased to tell you that Message Center is capable
of providing information in all supported Webmaster Tools languages (French, Italian, German,
Spanish, Danish, Dutch, Swedish, Russian, Chinese-Simplified, Chinese-Traditional, Korean,
Japanese, etc.), across all countries.

Right now the number of sites we're contacting is small, but we hope to expand this program over
time. We're also really happy that the Message Center lets us communicate with webmasters in an
authenticated way. As time goes on, we'll keep looking for even more ways to improve communication
with site owners, but right now, why not
[claim your site](https://search.google.com/search-console)
in our Webmaster Tools so that we can give you a heads-up of any issues that we see?
