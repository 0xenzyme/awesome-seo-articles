# How to Submit Your Website or a URL to Search Engines

Submitting your website to a search engine helps you get more traffic from search. It gets the search engine to crawl your pages and decide whether (and how) to index them.

The best part? Submitting your site to search engines is free. You don’t need to use a paid service.

This article will walk you through the step-by-step process of submitting your site—or specific page URLs—to the Google, Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, and Yandex search engines.

And how to use a tool like our [SEO Audit](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) to troubleshoot any indexing issues that occur after you’ve submitted your site.

## Do You Need to Submit Your Website to Search Engines?

It’s almost certain that search engines will eventually find your site. But adding your site to search engines has these benefits:

- **Peace of mind**: You’ve alerted search engines of your site’s existence, so you won’t have to wait and wonder
- **Faster crawling**: When you submit your site to a search engine, it may add it to its priority queue and crawl it sooner
- **Higher rankings for relevant keywords**: Search engines may be able to make more informed ranking decisions when you share your site structure instead of leaving them to figure it out

## How to Submit Your Website to Google

Submitting a site to Google involves two main steps:

### 1. Find Your XML Sitemap URL

An [XML sitemap](https://www.semrush.com/blog/xml-sitemap/) is a file that lists the pages on your site.

For example, here’s part of the XML sitemap for Semrush’s blog:

![xml sitemap for Semrush's blog showing a full list of URLs in plain text so crawlers can easily access them](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/86/9e/869eb25e05e6234f1aec44ed5a62dd4b/47102004cff847ded0d303b314dacf8f/AD_4nXe_FRkSUZWnH1fdjfvoYH7ZtNexvLhDZ35_BPfIbIdjyix7Fd8CSb7RZja-Vq84ukj-qkLvGvld_TQozDoBCk-YwFge4JF6GpzByPbjyoYqpNZR1Yai835JcK75TJyNcjdBPQNSTdSX3VkCBQp2krCswQx2.jpeg)

When you provide [search engines](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-engine-list/) with your XML sitemap’s URL, they can visit the sitemap to discover—and then crawl—your site’s pages.

A website’s XML sitemap URL is usually this:

`yoursite.com/sitemap.xml`

Or this:

`yoursite.com/sitemap_index.xml`

Try entering these URLs into your browser to view your site’s XML sitemap.

If this doesn’t work, your site’s [robots.txt](https://www.semrush.com/blog/beginners-guide-robots-txt/) file might show the correct sitemap URL to use.

Access your site’s robots.txt file at this URL:

`yoursite.com/robots.txt`

And look for your sitemap URL in this file:

![xml sitemap with the sitemap URL at the bottom of the file highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7d/46/7d46671403bc9cf1b1eea47c791d5a07/ed2b4cd912a9709268e71130c2fbe918/AD_4nXdkqr7cT_jqeuLL5SLFQgMaH40qcaqHbKkrauxJ8pUXslzXC7UDxCuxxwaCqD88JuUtBsXr_raGeea54P7k3wSmWO5PzvYbM0m9hZ1gxfoVZBzaaymUq6rtzNgQeN1wgVPAOT503-LfV3j-B68oUBlzOG0.jpeg)

## How to Submit Your Website to Bing

Add your site to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/about) if you haven’t already. Then, open Bing Webmaster Tools and click “**Sitemaps**” in the left sidebar.

Click “**Submit sitemap**” at the top right.

A “Submit sitemap” pop-up will appear. Type your sitemap URL into the given field. And click “**Submit**.”

## How to Submit Your Website to Yahoo

Bing powers Yahoo’s search engine. By submitting your website to Bing, you’ll also submit it to Yahoo.

## How to Submit Your Website to DuckDuckGo

DuckDuckGo draws most of its search results from Bing. So, submitting your website to Bing helps it appear on DuckDuckGo, too.

## How to Submit Your Website to Yandex

Open [Yandex Webmaster](https://webmaster.yandex.com/welcome/) and verify your site.

Once your verification is complete, click “**Indexing**” > “**Sitemap files**” on the left sidebar.

Type your sitemap URL into the “Add Sitemap file” field, and then click “**Add**.”

![navigation to "Add Sitemap file" on Yandex with a sitemap URL entered and "Add" clicked](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/95/f5/95f55699fc8c025bb51007406d30de5b/7720bea2ed0162648cc55186471b4340/AD_4nXdvIoBEIfdRQPMk9-pNWxu9RD3R2g3u8zVewEfxGckyNALwRclXq1R6O6MP3otGhDK5wKCXm6YmLUsam1mdWHxaEK8hbIjeJgtWiY76g0WIkC-lkk3cKzLH_wicUCnQpcuO7fFUj1AXyAH-Vs_fTKUT87xB.jpeg)

Yandex will then add your sitemap to its processing queue. Processing may take one to two weeks. If it successfully processes your sitemap, the status will say “ok.”

!["Processing queue" on Yandex showing a list of submitted sitemap URLs along with the date submitted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/09/f6/09f6f6729466e2dba7ee4cbd9b8e3696/e248833406bfb0ac07f928f6e80f1783/AD_4nXc0K_sxxixSCF-aWaaHA7rbHvxumhfoBZ9wJtqNWyIUeb1brvlPbuI-8y_boleIm8gr5SlNGnPS4kLKn4WRXa2-uPUnjxhYBtrUwpRYBsqm2UmtraPmGYUj6glZfiSf9TPGsqOXqCQ-cYqH1XLUcu-jX2F6.jpeg)

## How to Fix Indexing Issues

If your site has indexing issues, some (or all) of its pages won’t appear in the search results.

As a result, you’ll lose [organic traffic](https://www.semrush.com/blog/organic-traffic/).

Indexing issues include:

- [**404 errors**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-does-error-404-not-found-mean/): The web server cannot find a page at that URL
- **The URL has a** [**meta robots “noindex” tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/robots-meta/): This tag tells search engines not to index the URL
- [**Duplicate content**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/duplicate-content/): Search engines may not index a page whose content is nearly identical to another page’s

Use these tools to find and fix indexing issues:

### Google Search Console

Go to GSC and click “**Pages**” in the left sidebar to load the “Page indexing” report.

Scroll down the report. Under the “Why pages aren’t indexed” section, you’ll see the number of pages Google isn’t indexing and the reasons.

![“Why pages aren’t indexed” on Google Search Console with reasons like page with redirect, excluded by 'noindex' tag, not found, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bf/89/bf89fa899e6cf22075b6d5324a28c0a6/9532b35068c40202b0da82be8c338850/AD_4nXe_1J_6k2Z1e4uxKD6omvZLGYI_k6ljHtAQ0mr-RNcrj6aEr4O70nKB17kL7Fx48P9Fh5djnbQq6_BPyzgi0JbEd1c55h3MNP_ZcSYL-yVB9j8eW6YBFcba_dFyxyrLYk4TEL5O7eoTqKBoW9ZllCJxtQWW.jpeg)

Click any indexing issue, and then scroll down to see why specific URLs aren’t getting indexed. You can view up to 1,000 examples.

![a list of page URLs and the date they were last crawled for the "Page with redirect" indexing issue on Google Search Console](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1b/d0/1bd0596fd989dd47158b353cbabe479c/f041513b6b87da136a6e1f8864c1ae67/AD_4nXe4LaG2cp5q4x8L5RVCpj1esxKdHPLl4r74hp4ssjby7DWjz03o3ApQ2H2_aiIh14ropCgTJEKtOv6TzzpRvW3InFkN_3chENJYoYClttNIcyszX-5qFlNomddLC3hr4lCXm7259yBKNY6EXc-aEsjMDJ7G.jpeg)

## Should You Use a Website Submission Service?

Submitting your site to search engines directly is fast, easy, and free. So a search engine submission service is likely unnecessary.

Some webpage submission services claim they can get your site onto little-known search engines and directories like Ask.com and Yippy.

But the vast majority of people use Google as their search engine. As of May 2024, its global market share was over [90.8%](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share).

So, as long as your site is on Google, you’re in good shape.

Using a website submission service can also cause your [SEO rankings](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-ranking/) to take a hit.

Why?

Because some of these services involve you paying other sites to link to yours—which results in unnatural linking behavior that Google doesn’t like. If Google catches you doing this, it may penalize your site.

Some links you buy may also be [toxic backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/toxic-links-guidelines/) that hurt your rankings. Semrush’s [Backlink Audit](https://www.semrush.com/backlink_audit/) can help you find and disavow these links. But it’s easier to avoid buying potentially harmful links in the first place.

So, stick with the search engines’ official website submission options.

Submitting your site to Google and other search engines isn’t complex and doesn’t take long. As long as you follow the steps, your URLs should be indexed in no time at all.

But if you encounter indexing issues after submitting your site, use the [SEO audit](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) tool to troubleshoot and fix the problem.
