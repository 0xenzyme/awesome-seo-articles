## What reviewers say about Semrush's tradeoffs

While the themes above are the ones reviewers mention most often, no product receives exclusively positive feedback. The reviews also highlight a few recurring concerns.

### What are the most common complaints about Semrush?

Across G2, Capterra, and Gartner, the most common criticisms relate to pricing, the complexity of subscriptions and add-ons, and the learning curve that comes with using a feature-rich platform. Some reviewers—particularly first-time users—say the number of available tools can feel overwhelming initially. At the same time, many reviewers describe the interface as intuitive and say they became comfortable with the platform quickly.

For users who find the platform overwhelming at first, Semrush offers free resources—including [Semrush Academy](https://www.semrush.com/academy/), the [Knowledge Base](https://www.semrush.com/kb/), and customer support—to help shorten the learning curve.

### What do negative Semrush reviews say?

Across G2, Capterra, and Gartner, reviewers generally agree that Semrush is a powerful platform, but some—particularly solo users and small businesses—question whether the cost is justified if they're only using a small portion of its features.

Reviews suggest that perceived value depends largely on how extensively the platform is used. Businesses that rely on Semrush for keyword research, site audits, competitor analysis, reporting, and AI visibility often describe it as replacing several separate tools. Those using only one or two features are more likely to consider it expensive.

For businesses with simpler needs, Semrush also offers entry-level plans, allowing users to start with a smaller subscription and expand as their requirements grow.
