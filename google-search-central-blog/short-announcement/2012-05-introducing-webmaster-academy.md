# Introducing Webmaster Academy

Looking through all of the information in [Webmaster Central](/search) can feel
overwhelming, especially if you're just getting started with a website. This is why we're excited
to introduce a new set of educational materials in a program called
[Webmaster Academy](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide). Webmaster Academy
provides practical and easy-to-understand lessons for beginner webmasters to help you improve
your site with topics like getting your site in our index, providing search engines with helpful
information about your video and image content, and understanding
[Webmaster Tools](https://search.google.com/search-console)
features.

We've organized the content to represent what beginner webmasters should know in a way that's both
structured and modular, meaning you can go through the whole curriculum, or pick and choose your
own path. Once you've read the articles, you can easily delve deeper into each topic, as we
provide links to more in-depth articles. Most lessons are also accompanied by a video from the
[Webmaster Central YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@GoogleSearchCentral).
If you're looking to understand search and improve your site,
[Webmaster Academy](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) is for you!

Have feedback? Excellent. Post it in our
[Webmaster Help Forum](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community).
