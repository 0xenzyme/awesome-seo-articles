# Wix SEO: How to Rank Better with Your Wix Website

Wix is a popular website builder with many features and a beginner-oriented interface.

In this guide, we’ll examine Wix’s SEO-friendliness and how you can improve your Wix site’s SEO performance.

## Is Wix Good for SEO?

Wix is good for [SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-seo/) in the sense that it’s possible to build a top-ranking website on the platform.

Google’s [John Mueller has confirmed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DbgHkxqdu4c) that Google does **not** treat pages differently based on the [content management system](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/content-marketing-blog/content-management-system/) (CMS) used.

He also said that mainstream systems like Wix “either all work well for search out of the box, or can easily be tuned to do so with a few settings or plugins.”

But Wix does have some disadvantages. Which we’ll explore in the next section.

### Wix and SEO: Pros and Cons

Here are some advantages of using Wix:

- **Beginner-friendly**: Wix automates many SEO elements and guides users through basic SEO steps
- **Easy to connect to Google**: Connecting to Google Search Console and Google Analytics is straightforward with Wix
- **Robust SEO knowledge base**: Wix has a [comprehensive knowledge base article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/search-engine-optimization-seo) covering SEO for Wix sites
- **Advanced tools and features**: Wix offers a [redirects](https://www.semrush.com/blog/redirects/) manager, a [robots.txt](https://www.semrush.com/blog/beginners-guide-robots-txt/) editor (which lets you make changes to the file that tells search engines which pages to crawl), and the ability to addcustom [meta tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-tag/) (HTML that provides more information about your pages’ content)
- **Automated schema coding**: Wix automatically adds some kinds of [schema markup](https://www.semrush.com/blog/schema-markup/) (code that helps search engines understand your content better). This can make you eligible for [rich snippets](https://www.semrush.com/blog/rich-snippets/)—search results with extra, attention-grabbing elements.

Here are some potential disadvantages of using Wix:

- **Inability to edit your XML sitemap**: Wix does not let you manually edit your site’s [XML sitemap](https://www.semrush.com/blog/xml-sitemap/) (a file that helps search engines understand your site’s architecture). It creates and updates it automatically.
- **Code bloat**: Because Wix is a website builder, it can come with unnecessary code (“bloat”) that slows your website down. This can affect page experience metrics called [Core Web Vitals](https://www.semrush.com/blog/core-web-vitals/), which impact your site’s Google rankings and user experience.

## Should You Use Wix for Your Website?

You should consider using Wix if you’re looking for a beginner-friendly website builder. It has a drag-and-drop editor, lots of templates, 24/7 customer care, and much more.

Wix can also be suitable for advanced SEOs or web developers who want flexibility and customization options. Because it offers:

- **CSS editing**: Direct control over visual styles through CSS code adjustments
- **Full-stack dev tool**: Ability to rapidly build and deploy web applications customized to your needs
- **Headless option**: Flexibility to use any technology for the frontend, which is connected to the backend via APIs
- **Integrated development environment (IDE)**: Integrated tools for coding, debugging, and managing web development projects

Here’s a quick overview of some other CMS platforms that may be appropriate depending on your needs:

- **WordPress**: Offers more control and customization but has a steeper learning curve. Suitable for sites that may require scaling or complex functionalities. (Check out our [WordPress SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/wordpress-seo/) guide.)
- **Squarespace**: Known for beautiful designs and ease of use, it's a good middle ground between Wix and WordPress. (Check out our [Squarespace SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/squarespace-seo/) guide.)
- **Shopify**: If you're focusing on ecommerce, Shopify might be a good fit due to its powerful online sales tools and features. (Check out our [Shopify SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/shopify-seo/) guide.)

If you decide to proceed with Wix, here are nine ways to optimize your website:

### 3. Connect to Google Search Console and Google Analytics

[Google Search Console](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-search-console/) (GSC) monitors your site’s performance in Google Search and checks for technical issues. While [Google Analytics 4](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-analytics/) (GA4) tracks user behavior on your site.

Both free tools provide valuable insights into your site’s performance.

So, let’s learn how to connect them to your Wix website:

#### Connect Wix to Google Search Console

[Create your GSC account](https://search.google.com/search-console) if you haven’t already.

The next steps depend on whether you connected to GSC when following the SEO Setup Checklist.

If so, then you’ve already taken care of this.

If not, you’ll need to follow a few steps to [connect to GSC manually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-google-search-console-6081361).

You’ll start by entering your URL in the “URL prefix” module in Google Search Console. Then, click “**Continue**.”

![“URL prefix” module in Google Search Console](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4d/9a/4d9ac585155a29a897d46ead8a46d47f/1a1af84c5a852d539940f24e36b5f1a4/49MiPNWPbzD-vKO3Dt-uMomTzhWL5KEuPGMC7GftSXjkDv26_SO3tb6aaMGbXo3RmUc3eWjAtOEz59OJ16q995OY-QY_hATI6UwH75uPZX5npBnXjicL96PxJnnLQPv9YtZC7TFOqph8o57q9h0ZepI.png)

Then, copy the meta tag from GSC, save it in your Wix settings, and complete the rest of the verification process in GSC.

#### Connect Wix to Google Analytics

To connect your Wix site to GA4, you’ll first need to [create a Google Analytics account](https://analytics.google.com/) and copy your measurement ID.

In Wix, simply go to “**Marketing & SEO**” > “**Marketing Integrations**.” And click “**Connect**” beneath the Google Analytics section.

![Connect Google Analytics under Marketing Integrations page in Wix](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/49/95/499534ef077a7315da15745a0c71fb19/a86ea638dec66ef1dc1e2b3d50f8de55/8UnC9Jq_QrHsQFAOLSQLYcuUw2znT4Oyy-lm5KqdhHNubHXqn-v5HrwC-BXagIs0BnU5-lCJiuMdpXodprlSd7Dt6YnoirdBdaemfuVQTnvBM5EC7u2lDMWTqimYBwpio9ja_RNqU2kfCIwnWx_f7rY.png)

### 4. Create High-Quality Blog Posts

One of the most effective ways to grow your site’s [organic traffic](https://www.semrush.com/blog/organic-traffic/) (unpaid search engine traffic) is to create a blog and consistently publish high-quality content on it.

Wix makes it easy to add blog posts to your site. But it takes time and effort to create [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/) that appears high in search results.

You’ll need to come up with [blog ideas](https://www.semrush.com/blog/blog-ideas/) that are relevant to your brand and target audience. Then, try to write content that’s the best of its kind.

Here are some [tips for writing blog posts](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-write-a-blog-post/):

- Make sure your content is comprehensive, easy to read, and accurate
- Add value with unique insights, images, videos, data, etc.
- [Optimize your blog posts](https://www.semrush.com/blog/blog-seo/) for search engines **and** users
- Use subheadings, lists, and other formatting to break up big sections of text
- Never plagiarize. Ever.

There are many [blogging tools](https://www.semrush.com/blog/blogging-tools/) that can help you come up with and execute post ideas.

### 5. Optimize Your Title Tags and Meta Descriptions

Every page on your site needs a unique and optimized title tag and meta description. Here’s a brief overview of both:

- [**Title tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/): Tells users and search engines what your page is about
- [**Meta description**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/): Provides a more detailed description of your page’s content

Google may use both elements to create search engine results.

![Title tag and meta description shown on Google SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/46/3a/463acfc0c580b37d8061e58a9c885f73/5a0d94129490588f59cc2570fa8e387e/CieYRrlLjRBI_Q_m-SL1VCnaJ1jGkIDtRvrMHif9Fh6C2ScsYwzRyFrVlTDb7rBmv4vH_ERSA-baqscX8IiD8jTf3Z75BTjwTOz566_Zc2kxrWECCypafVUmElVGHuwdNwzFI8ju3bqInV2-SPRuyc4.png)

Here’s how to edit your title tags and meta descriptions in Wix:

In the editor’s left-hand menu, click the pages icon.

Find the page you want to edit in the list, click the three dots next to the page title, then select “**SEO basics**.”

![Navigating to "SEO basics" for Wix's page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2c/00/2c004a88ddef7e178d318fce281cf192/342fc06326f772b9db09f3a93915b1ab/Hpkk40OMBDkc4b16JlvSlNmiLbKsw91JbXo9paYYFR2sj8ODb48gSrbIwxETmhwVVX85Ig_h2WX4I4yGtDEmat2lM3H4qcRu-1fGkI_L3YEdttGPyYjiAz3hXuWfg8Jiu7howSjUlBtK--luFj8FjdE.png)

![A preview of title tag and meta description in Wix's edit page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/49/cd/49cdce8acc3e30b20c849341dbf6bc39/d056298b16f1ae5ca7f91e00333e3996/tddcYVugPYh2NFjWnSGY-QiLjIJdLRZop6Ui9yTiNKA6TegfgusyEAvr8fYm8K_LK3TYei21z31NYOK62_KEVKGHQ9vuI9oCnljgglSfTUJWmMwtuBEY_0_C6heam26SCBha5k-WGMK7TKaz2WIzWL4.png)

Here are some tips for writing optimized title tags and meta descriptions:

- Keep your title tags short (about 50 to 60 characters)
- Keep your meta descriptions around 105 characters
- Include your target keyword in both elements
- Make your meta descriptions actionable by using active voice and a [call to action](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-call-to-action/) (CTA)

### 6. Write User-friendly URL Slugs

The [URL slug](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url-slug/) is the final part of a page’s URL address.

For example, the slug of this article is **/wix-seo/**.

You should always construct user-friendly and descriptive URL slugs for every page on your site. It helps Google understand the page’s content. And it’s also good for the user experience.

In Wix, you can do that easily in the same “**SEO basics**” settings where you’re editing the title tag and meta description (see the previous section).

![Edit URL slug in “SEO basics” settings in Wix](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ef/06/ef066b644213b5324f316a60f4ca8493/58a725db1205009492c2a01dbe01e33f/tNDKQQpePZsvEvM_RA1B4HuODu9EzSvMjbZGelqaEA22shOojRmGjFh7CD8bcpUCegqXwc_E78wCohkFjxfkWzKSvAi8jKqDDSnPOCzqweq_VwGi9f7ZfBXZYQO1PdIQbP2jh8UPiRlL4xc-syT8XNQ.png)

To write optimized URL slugs:

- Keep your URL slugs short and descriptive
- Use hyphens (-), not underscores (\_)
- Use lowercase characters
- Avoid special characters

### 7. Add Internal Links

[Internal links](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/) are links that point from one page on your site to another page on your site.

A strong internal linking strategy can increase the authority and topical relevance of your key pages. And improve their rankings in search engines.

Of course, internal links are also navigational elements that help users move through your site and find what they’re looking for.

Adding internal links to your content in Wix is simple.

First, select the text you want to place the internal link on. And click the hyperlink icon.

That section of text is called [anchor text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/anchor-text/). It provides context about the linked page to both users and search engines.

![How to add internal links in WIx](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/de/d4/ded4e71dc2210cf21a6fea92a9506081/4255a6d7d2761fd302b810a7e6c89995/LWkjbwOSEMOeUoJjTh35WROT6-S2dkiG6unzNzC0-D2NcGpBTlKfxMQITi2ExSc4QLzMlR8_nc0B3uk4wKip72r5Ydq8ZYbtFlAVbecKC35bl6eKAzI1MZRL6-i5z2JzQwcLap1r5UFnf8nKGwcKfPM.png)

In the pop-up window, choose a page you want to link to using the “Which page?” selector. Then, hit “**Done**.”

![Choose a page you want to link to in Wix](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b2/15/b2154b2fbcdccd6d0e2b28d0f9324a60/367f99bf36d59f74e15381ec23c13b04/YY-45yVn3qUOjCJsRXoPywMPFkDHjjrtCBwG_rr-8uHpAhvdWxnAbGRKpwLZN8noVhbIOmoqLFWlwzUp1iuGS6ATixWI_MCSMgznpd3GnxLnxoMWCD1RKO1APh5rWoFSo5JHFpmiuTPBJqKlbm6LBoY.png)

Internal linking should be an integral part of your publishing process. Every time you create a new page, think about other relevant pages you could link to.

Once the new page is published, you can do the same in reverse: Add internal links to your new page from other published pages by updating them.

### 8. Optimize Your Site’s Images

Large images are a common reason for poor [site speed](https://www.semrush.com/blog/page-speed/).

That’s why [image SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/image-seo/) is a crucial aspect of your Wix website’s optimization process.

For starters, use images that are sized appropriately. So, if you’re displaying an image at a maximum width of 800 pixels, use an 800-pixel-wide image—not a 2,400-pixel-wide image.

Additionally, you can use a tool like [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) to **compress your images** before uploading them to Wix. This will reduce their size without losing too much quality.

Also, add unique [alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/) (descriptions of the visuals) to images on your webpages. These help Google and those using screen readers understand what’s portrayed in the image.

Add alt text to an image in Wix by clicking on the image, selecting the gear icon, and adding a description to the “What’s in the image? Tell Google” box.

### 9. Build Backlinks to Your Site

[Backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/) (links from other sites to your site) are a key SEO ranking factor.

Essentially, Google sees links from one website to another as votes of quality. Pages with a high number of quality backlinks tend to rank higher in search results.

That’s why [link building](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/) tends to be so important.

Here are some [link building tactics](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building-strategies/) you can try:

- Convince creators who’ve mentioned your brand without linking to add a link
- Create content that naturally attracts links ([link bait](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-bait/))
- Respond to expert source requests via platforms like [Connectively](https://www.connectively.us/)

Here’s another great way to find opportunities: Take a look at competitors’ backlinks with Semrush’s [Backlink Gap](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/gap/backlinks/) tool.
