# 9 Biggest SEO Trends of 2025 & How to Leverage Them

In this post, we'll cover nine search engine optimization (SEO) trends.

By the end of this post, you'll know where SEO is heading with the rise of AI and how to stay ahead of your competitors.

Let's start.

## 1. AI-Generated SEO Content Is Now Mainstream

[AI writing tools](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-writing-tools/) help create large volumes of content quickly, allowing websites to publish a greater number of pieces and target more keywords. And people are using AI tools now more than ever.

According to [research from CoSchedule](https://coschedule.com/ai-marketing-statistics), 85% of marketers use AI tools for content creation.

But content that’s generated solely with AI often lacks uniqueness and depth. And using AI to write articles on topics your competitors cover could result in you creating content that’s highly similar.

This is a problem because Google assesses content quality based on factors like uniqueness. It’s that quality rather than the actual method of using AI that matters, [according to Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content):

> *“Our focus on the quality of content, rather than how content is produced, is a useful guide that has helped us deliver reliable, high quality results to users for years.”*

Without proper editing and a concerted effort to include one-of-a-kind insights, AI content may fall short. This can affect your [search rankings](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-ranking/) as well as your ability to appear in experiences like [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) and AI Mode.

### How to Leverage This Trend

Generate the first draft of your content using an AI tool. Then, enhance the AI content with real examples, stories, data, and other unique details to ensure the final content reflects your brand's expertise.

This two-step approach helps you create unique content efficiently while still aligning with what Google prioritizes.

Try Semrush’s [Content Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/content/) to generate your first draft.

Start by using the [Topic Finder](https://www.semrush.com/content/topic-finder/) tool to come up with high-impact content ideas. Enter a primary topic, select your target location, and click “**Find topics**.”

From there, click “**Start writing**” and select “**Generate article with AI**” to create a full-length draft in [AI Article Generator](https://www.semrush.com/content/articles/create).

## 2. Alternatives to Google Are Becoming More Popular

Users are expanding their focus beyond Google when doing research, relying on other search engines and AI platforms.

Bing's search [market share](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share) has increased in recent years. And [more than half](https://www.emarketer.com/content/5-charts-search-2024-google-ai-retail-media) of U.S. consumers now start product searches on Amazon instead of traditional search engines.

And we’d be remiss not to mention how quickly AI search is growing. [Our research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/) shows visitors from AI platforms could surpass visitors from traditional search engines by 2028.

![Line chart of projected annual visitors by source, showing traditional organic search declining from 2025 to 2029, LLMs (including Google AI) rising and overtaking organic search around 2028, while total traffic stays steady.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fc/d6/fcd6dc11035f2f6d0aa501056a3842bd/bdd468895cd6a052dae515bfc1f49c02/image.png)

- **Strengthen your video efforts.** Publish YouTube videos with optimized titles, descriptions, and transcripts to capture video search traffic.
- **Apply technical SEO best practices.** Websites that are fast, mobile-friendly, etc., stand to perform better across traditional and AI search.

## 3. Matching Search Intent Is Even More Important

Search engines and AI platforms prioritize content that best matches what users are searching for (their search intent).

Semrush’s [ranking factors study](https://go.semrush.com/Ranking-Factors.html) confirmed text relevance as the most important Google ranking factor. Text relevance means how well your content matches what users want to find when they search.

![A list of the different factors influencing SERP position with text relevance being the most important Google ranking factor.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/36/8d/368ddf1115128a437413bad473abea75/beb5bd7aed0d0591c1d3178641ac278d/image.png)

And this concept is just as important for AI tools. In fact, alignment with intent might be even more important given the highly specific questions users tend to ask tools like ChatGPT.

This means your pages need to provide the most helpful information for specific queries.

### How to Leverage This Trend

Create new content that matches search intent and update old content to make sure it satisfies what users want.

The first step is to find the intent behind your target keywords by analyzing Google search results and AI tool responses.

For example, when you search for "iphone 16 vs samsung s25" in Google, the top results are all detailed comparison articles that analyze features and prices. Meaning users probably want a side-by-side comparison to make an informed decision.

![Google SERP for the term "iphone 16 vs samsung s25" with all the top results being detailed comparison articles.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c6/c8/c6c810596a645db302b9dac4aba9ed6c/c936e89311c51aaa07c4ff8ed9610815/image.png)

And when you search the same query using ChatGPT, you’ll see a detailed breakdown of features and considerations to help make a purchase decision. As well as source citations.

![ChatGPT results showing a detailed comparison of features for the term "iphone 16 vs samsung s25" along with source citations.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/66/2e/662e597b790f6adc4d38cbd315451009/c51e9e1f60ce9c06c9ae41f9e1915bb0/image.png)

That said, intent can sometimes be difficult to determine, especially when you consider results are often personalized to the user. So, use Semrush's [Keyword Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/) tool for a more definitive way to identify intent.

Open the tool, enter your keyword (or a list of keywords), and click "**Search**."

![Keyword Overiew tool with "best yoga clothes" entered as the term and "Search" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/57/18/5718b33445aa01354cb00bd79c0a5eab/660c97dc180222f67e3ea5fc78c818cf/image.png)

Check the "**Intent**" widget for one or more keyword intent types:

- **Informational**: Users want to learn something
- **Navigational**: Users are looking for a specific website or page
- **Commercial**: Users are doing research before buying
- **Transactional**: Users are ready to take action

![Keyword Overview report with the "Intent" column highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/03/55/0355adb181adc6d8c9eaac53cb07677b/1a458a7752f87c51a2688cd591b6918a/image.png)

Next, scroll down to the “SERP Analysis” section and study the pages you see there. Look at what subtopics they cover, how the article is formatted, etc.

![SERP Analysis on Keyword Overview showing the top ranking pages for a keyword with the "Open link" button next to a page highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bc/ca/bccadde4fb4940e5ababafe3bed2eeb9/7ecb1ca11e745a090d9ce2df69cdf966/image.png)

Next, create content that’s similar (or update your existing content) but with your unique spin.

## 4. Credibility Holds More Weight

Google aims to rank content that aligns with its Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness ([E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/)) framework. And that same general framework can also help you create content that’s more likely to be cited in AI responses.

E-E-A-T itself isn’t a ranking factor. However, there are real people who use that framework when assessing the quality of search results. And their feedback can inform future Google algorithm updates.

And consider that [research has shown](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09735) practices like incorporating statistics, adding quotes, and citing reputable sources can help you appear in AI responses.

### How to Leverage This Trend

[Create content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-creation/) that displays credibility by including:

- Actual results from campaigns you've run
- Lessons learned from failures
- Observations from years in your industry
- Niche-specific statistics from trusted sources
- Quotes for internal and/or external subject matter experts

It’s also a good idea to have author pages that highlight your team’s qualifications.

For example, every writer and editor on Bankrate has a dedicated author page that displays their credentials and background.

![An author bio on Bankrate detailing a writer's credentials and background.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/29/a8/29a86b954d7efd8e6cc201f3c81203a6/9c6823bdbeb15b8eb9d0501fa7bbbeaf/image.png)

And many Bankrate articles include a “Reviewed by” label, so readers know the information has been thoroughly vetted.

![A Bankrate article with the “Reviewed by” label highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a1/13/a11324732de53a69c08060f095aa8751/d5d5f21f7e6cd6cc230e18a78b8b9bc2/image.png)

Bankrate also demonstrates credibility by incorporating insights from relevant experts:

![Insights from a personal finance expert on an article on how to get a mortgage by Bankrate.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d4/bd/d4bd0066ad5fc084cfdc6364348e68c1/d1a51c3c157f68426b597331ac5954e2/image.png)

All of Bankrate’s efforts to demonstrate their expertise and credibility likely help them appear prominently in not only traditional search results, but also in AI search results.

In fact, Bankrate is one of the most-cited finance sources in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, according to our [AI Visibility Index](https://ai-visibility-index.semrush.com/).

## 7. Zero-Click Searches Have Changed Search Behavior

Zero-click searches continue to rise—only [40.3%](https://datos.live/report/state-of-search-q1-2025/) of U.S. Google searches resulted in clicks to organic results in March 2025—down from 44.2% in March of the previous year.

The decrease in clicks to search results means users increasingly find answers directly on Google's results page, like through [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) that answer user’s questions without requiring a click.

![Google SERP for the term "how to clean water bottle" with the AI Overview answering the question without requiring a click.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a1/8c/a18cecafe4a561d2e9957f9c4cca063c/aee94980373c45b3a08cd46b42ea6fae/image.png)

Other SERP features, like [featured snippets](https://www.semrush.com/blog/featured-snippets/) and [knowledge panels](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-knowledge-panel/), also contribute to fewer clicks as SEO expert [Aleyda Solis explains](https://x.com/aleyda/status/1988602828227494095):

> *“The much more prominent inclusion of SERP features above the fold (e.g., product carousels, video carousels, PAAs) many of which keep the user engaging more within the SERPs or refer to Google's own properties (YouTube) or partners (Reddit) rather than smaller/independent sites.”*

While fewer clicks might seem concerning, this trend allows you to gain exposure when you’re prominently displayed and showcase your expertise directly on the search results page.

### How to Leverage This Trend

Optimize for visibility in AI Overviews and other prominent results by structuring your content to be easily extracted and cited—this also increases the odds of appearing in other AI tools.

For instance, create content that directly answers questions in the first paragraph. AI Overviews often pull from content that provides immediate, clear answers followed by supporting details.

Use the [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/) to identify keywords where certain SERP features appear. And focus your efforts on optimizing for those opportunities.

Open the tool, enter a seed keyword (a broad term related to your niche), select a location, and click “**Search**.”

![Keyword Magic Tool with "buy clothes for newborn" entered and "Search" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ab/cc/abcc57ad8732edfffd8b288cb059ac79/9890b70441f35735290f963aa0c22389/image.png)

Then, click “**Advanced filters**,” select the “**Any**” drop-down under “SERP Features,” and check the box next to “AI Overviews.” Click “**Apply**.”

![Applying a filter to only show terms that trigger an AI Overview from the "SERP Features" filter on the Keyword Magic Tool.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5a/7c/5a7c48aa2b8541d5b794b048482eb3bb/b0b78faf34265490e25b69b358246481/image.png)

Now, you’ll see a list of related keywords. And it will be filtered to only show terms that trigger an AI Overview.

Visit the results pages for these terms to better understand what’s showing in the AI Overviews. For example, analyze what sources it cites and how those pages structure the answers.

![SERP for a term which triggers an AI Overview with the sources on the right-hand side highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e6/b1/e6b16841333ea5e0c09d3e893db581db/563d17a269a405549f1f0202af285684/image.png)

Then optimize your content to match. For example:

- If the AI Overview lists steps, ensure your content has a clear numbered list
- If the AI Overview provides a definition, lead with a concise definition in your content
- If the AI Overview compares options, create a comparison table or a clear pros/cons section

## 8. Conversational Keywords Are Key

[Keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/) that’s more focused on long-tail, conversational phrases is even more crucial in the AI era.

People naturally ask AI search platforms detailed questions like "what's the best way to remove red wine stains from a white cotton shirt" rather than typing "stain removal."

And we know AI systems split user queries into even more sub-queries when finding information to use in a response—this is called [query fan-out](https://www.semrush.com/blog/query-fan-out/).

![AI splits a user prompt into several sub-prompts, then combines retrieved information to form a a final reponse.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/87/74/877466c106301e5d7dfdbdcc14bd5bc7/b7c8b92547ea4659879dc07b2ea65f75/image.png)

Plus, very specific keywords tend to attract users who are highly motivated. And that means they may be more likely to convert.

### How to Leverage This Trend

Optimize your content with conversational keywords that match what your target audience is searching for using the [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).

Start by entering a seed keyword. Then add your domain to the "AI-powered feature" field to see your Personal Keyword Difficulty (PKD %) score—this shows how difficult it will be for your exact domain to rank in Google’s top 10 results for that keyword.

![Keyword Magic Tool with a keyword and domain entered along with "Search" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ae/74/ae7479e7a44099dcc175dccd1620fd5f/24661af2c4881b341ca22d0ae17db4d7/image.png)

Then, click on the “**Personal KD %**” filter, enter “29” in the “To” field, and click “**Apply**” to see keywords that are easy for your site to rank highly for.

![Applying the “Personal KD %” filter on the Keyword Magic Tool by entering "29" and clicking "Apply".](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/19/a5/19a568a2d6708a9842ecfe217ad3f8c6/a8fccb1cb243af8da205edc79d1472fa/image.png)

Look specifically for:

- Questions starting with "how," "what," "why," "when"
- Phrases with conversational modifiers like "for beginners," "without," "vs"
- Location or situation-specific queries ("in small spaces," "on a budget," "for seniors")

Many of these will be long-tail, conversational keywords that mirror how people search in 2025.

Review the list and select keywords that are most relevant to you. Then, create a [content calendar](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-calendar/) that maps out what content you’ll create and when.

## 9. Local SEO Has Moved Beyond Just Google Business Profile Optimization

Local search results are rich with features like local packs, knowledge panels, and image carousels, so your [local SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/) strategy must go beyond creating and optimizing your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) (GBP).

You need to build a comprehensive online presence that establishes your business as a trusted, authoritative entity. Which you can do by gaining online mentions, getting reviews, etc.

### How to Leverage This Trend

Focus on building your business's reputation and visibility across your entire online presence to improve local search rankings.

Take CAN Roof Construction, a roofing contractor in Boston, as an example. Their website has multiple service pages, each optimized for specific keywords, to make it easy for both users and search engines to understand exactly what the company offers.

![The footer section of a website with links to multiple service pages, each optimized for specific keywords.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a8/b8/a8b87a6372049d3ccad7b4cda6d76502/e9fdd3b4a3dae1d8bd21e4d58b05dbf0/image.png)

CAN Roof Construction also has listings on trusted directories such as Yelp and the Better Business Bureau. Consistent business details and positive reviews across these platforms strengthen their authority and credibility.

![The Yelp profile of a local business showing their website, phone number, reviews and rating, services offered, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a1/ab/a1abff5d6fb5b601dbb9c58be6919481/9f01aa14485613f722f2cd7a087a7b14/image.png)
