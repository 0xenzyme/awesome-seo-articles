# For webmasters: Google+ and the +1 button 101

Here's a video that covers the basics of
[Google+](https://plus.google.com/), the
[+1 button](https://www.google.com/+1/button/),
getting started on Google+, and how social information can make products, like Search, more
relevant. This video is for a range of webmasters (from personal bloggers to SEOs of
corporations). So, if you're interested in learning about Google+, we hope that with 20 minutes
and this video on YouTube (we have our own
[Webmaster Support Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@GoogleSearchCentral)!),
you can feel more up to speed with Google's social opportunities.

Speaking of Google+, if you join, please say hello! We're often posting and
[hosting Hangouts](/search/events#sign-up-for-online-seo-office-hours).
