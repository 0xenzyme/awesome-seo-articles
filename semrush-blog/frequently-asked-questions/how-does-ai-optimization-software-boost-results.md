# How does AI optimization software boost results?

AI optimization software helps you boost results by giving you all the information you need about your current visibility in AI platforms.

The first step is to measure your current performance before working to improve further. AI optimization software gives you the following information so you can boost your results.

- **Share of voice data**: This helps you see how dominant your brand is in your space. If you’re not the number one player in your industry, that means you have a lot of room for improvement
- **Sentiment analysis**: This shows whether AI systems describe your brand positively, negatively, or neutrally. You can then address negative perceptions quickly. And reinforce positive positioning.
- **Competitor analysis**. See how often competitors are being mentioned in AI responses. If they have more visibility in AI platforms than you, visit their website and try to understand why they’re so successful.
- **Content performance insights**: Identify which of your content pieces get cited most frequently by AI systems. This can tell you what formats and topics to prioritize in future content creation.
