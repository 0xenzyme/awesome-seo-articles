# 50 Small Business Statistics to Know

Paying attention to small business trends and statistics is valuable to anyone who owns or wants to own a small business. Because it helps you understand both risks and opportunities.

The below list of small business statistics provides a high-level overview of the small business landscape.

And they’re organized by category. So you can focus on which ones you’re most interested in learning more about.

But before the numbers, let’s start with why it’s a good idea to review small business stats in the first place.

## Why Statistics Are Important to Small Businesses

Reviewing small business stats can give you a baseline for strategic planning and decision-making. Which can lead to higher revenue and greater efficiency.

More specifically, making smart use of statistics enables you to:

- See how competitive you are in the overall market
- Understand the common challenges that can lead to small business failure
- Discover emerging trends and technologies you can leverage to fuel growth

In short, you’ll have information to guide you. So you don’t have to guess about what to do and what to avoid.

Now, let’s get into the numbers.

## 50 Small Business Statistics

The below list of statistics includes the most up-to-date information about small businesses from sources like the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA), the U.S. Chamber of Commerce, and more

## General Small Businesses Statistics

1. There are 33.2 million small businesses in the United States ([SBA Office of Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Frequently-Asked-Questions-About-Small-Business-March-2023-508c.pdf), 2023)

2. 63% of the new jobs created between 1995 and 2021 came from small businesses ([SBA Office of Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Frequently-Asked-Questions-About-Small-Business-March-2023-508c.pdf), 2023)

3. There are 61.7 million small business employees in the U.S.—that’s 46% of the private sector workforce ([SBA Office of Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Frequently-Asked-Questions-About-Small-Business-March-2023-508c.pdf), 2023)

4. A small business is generally defined as a small enterprise with less than 1,500 employees ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/sites/default/files/2023-06/Table%20of%20Size%20Standards_Effective%20March%2017%2C%202023%20%282%29.pdf), 2023)

5. The average small business with employees has a staff of 11.7 employees ([SBA Office of Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Frequently-Asked-Questions-About-Small-Business-March-2023-508c.pdf), 2023)

6. 99.6% of small businesses with employees have fewer than 500 employees ([Federal Reserve Board of Governors](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2022-october-availability-of-credit-to-small-businesses.htm), 2022)

## Small Business Success vs. Failure Statistics

20. 44% of small businesses reported revenue increases in 2022. And 18% recorded no change in revenue. ([Federal Reserve Banks](https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/survey/2023/report-on-employer-firms), 2023)

21. About 80% of small businesses survive their first year. And close to 50% remain open for at least five years. ([Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/bdm/us_age_naics_00_table7.txt), 2023)

22. Female-owned small businesses have an average annual revenue of $263,092. That compares to an average annual revenue of $469,372 for male-owned small businesses. ([Biz2Credit,](https://cdn.biz2credit.com/appfiles/biz2credit/pdf/Annual-Women-Owned-Business-study-2023.pdf) 2023)

23. In 2020, 1.02 million small business establishments permanently closed ([SBA Office of Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Frequently-Asked-Questions-About-Small-Business-March-2023-508c.pdf), 2023)

24. 82% of small businesses fail due to poor cash flow management ([U.S. Bank](https://spend.usbank.com/blog/cash-flow-monitoring-strategies-for-small-businesses-tips-and-best-practices/), 2023)

## Small Business vs. Large Corporation Statistics

25. Small businesses have accounted for over 40% of gross domestic product (GDP) over the past few decades ([SBA Office of Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/2018/12/19/advocacy-releases-small-business-gdp-1998-2014/), 2018)

26. Both small and large firms recovered all the jobs lost during the second quarter of 2020 by the third quarter of 2022 ([SBA](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Economic-Bulletin-June-2023.pdf), 2023)

27. In 2017, the average large company spent $330 million on research and development (R&D). But the average small company spent only $6 million on it. ([Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2019/08/the-gap-between-large-and-small-companies-is-growing-why), 2019)
