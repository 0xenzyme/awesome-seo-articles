# 89 B2B Marketing Statistics You Need to Know in 2025

Let's explore the most important B2B marketing stats for 2024 and beyond.

## B2B Marketing Priorities and Challenges

1. The number one priority for B2B marketers is growing a high-quality lead pipeline, with 37% naming it as one of their top areas of focus. ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))
2. Other top priorities for B2B marketers include implementing AI technology (35%), using data to drive decisions and measure performance (33%), and building relationships with new customers (32%). ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))

3. For 58% of B2B marketers, lack of resources is a challenge. ([CMI](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research/))
4. 45% find alignment between sales and marketing teams difficult. ([CMI](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research/))
5. 34% of B2B marketers struggle to keep up with new tech, including AI. ([CMI](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research/))
6. 25% of B2B marketers admit lack of strategy is an issue. ([CMI](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research/))

**Takeaways:**

Overall, B2B marketers are optimistic and focused on lead quality and AI adoption.

Despite technical advancements, marketing teams face age-old challenges like resource constraints and alignment with sales.

The outlook is positive, but marketers need to address these key challenges to fully capitalize on opportunities.

## B2B Marketing Team Statistics

8. Nine in 10 B2B CMOs feel good about their team’s ability to drive revenue. ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))
9. 56% of B2B marketing leaders are investing more in diversity, equity and inclusion efforts to build better, more diverse teams. ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))
10. Eight in 10 B2B CMOs say they have gender-diverse teams. And three in four say their teams are racially and age diverse. ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))

([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))

13. Only 20% of B2B marketers found collaboration difficult because of remote or hybrid work. ([CMI](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research/))
14. 44% of B2B companies have in-house marketing teams, 33% do their marketing both in-house and outsourced, and 21% outsource their marketing. ([Sagefrog](https://www.sagefrog.com/resources/2023-b2b-marketing-mix-report/))
15. 93% of companies who outsource their marketing find outsourcing effective. ([Sagefrog](https://www.sagefrog.com/resources/2023-b2b-marketing-mix-report/))

**Takeaways:**

B2B marketing teams are focusing on diversity and adaptability.

Despite organizational changes and fluctuation, CMOs feel confident in their teams' abilities. And there's a growing trend of blending in-house expertise with external talent to meet marketing goals.

## B2B Sales and Lead Generation Statistics

27. 84% of marketers say driving new business is their top priority. But only 27% say they are effective at it. ([Sirkin Research](https://www.sirkinresearch.com/state-of-b2b-marketing-2023-q2))
28. 48% of B2B marketers say driving enough leads is a challenge, and 24% reported difficulties with converting leads to revenue. ([Sirkin Research](https://www.sirkinresearch.com/state-of-b2b-marketing-2023-q2))

29. Seven in 10 B2B respondents have increased their budget for lead generation. ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))
30. 60% of B2B marketing budgets are allocated to acquiring new customers, whereas 40% are dedicated to retention. ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/lms-state-of-b2b-marketing/insights/2024/pdfs/LinkedIn-B2B-Benchmark-2024-Report.pdf))
31. B2B ecommerce has emerged as the most effective sales channel in 2024. 35% of leaders rank it first, followed by in-person sales (26%), video conferencing (12%), email (10%), and telephone (8%). ([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/the-multiplier-effect-how-b2b-winners-grow))
32. Five key strategies drive B2B sales success in 2024: advanced sales tools, highly personalized marketing, hybrid sales teams, tailored third-party marketplace strategies, and investment in an owned marketplace. Companies implementing all five strategies are two times more likely to achieve over 10% market share growth. ([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/the-multiplier-effect-how-b2b-winners-grow))
33. 69% of B2B professionals report sales is becoming more difficult. ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/eu/resources/research-reports/state-of-the-connected-customer/))

**Takeaways:**

B2B sales statistics show that [lead generation strategy](https://www.semrush.com/blog/lead-generation-strategies/) is the main focus for most marketers.

That being said, [ecommerce](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-ecommerce/) is a key trend. Since B2B customers increasingly expect Amazon-style digital experiences where they just click and check out.
