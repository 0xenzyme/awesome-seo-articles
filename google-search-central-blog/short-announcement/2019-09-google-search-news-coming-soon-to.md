# Google Search News: coming soon to a screen near you

The world of search is constantly evolving. New tools, opportunities, and features are regularly
arriving, sometimes existing things change, and sometimes we say goodbye to some things to make
way for the new. To help you stay on top of things, we've started a new YouTube series called
[Google Search News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=78GFh553Vbc&list=PLKoqnv2vTMUNDpWUa0TaFPSi7VYLBKV03).

With Google Search News, we want to give you a regular and short summary of what's been happening
around Google Search, specifically for SEOs, publishers, developers, and webmasters. The first
episode is out now, so check it out.

*(The first episode, now on your screen)*

In this first episode, we cover:

- Recent updates to [Search Console](/search/blog/2019/09/goodbye-old-search-console)
- Changes in the [webmaster office-hours](/search/events/join-office-hours) setup
- Advances for
  [rel=nofollow](/search/blog/2019/09/evolving-nofollow-new-ways-to-identify) and
  related attributes
- Changes in
  [review rich results](/search/blog/2019/09/making-review-rich-results-more-helpful)
- New meta-tags and attributes for your
  [pages' search previews](/search/blog/2019/09/more-controls-on-search)
- Some recent Webmaster Conferences

We plan to make these updates regularly, and adjust the format over time as needed. Let us know
what you think in the video comments!
