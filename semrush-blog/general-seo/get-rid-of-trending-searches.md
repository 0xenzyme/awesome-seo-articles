## How to Turn Off Trending Searches on Android

Turning off trending searches on an Android device (like a Google Pixel smartphone or Samsung Galaxy tablet) involves adjusting your Google settings on your device’s browser.

Here’s how to do it for the [Chrome](#if-you-use-chrome-on-android) and [Firefox](#if-you-use-firefox-on-android) browsers. And these instructions should also work for other browsers like Brave.

### If You Use Chrome on Android

1. Launch the Chrome browser on your Android device and navigate to google.com

2. Click the hamburger icon at the top left corner of the Google homepage

![Google homepage with the hamburger icon highlighted in purple](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/17/90/179093243a8a49c1af7b4efd0274f391/TBnsTemXV9Gx2_xOfGv7vB3irch0CxXKg9DLfrITdmCOpL5Ohhh6N9KpI_o-ZY4Oih8JluEzwbyPpkDuy0NPKNGZSPWjHQXtdSNR94NoVTRG8LtDtzS0vp2v_ZDpmWxgvlYVw5vYI4WlYDvzB0uRAGs.png)

3. Select “**Search settings**” in the left sidebar that appears

![Google “Search settings” button highlighted in purple](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3b/41/3b41729ede1388470153507bb68a87b0/owe9njyGHuIYXqj4Gxk2hdS7GuXPxrFA1jBlH7YQmncmyxWmLcu6FuvQyHjtk0MxsNEmXuN8xwx2KZSCR2uk83wftYHISa47hswY42W7BK6wU5ZrU-8KjiDq1Hrw4u9P3oRiF3AqpPte5Ey-cjJpfWc.png)

4. Tap “**Other settings**” at the top of the search settings page

![Google “Other settings” button highlighted in purple](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ff/67/ff67cd3f1548682e09efd14d719dc67b/oj36v9X5Fzf6f0HAHwIIdYsJeqF_fJ678jg5LunuT5cm9CCzqvi-3gd8HOn6Dq2LsChVj7YjrdJwDUYVOmyPTDDK3uiH4C-U_a3sZYm-3pxsmlV3rpoltJ2R940vO-eSdTHDM8nhUuhMBZKAeU_0Mko.jpeg)

5. Scroll down until you see the “Auto-complete with trending searches” switch, then disable it

![“Auto-complete with trending searches” switch highlighted in purple](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/74/f3/74f3029d4d41d04be46874528573079e/rILOWQJJexxO9XjR8I0STjgNnRppWRmtSD3SM87RpdQct6N8xEQnFSYSX3T_EtbMVUPW_92ZctapcHiyLm8QEaDevoaH5ILSB1oZd3V5jDBk06_sKy9TOqfXD02Ziz7G0T9Gat8iIqcG17UIVFz8quE.png)

### If You Use Firefox on Android

1. Open the Firefox browser on your Android device and navigate to google.com

2. Click “**Settings**” at the bottom of the Google homepage

![“Settings” at the bottom of the Google homepage](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d0/84/d0846fde77c7e5fc235b41c9cd0da697/6wcBkQhDCAFy5_IqLCCxIV2QDJPL-oqDD8sT2Rd_HALPpKKzpr4FMXpKosq_HrJA6jYoAuuMAM_KeGWNJmGPgN5BgE6nCJaIUJ5hjKdQ6bgPGF1ojdNRPeXYNSRsSckAQ-e9z1yawpnyGO06o0DtFxI.png)

![“Auto-complete with trending searches” section](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b2/6e/b26ee73fd01e285af2dcc07a0578dfa1/eOc-ZjcxKz7UvdOFvI4jzjN-Nor93AmA6NyU9weRwqflovkbysY8lPenO7Z6oHcVowIJu_SlaoQuM8TeMKsnMyExAoIXHySSbAhPMSyOADa24ECZLVnABhZzrptyN35qJHW4fOzf8zmjIQb-d-cy3AM.jpeg)

## What to Do If You Can’t Turn Off Trending Searches

If you’ve double-checked that you’ve followed the steps above correctly but Google is still showing trending searches, try these solutions:

### Solution 1: Restart Your Browser

The changes you’ve made to your trending search settings should take effect immediately. But if it seems like they haven’t, restarting your browser can help Google apply your new setting.

Close your browser and wait a few seconds. Then, open your browser again.

Navigate to google.com. Then click the search bar to see whether any trending searches appear there.

### Solution 2: Update Your Browser or Google App

Sometimes, a bug can cause trending searches to appear. Even if you’ve already turned them off.

And that bug may have been fixed in a newer version of your browser or Google app. So, try updating which one you’re using.

Visit these guides for instructions on updating your browsers:

- [How to update Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95414)
- [How to update Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/update-firefox-latest-release)
- [How to update Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204416)

And you can check for any available app updates on your mobile device’s app store. Meaning the Google Play Store for Android devices and the App Store for Apple devices.

## FAQs About Trending Searches

### What Are Trending Searches on Google?

Trending searches are search queries that are currently more popular than usual.

They look like this:

!["Trending searches" section in Google](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b0/ed/b0ed5dda60855c03563d3d52ef5f27e2/YukcujDJPuSe7wScbAozpDR0TPOUBIY2wsKbPhBW7ME5OaNB_KqeWudB3fglJgWrWLD7aK5PdZPSf75PdjVS6A7PfdjHUR3bqR9UdFIgstfPsr49rbDwHZMHxQ3_q9vbhiptamVUL9mFFTPZmTAmPgQ.png)

Trending searches often relate to the latest news and happenings near your location. And they change frequently.

### Why Does Google Showcase Trending Searches?

Google showcases trending searches to suggest search queries users might be interested in.

They give users inspiration on what to search for. Which can boost how much use Google gets.

### Should I Disable Trending Searches?

You can disable trending searches on Google if they distract from your search experience.

Remember: Disabling isn’t permanent, and you can always undo it later.

But disabling trending searches means losing one source of trending news.

### Can I Temporarily Remove Trending Searches?

You can temporarily remove trending searches on Google by disabling the “Auto-complete with trending searches” switch in your device’s browser or Google app.

To re-enable trending searches, simply turn that switch on again.
