# New third-party Sitemaps tools

Hello, webmasters, I'm Maile, and I recently joined the team here at
[Google webmaster central](/search). And I already have good news to report: we've
updated our
[third-party program and websites](https://code.google.com/sm_thirdparty)
information. These third-party tools provide lots of options for easily generate a
Sitemap—from plugins for content management systems to online generators.

Many thanks to this community for continuing to innovate and improve the Sitemap tools. Since most
of my work focuses on the Sitemaps protocol, I hope to meet you on our
[Sitemaps protocol discussion group](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community).
