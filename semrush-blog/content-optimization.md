# Content Optimization: 15 Tactics to Boost SEO & AI Visibility

## What Is Content Optimization?

Content optimization is the process of improving and updating your website content to improve its visibility in both search engines and AI tools.

When done well, it helps your content reach more users, appear in AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google’s [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/), and convert those visitors into customers.

Today, optimization goes beyond traditional SEO. It ensures every page is clear, relevant, and easy for both algorithms and AI systems to understand.

By the end of this guide, you’ll know how to optimize content to increase organic (unpaid) traffic, earn AI citations, and drive more conversions.

## Why Is Content Optimization Important?

Content optimization helps your website rank higher in search engines and appear in AI results. It also motivates users to take valuable actions like signing up, purchasing, or contacting your business.

Consistent optimization keeps driving results long after you publish. Search engines like Google favor content that’s fresh and recently updated.

A [Seer Interactive study](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/study-ai-brand-visibility-and-content-recency) found that almost 90% of AI bot activity focused on content published within the past three years, with the strongest preference for pages updated between 2023 and 2025.

For example, Semrush’s [link building guide](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/) has been updated several times since publication.

And it still ranks on Google’s first page.

![Google SERP with "link building" entered as the term which shows a link building guide from Semrush appearing in the third position.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/dd/54/dd54a6d3c830dc432df504104c8d833b/4839b3af748bc90186aaee8b67841fcd/image.jpeg)

It appears as a source in Google’s AI Mode.

!["link building" entered on Google's AI Mode which shows a link building guide by Semrush cited as a source.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2e/f3/2ef3b681475402fa3783a5fd618f977b/8ea219e208dfc5252b3ab2e025626281/image.jpeg)

And it shows up in ChatGPT’s web results.

!["link building" entered on ChatGPT which shows a link building guide by Semrush cited as a source.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ba/02/ba02887312ac7533bba67dfaec1f3185/d72640e403eb8dd76bb7b587773c9e6c/image.jpeg)

Search and AI tools are merging. [Generative engine optimization (GEO)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/generative-engine-optimization/) focuses on optimizing content for AI-generated responses in platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode.

Adding GEO principles—such as using conversational phrasing, full-question answers, and unique data—can increase your chances of being cited by AI systems.

Optimizing content this way improves visibility, drives organic traffic, and delivers better long-term return on investment (ROI).

## How to Optimize Content for SEO

Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you optimize your content for both SEO and GEO:

### 1. Match Search Intent to Improve Rankings

[Search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/) is the goal behind a user’s query. Google and other search engines aim to show content that best satisfies that goal.

When your content aligns with the user’s intent, it has a stronger change of ranking higher.

There are four types of search intent:

- **Informational**: The user wants to learn about a topic
- **Transactional**: The user wants to make a purchase or complete a transaction
- **Navigational**: The user is looking for a specific website or page
- **Commercial**: The user is comparing options before buying

![Types of search intent: informational, navigational, commercial, and transactional.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7b/7a/7b7a6dbd01e2aa09ec1b431371e1ddd3/c695b32184b7b1ae9a37cb8db978c196/image.png)

There are two ways to determine search intent.

First, enter your keyword into Google (or another search engine) and review the top results.

For example, searching for “influencer marketing tools” shows mostly list and comparison posts.

That means the keyword has both commercial and informational intent.

![Google SERP for the term "influencer marketing tools" showing lists and comparison posts indicating both commercial and informational intent.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3c/f3/3cf3f27da90daf79b639eb7678768f08/9774b02e19bf32f8fde036aa31afb203/image.png)

Second, use Semrush’s [Keyword Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/) tool.

Enter your keyword and check the Intent box to confirm the search intent type. For “influencer marketing tools,” the tool shows commercial and informational intent.

![Keyword Overview report for the term "influencer marketing tools" indicating informational and commercial intent.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b1/ac/b1acf066b512abced0c9cdfe79a490a3/083e1ded580a1fe74e0a45b1ef3f1ecb/image.png)

Once you know the intent, structure your content to match it.

If your post on “influencer marketing tools” only defines the tools, expand it to include comparisons and recommendations.

Because search intent can change over time, review important keywords regularly and adjust your content to stay relevant.

### 2. Use Secondary Keywords to Expand Reach

Adding secondary keywords helps search engines and AI crawlers understand your topic more deeply. It also broadens your chances to appear for related searches.

Each post should target one [primary keyword](https://www.semrush.com/blog/primary-keywords/)—the main keyword you want to rank for—and several [secondary keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/secondary-keywords/) that support it.

Ranking for multiple terms can attract more visitors and increase organic traffic.

Most content already ranks for multiple keywords naturally, but being intentional can boost performance. Focus on secondary terms with high traffic potential.

Use Semrush [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/) to find these keywords.

To get started, open the tool, enter your primary keyword, and click “**Search**.”

- [**Keyword Difficulty (KD %)**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-difficulty/): How hard it is to rank in the top 10 results

Then, naturally include them throughout your content, especially in headers, introductions, and key sections where they fit contextually.

### 3. Optimize for AI Prompts and Questions

Optimizing for AI prompts means structuring your content around the exact questions people ask in AI tools. This helps your pages surface in AI-generated responses.

When people use AI tools, they use more conversational phrasing than they do in Google searches.

For example:

- Google search: “benefits of CRM”
- AI prompt: “What are the benefits of using a CRM for a small ecommerce business?”

![ChatGPT interface with a response to the prompt "What are the benefits of using a CRM for a small ecommerce business?”.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9d/32/9d3250fb1a196127ba9ff851931da08f/fc1e6128a1f6dde4447668a49f933ea2/image.png)

Start with your first-party data.

Review sales calls, demo transcripts, and support tickets to see how your audience phrases questions.

You can also use Semrush’s [AI Visibility toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/visibility/). In the “Questions” report, you’ll find grouped queries related to your topic, such as pricing, competitors, or product experience.

![Questions report on the AI SEO toolkit showing query topics grouped by pricing, shopping experience, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a4/11/a411fe899568ce6476d73e0ec9788c04/a4625703b1ae1784154d03069b10daca/image.jpeg)

Address these prompts in your content by:

- Adding relevant questions to existing pages if they fit naturally
- Creating new pages or blog posts for questions that need deeper coverage

AI tools process natural phrasing more accurately when language mirrors real conversation. Write clearly and directly, avoiding unnecessary entities or filler words.

Writing for AI starts with writing for humans: concise, natural, and complete answers to real questions.

### 4. Make Your Content Easy to Understand

Clear, readable content keeps visitors engaged and helps search engines and AI tools interpret your message accurately.

Most users skim pages. If your content is dense or confusing, they leave quickly. t.

A Semrush survey found that [43%](https://www.semrush.com/content-hub/can-ai-content-rank-on-google/) of marketers and SEOs prioritize readability to improve both SEO and conversions.

![An Semrush survey finding that 43% of marketers prioritize readability to improve both SEO and content ROI.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5a/cd/5acdd1eacd2f0fe3f448590b3a8de11a/1551db9e971ab19f63b345cf521076e6/image.jpeg)

When your writing is clear and well-structured, it can perform better in search and AI-generated results.

Ways to improve readability:

- **Use a clear heading structure**. Organize content with H1, H2, and H3 tags. This improves navigation for readers and context for search engines and AI crawlers.
- **Write in active voice**. Active voice improves clarity and flow. For example, write “the reader searched for the keyword” instead of “the keyword was searched for by the reader.”
- **Apply the Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF) method**. Present key information first. Answer the main question at the start of each section.
- **Write for natural language processing (NLP)**. Use straightforward, conversational phrasing. Avoid jargon or long, nested clauses.
- **Use bullet points for key details**. They make information easier to scan and process
- **Add visuals**. Images, infographics, and charts make content more engaging and help readers grasp complex ideas
- **Check narrative flow**. Read the content aloud to catch awkward phrasing or pacing issues

Readable, structured content improves user experience and helps search engine crawlers and AI tools understand and surface your content accurately.

### 5. Keep Content Fresh to Sustain Visibility

[Updating content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/when-to-update-blog-content/) helps maintain accuracy, build trust, and sustain visibility in both search engines and AI tools.

Google treats freshness as a ranking signal, and AI systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity also prefer newer sources.

A [Seer Interactive study](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/study-ai-brand-visibility-and-content-recency) found that these tools are more likely to cite content published or updated recently.

For example, a blog post on influencer marketing trends from 2015 is outdated. But if you update it with 2024 insights, it becomes relevant again.

Wondering how to find outdated content?

Use Semrush’s [Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) tool to monitor performance. A noticeable drop in rankings can signal that a page needs updates.

![Rankings Overview on Position Tracking showing a keyword with a big drop in rankings and traffic.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/94/23/9423ddd8af244c7c06c3da59882834e9/4d8a82b2d1a172a97585275cdb7d7126/image.png)

When you find pages losing visibility, update them using these steps:

- Fix broken links to improve user experience
- Add new internal links to relevant content on your site
- Replace outdated information, data, and examples
- Update visuals such as images, charts, and infographics

Regularly refreshed content signals reliability to both readers and AI tools—and keeps your pages ranking longer.

### 6. Improve On-Page SEO for Better Search Performance

[On-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) focuses on optimizing individual pages to help them rank higher in search results.

Three key elements—[title tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/), [meta descriptions](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/), and [alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/)—play a major role in how search engines interpret your content.

- Title tags define your page’s main topic
- Meta descriptions summarize what readers will find on the page.
- Alt text describes images so search engines and screen readers can understand them

### 7. Optimize Your Page for SERP Features

[SERP features](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/) are enhanced results on Google’s search pages that go beyond standard organic listings. Ranking for them can increase your visibility and click-through rate.

Some examples of SERP features include AI Overviews, featured snippets, local packs, People Also Ask, and knowledge panels.

Next, optimize your content for the SERP feature you want to rank for.

For example, if your target keyword shows a featured snippet, apply these best practices:

- **Use question-based headers**: Format H2s and H3s as full queries, such as “What is search intent?” to help Google identify relevant sections

### 8. Add Semantically Related Keywords

Using [semantically related keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semantic-keywords/) (terms that are conceptually linked to your main topic) shows search engines that you’ve covered a subject in depth, improving your chances of ranking higher.

For example, if your keyword is *“how to grow tomatoes,”* related terms could be *“organic gardening,”**“best soil for tomatoes,”* or *“tomato plant care.”*

These keywords also help AI crawlers better understand your content’s context.

These aren’t the exact keywords you want to rank for, but they’re conceptually linked. So, incorporating these into your content can help you compete more effectively with your competitors.

### 9. Optimize Visual Content

Visuals like images and videos make content more engaging and easier to understand.

But to support SEO, they must be properly optimized. Large, unoptimized files can slow down your site and hurt user experience.

Here are a few image optimization tactics to consider:

- **Add descriptive alt text**: Use clear, natural language that includes relevant keywords when appropriate. For example, “Brown dog jumping over a white fence in a grassy field.”
- **Compress file sizes**: Use tools like [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) or [JPEG Optimizer](https://jpeg-optimizer.com/) to reduce file sizes without losing quality
- **Use descriptive file names**: Replace generic file names like “image123.jpg” with keyword-rich names such as “seo-strategy-guide.jpg”
- **Provide captions or a transcripts**: Add captions for images and transcripts for videos to make content accessible and help search engines understand context

***Further reading****:* [*Image SEO: How to Optimize Images for Search Engines & Users*](https://www.semrush.com/blog/image-seo/)

Following these guidelines can improve your chances of ranking well in search results and getting referenced by AI tools:

#### Include Author and Contributor Bios

Hire writers and team members with industry experience and showcase that using bio pages.

Consider including years of experience (e.g., 10). And in what field the contributor specializes in (e.g., content marketing and SEO).

This shows the audience the writer has the experience and expertise to back up their statements.

#### Include Expert Insight

Interview subject matter experts and use their quotes to add value and insights to your content—or include experts in the review process.

Like this Semrush blog post:

![A quote from a subject matter expert included in a blog post by Semrush.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/86/50/8650da0994dc3048cd8ef4172ca90a3d/33b9666b516f34a5ddc5dac494cb4828/image.png)

![Google's documentation highlighting the importance of expert-driven content.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3e/84/3e844bc0e7d90d76bdf17cf0223895f6/3105a50576ec54bb84b76de5e5f1aae6/image.png)

#### Use Credible Sources

Support all claims with research from reliable sources, such as:

- Industry publications and authority blogs
- Academic journals and research studies
- Government or institutional reports
- Trusted news outlets

### 11. Cover the Topic Comprehensively

It also signals to Google that your page fully satisfies user intent, which can improve rankings.

## How to Optimize Content for Conversions

Optimizing your content for conversions (desirable actions like signing up for a newsletter or filling out a contact form) increases the chances they’ll take that desired next step.

Here’s are a few ways to do that:

### 13. Demonstrate the Value of Your Product or Service

Showing your product or service in action helps readers understand how it solves their problems.

When readers see clear, relevant use cases, they’re more likely to convert.

For example, many Semrush blog posts include workflows that demonstrate how to use Semrush tools to achieve specific goals.

Like this one showing readers how to find voice search keywords using the [free keyword tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).

![A blog post by Semrush showing a workflow to help readers achieve a specific goal using the tool.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e6/da/e6daf745892aaf05e52a368ac2bc2fae/6d038d1087918199eb158a8109c94e25/image.png)

However, avoid forcing product mentions.

Only include them where they naturally fit and genuinely help readers achieve their objectives.

Demonstrating real, practical value builds trust and keeps your content persuasive without sounding promotional.

### 14. Add Social Proof to Build Trust

Social proof, such as testimonials, case studies, and success stories, shows potential customers that others have benefited from your product or service.

It builds credibility and increases conversion likelihood.

For example, in a post about influencer marketing campaigns, you could feature a short case study showing how a brand used your influencer search software, followed by a direct customer testimonial.

A clear, authentic quote adds weight and emotional trust.

Insider uses this approach effectively. The company includes five-star reviews in its content to address a key audience challenge (MarTech implementation) and reassure readers that its solution delivers results.

![A page on Insider which includes five-star reviews in its content as social proof.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/17/6c/176cdbbab4395f6dca7d8b2fd3cd0c3f/c7ab4ef8e8f9036f29e8ee38b964edd8/image.png)

### 15. Use Calls to Action That Fit the Reader’s Journey

A [call to action](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-call-to-action/) (CTA) tells readers what to do next.

Clear CTAs increase engagement and guide users toward conversion, such as signing up, contacting your team, or making a purchase.

Common CTAs include:

- Sign Up Now
- Contact Us
- Buy Now

The key is to match the CTA with the reader’s stage in the journey.
