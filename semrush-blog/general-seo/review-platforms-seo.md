# The 9 Best Review Platforms for Businesses in 2025

Review platforms can help you build trust with your audience, improve online visibility, and gain a competitive edge.

To choose the right review platform(s) for your business, consider:

- Which platforms are popular with your target audience
- How much effort and expense is needed to remain active on each platform
- What features you want from your chosen platform(s)—e.g., review widgets

We’ve rounded up some of the best online review platforms to help you make a decision:

## 1. Google // Best for Local Search Visibility

Google reviews can boost your business’s visibility in local search results, which can help you convert more searchers into customers.

[According to Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en#zippy=%2Cmanage-respond-to-reviews), “High-quality, positive reviews from your customers can improve your business visibility and increase the likelihood that a shopper will visit your location.”

These reviews can appear in local search results like below:

![A Google Business Profile is open from the local map pack and shows customer reviews.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/67/44/67449b6955cb4bff477ae270053fa0ec/67e97538ac2d8217c71eabf18509c205/AD_4nXfakPtoRgcPwCHVwKhiwrQCnY_MgdLI-aHj2REhaeExk0wFJR4eLrbFCqL-4Krobpqmh8WK4RgLeWLjZvK_pYQTlThg-NNGQWhMtJ-3OdlcmqriHcM00N3jmnV1Na4CRYOeT2b47w.png)

To start getting Google reviews, add or claim your free Google Business Profile (the listing that appears in Google’s map-based results). And [verify your location](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/verify-my-business-google/).

Then, search “my business” in Google and click “**View profile**.”

On the next page, click “**Read reviews**.”

[Respond to your Google reviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-reply-to-google-reviews/) in the pop-up that appears. And report or flag any inappropriate reviews.

You can also copy a direct link to your Google reviews page. And share the link online to make the review process easier for customers.

![Google review link can be copied and sent via email, WhatsApp, and Facebook.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f1/82/f18249f360243883bb97a9cfff1f373b/d226cf46569e6d4fd3b1ec8aa72efaae/AD_4nXfbdp38fR5-jxbs4ZcxawJFM-5mzwSy6xB7KGuolCunMEbltJcwzI7mUIpyDOofpjJlanzHeCiaTXur2yPQ_YpxhLw_wOvbpJZO80p-taJBcK0-Z5x6_0pm8dus-53Jg-kMtgBaVg.jpeg)

Or turn the link into a scannable QR code to display on in-store posters, business cards, and other printed materials.

For more tips and strategies, check out our guide to [asking for Google reviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-ask-for-google-reviews/).

## 2. Facebook // Best for Social Engagement

Facebook reviews are valuable because Facebook is the [world’s most popular social platform](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-popular-social-media-platforms/), and many people use the platform to engage with businesses.

Plus, Bing sometimes displays Facebook reviews in search results:

![A local search shows business profile results with Facebook reviews.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/47/5d/475dd232651ae1552208208e59c7ad8d/9ea4ddee82c32e233de300b511a7ce1c/AD_4nXd79TjpBiNzXxTfU4PFBYiGJrpFFXm95DIi0JVlHFP7qjv4JT15fXg6SUvmIcj8aizvF_Wc8BjSgpdutx0hKbChLLMKdLQ6duhtmJB2TII5Kdy73oMAgsOIymvPBfOjjvvbOVAm_Q.png)

To start collecting Facebook reviews, [create a free Facebook business Page](https://en-us.facebook.com/business/help/473994396650734?id=939256796236247).

From your account, click on your profile picture, then “**Settings & privacy**” > “**Settings**.”

Then, go to your “**Page and tagging**” settings. And toggle on the switch beside “Allow others to view and leave reviews on your Page?”

Go to your page’s “**Reviews**” tab to see your reviews.

![Facebook reviews tab shows percentage who recommend the business and the number of reviews.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/de/11/de11a97fd21ff8481b5e7aee5399af07/2a0c7088d2fa259b4a4efd1bdf501af0/AD_4nXdT7b_A_coe_PX1wcE-4SyKelc-E6sSOZheezi23HIXQ5qxp0B9-G7GBE3EMWTGlhCSJeSY7-C-W_wvAF_LjosvlKF_G8VHC8x9-G1fNbGDDDoEvJ_nHeTwQ-wf_u0DK7VJkOzjGg.png)

Copy the URL for this reviews page so you can link to it directly. The page will ask visitors if they recommend your business and prompt them to leave a review.

## 4. Tripadvisor // Best for Travel and Hospitality

Tripadvisor is a customer review platform that gets 78.8 million travelers U.S. visits per month, according to [Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/) data.

Earning positive reviews on a regular basis can help you rank prominently in Tripadvisor’s search results. Which may convert more users into customers.

![Tripadvisor business listings show a picture of the business or location, number of reviews, and customer rating.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d7/25/d725702250335792bc750fe752415c12/e8237051d50d9767f71cd6a07ff43b31/AD_4nXfgnzVWbKLGwXbILV3QNbM4yWr72bRiH3RHllppy3bqE9yZc-xVwyAoT8hb5KMgJosDp6AMuaYQObSlgy7L1K5nORVM5CEbQpLB3KsDvM2Qy4g-s8LFZbatdaearsnIw7Rp_CFIvg.png)

To get started with Tripadvisor, [find out](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Owners) if your business is already listed:

- If so, follow the process to claim the listing
- If not, click “Add a missing place” to add the listing yourself

After setting up your free account, read and respond to reviews via the Management Center.

Once you earn some good reviews, consider installing a [Tripadvisor widget](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Widgets) on your website. To display your average rating, encourage people to leave reviews, and more.

## 5. G2 // Best for SaaS Businesses

G2 is a customer review site for software-as-a-service (SaaS) businesses that features more than [2 million](https://learn.g2.com/2-million-reviews) reviews.

G2 assigns every profile a [G2 score](https://documentation.g2.com/docs/research-scoring-methodologies) “based on reviews gathered from the user community as well as data aggregated from online sources and social networks.”

The higher your G2 score, the higher you can rank in relevant categories and search results.

![Sorted by G2 Score, business listings show their star rating, number of customer reviews, and other credentials.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/10/11/10111f2ca3ce646ed2ca0dc049f14803/fb687bb9892d26f6abd8bee32941e901/AD_4nXfsNVIYz0PQZHiueBWYkFXBD5aw9wwSWywrsjdE5MY8WcVyCcizgXRnZrrYVB1C8vTUxJpvgYw4ikTT9hBZOAM4gImv2xA479AsnC1qAOx6Y0KilkyOmY9L0CDdgi71R2lcMq5R4g.png)

To start getting G2 reviews, do one of two things:

- Claim an existing profile and wait for G2 to verify your claim
- [Request a profile](https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile), wait for G2 to approve your request, then claim your profile

G2 offers a free plan that lets you create a basic profile and collect reviews.

You need a paid G2 subscription to respond to reviews, add custom questions to the review form, or access other advanced features.

## 6. Trustpilot // Best for Ecommerce and Global Brands

Trustpilot is a popular review site that lets customers review almost any online business.

Customers rate companies out of five and provide written feedback. Trustpilot then calculates a star rating (Trust Score) based on numerous factors, including the frequency and recency of reviews.

![Trustpilot business listing shows Trust Score and individual customer reviews.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/89/59/89599d950969de37930af4085cf5c110/25b0eb41a5086e29ff22140da377d0af/AD_4nXc-pqR5J2uUPJIRMdGbJ3lsykVvhg8ZXyxKyimZbgr8ZZjoXhU0C8b_iWUbZ7NddXbbab_mZkWliqiHhbqSCGBoNCOZSkCjtpoZ8Lz3tbOcrmcstAbLiC87qS6zEegspyrhIlS4oA.png)

To get started, [create your Trustpilot account](https://business.trustpilot.com/signup). And claim or add your listing.

The free plan lets you customize your profile, collect and respond to reviews, display a review collection widget on your website, and more.

Choose a [paid plan](https://business.trustpilot.com/pricing) for advanced features like custom website widgets, ad-free profiles, and review tagging.

![A website includes Trustpilot widget that shows the business's overall rating and a few individual reviews.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0b/78/0b78cd877205ddb3f9d30304a5ab13b1/7e52b3913123b0c87d5952a8839b472f/AD_4nXdV0ZjqsmjQJU1rm9yMRYB60Uq6ML_Jr_Mf0w9zIS1fHW2o5wMp2K-YqU3WgpjqWxPxcWcOjJSqiEOyZo9N3SPgCO-kxeWMuO2--o2yNg6rbnqBZh276NOZRyTI7dXiq7WjTdgilQ.png)

## 7. Angi // Best for Home Service Businesses

Angi is a reviews website that helps homeowners find and hire home service professionals like plumbers, roofers, electrical workers, and landscapers.

Optimizing your profile and collecting good reviews can help you get more leads and jobs.

![Angi reviews include a star rating and description of the customer's experience.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d4/36/d436c8545022e12943c07c30305b577b/ec8d96abc8e2e3bebbce5fff6790205d/AD_4nXdTbi4xJoEPJKGMnKYgXxmrEsdkJ4jXfEVtDbBYybOBeO590v8s1ZU0ycnYug47YVF0aDXK2Be6XLAT2x7V6GEoj_-w3pioj7GfWnNCBTtFpSoWbKpyH9mj7dtfTyUzLzOc3zOR.png)

You can [register your business on Angi](https://signup.angi.com/pro) for free. And manage your profile, quotes, and reviews through the Angi website or app.

But you need to pay a “lead fee” every time you accept an opportunity through the platform.

After completing a job, ask the customer to leave a review on your profile. (Whether they came through Angi or not.)

You can use Angi’s “Request a Review” feature to send review requests via email or text message. Use the communication method that you’ve already used with the customer.

## 8. Better Business Bureau // Best for BBB-Accredited Businesses

The Better Business Bureau (BBB) has an online directory that attracts over [140 million](https://www.bbb.org/all/business-resources) users per year.

The organization is a trusted source of consumer advice in North America. So getting featured on this reviews website can be a great way to build trust with consumers.

![A local search for a general contractor shows BBB Accredited businesses and their BBB rating.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ba/70/ba702ede88dfbebe36907f3bf6c1b6f5/a45a30c7e07753752e51f2a6fda31b07/AD_4nXfsY4NdiAu9erNj2Br76y5v3r-BTu8EXsPP5_p8jxbp5NX91aXxg_-eR8eIl3qg3Qdd1VO2M5lPI6gEsP3scy4FTXS_c-wSPy1ivOAhg5Ap7fTCOMaARpOWS5-jPzGR5sYETZr8wg.png)

You can list a non-accredited business on the BBB directory, but BBB-accredited businesses are more likely to succeed on the platform.

If you’re based in North America, you can [apply for BBB accreditation](https://www.bbb.org/get-accredited) online. You’ll need to pay a fee and prove your business meets the required standards.

Once you have a BBB login, you can optimize your business listing and respond to reviews.

## FAQs

### What Are the Benefits of Customer Review Platforms?

The benefits of customer review platforms is that they help you build trust with consumers and potentially convert users into customers.

Good reviews reassure potential customers about your product’s quality and your company’s reliability.

And potential customers generally see reviews hosted on independent platforms as more trustworthy than reviews hosted on your own website.

Review sites also boost your online visibility because they let you reach users on different platforms. And potentially influence your visibility in search engine results.

### How to Respond to Customer Reviews

Respond to reviews quickly and strategically to show you value customer feedback.

Responding to negative reviews can also reduce the risk of reputational damage.

Start by thanking the user for sharing their review. Then, provide a professional response tailored to their feedback. If you’re [responding to a negative review](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-respond-to-negative-reviews/), take responsibility, provide an apology, and offer a solution.

If you receive a fake or inappropriate review, report it to the review platform.

### How to Display Customer Reviews on Your Website

Display customer reviews on your website using business review site widgets.

These widgets can help you convert more visitors into customers. And encourage more people to leave reviews.

Generally, you install the widget by copying an HTML code and saving it in the relevant section of your website.

For example, you might want to display a reviews carousel on your homepage:
