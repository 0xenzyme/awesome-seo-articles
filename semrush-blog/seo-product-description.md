# SEO Product Descriptions: What They Are & How to Write Them

## What Is an SEO Product Description?

An SEO product description is written content that’s designed to inform shoppers about a product, convert shoppers into customers, and increase the product’s visibility in unpaid search results. Ecommerce sites can use SEO product descriptions on product pages to rank higher for relevant keywords in Google.

For example, here’s a product description from Hydro Flask:

![an example of a product description from Hydro Flask](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/25/18/25187218192e6a9650f382e923f4ce17/u1cHEFJVUFqj4wmykBZsHFR-66oJZ5-5nmgJemqbepxw8DV-3Lnc8_w9JGh8M7VH-hB_12gKCRDuUmUGga2dyUKh5U0oa3ry1NNf-Fh4R_nWV5vtA0R-vNngHyn_EX3SQHRKPaDyW2M75_UlpBvU7fg.jpeg)

This helps the page rank for keywords like “64 oz water bottle”:

![an example of "64 oz Wide Mouth" water bottle from Hydro Flask in Google SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/49/d5/49d568021af89e18cc0a53a392feff1d/K2njpb1rA2aP9XLP4kw4bQfQLoJ_AU7kPPFLLZ2Vx2atRAuQ1AtQY_FCd1le6HHWFdEWIga6pITnbo9oxpNKqOcR1wKeZljQQR9f6VcR7CssPI53N4o5XpD4tHNFg4cjCzvh5InjqlsChilRoFv11sU.png)

This keyword alone gets 9,900 searches per month, according to Semrush’s [Keyword Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/) tool. And it has commercial [search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/) (i.e., users are doing research before making a purchase decision).

![search volume and intent metrics highlighted for “64 oz water bottle” in Semrush’s Keyword Overview tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/e4/1ae40494a311e3ef249717e857c3eaf6/0nEB_51U-WV8DwjjfTNeu3CbfGVdJ30bh2mjT7ppqDovir__QifwibSnhpvH2vdd9S_prUHf0gSNBQMIrGm0sOxVE_o74r5RQzwBly68wTwWDXTLtscJiSZYMq-fFYX38mKMMeXEXiiDYv_gONfihUs.jpeg)

So, you can see the value in doing SEO for product pages.

You can also use SEO product descriptions in other locations, such as:

- **Online marketplaces**: Adding optimized descriptions to listings can help you [rank higher on Amazon](https://www.semrush.com/blog/amazon-seo/) and other marketplaces. And get your third-party product pages ranked on Google.
- **Social media platforms**: Some [social media platforms](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-popular-social-media-platforms/), like [Facebook and Instagram](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/learn/lessons/add-products-to-your-shop-catalog), allow you to create product catalogs. SEO descriptions can help your products get discovered through search.
- **Google Merchant Center**: Effective [product feed descriptions](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324468) can improve performance of your [Google Shopping ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-shopping-ads/). And help you get [free product listings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9455788) on Google surfaces.

## How to Write SEO Product Descriptions

Learn how to write a product description that generates [organic traffic](https://www.semrush.com/blog/organic-traffic/) and conversions.

Here are seven best practices:

### 1. Optimize for Target Keywords

When doing SEO for products, [target keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/) are search queries you want to rank for.

Using target keywords in product descriptions can help you rank higher in search results. Because it helps prove your product is relevant to the user.

To find what people search on Google, perform keyword research with Semrush [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).

Enter a word or phrase related to your product.

Then, choose your country and click “**Search**.”

![search for "water bottle" in Keyword Magic Tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fa/f1/faf151ee973ee49436850ef6475c93c1/o22WCCiwyrGoOgVjN4yTRVBwwC3kbmp0KOqhWTFeOYTQg377THFXGs_kxRZwSXiynAnqn8HvwBfe57zRONLciuNypx6_VsOH52WiLPQqudOrStomMiYjUl-L5zvk6dJimULPgnqU5e8n1axQv4aarZE.png)

You’ll see keywords that contain your starting term(s) or a close variation.

To focus on keywords that people use while shopping, filter for transactional search intent.

Like this:

![filter for transactional search intent in Keyword Magic Tool results for "water bottle" keyword](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a9/3f/a93f6939288579a8849e28f1a432180c/i6EeuhvcsRC5OzxUGU1b1pRC3bgxAZyW0uUfI4tozzFwMtAo1D3BQFv68EdgdKLnlPez9TvoNlcVgg-fSNe4MBHmZH03VcLyREIqn80Evg_x0BzAZbZ1EOHEFLRoj5Q24zRLtVudboSurFyKLbroqc0.png)

If you’re optimizing your product feed, filter for keywords that trigger product listing ads. And other Google Shopping-related [SERP features](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/).

Like this:

![an example of filtering for for keywords that trigger product listing ads in Keyword Magic tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8f/60/8f60c88ded411d891d3fc827487127be/zCahE2kdFLVTXeneDhtOt7PKEikA_-XbVrTXXQvXlIyn4aGk7WSEJKVL450X-N3HOVu84biexX5_qJDmHoVSc1KFqMApbtaup_VKhCa0MsFfBRirzVgbFBqnUdKw2JeHZCz5eEVQTRtvPsb2UJ1qgoY.png)

Once you’ve finished applying filters, [choose target keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-keywords-for-seo/). Making sure your product matches what the searcher is looking for.

Generally, more specific keywords are better for SEO product descriptions. Even though their [search volumes](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-search-volume/) (average number of monthly searches) are likely to be lower.

Why?

[Long-tail keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-long-tail-keywords/) tend to have lower [keyword difficulty](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-difficulty/) scores (KD %). Meaning it’s easier to rank on [Google’s first page](https://www.semrush.com/blog/get-on-first-page-of-google/).

![volume and keyword difficulty columns highlighted in the results for "water bottle" related keyword](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e8/63/e86386d1a3e1c0f7a69eedcaf8530a44/YbL09QOhHtQvXgYGYIprzU8bEBi0K_DmE8roxIabEQCTiMi358-v1OeVCSbz_QVlbZRC8vxYpDFIdlOT3Bh4oFpmI9qGexAhZrWlFhFfM9nNq0ff_V3FRRUWjGkuMzQ47HU9QSwjvak8EWY4Y37xp74.jpeg)

Plus, specific keywords attract highly targeted traffic. Meaning your sales conversion rate should be higher.

Think of it this way:

Who’s more likely to buy a 64oz stainless steel water bottle: Someone who searches for that exact thing, or someone who searches “water bottles”?

Whatever keywords you choose, incorporate them naturally.

**Don’t** write something like this:

*"Stay hydrated with our 64oz Stainless Steel Water Bottle, your ultimate hydration partner! This 64oz stainless steel water bottle, made from quality stainless steel, is the water bottle for all your hydration needs. Forget about other water bottles, this stainless steel bottle with a 64oz capacity keeps your water cool all day. If you're in search of a water bottle, then our 64oz Stainless Steel Water Bottle, featuring stainless steel construction, is the best water bottle choice for you."*

Forcing keywords into your SEO product descriptions is a practice known as keyword stuffing. It’s against Google’s [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). And it can put potential customers off, too.

***Tip**: Find keywords for your Amazon product descriptions with the [Keyword Wizard for Amazon](https://www.semrush.com/apps/keyword-wizard-for-amazon/).*

### 2. Adopt the Right Tone of Voice

Choosing the right tone of voice—and using it consistently—helps you build a [brand identity](https://www.semrush.com/blog/build-brand-identity/). And engage your [target audience](https://www.semrush.com/blog/target-audience/).

So, it’s important that it shines through in your SEO optimized product descriptions.

For example, [Firebox](https://firebox.com/freshly-baked-bread-slippers) uses humor to emphasize the quirkiness of its products:

### 3. Prove Your Product’s Value

To persuade people to buy your product, you need to show the value that’s in it **for them**.

So, use these tips when writing product descriptions:

#### Translate Features into Benefits

It’s important to explain the benefit behind each product feature.

For example, people don’t really care that this Dyson vacuum cleaner has nylon bristles. But they do care that those bristles “remove stubborn dirt and pet hair from carpets and rugs.”

![an example of Dyson vacuum cleaner product description: "Stiff, dense nylon bristles agitate and remove stubborn dirt and pet hair from carpets and rugs.”](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c1/82/c182653c9bb23ddce99918cb15f32548/t2yXhaV2bELdGEH0korOVljKmzMTiliNPD9r23LXAHxZfS7hah5PmPzo5l2-cWH1TcT2kySwrTOyYd9u2e061JWG7EJBAsrHmORPDoSyHbNwsMAOMSvtiFe3pKOtHwF1JmYHZbBpI8xNk7PUwqVbqaU.jpeg)

#### Use Lifestyle Copywriting

Lifestyle copywriting is about selling the experiences a product can offer.

It paints a picture of a life the target audience desires. To tap into their aspirations and emotions. And, ultimately, prompt purchases.

Let’s say you’re writing an SEO product description for hiking boots.

As well as talking about features and benefits, you could talk about adventures in the wilderness. Fun days out with the family. Or whatever appeals to your target audience.

If you haven’t already, create [buyer personas](https://www.semrush.com/blog/buyer-persona/) (profiles that represent each type of target customer).

Throughout this process, you’ll define your target customers’ goals, values, and pain points. And document them for easy reference.

Here’s an example created with the [Semrush Persona tool](https://www.semrush.com/persona/):

![an example of a buyer persona created with the Semrush Persona tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1f/3a/1f3ac0458103b541b3b37b3813072770/ch2Y3qL7UvRb-mJFuqEKJde1P_prA_Hvj7SknHOCW4_DHVpjhSQr8vCNyo-ayb2pCZKMCER50_iwko15z4v4FMl_b_k7BCZaO6-ASG6HNrVCdT181zw7zBRPkL4knPjf0C-gel7ifPYjAtNPLv0UKwM.png)

#### Support Your Claims

When making claims about your product, you need to back them up. In order to build trust with your potential customers.

Many marketers fall into the trap of making generic and unsubstantiated claims—for example, saying that a product is “easy to use.”

Instead, explain how to use the product. And let readers come to that conclusion themselves.

Facts and figures can also inspire confidence in your products.

For example, here’s part of Amazon’s product description for the Kindle Paperwhite:

*"Kindle Paperwhite is IPX8 rated to protect against accidental immersion in up to two meters of fresh water for up to 60 minutes, and up to 0.25 meters of seawater for up to 3 minutes."*

### 4. Improve Readability

Make your SEO product descriptions easy to read. So that shoppers can find and absorb information more easily.

Here are a few simple tips you can use:

- Use proper spellings and grammar (tools like [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) can help)
- Replace complex words with short, familiar words
- Remove unnecessary words to keep your writing concise
- Keep sentences short (under 20 words)
- Avoid the [passive voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/passive-voice-vs-seo/)
- Break up text using line spaces, headers, bullet points, etc.

### 7. Include Useful Information

Give shoppers all the information they might need to make a purchasing decision.

Product descriptions should include features, benefits, and technical specifications. But the best descriptions can also include usage and maintenance instructions, packaging and environmental information, FAQs, comparison tables, testimonials, and more.

Here are some other elements to include on your [product pages](https://www.semrush.com/blog/product-page/):

- [Call to action](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-call-to-action/) buttons (e.g., “Buy Now”)
- Product images and videos
- Pricing and special offers
- Delivery and returns information
- Stock availability
- Links to related products or categories
- Customer reviews
- Awards and accolades
- Social sharing buttons
- Live chat

Amazon, Apple, and Walmart are the biggest retail sites in search, according to the [Semrush Rank](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/ranks/rank/) for February 2024. So, try looking at their sites for product SEO examples.
