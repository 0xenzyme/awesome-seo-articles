# What is AMP?

![](/static/search/blog/images/import/53c6547ae28b6c8195e80d687f6acfbf.png)

Users today expect mobile websites to load super fast. The reality is that it can often take
several seconds. It is no surprise that
40% of people abandon a website that takes more than 3 seconds to load.
To reduce the time content takes to get to a user's mobile device
[we started working](https://blog.google/products/search/introducing-accelerated-mobile-pages) on the
[Accelerated Mobile Pages Project](https://amp.dev/), an open
source initiative to improve the mobile web experience for everyone.

Accelerated Mobile Pages are HTML pages that take advantage of
[various technical approaches](https://medium.com/@cramforce/why-amp-is-fast-7d2ff1f48597#.9v8f7c3i0 class=) to prioritize
speed and a faster experience for users by loading content almost instantaneously.

Later this year, all types of sites that create AMP pages will have
[expanded exposure](/search/blog/2016/08/amp-your-content-preview-of-amped) across the entire Google
Mobile Search results page, like e-commerce, entertainment, travel, recipe sites and many more.
Visit the ["Who" page](https://amp.dev/about/use-cases) on AMPProject.org for a
flavour of some of the sites already creating AMP content and try the demo at
([g.co/ampdemo](https://www.google.com/webhp?esrch=AcceleratedMobilePages::Preview)) to see AMP versions of pages
labeled with
![The AMP Logo](/static/search/blog/images/import/ebc0e62333601481d10ab8f1c9c83197.png).
