# 9 Best Google Business Profile Management Tools of 2025

Managing your Google Business Profile is key for improving local search visibility, and some tools can help you do it more efficiently.

Whether you’re a local business or an agency with [local SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/) clients, GBP management is an ongoing task. It involves updating listings, posting new content, and responding to reviews and messages.

When done manually, it can be time-consuming. Thankfully, Google Business Profile management tools are designed to assist you, and we’ve done the legwork to find and test out the best ones.

## What Is Google Business Profile? (Formerly Google My Business)

[Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) is a free tool that allows businesses to create custom listings that may get prominent placement in Google search results.

Like this one:

![A Google Business Profile of a cafe along with its address, working hours, price, menu, photos, reviews, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a0/a9/a0a9012e6270d9f2b6fe42ac04377a16/961ae0f07bebd2afb46e682970d120c0/image.png)

It can help your business:

- Reach new audiences looking for businesses like yours
- Rank for more [local keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-keyword-research/) in Google search results
- Generate phone calls and inquiries to your business
- Get more clicks to your website

Plus, updating your profile with accurate and [fresh content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/fresh-content/) can help you gain visibility in the [Google Local Pack](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-local-pack/). Which Google shows to users looking for nearby businesses.

![Google Local pack showing a list view of local results on the left and a map view on the right.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f1/fa/f1fa14e1b79851fb212b841398a5f0c3/f9641d07344ce01887e2a78b0d579156/image.png)

The tool was formerly called “Google My Business” (GMB) but was renamed “Google Business Profile” (GBP) in 2021.

## Why Should You Use a Google Business Profile Tool?

A dedicated Google Business Profile tool can increase the visibility of your business in Google search results and ease the workload of managing your profiles.

According to [Birdeye](https://birdeye.com/blog/state-of-google-business-profiles/), verified Google Business Profile listings average 200 clicks per month. And local businesses average 105 website visits.

Use GBP tools to:

- Help your listing rank for [keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/) relevant to your business
- Provide more information to users who are considering your services
- Gain higher positions in the Google Local Pack (and possibly more clicks, calls, or foot traffic)
- Track, respond, and [generate more reviews](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/how-to-generate-reviews/) on your listing

But which tool is best for you may depend on how many listings you manage. Or what you need the most help with.

**My Favorite Part:**

Like this:

## How to Choose a Google Business Profile Management Tool

The best [local SEO tools](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-seo-tools/) for you will always depend on your unique needs and pain points.

But here’s a process you can follow when evaluating and choosing tools:

1. Confirm how many listings you have (**Note**: Some tools won’t support hundreds of listings)
2. Determine the features that are most helpful to your marketing workflows
3. Compare subscription prices and check for feature quotas or limitations (which may add costs)
4. Test out your top tools and determine your favorites before committing
