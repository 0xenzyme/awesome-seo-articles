# Bringing the conference to you

We're fortunate to meet many of you at
[conferences](/s/results/search/blog?q=conferences),
where we can chat about web search and
[Webmaster Tools](https://search.google.com/search-console).
We receive a lot of good feedback at these events: insight into the questions you're asking and
issues you're facing. However, as several of our
[Webmaster Help Group](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community)
friends have pointed out, not everyone can afford the time or expense of a conference; and many
of you live in regions where webmaster-related conferences are rare.

So, we're bringing the conference to you.

We've posted notes in our Help Group from conferences we recently attended:

- [John Müller covers SMX Stockholm](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/t/75cf049405ad687b/ebdcb36a77c7fc0e)
- [I recap the AMA's Search Engine Marketing conference](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/t/b505e25e90edb7f3)

[Jonathan](https://groups.google.com/groups/profile?enc_user=7R709zkAAAC0ZCEBAysSlShC_gPAdXUZ6pC83CPQcZTBRk4tCi5-z22zvE-Pg6zMb-JWoHM21aHjxVrrD5b9FzF5fEAvsymn)
and
[Wysz](https://groups.google.com/groups/profile?enc_user=SXrHxjUAAAC0ZCEBAysSlShC_gPAdXUZ_xHoXFa-OZBAxALbs4dIlIG_Y8-NyZA-1XhIH6nPfUzIgJw9QHTrXt9e__Js8L8H)
will post their notes from
[PubCon](https://www.pubcon.com/vegas-pubcon-2007.htm), while
[Bergy](https://groups.google.com/groups/profile?enc_user=jQzWBjQAAAC0ZCEBAysSlShC_gPAdXUZW3PX6BJxO179vbYKVN4vMve_sSnwDnMTSwYYsrTi1zVTofQ0MIAkuDMa7lXaxMYO)
and I will cover
[SES Chicago](https://www.searchenginestrategies.com/chicago/index).

If you can make it to one of these, we'd love to meet you face to face, but if you can't, we hope
you find our jottings useful.
