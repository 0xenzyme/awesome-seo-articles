# How to Monetize a Blog (& Grow Your Revenue)

## 7 Ways to Monetize a Blog

The best way to monetize your blog depends on a variety of factors. Such as your subject matter, [target audience](https://www.semrush.com/blog/target-audience/), and financial goals.

It’s smart to experiment with different methods. And create multiple income streams so you’re not reliant on just one.

Here are seven popular ways to monetize a blog:

### 1. Publish Sponsored Posts

Sponsored posts are promotional articles that another brand pays you to publish.

Here’s an example of a sponsored post. Wherein Walmart paid lifestyle blog For The Home to publish an article:

![a blog post says "this post is sponsored by walmart" near the top of the page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ff/8f/ff8faf859c132f6cdeedbe8baa23ea58/55b68823030d461967d9438c64b21ff1/AD_4nXeQFGP04YervIDPiYEf_bw2xOkEWVlSxAa-GU7R8FgHBSl_ooMyl8B_VTt-EnjUNnY6eca2jVWulptHsrjzELk3FShNe_EAsSFodvvFALf1jbyzF0A3E-FAFQ4SHHStdUmkD5Q9f40be_AzRq68ocY-kuQ.png)

You or the other brand can create sponsored content.

Either way, you must:

- Ensure the post offers genuine value to your readers
- Clearly label the content as sponsored

Otherwise, you could harm your reputation, break advertising laws, and violate [Google’s spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). (The latter could lead to a [Google penalty](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-penalty/) that harms your search visibility.)

One of the best ways to get started is to contact brands you love. And propose content ideas that will benefit you both.

Inform the brands about key metrics such as:

- **Traffic**: How many visits you get per month, on average. This gives the brand an idea of their potential reach through your blog. You can monitor your traffic through [Google Analytics 4](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-analytics/) or another web analytics tool.
- **Audience demographics**: Your visitors’ age, gender, location, etc. To show brands who exactly they’ll be reaching by partnering with you. You can learn about your visitors through Google Analytics 4, [One2Target](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/one2target/), or another web analytics tool.
- **Search demand**: How many people search for content around your proposed topic. You can discover popular and trending topics with Semrush’s [Topic Research](https://www.semrush.com/topic-research/) tool.

And any other data that will make the sponsorship more appealing.

You can get an idea of potential earnings by looking at competitors’ pricing.

### 2. Join an Ad Network

Ad networks aggregate ads. And then serve those ads on relevant websites that are a part of the network.

Joining an ad network makes it easy to enable advertisements on your blog. And make money from them. Like this:

![sidebar ad on a recipe blog is for rare beauty makeup products](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1e/da/1edad38d426c5e593cbb54bebce59b82/1aa48dc7e5d7d338214bff471911c00a/AD_4nXc-b93yhTOZOLmtj1RRJj64cUs4cFS977x8ZACZhp5pzCkMJSoIEkqzSdPMuxzfktPYvbL2sYR5E5HhWgX2k8YsfFxL86mLToulhhHUliQPIULx661qYKSPZMumPW9wHXS2nM6ngRRQVkzLGUsN4CtinPE.png)

Generally, this is how it works:

1. You sign up to an ad network and provide information about your blog
2. You design or choose ad units (spaces for certain kinds of ads) and add the code for them to your site
3. An automated auction determines which ads appear and how much advertisers pay
4. You earn a cut of the revenue collected from advertisers

One of the most popular ad networks is [Google AdSense](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-google-adsense/). And it has a [revenue calculator](https://adsense.google.com/start/#calculator) that lets you estimate your earnings.

According to the tool, an American finance blog getting 50,000 monthly page views could earn around $2,000 per year:

![Google AdSense tool shows how much you can make monetizing your blog](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/52/40/5240c19d823eb7fe2c72d894f72ce821/49671d9371a1e68eb231e6937a5a196c/AD_4nXd_vwOTFNlLQBl3CC7UrnGhwDCXmbaDXh4GDAp1FLKqmDOvNKUM1nT4USvETVVFXvgTocCLMLQwT5T_Ib3OrYdcHEtk9Nj7lD-pH7TAx5tkFEbsnU_rAWLD4mhOrwxpDXIqcXFp-F_NCSWhVUFBwZZyJfp2.png)

Other popular ad networks include Media.net, Ezoic, and Raptive.

The right platform for you depends on various factors, including:

- **Niche and audience fit**: Some networks cater better to specific niches or audience demographics
- **Ad format variety**: Choose a network that offers formats suitable for your content and layout
- **Revenue potential**: Evaluate potential earnings based on the network’s payment models and rates
- **Minimum payout threshold**: Ensure the payout threshold is achievable for your earnings level
- **Integration ease**: Consider how easy it is to integrate the ad network with your site

### 3. Become an Affiliate

[Becoming an affiliate](https://www.semrush.com/blog/affiliate-marketing/) means promoting a brand, service, or product in exchange for a commission on resulting conversions.

Generally, this is how it works:

1. You sign up for an affiliate program (offered by a specific brand) or an affiliate network (which connects you with multiple brands) and provide details about your blog
2. If you’re approved, the brand provides you with affiliate links that allow them to track your referrals
3. You share affiliate links with your readers (making sure to disclose them)
4. You receive payment based on the sales or other types of conversions generated

For example, Epic Gardening is a member of [Amazon Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/) (Amazon’s affiliate program).

This means they can monetize blog posts by adding affiliate links. They earn up to 10% commission when readers click links like these and make a purchase:

In addition to adding affiliate links to their posts (where natural), some affiliate marketers create online stores populated with affiliate products.

Here’s an example from the Pick Up Limes blog:

The amount you can earn from affiliate marketing depends on various factors, including:

- **Rates/fees**: Brands offer varying percentages or flat fees for each conversion. The amount may differ depending on the product category, your overall performance, customer location, etc.
- **Cookie duration**: The length of time that your affiliate tracking cookie is stored in the user’s browser. A 30-day cookie duration means you get credit for sales made within 30 days of the click.
- **Attribution model**: When multiple affiliates are involved in a sale, the attribution model determines who gets paid. Last-click attribution means the affiliate whose link was most recently clicked gets full credit.

It’s important to understand the full payout terms before entering a partnership.

### 4. Launch a Membership Program

Consider launching a blog membership program that offers perks in exchange for a regular subscription fee.

For example, Stratechery Plus members pay $15/month or $150/year for regular exclusive content:

![blog membership landing page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fe/5c/fe5ccde92053d12d6e0e22b8ba44f95d/26a3c2c5ca858201cd7e261870902ee3/AD_4nXd6vEabmCkEGa6yd1R5yEUZwh20qS2AgT3_GdjYn37HdDZOOZxjrtrfG4Su8VSe2zDRTBg-TA1deKA6IfumKElfUUyKWWUF_iXSKlmevF-XQK1mlmCgsY3e-T6R4upDU5cHw7gaPM729fN0fInhRKj18Z_p.png)

It’s also common to give subscribers access to ad-free content, virtual events, and private online communities. But you should design plans to suit you and your readers.

There are a few ways to enable memberships on your blog:

- Install a plugin like [MemberPress](https://memberpress.com/) or [Restrict Content Pro](https://restrictcontentpro.com/)
- Use a subscription-based publishing platform like [Substack](https://substack.com/) or [Ghost](https://ghost.org/)
- Offer perks through a third-party platform like [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/en-GB) or [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/)
- Build a custom solution with the help of a web developer

Memberships typically work best if you’re an established blogger with a loyal readership. Because people need to trust you and your content before they part with their money.

## How to Maximize Blog Revenue

Whatever blog monetization methods you use, these tips can help you earn more revenue.

### Choose a Profitable Blog Niche

If you haven’t launched your blog yet, choose your niche (main subject matter) with profitability in mind.

Some [blog niches](https://www.semrush.com/blog/blog-niche-ideas/) are easier to monetize than others because they:

- **Align with commercial interests**. For example, it’s easier to sell products or ads on blogs about fashion than on blogs about mathematics.
- **Attract less competition**. If your blog fills a gap in the market, you may find it easier to attract loyal readers. But low-competition niches can have smaller audiences.
- **Have wide appeal**: Blogs with wide appeal (e.g., global blogs related to popular topics) can attract more visitors. However, it can be harder to stand out in these niches.

The right blog niche lies at the intersection between your interests, your expertise, and potential profitability.

![a successful niche is at the intersection of interest, profitability, and expertise](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/73/8d/738daccfbcba00ef2e34f6f4604d6e3f/5ab28f069591aa0775d729603ce5d4d7/AD_4nXf3OR1ojoO8wMIF_LWeOd2s4wIhH6SKQvfXgXH__cooc4bG1d-TkbffulPokNBl0_Vlqsq-wwIf7OiSyuMA5GCCKONGE5oDkgc55bE5s7GAJUTp5F02_uoKtwPzDrjyM2xUk4H6XUt3_DgiT-WgP3qxpOUE.png)

### Focus on Content Quality

Publishing [quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/) is how you create a blog that people want to read—and brands want to sponsor.

Quality content demonstrates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness ([E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/)):

- **Experience**: Write about things you have first-hand experience with. And demonstrate this experience through your content. That way, readers are more likely to trust your opinions.
- **Expertise**: Focus on topics within your knowledge and skill sets. And make readers aware of your credentials. This is particularly important for [YMYL](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ymyl/) content that can affect readers’ wellbeing.
- **Authoritativeness**: This largely refers to your reputation in your niche. It should build naturally over time, but you can use [digital PR](https://www.semrush.com/blog/guide-to-digital-pr/) and [link building](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/) tactics to boost your authority.
- **Trustworthiness**: Make sure your blog content is accurate, honest, and reliable. Perform in-depth research and give readers a reason to trust what you say.

You should also tailor your blog to the specific needs, interests, and preferences of a target audience.

This helps maximize engagement. And makes your blog more attractive to advertisers and collaborators looking to reach the same kinds of people.

If you need help identifying and defining your audience, use Semrush’s [One2Target](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/one2target/) tool. It tells you about the users who visit the site(s) of your choice.
