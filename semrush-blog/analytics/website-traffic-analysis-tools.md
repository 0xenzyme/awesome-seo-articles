# 10 Best Website Traffic Analysis Tools for 2026 (Features & Pricing)

## What Is Website Traffic Analysis?

A website traffic analysis is a way of measuring and evaluating the number of and behavior of visitors to your site.

This can involve tracking metrics like views, bounce rates, traffic sources, and more.

And website traffic analysis tools are how you gain that information. To help you gain insights you can use to optimize your site's performance and improve the user experience (UX).

## Why Is Website Traffic Analysis Important?

Performing a website traffic analysis lets you:

- Identify which [marketing campaigns](https://www.semrush.com/blog/marketing-campaign/) are working by seeing which channels drive the most traffic
- Identify low-engagement pages that need to be improved
- Find high-traffic pages you can further improve by [optimizing the content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-optimization-guide/)
- Understand your different audience demographics to tailor content and marketing strategies effectively
- Detect [technical issues](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/) that might be affecting your site's performance

## 10 Top Website Traffic Analysis Tools

Now that you know more about the benefits of this type of analysis, let’s get to the best tools for the job.

## 1. Traffic Analytics

[Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/) provides estimates about any website’s traffic. And makes it easy to do a competitive analysis.

It provides a look at:

- How many visitors a site is getting
- Where they’re coming from
- How many of them are new vs. returning
- What pages they’re landing on

It breaks down data by geographic location, channels, and device.

Open the tool, enter your domain, and select the location. Click "**Analyze**."

## 4. Google Search Console

[Log in to GSC](https://search.google.com/search-console) (or create an account) to gain access to reports that help you understand and improve your site's presence in Google search results.

It reports on the total traffic (expressed as clicks) you get from search results.

![Google Search Console displaying an overview of a website’s performance with a line graph of web search clicks.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/83/40/83403b2daa5099147397136c8a9c961e/308c60244614680667b216a7f21910e4/image.png)

And GSC enables you to identify technical issues that are preventing your pages from being indexed. So you can optimize your site for better visibility.

You can also use it to inspect specific URLs, verify that your site is mobile-friendly, and submit your sitemap to Google.

### What We Like

GSC’s URL inspection feature allows you to ask Google to inspect your page again if you've updated it. This helps changes appear faster in search results, so the page can start driving traffic that much quicker.

And it’s simple to use. Making it easier for you to keep tabs on how your website is performing in search.

### Pricing

Google Search Console is free to use.

## 5. Bing Webmasters Tools

Bing Webmaster Tools is a free tool that shows you how much traffic your site gets from Bing.

It reports on clicks, impressions, average CTR, and average position — similar to Google Search Console, but for Bing.

![Search performance dashboard in Bing Webmaster Tools](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d0/12/d01247450ab52e0912aa9998ced13622/0c5d687f0e8db243b094d9f301d09c27/image.png)

You can also use it to:

- Submit your sitemap to help Bing crawl and index your pages
- Inspect individual URLs to check their indexing status
- Identify and fix technical SEO issues affecting your visibility
- Run keyword research to find terms people search for on Bing

### What We Like

Bing's market share is small compared to Google's, but it's not negligible.  And Bing Webmaster Tools gives you a straightforward view into your site's traffic coming from Bing. It's easy to set up and report on key metrics like clicks, impressions, and average position — in one place.

### Pricing

Bing Webmasters Tool is completely free
