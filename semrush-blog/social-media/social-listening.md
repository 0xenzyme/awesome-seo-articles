# Social Listening: A Complete Guide for Marketers

## What Is Social Listening?

Social listening is the practice of monitoring and analyzing social media conversations about your business to learn more about your audience, so you can make changes to your strategy to improve your results.

It helps you understand audience sentiment, gives you insight into industry trends and market changes, and reveals what your audience thinks about your competitors.

For example, X (formerly Twitter) posts like this provide valuable insight into how people feel about Semrush's AI tools.

![Tweet by Aleyda Solis commenting on AI features and Semrush's Copilot AI Assistant](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b8/9b/b89b4d1f471889f4b8ab5234c163896e/f81a22b1f6c2f56f22c9ebe86b7d87c2/AD_4nXcLFElTycDYMBsAna9YDik9QraTwg7Z6Y8y53e5_JEX4BuAgetMAFj6dIdz7sJcJ8PlcE8ws87Jg1P1Of2vfKKu6QAMj7nWMlqQYtkb-d-3UNdr5n-dnK6P6m9-cXuIuHUNTCkOJkxQkhb2lI9qkGkcR4s8.png)

These insights can inform your advertising campaigns, how you develop your products or services, and more.

### Social Listening vs. Social Monitoring—What’s the Difference?

Social listening is sometimes used interchangeably with the term [social media monitoring](https://www.semrush.com/blog/social-media-monitoring-tools/). But they’re not the same.

Social monitoring is more about actively responding to customer messages, concerns, and complaints. Which can improve your online reputation and user satisfaction.

For example, Apple would use social media monitoring to respond to customer feedback like this:

![Tweet to Apple customer service asking for support to unfreeze an iPhone 13](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3f/f2/3ff23b961d129270600a4cc939cfdc06/bc5771c7051ac02164a1d218f5c9b620/AD_4nXcNvvgTo_8S1358Y-eQVb1LcUuKR6Yt_q2Ilm6zvSgJUigTqn08N4Z_9a3b3H4N_ddOxd5eAUeTybQRbXHV-PpiM8V1GLIercL17IJ-MqczDlEyEePQXm4ykj8Y0FOd26Aw47o0KQ9rt6s8JUMgg5SRXpJ2.png)

Social listening takes it a step further. By involving analysis that helps you refine your marketing and business strategies.

And social listening tools like [Brand Monitoring](https://www.semrush.com/apps/brand-monitoring/) make it easier by providing a breakdown of the number mentions your brand receives, your estimated reach, an overview of audiences’ sentiment, etc.

![Brand Monitoring's Analysis report showing mentions, reach, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c3/98/c3988fd6f756eee32e9c9cb63ed05cf8/b612cdb3cc0c99faeb402741b5151611/bz%20AD_4nXdU3WuwwZUym-vpesxt87iwlg3mSZbP0LH1GsR1b7hjupWojTpzO42jtqBV1Q0qqiY3-6ebBdszpVBFgKH7BKHr5Afr7kAy2Oh3d3-3dVsH86x-XM2wv11sJmStoitvpThISmRBmG6jaK7_uG2Q6NiySdja.png)

Let’s say you have a skincare brand and discover your brand has more negative mentions than positive ones. And common customer feedback themes like mixed feelings about the product’s scent and a desire to see more details about ingredients.

Using these insights, you can make targeted product and marketing improvements.

## The Benefits of Social Media Listening

### Gain Audience Insights

Social listening lets you understand social media audiences on a deeper level. Helping you intervene if needed and refine your social media campaigns over time.

For example, use social listening to learn:

- Topics that interest your target market
- Common pain points
- What people do and don’t like about your brand
- Who your brand advocates are

Take outdoor clothing brand Patagonia, for example.

The brand knows its customers are interested in sustainability. So, it uses messaging that will resonate with them on social media.

![Patagonia Instagram reel with natural cooton field video detailing their mission to source organic cotton](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d6/e3/d6e34ece12c96340a6ddcb77274741b0/8fd4274eed05c4e06288d9d29bc408f2/AD_4nXfzUnBXAoK9oFPMewitR6rRFsr2PBzXXklASrf_0mZFfsZ3DfKyHkZyGnxPMa8O484cNHMJCk1J769KfR3pHIydkmQK0hwRGGLR1d7NW9q2-jiEsAFX_ZZ9GOYbl2aCwGSImx6b7jbw7yPW4Yk4DMws4bo.jpeg)

This information can also help you create highly targeted [social media advertising](https://www.semrush.com/blog/social-media-ads/) campaigns. To reach audiences most interested in what you sell.

### Uncover Trends

Closely listening to social media conversations reveals trends that can shape your marketing and business strategies.

Many social listening tools let you set up hashtag, keyword, or phrase alerts to monitor relevant discussions.

For example, a fashion brand could set up alerts for “fashion trends” or “#TheMetGala” to stay on top of trending content.

### Find Influencers

Social media listening can show you which influencers engage with your posts, talk about your brand, and are popular in your industry. Which can lead to mutually beneficial [influencer marketing campaigns](https://www.semrush.com/blog/influencer-marketing-guide/).

The benefit of an influencer campaign is that it can expand your social media reach. And expose your brand to more people.

## How to Get Started with Social Listening

### 1. Create Clear Goals

Defining what you want to achieve with social listening ensures your efforts align with your overall marketing and business goals.

Set goals using the specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) framework. This will help you stay focused and measure what matters most.

![SMART goalsetting poster with acronym in rainbow colors](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c9/1a/c91a9d1427aa1446aa21b913867174ee/52c75f05048b202d3fc0b0fc60bc6686/AD_4nXdU8NV5W4bV_YiX-sCGuxko-SvmhznqiZZnAXth_fweyvv-ukWYnb64XkpV-ri6rMcP7dCqgKSJKikX1AAiNylu91xNcBQb4mMxnjacCsxxflx946RDuFfNJCHxGyMKMN_NRcV4M6Fq64kIO8YINFQQW44T.png)

Let’s say your business is a vegan restaurant based in Portland, Oregon and you want to break into a new market in Seattle, Washington. A SMART goal that supports this company goal could be:

**“Increase positive brand mentions in the Seattle area by 15% over the next three months.”**

### 2. Identify the Right Social Media Channels

It’s a good idea to home in on the social media channels that your audience uses most. So you can tailor your social listening efforts accordingly.

Consider that people communicate differently across platforms.

For example, X users often include multiple hashtags and tend to tag brands in posts.

Like this:

![Tweet showing two pairs of Adidas Sambas, one child's, on adult's, with sambas hashtag](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e8/b3/e8b3f37a30afd1ba0260b1ad068a4ba9/e7e7926e50d88a659523b7a2d316a764/AD_4nXelzsdZCrzpq3tlhvUDODR91IDnKDXuzVIwOLrBPpq0PAIblrWUnrhcyNYAzL2bEUgEbMxmhsrzuBT4eid6YWdrDPCtIv_5-rtjDcXLJbvPLMgAiUswPy4EQN61D2mTzUwwfF77Uhp-a5ga5BmTsyvQXvkO.jpeg)

So if you determine that X is a place where your target audience hangs out, that means you should make sure to prioritize monitoring hashtags and tags. But if your audience is more active on LinkedIn, you might be better off focusing on specific keywords.

How do you know which platforms to focus on?

Go back to [Market Explorer](https://www.semrush.com/market-explorer/) and head to the “**Audience**” tab.

Then, scroll down until you see the “Social Media” section.

!["Social Media" module in Market Explorer's Audience report](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/dc/40/dc408777870a57fa57869874f54d8cf6/1b1798f6d85d72794090424dccb11a9d/AD_4nXet7K9XonH3QAnTXgDz_sVhHX7qA3cNlr8goI8uQyiQQDt5ChfFJF9oEjSHv2c-XOxN1SKunoA6sjX1yA3o18xSxwWlQLU3CUXRYYVxmeVt0mjmsDVHA7MOwTE1bKW3Q_eC9lf3djxm01V59R6ww_xDpNLI.png)

Here, you can see the platforms your audience uses the most (and least) frequently.

### 3. Choose Relevant Keywords and Topics

Next, select keywords, hashtags, and topics that align with your business and priority platforms. So you can track impactful conversations and ignore those that aren’t relevant.

Here are some ideas of what to track:

- Company name or handle
- Product or service names
- Industry or market terminology
- Competitor brand names and handles
- Competitor product or service names
- Brand hashtags (e.g., #Nike or #JustDoIt)
- Key employees (e.g., the CEO, CFO, or other representative)
- Industry hashtags

### 4. Monitor Conversations

You’re now ready to start tracking relevant conversations. And using a social listening tool can save time.

For example, the [Brand Monitoring](https://www.semrush.com/apps/brand-monitoring/) app allows you to keep an eye on your and your competitors’ mentions, evaluate your reach, and analyze audience sentiment.

The tool categorizes mentions as positive, negative, or neutral. Helping you understand whether you need to make adjustments to improve perception.

![Brand Monitoring's Mentions report showing a list of the most recent mentions](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c8/1a/c81ae4104b831d6e212a0ca7665c4f71/9c194e264099d67aac8af01616db5fa2/AD_4nXe_6-B-rLU-8X0MoilxlDC82HloSrNWyuIOpdF1w5mJusE14eh0zla_sr6sxwkPSI9ULZzeBDNVkGuQHjel_fu65bGqrjc0Y_DNSITB8dDbCEY6qSecvlnhXW1WLCk9UexsVaiB125JeMlZHvL5DgV1sOI.png)

You can also monitor keywords to tap into relevant conversations in your industry.

And be sure to set up regular email reports to ensure you never miss important notifications related to your brand. Which can help you mitigate any urgent feedback or customer concerns.

![Email notification options in Brand Monitoring](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8b/94/8b949428cd423eb6d54b981c41da4894/514b8aacfe439779a7dc6db6030761a4/AD_4nXeeqGmYHQtsIYdGOrq197TP1AeqUAfQFg4BC6_t-GZWCmgNraVpws5JH-7fSJ4qZK0igJx8dpYbgPso4yf8mtNBHLvpITWe7rAxMLvKrDd8uwK0ZsZU7sC-nYhkw3RSryG4fQuhnxlJ3zcgu5NeXlWg9jrx.png)

### 5. Analyze Data

Look through your social listening data to get a fuller picture of what audiences are saying about you, your competitors, and relevant topics.

For example, you might gain a deeper understanding of sentiment by evaluating specific word choices:

- **Positive**: “love,” “awesome,” “best,” “fantastic,” “perfect”
- **Negative**: “terrible,” “awful,” “bad,” “rubbish,” “hate,” “worst”
- **Neutral**: “okay,” “average,” “mid,” “alright,” “not bad”

![Positive, negative, and neutral feedback curve alongside accompanying smiley faces](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3e/2f/3e2f9d01114722e82e00ac4a5e4319dd/c6e928c42d140ddceb0ed0dffe18f1a9/AD_4nXe0PThjyMIYmlkCmGKEve5qx_OtW3lu3c3W1B-aacMNg1XqmZvjV-E73fpiNYC5VwZQYfwXU02pqtvlel3iWsdl14wzrFkS9Vi4OxrfrHno_ytTcB-k-81RrleE1_Xd2VulFDYCpI1UyWEHX4G_xtUYgUY.png)

Consider the context of the information as well.

For example, is a customer being sarcastic when they say “It was the best customer service ever”?

And look for patterns and trends. Like a growing number of mentions. Or use of a relevant keyword alongside a new term you haven’t seen before.

Be sure to analyze your data for quantitative and qualitative insights.

### 6. Take Action

Use your analysis to determine what steps to take. So you can improve your social media, marketing, and overall business results.

For example, look at your audience sentiment analysis.

If your audience sentiment score is low, consider how to increase this metric. Like by improving your customer service response time or engaging with users more frequently.

Alternatively, if your sentiment score is high, see if you can spot a trend. Such as which products or services contribute most to your high score.

Use this information to inform the types of posts you create as well as what products or services your company launches in the future.
