# Top 10 Content Repurposing Tools for 2025

You spend hours writing high-quality content for your audience. But if all you’re doing is publishing it and moving on, you’re missing out on a powerful strategy: content repurposing.

What does this entail?

Taking existing content and repurposing it into other formats.

Sounds time-consuming? It doesn’t have to be. Content repurposing tools can help you take advantage of AI to create dozens of content assets from a single piece of content.

Turn blog posts into video content, Instagram posts into infographics, and much more.

Here are the top 10 content repurposing tools for 2024:

## 3. ChatGPT

[ChatGPT](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-chatgpt/) can serve as a versatile content repurposing tool.

For example, you can feed it an article and ask to make it into a LinkedIn post, an X (formerly Twitter) thread, or a newsletter email.

![ChatGPT-generated Twitter posts](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5e/ad/5ead1ef72df88e28fccd0d9715627f0e/139f648a6085d0dc996d944ac42a0b16/pH6CNdfeyMzIF_ezRjQlhNWO2LBk7dAepGEm00qoIoT6Qu8DGbozTrwV-c5aDD0PbVnjzfkIY6_JpF0GXgn9u9LgLdcTD66bqUNHnZvLrIrdVwhprQZzM8s7Cc8_NcbtaKupln9MotUP7dSJ_ccoN3g.png)

Or you could tell it to turn your blog post into a video script. You could even ask it to give you ideas for how to repurpose your content.

Although it isn’t a dedicated content repurposing tool, ChatGPT is a good option for small or one-person marketing teams that don’t have much time to spend repurposing content.

### What We Like

The possibilities for repurposing written content are endless—all you need is a bit of creativity when crafting prompts.

### Pricing

Currently, the version of ChatGPT that uses the GPT-3.5 model is free to use. The Plus plan ($20/month) gives you access to GPT-4, faster response speeds, and beta features.

The software summarizes your written content and adds relevant images or video footage from its library.

Due to its extensive feature set, it’s suitable for both small businesses and enterprises.

## 5. Repurpose.io

[Repurpose.io](https://repurpose.io/) is designed to help marketing teams repurpose their existing content and turn it into social media posts.

The idea behind the tool is that you only need to create one piece of content (e.g., a TikTok video), and then Repurpose.io will automatically repurpose it into various formats you can use across all your other marketing channels.

It supports all of the major social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, and Pinterest.

The software can also help you convert podcast episodes into multiple video clips. All you need to do is paste in your podcast feed URL. The tool will do the rest.

Repurpose.io will also resize your videos automatically for each platform and add captions.

Apart from marketing teams, Repurpose.io is also suitable for influencers, podcasters, and livestreamers.

### What We Like

The option to create automations to repurpose your content on autopilot (e.g., reupload every new TikTok video you post to Instagram Reels) is a valuable feature. This can save a lot of time for teams with complex content repurposing workflows.

!["Workflows" section in Repurpose.io](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a1/78/a178069a98db4d7b178d9904606b980a/ae7b187216b8c202e83ef386df11b41d/n-5BrjVXRsYtcSfnKRYpkyMMpvNbXjLRw3e-IvMfVsDHXjGTA7J7pKKESJAyvnn2VxHGtD9j4RW1qO-zlFOBbRsh1JzH9uddStw2palRpN5F94roS1pbd4rE5Z6DJ7hftxUoWtDUw24sKRjgrNxlf0A.jpeg)

### Pricing

Repurpose.io offers a free 14-day trial you can use to test out the software.

Paid plans include:

- Content Marketer ($25/month)
- Agency ($125/month)

## 6. Canva

You already know about and might have used [Canva](https://canva.com/). But did you know about its content repurposing capabilities?

Canva is a highly versatile tool for content repurposing.

Turn existing content into dozens of new pieces across a variety of different formats. Including infographics, ebooks, social media graphics, presentations, and more.

Its “Magic Switch” tool can help you repurpose your content faster by resizing your designs for multiple channels at the same time. Then, there’s “Magic Write,” Canva’s [AI tool](https://www.semrush.com/blog/best-ai-tools/) that can help you generate captions and social posts quickly.

You can also use hundreds of templates to quickly repurpose your existing content.

The software features a drag-and-drop editor that’s easy and intuitive to use, even for those without graphic design experience.

![Canva's drag-and-drop editor](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6a/a1/6aa178433fb2ce13ed363ddd9006f15f/e1b8b983ebce7aa53bed67745391ccf5/VWNgMZ-2dNY1BhWiBoINiqgcfokc9YBaZFizScDl3HQl5S1-kAWiXkAER8V-3PnQ021oivPcY18l6ZFyJVLxE2pWY9XfWWfwRm5Fg0vsxb0aNOzQpfxZbmEW-lM4ygvpdQa3ImJgFwwWn831hQc6SQs.jpeg)

## 7. 2short.ai

[2short.ai](https://2short.ai/) is a content repurpose software that analyzes your video content to identify the most engaging parts and turns them into short clips.

![A short clip generated by 2short.ai](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/70/ed/70ed349eb0299fcf8dd0b119e4f9ae21/d101f220da5147e3bb37cc0731c42ebf/WD_Pq4VvTxAfcAExIZkTURJl79wtNA2WmSGXO_McQJ0D64VVJafERxP91h8VUjnPumoKN776XFlnNkVazwMbfTq_PfOhMtYbLCF4MaE-Ov7K-qFAVhF6uSNM-BKvKMR03jaNUB0E7vFKTSRFO-nDuHc.jpeg)

2short.ai supports a variety of aspect ratios to align with the video requirements of all the major social media platforms. It also has a very useful facial tracking feature that helps keep speakers at the center of the screen.

### What We Like

You can access all of 2short.ai’s features on every pricing plan—the only difference in plans is the number of minutes included for AI video analysis and exporting.

### Pricing

2short.ai offers a free Starter plan, which includes 15 minutes of AI video analysis per month.

Paid plans include:

- Lite ($9.90/month)
- Pro ($19.90/month)
- Premium ($49.90/month)

Automata supports more than 150 input/output combinations. It can turn videos into blog posts, blog posts into email newsletters, newsletters into social threads, and so on.

## 9. Wavve

[Wavve](https://wavve.co/) can help you repurpose your audio content. It turns your podcasts, audiobooks, and talks into social-media-ready video clips. You can use it to create trailers for your podcasts and ebooks and share episode highlights.

!["Create Wavve Video" window](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0b/43/0b4374621b5fb18a4677d69a9c1a7967/b919af137d56925d27107f92c82b4eea/ySX_zHAI-xqJhtFyQc_b_j_gWlPKTwFDoJnzIdmHCX4l0Q3U6X_E2vE_YOjUXHMkADiFqoXN8VDC3cgZ0J1EI5-ByWel8W89u7JyLtUGNj8q8mPAEbEBEU1J8Ng0jr3pmPG04nJozUwurZnkdQImFPw.png)

Wavve lets you customize the clips it creates by using the built-in drag-and-drop editor, which allows you to add images, text, and animations.

The software can also transcribe audio and add captions to your clips.

### What We Like

Wavve integrates with Zapier to help you automate your repurposing workflow. For example, you can create a Zapier automation (called a Zap) to have Wavve create video clips from your new podcast episodes and post them to YouTube automatically.

### Pricing

- Unlimited ($19.99/month): Includes unlimited video generation minutes, transcriptions, and more

## 10. Vidyo.ai

[Vidyo.ai](https://vidyo.ai/) is an AI-powered platform that turns your long-form video content into short clips you can share across social media. It also captions all the clips automatically using AI.

The software can detect scene changes in your videos automatically, ensuring high-quality clips from interview-style content.

It can generate 30-45 short clips from an hour-long video.

### Pricing

- Pro ($29.99/month)

## Which Content Repurposing Tools Are Right for You?

There’s no single best content repurposing tool. The right tool for you will depend on your content needs and repurposing workflow.

All of the content repurposing tools above are great options, so consider your needs, the platforms you use, and your budget.

Whether you turn blog posts into social media video clips with [AI Video Marketing Automator](https://www.semrush.com/apps/ai-video-marketing-automator/) or repurpose existing video content using Vidyo.ai, these tools will expand your reach and save you time.
