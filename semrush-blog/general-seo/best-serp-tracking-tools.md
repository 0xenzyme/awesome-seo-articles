## 1. Position Tracking

**Best for**: Tracking keyword rankings across multiple search engines and AI tools

Semrush’s [Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) tool tracks keyword rankings and SERP features across locations, devices, and search engines.

You can monitor daily ranking changes, compare visibility with competitors, and group keywords with tags.

Position Tracking also shows which [SERP features](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/) your site appears in, such as featured snippets, AI Overviews, and local packs.

Set up custom alerts to get notified when rankings rise or drop, so you can respond quickly.

To get started, open the tool, enter your domain, and click “**Set up tracking**.”

![Position Tracking tool start with a domain entered and "Set up tracking" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7d/f7/7df77037c25a0c4985220002467f51f9/78e96894de547b3cbe5c350e2d98fffc/image.jpeg)

Select a search engine (or AI tool), device, and location. Click “**Continue To Keywords**.”

![Targeting settings on the Position Tracking tool with a search engine, device, location, and language selected.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/46/08/460828fa536698cb41696ba66203fbdd/34a62f107065b8121fd30f7fe32d1abd/image.jpeg)

Add keywords and click “**Start Tracking**.”

![Keyword settings on the Position Tracking tool with keywords added and "Start Tracking" clicked.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0c/b6/0cb63a473c1ede4f00839ba330a631be/6f12f3a6db952c25489695f28112f932/image.jpeg)

After setup, Position Tracking generates a keyword ranking report. Go to the “**Overview**” tab and scroll to “Rankings Overview” to view all tracked keywords and their current positions.

![Rankings Overview on Position Tracking showing how tracked keywords perform over time.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/17/8b/178bf164192a7e6818f4a5bfee61c2e9/d023a118febe6c9bcf68fe8acaa5a7b2/image.jpeg)

## 3. Semrush Sensor

**Best for**: Monitoring Google algorithm updates and SERP volatility

[Semrush Sensor](https://www.semrush.com/sensor/) tracks daily changes in Google search results to show when volatility spikes, so you can determine whether ranking drops comes from a broad algorithm update or a site-specific issue.

You can monitor volatility across 25+ industries, including finance, health, and real estate.

Semrush Sensor also tracks how SERP features, such as [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/), change over time. This helps you see when Google is testing or expanding AI-generated results that could affect your site’s visibility.

![SERP Features Occurrence showing how SERP features like Featured snippets, FAQs, and AI Overviews, etc. change over time.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/85/31/8531fd73fe48dca8208dd22c8b72a937/2639ec1ab520b06e71a7518f874e55ee/image.jpeg)

### What I Like

When several clients experienced sudden ranking drops in Q3 2022, Semrush Sensor showed a sharp spike in the daily score.

That confirmed a broad Google update and ruled out site-specific problems, which saved me hours of manual troubleshooting.

Free to use.

[Map Rank Tracker](https://www.semrush.com/map-rank-tracker/) shows exactly where your local business ranks on Google Maps for selected keywords and is part of the Semrush [Local Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/local-business/).
