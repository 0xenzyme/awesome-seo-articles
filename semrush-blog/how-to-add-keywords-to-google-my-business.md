# How to Add Keywords to a Google My Business Profile

Google My Business (GMB), now called Google Business Profile (GBP), is a free tool that lets you control how your business shows up on Google Search, Google Maps, and Google Shopping.

Adding relevant keywords to your Business Profile can make your business more visible and drive local search traffic to your website. Here’s how to do it.

## Access Your Google Business Profile

If you haven’t set up your Google Business Profile yet, do that first. Learn how in our [Ultimate Guide to Google My Business](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/). Then head back here to start adding your keywords.

If your Google Business Profile is all set up, login to your Google account and go to the [Google Business Profile page](https://www.google.com/business/).

Click “**Manage now**.”

![Google Business Profile page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/39/b6/39b61d50f6e833ae76b3abd8139e8611/AhlIMy9Tv-Otw8YqU_LdXwpS3lYklw8jZaHgwxhFzr3tC4Kgw0cFIpCTtlXRD198Xc4MliXig9xkgKHSkeZwf71yGKwRNi8PG3I2EHChuy8hGYFnbL_for3F9kFy5d6Myq_sAVXGfcaMFXpuk_69EPE.png)

You’ll see a menu with various ways to interact with and adjust your company’s profile.

![A menu in Google Business Profile](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ac/a3/aca3c246db68271f4e111d851064f1db/SjprHgdn51zrC4GaVcasIWZSVqr4gic5ONBSrWML4AKXV3E6F5rLPTbAEFlngFtq8Ouo7aEGbG5l6XcpGB88z70H-4iu_C81CJo6LfK99jnQZ2cIJIv6i93bvwiHRzxFQConTrklq-AvrWniyfTEWLs.png)

We’ve created a dummy business profile that we’ll use to show you how to add keywords.

![A dummy Google business profile titled "My Floristry Business"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/63/0f/630f0b25e6c25bcfbf69bc50842bb172/8466TKIx8cisnq3BapIhqJDOPRczz9NWeJyshAhfWrC3XE-WZhY4uw28slZJP2qhpT5qfJyPerA9GPQIo0FSywEFKLddc3tD2D9xGqFe49YTfHRq8LFQhlTzvkABp9dm5FGhK5ODHHqKsIgSv4oyPTc.png)

Click the “**Edit profile**” button to start using the keyword tips in this guide.

## Add Keywords as Business Categories

The keyword in your Business category field is the most foundational keyword for pointing people to your business. Make sure yours describes what you do accurately.

Our profile contains “Florist.” This targets the broadest customer base.

![Business category field highlighted on "Business information" page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c0/2e/c02ec7fd20604cd1a5609cd8e07af18e/6qM0587YKf3ElEOEZE9208T_pNLgT_adZeOlvxgxXmT5M9USgf6B5hjWWrKRBmvruJiYZLD3wLJZEONnO6G4_jRJ1gSYJboLdoED48FWV9wbqNev-u0XPFHppqmc_rh5-tOrRq49V4Fa7euhkCNyifk.png)

A search for “florists in New York”, for example, brings up a list of local businesses that self-categorize as florists.

![Google search results for search for “florists in New York”](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ee/6c/ee6c0e871f090eaeffa381a154a827d4/sC8ftS7vXXPXNAA6nRNOvPjG6pVUpd-HHSoSxoGzUigIGh8B9pgm-LDdxgUPaPf41AZ-2FonbIvHd4fRIahwS5wJECU4HSrd_kNUxmQxlH128NGJeZjTg4OyyEMWELxOHQ54ZaXrogrpor5ja6qK45o.png)

## Add Keywords to Your Business Description

Adding keywords to your business description helps Google and people searching on Google better understand what you do, who you serve, and what you sell.

Weave relevant keywords naturally into the descriptive text and [avoid keyword stuffing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-stuffing/). This ensures your description is valuable, relevant, and easy to understand.

A great business description explains:

- What you offer
- What sets you apart
- Your history
- Anything else that’s helpful for customers to know

Here’s an example from a florist in San Diego.

![Google Business profile from a florist in San Diego](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f4/7f/f47f6c930ebd2d09a0f0089e8891f403/yqtNxUxyW03wfn5JXSbtBwTT5yPUVW4EQUYc3BRoNf5XzH3w9dNtDlVcicjyiWrvyB4_S8WFTSaG4vbl3VKyg6FSf0l6GdgIMPT1VEvMHGSQWQNW5-sYK808Htk2JsIDeih50xb50BPkK7hKV0j27Ck.png)

Find the “Business information” window again. Click the “**Description**” field to open a text box.

![Description field highlighted on "Business information" window](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9e/8c/9e8ca0f295d56b96906e6cc1aa3d4446/q3ig297KLF3kM56Cdji3y1IaRhsbzX1qRCUfZvJERcyR_hR7kPTBUCcg8FPX_qIzUFEs4YvuOOiq3JwErrf7onpYy-wKaDuFfJxJSkj5S_aV2lN3wPTYG8n4WEIJGoHDCEOiKRDruRl0HDPFCW46STw.png)

Type or paste in your business description which uses the specific keywords you want to include.

In this example we’ve included search terms that our florist’s ideal customers may enter into Google. Like “wholesale flower orders,” “skilled florists,” and “locally sourced flowers.”

!["Description" window](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2a/aa/2aaaa40eb2e3bb79531410d77a590a2e/Aoq4i0dyR71Jqc0sy1E-rsXQPohPw5XKouzkVAct2t6N4XdCFQndISwgzvcu66jJi_XhPnWFbL-xspDaXoMcLg0-C9I4xD78D3wloHVwQyTPv5E7u2r7optXurEo2krDw21SP4jRx0n_8zSsHVyTGJQ.png)

When done, hit “**Save**.”

You can always revisit this description to make tweaks as your business evolves.

!["Products" popup window with a list of added products](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fe/74/fe74d26cdc2550844aa8515f38edbc84/pd1Bf8n3N8iBcfJdG4vsZfU3Ux7iye0W4hxkjYUxzRWTmAEzXlhjpJYKKFyuHIcaqbCZhiaXNI6bC0FwH59y7Hr74bKhyhlwzywRIfUDMbNByjCvaadvdrOcs-2zCi5v8tb0GScFyB2GAm309v68Ly0.png)

## Add Keywords as Business Attributes

Adding factual attributes to your profile makes it more likely your business will show up in your target customers’ search results.

Factual attributes are predefined descriptions of something your business has or does. Like whether your restaurant has outdoor seating or whether your store offers delivery.

For example, if we search “Denver donuts delivery,” the top Places results are businesses that confirmed when building or editing their Google Business Profile that they deliver.

![Google "Places" section for “Denver donuts delivery” search](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/87/9c/879c7aeb2c577ed504aef0fbc3a4fb5d/NDwtXTfdAq6sSro9By5fFyD190n9LtaT5MAtjQxAhznpVTXoAXUO86u2Zjg3Kdg_S-DdL6WcmnLVWDZSpHTMcqOTiqAO_32vTsXtuNH1mrfolNHqWEk3SidCOJlcQdbhlLmGEKpbAm64oArQGxcqfTw.png)

Notice: in the Places results list, “delivery” is in bold each time. That’s Google highlighting the results’ relevance. As it does with keywords in general search rankings.

![An example of a search result with “delivery” in bold](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c6/30/c630439a9400d766ccdc058158cf41a3/0WgoecH_VYZBlU2ROHQhjZmXwGmW-1DB_D5jgS6hNdXeT-yk51o8zt5k3MrtypG2zHw1KEdXgJHTwX1d9hj987-RktJysY9Xauwoh6GlNRPmtlPoySy0rm9j50MMtLJP33em0RPWlqywBlMKCOBKgy0.png)

***Note:** Factual attributes differ from **subjective** **attributes**, like whether a cafe is child-friendly or suitable for groups. Google sources subjective attributes through customers’ opinions.*

To add factual attributes for your business, find the “Your business on Google” menu again. Select “**Edit** **profile**.”

On the “Business information” page, select “**More**” from the top row of tabs.

![“More” button highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/3d/b6/3db65b54c8d24b1e72d01e09329ff2cf/rOiEUmQynLyJ0GDwhV-8Tml1qn7yC2oNsv-PB7so4e1gZMccQDuCkQnngdm65WUpsYLCWd4dtbTpuEuEgS8Nu8wR4_n4FlCioFYAn_n6yxGqeaqtNRpxR6FZI1P5pjI8o1Zo3RDE0opZNvJPMmIhRgs.png)

You can add a range of attributes, from seating and delivery options to payment methods and amenities.

![“Business information” page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/20/cc/20cccf8ca4cec66afbffac386b2cb49f/2eK-TbWBz9kgR73kom7WrOjy-0m2E8AeIx3r8uay8cCrmfUi3BZgPjYDxLmMXvC3g4l-KJ7VRBVi3DCaSTLQ7dzoVneChTrxZWE9ahu6lszNM8dgr82FwEot_pC2qWGwQRYqWL3h7qFC-1W8Y2tr9jQ.png)

![“Save” button highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c0/0c/c00ce3162410a9aae68fdfa0d96578d0/ZzqJDVyjTeUKup1jyRNVY5TL09X0WR_smGxfana12ZeUPDJBoXROYnvEN5XdOSPcAx8FEWzw50AwvPwgBc-QhJ4kVKDO-UFo6hYatx2mitCp2L9qwmO2P-g3FZFBfJShrCYZ8kkk7Fp_z_TEtcF61Lk.png)

Add as many attributes as are relevant and accurate for your business.

Revisit your profile at least monthly to check that your attributes are still correct. Otherwise you risk showing up for irrelevant searches or misleading potential customers.

## How to Find Keywords That’ll Drive More Traffic

The right keywords can increase relevant search traffic. They improve discoverability and help your audience find your business when they search.

The easiest way to find keywords worth including in your Google My Business profile is with a keyword research tool.

Here are some easy techniques to start.

***Tip:** Create a free [Semrush account](https://www.semrush.com/signup/) to get 10 free searches per day. Then try the following methods for yourself.*

### Use a Seed Keyword to Generate Fresh Ideas

A seed keyword is a broad keyword that describes your niche. You use it to discover more relevant keyword ideas.

Our florist might use the term “flowers.” We’ll run with that example throughout this guide.

To start, enter your seed keyword into a keyword research tool. The [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/) is a great option.

!["flowers" entered into Keyword Magic Tool search bar](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/97/34/97342c05fed516bf1997290221809528/rCDdPyp-NGA1N2GZAw77FbCTrdkbKhppeFsSpUBIXDmYHVVPHhEWaRM2bME9phXHWu-gng6XU_wrSUzT_YEmwtHD4QJxUc-tmc--IWwn9o-0oMCQ1VrnXwdRzWVrslkrcPam9AHKEPVh2k-D90UOLQs.png)

Choose your target location and hit “**Search**.”

!["Search" button highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bd/c7/bdc76b518f479d938c16aece0404d3c3/CGAicQtqcTjoGtU3-Vxrisz9ziXgfETiCIl2A3GX_cheI_scnuPFDR4Mjfs_RO1FgmXLZsCo6-shbjG0FAclz-D4sfhpk9FZlF6PN0bsXw7nX4uf8cjMLlVdG9Wv_CmJ5vh65NQdf09BXLdGT2kABXg.png)

The tool will list all the keyword suggestions relevant to your chosen seed term. In this case there are more than 1.4 million.

![Keyword Magic Tool results for the "flower" search](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/38/4f/384f553075253d63f70c85a6ca73f7f0/_V-aIzSl4Yavrf2up5MlH_EfmChi1ozNIXwPnJ6jTMKGvQTNyThlDqZu8gQ2SzTSkQ8W-PtzBHlW9AQkbhXpfrkKmlYhQLD8tKeLNm6i7O8kNDosk-qKYe6xYrJzISSG381zhbvOFa9nVDV24U8zbyk.png)

Some of these terms will be irrelevant or too competitive.

For example: The keyword phrase “flowers miley cyrus” refers to a pop song. Using that keyword would attract the wrong traffic. And “flowers” by itself would be difficult to rank for at first because it’s highly competitive. Notice it has a monthly search volume of 1 million and the highest keyword difficulty.

Thankfully we can dig deeper into our niche using the **topical groups and subgroups** in the column to the left of the main list. There we should find relevant keywords that will help us stand out.

![Groups and subgroups for "flower" in the column on the left](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2a/a5/2aa515b3cdb087bf6d5c4e19a1a3a8fc/vKFjjnA4eoyRHNtmy7X3UI2wucCE3mnsKFdb5r9c0aXTi6jjP4on3cgjMJ6TqEJJTzMLDXp1JFUTB82umWjV4nRSr-Xp-zbxPgF3DsmZD4NC13j7YId3BD1BPdsq4DsnQj7noywbriKy0G_RqLhgv8g.png)

Hide irrelevant keyword groups by clicking the eye icons next to them.

## 5 Pro Tips to Optimize Your Google Business Profile

Now you know how to add the right keywords to Google My Business.

How else can you optimize your profile for more traffic?

Here are five tips to increase clicks and conversions through Google Search, Maps, and Shopping.

### 1. Review NAP Accuracy and Consistency

Your company’s name, address, and phone number—known as “NAP”—are the most important pieces of information in your Google Business Profile.

Why?

Because these details help potential customers quickly identify and discover your business. Keeping them current makes that possible.

And keeping them consistent everywhere your business appears online prevents confusion.

First ensure everything is correct in your business account.

Open the “Business information”window by selecting “**Edit** **profile**.”

![“Edit profile” highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/60/9a/609a84a7f21a1a2a2207643b3be800ff/X5vPw-Dq8-Vf8K1nwYUynfj3FbH8nqCbQfPCfqBZ5EEVqa1wTTmxSqU-flRMG1FnTKrIvhDrf3CXQGxGqXOxr0e5PV53smRQ7-KPz4vzmu32wg_M9gw_p545ytJIglQROFVCfsk9annUZknwFDDLopM.png)

On the “About” tab, look for your “Business name.”

![Business name field highlighted on "Business information" page](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a8/77/a877c38b5ed5ff31ce0b96592370d3ee/XBoPctiiANdaOmx0OStnvwrNLXKV7S3mG1dvEZCPU-RUEq7VtoeTOCRPfYLIUk3JA_CMwpHn00syrU0W01uSp55HB3klC-2nxvs0XC9Lz9yf9yUFtif6M8S3NypjBiydk0AN3Pt6f-paIkJWXKJXuc4.png)

Click the existing entry to edit it.

Match all other branding to the letter. Make sure the name is capitalized consistently everywhere it’s listed and watch out for regional spelling nuances (e.g., “color” vs. “colour”).

Click “**Save**,” to capture any changes.

Then hit the “**Contact**” tab at the top or scroll down to find your listed “Phone number” and “Business location.” Check them for accuracy and click existing entries to edit them if needed. Click “**Save**,” for any changes.

!["Contact" “Phone number” and “Business location” sections highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ec/29/ec292847d559f7c1ab2a769b1abc793d/pnTdX3HdJR5XEMkAuXTCNrJd47cf-xzu5vIOSDgF0aRTGdK1K6lEDsx6WlkqaCgqS9G3P5ABQGPTwBnAhpAA1EWZtP7OtgVwWPNRjDCa1pyKA2_A1r5KI4QLwO827lXToZ2EJgNrBmjf7A7KnnJZ6wU.png)

The easiest way to keep NAP info consistent across multiple directories is to use the [Listing Management](https://www.semrush.com/listings-management/start/start/) tool.

It allows you to:

- Edit multiple directory records in one go (70+ for the US, 40+ for other countries)
- Keep listings free of duplicates, which optimizes your company’s visibility
- Get reports on how often people search for your business and how often Google displays it

***Further reading:** Learn more about the Listing Management tool and the benefits of keeping NAP records consistent in our [Knowledge Base guide on Listing Management](https://www.semrush.com/kb/847-listing-management).*

That means it’s essential to keep track and reply when appropriate.

### 5. Prioritize User Experience

Great user experiences signal to Google and potential customers that they should visit your page. After all, great experiences are what Google strives to provide for searchers.

Give users the necessary information to assess your business and make buying decisions. This includes essentials like accurate opening times, attributes, and contact information.

And inform customers about changes to your business even when they’re not positive or exciting.

Increasing your costs? Prepare customers and explain your reasons.

Closing early? Post about it to prevent disappointment and reputational damage.

Finally, own up to and address mistakes and issues that customers highlight in messages and Google reviews. This could save you from bad press and even get you some positive press.
