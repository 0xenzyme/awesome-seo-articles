## Top 5 trending SaaS startups

Here’s a quick look at the top 5 startups with the greatest growth in organic traffic last year (graphs come from [Ahrefs’ Site Explorer](https://ahrefs.com/site-explorer)).

### 1. OneShot.ai

**Year founded**: 2020

**Location**: Gerrards Cross, Buckinghamshire, United Kingdom

**Estimated revenue**: $6.50M

**Total funding**: $4.75M

**Organic traffic growth (12mo)**: 24,944%

![Oneshot.ai growth.](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/oneshot-ai-growth-.png)

[OneShot.ai](http://oneshot.ai) is revolutionizing outbound sales through its autonomous platform. By leveraging advanced machine learning and generative AI, the platform automates tedious manual prospecting tasks, enabling sales teams to focus on building genuine relationships and providing value to customers.

### 2. Litespace

**Year founded**: 2021

**Location**: Kitchener, Ontario, Canada

**Estimated revenue**: $5.50M

**Total funding**: $0.05M

**Organic traffic growth (12mo)**: 10,509%

[Litespace](https://www.litespace.io) offers an AI-driven platform for employee engagement and workplace culture. The platform offers tools that foster a cohesive work environment, bridging the gap between in-office and remote team members.

### 3. Complyt

**Year founded**: 2021

**Location**: Ramat Gan, Israel

**Estimated revenue**: $9.61M

**Total funding**: $5.00M

**Organic traffic growth (12mo)**: 10,114%

[Complyt](http://complyt.io/) offers compliance automation software aimed at helping businesses manage regulatory requirements effortlessly. Their platform is designed to streamline compliance processes, reducing the burden on organizations and ensuring adherence to industry standards.

### 4. Mastt

**Year founded**: 2019

**Location**: Sydney, Australia

**Estimated revenue**: $5.50M

**Total funding**: $8.68M

**Organic traffic growth (12mo)**: 7,854%

[Mastt](https://www.mastt.com/) provides a project management SaaS solution tailored specifically for the construction industry. The platform addresses the unique challenges of construction project management, offering tools to enhance efficiency, collaboration, and oversight.

### 5. The Liven

**Year founded**: 2014

**Location**: Kyiv, Ukraine

**Estimated revenue**: $0.50M

**Total funding**: $0.00M

**Organic traffic growth (12mo)**: 7,247%

[The Liven](http://theliven.com/) is a mobile application designed to support users in their personal growth and emotional well-being. It provides a comprehensive system of tools and resources grounded in behavioral psychology, offering a unique and personalized approach to self-discovery. Liven helps users understand their thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, enabling them to make positive changes and live more fulfilling lives.
