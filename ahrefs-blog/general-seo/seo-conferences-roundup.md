## 1. SMX Munich

![](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/word-image-195357-1.jpg)

**Date:** March 9-11

**Location:** Munich, Germany

**Ticket Prices:** From €999

**Website:** [smxmuenchen.de/en](http://smxmuenchen.de/en)

**Notable Speakers:** Alex Schultz (Meta), Wil Reynolds (Seer Interactive), Aleyda Solis (Orainti), Dana DiTomaso (Kick Point), Frederick Vallaeys (Optmyzr), Jes Scholz

Ready for Europe’s biggest search marketing party? SMX Munich brings together 60+ sessions focusing on where AI, SEO, and PPC collide.

With industry heavyweights like Alex Schultz from Meta and Wil Reynolds sharing the stage, you’ll walk away with fresh perspectives on AI Overviews, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and what’s actually working right now. All the keynotes and **the** pub quiz are in English (yes, there’s a pub quiz!), making it perfect for international attendees.

Plus, the networking scene is unbeatable; you’ll be swapping strategies with search pros from across Europe.

## 7. SEO Week

**Date:** April 27-30

**Location:** New York City, USA

**Ticket Prices:** From $1,099

**Website:** [seoweek.org](https://seoweek.org/)

**Notable Speakers:** Michael King (iPullRank), Crystal Carter (Wix), Wil Reynolds (Seer Interactive), Ross Simmonds (Foundation), Jori Ford (FoodBoss)

Four days. Forty speakers. Four themed days that’ll blow your mind. SEO Week takes over NYC with focused 30-minute sessions that pack a punch.

- **Day 1 - Science:** Data-driven SEO approaches and technical foundations

- **Day 3 - Ecosystem:** Platform updates, algorithm changes, and industry dynamics

The four-day format allows for deep exploration of each theme with expert insights you can implement immediately. Plus, the workshops and meetups mean you’ll leave with both knowledge and connections.
