# 93 Online Advertising Statistics You Need to Know for 2025

Staying informed of the latest advertising industry trends is key to achieving your goals as a digital marketer.

It eliminates guesswork, helping you adapt your marketing strategy. So you can align with shifting consumer and competitor behaviors, emerging platforms, and changing preferences.

With that in mind, we’ve created a compendium of the most important advertising statistics. Keep it handy to guide your digital ad spending in 2024 and beyond.

Let’s dive in.

## Mobile Advertising Statistics

Most internet users have smartphones. That makes mobile marketing essential if you want to reach and engage customers online.

Here’s what the data says about the current state of mobile advertising:

- 95% of internet users browse on a mobile device at least some of the time ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/global-digital-overview#:~:text=The%20vast%20majority%20of%20the,of%20the%20world's%20web%20traffic.))
- Mobile devices account for approximately 57% of people’s online time ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/global-digital-overview#:~:text=The%20vast%20majority%20of%20the,of%20the%20world's%20web%20traffic.))
- Mobile devices account for 53% of the world’s web traffic ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/global-digital-overview#:~:text=The%20vast%20majority%20of%20the,of%20the%20world's%20web%20traffic.))
- There are 5.56 billion unique mobile phone subscribers worldwide—that’s 69.1% of the population ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot))
- $205.4 billion of total social media ad spending will be generated through mobile by 2027—up from $170.3 billion in 2023 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/dmo/digital-advertising/social-media-advertising/worldwide))
- Advertising drives $2 of every $3 spent on mobile ([Data.ai, 2023](https://www.data.ai/en/insights/market-data/state-of-app-revenue-2023/))
- Paid mobile YouTube ads are 84% more likely to capture viewer attention than traditional TV commercials ([Google](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/marketing-strategies/app-and-mobile/youtube-mobile-advertising-statistics/))
- Over 70% of YouTube views are on mobile devices ([Khoros, 2022](https://khoros.com/resources/social-media-demographics-guide#you-tube-demographics))
- 96% of users access Facebook via mobile devices ([Khoros, 2022](https://khoros.com/resources/social-media-demographics-guide#facebook-demographics))
- Mobile ad revenues reached a record $154.1 billion in 2022, up 14.1% year-over-year (YoY); mobile now has a 73.5% market share of total internet ad revenues ([IAB, 2023](https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/IAB_PwC_Internet_Advertising_Revenue_Report_2022.pdf))
- TV ads (30.6%), social media ads (27.7%), website ads (22.3%), mobile ads (20.6%), and video ads (16.9%) all feature in the top 15 sources of brand discovery ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot))

We’ve long known mobile marketing is important. These stats confirm that consumers’ love for smaller screens continues to grow.

Spending increases tell us advertisers see this trend too. They’re responding by allocating even more of their budgets to mobile search, social, and video marketing. To keep pace with the competition, you’ll need to do the same, and use the right ad formats.

## Social Media Advertising Statistics

The social media marketing landscape changes frequently. It aligns with consumers’ evolving preferences around content consumption.

Stay on top with help from these social media advertising statistics:

- More people discover new brands, products, and services via social media ads than by word-of-mouth recommendations, brand and product websites, and consumer review sites ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot))
- Social media ads are the top source of new brand discovery for internet users aged 16-24 ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot))
- The top three social media channels based on potential ad reach in the United States are:
  - Facebook: 188,050,000
  - Instagram: 161,700,000
  - TikTok: 150,000,000+

([DataReportal, July 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot)) ([TikTok, March 2023](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/150-m-us-users))

- The average click-through rate (CTR) for a social media ad is 1.36% ([Statista, Q2 2023](https://www.statista.com/statistics/872099/social-media-advertising-ctr/))
- Social media ad spending is projected to reach $207.1 billion in 2023 ([Statista, 2023](https://www.statista.com/outlook/dmo/digital-advertising/social-media-advertising/worldwide))
- The average U.S. social media buyer is projected to spend $779 via social networks in 2024, and $937 in 2025 ([Insider Intelligence, 2022](https://www.insiderintelligence.com/content/us-social-buyers-spending-more-than-ever))
- There are 4.88 billion social media user identities—equating to 60.6% of the population ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot))
- Social media users are currently growing at a rate of 9.6 new users every second. ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/global-digital-overview))
- The average social media user spends 2 hours and 24 minutes using social media every day ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/global-digital-overview))
- The average social media user uses 6.7 platforms per month ([GWI/DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/social-media-users))

Brands have traditionally used social media as a promotional tool to build awareness and engage with target audiences. That remains crucial.

But now, consumers can buy products directly on social platforms. The advent of social commerce introduces a new dimension to marketing strategies.

![A social media ad for lemon flavored shower gel by Sakura](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/05/15/0515c4c12c35198fa0731528f23af349/d4ffab14251ed3ff56e394dae61ab21b/nolmNE8HQjl9srOmvdOyV7c6FBDsnReAF-TjDOASNYlQrZ4VCxDmtaniHN3GFwl4I-v0YpIfxAB-ZOtNJpzvhBakRmgpCs4jfFt-lHRCrv-upt7VxaK5Oub_acFHo0VIfTYx5NeOpAPjM8VhPFlzQYs.jpeg)

With consumers now able to buy through direct links (like in the TikTok [example above](https://shop.tiktok.com/business/en)), it’s vital to vary your online ads. Use some to drive traffic to your online store, and others with more direct language to encourage quick sales.

## Instagram Advertising Statistics

[Instagram’s](https://business.instagram.com/) advertising versatility makes it a marketer’s dream.

From sponsored content and boosted posts to Stories, Reels, and shopping links, there are plenty of ways to reach and engage your ideal customers.

![Instagram’s advertising landing page for Stories Ads](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e3/39/e339f454d18fefb7fdf13fbdf6e57042/2a07eb035b4f62ce1571ab2709bbbf81/_VOkbrMqGM28nAayuSUaez4KOY5nxCMzwn6zkvS9kkmx7-7KlFkVapuP-CuKRKRbi0a320kYA9iamOdW8JEzxQAMs797HNtL9rwz79VytZkeEO41-YajrpQzt6GpB4su5ukurIVhG2RnmUWIiOzYAKM.jpeg)

Here are the Instagram advertising statistics you need to know:

- Instagram has the second largest reach of all [social media advertising](https://www.semrush.com/blog/advantages-of-social-media-advertising/) platforms in the U.S., with 161,700,000 users ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-july-global-statshot))
- An estimated 71% of U.S. businesses have Instagram accounts ([Khoros, 2022](https://khoros.com/resources/social-media-demographics-guide#instagram-demographics))

Competition will always be strong on Instagram. But the fact that users spend so much time on the app and so many are open to learning about new brands and products means it should still be high on your advertising agenda.

What’s more, the platform’s vast global reach makes it ideal for companies with diverse target audiences.

TikTok’s rapid rise in popularity makes the channel difficult to ignore [for online advertisers](https://www.tiktok.com/business/en-US). Especially since it offers a wide range of ad formats and targeting options.

- On average, TikTok users open the app eight times per day ([Khoros, 2022](https://khoros.com/resources/social-media-demographics-guide#tik-tok-demographics))

Providing instant gratification through short, impactful clips is the best way to succeed on TikTok.

With ad costs dropping and production tools more accessible than ever, video advertising is an increasingly appealing option for marketers at all levels.

Unsurprisingly, the stats show video and audio go hand in hand to create the most engaging ad content. Using a mix of music and narration across your videos should get you the best results.

## YouTube Advertising Statistics

More than a video hosting site, [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/ads/) is where billions of people turn daily for information and entertainment.

And given only 80 million (at the [last official count](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-music-premium-80-million-lyor-cohen/)) have ad-free Premium accounts, we can infer that the vast majority consume ad content regularly.

So where does it sit in the advertising landscape? Here’s what the data says:

- 84% of U.S. adults living in urban areas, 81% of those living in suburban areas, and 74% living in rural areas report using YouTube ([Pew Research, 2021](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2021/04/07/social-media-use-in-2021/))

- YouTube viewers say they’re 2x more likely to buy something they saw on YouTube ([YouTube, 2020](https://www.youtube.com/ads/))
- 70% of YouTube viewers have bought from a brand after seeing its content on YouTube ([Google, 2020](https://blog.google/products/ads/new-ways-to-drive-action/))

## Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising Statistics

[PPC](https://www.semrush.com/ppc/) is a great way to target specific audiences and get fast results. It’ll get you in front of users on social media, search engines, and online marketplaces. And with minimal financial risk, since you only pay when someone clicks through to your content.

![A graph showing how PPC advertising gives quick results in terms of time and traffic](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/91/cf/91cf23c07ba9a046304bbd943cb36350/d1a95bf4654760164e39045c2374a41f/i5hTCjvJ6LODvjdHJ0Kpevnuuz1FxgQNRFj09F-0I_q5F8QbolUa_kqK8I_cqdbotIqF0NksTW-D0u3AzMRE4-YIY9k3PaxZHsWI3p1dNU549IXGoyIgto7BtieCD3upPbydPKgcG05awbuiYs0zmTU.jpeg)

Here are some key statistics about PPC:

- The top 10 most popular platforms for running PPC campaigns are:

1. Google (98%\*)
2. Facebook (76%)
3. Instagram (64%)
4. Bing (60%)
5. YouTube (59%)
6. LinkedIn (42%)
7. Pinterest (15%)
8. TikTok (15%)
9. X (formerly Twitter) (14%)
10. Amazon (11%)

\*Percentages show how often a platform was part of survey respondents’ digital advertising strategies ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))

- 42% of marketers spend less than $50K/month on PPC, 39% spend between $50K and $500K, and 18% spend more than $500k/month ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))
- 97% of PPC freelancers and specialists have adopted Responsive Search Ads in Google; 81% have adopted Responsive Display Ads ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))
- 71% of those using Responsive Search Ads in Google were satisfied or neutral about the results. 66% of those using Responsive Display Ads in Google were very satisfied, satisfied or neutral about the results. ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))
- Based on TrueClicks' [State of PPC 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf) survey responses, PPC professionals spend most of their time on:
  - Search query mining
  - Reporting
  - Writing ads
  - Budget management
  - Campaign creation
  - Ad testing
- 45% of PPC professionals use Semrush for competitive analysis ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))
- 31% of PPC specialists use [AI writing assistants](https://www.semrush.com/apps/ai-writing-assistant/) to craft ad copy, with the most popular tool being Grammarly (17%) ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))

While Google dominates the PPC market, advertisers continue to run successful campaigns on other platforms.

So there’s an opportunity to get your brand and products in front of your audience wherever they spend the most time.

The data also shows PPC specialists are quick to adopt new, helpful features. Keep an eye on your preferred channels’ announcements for fresh opportunities to increase engagement.

Google dominates search, which means it also [dominates search advertising](https://ads.google.com/home/). That alone makes it impossible for marketers in all industries to ignore.

Here are the latest stats on Google Ads:

- Search advertising revenue reached $84.4 billion in 2022—making up 40.2% of total internet advertising revenues ([IAB, 2023](https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/IAB_PwC_Internet_Advertising_Revenue_Report_2022.pdf))
- Google is the most-used platform for PPC advertising ([TrueClicks, 2022](https://trueclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/The-State-of-PPC-2022-Global-Report.pdf))
- The average Google Ads click-through rate across all industries is 6.11% ([Wordstream by LocaliQ, 2023](https://www.wordstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2023-LiQ-GoogleAd-Benchmarks-Updated.pdf))
- The average [Google Ads cost](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-ads-cost/) per click (CPC) is $4.22 ([Wordstream by LocaliQ, 2023](https://www.wordstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2023-LiQ-GoogleAd-Benchmarks-Updated.pdf))

- Arts and entertainment businesses experience the lowest average CPCs ($1.55), while attorneys and legal services have the highest ($9.21) ([Wordstream by LocaliQ, 2023](https://www.wordstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2023-LiQ-GoogleAd-Benchmarks-Updated.pdf))
- Career & employment companies pay the highest average cost per lead ($132.95), while automotive repair and service firms have the lowest ($21.12) ([Wordstream by LocaliQ, 2023](https://www.wordstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2023-LiQ-GoogleAd-Benchmarks-Updated.pdf))
- Long-tail keywords, in aggregate, make up the vast majority of all Google searches ([Semrush, 2022](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-long-tail-keywords/))
