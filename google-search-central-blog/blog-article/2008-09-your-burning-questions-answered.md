# Your burning questions - Answered!

In a recent [blog post](/search/blog/2008/08/impact-of-user-feedback-part-2-and-more)
highlighting our
[Webmaster Help Group](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community),
I asked for your webmaster-related questions. In our second installment of
[Popular Picks](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e213eec10610a481),
we hoped to discover which issues webmasters wanted to learn more about, and then respond with
some better documentation on those topics. It looks like it was a success, so please get clicking:

- **Image Search ranking:** Starting things off,
  [JimAtArtsnovaDotCom asked](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e213eec10610a481/#c73d8d6d53850aeb)
  about how Google indexes images. Evan details the factors that account for a
  [site's images appearing in Google's image search](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e37227930654391e).
- **Sitelinks and Webmaster Tools:** Maile tackled
  [a request from Red Cardinal](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e213eec10610a481/#f968695a7cac467d)
  by providing a more detailed
  [explanation of sitelinks](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Tools/browse_thread/thread/63b915eed3386a60),
  particularly what happens when they are blocked by a user.
- **Reconsideration Requests:** Want to learn more about reconsideration requests? In response
  to
  [a message from Chibcha](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e213eec10610a481/#369f15dbd44b1a2d),
  Susan provides additional context on
  [when you should file a reconsideration request](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/97db1e0a05f4357d)
  and other tips to address your site's performance.
- **Redirects:** In response to
  [Cristina's question](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e213eec10610a481/#663ff183c887de3e)
  about site moves and redirects, John explains how
  [Googlebot handles absolute and relative URLs in your redirect to a new domain](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/ebffe06e5399d192).
- **Communication with webmasters:** Finally, I responded to
  [a message from bluegill01](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/e213eec10610a481/#16ce6f63f424a065),
  noting the scenarios when you can expect to
  [receive a message from Google](https://groups.google.com/group/Google_Webmaster_Help-Indexing/browse_thread/thread/6fa677629a9e2a70).

Thanks again for your questions! See you around the group.
