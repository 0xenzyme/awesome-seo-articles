# What Is SEO? An Intro to Search Engine Optimization

## What Is SEO?

SEO stands for search engine optimization. It is the process of making your website more visible in search engines like Google without paying for ads.

SEO is sometimes called SEO marketing, SEO optimization, or organic search marketing. It can help you earn organic (unpaid) rankings like this:

![Google's top organic result for "best hiking boots" is REI with a page called "The Best Hiking Boots of 2024: Tested"](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/81/ff/81ff4fe9fde07c88ed260c53de2ebd12/4968bf3ac1559ab865bfc0067ef32f0c/AD_4nXdF9KqVAmim75h0vkUgTX7l8HduFyenAgG8EJrRZQlynT40uGw0N1PCcgRzj2ZRUMjrsGlkD482ReWzc5bflcBB7MlMpUD5iosbvo2Eg4JtNELXBWHp7980RZ4mVUZO4NOBJi_k7g.png)

As a result, you can gain more brand awareness and traffic.

SEO involves making your site more appealing to both users and search engines.

The four main elements in a well-rounded SEO strategy are:

- **Keyword Research**: Finding out what your target audience searches for
- **On-Page SEO**: Optimizing webpages and their content
- **Off-Page SEO**: Getting other sites to link to your site, which can boost its reputation
- **Technical SEO**: Improving your site's overall performance and accessibility

You will learn more about these elements later.

### Search Engine Marketing vs. Search Engine Optimization

[Search engine marketing (SEM)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-engine-marketing/) promotes a business through unpaid or paid results on search engines, often both.

- **SEO** is the subset of SEM that focuses on organic results. It generates traffic that you don’t have to pay the search engine for.
- **Pay-per-click (**[**PPC**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-ppc/)**)** marketing is the subset of SEM that focuses on sponsored (paid) results. It involves setting up ad campaigns, paying to run them, and generating traffic.

![SEM is a combination of SEO, organic search results, and PPC, paid search results](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/84/99/8499b296952203c1431854b6234ac2d7/3fbc6908ccfab755c2e45e0cc693cfe2/AD_4nXet-oU9tzRFCz-dWBLcuuQr9FZG1K02EjlYWvut2nz4VZPQkSFcJKSEDJgJaK6kfQEt9Bs39Q7C3qK3oWp2JgSaNaYR-x5eDfkYSAYKrRhV9HOOHVKa1kz1UlaJUB5r2rpF4SFCkw.png)

Paid results don’t always appear on search engine results pages ([SERPs](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp/)). When they do, they usually appear at the top with a "Sponsored" or "Ad" label.

Like this:

![The paid result has a bolded "Sponsored" label while the organic result does not](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9b/cf/9bcf7d9b2e810bc26bc70dcb49782d18/868e5daf68bc5fdb1c5bd951231b1f56/AD_4nXeXBqnsBFV9WZPV_6k4qAz8argwkCVF8evJzlpAq7_LnoygDKyXR0aylWw1gYAaFAh2KBrJyO9PEbdqOtotjl3KCIZa8V_5snZe_2WuY3Jd_iDmeQymumbmkIYqUTS09LYMg3s4.png)

PPC can give you immediate visibility for high-value keywords, whereas [SEO can take weeks or months](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-long-does-seo-take/) to show results.

However, organic results tend to have higher click-through rates because users often trust them more than ads. Organic results can also generate sustained traffic at little to no ongoing cost.

## Why Is SEO Important?

[SEO is important](https://www.semrush.com/blog/importance-of-seo/) in digital marketing because it helps you capitalize on the organic brand awareness and traffic that search engines generate.

For example, this brand’s SEO strategy yields around 5.9 million monthly visitors, according to [Semrush’s Organic Rankings tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/):

If you don’t use SEO, you’re unlikely to rank prominently for relevant terms. This limits your ability to reach new prospects and persuade them to convert.

Users will probably go to a competitor instead.

A higher ranking typically translates into more [impressions](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-impressions/) and a better [click-through rate](https://www.semrush.com/blog/click-through-rate/).

Since website SEO improves the user experience, it can also lead to better engagement and conversion rates, plus increased traffic from other sources—not just search engines.

Ultimately, SEO can deliver a good return on investment.

## How Does SEO Work?

SEO works by aligning your website with how search engines define good search results.

Search engines use complex processes and criteria to rank results, aiming to provide users with the best search experience.

By optimizing your website and content according to these systems, you can gain more visibility.

However, **SEO is not a perfect science**.

Search engines are somewhat secretive about the systems they use and constantly change them to improve search results.

Therefore, testing, analysis, and adaptation are crucial to SEO success.

### How Do Search Engines Work?

Search engines work by crawling, indexing, and ranking content on the internet.

Here’s what each process involves:

- **Crawling**: Search engines use automated programs called bots or crawlers to explore the web and discover content. They primarily follow [hyperlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ahref-link/) to find new pages.
- **Indexing**: After analyzing the content, the search engine determines if it’s eligible for indexing. If it is, the content is added to the database of possible search results.
- **Ranking**: When a user enters a query, the search engine tries to understand the [search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/). It then uses a complex algorithm to present the best results from its index.

![How search engines work: you publish content on your site. Google bots or "spiders" crawl your site and review pages. Google indexes your page. When your content appears in search results, it can take various forms, such as traditional search listings (the standard blue links).

Google also displays [SERP features](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/) (non-standard results) to enhance the search experience.

For example, this SERP includes a popular products section:

The SERP also includes [rich snippets](https://www.semrush.com/blog/rich-snippets/), which are normal results with extra elements like images and star ratings:

Factors that influence your presence, positioning, and appearance on the SERP are known as ranking factors. They include:

- The relevancy and quality of your content
- The presence of keywords on your page
- The number and quality of links from other sites, which can boost your reputation
- How you use [schema markup](https://www.semrush.com/blog/schema-markup/) to help search engines understand and categorize information on your site
- The speed and usability of your site

## Types of SEO

Here are some of the most important types of search engine optimization marketing:

### On-Page SEO

[On-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/) is the process of optimizing webpages to improve their search visibility. It focuses largely on using [keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords/) effectively.

Keywords are relevant search terms you want the page to rank for. They help show search engines and users that your content is relevant to their search.

Incorporating your keywords into the body content and the following locations **when it is natural to do so** is best practice:

- [**Title tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/): The HTML page title that can appear in SERPs
- [**Meta description**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/meta-description/): The HTML page summary that can appear in SERPs
- [**H1 tag**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/h1-tag/): The HTML element that contains the on-page title
- [**Subheading tags**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/header-tag/): HTML tags that contain subheadings (H2s, H3s, etc.)
- [**Alt text**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/): HTML image descriptions
- [**URL slug**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-a-url-slug/): The unique part of the webpage address

Avoiding keyword stuffing—forcing in keywords where they do not belong—is also important because Google considers this a spammy practice. Violating [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) can lead to a penalty that harms your search visibility.

### Off-Page SEO

[Off-page SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/off-page-seo/) refers to actions you can take **outside** of your website to improve its search visibility.

It most commonly involves [**link building**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building/), which is the process of getting other high-quality websites to link to your website.

These links are called [**backlinks**](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-backlinks/), and they can help your SEO by signaling that your website is useful and trustworthy.

![external websites provide backlinks that allow users to click through to your site](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0b/72/0b72b7e4c8c5fba65e9cdcd871b8c60c/b41801b607ded199eb0f2c0be7d719d8/AD_4nXc7jt4eGCnzVxM755sjiQjVm4emji9pBi25gdhBao_njIthqLBJy83CuKrKZ4vIvY4xXKb_cfjjq1No6dKp1lchnCTg0VHj8slomvGP-3PEEY9DI_nU9rttR3bfQL0blzohE2tifg.png)

Other off-page tactics like [social media management](https://www.semrush.com/blog/social-media-management/), [online reputation management](https://www.semrush.com/blog/online-reputation-management/), and [email marketing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/email-marketing/) can indirectly benefit your SEO by driving brand awareness and engagement.

### Technical SEO

[Technical SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/) involves improving the technical aspects of your website to enhance its search visibility.

Ensuring that search engines can crawl and index your website properly is the most important aspect of technical SEO. This may involve:

- Using [meta robots tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/robots-meta/) and [robots.txt](https://www.semrush.com/blog/beginners-guide-robots-txt/) to control the behavior of search engine bots
- Creating an [XML sitemap](https://www.semrush.com/blog/xml-sitemap/) to help search engines understand your site structure
- Building [internal links](https://www.semrush.com/blog/internal-links/) to assist search bots and users in navigating your site

After ensuring proper crawling and indexing, focus on user experience factors such as page speed, website security, and mobile-friendliness.

### Image SEO

[Image SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/image-seo/) is the process of optimizing images on your website to make them appear more prominently in search results and enhance your overall SEO performance.

For example, image optimization can help you gain exposure like this:

![A carousel shows tent size images from various sites at the top of the SERP.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/24/d1/24d1120d51d6cbc02b6c17c65d968b89/b5d334dd7b27fcd23ffa1bc2f155ca14/AD_4nXcyGeUJSgm8TnKQtqtJ6Mf0fgvSGZJ2ZVt7ZfzuLQm7xl0dTbdnpLGjvQSk2BTYp4dsk69TT5hHQMgcaYTMDO-NSMg1adOh7MUBFIaFQtTDtHUHZ3V4Tm4rS-S-5V8vl0zYOOCT.png)

Common image SEO tactics include:

- Resizing and compressing images to improve page load speed
- Adding [alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/) (HTML image descriptions) to help search engines and users with screen readers understand what images depict
- Ensuring images are visually appealing and useful to your audience

According to [Semrush Sensor](https://www.semrush.com/sensor/) data from September 2024, Google displays images in the top 20 results for over half of U.S. SERPs. Many users also go directly to Google Images.

Therefore, image SEO provides a significant opportunity to drive traffic to your website.

### Video SEO

[Video SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/video-seo/) is the process of creating and optimizing videos for maximum visibility in search results. This includes results on search engines like Google and video platforms like YouTube.

![Videos listed on the SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f9/98/f99827d8f65282dd47ad11628ae38e91/b9743eda82f0700851ca81a63c16e8cd/AD_4nXdiu-5TvGPCKXMqg--VHTBXGOq7Z-I2ibWpvcNvwEEatOkrlfpAKPFzvhooWOUHzYIjk70xNYFobvD7n3l8HgxsR8uW9tBV0JExGWFQfydFa_5_36dlg2DZ4q-2nTHmJiykiG4esQ.png)

Common video SEO tactics include:

- Performing [YouTube keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/youtube-keyword-research/) to discover what videos users search for
- Adding keywords to your video title and description
- Using schema markup to help search engines understand embedded videos

### Local SEO

[Local SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/) is the process of improving your visibility in location-based search results.

For example, when someone searches "outdoor store near me" or "[CITY] outdoor store":

![A local search shows relevant places and a map of the area on Google Maps](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/07/93/07936c8fd435e195e7aab1ed85fb1eab/c2ea6087cd1ab1615dd4dde8913eca3f/AD_4nXemXq1Nj9hsqxu5WVIZBmGO9I9nNGJyagmlZB9goU4AGCBJlPPMwSsKhosQbm8HvDDg-4kREOjRErLwlI27kC1W68nhSW9JkhSWttGc1ABhifAkJ-0d0gJScDPE3Y1KMCQBTTHRFg.png)

Local SEO is important if you serve customers in a specific area or have brick-and-mortar locations people can visit.

One of the most important factors in local SEO is optimizing your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) because Google evaluates and ranks profiles (rather than webpages) in map-based results.

![A "place" is expanded to show its Google Business Profile.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5e/4d/5e4d554ad73cf274ffba5e73d9fd2335/98b61824c86962b8b8fb3570f81887c1/AD_4nXcK79z_uStouEIrRWWhiU64HMkNCKpTSADwjJar__6H_YZrWMb-EJN8gyItWYntt24yEChgABh5v5ePGWGQAOVfR9nJY-tEr1PoB4nJROFI29nW0pLHDvmqENXRpJu90hn3GNkTCA.png)

Other tactics include:

- Listing your business in online directories
- Encouraging and responding to Google reviews
- Publishing localized content on your website

### Create Quality Content

To rank prominently for your target keywords, you need to create [quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/)—accurate and original content that satisfies search intent.

For example, people who search "best [PRODUCT TYPE]" are typically looking for product recommendations, reviews, and comparisons to help them decide which specific product to purchase.

High-ranking brands address that intent by conducting in-depth tests and sharing their findings in a well-written and well-presented blog post:
