# How to measure SEO share of voice using Semrush

Rankings alone don’t tell you how much visibility your brand is actually capturing in search.

You could rank for thousands of keywords and still miss a large portion of your market’s search demand.

That’s why many marketers track SEO share of voice alongside rankings and traffic.

In this guide, we’ll show you how to measure SEO share of voice in Semrush across traditional SEO, local SEO, and AI search.

## What is SEO share of voice?

SEO share of voice (SoV) measures how much organic search visibility your brand captures compared to competitors.

It shows the percentage of estimated organic traffic (across a defined set of keywords) that your site earns versus other ranking sites.

![img-semblog](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1b/4a/1b4a6640e8ae5fb2cc4febfd10de4872/316edae7a2d11c40c831e13410bb3af1/image.png)

For example, if your tracked keywords generate an estimated 100,000 clicks per month and your site captures 6,000, your share of voice would be 6%.

Semrush offers multiple share of voice tools to measure your visibility across traditional SEO, local SEO, and AI search.

We’ll cover these later in the article.

## Why does SEO share of voice matter?

SEO share of voice helps you understand how visible your brand is in search compared to competitors based on actual traffic potential.

That’s important because simpler metrics (like total keyword rankings or keywords in top positions) can be misleading.

Those counts treat a keyword with 10 monthly searches the same as one with 100,000.

So, you could rank for thousands of keywords and still capture only a small portion of your market’s total search demand.

Share of voice accounts for search volume, which makes it a stronger indicator of your visibility across the real demand in your market.

It helps you:

- Understand how much of your market’s search demand you own compared to competitors
- Identify gaps where competitors are capturing more high-value traffic
- Measure the impact of your SEO efforts over time

First, create a project for your site by clicking “**+ Create project**” and add:

- The domain, subdomain, folder, or URL you want to track
- Your target search engine
- Your chosen device type (desktop or mobile)
- Your location (country, city, street, or ZIP code)

## Frequently asked questions about SEO share of voice

### 1. How does Semrush calculate SEO share of voice?

SEO share of voice is based on the estimated organic traffic your site receives from your tracked keywords compared to the total available organic traffic from those keywords.

This estimate takes into account keyword rankings, keyword search volume, SERP features, and expected click-through rates for each position.

### 2. What’s a good SEO share of voice percentage?

There’s no universal benchmark for a “good” share of voice percentage because it’s a comparative score that depends on your industry, competition, and keyword set.

In general, a higher share of voice means your brand captures more visibility and potential traffic within your market.

Instead of focusing on a specific number, monitor whether your share of voice is growing over time compared to your market competitors.

### 3. How is SEO share of voice different from share of search?

SEO share of voice measures your visibility across a defined keyword set based on rankings, search volume, and estimated traffic.

Share of search usually measures how often people search for your brand name compared to competitors.

In other words, share of voice focuses on search visibility, while share of search focuses on brand demand.

### 4. Can you track competitors’ share of voice in Semrush?

Yes. Semrush lets you compare your share of voice against competitors in tools like Position Tracking, Map Rank Tracker, and the AI Visibility Toolkit.

This helps you identify which competitors capture the most visibility for your target topics and locations.

### 5. How does Semrush calculate AI share of voice?

AI share of voice is based on how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers and how prominently it appears compared to competitors.

This includes factors like brand mentions, placement within responses, and topic visibility across AI platforms.

Brands that appear more frequently and more prominently in AI-generated answers tend to have a higher AI share of voice.
