# Is technical SEO hard?

No, technical SEO isn’t hard. The fundamentals, such as making your pages easy for search engines to access, [fixing broken links](https://www.semrush.com/blog/broken-link/), and [improving page speed](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-improve-website-performance/), are straightforward. With a few simple tools and hands-on practice, you can build these essential technical SEO skills over time.

As you go deeper into technical SEO, you may face more complex tasks. Completing tasks such as [optimizing your site’s architecture](https://www.semrush.com/blog/website-structure/), fine-tuning how pages load, and making code-level improvements may require deeper knowledge of web technologies and best practices. At this more advanced stage, having basic coding skills or working with a developer can help.

By having patience, continuously learning, and seeking expert support when needed, you can steadily gain confidence and skill in all areas of technical SEO.
