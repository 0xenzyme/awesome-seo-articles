# 42 YouTube Stats to Know in 2025

## The Value of YouTube in 2024

Whether you have a channel or are running paid ads, [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) has become the place to be for building brand awareness, connecting with users, and growing sales.

With [2.49 billion monthly logged-in users](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-october-global-statshot), YouTube is the second-most popular social media channel.

From 2022 to 2023, its [global advertising revenues](https://www.statista.com/statistics/289658/youtube-global-net-advertising-revenues/) increased by 8% to hit about $31.5 billion.

And creators take home approximately [55% of the revenue generated](https://influencermarketinghub.com/how-much-do-youtubers-make/) on their channels.

These numbers largely stem from people turning to social media apps like YouTube for entertainment and trusted information.

So much so that Google has added a dedicated “Perspectives” filter to surface relevant social media content to searchers.

![“Perspectives” filter on Google SERP](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0e/84/0e84f77d7132f507ef22d9156e923a7e/4ca6b0a94b1e2525e8fdb4d1712bcce1/9TDhVgqCSJgyRsJHyvwFAHpK5EIPxxNLftgnlVpjNXWETfpIF7sppx-gfo-ec5gXqvvep7CWkZCsKiRP17bNkhsNTr6jbimQ9PexgWQgdwk-HG7-z6TxN7ZcfVhJTLDgbgJFjIDTw4GS.jpeg)

And, as of February 2024, YouTube videos now appear on [search engine results pages](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp/) (SERPs) for over 791.3 million search queries in the United States alone—with a clear upward trend in recent months:

![Positions overview for "youtube.com" shown in Semrush's Organic Rankings tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e0/d3/e0d381bc5488476f364ea85f8fad79ff/21a1adeb1d50870bbbd2d5ab83a46351/5kWAvahGDTqbiPEcYOqAfK-YLTqnZfuiccK_L04G_TsyWs5W3xisUfa5TVi03I9783tVVB8d9P6PpcHWz1B0sNzhrKF_v641KBEijWdC5Whovnw8sJR4K8S1Wt3V5_yLQnZhKRdFnrLU.jpeg)

Check out our full list of recent YouTube stats that emphasize the platform's massive reach and marketing potential. As well as key demographics, organic search trends, and more.

## YouTube Growth Stats

YouTube continues to be one of the world’s most popular online platforms. For both individual creators and marketers, this popularity means significant opportunities to grow their audiences.

- YouTube is the second-most visited website in the world ([Semrush, 2023](https://www.semrush.com/website/top/))

![A list of most visited websites in the world in January 2024](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e5/73/e5733d381116dc92ce55ec234ceec413/40c504c622b9f2d01fdeca47f711856c/pbQI549Gjn8fvqpVmzEwH0mYFZglmiNjkam197nMyirGLYY0JQRdHRCrGP7ipevS-gGK3bft5qic0Yw0_raMjmt6SUTnNKU5ZJpdkGFF89CZsCEYihyNefnnuOFa-Nm1cqYiOQe6vXvw.jpeg)

Its viewership has increased by around 500 million users since 2022 ([TechCrunch, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/25/google-says-2-billion-logged-in-monthly-users-are-watching-youtube-shorts/)).
- Over 500 hours of content are uploaded every minute on the platform ([YouTube](https://blog.youtube/press/))
- On average, YouTube Shorts get over 70 billion daily views ([YouTube](https://blog.youtube/press/))

## YouTube Use Stats

Understanding how frequently (and how long) viewers visit YouTube can help you determine the length, volume, and format of your videos.

- Through April and June, Android users spent a monthly average of 27 hours and 26 minutes on the YouTube app ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-october-global-statshot))
- Android users opened YouTube on 63.4% of all days between April and June ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-october-global-statshot))
- In 2023, the average YouTube visit length across devices was nearly 36 minutes ([Semrush Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/overview?dateFrom=2023-01-01&displayDate=2023-12-01&q=youtube.com))
- 87% of surveyed users said they watched at least four different YouTube formats (e.g., Shorts, live streams) over the past year ([YouTube, 2023](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/documents/YouTube_Culture__Trends_Report_2023.pdf))
- Global viewers collectively watch an average of 1 billion hours of YouTube content per day on TVs ([YouTube, 2024](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/2024-letter-from-neal/))

## YouTube Traffic Stats

YouTube gets a ton of traffic. We found out just how much and where people were coming from using [Domain Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview/) and [Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/):

- On average, YouTube receives around 8.8 billion visits every month through organic searches ([Semrush Domain Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview/?q=youtube.com&searchType=domain))
- YouTube had 1.3 trillion visits in 2023, with direct traffic serving as the top source (83.6%) ([Semrush Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/overview/?displayDate=2023-12-01&dateFrom=2023-01-01&q=youtube.com))
- YouTube had 183 billion visits from organic searches in 2023 ([Semrush Traffic Analytics)](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/overview/?displayDate=2023-12-01&dateFrom=2023-01-01&q=youtube.com)
- Mobile traffic made up around 88% of 2023 traffic. And desktop traffic made up approximately 12% ([Semrush Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/overview?dateFrom=2023-01-01&displayDate=2023-12-01&q=youtube.com)).
- With 270.8 billion visits, the United States was the top traffic source to YouTube among countries in 2023 ([Semrush Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/geo?displayDate=2023-12-01&dateFrom=2023-01-01&q=youtube.com))

## YouTube Paid Advertising Stats

With so many eyes on YouTube videos and access to detailed ad performance data, YouTube is the advertising platform of choice for many brands and marketers.

- YouTube received 598.3 million visits from paid social and paid search traffic ([Semrush Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/overview/?displayDate=2023-12-01&dateFrom=2023-01-01&q=youtube.com), 2023)
- On average, YouTube received around 21.6 million visits every month from paid traffic ([Semrush Domain Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/overview/?q=youtube.com&searchType=domain))
- YouTube generated $31.51 billion in worldwide ad revenue in 2023. This is nearly 8% higher than the 2022 revenue of $29.2 billion. ([Statista, 2023](https://www.statista.com/statistics/289658/youtube-global-net-advertising-revenues/))
- With an audience of 462 million users, India had the largest YouTube ad reach [(DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-october-global-statshot))
- YouTube ads can potentially reach 30.9% of the world's population ([DataReportal, 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2023-october-global-statshot))
- In comparison to using horizontal videos in YouTube Shorts, using verticals in [Video Action Campaigns](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10147229?hl=en) can lead to between 10 to 20% more conversions ([YouTube, 2023](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/2023-ads-on-youtube/))
