# 11 Best Keyword Tracking Tools for 2025 (Tried & Reviewed)

There are lots of SEO keyword tracking tools. And picking the best one for you can be challenging.

In this article, we’ll cover 11 keyword tracking tools on the market and help you find the one that fits your needs. Here's a quick overview before we dive in:

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No | Tool | Key feature | Key limitation | Pricing |
| 1 | Semrush | Potential Growth metric — an AI traffic prediction metric that grades keywords by how much traffic they could bring you, so you know which ones to prioritize. | Higher entry-level pricing than other budget-friendly options on the list | 7-day free trial, then from $139.95/mo |
| 2 | Ahrefs | Parent Topic feature that shows when multiple pages could be consolidated to rank for a broader, higher-traffic keyword. | Weekly updates only, and there's no free trial. | From $129/mo |
| 3 | SE Ranking | Content Score that compares your page to top-ranking competitors and tells you exactly what to optimize. | Lacks the depth of more established platforms. | 14-day free trial, then from $65/mo |
| 4 | AccuRanker | Autocorrect tracking that captures your rankings even when users misspell your brand name, so the data reflects how Google actually treats the query. | Pricing scales steeply as you add more keywords. | 14-day free trial, then from $129/mo |
| 5 | Serpstat | SERP storm metric that signals when ranking changes are likely caused by an algorithm update rather than your own optimizations. | The interface can feel dense for newer users. | 7-day free trial, then from $69/mo |

## 11 Best Keyword Tracking Tools

I tracked 30 keywords for each tool (except for Google Search Console) to see which features I liked best and how each tool compares.

Here’s what I found:

### 1. Semrush

- **Best for**: Marketers, SEOs, and agency professionals who want accurate, in-depth data that’s easy to understand and personalized to their sites
- **Free basic version available**: Yes
- **Pricing**: Pro ($139.95/month), Guru ($249.95/month), and Business ($499.95/month)
- **Free trial period**: Seven days

Semrush’s [Position Tracking](https://www.semrush.com/position-tracking/) tool lets you monitor daily search engine ranking changes across a set of custom keywords for specific devices and locations (down to the ZIP code). And it integrates with Google’s Looker Studio for those who want to build custom reports.

Besides displaying standard ranking changes, Position Tracking:

- Shows you how your competitors are performing
- Highlights [keyword cannibalization](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-cannibalization-guide/) issues (when multiple pages rank for the same keyword and have the potential to harm your rankings)
- Shows which [SERP features](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp-features-guide/) (e.g., featured snippets, local packs, AI Overviews, etc.) appear for a keyword and which ones you rank for
- Gives you metrics like [share of voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/share-of-voice-how-to-increase/) and visibility to help you understand your overall organic performance
- Lets you tag keywords to organize and keep track of large datasets

And you can set custom email alerts that ping you when certain rankings change.

Get started with Position Tracking by following our [configuration steps](https://www.semrush.com/kb/548-configuring-position-tracking).

Once the tool is ready, visit the “**Landscape**” report for a summary of your progress.

You’ll see your top keywords. And which keywords had a positive and negative impact (the keywords that saw the biggest increases or drops in rankings during a given period).

![Semrush's keyword tracking tool shows top keywords, those negatively impacted, and overall visibility metrics.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ad/7b/ad7bcace86d9c0147d3615e1cba73dca/1f0b21f4c6f08d335fca7f53be9728ea/AD_4nXe_z7BmtWXC9ClgUy5ASY3Xr0mY4F3Tvp4JWq-rRYaAv0DRmUv3nFu911Vg1RKFtfLtVg5YBkc1Dv3fFTGV76nL6z-a9T-jd8MFNgNVPWyNkJ4KnPw5mfin02B6Xe2UG-pcNIYBBg.png)

#### What I Like

Semrush’s Potential Growth is an AI traffic prediction metric that grades keywords based on their potential to bring you traffic.

Sorting keywords by “**Your Pot. Growth**” helps you spot keywords to prioritize based on how much your traffic could increase by optimizing for each keyword.

![Potential growth appears as a column in the keyword tracking table and the visibility graph.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/5f/a4/5fa45de090b04a44f8dcafd1c9abb98b/6893d66230529d2205e70b3e1d911f12/AD_4nXcovVU_vvLCDKlVZld-Oe6N3tpgPqpFqKvKpGdH4bY39hsYjj0WVUqvlfOmIYbac0z7w82cgOMWKqTJSDVRKJCt6Oft0zm1_D8Wbjrz8VFg2CsnRKe5WRW45cN64inQwL1twNvtDw.png)

And the “SERP Features” chart (under the “**Landscape**” tab) gives you a quick assessment of your SERP feature performance.

![SERP features are tracked for keywords and shows how many the domain ranks for.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/30/38/303815af5c0d30f27c9726cf42cf74df/80ac6a816e52e89c7a8801af003974c4/AD_4nXeHwjC5caUBTlTwziXcppcsdgdMjLq8hj7u3dqrc0e3vvOf9SAguuBF3U7lKxTXLuhv6jbzqbTSayaGmQ0JXJ9QwZ74dTtK9HsmyXe3Q6opbkw8fkJ4ddYAErzRMV-m3uy-VXR_ig.png)

Simply click on a SERP feature bar to view all of its keywords—both the ones you rank for and the ones you don’t.

### 2. Ahrefs

- **Best for**: SEOs who want rich keyword data
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Lite ($129/month), Standard ($249/month), Advanced ($449/month), and Enterprise ($1,499/month)
- **Free trial period**: No

Ahrefs’ [Rank Tracker](https://ahrefs.com/rank-tracker) is a keyword tracking tool that gives you weekly updates for each keyword—along with an overall summary—including metrics like [share of voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/share-of-voice-how-to-increase/), average position, estimated traffic, and SERP features.

![The keyword tracking tool shows a table for the keywords and their metrics.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/4b/c7/4bc70ea67dc6a5c3c1bbe2133e810184/8ca286b1ae9517f6172b6306c32b611a/AD_4nXeDTxjSqMUGiJP7eBVpPEX6209FBudDCGXPIMcbFAi49sZM78kpicUMpPX8N1peWqzB4rMUf9mbqxXQiMY_rKS4Sh-_37P3AmgYfgUhbQUUqlfMvM7_CFMxuIImwYMOBiHFEqHGXQ.png)

You can track local keywords down to the ZIP code level. And you can compare yourself against competitors to potentially spot opportunities where you can outrank your rivals.

![A competitor is added to the table for comparison.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9a/8a/9a8adc623d9a6312d0f2b247245fa6ed/8deb29f336fe08bc7295e1586124d9b0/AD_4nXdE7VCEF9aTbHpG84h7PlSmaLlUWAdTMGNfZMGwDZiAoZXW_fAeBOaDV3xSZ8LhHjmg8nlCo0gNKA1GBPFgNWuZUwLCH5fRJmBkvPzbsmkOEEH5NHWViNzy-zvhBoXq-9a4MAfKmg.png)

Ahrefs’ Rank Tracker includes information on SERP features like featured snippets, local packs, and knowledge panels.

You can also group and review keywords with custom tags. And toggling on “Multiple URLs” shows potential keyword cannibalization issues.

![The keyword table shows potential issues and the URL in question.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/91/70/91702f349f8efc40a39728f5ebf5bae9/7247b5582a65735612b3285ac60c84fe/AD_4nXesTyiZcXhYQTwYg8Uhq4RSAwpBkF_t4ggGGebpaCLabRx5-uyX6dEL_jJxPkVdEanOS5x3pazPD4GEJz6JAuGKJzJ7QkrCu5GrSRn0Rz_lkMV9ZrMCj96pP1GfVGEDitClfZ5v.png)

#### What I Like

I like how Ahrefs’ keyword tracking tool includes a “Parent Topic” column, which helps users consider whether they can optimize a page for a broader keyword to capture more traffic.

### 3. SE Ranking

- **Best for**: SEOs who need hyper-frequent rank data (e.g., ecommerce, news, or teams running time-sensitive SEO campaigns)
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Essential ($65/month), Pro ($119/month), and Business ($259/month)
- **Free trial period**: 14 days

SE Ranking’s [Rank Tracker](https://seranking.com/position-tracking.html) helps you monitor your rankings across Google, Bing, Yahoo, and YouTube.

You can see which keywords you’re tracking, how their positions change over time, and whether your pages appear in SERP features.

You’re able to track keywords using countries, regions, or ZIP codes.

And a drop-down next to each keyword reveals its ranking history. So you can evaluate performance at a glance.

### 4. AccuRanker

- **Best for**: SEOs with large keyword datasets
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Starts at $129 for 1,000 keywords
- **Free trial period**: 14 days

[AccuRanker](https://www.accuranker.com/) is a keyword tracking tool that offers keyword position monitoring for your site and competing sites with daily updates.

Like other keyword tracking tools, you get metrics for each keyword like average position, SERP features, and share of voice. AccuRanker also has reports for tracking your keywords within AI Overviews.

![AI Overview report shows historical keyword count, distribution of URLs in AI Overviews, top urls, top domains, and more.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/55/b9/55b93f70c519603e9ec146419631c77d/ed3779e7c518b07a3df48d9997be1498/AD_4nXc8j_E_psgiFBDoeatauQSV-KNxt2mn_u9Ch7_8XztImTP0UEJS6GvNP18bt3xzx891C9jnmP9jBKfu1xSC2I331hG56YiDwPaPnVCV-RNmyQ2mVV3zRytQ_1v5FyMNq1BR9FepuQ.png)

You can also see how many pixels your site appears from the top of the SERP. Giving you a clearer picture of how visible your listing really is.

![Keyword table shows metrics for each.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c6/0a/c60ace7d5769f29f298b6308179cc14b/d5142b69dc7af49002fa6c9a9211ec84/AD_4nXfQ_pHdURQGAXnrE7vV4AiZw72Kyg5busc-97FtfM99HIs5iH4nofD9vUulGexh42f0wgVvUqPqZtyho1TWLquweVmscjL7PXtXM3ySlU5P4Kxb4uzoNZNu3Kz_gSzma8kAXXWGyQ.png)

This adds important context to your rankings. Because even if you’re in position two, you might still be buried below ads or other SERP features.

### 5. Serpstat

- **Best for**: Teams managing large websites with extensive content silos
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Individual ($69/month), Team ($129/month), and Agency ($499/month)
- **Free trial period**: Seven days

Serpstat’s [Keyword Rank Tracker](https://serpstat.com/rank-tracker/) tracks keywords across devices and countries. It also lets you monitor your site against the competition.

You can see metrics like position, visibility, and ranking changes.

![Project overview shows keywords in the top spot, top 2-3, and so on as well as a keyword ranking table.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f2/a2/f2a2e62ee36aeb57cc30d9f333f3435b/783247a91a239de21d52c8da70fed735/AD_4nXcATpGh2JszMDR08YjUgTG6EzJY261zbW-N7aU91E1TjQunKt3dGnkjfW5F_BeTxSvjv5abdURCIolZ3oPQq02lqmeeLHP9G-DjKnJxR4hJTpOaYe2RvedDcdMz-vKMCT0D2syN.png)

Serpstat also tracks SERP features like AI Overviews. And tells you whether you or another domain holds available SERP features.

You can choose to get keyword updates daily, on certain days of the week, or monthly.

And Serpstat’s “URL in SERP” column lets you manually enter the URL for the page you want to rank for, so you can compare it to which page is actually ranking. To help you identify pages with keyword cannibalization.

![URL in SERP column is highlighted in the keyword ranking table.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d8/3b/d83bfc2944b9787f146ed4d5ba7c0b0d/4c6c1a6756db870d6f74f572e45731fd/AD_4nXdkdx6OZ8d53yEVDbWi-yGRJ2-MABIEOpBSNoe9yAsjm5fq4f1yjDEAg7VHP0R1XJFQDvIjREKCUud_2_8ZNayqDn8At1Gj54rmejipSYsmEhNeNtlWyOkuFlF-r8cufV-Y0XE2og.png)

#### What I Like

Serpstat’s storm metric can help you figure out if a fluctuation in your rankings is from an algorithm update or something else.

“Keyword storms” indicate how much the search engine results have changed for a given keyword over time. A higher percentage (greater than 40%) means more rankings have shifted. While a lower percentage means the results have remained relatively stable.

So, say you notice your rankings start fluctuating for an important page. Reviewing the SERP storm metric might give you clues as to why.

![Keyword storm area is highlighted in the tool and shows a line graph.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b2/60/b260372b0cf7c655e7d26ad3bc17877b/1847eaf3b12cf14c2db27f7ddb66b8f0/AD_4nXej50f8SI8-y_XEI1J5aG3SH1wrxhU_1OcUWcqwHLs5Rv3BHKqN6zQnY8BmiXxvDtMB5WZvDbJDHCTi5Ah4dwZcIl-K5TMYTQCHAnt9FDjY-XGL_7oYNPXFgK01z9JDBPFAZXgw.png)

You can also visit Google’s [Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/products/rGHU1u87FJnkP6W2GwMi/history) to confirm a suspected update. And sometimes Google will publish what they hope to accomplish with updates on [their blog](https://developers.google.com/search/blog). So you can adjust impacted content.

So you can see how your site has performed for your keywords over time—even before you started tracking them. This can make it easier to spot long-term trends. And measure your past SEO efforts.

![For a keyword, we see the position over time.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2d/86/2d86c9da873733f18d4cf88a144015f3/a629207ac40350e1db9850573b7283fc/AD_4nXfERW405sBunVeCZjaCXyvX1F1whLolTkPnrA4GzL7zVH59oCdv6wIQkcBa1vb508MKDyvrfWTTCT1mloxo3hlufbwObkWVomb6ObPZgOYANarQW1j6YOOuo_dIHtxvkOsZT_ys.png)

### 7. Moz Pro

- **Best for**: Beginners and teams that can stay on track with weekly updates
- **Free basic version available**: No
- **Pricing**: Starter ($49/month), Standard ($99/month), Medium ($179/month), and Large ($299/month)
- **Free trial period**: Seven days

## Pick a Tool to Start Keyword Tracking

Choosing a keyword tracking tool depends on your goals, budget, team size, and other specifics.

Here are some tips that can help:

1. **Clarify your goals.** Decide how many terms you need to track, whether you need to monitor specific campaigns, and whether you need to generate reports for clients.
2. **Define your budget.** Factor in the base cost, plus any extras like multiple users, integrations, or advanced reporting features.
3. **Match the tool to your team.** Prioritize simplicity if you’re a beginner. Look for features like API access, unlimited users, and role-based permissions if you work in a larger team.
4. **Start with a trial.** Take advantage of free trials to explore the interface, test reporting speed, and evaluate data quality.

Additionally, look for tools that provide timely updates.

The SERPs can change often, as seen in Semrush Sensor.

![Semrush Sensor tool shows SERP volatility for the last 30 days across all categories.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c5/f2/c5f2562ab7bed96da737663c3267d726/4f3de44fc9821b458abf6e941214bb1d/AD_4nXdxFxfBvX724qzU281ty9ybKYlQr90uqxtmniKapXM42il2vggyJ5lWvrFp78_HIj1_xS-YtcCdonKBga1Hi0WCHMF2-VLOSbgXBDq6xepkN4LpccMiKCFiiHtXpAYtXbE88SCI4A.png)
