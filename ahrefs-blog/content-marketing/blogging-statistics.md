# 73 Blogging Statistics for 2024

Are you curious about the state of blogging in 2024? Then look no further.

We’ve curated, vetted, and categorized a list of up-to-date statistics below.

## Top blogging statistics

These are the most interesting blogging statistics you should know.

1. 71% of marketers report that content marketing has become more important to their organization in the last year. ([Content Marketing Institute](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-research-trends-statistics))
2. There are more than 600 million blogs out of 1.9 billion websites worldwide. ([Web Tribunal](https://hostingtribunal.com/blog/how-many-blogs/))
3. 77% of internet users still read blogs. ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/the-benefits-of-blogging-20-stats-business-owners-need-to-know-infograph/511816/))
4. Nearly 26% of people in the U.K. aged 5–18 are reading blogs ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/299097/blog-reading-by-young-people-in-the-uk/))
5. There is a strong positive correlation between word count and backlinks, but only up to 1,000 words. For posts longer than 1,000 words, there is a strong negative correlation between word count and backlinks. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/blog-post-length/))
6. There is a moderate correlation between content length and organic traffic, but only up to 2,000 words. For posts longer than 2,000 words, there is a moderate negative correlation between word count and organic traffic. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/blog-post-length/))
7. 80% of bloggers are driving strong marketing results. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
8. 50% of bloggers publish weekly or several times per month. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
9. Bloggers who publish the most are most likely to report “strong results”. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
10. 74% of bloggers use 1-3 images per post. 2% of bloggers add 10+ images to a typical blog post. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
11. 59% of links shared on social media are shared without ever being read. ([Chicago Tribune](https://www.chicagotribune.com/business/blue-sky/ct-share-this-link-without-reading-it-ap-bsi-20160618-story.html))

## General blogging statistics

What’s the state of blogging in 2024? Here are some statistics that may surprise you.

1. WordPress powers over 43.2% of the internet. ([W3Techs](https://w3techs.com/))
2. ~70 million new posts are published on WordPress each month. ([WordPress](https://wordpress.com/activity/)).
3. 77 million new comments are added to WordPress posts per month. ([WordPress](https://wordpress.com/activity/)).
4. Tumblr hosts over 518 million blogs, while WordPress hosts over 60 million blogs. ([Web Tribunal](https://webtribunal.net/blog/how-many-blogs))
5. 3 billion blog posts are published each year worldwide. ([GrowthBadger](https://growthbadger.com/blog-stats/))
6. There are over 31 million active bloggers posting at least once per month in the United States. ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/187267/number-of-bloggers-in-usa/))
7. More than half of consumers will stop what they are doing if they encounter issues when viewing content ([Adobe](https://www.slideshare.net/adobe/2019-adobe-brand-content-survey))
8. 44% of buyers say they typically consume three to five pieces of content before engaging with a vendor ([Demand Gen Report](http://e61c88871f1fbaa6388d-c1e3bb10b0333d7ff7aa972d61f8c669.r29.cf1.rackcdn.com/DGR_DG154_SURV_ContentPref_March_2021_Final.pdf))
9. From 2023 to 2027, the global content marketing industry is expected to grow by $584.02 billion. ([ReportLinker](https://www.reportlinker.com/p04793441/Global-Content-Publishing-Market.html))

## Blogging revenue statistics

Do bloggers really earn money? Or is it just a pipe dream? Check out these statistics on blogging revenue.

1. The most popular monetization method for bloggers is Google AdSense, followed by affiliate marketing. However, for high-income bloggers, AdSense ranks third; these bloggers are 2.5 times more likely to sell their own product or service than use AdSense. ([GrowthBadger](https://growthbadger.com/blog-statistics/))
2. Bloggers make most of their income from ads, affiliate products, sponsored product reviews, selling their own products, and online courses. ([RankIQ](https://www.rankiq.com/most-profitable-blog-niches-study/))
3. 45% of bloggers who earn over $50,000 per year sell their own product or service, while only 8% of lower-income bloggers do that. ([GrowthBadger](https://growthbadger.com/blog-statistics/))
4. 72% of bloggers making $2,000+/month use either Mediavine or Adthrive as their ad management company. ([RankIQ](https://www.rankiq.com/most-profitable-blog-niches-study/))
5. The most profitable niche is the food blog niche. Food bloggers have the highest median monthly income ($9,169) as compared to bloggers from all major niches. ([RankIQ](https://www.rankiq.com/most-profitable-blog-niches-study/))
6. The niches that have the highest percentage of blogs with over 50,000 monthly sessions are food (42.8%), lifestyle (13.3%), and travel (10%). ([RankIQ](https://www.rankiq.com/most-profitable-blog-niches-study/))

## Blogging challenges statistics

How difficult is blogging? What challenges are bloggers going through? Here are some stats.

1. Engagement drops for posts with a reading time longer than seven minutes. ([Medium](https://medium.com/data-lab/the-optimal-post-is-7-minutes-74b9f41509b))
2. The biggest challenges for content marketers is generating leads and measuring ROI. ([Siege Media](https://www.siegemedia.com/strategy/content-marketing-trends))
3. 50% of bloggers struggle to find time and to attract visitors. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
4. 73% of people admit to skimming blog posts, while 27% consume posts thoroughly. ([HubSpot](https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/53/assets/hubspot.com/research/reports/HubSpot%20Content%20Trends%20-%20Generational%20Fault%20Lines.pdf?t=1511049274375))
5. 75% of the public prefers reading articles under 1,000 words. ([Contently](https://contently.com/2019/01/14/mid-range-blog-post/))

## Blog content statistics

With so much competition, plus AI, bloggers need to create higher-quality content. Check out these statistics on content and quality.

1. The average blog post takes 3 hours and 51 minutes to write. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
2. 35% of bloggers who spend 6+ hours on a typical article report “strong results.” ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
3. The average blog post length is 1427 words long. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
4. Only 3% of bloggers regularly publish 2,000+ word article. Most bloggers publish articles in the 500-1500 word range. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
5. The longer the typical blog post, the more likely the blogger is to report “strong results.” ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
6. 76% of bloggers publish educational content. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
7. 25% of bloggers use video. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
8. 47% of bloggers are conducting original research. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
9. Nearly 3x more bloggers work with an editor now than in 2014. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
10. A business that spends more than $4,000 or more per post is about 2.6 times as likely to report its strategy as “very successful” than a business that spends $0-$500. ([Siege Media](https://www.siegemedia.com/strategy/content-marketing-trends))
11. 62.3% of content marketers are creating interactive content as part of their content marketing strategy. ([Siege Media](https://www.siegemedia.com/strategy/content-marketing-trends))

## Blogging SEO statistics

To get consistent traffic to your blog, you first need to get your article indexed and ranking in the search engines.

1. 68% of online experiences begin with a search engine. ([BrightEdge](https://videos.brightedge.com/research-report/BrightEdge_ChannelReport2019_FINAL.pdf))
2. 96.55% of pages get no organic search traffic from Google. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-traffic-study/))
3. Only 1.94% of all pages get between one and ten monthly search visits from Google. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-traffic-study/))
4. The top-ranking page gets the most search traffic only 49% of the time. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ranking-number-one-is-overrated/))
5. There’s a positive correlation between the number of websites linking to a page and its search traffic. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-traffic-study/))
6. There’s a positive correlation between the number of websites linking to a page and its ranking position. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-traffic-study/))
7. Only 5.7% of pages will rank in the top 10 search results within a year of publication. ([Ahrefs](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank-in-google-and-how-old-are-top-ranking-pages/))
8. The average top-ranking page also ranks in the top 10 search results for nearly 1,000 other relevant keywords. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/also-rank-for-study/))
9. There’s no correlation between Flesch Reading Ease scores and ranking positions. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/flesch-reading-ease/))
10. 68% of SEOs use content as a way to build links. ([Aira](https://aira.net/state-of-link-building/))
11. 74% of bloggers are updating old content. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
12. Bloggers who pay more attention to keywords more often, are more likely to report success. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
13. 66% of bloggers are driving traffic to their content via SEO. ([Orbit Media](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/))
