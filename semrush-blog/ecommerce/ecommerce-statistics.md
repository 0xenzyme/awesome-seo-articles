# 31 Ecommerce Statistics to Know About in 2025

If you sell products or services online, knowing the facts about ecommerce is key.

Why?

Because it can help you understand what the competition is up to, reveal untapped opportunities, and more.

With that in mind, here are the top 31 ecommerce statistics.

## General Ecommerce Industry Statistics

1. There are more than 27 million ecommerce websites on the internet. Shopify is the most popular and is used by more than 5 million websites. ([BuiltWith](https://trends.builtwith.com/shop/traffic/Entire-Internet), 2024)

![Pie chart displaying the top ecommerce platforms used around the world](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/43/dc/43dc03afed13426c808f8827624da9ba/e895fbfb5b1eb06e416675a71dd7cb4b/AD_4nXd7EC-f5TsRBW4CDAsaTa62UorDrYY0BX68dR1_dvuxNpadJg5TuvwbLRTPWxULYagqvc2bGvgKkPB6Pm7mN6cMQoxGPkgWiKda-CGqY8LA8VHaKzkG2iiqnlRfc5j-ry0xmnX4GUz0xnrquyYbg-Pugkxm.png)

**Image Data Source**: BuiltWith

2. Global ecommerce sales reached an estimated $5.8 trillion in 2023. And Alibaba is the largest ecommerce retailer in the world, with a 23% market share. ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/379046/worldwide-retail-e-commerce-sales/), 2023)
3. Amazon is set to surpass Alibaba in global sales by 2027 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/379046/worldwide-retail-e-commerce-sales/), 2023)
4. Amazon is expected to reach nearly 41% of total ecommerce sales in the U.S. in 2025 ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/us-amazon-ecommerce-forecast-2024), 2024)
5. The average number of products per ecommerce order is 4.41 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1363180/monthly-average-units-per-e-commerce-transaction/), 2024)
6. The average ecommerce conversion rate is 3.52% ([Dynamic Yield](https://marketing.dynamicyield.com/benchmarks/conversion-rate/), 2024)
7. The average ecommerce cart abandonment rate is 70.19% ([Baymard Institute](https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate), 2023)
8. The top reason for cart abandonment is that the additional costs (e.g., shipping fees) are too high ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1228452/reasons-for-abandonments-during-checkout-united-states/), 2024)
9. More than 90% of U.S. shoppers typically look for deals and discounts before making an online purchase ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1154843/us-adults-search-deals-online-purchase-coronavirus/), 2020)
10. Millennials and Gen Z shoppers are more likely to wait for discounts before purchasing something online. Gen X shoppers are the least likely to wait for discounts. ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1395897/shoppers-waiting-for-discounts-before-buying-online-age/), 2023)
11. Free shipping is the most important ecommerce delivery criterion for U.S. shoppers ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/forecasts/1448843/top-e-commerce-delivery-criteria-united-states), 2024)
12. Ecommerce businesses lost $48 billion to fraud in 2023 ([Juniper Research](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/ecommerce-losses-online-payment-fraud-48bn), 2024)

Given the huge footprint Amazon and Alibaba have in the ecommerce space, it’s a good idea to use those types of platforms if it makes sense for your brand.

## Mobile Ecommerce Statistics

13. Smartphone devices accounted for 77% of online retail store visits in Q1 of 2024 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/568684/e-commerce-website-visit-and-orders-by-device/), 2024)
14. Two-thirds of online purchases are made with smartphone devices ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/568684/e-commerce-website-visit-and-orders-by-device/), 2024)
15. Worldwide mobile ecommerce sales reached $2.2 trillion in 2023. And they’re expected to surpass $3.4 trillion by 2027. ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/chart/13139/estimated-worldwide-mobile-e-commerce-sales/), 2023)
16. Mobile ecommerce sales account for 60% of all ecommerce sales globally ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/chart/13139/estimated-worldwide-mobile-e-commerce-sales/), 2023)
17. More than three-quarters of U.S. adults make online purchases using a smartphone ([Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2022/11/21/for-shopping-phones-are-common-and-influencers-have-become-a-factor-especially-for-young-adults/), 2022)

### Online Payment Statistics

28. Digital wallet payments account for about half of all ecommerce payments around the world ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1111233/payment-method-usage-transaction-volume-share-worldwide/), 2023)
29. The estimated transaction value for ecommerce digital wallet payments in 2023 was $3.1 billion ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1111233/payment-method-usage-transaction-volume-share-worldwide/), 2023)
30. Digital wallets are expected to make up 61% of ecommerce transaction value—for a total of $5.4 billion—by 2027 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1111233/payment-method-usage-transaction-volume-share-worldwide/), 2023)
31. PayPal is the most popular ecommerce payment brand among U.S. shoppers ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/forecasts/997132/biggest-e-commerce-payment-brands-in-the-us), 2024)

### What These Stats Reveal

Digital wallets are steadily overtaking credit and debit cards as ecommerce payment options.

If you haven’t done so already, you should work on implementing multiple digital wallet payment options to give customers more ways to pay. And possibly [optimize conversion rates](https://www.semrush.com/blog/conversion-rate-optimization/) among shoppers who aren’t comfortable sharing their credit or debit card information online.
