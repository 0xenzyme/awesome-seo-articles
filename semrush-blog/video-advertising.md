# What Is Video Advertising? + How to Do It Effectively

## What Is Video Advertising?

Video advertising is a paid marketing tactic that uses video content to promote products, services, or brands in an engaging way—often on social media platforms.

For example, here’s a YouTube video ad promoting software company Parallels:

## Why Is Video Advertising Effective?

Video advertising is effective because it captures audience attention more powerfully than text or images.

Specifically, videos can:

- **Boost recall**: Viewers are [20% more likely](https://www.wyzowl.com/power-of-video-ads/) to remember video ads compared to static ads
- **Build trust**: More than [90% of consumers](https://www.wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/) say video quality impacts their trust in a brand
- **Boost engagement**: More than [80% of people](https://www.techsmith.com/2024-video-viewer-trends-report.html) use videos to learn new things
- **Drive sales**: Nearly [85% of marketers](https://www.wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/) report increased sales from video content

## Types of Video Ads

Below are the main types of video ads you can experiment with:

### In-Stream Ads

In-stream ads are videos that play before, during, or after other videos on platforms like [YouTube](https://www.semrush.com/blog/youtube-marketing/).

They come in a few forms: **skippable ads, bumper ads**, and **non-skippable ads.**

**Skippable ads** include a “skip” button that users can click after five seconds:

![A skippable YouTube video ad with the 'Skip' button highlighted on a video about learning to code.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/e7/ee/e7eeaeaf410ce18c07932012dee26e57/427d9709f88afbeb5bc3172e9f700a75/AD_4nXdxNEfMw4Lslc2OaY8TJ9bdBLZMam6itsR6kWvEuXPXuHq3WTlGrrO62H7q7iPqhNacJJC3IGdXnPolir-Hfdefz1eGH7WSMt89Ld4VmQvGGdtahvZ1OgzadIKXq6cC5k9PBbZT.jpeg)

**Bumper ads** last up to six seconds and can't be skipped—perfect for short, memorable messages.

**Non-skippable ads** must be watched in full before the main video. They often run for 15 or 20 seconds (or 30 seconds on TV)

### Social Media Video Ads

[Social media ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/social-media-ads/) appear across platforms like Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn and show in users’ feeds next to organic (unpaid) posts.

Like this ad on Instagram. Unlike normal posts, it has a “Sponsored” label and a “Subscribe” button.

Some social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok also offer **shoppable ads** that let viewers tap on items in the video to purchase them.

### Native Video Ads

Videos that are [native ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/native-ads/) blend in with the surrounding content on social media, websites, or apps.

Native ads have a unique advantage: Instead of standing out as obvious ads, they fit seamlessly into what users are already watching. Which can make these ads seem more trustworthy.

### Over-the-Top Ads

Over-the-top (OTT) ads are similar to TV commercials and appear on streaming services like Netflix, Hulu, and Disney+ to reach viewers during their favorite shows and movies.

These ads usually run 15-30 seconds and can't be skipped.

They're becoming more popular as many consumers have switched from cable TV to streaming services. But unlike traditional TV commercials, they allow you target specific audiences based on viewing habits and demographics.

## 5 Best Practices for Creating Effective Video Ads

Follow these five proven tactics to improve your video ads.

### 1. Understand Your Audience

Defining your [target audience](https://www.semrush.com/blog/target-audience/) is the first step because it informs how you craft your video ads and which platform(s) you choose.

What works for teens on TikTok probably won't work for senior executives on LinkedIn.

To learn more about who you want to reach, start with [audience research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/audience-research/).

You can do this by reading industry reports, distributing surveys, and running focus groups.

To streamline the process, use Semrush’s [One2Target](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/one2target/) tool.

Open the tool, enter at least one competitor’s domain, and click “**Analyze**.”

You’ll first see key insights about the audience, including their age, gender, and location.

![Demographics report on One2Target with the age, gender, and location widgets highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/55/66/556630284930e0b4ee57944c0aff40f6/5ff15bd1e1f504785387c832dcb5bd9b/AD_4nXdre1E1J9eApJ80lOspW9OJ10znD0jSY17bDnIbtcfxfAPSNQ2Zmy4fnz_QFxdmnsQe_J5kz5jJqHjXBkcp9RMhlmDEA5jD5EoYCznMVpOoky11y3oBfVOv5y3ggImVdtSgLp5x.jpeg)

### 2. Hook Viewers in the First 3 Seconds

Grab attention early with bold visuals and strong messaging—and mention your brand early.

For example, you can show your product or service in action, share your biggest benefits, or open with an attention-grabbing question while viewers are most engaged.

### 3. Design Ads for Mobile

Many people watch videos on their phones, so your ads need to be mobile-friendly.

To optimize for mobile viewing:

- Use a vertical format (9:16 aspect ratio) for stories and shorts
- Stick to a resolution of 1080 x 1920 for the best quality
- Keep videos short—five seconds or less
- Make easily readable on small screens

Platforms like Meta Ads Manager let you trim longer videos right in the tool, like this:

!['Trim video' window on Meta Ads Manager with an option to trim video ads directly inside the tool.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/20/5c/205c555f23bbbb57c1109a804684d0c2/f183153071aef24fd81a3f2c3027279b/AD_4nXc7HD7_KA9o_6C3ukPfKdXcDv4TUY1cAwTpWnUw7pG_ILQHSP_i6_BFqpQOMlUqqExTquX4ac_mgFR3y-DH8tmZo5q8hylV_bAQSEaCfx9Pj3KT1ILx7inQKeFnz6xFpsMHZ4z8.jpeg)

### 4. Always Add Subtitles

Include subtitles to ensure your message gets across to users who are watching without sound.

Over [90% of users](https://www.nexttv.com/news/mobile-videos-often-watched-without-audio-study-finds) watch videos without sound, as people browse in public, at work, or in bed—places where they can't or don't want to play audio.

There are two main ways to [add captions to video ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-add-captions-to-video/): automatically or by creating a SubRip subtitle (SRT) file.

If you’re using Meta Ads Manager, click “**Captions**” after you’ve uploaded your video.

From here, you can select “**Generate Automatically**” or upload an SRT file.

!['Captions' window on Meta Ads Manager with options to add subtitles by uploading an SRT file or generating them automatically.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d3/27/d327227524f6c0119039097d14ef9b66/b12435d68ab37a08f026184611479069/AD_4nXfXk6ijcUy_oehNIqA5u3Tpbg-hBIIOwU_VuMWhL6sueC3yGAMB1_PYhyWlD9_c7BsJxBEIhx5rSOpa_pfPieIP8l48gwk3FYwCUskOtk8F6Z-16fQYM2zQHxnP02HwMrNU4rP3wQ.jpeg)
