# Why is a content audit useful?

A content audit is useful because it helps you understand whether your website’s content is relevant to your audience and performs in a way that supports your goals.

Even if you did everything right when you published the original content, things change over time. Search engine algorithms evolve, your business goals can shift, and your audience’s interests and intentions may move in new directions. By regularly reviewing your content, you can adapt to stay competitive.

A good practice is to conduct a content audit at least once a year, or more frequently if your industry changes quickly or you produce a lot of new content.

Typical tasks involved in a content audit include:

- **Inventorying content**. Listing all pages, posts, and media to understand what exists.
- **Evaluating quality**. Checking if content is accurate, readable, and up-to-date to increase the likelihood that visitors will trust and enjoy it.
- **Measuring performance**. Looking at metrics related to traffic, engagement, and conversions to see what works and what doesn’t.
- **Assessing SEO alignment**. Ensuring that your content uses the right keywords, follows best practices, and matches user intent.
- **Identifying gaps**. Uncovering missing topics or content types that your audience wants but can’t find on your site.
