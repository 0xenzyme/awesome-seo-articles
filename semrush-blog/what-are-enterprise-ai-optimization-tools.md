# What are enterprise AI optimization tools?

Enterprise AI optimization tools help large businesses monitor how AI systems describe their brand, benchmark performance against competitors, and identify opportunities to improve visibility in AI results.

Semrush [Enterprise AIO](https://www.semrush.com/lp/enterprise-aio/en/), [Scrunch](https://scrunchai.com/), and [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) are some enterprise AI optimization tools on the market.

These tools can help prepare your business for the next era of marketing. More and more people are using AI chatbots to find information online. If you're able to optimize your brand's presence in AI platforms, you can generate more brand awareness, traffic, and leads for your business.

Here are the key features these enterprise AI optimization tools provide:

- **Brand visibility tracking.** See how often your brand is mentioned in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, and other AI platforms—and in what context it appears.
- **Competitor benchmarking.** Measure your AI visibility against competitors to identify who dominates your category
- **Content citation insights.** Find out which of your web pages are most frequently referenced by AI platforms, showing you what formats and topics perform best.
- **Sentiment monitoring.** Track whether AI platforms describe your brand in a positive, neutral, or negative way, and fix the negative sentiment before it spreads.
- **Gap analysis.** Uncover missing content topics where creating new resources could boost AI visibility.
