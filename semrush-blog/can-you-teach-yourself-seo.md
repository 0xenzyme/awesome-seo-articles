# Can you teach yourself SEO?

Yes, you can teach yourself SEO. Many successful SEOs started their careers by teaching themselves, proving that teaching yourself SEO is not only possible but also practical.

Start by exploring free online guides, tutorial videos, and reputable blogs to build a solid foundation. Then apply what you learn to personal or practice websites. With consistent practice, you can gain confidence in applying [keyword research](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/), [on-page optimization](https://www.semrush.com/blog/on-page-seo/), and [technical SEO strategies](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo/).

Over time, teaching yourself SEO can help you save costs, gain greater control over your website’s growth, and even boost your career prospects.
