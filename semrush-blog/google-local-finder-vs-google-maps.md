# The Difference Between Google Local Finder & Google Maps

Quick Summary

- Google Local Finder and Google Maps serve different purposes, but both are crucial for local SEO
- Local Pack and regular SERP are separate local result types and have unique ranking factors
- Semrush Local simplifies local marketing, helping businesses improve their local presence across search results

Gone are the days of search results being a simple case of positions 1 to 10 on the first page of Google. With more specific user demands, as well as the rise of [mobile device](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/local-mobile-search/) use and voice assistants, [local search](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/local-search-algorithm/) has evolved.

From Local Finder to regular [SERPs](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/reliable-serp-checker/#:~:text=Effective%20local%20SERP%20checkers%20like,organic%20positions%20for%20specific%20keywords.) (search engine results pages), you need to understand how Google presents your business. Let’s break it down:

## Google Local Finder vs. Google Maps

While Google Local Finder and Maps both aim to help users find local businesses, they serve different functions.

**Google Local Finder** provides the most relevant business results based on the user's search query and location. Their discovery intent is a key signal for the results, as the searcher is likely looking for a specific business around them (e.g. “hairdresser nearby”, “thai food near me”).

It features an [image](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/google-business-profile-photo-size/), NAP (name, address, phone number), and average review score pulled from the web and [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/add-business-to-google/). Local Finder also shows business locations pinpointed on a map.

![Google Local Finder results for car mechanics in Charleston.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ee/d9/eed91eeee84e257061c25732bcb25070/5b18ac7a4411865ece5a9a6b6c6d700b/AD_4nXf9Y2YbVbqCV5GjrQ5HyqRuZBGu4w703Bk_DGmDoZF-d3e0qwpQ8WgfDgNw0-kx4t_XASOVHZd6bEyfoeFwNNt16U6swqNS1645eQ-CcvRuOk7N5VVmE_oqj_Efe1NS0B5wO9Sx.png)

Access Local Finder by clicking “more places” on the Local Pack, which appears in the Google SERP.

**Note:**Local Finder works differently in the U.S. and Europe—more on that below.

Meanwhile,[**Google Maps**](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/how-to-boost-your-google-maps-ranking/) is a comprehensive mapping service that provides geographic information, navigation directions, and local business listings.

It primarily focuses on helping users find locations and get directions.

![Google Maps results for the same search as the Local Finder.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2b/ce/2bce79aca35d506da0563cd26cf7bb98/5597ccf96660226265366b90802ef5f5/AD_4nXed5cZFJicTb6UXR-U5jZZOmD4zEz9e9_HRXz2w8crGzxlG0-KxS0LfQf8yazabtkxxblQUDP-vxIM0g16JSOI98gmAwifLgi3NY0HGUBWJKptq0gGWZwWMlJIV_LHlx4pUjX-A.png)

Maps also allows users to view satellite images and explore areas through Street View.

Like Local Finder, Maps results also include local business operating hours, photos, reviews, and contact information. Hence, Maps is also influenced immensely by a business’s Google Business Profile.

Both tools are useful for finding products and services, so businesses should factor both into their local marketing strategy.

## Local Finder vs. Local Pack vs. Organic Results

The Local Pack (also known as the [3-pack](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/google-3-pack/), Map Pack or Snack Pack) is a separate Google Search result type from Local Finder. The two are similar and often confused, but there are some key differences.

The Local Pack appears at the top of the Google SERP when users search for local products and services. It includes a map and three business listings, providing essential information such as ratings, addresses, and operating hours.

![The Google Local Pack.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2f/4f/2f4fc79071f2721c8979ebc628bd3b35/1f3c409c4120af9a150e4e5e5c237f12/AD_4nXeXeTqX0_e1lEqC81p64zodBwFm_LytVpY8xsp2H6W_RmgPZeKPlBEEMxuYG4i037kecOs_DrOwMoDeYUj08dBloYtq04vvLLgXvLygWJ_xrhISWVUn7GmwHY9fTWWlguaAYk5ixA.png)

Good Local Pack rankings display prominently and often receive a high click-through rate. Businesses that appear in the Local Pack benefit from increased visibility and a greater likelihood of attracting local customers.

Clicking “more businesses” on the Local Pack takes users to the Local Finder. Regular organic results appear below the Local Pack. These include websites, directories, and other relevant links based on the search term.

![Organic results for local car mechanics.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b0/dd/b0dda0b7d479fbe742e651cf717d6f98/b6f1eb47e10b5d88e727252575c1cbc8/AD_4nXfmF1iJ8cIc9x4zrMcb1ni2ofBTNZrIY_usBibmzUs6eKd0EMOMGhY_ch8pVL88L_GhrynXkADFUEcPklibAqHZpsTXENKadiyorpnmzEH4jJCwmbjzHYmxycQLJ7auzxv4OPyc2w.png)

To effectively optimize your business for Google local search it is important to understand that there are four types of local search results:

- Local Pack
- Local Finder
- Google Maps
- Localized Organic Search Results

There is a definite overlap in ranking factors between all of these. For example, Local Pack results are very often the top 3 you will find in Local Finder.

However, this is not always the case, and searches with different intentions can show different results for the top 3 in the Local Pack and Local Finder.

For example, a “flower shop near me” search could display different flower shops in the Local Finder based on their hours of operation.

![A flower shop near me search in Key Biscayne, Florida shows three closed shops in the local pack.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/77/5e/775ee15a0e20be6425ba1b1c6d5483e3/eed99414fc5594be5b034e2e32854e42/AD_4nXeLgYuPBuNdaTSX9OnoVCk8iAKmtfk1uVPXBJZ496eD9r9ofOlOFfcXjyr4N8UiS_6LqJOWf4x85zIoxsfx5HrML0TnTmoChNmmUBagf4IBrprZsP3cAgIlcDRptEVqLlG_EIhmQQ.png)![Upon clicking “more places” in the Local Pack, Local Finder highlighted a series of different flower shops near Key Biscayne that were open.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9b/dd/9bddb563fe3243dea08db31db4a67f52/5898a71361a00ed5d886e713eb29ae1f/AD_4nXcVDi7IZwaDkkJ4Nnqs1N1BipMTlwYFD4N1YKyVSoKAiKPueHyNB6p-2xqrXm7k0eyHuG702T7KVpBQK6K9BjBou_k3GyltaK96bwQQh3J6yHaeYHM0LIyujwTcvjNBrd2loUmfJA.png)

## Local Finder in the U.S. vs. Europe

SEO experts around the world are reporting differences in how Local Finder works in the U.S. versus European countries. In our own experiment, we found that desktop U.S. Local Finder results display business details to the left and a map to the right.

![U.S. Google Local Finder results on desktop.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f5/f4/f5f4dcc5a23a4235cd81970ffc8e0465/354d5eca9181775412d6fa392794c1ae/AD_4nXfWHgYgSI3IFASLZYZFlDF3g3CdcIO_fKhvpWgq6l_JWmuQpGQg1qpounkPDPSjwNLk8x6pLeW3S_XE12iaNaoMlwXxmfMt8T2CskU7J2oWBsUy5u0I8bmOhrkQYLAjuFKlrPFB.png)

However, the same search conducted in Madrid essentially resulted in a longer version of the Local Pack. So in essence, [Google Maps doesn’t appear](https://www.thelocal.com/20240307/why-google-searches-in-europe-no-longer-show-maps) in Europe for search anymore.

![European Local Finder results on desktop.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/cf/3e/cf3ec0eceabed8d0cad64dc15b91198f/66f4c8392a28de431818bd4d9e9a48af/AD_4nXd2kj9YvRpKkrcJKY8Wa5QnU8d5irj0JjAvfzBLQ2afSQoveP7lgeEdMcMwLzFnwomQyw8jdFGwLXQ9lL6sUp8DsoK9xMKOG1FGAz2HIH3Dm3tw9MBPrBWJ2JBrnW4UgIMA6SDjjg.png)

This is part of Google’s response to Europe’s new [Digital Market Acts (DMA)](https://searchengineland.com/google-changes-digital-markets-act-compliance-438158), which came into force in March 2024. Among other things, the legislation aims to prevent “self-preferential” results, like sending users directly to Google Maps.

By keeping users on the SERP, Google doesn’t take them to another of its services as a solution. Cross-continental mobile results are more similar, although there are slight differences to how U.S. Local Finder results look…

![Mobile Local Finder Results in the U.S.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b5/43/b543bba1235ef382f7d172dd7c8780f6/63a0716c10430bb11e67e3dc4a04ae8a/AD_4nXf6R48eidLmcXZgJSwIXl4SIk7kijpK4UmyiG_UazIegxqAdMIgvdo8gdnQAwSB0CJDQmaMeRhxPU-1QgmCXVcxzAPA_jhwN78DWRAmWiua4uxJxDblVIfZp42ZWaW_YqcLPUh2xw.png)

…versus Europe, which automatically gives you the long Local Pack-style results without having to click “more places.”

![Mobile Local Finder Results in Europe.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/76/47/7647937a093d4693e63646eeeb1b039d/892aa4ef6dbdc22e0d548bb17339f6f6/AD_4nXfgkKIDLlIR2TeGjW025EH8krhxMq54dNrxb94oR1MMBIgBzkXvJAfZ2ZJ6K8nIlvvXWkkZR0knXUoUY2zcgefPcp-Nqx9oWojEh3eP7_osWM_FfCYs2jBp-6o3s0MHf6OnVFmR0g.png)

Another difference between U.S. and European results is that American users see only one Local Pack at the top of the page. In Europe, there are multiple Local Packs as they continue to scroll.

These secondary Local Packs don’t have the “more places” option to extend results.

![A second Local Pack on a European Google SERP.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/72/b6/72b61656debaefe01e65415c0f045fa7/9dc4949813ee5f0f7cfede1f945d2abe/AD_4nXcuxeNGIQqX621VOY3MkaHifp4yDeMW6jhQ0jVvHQoNH_4sLbAowuXfJhhHbsMWHHWA_GiPhow6m21F3E-_yATYDFM3EqBnCuOEd3fzzfQH6bw-PNOX2WS7-QUBaM4iL2vwCsXQfQ.png)

Google has said that the Digital Market Acts (DMA) could negatively impact businesses who rely on local search.

Oliver Bethell, director of EMEA competition at Google, said, “Changes to our Search results may send more traffic to large intermediaries and aggregators, and less traffic to direct suppliers like hotels, airlines, merchants and restaurants,” reported [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-changes-digital-markets-act-compliance-438158).

## How to Rank in Local Finder & Google Maps

The way businesses rank in Google Maps results and Local Finder are similar. Both platforms factor in what Google itself has revealed to be the three main [local SEO ranking factors](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/local-seo-ranking-factors/):

- **Relevance**: How closely a business listing matches the user’s search query
- **Proximity**: The physical distance between the business and the user’s location
- **Prominence**: How well-known and reputable the business is, based on reviews, citations, and online presence

To give your business the best chance of ranking well for the three factors, include the following in your local strategy:

- **Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization**: Complete your profile with accurate information, including business [name, address, and phone number (NAP)](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/nap-seo/). Also fill out your operating hours and publish high-quality images. Update any changes in your business’s essential details as soon as possible.

- **Positive reviews**: Encourage satisfied customers to leave [five-star reviews](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/5-star-review-responses/) on your Google Business Profile. Respond promptly to show that you value customer feedback and engagement (you can use AI tools to help with this).

![Google reviews for local car mechanics.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a9/8c/a98c7a6d4fa976f781ea0aae2eb97b3e/3c1400989e7eff2937ceec756837c5a0/AD_4nXduv5v6SbbO83OP8BO7nii9zs_oavltxcn-vUxNBcF2Iayla_bBP2tDoYWHICXSEwWqn6TpyxplmEEOpQjAWWlOlatQH8VXQbrY5ADmBGiIo7i8nTuePhilVkoRAiy8BAHijzRp_Q.png)

- **Consistent information**: Maintain uniform NAP information across all online directories and listings. Inconsistent information can confuse customers and negatively impact your local rankings.
- **Local keywords**: Use relevant [local keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-keyword-research/) in your business description and services to improve visibility

To rank higher in Google Local Finder, businesses should also follow guidelines for ranking in organic search results:

- **Content quality**: The relevance and usefulness of the website content to the user’s search query
- **Backlinks**: The number and quality of external sites linking to the website.
- **Keyword usage**: How effectively the website uses relevant keywords in its content and metadata
- **Website performance**: Factors such as page load speed, mobile-friendliness, and overall user experience

Local businesses should optimize for both local search result types to maximize their strategy's impact. Developing a robust Google Business Profile is a crucial step for improved rankings for each result type.

The process of making a strong Google Business Profile includes crafting a concise, keyword-rich business description, selecting relevant categories and attributes, utilizing high-quality images, creating engaging Google Posts, showcasing featured products, managing reviews proactively, and adding helpful Q&As.
