# What Are SERP Features? Types & How to Optimize for Them

## What Are SERP Features?

SERP features are distinct search results that stand out from traditional organic (unpaid) search results (the blue links) that aim to improve the user experience by providing quick answers and additional information directly on results pages.

And they can take several forms.

Like this featured snippet:

![the featured snippet serp feature appears for the search "why olympics are held every 4 years." the featured snippet provides a brief answer and source.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bd/70/bd7084c8bd26f37e472f38097cf040bb/400799d82325b42eaa728628c84a742e/AD_4nXdclHEl8v668qQH--DvLjEgIYkHa8MoPsKzcDzYGdTIlPrEPa1MtpdQEJLRTX-K7kP9A34c-McLtOewvWCJdvnI4-LkHiFJdMMMlyER-86B9dz8JSjuUmEneUwpktmyvx_8KPsuGQj3UqrNPhgi7qTxQsLn.png)

Some other common Google SERP features include AI Overviews, People Also Ask (PAA) boxes, image packs, video carousels, and knowledge panels.

Over time, Google has continuously added new SERP features. You’ll rarely see search results without them.

[Semrush Sensor](https://www.semrush.com/sensor/?db=US&category=&utm_campaign=serp-features&utm_source=backlinko.com&utm_medium=referral) data from October 2024 shows that just 1.53% of Google search results appear without any SERP features.

## Why Are SERP Features Important in SEO?

SERP features are important because they can drive more organic traffic to your site.

Why?

Since these features take up more space and appear prominently in search results, they’re more likely to stand out to users. And attract clicks.

In the SERP below, you can clearly see that the AI Overview, featured snippet, and PAA box are more visible than the organic listings.

![SERP Features like AI overview, featured snippet, people also ask all appear on the same SERP above organic results](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/c9/62/c962175cef977b2382393399289b5001/d1a175339d5897f0a5c1bce6d62771a5/AD_4nXdEdtN2uDuthPiGTLm567YrC5_AlffoeCE3XAmmYeNoNg_OXqDuUnEwplUu38lCNCG8YJLUhSiid8wR-fmUGm8y3WJ09_RVYs0mAGuZWAVTvIHD7vd983ttOCNhLIkQrR0-TQ4pwyTDW-g0Zn5jhxY-qQTq.png)

And securing SERP features can signal to searchers that your content is trustworthy and reliable. Because Google probably wouldn’t display your webpage so prominently if it didn’t view it as credible.

In short, SERP features are worth going after.

## 10 Google SERP Features & How to Optimize for Them

These are some main types of SERP features you may see on Google:

### 1. Featured Snippets

[Featured snippets](https://www.semrush.com/blog/featured-snippets/) directly address users’ queries with a succinct explanation pulled from a webpage and include a link to the source page.

They usually show near the top of the SERP—above the organic search results. And look like this:

![featured snippet example includes images as well](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/aa/9e/aa9e0fff62596a4c451cd9c0f824ef4f/5c605899b9ca4bb6798ee9e1221d1511/AD_4nXf8bhW6fxuj8PxKlrRhbq5Dfhhlmi3CWprszdPcQKtTlqh7BOOoCFJi13UkKD-WhFBtrJZ_-pJQ4TtxR-nlTyy3s-O5vvxQGMLE5A4R0-T66efhu3ju9Pbz6jXsqVnvxsIng24uhDPimJcJswROetnEuf6P.png)

These snippets come in different forms. Such as short paragraphs (like the above example), lists, and tables.

How do you earn featured snippets?

Start by identifying relevant queries that trigger this SERP feature.

Use Semrush [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/) to find them.

Enter a broad term related to your niche, choose your location, and click “**Search**.”

![search for keyword in tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/86/48/8648fe1dcb9cdab7c2cdf3287c4848ce/58822603f715843081565dc9e77578a6/AD_4nXd663v-7uHEU_JMySE3k4X0IxLtmnTxduAif2MewPMy8mE3uWb002_bUli-XfmzIKqAQ7jpJQpiFvTVnI0Rj7XzQKryRLzvp4dcBbNdGg_bStLiN67sVyG5fSditzaW1h0vn2q6m_LVZLYkpZZYIT0HuiIU.png)

You’ll see a list of related keywords and any SERP features those terms trigger.

![keyword and serp features column highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0f/60/0f608abeba4e38077acd8d7bd3b23cb6/1dffff741e3c246d1d1e6437bf27d673/AD_4nXeBtXOaVsigbiyaqSTXMrjhazkzXGYYqYK4nwkG2vFXgoNl-Qr6tK-3u_M1Vj_Fv2NZJuUTmFgWpIic3MKK8donDzB_0TQFUhJRkaKGaacTTsZ164F0QHFJd4YrYL0qW3Ef4zfKlXBIu__n65mPIAhSkng.png)

Click the “**Advanced filters**” drop-down. And select the checkbox next to “Featured snippet” from the list under “SERP Features.”

Then, click “**Apply**.”

![filter for keywords that prompt a featured snippet](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f6/4a/f64a194fbdac2d642d494c9646815285/6307f9d13cd3585bdec0159fca281e55/AD_4nXdDjYzQVEqzrPLSPb2sPb2YVC12LqdorPLkqbyd4atcgimcCNDDpzy2ipgDMqwIvRlNHViK_Q3xbzLD-Z0EvaTeHm1gXz1HsvlwNqYCLffdue4TQaLBf6tbKBNOcMQKWRsiaJKSBE1UR23LaBjHu0uVX6yE.png)

The tool will then show you all the keywords related to your initial query that trigger featured snippets.

Browse the list to find terms you want to craft content around.

Next, click on the magnifier icon to the left of the “SERP Features” column to open a search results page for the keyword you want to target.

![view SERP button highlighted](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/ea/69/ea695b25e011c82b4130c2c527212864/96ef18fbe00a397a61112dc4fa9ded31/AD_4nXfvv3nhga5T4EDXUrVfCu9Yjhx97BfRgZXZQGKPp1c4phtMEZIFL2jHCiUqwGOubhYkEec2QtCtCN6IU8B-Nn0WtlOahWfI_lc7Ysd-hBTdZCyKnm5N61mdtav0Wx-OhJBoT_tsfqmx1ImssAPUMeo4dXY.png)

This lets you study the featured snippet in greater detail.

![SERP shows featured snippet and more](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/2a/c0/2ac0dad9988f1649704ec52e22834432/64904d338d99bfae7c9b8c661234fb85/AD_4nXem1-0Q62k8V9Pw6uJSWlr2_ge809qZcL3-reFf_d5f0t9WDG6yJ7n-kxqFJY61O2SHjHTG2Nyg7CFczfCKCTNeZPV6fxTzDUmhXJ0cdI_l-nWngAiPAQMWyB6Cvq_cg6ZigmnvPv-GFMnfO8TkMvd-LA1x.png)

Check the snippet type. And analyze the content on the page it’s pulled from—specifically the section that appears as the featured snippet. This will help you understand what it takes to appear there.

While there’s no guaranteed formula to earn a featured snippet, you can optimize your content to increase your chances of winning one.

Start by aligning your content format with the snippet style currently ranking for your target keyword.

Then, optimize for that specific snippet type.

Here’s how:

- **Paragraph snippet**: Use your target query as a subheading and answer it immediately in a single, concise paragraph. Make sure to provide accurate and objective information.
- **List snippet**: Use a numbered list in your content if a specific order or sequence is needed (e.g., “steps to install solar panels”). And bullet points for unordered lists (e.g., “spaghetti ingredients”). You can also use [heading tags](https://www.semrush.com/blog/header-tag/) to reinforce both list types.
- **Table snippet**: Present information in a simple table with clear, descriptive headings. And apply relevant [HTML table tags](https://www.w3schools.com/tags/tag_table.asp), so Google can scan it easily.

### 2. AI Overviews

Google’s [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) are short, AI-generated summaries pulled from top-ranking pages that appear at the top of SERPs—above every other type of result.

This SERP feature can show for any type of query. But you’ll see it more often for “what is/are [keyword/topic]” keywords and other informational queries.

Like this one:

![AI Overview provides a longer explanation than a featured snippet and provides sources to the right](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b5/69/b5693a411bbac2c2adc5c337b543a2a3/a99aa59163edd49286cddd1d2eae99e6/AD_4nXecvmY9r8-Dp9L2ABsxwNynPBZDbJkjYdpbif5SQUn30G3vTcFISeIe0uu-kfT6gF1oOstYrEX_gsmMACtquEgtIjZscL4QmoJQTUIIyFIv97Dl5443KETG3Sxfc7rtybaLL6UePuUZkXYCWwC6PEQGjhER.png)

You don’t necessarily have to do anything special to optimize your content for AI Overviews.

Just follow [SEO best practices](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-best-practices/) like:

- Ensuring your pages are crawlable and indexable
- Creating helpful, [high-quality content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/quality-content/) that meets searchers’ needs
- Demonstrating Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness ([E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/)) in your content
- Optimizing your pages for target keywords
- Keeping your content up to date

### 3. Popular Products

Popular products carousels show products related to a user’s search query when relevant and include images, prices, ratings, and links to the corresponding [product pages](https://www.semrush.com/blog/product-page/).

This feature usually appears when you search for a specific product category or brand.

Here’s what it can look like:

![search for fishing rods shows popular products and allows you to refine results](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1f/00/1f000a363f3084d5695356fc1a1fa142/797bc20b7d56e8ddd62d23517887b0fb/AD_4nXf7e3HlHtMw9O0-viR1S3FpzS_X0zzYyM2B06aCHtGTdF-wUtfue04_wniklXyAhAvx344zj3sQyckj1tSLZ_5XnP6qVHUKgYN190G2aoYJTKan_mNc_bKoicIZuTU4RAGbGhl_iuUJcyC3BN3dJO6yVarG.png)

Popular products carousels help users quickly compare products from different brands/retailers directly on SERPs. And they can even filter it based on their preferences.

These SERP features are also useful for businesses. Because popular products carousels can be an effective way to drive more traffic to product pages. And possibly increase sales.

Follow these tips to optimize your product pages for popular products carousels:

- Create a [Google Merchant Center](https://www.google.com/retail/) account and add your product feed. This helps Google display your products along with important details about them.
- Add [product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product) (a specific type of code that includes information about products) to your product pages
- Use high-quality product images on your webpages. And include relevant keywords in your product titles and descriptions.

### 4. Local Pack

The [local pack](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-local-pack/) (also known as the map pack) is a set of business listings that appears for search queries with local intent—meaning users are trying to find nearby products, services, or amenities—and is usually at the top of the SERP.

Some example queries this SERP feature might show for include “real estate lawyer chicago” and “japanese restaurants near me.”

And a typical Google local pack includes:

- Three business listings with key information. Like names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings/reviews, operating hours, and website links.
- A map showing where the featured businesses are located
- An option to view more results

![local pack serp feature shows local businesses, their google business profile, and google maps](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/aa/db/aadbb4be9ecd154c6e3d75966daa6838/95c55909f51c9423bb936f9aabee6b97/AD_4nXeyBiddMERPOT5H89WTrYCfN5Pjd03qd79-r185E3_Bb1UEzX5TJQv86ixcG9utcvbq7u-Dr-l12UGivwK2AuFPA4Ub9jT4E5JQMuMkKpkN_QFrLTori1j-jciSzgskz6qT06_7-wp9G3yA8KU1u_j1u2U.png)

If you’re running a local business, appearing in local packs can significantly boost your online visibility and in-person visits.

Here's how you can increase your chances of showing in the local pack:

- Create your [Google Business Profile](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) (GBP)—a free listing that appears in Google results. And keep it updated with current and accurate information about your business.
- Get name, address, and phone number (NAP) citations on business directories and social media profiles
- Target [local keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-keyword-research/)—location-specific queries that people use to search for businesses like yours. And optimize your website content (i.e., blog posts, product or service pages, and other website copy) by incorporating these terms.
- Implement [local business schema markup](https://www.semrush.com/local/blog/local-business-schema/) (schema markup is another way to refer to structured data) on your website
- [Build backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/link-building-strategies/) from relevant local business websites and publications to your site to boost your authority
- Get customer reviews and respond to them

***Further reading****:* [*Local SEO: What Is It & How to Do It*](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-local-seo/)

### 5. People Also Ask Boxes

[People also ask boxes](https://www.semrush.com/blog/people-also-ask/) display a list of questions related to the initial search query as well as quick answers and links to the source pages.

This feature lets searchers explore different topic angles. Without performing additional searches.

You’ll see a brief answer and a link to the source page when you click a question. And new questions will be added to the list as you click questions.

You can easily find relevant PAA questions for your topic with the [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/).

Enter your topic or a broad term related to your niche and click “**Search**.”

### 6. Rich Snippets

[Rich snippets](https://www.semrush.com/blog/rich-snippets/) (also called rich results) show extra information about your page directly within otherwise standard search results.

These snippets can include elements like images, ratings, and prices. Making search results look more informative and visually appealing.

For example, Google shows image snippets along with search results for this query:

![images appear alongside results when searching for lilac bicolor ragdoll kitten](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/43/ec/43ece867cb0cd0fe2d2ecb821e5eeca7/31a748a2a9421bf4afe01ece8e41c6d0/AD_4nXetKgiotrcmvfsxKDseoXBtVfBtttEuTmc5jw4306escHzD2BdDd1cDRUCl0lNmF3NmL6MfjdevWb5JvpQSw62Bd6L7HXZIoF19cQ6YDOoyOpfDtA77nCOLJs_U8gKOFyUY2gE4Jcj6XxMV6xvy0HCRqfLt.png)

Common types of rich snippets include:

- **Review snippets**: Show ratings associated with the ranking page
- **Recipe snippets**: Provide details about recipes, including ingredients, cooking time, and nutritional information
- **Product snippets**: Display information about products. Such as price, availability, and reviews. They’re particularly useful for ecommerce sites.
- **Event snippets**: Highlight event details like date, location, and ticket availability
- **Sitelinks**: Lead to other important pages or sections of the ranking website

Google pulls these extra details from schema markup found in the webpage’s HTML.

So, it’s a good idea to add relevant schema markup to your pages to make them eligible to display rich snippets.

### 7. Discussions and Forums

The discussions and forums SERP feature shows conversations from places like Quora and Reddit that are related to the user’s search.

This feature often appears for search queries asking for advice or experiences. Such as “best training shoes for wide feet.”

![discussions and forums serp feature lists forums on reddit and two other related websites](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0a/38/0a38d321976d35986b24d9719134e775/ac346319d978fc1f53513c744fa199db/AD_4nXct7_xgTT6tJAtj5hYhetznvXm9YL5X_NU3Sw8vvxC5VkQLSz9d-fZGmnDEJap8CRxP99lHuo9GMvXQi2g_8sSF59oVB2HAOnr464Yo-tQKUZ_62CzjvBP-_B8IakOB3DusVl66ZKaIaeWFPjav358lDWjK.png)

Note that you need to have an owned forum to appear in this SERP feature. But being active on popular forums can drive people to your answers. And eventually to your site via links in your answers.

But only include links if they're relevant to the discussion. The goal is to provide value—not to promote your site or products.

### 8. Image Packs

An image pack is a row or grid of images related to a search query and typically appears near the top of the SERP.

Google displays it when images are more likely to help users for a given query.

Like for this search:

![results for husky pups shows image pack serp feature. we see six different images of husky puppies pulled from various sites.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/f7/a4/f7a436162df5cff0e4feecbb26421403/458d30245db363ff9b539929b5c95fe2/AD_4nXc85bTIzAsVzT-9hAjJyxG6z-e5bZ1Zgxe-gXA64-qtZAt-NG0iwuNd3liZDQVtHJACSKXNvTn10SeBfbydLhNzJAs2eJF5Ez2rGk0exGtQw9KhsvY7sBwHh6sK0KgpYqLWaCxzE8LDZMRNheeoR82DRs_T.png)

Follow these tips to help your content appear in image packs:

- Use images that are relevant to your page’s content and add value to the message or information you're conveying
- Give relevant names to image files and include keywords (e.g., instead of "image123.png" use "fresh-chocolate-chip-cookies.png”)
- Compress the file size without compromising quality to help images load quickly—page speed is a factor in SEO
- Use [structured data for images](https://schema.org/image) and image sitemaps to provide Google with more information about the images on your site
- Include concise, descriptive [alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/) for each image. This helps Google understand what the image is showing.

***Further reading****:* [*Image SEO: How to Optimize Images for Search Engines & Users*](https://www.semrush.com/blog/image-seo/)

### 9. Knowledge Panels

A Google [knowledge panel](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-knowledge-panel/) is an information box about an entity like a person, place, or thing that appears on the right side of the SERP.

Like this:

![when searching "tesla" the company domain and top stories appear in results with a panel on the right side containing more info about the company like founders, ceo, latest models, headquarter location, and more](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/a5/e8/a5e8714e66eeb8888bfe543e1aad53b9/9d2c47c2ed0505955d41702e0c97597e/AD_4nXe61VepeH71PMM1HumsTKCeAiYvZCM4DK5Cu9Wm3__fBWptvg-op6KkTmwVYhvh2wwERVjIy8INdBAwa2UbHWJcp2bFzDm4SsT8RYiKXWwyVCa4h8MNjE8c6R_uKeRnkrzAewRk-3f6bnCyKKi3WGEY8tLT.png)

Knowledge panels pull information from [Google's Knowledge Graph](https://www.semrush.com/blog/knowledge-graph/) (a large database about entities). And typically include details like descriptions, facts, and related links.

In addition to boosting your visibility and traffic, appearing in a knowledge panel can enhance your brand's reputation and authority.

One of the best ways to increase your chances of showing in a knowledge panel is to create a dedicated company page (like an "about us" page) that only links to reputable websites you trust.

This way, Google can easily verify your information and be confident that it's true.

Other tips include:

- Claiming and verifying your entity’s knowledge panel—if one exists
- Creating and optimizing your company’s Google Business Profile
- Reaching out to sites that inaccurately describe your brand, providing them with an accurate description, and asking them to make corrections
- Using [organization schema](https://www.semrush.com/blog/schema-markup-for-company-corporations/) to help Google understand your company

### 10. Video Carousels

Video carousels are collections of videos related to the search query that show when Google thinks it may be better answered with video content than text.

For instance, the search “handstand pushups” shows a video carousel featuring tutorials on how to do this exercise at the top of the SERP. Because watching someone do handstand pushups is easier to understand than reading about them or looking at stagnant images.

![video carousel serp feature shows videos of the answer in action](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d8/ff/d8ff2605636b26312f4094f56bbc7063/811b06a4873ed8a8ec3aac62014c8b92/AD_4nXcM9wwPHa9ZhZbuH4kOJoq3naXfiPuCmEgF55JxbG0t-m3J2MOYXK_vJ2fDqOD_X7yc6Xq0lF7FPzDFIOjWHmzO6-j-ZgBW7euj3hntZZJmPatowaDXV6FFvbUBb_MbK2z5uFaYMDJI0qRrTQyw-aqMVuec.png)

To improve your chances of appearing in video carousels:

- Create high-quality, useful videos that address users’ [search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/) (what searchers are trying to achieve)
- Write descriptive titles and file names for your videos. And optimize them with relevant keywords.
- Create engaging thumbnails that stand out
- Upload videos to YouTube. Most videos that appear in video carousels are hosted on YouTube.
- Use the [VideoObject schema markup](https://schema.org/VideoObject) if you host videos on your website

Here’s how:

Launch the [Organic Rankings](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/) tool.

Enter a competitor’s domain (e.g., “starbucks.com”), choose your location, and click “**Search**.”
