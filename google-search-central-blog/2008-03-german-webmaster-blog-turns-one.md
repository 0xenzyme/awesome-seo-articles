# German Webmaster Blog turns one

![the  German Webmaster Communication Team](/static/search/blog/images/import/7bf9b3657a2b3f66e26e796a8274bdd1.jpg)

Our
[German Webmaster Central Blog](/search/blog?hl=de)
celebrates its first birthday and we'd like to raise our glasses to 57 published posts in the last
year! We enjoy looking back at an exciting first year of blogging and communicating with
webmasters. It's the growing webmaster community that made this blog a success. Thanks to our
readers for providing feedback on our blog posts and posting in the
[German Webmaster Help group](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community?hl=de).

Over the past year, we published numerous articles specifically targeted for the German
market—topics varying from
[affiliate programs](/search/blog/2007/10/partnerprogramme-knnen-eure-website?hl=de) to
[code snippets](/search/blog/2007/10/risiken-bei-codeschnipseln-von?hl=de). We also
translated many of the applicable English posts for the German blog. If you speak German
(Hallo!) come check out the [German Webmaster Blog](/search/blog?hl=de) and subscribe
to our feed.

Hope to see you soon.
