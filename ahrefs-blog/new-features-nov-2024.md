# PDF custom reports, Patches, Ask AI in Content helper and more (Nov 2024)

This month we’re introducing PDF exports in Reports Builder, lots of new features in AI Content Helper, Patches (!!) in Site Audit, and more.

Let’s get right into it.

## AI Content Helper

**AI recommendations**

We’ve added topic-specific AI recommendations. Just click on “Get recommendations” and our AI will:

- Analyze both your content and your competitors’
- Show you how to address the content gap with specific examples.

![](https://ahrefs.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ai_recommendations.gif)

**Use AI to improve your text**

Also new is a feature that lets you use AI to improve your text. Just click and drag to select some text, and hit Ask AI. From here, type in your own request or choose an action from our presets. You can:

- Replace the text entirely
- Add it below
- Or even regenerate it for fresh ideas.

**Manage access permissions**

If you’re on an Enterprise plan, you can now manage access permissions in AI Content helper. You can:

- Make documents private
- Share them with specific people in your workspace

## Dashboard

**Export PDFs in Reports Builder**

Good news – all those custom reports you’ve been building in Reports Builder? You can now export them into PDFs! Share your SEO and marketing insights with your team, manager, clients, and your cat. Just hit Print to PDF and you’re good to go.

**Additions to GSC performance charts**

In the GSC performance charts on your Dashboard and Rank Tracker, we’ve added:

- Google updates
- Content changes
- An improved x-axis with years.

This makes it much easier to cross-reference what could be affecting your impressions and traffic numbers.

## Site Explorer

**Filter by paid positions**

We’ve added a filter for paid positions in the Organic keywords report in:

- Top ads
- Bottom ads
- Paid sitelinks

This lets you find the keywords your website is already ranking for organically so you can bid on them.

## Keywords Explorer

**Explore Keywords Explorer’s entire database**

Before, you had to run an empty search in Keywords Explorer to explore its entire database. Now, just click on this link on the landing page to access this data.

## Site Audit

**Auto-submit to IndexNow**

We’ve added the option to auto-submit to IndexNow, which is open-source protocol that lets web publishers inform participating search engines when changes are made to a website.

Just enable it in your Project settings, and Site Audit will automatically submit your site’s content changes to IndexNow during crawling. So, no need to submit changes manually anymore!

You can track auto-submissions using the new fields in Page Explorer. Just hit “Columns” and be sure to check “IndexNow auto-submission” to enable the fields.

**Available with**: “Pro” project boost. We’ll go through project boosts at the end of this post.

**Patches**

We’ve launched Patches! Patches are fixes for your website that you can create and make directly in Site Audit issue reports.

So if Site Audit reported an issue with titles or meta descriptions on your site, you just need to click on “Patch it”, enter new values, and hit publish to fix them at once – no developers needed.

Learn more about Patches in [our help article](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9775727-how-patches-work-in-site-audit).

**Available with**: “Max” project boost. We’ll go through project boosts at the end of this post.

## Rank Tracker

**YoY performance in GSC Overview**

In the GSC Overview report, you can now click on “Years” to compare your website’s performance year-on-year in the performance chart.

## General

**APIv3: New features in Keywords Explorer Overview**

We’ve added a bunch of highly requested new features in APIv3’s Keywords Explorer Overview endpoint. You can now pull data for:

- Target filter
- Empty keywords parameter
- Mobile and desktop distribution
- Monthly volumes

Read more in our [API documentation](https://docs.ahrefs.com/docs/api/keywords-explorer/operations/get-a-overview).

**Project Boosts**

We’ve launched Project boosts! This is a new type of add-on that’s charged per project. You can boost any project from your Dashboard to unlock advanced features, like Auto-submit to Indexnow and Patches.

Learn more about project boosts [in our help article](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10130957-what-are-project-boost-add-ons).
