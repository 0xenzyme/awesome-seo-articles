# What is SEO analysis?

[SEO analysis](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) is the process of examining a website’s content, structure, and performance to identify areas for improvement that will increase its visibility in search engine results. Reviewing elements such as content quality, backlink volume (the number of links on other sites pointing to your site), and keyword use, you can identify what’s effective, what’s not, and what adjustments are necessary.

Taking actions based on an SEO analysis can result in better search engine visibility and greater control over how your website appears in search results.

An SEO analysis typically involves these steps:

- **Keyword research and evaluation**. Find the words and phrases people use when searching for topics relevant to you. Check which keywords you already use, then look for new ones that can help more visitors discover your site.
- **On-page content review**. Look at your title tags (HTML titles that can show in search results), meta descriptions (HTML summaries that can show in search results), headings, and the quality of your content. Make sure content is clear, helpful, and uses the right keywords, so search engines will easily understand your pages and be more likely to rank them.
- **Competitor analysis**. Identify what your competitors are doing well. Learn from their strategies and find ways to stand out and attract more visitors.
- **Backlink analysis**. Check which websites link to your pages and see how strong those links are. Aim to get more high-quality links from reputable sites to boost your website’s authority.
- **User experience and engagement analysis**. Pay attention to how people use your site—where they click, how long they stay, and what makes them leave. Use this information to make your site easier, faster, and more enjoyable to use.
- **Technical audit**. Ensure your website is technically sound. Check how fast it loads, whether it’s mobile-friendly, and if search engines can easily locate and understand all your pages.
- **Analytics and reporting**. Use simple analytics tools to track your site’s performance over time. Measure what’s working and what needs fixing, then take clear actions to improve your results.

Performing an SEO analysis doesn’t require professional assistance or paid tools. Although the process may feel daunting at first, focusing on small, manageable tasks—such as evaluating your content and refining building backlinks—provides a practical starting point and can deliver noticeable results.

Over time, you can learn additional skills, make steady improvements, and better track your progress.
