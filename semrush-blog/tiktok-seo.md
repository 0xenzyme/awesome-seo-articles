# TikTok SEO: The Ultimate Guide to Boosting Your Profile Visibility

TikTok has [more than 1 billion active users](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/tik-tok-statistics/) worldwide. This large audience presents many opportunities to increase your brand’s visibility.

To appear in front of your target audience, optimize your TikTok content for search engines.

In this article, learn what TikTok SEO is, how it works, and five ways to get more views.

## What Is TikTok SEO?

TikTok SEO (search engine optimization) means improving your videos so that the right audience will see them. The goal is to appear on users’ For You Pages (FYP) and rank higher in TikTok’s search results.

The FYP is TikTok’s homepage, showing fresh content with each swipe. The TikTok algorithm curates these videos based on user engagement behaviors, including views, likes, and comments.

TikTok also includes a search feature. The FYP and search results pages help users discover new content. Both pages provide valuable opportunities for brands to increase visibility.

## Why Is TikTok SEO Important?

Optimizing TikTok content helps your brand appear on your audience’s FYP or in their search results. TikTok SEO tactics, such as using hashtags, help the algorithm identify and categorize your content.

For example, if a user often likes financial advice videos or searches for related financial topics, TikTok’s algorithm will recommend similar videos and accounts to that user.

TikTok’s search function also attracts users who treat the platform like a search engine.

![TikTok’s For You Page showing a video from the user @claythehustler about financial and passive income tips](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6a/e6/6ae6b6443ffd67b591a5f5aae9edb310/81aac2ff66296e000fb562031392a9dd/AD_4nXcuO5ZQNEmCafTxR1OkukBuWwUvzGUFxDF6P3jQehoPTW1oPo_fYpK25w9duH9rQvM5LvxC_7FJ4LZa1z62KSDL6GwhGKS77iF1UGaB8aBpdk0KgFgh3pEvbeNXDZ0Feb6OCvSd.jpeg)

Younger audiences often use [TikTok instead of Google Maps or Google Search](https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/12/google-exec-suggests-instagram-and-tiktok-are-eating-into-googles-core-products-search-and-maps/). TikTok provides entertainment, information, and inspiration.

Brands must learn which content types and terms users search for to meet them where they are.

## TikTok SEO Ranking Factors

TikTok has not fully revealed how its algorithms rank content. However, it has [confirmed certain factors](http://v):

- **User interactions**: TikTok shows users more videos like the ones they have liked, shared, commented on, or favorited. The platform also recommends content similar to what a user creates.
- **Video information**: Hashtags, sounds (audio clips), and captions help TikTok identify and categorize a video
- **Device and account settings**: TikTok recommends content based on language preferences, country settings, and device type. Users in English-speaking regions or with English as their primary language will see more English content.
- **Value to user**: Watching a video to the end signals strong interest. TikTok weighs completion rates more heavily than factors like geographic location.

TikTok’s algorithms evolve, and the company keeps many details private. Test different strategies or follow advice from trusted experts to find what works best.

### Expert Advice on TikTok SEO

Fashion influencer and creative director Chriselle Lim, who has more than 2.8 million followers, [attributes her TikTok success](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6DRyaOkciDo) to several tactics:

- **Posting consistently**: Lim recommends posting at least three times a day. Frequent posting often helps the algorithm show content to more users.
- **Using specific hashtags**: Lim avoids broad hashtags. Instead, she uses long-tail keywords like #feminineandproud and #bouncyskinisin
- **Engaging with the audience**: Lim spends 10-15 minutes after each upload liking and replying to comments
- **Participating in trends**: Lim leverages trends to reach new audiences. She suggests putting a unique spin on trends to stand out.

- **Experimenting with content**: Allebach made more than 50 videos before going viral
- **Understanding TikTok culture**: Identify popular songs, memes, and lingo. Decide when to use them.
- **Effective captions and hashtags**: Allebach suggests short, in-video captions for immediate clarity. He also recommends pairing hashtags with relevant viral trends (e.g., #erastourtaylorswift).

## 5 Ways to Boost Your Profile Visibility with TikTok SEO

### 2. Include Keywords and Hashtags

Keyword research is crucial for TikTok SEO. Include relevant keywords in on-screen text, captions, and titles.

[Canva](https://www.tiktok.com/@canva), for example, uses phrases like “How to replace objects in images with Magic Edit” to reach users searching for that feature.

![four videos from Canva’s TikTok account](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/8c/4d/8c4df30bd12e86494905b391db00316a/e56e42ec82316fced845c1e548f2c15c/AD_4nXf9dd3eZoHgxeq0pP9MhKaVgV0Yr31yObzxsOYPYWeW6LKdTpJ74F4BrR_9pTHJo5y89MkH5Zz9S8TNFGZ7wvQ2r291peVNVnza0SLPV3fZYtYpbivbzPK-CKgz0-PTA2P8Ea7Tkg.jpeg)

Use a handful of relevant hashtags instead of many broad ones. TikTok’s search feature helps you find related hashtags. Consider both popular and niche hashtags. More popular hashtags have higher competition, so mix them with more specific terms.

TikTok’s [Keyword Insights tool](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/keyword-insights/pc/en) helps you track keyword popularity and click-through rates. Use these findings to refine captions and hashtags.

![TikTok’s Keyword Insights tool](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/7a/6d/7a6db598d50ea019a29a9790a54edcea/b8e7b584f6d58b17d22d381676d7ee50/AD_4nXeSbx1erPn-KrtjU1oZLl833_Koz8bSdSAzgkBeFKmoS8jUrVWED6X0d72sfsciS8CxGE26QOpnTGqXWw-VnPrya3J4dSpCwa53YDPXhNHjIp19LnWjbPAR9dTtGjjchtKFn5cApw.jpeg)

**Key takeaways**:

- Use relevant keywords in titles, captions, audio, and hashtags
- Choose a small number of highly relevant hashtags

### 4. Engage with Your Audience

Comments and likes on comments also count as user interactions. The algorithm sees these as indicators of engaging content. Replying to comments encourages more conversation. More conversation creates stronger signals that your video interests users.

Chipotle, for instance, shows a personal touch in replies, which invites more likes and comments. Other users notice this engagement and may participate too.

- Building relationships in the comments may increase visibility

Collaborations can mean appearing in another brand’s video, using each other’s products, or creating [duets](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/duets).

For example, Zach King collaborated with McDonald’s. When users search Zach King’s name, McDonald’s also appears, allowing curious viewers to discover new branded content.

## Where Is TikTok SEO Headed?

TikTok is adding features to improve brand safety and user experience. Users can see [why they receive certain recommendations](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/learn-why-a-video-is-recommended-for-you) and reset their FYP, which helps them discover new content.
