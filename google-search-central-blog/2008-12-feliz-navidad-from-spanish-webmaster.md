# Feliz Navidad from the Spanish Webmaster Central team!

About three and a half months ago we
[kicked off the Spanish Webmaster Central blog](/search/blog/2008/09/el-blog-de-google-para-webmasters-ahora)
hoping to reach many webmasters. Given the time of the year, we would like to say a big ¡Muchas
Gracias! to all our readers.

It's been both a pleasure and a great opportunity for us to share our knowledge and hear your
feedback. A few of this year's highlights:

For the blog, we had:

- Matt Cutts talking to us in a 3 part interview (see
  [part 1](/search/blog/2008/09/entrevista-con-matt-cutts-parte-1),
  [part 2](/search/blog/2008/09/entrevista-con-matt-cutts-parte-2), and
  [part 3](/search/blog/2008/09/entrevista-con-matt-cutts-parte-3))
- A series of videos explaining how to use Webmaster Tools (See all parts:
  [1](/search/blog/2008/09/herramientas-para-webmasters-ahora-un),
  [2](/search/blog/2008/10/herramientas-para-webmasters-cmo),
  [3](/search/blog/2008/10/los-sitemaps-al-descubierto),
  [4](/search/blog/2008/11/y-si-no-quiero-que-salga-en-google),
  [5](/search/blog/2008/11/mejora-la-indexacion-y-rendimiento-de), and
  [6](/search/blog/2008/12/comuncate-con-google_19))
- The [links series](/search/blog/2008/10/links-information-straight-from-source) and the
  [`404` series](/search/blog/2008/08/its-404-week-at-webmaster-central).
- [Google's SEO Starter Guide in Spanish](/search/blog/2008/11/googles-seo-starter-guide).

As for conferences, we had the chance to talk to some of you in:

- [SMX Madrid 2008](https://www.searchmarketingexpo.es/smx-madrid/2008/).
- [Google Search Masters 2008 in Mexico](https://www.googlesearchmasters08.com.mx/).
  We even have some
  [video footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U6cq-p1vGGk)
  from this conference, including a
  [session about the Help Group](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTAUDkSyhgY)
  :)
- [Congreso de Webmasters 2008](https://www.congresodewebmasters.com/).

And last, but not least, the
[Spanish Help Group](https://support.google.com/webmasters/community?hl=es),
with lots of interesting stories.
