# Niche Driven SEO Guide: Definition, Benefits, and Strategy Explained

## What Is Niche Driven SEO?

Niche driven SEO is a marketing strategy that helps niche sites with smaller audiences get more visibility in search engines.

It involves choosing specific SEO niches and optimizing each site and content based on keywords and phrases relevant to a specific market segment.

Unlike general [search engine optimization](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-seo/), niche SEO targets a smaller audience—a select group of people interested in a specific product or service (i.e., a **niche**).

There’s less competition for these lesser-known subjects. But because there’s a smaller market, niche businesses must work harder to stand out.

For example, there are plenty of regular coffee companies out there. But let’s say there’s one that specializes in a particular type of java, such as organic cold brew coffee.

Whenever users search for “organic cold brew,” this niche brand appears at the top of the organic search engine results pages ([SERPs](https://www.semrush.com/blog/serp/)).

Here’s what this looks like:

How did this brand get there? It used niche driven SEO tactics to rank higher and drive traffic.

## What Are the Benefits of Niche Driven SEO?

Niche SEO can be a low-cost way of getting in front of the people that matter: your target audience.

Here are some of the main benefits of this approach.

## Low-Cost, Long-Term Strategy

Niche driven SEO is a long-term strategy that can be inexpensive (or even free) compared to other digital marketing campaigns like paid ads.

Here’s how [SEO compares to PPC](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-vs-ppc/) (pay-per-click) advertising:

![An infographic showing how SEO compares to PPC (pay-per-click) advertising](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/29/ae/29ae1b0f6bd6674655a7f92eaa200593/2cf74a5e97fa7630a21072324012c25d/BTX6GbkY1IkkOE6iqWJtX8o39bQSkRVVf5IxINLBCbNmgo9LSn0IVT9Ebm7gheCUCqQg8x64nd_CwalmA-0iD2VhrfhTDHBk56CyZrwOmIHjzYFZ0mjxPUwN1DLT-hRvqmLmjdqEBkC5TpugNcxS7XY.jpeg)

Unlike PPC (where big-budget companies thrive), everyone starts on a level playing field with SEO. You can’t buy organic traffic.

Because niche SEO is an ongoing process, it may take longer for your audience to find you. But when they do, you’ll be more likely to get the sale since you have exactly what they’re looking for, and there’s less competition.

Let’s look at an example of someone searching for “best organic cold brew brands.”

In this case, the user’s [search intent](https://www.semrush.com/blog/search-intent/) (the goal of their search) suggests they’re looking to research top brands before making a purchase.

To meet this intent, you write an article comparing the best organic cold brew brands. You’d start by researching niche keywords using a tool like [Semrush’s Keyword Magic](https://www.semrush.com/features/keyword-magic-tool/) (you can run up to 10 free keyword searches per day on the free plan).

Once you have your keywords, you’ll craft an outline for the article. In addition to including other brands, you objectively discuss your own brand on the list, with a clear link to buy your product.

If your content is high quality, your article may rank well on search engines.

When users read your niche content, they’ll find exactly what they’re looking for and see your brand as a solution to their problem.

This can lead to a sale that costs little to nothing to acquire—especially if you research keywords and write the content yourself.

## Increases Visibility and Conversions

Users who see your name frequently when they search for niche products are more likely to explore what you offer.

And the more awareness and site visitors you get, the more potential there is for them to convert and become customers.

Our [State of Content Marketing Report](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/) illustrates how many marketers focus on these outcomes. The top key content marketing goals for 2023 were “attracting more traffic” and “increasing brand awareness.”

## Builds Trust and Loyalty

If a site ranks highly in the SERPs, it could be because search engines view its content as trustworthy. And while that is just one factor of many that can influence rankings, it’s an important one.

High-quality content that inspires trust will boost the credibility of your business with your audience, too.

To ensure the most accurate and helpful information is featured in search results, Google assesses content [based on the acronym E-E-A-T](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/12/google-raters-guidelines-e-e-a-t). That stands for experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness:

![An infographic on what E-E-A-T stands for](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/19/87/1987aa0c99d07cb4ae11bdf0c3936366/1c66a6f46d01da00e3f4747dc4fdeda9/qSlCUtCbTfLJVOcoRPOXTqxgoBfyEyA8L-GPZMj1KPTAuFwiFBQN5dF0dRkOi6mzRomLF4ITGSvF66mSXvPeJTJ3Y-o-SdheREj7_L1b81NKg50Hhe7MStT7lhivsYE7OAyRb1q-xT50txGs0Hi1-Ic.jpeg)

If your website and articles demonstrate these qualities, Google may rank you higher.

And when you rank highly for specific keywords, your audience is more likely to see you as a leader in your industry.

As users see your content more often when they search within their niche, they’ll start to consider you an authority in your market and engage with your brand.

Customers who engage regularly with a brand are more likely to [connect emotionally](https://www.mycustomer.com/customer-experience/loyalty/the-biggest-miss-on-business-radars-why-emotional-attachment-is-the-key) with it, which can lead to advocacy and loyalty—and long-term revenue.

## How to Create a Niche SEO Strategy

A niche driven SEO strategy involves optimizing your site, creating high-value content based on your audience’s needs, and tracking the results.

But it also includes more indirect tactics, like building relationships with publications and influencers in your industry.

Follow these steps to create a strong niche SEO strategy for your business.

## Understand Your Audience

Customers make purchasing decisions based on their needs, challenges, and goals. If you can understand and relate to them, you’re more likely to connect with people and make a sale.

According to our State of Content Marketing Report, the top factor leading to content marketers' success was audience research.

## Optimize Your Niche Site

It also reduces the chance of people getting to your site and leaving quickly because of a poor user experience (UX).

For instance, in the above results, “channel islands surfboards” and “lost surfboards” have the same search volume. But their KDs are 29% and 38%, respectively.

As a niche site, you’re also likely to benefit from [long-tail keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-long-tail-keywords/). These are highly specific search queries you can target to help increase niche traffic.

Instead of “surfboards,” a long-tail keyword would be “surfboards for tall beginners.”

While there are some exceptions, these search terms generally have the following benefits:

- **Lower keyword difficulty** makes long-tail queries easier to rank for
- **Lower search volume** means fewer competitors vying for the top spots
- **Higher chance of a resulting purchase** because the searcher knows what they’re looking for (you can analyze search intent with the Keyword Magic Tool)

## Create High-Value Niche Driven Content

High-value content helps engage and convert potential customers. Paired with keyword research, it can increase organic traffic by turning your small business into a helpful online resource.

According to content marketers in our State of Content Marketing report, the top three most important tactics to boost ranking are:

- Creating more content and posting more often (55%)
- Improving content quality, making it more valuable (53%)
- Creating more research-driven content (37%)

Doing all of these at once is a winning combination.

Once you’ve got the hang of writing articles, move on to more advanced types of content creation, like videos, podcasts, and interactive tools (e.g., calculators and quizzes).

Whichever format you choose, focus on your target audience and their needs when creating high-quality content.

Instead, showcase firsthand experience and offer unique insights and actionable advice to differentiate your brand.

Figure out what your audience wants from a specific search term and position your product or service as a solution to their problem.

Use these tips to make any type of content more valuable:

- **Align with search intent**: If your audience is still in the consideration phase, make sure your advice is knowledgeable without being pushy
- **Map out your framework and outline**: Plan ahead to make the most of your time and resources
- **Create something better than the rest**: If you want to rank well, your content needs to be original, in-depth, and engaging to outdo your competitors
- **Add visuals to written content**: Graphics can help readers visualize your point and break up walls of text into more manageable chunks
- **Stay up to date on industry news and trends**: Include the most recent stats and figures to build your reputation as a leading industry source
- **Optimize each web page**: Use On-page SEO strategies like updating title tags, internal links, and meta descriptions
- **Include expert insights**: Incorporate quotes and data from experts in your niche to increase your chances of having them share it

Checking off each of these steps will help build your credibility and reputation as an authority in your niche.

## Track Important Metrics

Choosing and tracking key metrics helps you measure the progress of your niche driven SEO strategy. Comparing these with your competitors can highlight what’s working and what isn’t.

For example, a conversion happens when a prospective customer takes a desired action on your site (like buying or downloading something). A lead is a potential future conversion.

According to our recent report, the most popular way marketers calculate ROI is by measuring revenue from content-generated leads and conversions.

![Answers to "How do you measure content marketing ROI?" from State of Content Marketing Report](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/1a/aa/1aaace82c2e125774b8894e70a322846/058716d2f1cb51772cd3f9267e4203f9/TtLTDyFNW1LsYSzY_Uz1wz7TJ_1Rf7i7Q4aesdVYWSSzdd6evK4EpwPjs0mxltFjpaKMyNUNcBp47Qrv4NE70UOKV3y0ahHF06BLEWoue7g4wi8wK4-vIhBeJJ2tKuSegc_DdGbiL3V2Rrgqhu-SdUU.jpeg)

While these metrics are important, they aren’t the only numbers marketing experts use to determine success.

You may also wish to track metrics like:

- **Organic traffic**: The number of people who visit your website
- **Backlinks**: The number of links on other websites that point to yours
- **Keyword ranking**: The positions of keywords that are driving traffic
- **SERP visibility**: How visible your brand is in organic search results
- **Click-through rate (CTR)**: How many people click on your SERP listing
- **Time spent on page**: The average amount of time users spend viewing a specific page
- **Bounce rate**: The percentage of users who land on your site and leave without taking any action
- **Authority Score**: The level of authority your domain has within search engines

The optimal goals for these metrics will be unique to your business. If you’re a new company, your goals may reflect that you’re starting from scratch. More established businesses may want to double traffic to their site.
