# 26 AI SEO Statistics for 2026 + Insights They Reveal

SEOs need hard numbers to see what’s changing as AI platforms are impacting organic traffic and reshaping search behavior—and how fast.

Let's look at the latest AI SEO statistics to guide your strategy in 2026.

## AI SEO Statistics Highlights

- Google [AI Overviews](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews/) reach 2 billion monthly users
- Website traffic from AI search may surpass traffic from traditional search by 2028
- Roughly 60% of searches now yield no clicks
- Almost 70% of businesses report higher return on investment (ROI) from using AI in SEO
- Nearly 35% of Gen Z people in the U.S. use AI chatbots to search for information

## AI Adoption and Market Growth Statistics

AI adoption is accelerating, with many users now relying on AI-generated answers daily.

Here's the data:

### 1. ChatGPT Has 700 Million Weekly Active Users

ChatGPT’s massive user base makes it the fourth most visited website globally, [according to Semrush data](https://www.semrush.com/trending-websites/global/all/). It gets over 5 billion monthly visits.

![Top websites worldwide showing ChatGPT as the fourth most visited website globally with over 5 billion monthly visits.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/50/28/50284ad46167d5e9757d9cc252e1bf21/551f12833c726e2b85dfa77489b2858c/image.jpeg)

Interestingly, adoption rates in lower-income countries are over four times higher than in high-income countries.

(Source: [OpenAI, 2025](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/))

### 2. Google AI Mode Has 100 Million Users in the US and India

[AI Mode](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-ai-mode/) is now in more than [200 countries and territories](https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-expands-languages-locations/) and has strong adoption in the first two markets it became available in.

The rapid growth of AI Mode supports the idea that Google is betting on conversational search as the next mainstream format.

(Source: [Google, 2025](https://blog.google/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2025/))

### 3. AI Search Traffic Is Up 527% Year Over Year

The 2025 Previsible AI Traffic Report tracked 19 GA4 properties and found traffic from large language models (LLMs) rose from about 17,000 to 107,000 sessions when comparing January-May 2024 with the same period in 2025.

Some sites are now reporting over 1% of total sessions coming from platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Copilot. While that share remains small overall, the growth as a whole highlights how AI platforms are beginning to play a measurable role in website discovery.

(Source: [Search Engine Land, 2025](https://searchengineland.com/ai-traffic-up-seo-rewritten-459954))

### 4. AI Search Traffic May Surpass Traditional Search Traffic by 2028

AI platforms are expected to drive more website visits than traditional search engines in the next three years.

![Projected annual visitors by source from 2025 to 2029 showing LLMs overtaking traditional organic search.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d5/2d/d52df22d2693a6f586fbb0a2f6db9248/84734a3347cfeb1c4982b007efd11b99/image.png)

If AI Mode becomes the default Google experience, the transition to more traffic from AI search could happen even sooner.

(Source: [Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/))

## AI Overview Statistics

AI Overviews are reshaping how users find information on Google.

Here's the latest data:

### 5. Google AI Overviews Now Reach 2 Billion Monthly Users

AI Overviews that summarize information directly on results pages are highly visible and accessible now that they’re available in more than 200 countries.

SEOs are beginning to change how they’re tracking search visibility. Traditional rankings still matter, but appearing in AI Overviews is becoming an equally important metric.

(Source: [Google, 2025](https://blog.google/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2025/))

***Further reading****:* [*How to Research and Analyze AI Overviews with Semrush*](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overview-research/)

### 6. Over 88% of Searches That Trigger AI Overviews Are Informational

Nearly nine in 10 queries triggering AI Overviews have informational intent, meaning that searchers want to learn about something.

Here’s how AI Overview appearance varies across other intent types:

- Commercial queries (terms users search when they’re considering options) account for 8.69% of terms that trigger AI Overviews
- Transactional queries (terms users search when they want to take action) account for 1.76%
- Navigational queries (terms users search when trying to find a website or page) account for just 1.43%

![Share of keywords that trigger AI Overviews by search intent from October 2024 to March 2025 showing roughly nine in 10 queries having informational intent.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/49/41/4941b8536d23fcbb58d3a05371a97888/2213aac02bb8a4906a6c622ac4152526/image.jpeg)

If your content educates, explains, or defines topics, AI Overviews can directly impact your visibility. Being featured within these summaries helps your brand stay visible as AI results become a larger part of Google Search.

(Source: [Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/))

### 7. Most Searches That Show AI Overviews Have No Ads or Very Low CPC

Nearly all keywords triggering AI Overviews (95%) either display no paid ads or have minimal commercial value.

Commercially valuable keywords—especially those with a [cost per click](https://www.semrush.com/blog/cost-per-click/) (CPC) above $2—remain largely untouched.

Deprioritizing commercially valuable keywords for AI Overviews likely allows Google to protect its ad revenue while continuing to experiment with AI for informational searches.

(Source: [Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/))

### 8. Over 68% of Terms That Trigger AI Overviews Get 100 or Fewer Monthly Searches

AI Overviews appear most often on low-volume queries.

![Share of keywords by search volume showing AI overviews appearing most often on low-volume queries.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/89/a0/89a00b388989b3d850a9a4abc575e9b2/8e0ce8557736672939b1a2871945cefd/image.jpeg)

And almost 80% of keywords that trigger AI Overviews fall into the 0%-40% [keyword difficulty](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-difficulty/) range, meaning those terms aren’t very difficult to rank for.

Your niche content based on [long-tail keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-choose-long-tail-keywords/) may face the highest AI disruption risk. Because AI Overviews appear most often on low-competition, informational searches and absorb attention that might otherwise go to organic listings.

Still, earning a citation within AI Overviews helps you maintain visibility where users first see results.

(Source: [Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/))

## AI Search Traffic and CTR Statistics

AI in search is changing both [click-through rates](https://www.semrush.com/blog/click-through-rate/) (CTRs) and browsing behavior.

Here are some key stats:

### 9. Roughly 60% of Searches on Traditional Search Engines Yield No Clicks

AI summaries and other features answer questions right on the search results page, so users often don't need to visit any websites.

For example, if you search “phone photography tips,” you’ll likely see a “From sources across the web” feature and an AI Overview before the regular blue links.

![Google SERP for the term "phone photography tips" with the “From sources across the web” feature and an AI Overview appearing at the top.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/0f/a5/0fa56b7952e6735512273e7aba7ad6ec/45075da91bc0e84e3231aeb76218becc/image.jpeg)

The presence of SERP features that aim to address the user’s query reduces the chances of ranking sites getting [organic traffic](https://www.semrush.com/blog/organic-traffic/) through traditional search rankings.

But appearing inside SERP features—whether as a cited source or brand mention—still gives you valuable visibility.

(Source: [Bain & Company, 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/goodbye-clicks-hello-ai-zero-click-search-redefines-marketing/))

### 10. Only 8% of Users Click a Traditional Link When an AI Summary Appears

When Google shows an AI summary, only 8% of users click on the regular search results below it. Without a summary, that number nearly doubles to 15%.

The comparatively lower clicks among results with AI summaries suggests that SEOs need to think beyond organic traffic.

Focusing on clarity, authority, and content that AI systems recognize as trustworthy will likely improve visibility in AI summaries that can lead to results later on.

(Source: [Pew Research, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/))

### 11. About 1 in 4 Searches with AI Summaries End Without Further Action

About 26% of searches that show AI summaries end without any additional clicks. For searches showing only traditional results, that drops to 16%.

The lack of action on results pages with AI summaries suggests that many users treat AI summaries as complete answers—not a starting point for deeper exploration.

(Source: [Pew Research, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/))

### 12. Being Featured as an AI Overview Source Increases CTR from 0.6% to 1.08%

Sites that appear as sources inside AI Overviews see a small but noticeable boost in click-through rate, based on research from across 7,800+ queries.

The increase is modest, yet it reinforces a new reality—visibility within AI summaries now matters almost as much as traditional search rankings.

(Source: [Seer Interactive, 2024](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/how-ai-overviews-are-impacting-ctr-5-initial-takeaways))

### 13. Half of ChatGPT's Cited Links Point to Business and Service Websites

Business and service sites account for 50% of all the sources ChatGPT cites.

News and media sites account for 9.5% of ChatGPT citations, blogs and content sites account for 8.3%, and ecommerce sites account for 7.6%.

![A donut chart showing ChatGPT citations by website category with 50% being from business/service sites with the rest from news, blogs, ecommerce, forums, etc.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/97/0e/970e047274c6819af03bcaf61e71f58b/bdf4a9fa25f22c2d55eb177bb8ac0c37/image.png)

ChatGPT’s tendency to link to business and service sites suggests strong potential for businesses. High-quality, informative content on company sites has a real chance of being cited by AI platforms like ChatGPT.

(Source: [Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/))

### 14. ChatGPT Users Click an Average of 1.4 External Links per Visit

ChatGPT users click out to external websites about twice as often as Google users—1.4 links per visit compared with 0.6 from Google.

This difference reflects how people use each platform. ChatGPT users may open links to verify AI-generated answers or explore topics further. Google users might trust the answers more or just tend to find what they need.

(Source: [Momentic, 2025](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/google-reach-chatgpt-click-throughs))

## AI Search Performance Statistics

AI search creates new opportunities for websites to gain visibility and traffic.

Here’s the data:

### 15. The Average AI Search Visitor Is Worth 4.4x More Than a Traditional Organic Search Visitor

Visitors arriving from AI search platforms are far more valuable than those from traditional search—at least from a conversion perspective.

![A line chart comparing estimated LLM versus traditional organic search value from 2025 to 2029 showing LLMs rising and organic search falling.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/24/26/24262d5728b7eecfd9770961696d6e32/c84c642d6c678a11ab705d442279f187/image.png)

Even if you don’t get much traffic volume from AI search, optimizing for AI visibility can still have a noticeable impact on your business.

(Source: [Semrush, 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/))

### 16. AI Referral Visits Have a 27% Lower Bounce Rate Than Non-AI Traffic for Retail Sites

Shoppers arriving to retail sites through AI platforms tend to be more engaged. Plus, these visits are 38% longer and involve viewing more pages.

Visitors referred by AI seem to be arriving at their end destination with stronger intent. Which means creating and distributing content that provides accurate information about your business is key for getting the most out of visits from AI platforms.

(Source: [Adobe, 2025](https://business.adobe.com/blog/ai-driven-traffic-surges-ahead-in-q2))

## AI SEO User Behavior and Trust Statistics

People don’t use AI search the same way they use traditional search.

Here are the numbers that prove it:

### 17. Roughly 80% of Consumers Use AI Summaries for at Least 40% of Their Searches

Most people now rely on AI-generated answers for information, getting what they need directly on the results page instead of clicking through to other sites.

Instead of focusing only on rankings and clicks, it’s now important to understand how your content appears inside AI answers—and whether your pages are credited as sources.

(Source: [Bain & Company, 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/goodbye-clicks-hello-ai-zero-click-search-redefines-marketing/))

### 18. About 70% of Users Read Only the First Third of AI Overviews

Most readers move on after scanning just the opening section of an AI Overview, so the information and sources shown in the first section get the most attention.

Earning visibility early—through brand mentions or citations—in AI Overviews can make a bigger difference than appearing later in the expanded text.

![The different brand mentions and sources on the right appearing in the opening section of an AI Overview highlighted.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/13/f8/13f898c3b362d731e22edcf3fd690ea0/90bc30a750dca0ef3034dda54079d25c/image.jpeg)

(Source: [Growth Memo, 2025](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/the-first-ever-ux-study-of-googles))

### 19. Over 80% of Users Are at Least Somewhat Skeptical of AI Overviews

Only a small fraction (about 9%) of users always trust the AI answers they see.

Most people fall somewhere in the middle. They use AI summaries but remain cautious, reflecting ongoing doubts about how accurate these results really are.

(Source: [Exploding Topics, 2025](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/ai-trust-gap-research))

### 20. Only About 19% of Users Click Through to the Sources Cited in AI Overviews

Most people read the AI Overview summary and move on without visiting the actual sources. Which limits how much traffic you can expect, even if you're cited.

Still, being mentioned can help with visibility and brand recall. When users see your site repeatedly in AI summaries, they’re more likely to recognize your brand later or search for it directly.

And you’re still more likely to be clicked when appearing in an AI Overview compared to a traditional search result underneath an AI Overview.

(Source: [Exploding Topics, 2025](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/ai-trust-gap-research))

### 21. Nearly 35% of Gen Z Use AI Chatbots to Search for Information

People in the U.S. between the ages of 16 and 27 use AI search tools more than any other age group.

That said, Gen Z users also rely on traditional search engines and visual platforms like YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok to find information.

Brands that start optimizing their content for AI platforms can stay visible with Gen Z audiences, who use AI platforms alongside other channels.

(Source: [Claneo, 2025](https://www.claneo.com/en/state-of-search-us/))

### 22. Over 40% of Users Say They've Seen Inaccurate or Misleading Content in AI Overviews

The relatively high share of people who report seeing inaccurate content shows there’s a trust gap.

Brands need to publish accurate, well-sourced content to avoid adding to misinformation and keep an eye on how their pages appear in AI summaries. If something is misrepresented, use Google’s feedback option to flag the error and suggest a correction.

![Flagging an error on an AI Overview by clicking the thumbs-down icon followed by "Report a problem" from the window that appears.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/71/b2/71b2ba944b4c4e53cbc22ade6830fcf4/699497b9107e9c9d25f407bc63b749a3/image.jpeg)

(Source: [Exploding Topics, 2025](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/ai-trust-gap-research))

## How to React to These AI SEO Statistics

The above AI SEO statistics highlight three big shifts:

- Consumers are transitioning from traditional search to AI search
- Organic traffic and click-through rates from search are declining
- Showing up in AI search results is becoming critical for driving higher-quality visitors to your site

So, how should your [SEO strategy](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-strategy/) evolve? Here are three actions you can take:

### 1. Start Tracking Your AI Visibility

Tracking AI visibility shows how often your brand appears in AI results compared to your competitors. So, you can adjust if needed.

Semrush’s [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://semrush.com/ai-seo/overview/) lets you track your presence across AI platforms in a variety of ways.

Go to the [Visibility Overview](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/overview/) dashboard and enter your domain to see your score (measured on a scale from 0-100) to see how visible you are in LLMs compared to your competitors.

![The AI Visibility score highlighted on the Visibility Overview report.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/b0/2e/b02e02eba64bbd55b81c0b04c1615c9d/a38d6ff62f396a0e97a028b5386b7ea6/image.png)

Scroll down to “Topics & Sources” and select “**Cited Pages**” to see which pages from your site are referenced in AI-generated answers.

![Topic & Sources on Visibility Overview with the "Cited Pages" tab highlighted showing a list of pages referenced in LLM answers.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/50/98/5098fffcac96bf204030ad20c8b21039/b8aee9b636701a053e9d5dfa1c8cdf4d/image.jpeg)

Click any URL to view the exact prompt where the page is cited. If you see “Missed” in the “Your Brand” column, that means your URL is cited, but your brand isn’t mentioned in the AI answer.

Click “**Monitor**” at the end of the row to set up prompt tracking.

![A URL expanded on the Cited Pages report and "Monitor" clicked to track a missed brand mention.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/9b/2c/9b2ce8e1d05ccd477eed31af35104097/cccb57017323b73c3cc819e41a6e0577/image.jpeg)

### 2. Get More Results from the Same Visitors

Even if AI search reduces overall clicks, improving how well your pages convert visitors contributes to continued growth.

To see what strong conversion looks like in practice, I analyzed five B2B sales prospecting tool websites in Semrush’s [Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/traffic-overview/).

Among the five websites I analyzed, Apollo.io stood out for having the highest purchase conversion rate.

![Comparing the "Purchase Conversion" rates for five brands on the Traffic Analytics report with "apollo.io" having the highest rate.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/56/f7/56f788bb383ec129a480dc15a6c342f8/4f3fd4ed1991fd735ac5e6b12ce5784c/image.jpeg)

While four out of five sites promote a free trial above the fold on the homepage, Apollo.io makes sign-up easier by offering multiple options, including using a Google or Microsoft account. That likely removes friction and helps visitors start faster.

![Apollo.io offering multiple sign-up options from their homepage including using a Google or Microsoft account.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/bb/b9/bbb90db08040278f6c51b95b64551f34/afbddb9faa85864ce02cd69d8aa5e057/image.jpeg)

Apollo.io’s homepage communicates social proof very clearly as well. The brand highlights real results—specific percentages of cost savings, sales growth, and productivity gains—so visitors instantly see measurable outcomes that matter to them.

![Apollo.io communicating social proof clearly on their homepage through brand logos, a customer quote, and specific results they've helped achieve.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/43/88/4388b3c439dfa88378948afb8358ae3b/a8040fa3b4ce0f12270a009b63b5d482/image.jpeg)

One other site in my analysis includes an FAQ section on the homepage. But Apollo.io’s stands out for how it directly answers buyer questions to keep visitors engaged when they might otherwise leave.

![The Frequently asked questions section on Apollo's homepage addressing relevant questions a prospective buyer might have.](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/d8/87/d887a0fdd0a69b2b7555e4a0f707e848/e531a46c146ac5bdc3ea39db99c074cd/image.jpeg)

Apollo.io also focuses on its AI capabilities. It clearly lists use cases.

In short, Apollo.io lowers effort, shows tangible proof, and guides users toward the next step to retain value from existing traffic even as AI search reshapes discovery.

You can similarly offset potential traffic drops from AI search by turning more of your existing visitors into customers.

Start by finding your conversion rate. Compare it with your competitors’ purchase conversion rates using [Traffic Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/traffic-overview/) to set a realistic benchmark, then optimize your key pages for higher conversion.

Here are a few quick ways to start optimizing your site for conversions:

- Make sign-up or trial options easy to find and complete
- Use specific proof (numbers, results, or case studies) instead of generic claims
- Add short FAQ sections to address common doubts
- Repeat key CTAs at logical points throughout the page

***Further reading****:* [*6 Steps to Perform Conversion Rate Optimization*](https://www.semrush.com/blog/conversion-rate-optimization/)

### 3. Redefine Success Metrics for the AI Search Era

Success in SEO now means looking beyond rankings and traffic to understand how visitors engage and convert.

AI summaries and experiences affect how users find and interact with your content.

With that in mind, here are the key metrics worth tracking in the AI search era:

- **AI visibility**: Measure how often you show up in AI results across platforms like Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT. The more you appear, the more AI systems recognize your site as a trusted source.
- **Engagement quality**: Track metrics such as time on page, scroll depth, and repeat visits. These signals reveal the quality of the traffic you do get.
- **Conversions**: See if visitors from AI or traditional search are turning into leads or customers.

Together, these metrics give a more complete picture of SEO performance.
