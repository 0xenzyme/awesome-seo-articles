The resulting document will help you:

- Plan and create content that supports your business goals
- Make the most out of your available resources
- Keep all your team members aligned

### 1. Set Content Goals

First, define your general [content marketing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-content-marketing/) goals and how you’ll measure them.

This will give direction to the rest of your [content strategy](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-marketing-strategy-guide/). And allow you to track your success in the future.

Set goals that are SMART:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S** | Specific | Precise, unambiguous goals keep your efforts focused. Break down broad objectives into clearer sub-objectives. |
| **M** | Measurable | Tracking your progress objectively allows you to make data-led decisions. Translate obscure objectives into quantifiable ones. |
| **A** | Attainable | Over-ambitious goals can demotivate your team. Create realistic goals by considering your resources and referring to relevant benchmarks. |
| **R** | Relevant | Content goals should align with your broader [marketing strategy](https://www.semrush.com/blog/marketing-strategy/). You’ll see better results when everyone works toward similar objectives. |
| **T** | Time-bound | Motivate your team and improve tracking by setting a deadline for each goal. But make sure you have enough time to make an impact. |

According to [Semrush’s State of Content Marketing report](https://www.semrush.com/goodcontent/state-of-content-marketing/report/), the most common content goals are:

- Attract more traffic to our website
- Increase brand awareness
- Generate leads
- Generate sales/revenue
- Improve customer loyalty and engagement

The SMART version of “Attract more traffic” could be “Increase website traffic by 25% over the next six months by optimizing existing content for SEO and publishing high-quality blog posts twice a week.”

(You might want to refine your goals later. Once you have more information in your strategy.)

When you’ve decided on your goal, add it to your content strategy template. And note how you’ll measure it. For example, you might use [Google Analytics 4](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-analytics/) to measure traffic.

![content goals section of the content strategy template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/fc/21/fc21305bc33eb456ddacc18f4723b1c1/ea374eea4d35679f424c54cf76fb0db4/AD_4nXfy2tlrSaumGuTER6yAh8xCK5cbkFKeSIZ0zU-iNd1MKAjVaoLpTR4JWPmulg23i28NsBzlY-qEX2BoyRlTDjzUL_Rw_NkhcZq8K7RCIasU8bT6byovsGKxa9rsD-5RnepqRIbaIvPV_wWzbg1-5mc61ggg.png)

This process ensures that your goals are practical and clear.

### 5. Audit Your Content

A [content audit](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-audit/) is an analysis of your existing content. You should review every page on your website and decide what to do with it:

- **Keep**: The content is helpful and performing well. So, it should be kept as-is.
- **Improve**: The content could be improved or is not performing as well as it could. Improving this content could be a cost-effective way to get more traffic and/or conversions.
- **Remove**: The content offers no value and has little to no potential. It should be removed, as it could be doing more harm than good.

Implementing these changes tends to be more cost-effective than creating new content. Plus, it gives you a solid foundation to build upon.

To determine if content should be improved, ask yourself:

- Is the content helpful, comprehensive, and accurate?
- Does the content demonstrate [E-E-A-T](https://www.semrush.com/blog/eeat/) (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness)?
- Does the topic have business potential?
- Does your audience want or need this content?

To determine if content is performing well, ask yourself:

- Does your content fulfill its original goals?
- How much traffic does the page generate?
- How many conversions does the page generate?

### 6. Identify Content Gaps

It also helps you build a more robust [content marketing funnel](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-marketing-funnel/). In other words, content that nurtures users at every stage of the buying journey.

### 7. Establish Content Guidelines

In the final section, establish and outline your content guidelines.

These are the principles of content creation. Which help your team stay aligned and produce on-brand, goal-oriented content.

Start by creating and linking to key resources, including:

- **Tone of voice guidelines**: A document that defines your brand’s [tone of voice](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-define-your-tone-of-voice/). This is the personality that should come through in all your writing.
- **Content calendar**: A schedule detailing the content you will publish. Use our [content calendar template](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-calendar/#start-filling-out-your-content-calendar) to create yours.
- **Style guide**: A document outlining your design standards. This helps you produce visual content that consistently fits your brand identity.

Also jot down key details for easy reference. Like this:

![content guidelines section of the content strategy template](https://static.semrush.com/blog/uploads/media/6f/75/6f75052f7e5d01895c97f29088e3618c/f8015d0e7a418dbf55f639e547ff57c3/AD_4nXdd7V_NVgUHwpK_qxAjCmcVjuy3y41mSj5bcF_jKr6jieO2s8Nc-VtHFRlDeXjw3YLoNQkbGpbxq8bOVQttfyq15PbnHYjIbY6fwztX2JY_kZAQsxj7ny0LCK5a7pjIBWHTfpHRa6I316k3yXag35JNp_wV.png)

Next, decide what [types of content marketing](https://www.semrush.com/blog/types-of-content-marketing/) you want to engage in. And build out the guidelines for each. Making sure to reference all the information in your content strategy template so far.

- **Format**: The type of content (blog post, video, ebook, etc.). Consider your buyer personas, resources, etc.
- **Topics**: The topics you want your content to cover. These should be relevant to your brand, interesting to your audience, and compatible with your chosen format.
- **Frequency**: How often do you want to create and publish this type of content? Consider your available resources. And remember that quality trumps quantity.
- **Distribution**: How will you amplify your content to get more people to see it? You can [distribute content](https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-distribution-guide/) via social media, email newsletters, etc.
- **Notes**: Anything else that’s important to note. For example, the main purpose of the content.

## How to Customize and Share Your Document

To rename your website content strategy template:

1. Click the current title in the top-left corner
2. Type in your preferred title
3. Hit Enter/Return on your keyboard

To duplicate a table:

1. Use your mouse to highlight the entire table
2. Right-click your mouse and click “**Copy**”
3. Place your cursor where you’d like to add the table, then right-click and select “**Paste**”

To add table rows or columns:

1. Place your cursor next to the desired location for the row/column
2. Right-click your mouse and click “**Insert row above**,” “**Insert row below**,” “**Insert column left**,” or “**Insert column right**”

To change table colors:

1. Highlight the cells you’d like to change
2. Click the paint can icon in the toolbar
3. Select your preferred color

To move your document to another folder:

1. Click the arrow folder icon at the top
2. Choose your preferred location in your Google Drive
3. Select “**Move**”

To export your document:

1. Click “**File**” > “**Download**”
2. Select your preferred file format
3. The file will save to the default location on your device

To share your document:

1. Click the “**Share**” button in the upper-right corner
2. Add specific people and/or enable general access
3. Choose Viewer/Commenter/Editor permission as required
