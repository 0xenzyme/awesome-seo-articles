# Research study of Sitemaps

We've been tracking the growth of
[Sitemaps](https://www.sitemaps.org/index.html)
on the web. It's been just 2 years since Google, Yahoo and Microsoft co-announced the
[Sitemaps rule in robots.txt,](/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview)
and it is already supported in many millions of websites including educational and
[government](https://www.google.com/publicsector)
websites! At the WWW'09 conference in Madrid, Uri Schonfeld presented his summer internship work
studying Sitemaps from a coverage and freshness perspective. If you're interested in how some
popular websites are using Sitemaps, and how Sitemaps complement "classic" webcrawling, take a
look:
[Sitemaps: Above and Beyond the Crawl of Duty](https://www2009.eprints.org/100/)

![plot showing the efficacy of sitemaps when it comes to url discovery](/static/search/blog/images/import/f847997654e4e64cfa4f8dab70f09d47.png)

At Google, we care deeply about getting increased coverage and freshness of the content we index.
We are excited about open standards that help webmasters open up their content automatically to
search engines, so users can find relevant content for their searches.
