# More stats!

We've just launched some new features. The biggest change for those of you already using Sitemaps:
if you've
[verified](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080)
your site, you'll see substantially more stats and error details.

The biggest change for new users: you can now
[add a site](/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview#add_site)
to your account even before create a Sitemap for it. Once you've added and verified your site,
you can see all these new stats and errors.

## New stats: how the Googlebot views your site

The
[new stats](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080)
we're showing you are all about letting you know how we see your site.

With **query stats**, we show you the top Google search queries that return pages to your site
as well as the top queries that caused users to click on your site in the search results.

![query stats feature in webmaster tools](/static/search/blog/images/import/7d6539ce56810047a0f4f026de128b10.jpg)

With **crawl stats**, you can see how we view crawled pages. You can see a distribution of the
pages successfully crawled and the pages with errors as well as a distribution of PageRank for
the pages in your site.

![crawl stats feature in webmaster tools](/static/search/blog/images/import/e5e70ab2a1bb830da824e4222b33bbf5.jpg)

**Page analysis** shows you what we detect about the content and encoding of your pages.

**Index stats** provide an easy way for you to use our advanced search operators to return
results about how we see the indexed pages of your site.

## Mobile stats

You can now verify your mobile sites and see stats for them.

## More detailed errors

Now you'll have more details about problems we had crawling your site. We report on 40 different
types of errors in 5 categories.

![error reports feature in webmaster tools](/static/search/blog/images/import/9ef3b3300ab87611e85923497510aec4.jpg)

## Adding a site

Even before you have a Sitemap for your site, you can still take advantage of all the statistics
and error information we have available. Simply create a
[Sitemaps account](https://search.google.com/search-console) and
add a site to it. Once you verify site ownership, we provide the full range of statistics and
error details. Of course, we encourage you to add a Sitemap so we can learn more about your site.
