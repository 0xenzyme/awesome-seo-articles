## Affiliate marketing challenges and risks

- 63% of businesses worry about affiliate marketing fraud within their programs. ([WPBeginner](https://www.wpbeginner.com/))
- Businesses lost $1.4 billion in revenue due to affiliate fraud in 2020. ([WPBeginner](https://www.wpbeginner.com/))
- During the Covid-19 pandemic, 56% of affiliate schemes witnessed a surge in revenue. ([We Can Track](https://wecantrack.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-statistics/))
- 45% of affiliate marketers report difficulty finding quality affiliates, making recruitment a key challenge. ([Rakuten Advertising](https://www.rakutenadvertising.com/insights/affiliate-marketing/))
- 59% of marketers say managing affiliate relationships requires more effort than other digital channels. ([AffStat Report](https://www.affstat.com/))
