# Webmaster Academy now available in 22 languages

![](/static/search/blog/images/import/1d1d7f3aa2b451ce690f0792dbe96fcd.png)

Today, the new [Webmaster Academy](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) goes live
in 22 languages! New or beginner webmasters speaking a multitude of languages can now learn the
fundamentals of [making a great site](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide),
[providing an enjoyable user experience](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), and
[ranking well in search results](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide). And if you
think you're already familiar with these topics,
[take the quizzes](/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) at the end of each module
to prove it :).

So give Webmaster Academy a read in your preferred language and let us know in the comments or
[help forum](https://support.google.com/webmasters/go/community)
what you think. We've gotten such great and helpful feedback after the
[English version launched this past March](/search/blog/2014/03/introducing-new-webmaster-academy_18)
so we hope this straightforward and easy-to-read guide can be helpful (and fun!) to everyone.

Let's get great sites and searchable content up and running around the world.
