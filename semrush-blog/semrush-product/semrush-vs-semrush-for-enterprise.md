# Semrush vs. Semrush for Enterprise: Which one is right for you?

Are you choosing between Semrush and the Enterprise platform for your brand visibility strategy? In this quick guide, you'll learn everything you need to make the right decision.

Here's the short answer: Semrush is for small to mid-sized teams who want to grow their online visibility across Google and AI search, create content, research competitors, and launch effective ad campaigns. Semrush for Enterprise works best for large organizations that need to govern and scale that visibility across multiple brands, markets, and teams.

Both solutions run on the same foundation. But the scope, customization, and automation capabilities are built for different needs.

## Semrush vs Semrush for Enterprise: In a nutshell

The main difference is depth and scale. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) gives teams an accessible way to manage and grow their search visibility — from Google to LLMs like ChatGPT — and provides powerful tools for competitive intelligence, market research, and more. [Semrush for Enterprise](https://www.semrush.com/enterprise/) builds on that with deeper segmentation, advanced automation, and governance controls.

| Plans from $139/month, with Semrush One plans starting at $199/month combining SEO with AI visibility. Additional toolkits like Traffic & Market and Advertising Toolkit are priced as add-ons. | Custom enterprise pricing |

## What Semrush and Semrush for Enterprise have in common

Both Semrush and the Enterprise platform help teams track and grow visibility across traditional search and AI platforms from a single place.

With both platforms, you can:

- Track visibility across Google AI and AI platforms like ChatGPT (although Enterprise gives you access to more LLMs)
- Monitor rankings, brand mentions, AI citations, and competitor visibility
- Analyze SEO and AI visibility together across keywords, prompts, citations, etc.
- Access Semrush's core search and AI visibility datasets, including 27.9B keywords across 142 geo databases and 261M+ real AI search prompts
- Identify opportunities to improve how your brand appears in both search engines and AI-generated answers

## How to choose between Semrush and Semrush for Enterprise

To choose between Semrush and the Enterprise platform, answer these four questions:

**1. How big is your team, and how many markets or brands do you operate in?** Semrush covers the needs of solo marketers and small teams focused on one brand in one market. The Enterprise Platform serves teams managing visibility across multiple brands, regions, or business units with distributed local execution.

**2. Do you need governance and role-based access across teams?** Semrush supports one user by default and offers user add-ons at $45 - $100 per user, depending on the plan. The Enterprise Platform includes 40+ users, role-based permissions, multi-workspace controls, and unlimited guest users. Large distributed teams that need to collaborate without conflict will benefit from using the Enterprise Platform.

**3. Are you reporting visibility metrics to executives and connecting them to revenue?** Semrush serves practitioners reporting on rankings, visibility scores, and content opportunities. The Enterprise Platform equips CMOs and VPs leading large teams to connect organic visibility directly to pipeline and revenue conversations.

**4. What's your budget model — self-serve subscription or a custom enterprise contract?** Semrush offers transparent, predictable pricing with no sales conversation required. The Enterprise Platform delivers custom, contract-based pricing structured around the individual needs of each brand.

If you need to build a complex strategy for a multinational brand and have substantial resources allocated to your goals, Enterprise is the better fit. If you need to start growing your visibility today and your budget is limited, Semrush is a better choice.

## FAQs

### What's the difference between Semrush and Semrush for Enterprise?

Semrush is a brand visibility platform built for smaller teams, covering SEO, AI visibility, content, advertising, competitive research, and more in a self-serve setup. Semrush for Enterprise adds advanced automation, customization, governance controls, forecasting, and executive reporting for large organizations operating across multiple brands, markets, and teams.

### How much does Semrush cost?

Semrush plans start at $139/month. For combined SEO and AI visibility, Semrush One offers three plans:

- Starter: $199/month
- Pro+: $299/month
- Advanced: $549/month

Each plan includes different tracking limits and website allowances. Additional users are available as separate add-ons.

### How much does Semrush for Enterprise cost?

Semrush for Enterprise uses custom pricing based on your organization's size, workflows, reporting needs, and data requirements.

### Does Semrush include AI visibility tracking?

Yes. Semrush includes AI visibility tracking across major AI platforms and search experiences, including ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Perplexity.

### Can I upgrade from Semrush to Enterprise later?

Yes. Many teams start with Semrush and move to Enterprise as their reporting, automation, and collaboration needs evolve.
