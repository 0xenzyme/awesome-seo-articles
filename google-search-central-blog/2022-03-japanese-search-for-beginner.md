# Google Search for Beginners YouTube Series now available in Japanese

Check out the [English Google Search for Beginner series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKoqnv2vTMUOHPb5IJIn-7egNRmsvbPIE) or the [Japanese Google Search for Beginner series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKoqnv2vTMUNgFk9I7_A2cNrsi6b9gB77)!
